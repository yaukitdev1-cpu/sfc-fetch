The Hong Kong Monetary Authority (the “HKMA”) and Securities and Futures Commission (the “SFC”) welcome comments from market participants and interested parties on the subject consultation.

Please note:

    • If you wish to submit the viewpoints or ideas of the organisation you represent, please make sure you include the name of the organisation in your submission.
    • If you wish to suggest alternative approaches, we encourage you to send proposed amendments to the consultation document for our consideration.
    • The names of the respondents and the content submitted may be published on the HKMA and/or SFC Web site and in other documents published by the HKMA and/or SFC. Please see Personal Information Collection Statement for details.

If you wish to remain anonymous as a respondent, please so indicate in your submission.
