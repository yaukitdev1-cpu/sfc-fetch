The Hong Kong Monetary Authority (the “HKMA”) and the Securities and Futures Commission (the “SFC”) welcome comments from market participants and interested parties on the subject consultation. Comments on our proposals on the updated list of Financial Services Providers must reach the HKMA or the SFC by no later than 25 May 2019 and comments on our other proposals must reach the HKMA or the SFC by no later than 25 June 2019.

Please note:

  • If you wish to submit the viewpoints or ideas of the organisation you represent, please make sure you include the name of the organisation in your submission.
  • If you wish to suggest alternative approaches, we encourage you to send proposed amendments to the consultation document for our consideration.
  • The names of the respondents and the content submitted may be published on the websites of the HKMA / the SFC and in other documents published by the HKMA / the SFC. Please see Personal Information Collection Statement for details.

If you wish to remain anonymous as a respondent, please so indicate in your submission.
