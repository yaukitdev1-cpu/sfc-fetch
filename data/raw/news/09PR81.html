The continuing fall-out from the global financial crisis has highlighted the necessity of investors staying up-to-date with the status of and risks impacting any investment in credit-linked notes (CLNs). The Securities and Futures Commission has therefore issued a circular to CLN issuers reminding them of their risk-disclosure duties.

The circular stresses the importance of timely disclosure of meaningful and easy-to-understand information about risks associated with the notes so that investors can make informed decisions or conduct risk analysis of their credit exposure. Specifically, issuers are urged to disclose in a timely manner how overseas corporate failures may affect the performance of the notes.

The circular specifically urges issuers to disclose, among other things, any defaults by entities named in the underlying portfolio of collateral debt obligations backing a CLN as the note may be terminated early due to collateral default. This would most likely result in the amount recoverable by investor being significantly less than the original investment amount.

For investors holding retail CLNs, they should contact product distributors for up-to-date information on the on-going risks associated with the notes.
