Popular Canto-pop singers and artistes are helping the SFC to educate the public to invest wisely.

They will participate in a new SFC drama series to be broadcast on Commercial Radio One (CR1) starting 6 December 2003. CR1 has invited artistes like Mr Alex Fong, Mr Dexter Young and Ms Vindy Chan to act the voices of some of the characters.

The series, entitled "Talk on Wise Investing", is part of SFC’s continuous effort to provide investors with more information about investing.

There will be 15 five-minute dramas of different themes. Each drama will conclude with an interview with a market professional or an SFC representative on the do’s and don’ts of investing.

Commentator Mr Lennon Chan Wing Luk and the President of the Hong Kong Society of Financial Analysts, Mr Richard Mak, are among those who will appear on the show.

Mr Alex Fong plays the role of a cautious novice investor. In the first episode, he disagrees with his wife’s suggestion to subscribe for an IPO share without evaluating the company’s fundamentals whereas in the second episode he is keen to find out if he is still subject to pooling risk if he does not use the credit facility on his margin account (Note 1).

Other episodes will cover topics like fees and charges imposed by intermediaries in stock investment, rights of shareholders in listed companies’ corporate actions and how small investors can assess corporate governance. The series will also highlight the benefits and risks of investing in products like guaranteed funds, hedge funds, index tracking exchange traded funds, exotic warrants, bonds, commodity futures and equity-linked deposits (Note 2).

To listen to "Talk on Wise Investing", investors can tune in to CR1 (FM88.1 - 89.5) at 11am every Saturday. The series is part of the "Stockaeologist" programme.

After each episode, the audience can call CR1 at 1872 881 and participate in a quiz. The first two callers who answer the host’s questions correctly will each receive an SFC souvenir. The audience can also provide their views and feedback on the programme to the SFC by calling the Investor Hotline at 2840 9333 or sending an email to investor.info@hksfc.org.hk.

Since 1998, the SFC has jointly run investor education radio programmes with various radio stations. Some of these radio series are now available on the "Studio" section of the SFC-operated Electronic Investor Resources Centre (eIRC) at www.hkeirc.org.
