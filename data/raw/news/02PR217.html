The SFC has begun a monthly forum with brokers to discuss issues of common interest and to involve them in the early stages of policy consultation.

The regular meetings help deepen mutual understanding between the regulator and the practitioners, and will benefit the Hong Kong market as a whole.

The SFC has always maintained close contact with market practitioners. The new monthly meetings provide a regular platform for the industry and the SFC to openly and frankly exchange views on market and regulatory issues. It also serves as a channel for the Commission to soft consult the industry on its policy initiatives before the formal public consultation.

The SFC is pleased that representatives of the following broker groups are participating:

  • Hong Kong Association of Online Brokers
  • Hong Kong Securities and Futures Industry Staff Union
  • Hong Kong Securities Professionals Association
  • Hong Kong Stockbrokers Association
  • Institute of Securities Dealers

The first meeting was held today at the SFC. Participants exchanged views and constructive comments on various issues, including the current state of the industry, licensing arrangement under the Securities and Futures Ordinance, and compliance with the Financial Resources Rules and Code of Conduct, etc.

SFC Chairman Mr Andrew Sheng, who chaired the meeting, said: "We treasure every opportunity to communicate with the industry. The participation of the key brokers' groups in the meeting means that we can listen to a broad range of views. On the other hand, the SFC can explain its work to a cross section of the brokerage industry so that our policies can be better understood and appreciated by market practitioners."

The SFC has similar dialogues with other parts of the industry, such as fund managers and investment bankers. A presentation was given to over 110 members of Hong Kong Investment Funds Association on 1 November 2002, covering issues relating to application for fund authorization. The SFC also took the opportunity to communicate its philosophy and explain its regulatory stance to the participants with a view to raising the Commission's transparency.

"We will continue to work in partnership with practitioners in different sectors to enhance the quality of our market," said Mr Sheng.
