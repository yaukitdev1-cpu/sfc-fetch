The SFC today announces the new eligibility criteria and on-going obligations for sponsors, concluding the two-stage initiative of the SFC and Stock Exchange of Hong Kong to raise sponsor standards in Hong Kong.

Mrs Alexa Lam, SFC’s Executive Director of Intermediaries and Investment Products, said: “The SFC will regulate sponsors and compliance advisers more effectively in two aspects under the new regime. First, only those corporate finance advisory firms that meet the stringent eligibility criteria may act as sponsors. Firms should not assume that they will automatically pass the test. Sponsors and compliance advisers and their management are also reminded that they will be held responsible for their work.”

“Second, the SFC will inspect sponsors and compliance advisers regularly and on an ad-hoc basis to review their compliance with the regulations. The SFC will not hesitate to take action against firms for substandard work,” she said.

Mrs Lam said enhancing the sponsor regime was a key SFC response to the regulatory challenge to promote high standards of corporate governance and behaviour in the Hong Kong market. This is crucial to Hong Kong maintaining its status as a fund raising centre.

Last June, the SFC consulted the public on tightening the regulation of sponsors (Note 1). The SFC received 14 submissions from market practitioners, professional bodies, and individuals. In general, respondents supported the proposal to impose specific eligibility criteria and ongoing compliance obligations on sponsors.

The SFC has adopted the majority of the proposals and the details are set out in the Consultation Conclusions on the Consultation Paper on the Regulation of Sponsors and Compliance Advisers released today. The requirements will be introduced as the Guidelines for Sponsors and Compliance Advisers and shall come into effect on 1 January 2007.

Key requirements in the Guidelines for Sponsors and Compliance Advisers

A sponsor must meet, among others, the following eligibility criteria:

a) Sufficient expertise and resources – A sponsor shall have sufficient expertise and resources to handle sponsor work, and a minimum paid-up capital of $10 million. Professional indemnity is not mandatory;
b) Effective internal controls and management supervision – A sponsor’s management must ensure the sponsor’s compliance with codes and regulations. Management is responsible for the firm’s sponsor work; and
c) Transaction teams and Principals – A sponsor shall appoint at least two Principals; each transaction team should be supervised by at least one Principal.

Besides eligibility criteria, a sponsor must also comply with the following on-going obligations:

a) A sponsor shall carry out annual assessments of its internal systems and controls to ensure that they remain effective;
b) A sponsor shall maintain proper and updated records; and
c) Staff undertaking sponsor work shall have sufficient relevant continuous professional training.

Corporate finance advisory firms must at all times be qualified to act as sponsors under the Guidelines for Sponsors and Compliance Advisers in order to be eligible to act as compliance advisers.

The SFC will assist the industry in the transition. For example, the SFC will post FAQs on the transitional arrangements on its website.

To minimise any duplication of regulatory responsibilities in the areas of sponsor regulation, the SFC and the SEHK will work together to streamline the relevant Listing Rules requirements. The SFC will also continue to co-operate with SEHK in taking enforcement actions against IPO sponsor misconduct and failings.

The Consultation Conclusions is available at the SFC office and on the SFC website at www.sfc.hk. The Guidelines for Sponsors and Compliance Advisers is attached as Annex I to the Consultation Conclusions.
