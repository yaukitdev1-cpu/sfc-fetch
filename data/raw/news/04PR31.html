The SFC today publishes the February issue of SFC Alert, with a new look. The SFC Alert is a bi-monthly newsletter for both investors and intermediaries.

The SFC Alert now brings to readers more regular features, including Senior Management Interview, FAQs and useful lists of recently published circulars, investor education materials and other publications. The articles are more concise, with clear online references for those who are interested in the details. The layout is more reader-friendly while the newsletter is expanded from four to six pages to cover more events.

Leading the new SFC Alert is A fruitful year – 2003 in review, which highlights the SFC’s achievements in the past year and its priorities in 2004.

Inside, Mr Peter Au-Yang, Executive Director and Chief Operating Officer, kicks off the series of Senior management interview. Mr Au-Yang talks about why he joined the SFC and how he manages the Commission’s finances. Meanwhile, with tight expenditure control and increased income due to the buoyant market turnover, the SFC reported an accumulated surplus of $65.2 million for the nine months to December 2003. Quarterly results has more details.

A feasible blueprint on regulation of listing presents in brief the SFC’s response to the Government’s consultation on the proposals to enhance the regulation of listing. Be a professional and act like one emphasises the importance of quality work by listing sponsors.

On page four, Proposals to benefit Hong Kong’s 1,000,000 investors covers the four measures proposed by an SFC working group to address the risks involved in securities margin financing and the pooling and re-pledging of client collateral by securities margin finance providers.

FAQs, a new regular column answering questions from investors and intermediaries, explains why investors are charged a scrip fee even though no dividend has been declared by the company concerned.

Investor focus, another regular column, walks investors through the procedures of applying for new shares in an initial public offering.

On page six, Meet Dr Wise introduces to readers a new SFC investor education column which began last December.

The February issue of SFC Alert is the 22nd issue of the newsletter, which was first published in October 2001. Copies of the current issue of SFC Alert are available at the SFC office. All issues are available on the SFC website.
