The SFC warmly welcomes the re-appointment by the Financial Secretary, Mr Antony Leung, of Mr Daniel Fung, SC, Mr Thomas Brian Stevenson and Ms Anna Wu as Non-Executive Directors of the SFC.
 
"We depend a great deal on our Non-Executive Directors for their invaluable insight and active participation in the Commission's affairs," said SFC Acting Chairman, Mr Mark Dickens. "All of us at the Commission look forward to continuing to work closely with Mr Fung, Mr Stevenson and Ms Wu."
 
Mr Fung, Mr Stevenson and Ms Wu have been SFC Non-Executive Directors since January 1999.
 
Mr Fung now chairs the SFC’s Remuneration Committee. Mr Stevenson and Ms Wu are the chairman and deputy chairman of the Commission’s Audit Committee respectively.
