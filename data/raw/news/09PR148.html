The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) invites the public to participate in its “Investment Triathlon” financial quiz, which will kick off on 13 October 2009.

“The Investment Triathlon has been running for three consecutive years and has become a regular feature in our investor education campaigns. The quiz provides an opportunity to help investors understand the risks of financial investing, as well as their rights and responsibilities,” said Mr Martin Wheatley, the SFC’s Chief Executive Officer.

Three rounds of the quiz will take place via print and broadcast media. Winners will be awarded prizes including cash totalling $50,000 (Note 1).

In the first round, Hong Kong Economic Times will run a set of practical investment-related questions on three consecutive Tuesdays from 13 October. Answers can be submitted to the SFC via the InvestEd website (www.InvestEd.hk).

In the second round, 16 contestants will appear on the “Investment Era” programme to be aired on Radio 1 of Radio Television Hong Kong (RTHK). Industry professionals and an SFC executive will also discuss a range of investment issues during the programme to promote important educational messages (Note 2).

Eight winners from the second round will proceed to the final show which will be broadcast on Cable TV on 30 January 2010.

The SFC would like to thank the prize sponsors, our media partners and all supporting
organisations (Note 3).

Rules of the Investment Triathlon are attached. Investors can contact the SFC at 2840 9222 or InvestEd@sfc.hk for enquiries.
