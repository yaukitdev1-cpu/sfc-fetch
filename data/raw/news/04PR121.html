The SFC has today obtained judgment from the Honourable Justice Suffiad of the High Court of Hong Kong, under section 213 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance, enabling it to apply to the Court for an Order appointing Mr John Lees as the administrator in respect of the property of Mr Charles Schmitt, the Chief Executive Officer and major shareholder of Charles Schmitt & Associates Limited (CSAL).

The SFC’s application followed a restriction notice it served on CSAL on 15 June 2004 and an interim injunction obtained in the High Court on 15 June 2004 to preserve Mr Schmitt’s assets.  The injunction is in force until trial or until further order.

Mr Lees is independent from CSAL and Mr Schmitt. His appointment will aim principally to protect the interests of the clients of CSAL. In order to do this, the SFC will seek appropriate powers from the Court to enable Mr Lees to administer the property of Mr Schmitt and any other property he holds or controls. 

It is anticipated that the administrator will contact the clients of CSAL when he is in a position to do so. Meanwhile, clients may contact the administrator at 2842 5032 for enquiries.
