The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) invites the public to participate in an investor education event on 16 January 2010 to help them review their investment perspectives following a year of volatility in the global financial markets.

Investor Day 2010, entitled “Market outlook and investment attitude for 2010”, is jointly organised by the SFC and The Open University of Hong Kong (OUHK). Renowned academics and seasoned professionals (Note 1) will share their insights on the global economic outlook, inflation and its implications on investment, as well as a practical guide to understanding financial reports of listed companies (Note 2).

“The global financial turmoil may have subsided, but market volatility will not go away soon. Recent volatility suggests many risks remain in the global markets and investors should stay vigilant,” said the SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, Mr Martin Wheatley.

“This event provides investors with tools to establish a healthy approach to investing. We have always encouraged investors to regularly review their investment strategies and consider whether their portfolios still suit their circumstances. The New Year seems like a good time to do that,” he added.

The full-day event will take place on the OUHK campus.

The public can log on to the OUHK website (http://www.ouhk.edu.hk/lipace/invest) or the InvestEd website (www.InvestEd.hk) to enrol. Enrolment is on a first-come-first-served basis. Admission is free.
