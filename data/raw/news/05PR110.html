A Preparatory Working Group (PWG) has been formed for the development in Hong Kong of an evolving global standard for electronic communication of business and financial data.

Known as XBRL, the eXtensible Business Reporting Language is being considered for adoption in major markets including the US, UK, Japan and the Mainland in the areas of regulatory filings and the filing of corporation tax returns. XBRL greatly increases the speed of handling of financial data, reduces the chance of error and permits automatic checking of information.

Today, representatives from various government agencies, regulatory authorities, and industry and professional associations attended the inaugural meeting of the PWG. A list of the participating organisations is in Note 1.

What is XBRL?

The idea behind XBRL is simple. By providing an identifying tag for each individual item of data, it facilitates standardisation for processing of accounting information. Each individual accounting class of asset is given a unique XBRL tag according to International Accounting Standard.

Previously, without XBRL, such consolidation would require manual (and therefore costly) adjustment of each category of accounting information into a standard format. The introduction of XBRL tags enables automated processing of business information by computer, reducing costly re-classification and facilitating analysis (Note 2).

The PWG

The PWG is formed on a voluntary basis to take forward the development of XBRL in Hong Kong. Its key objectives and action areas are:

  • Identify the regulatory and business applications of XBRL for financial reporting environment in Hong Kong;
  • Recommend to adopt the relevant accounting standards and regulatory requirements for XBRL implementation;
  • Prepare to form the "XBRL Jurisdiction" in Hong Kong (e.g. XBRL-Hong Kong); and
  • Come up with a time frame/roadmap for XBRL development in Hong Kong and co-operation with other established XBRL Jurisdictions around the world.


Under the proposed framework for the PWG, there is a Steering Committee which provides the necessary leadership and direction. SFC Chairman, Mr Andrew Sheng, is Convenor of the Steering Committee.

In his opening remarks today, Mr Sheng said: "As an international financial centre, Hong Kong must not fall behind in this growing trend of XBRL development."

The development in the Mainland is of particular relevance to Hong Kong. Mr Sheng said: "With an increasing number of Mainland companies co-listing their H and A shares in Hong Kong and the Mainland, it is important for Hong Kong to start thinking how to align the information reporting standards and requirements of the two markets so as to allow the users of financial information in both markets to enjoy the benefits brought about by the advance in technology. Such benefits include the enhancement in the transparency, availability and accessibility of financial information that would result in better quality of the securities market."

In this connection, both the SFC and Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited (HKEx) have agreed to support in promoting the awareness and use of XBRL in Hong Kong. The SFC will work with HKEx to develop a pilot project to enable the submission of financial information by listed companies to HKEx and the SFC using XBRL.

Three Sub-groups are also formed under the PWG to focus on different aspects of XBRL implementation: Regulatory and Business Applications, Taxonomies, and Technology Architecture and Tools (Note 3).
