SFC’s Chief Operating Officer, Mr Peter Au-Yang, today explained the SFC’s revised estimates for 2005/06 and the proposed estimates for 2006/07 at the Legislative Council Panel on Financial Affairs.

Mr Au-Yang said the SFC had budgeted a mild increase in operating expenditure for 2006/07, as compared to the 2005/06 estimates. The increase was mainly attributable to increase in personnel expenses, staff training and development, and professional and other expenses.

Mr Au-Yang said: “There will be lots of challenges ahead of us and our workload is expected to increase further as we assume new regulatory functions and continue to enforce the Securities and Futures Ordinance.”

“In 2006/07, we shall continue to tightly control all our expenditure. However, if required, additional resources will be allocated to areas which are considered necessary to improve the quality of our market or to enhance Hong Kong’s status as a preferred fund raising centre in the region,” he said.

The SFC has not requested for any appropriation for 14 years in a row.
