The SFC is pleased to announce the Public Shareholders Group membership for 2006-2007.

The SFC has appointed 12 members to the Group for a one-year term, effective 1 July 2006.

Two existing members retired from the Group. They are Professor Andrew Chan and Mr Vincent Duhamel. The remaining members have been re-appointed.

The Public Shareholders Group is a standing committee established under section 8 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance. The Group’s members include representatives of retail and institutional investors, professionals, academics, prominent advocates of investors’ rights, and the current Chairman of the Consumer Council. Last year, the Group held five meetings to discuss a wide range of topics. Views gathered from the Group have assisted the Corporate Finance and other divisions of the SFC in considering issues relating to shareholders’ interests and formulation of regulatory proposals.

Mr Brian Ho, SFC’s Acting Head and Senior Director of Corporate Finance and Chairman of the Group, said: “The Public Shareholders Group has played an important role in providing insight and feedback to the Commission over the last year. The Commission very much appreciates members’ generous support and valuable advice. We wish to thank especially Professor Andrew Chan and Mr Vincent Duhamel who are retiring from the Group. We also welcome all the re-appointed members and look forward to working closely with them.”
