The SFC today releases a document setting out the regulatory challenges and risks that it faces and the strategies to address them.

The document is the result of a comprehensive analysis of the environment in which the SFC and the market operate. It provides a strategic framework for the SFC for the next three financial years. This is the first time in recent years that the SFC has articulated at a high level the regulatory challenges and published co-ordinated responses to them.

Four major regulatory challenges have been identified, and for each a set of initiatives will be in place to ensure that the statutory regulatory objectives of the SFC are met.

The four challenges are:

1. Improving standards of corporate governance and behaviour;

2. Tackling risks arising from complex and structured products, and mis-selling to retail investors;

3. Addressing the consequences of a market or economic downturn if and when that happens; and

4. Keeping Hong Kong at the forefront of international financial markets and promoting regulatory best practice.

A total of 21 initiatives in the four areas of regulation, enforcement, education and market facilitation have been mapped out to address the above challenges and associated risks, and to achieve the objective of putting “Investors First”. Some of the initiatives are already in place and are on going.

SFC Chairman, Mr Martin Wheatley, said: “This document shows how we will meet our strategic objectives through regulation, enforcement, education of investors, and market facilitation. As a responsible regulator, we need to focus on issues of the greatest importance and prioritise accordingly. This forward looking plan helps the SFC determine the major initiatives to pursue in the next few years. The SFC looks forward to working with the Government, other agencies and market participants to achieve the objectives of the plan.”

The SFC also acknowledges that it must have proper regulatory processes and attract the best possible talent in order to bring the plan to fruition.

The document, “Regulatory Challenges and Responses”, is attached for your reference. It is also available on the SFC website.
