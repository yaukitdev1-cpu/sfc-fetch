The SFC is pleased to announce that it has exchanged Side Letters with the Monetary Authority of Macau (AMCM) under an existing Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) to strengthen regulatory co-operation.

On 14 July 2000, the SFC signed an MOU with the AMCM on mutual assistance and exchange of information. The Side Letters explain how the SFC’s and the AMCM’s requests will be handled by each other subject to the MOU, by setting out details on what information will be provided and how information will be provided.

Mr Andrew Sheng, Chairman of the SFC, and Mr Anselmo Teng, Chairman of the AMCM, exchanged the Side Letters today at the office of AMCM in Macau. The two chairmen also took the opportunity to discuss market matters of mutual interest.

Mr Sheng welcomes the exchange of the Side Letters. He said: "The Side Letters further strengthen our co-operation with AMCM and we will continue to work in partnership with our Macau counterpart to enhance the quality of our respective markets."
