The SFC will gazette on 30 September 2005 an amendment to section 11.3 of the Introduction to the Hong Kong Codes on Takeovers and Mergers and Share Repurchases to increase the membership of the Takeovers Panel from 30 to 40. The amendment is intended to facilitate the expeditious and efficient dealing of matters by the Panel. The effective date of operation of the revised provision is 1 October 2005.
