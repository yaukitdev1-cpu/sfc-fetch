The SFC has appointed four new members to the SFC (HKEC Listing) Committee.

The SFC welcomes Mr Stephen Clark, a Corporate Finance Managing Director at HSBC, and Mr Carlson Tong, a partner at KPMG, as new market representatives. Mr Eric Cheng, SFC’s Senior Director of Enforcement, and Ms Tina So, Director of Investment Products, have been appointed as new SFC representatives. They will join the six existing members. A full list of Committee members is provided in Note 1.

The SFC (HKEC Listing) Committee was established pursuant to the Securities and Futures Commission Ordinance following the listing of Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited (HKEx) in June 2000. It exercises powers and functions equivalent to those of the Main Board and GEM Listing Committees of The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited when real or potential conflicts of interests arise between the interests of HKEx and those of the proper performance of the Stock Exchange’s listing functions. In such an event, it is desirable for the relevant Stock Exchange functions to be exercised by the SFC.

The framework for the regulation of HKEx (as a listed company) and other applicants for listing and issuers by the Commission in cases of real or potential conflicts of interests on the part of the Stock Exchange is provided in Chapter 38 of the Main Board Listing Rules and Chapter 36 of the GEM Listing Rules.

The new appointments are made as a result of changes to Main Board Listing Rule 38.06. The rule changes are being announced today by the Stock Exchange and will take effect tomorrow upon publication of the Stock Exchange’s announcement. The rule now provides, among other things, that the Committee shall consist of not fewer than 10 members, comprising at least five individuals (not being directors or employees of the SFC) with experience of the securities market in Hong Kong who are appointed by the SFC and at least five representatives from the SFC.

The current membership of the Committee comprises three market representatives and three SFC representatives. The rule changes require the SFC to appoint two new market representatives and two new SFC representatives to the Committee. Both Mr Clark and Mr Tong are appointed for a term commencing today and ending on 31 March 2004. The two new SFC representatives are appointed without a fixed term.

An SFC spokesman said: "The Committee is a voluntary body which owes its success to the dedication and expertise of its members. The SFC is confident that the significant experience and expert knowledge of the Committee members in the securities and financial markets will ensure the continued success of the Committee."
