The SFC today publishes a special Dr Wise issue, How much do you know about REITs?

In this issue, Dr Wise notes that Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs) has in recent weeks been the centre of attention in the market.   Dr Wise highlights the characteristics of REITs, and explains the differences between REITs and other investments such as unit trusts, bonds and property stocks.

He reminds investors that the SFC has published an investor education leaflet called “Real Estate Investment Trusts – What You Have to Know”, and produced “Property Yields”, a television documentary-drama, to enhance the investors’ knowledge about REITs.  Both the investor education leaflet and the drama are available on the SFC operated Electronic Investor Resources Centre (eIRC) at www.eirc.hk.

Dr Wise is a fictitious character. He represents the collective wisdom and knowledge of market specialists and experts at the SFC. Dr Wise, through his column, talks to investors about the key issues of investing and regulatory thoughts.

Dr Wise's Column is published on the last Tuesday of every month, starting from December 2003. From time to time, additional articles may be published on topical issues. The column is posted under the “Features” section of the eIRC. Investors are welcome to send their feedback and questions to Dr Wise by email at investor.info@sfc.hk.
