The SFC has been able to keep its expenditure for the current year within budget and has proposed only a modest increase for 2007-08 to ensure it discharges its regulatory functions effectively, SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, Mr Martin Wheatley told legislators today.

For the current year ending 31 March 2007, the SFC expects its revenue to reach $1,153.5 million due to robust market activities. With estimated total expenditure at $555.2 million, a surplus of $598 million is projected. Mr Wheatley said at a Legislative Council Panel on Financial Affairs meeting today that the SFC was consulting the Financial Secretary about how it might utilise the surplus reserves.

For 2007-08, the SFC projects a revenue of $1,077.8 million. The SFC has budgeted a 7.9% increase in operating expenses to $599.3 million, mainly due to an increase in personnel expenses arising from pay and benefits adjustments to address retention concerns, and posts to cope with new responsibilities.

Mr Wheatley said: “Despite substantial increases in our workload, which has become increasingly complex, we continue to exercise discipline in tightly controlling our headcount and we have budgeted for only a very modest increase in staff numbers.”

“There will be many challenges ahead of us and our workload is expected to increase further as we assume new regulatory functions and continue to enforce the Securities and Futures Ordinance. In particular, the statutory backing of the HKEx Listing Rules will introduce additional responsibilities that have implications for our staff establishment in future years,” he added.

The SFC has not requested any appropriation from LegCo for 15 years in a row.
