The SFC releases today a Consultation Conclusions paper on the proposed revisions to the Prevention of Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing Guidance Note.

The SFC issued a consultation paper on the revision of the Guidance Note on 29 April 2005 for a consultation period of six weeks. The SFC received comments from several industry practitioners and professional organisations, and a law firm which submitted comments on behalf of 12 international investment firms. All comments including those submitted by the respondents after the end of the consultation period were considered by the SFC in preparing the Consultation Conclusions.

The overall response to the consultation was positive. The respondents generally acknowledged the need to enhance the regulatory framework to combat money laundering and terrorist financing. Specific comments focused on issues relating to the customer due diligence requirements, and sought clarification on the interpretation of some of the provisions in the proposed revised Guidance Note.

The revised Guidance Note seeks to:

(1) bring our requirements on prevention of money laundering and terrorist financing on a par with the latest standards and principles set by the Financial Action Task Force on Money Laundering and the International Organization of Securities Commissions; and

(2) build in a fair degree of flexibility to allow firms to implement the provisions on a risk-sensitive basis. This is achieved by adopting a principle-based approach (rather than a prescriptive one) and giving guidance on areas where clarification of issues concerning the practical application of the Guidance Note is necessary.

Licensed corporations and associated entities are encouraged to comply with the revised Guidance Note as soon as possible, and in any case they should do so not later than 30 April 2006, when the revised Guidance Note will become effective. The revised Guidance Note will be published in the Gazette in due course.

Copies of the Consultation Conclusions are available at the SFC office and on the SFC website.
