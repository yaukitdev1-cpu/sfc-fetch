The SFC made significant achievements in four priority areas last year, Mr Andrew Sheng, Chairman of the SFC, said at a media briefing today.

Mr Sheng reviewed the financial position of the SFC for the first nine months of the current financial year (Note 1), and reported the operational highlights for the year 2004.

Accumulated revenue up to 31 December 2004 was $456 million, while expenditure (including depreciation) was $334 million. These resulted in a surplus of $122 million for the three quarters.

Mr Sheng noted a continuous recovery in the stockbroking sector. Total gross securities commission income for the industry was $16 billion for the 11 months to November 2004, already 23% higher than the $13 billion for the whole of 2003. Total net profit was $7 billion, 80% higher than the $3.9 billion for 2003. Employment in the industry was also up 7% from the beginning of 2004.

Mr Sheng reviewed the achievements last year and discussed some of the ongoing and future projects in the four priorities: keeping up market standards, facilitating market development, enforcing the law, and protecting and educating investors.

An outline of Mr Sheng’s presentation is attached.

SFC Review of 2004

 
 

Mr Andrew Sheng
Chairman
Securities and Futures Commission
 
 
25 January 2005


1. Healthy surplus for SFC

  • Revenue for 9 months to 12/04 at $456 mn (23% increase over the same previous period)
  • Expenditure at $334 mn, close to the approved budget of $333 mn
  • Surplus at $122 mn ($65 mn for the same previous period)

 
2. Recovery in brokerage sector

  • Average daily stock market turnover (Jan/04 - Nov/04) at $16 bn – ($11 bn for the whole of 2003)
  • Gross securities commission income at $16 bn (11 mths) (23% higher than the $13 bn for 2003)
  • Total net profit of $7 bn (11 mths) (80% higher than the $3.9 bn for 2003)
  • Brokers employed 9,300 licensed persons in Sep/04 (7% up from 8,700 in Dec/03)

 
3. How we did in 2004

  • Four priorities in 2004

- Keep up market standards
- Facilitate market development
- Enforce the law
- Protect and educate investor

4. Keep up market standards

  • HKEx’s Code on Corporate Governance Practices (1 Jan 2005) – significantly enhancing quality of HK listed companies
  • Sponsors and IFAs – Listing Rules amendments on 1 Jan 2005; SFC will consult on eligibility requirements of sponsors and IFAs in 1H/05; theme inspection on sponsors in 2H/05


5. Keep up market standards (2)

  • Successful implementation of dual filing - public consensus that the quality of new IPOs has begun to improve
  • Consulting on proposed amendments to the Stock Market Listing Rules (SMLR) to codify the more important Listing Rules requirements (consultation ends 31 March 2005)

 
6. Keep up market standards (3)

  • Providing $5 mn seed funding to the FRRP and IIB for enhanced oversight of the accounting profession
  • Completed theme inspection on investment advisers to tackle mis-selling
  • Consulted the market on measures to address risks arising from securities margin financing – proposals well received by LegCo’s Financial Affairs Panel.

 
7. Keep up market standards (4)

  • Guidelines to address analyst conflicts of interest to become effective on 1 April 2005 – based mainly on IOSCO principles, fine tuned to suit HK market conditions
  • Participated in the IOSCO Task Force that issued the IOSCO Code of Conduct Fundamentals For Credit Rating Agencies worldwide (Dec 2004)

 
8. Facilitate market development

  • Consulting on a review of the Codes on Takeovers and Mergers and Share Repurchases
  • Soon to consult on a thorough update of HK’s regime for offering of securities
  • Devising measures to facilitate  processing of UCITS III funds
  • Enabling investments in SFC-authorised REITs by SFC-authorised funds; to consult on investments in overseas listed REITs in Q1/05

 
9. Facilitate market development (2)

  • To consult on overseas investments by SFC-authorised REITs in Q1/05
  • To consult on a review of the Guidelines on Hedge Funds in Q2/05
  • Consulting on streamlining the disclosure of interests regime (Part XV of SFO)
  • Consulting on suspending investor compensation fund levies
  • Revamp of Stock Warrant Market in 2001/02 working

 
10. Facilitate market development (3)

  • Agreed with HKEx on its model for a Scripless securities market
  • Supervised HKEx’s launch of the Derivatives Clearing and Settlement System and the related Common Collateral Management System for Derivatives
  • FinNet reaching critical mass with Credit Reference service, used by all banks in HK


11. Enforce the law

  • Priority in corporate misconduct and market misconduct
  • First listed company disclosure prosecution under dual filing in September 2004
  • Listed company investigations increased to 19 cases in the 18 months since SFO to Sept 2004 (8 in the preceding 18 months)

 
12. Enforce the law (2)

  • 13 successful market manipulation prosecutions in the 18 months (10 in the preceding 18 months)
  • Referred 20 cases for Police or ICAC investigations in the 18 months (12 in the preceding 18 months)
  • Proportionate sanctions against misconduct with disciplinary fines under SFO.   Many backlog cases cleared

 
13. Enforce the law (3)

  • Settle to achieve better outcomes and best use of resources

- Towry Law case set record with $255 mn payment offer to investors
- Flexibility on terms of settlement where public interest warrants

14. Educate investors

  • Dr Wise’s Column – an effective channel to educate and alert investors on market issues (e.g. warrants & concept stocks)
  • Highly rated TV drama, and radio programme with RTHK
  • Overwhelming response to teachers' workshops
  • Revamped SFC and eIRC websites, and online investment simulation games  

 
15. International

  • SFC heavily engaged in IOSCO Technical Committee work, as Chair since October 2003
  • Signed Letters of Intent with the Indonesian Capital Market Supervisory Agency and the Thailand SEC, working towards mutual recognition of investment products
  • Working on similar regulatory co-operation with several other jurisdictions for the creation of a pan-Asian securities market
  • Signed MOU with the Philippine SEC
  • Host city of IOSCO Annual Conference 2006

 
16. 2005

  • Continue to enforce SFO, with emphasis on raising market quality
  • 2005/06 likely to be surplus year financially, but probably not as large as 2004/05

 
17. Thank you
