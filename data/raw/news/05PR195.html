The SFC is pleased to announce that the 31st IOSCO Annual Conference, to be held in Hong Kong on 5-8 June 2006, is open for registration. IOSCO members and interested parties can now register online at www.iosco2006.org (Note 1).

The conference website provides details including information on the conference venue, the preliminary programme, registration fees, choice of hotels and their special rates. It is presented in the four official languages of IOSCO, namely English, French, Spanish and Portuguese, plus Simplified Chinese. The website will be updated continuously with new information when available, including details of the Public Panel topics, the evening programme and post-conference tours.

Participants can register online or print out the registration form and return it by post or fax.

Enquiries about registration, hotel reservation and other logistical arrangements can be made to the Conference Secretariat, International Conference Consultants Limited.
