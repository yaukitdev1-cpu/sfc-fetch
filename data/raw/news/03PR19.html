The SFC today publishes the February issue of SFC Enforcement Reporter, summarising the Commission’s enforcement actions in the month of January 2003 and discussing the compliance issues highlighted by those recent cases.

In January, the SFC filed a bankruptcy petition against Mr Law Siu Kong, Christopher, the sole proprietor of Lawsons Securities Company, under the Securities and Futures Commission Ordinance. This is to protect the client and business assets held by Law and the firm. Law is already the subject of a restriction notice and a mareva injunction order. The petition will be heard on 19 March.
 
During the same period, the SFC disciplined five intermediaries for various misconduct, including entering into placing arrangements that created a false and misleading appearance of a greater demand for derivative warrants, breaches of licensing conditions, poor audit trails and improper complaint handling.
 
Since 1 April 2002, the SFC has successfully prosecuted 29 people and disciplined 62 entities.
 
The February issue of SFC Enforcement Reporter is the 4th issue of the newsletter, copies of which are available at the SFC's office. This issue and all the previous issues are also available on the Commission's website at www.hksfc.org.hk, under the section “Press Releases, Publications & Speeches – Publications”.
 
The public are welcome to subscribe to the newsletter by registering for the SFC Website Update Email Alert service on the Commission’s homepage. They will then be able to receive the newsletter via email.
