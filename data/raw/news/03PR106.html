The SFC is pleased to announce the appointment of members to the Takeovers and Mergers Panel and the Takeovers Appeal Committee for the year 1 April 2003 to 31 March 2004 (please see attached list).

The SFC welcomes Mr John Maguire as new member of the Panel. The SFC also welcomes the re-appointment of Mr Kevin Westley as Chairman and Mr Clifton Chiu and Mr Henry Fan as Deputy Chairmen of the Panel.

Mr Frederick Ma stepped down from the Panel on 29 June 2002, prior to his appointment as Secretary for Financial Services and the Treasury on 1 July 2002.

The SFC also welcomes the appointment of Mr Timothy Freshwater and Mr Rajat Jindal as Chairman and Deputy Chairman of the Takeovers Appeal Committee.

The Panel and Appeal Committee are voluntary bodies which owe their success to the dedication and expertise of their members. The SFC is confident that the significant experience of members in takeovers and mergers, and in the securities and financial markets will ensure the continued success of the Panel and the Appeal Committee.
