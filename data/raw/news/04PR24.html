It has been more than 10 months since the new regime under the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO) was implemented on 1 April 2003. The SFC would like to issue the following update and reminder on matters relating to licensing and migration.

Licensees migrating into the new regime

Migration to the new licensing regime by intermediaries continued steadily. As of the end of December 2003, about 7,400 licensees representing 42% of all those migrating had lodged their migration applications under the new regime, up from 25% as of the end of September 2003.

Early migration to enjoy discount on annual fees

The SFC reminds intermediaries registered or exempted under the former regime that they can enjoy a 5% discount on annual fees during the transitional period if they submit their migration applications before the end of March 2004. The SFC issued a circular to licensed corporations on 27 January 2004 to draw their attention to certain matters concerning migration such that the application process can be expedited (Note 1).

Samples of completed licensing forms

In a continuous effort to facilitate the provision of investment services by intermediaries, the SFC has posted on its website samples of completed licensing application forms. The sample forms are in respect of applications for new licences by a corporation or an individual, miscellaneous applications such as change of accreditation and variation of regulated activities, and notifications such as cessation of business and change of auditor. These samples complement the completed sample forms for licensed persons migrating to the new licensing regime published earlier (Note 2).
