The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has joined with Radio Television Hong Kong (RTHK) to create “SFC in Action 3”, a law-enforcement television drama premiering on TVB Jade and RTHK TV 31 on Wednesday, 5 August (Note 1).

“SFC in Action 3” is the third series of programmes (Note 2) highlighting the importance of vigorous enforcement actions for safeguarding the integrity of the market and protecting the interests of investors. The new series consists of six half-hour episodes each built around a different type of enforcement case, namely insider dealing, market manipulation, unlicensed activities, corporate mis-governance, misappropriation and misleading research reports. 

“This is a dramatised version of the SFC’s enforcement work, but the underlying message is very serious:  for wrongdoers in our financial markets, the consequences will far outweigh the gains,” said Mr Mark Steward, the SFC’s Executive Director of Enforcement.
