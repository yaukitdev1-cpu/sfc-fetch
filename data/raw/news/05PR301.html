The SFC today publishes the December issue of Dr Wise's Column, Disclosure of Interests Enhances Transparency, at its investor education portal, the Electronic Investor Resources Centre (eIRC) at www.eirc.hk.

In this issue, Dr Wise explains that the overriding objective of the disclosure of interests regime is to provide investors with more complete information on a timely basis to enable them to make informed investment decisions. Under the regime, the substantial shareholders and directors of a listed company are required to disclose their interests and changes of their interests in the company.

Dr Wise reminds investors not to simply look at the figures reported by substantial shareholders and directors. In order to have a clearer picture and avoid double counting, it is important to read the details of a disclosure, including the event leading to it, the nature of the interests, the price and quantity of shares involved, the capacity in which the filer holds the interests, the holding vehicles involved and the identity of the ultimate controller.
