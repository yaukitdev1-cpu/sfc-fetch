The SFC’s first cross-media financial knowledge quiz has drawn around 600 contestants, with half of them having answered the questions correctly.

The first stage of the Investment Triathlon has been concluded. Twenty of the participants who have answered the questions correctly were drawn to take part in the second stage.

While many participants scored well, there were areas where some investors appeared to lack an understanding of some investment basics. For example, a third of the participants misunderstood the significance of the ex-dividend date, and mistakenly thought that investors were entitled to dividend payments even if a stock is bought on the ex-dividend date (Note 1).

The SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, Mr Martin Wheatley, said: “The result shows that some investors would benefit from refreshing their understanding of investment basics. The second leg of the triathlon will be broadcast soon and while the contestants battle it out, listeners can use the opportunity to enhance their investment knowledge.”

The second stage of the competition will start this Saturday (13 October 2007). The 20 participants will be randomly paired to compete during the “Investment Era” programme of Radio Television of Hong Kong over the coming 10 weeks. After listening to a radio drama on a specific topic (Note 2), the two contestants will have to compete to answer five questions. The ten winners will proceed to the final, which will be broadcast by Cable TV in early 2008. All participants who have answered the questions correctly in stage one will be invited to witness the crowning of the champion, who will take home $30,000.

The Investment Triathlon is a new SFC investor education initiative for the public. This competition aims to help investors understand financial investments and their risks (Note 3).
