The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has begun today a two-month consultation to solicit public comments on proposals to expand the lists of “specified” local and overseas stock and futures exchanges contained in Schedule 1 to the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO).

The proposed amendments are in response to the Government’s initiative to offer tax exemptions to offshore funds engaged in futures trading as a means to reinforce Hong Kong’s status as an international financial centre.

“These are technical amendments to add nine exchanges for the purposes of facilitating the development of Hong Kong as an international financial centre in general, and an asset management centre in particular, by expanding tax exemptions under the Inland Revenue Ordinance for offshore funds engaged in futures trading,” said Mr Martin Wheatley, the SFC’s Chief Executive Officer. “The SFC is also taking this opportunity to update the names of several of the exchanges in the lists.”

In connection with the SFO and subsidiary legislation made under it, exchanges in one or both lists are given the “specified” status for certain purposes relating to licensing, recognised counterparty status, disclosure of interests and price stabilising. A number of other Ordinances also refer to one or both lists (Note 1). Although several exchanges have had name changes, the lists have not been updated since 2002.

Until 21 April 2011, comments may be submitted to the SFC by e-mail to specifiedexchangesconsult@sfc.hk, by post or by fax to 2521 7884.

Note:

  1. The list of specified stock exchanges is referred to in the Mass Transit Railway Ordinance (Cap. 556) and Occupational Retirement Schemes Ordinance (Cap. 426). Both lists are referred to in the Trustee Ordinance (Cap. 29), Gambling Ordinance (Cap. 148) and Inland Revenue Ordinance (Cap. 112).
