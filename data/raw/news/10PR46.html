The Securities and Futures Commission’s (SFC) first ever TV drama drives home the message that an effective enforcement regime is vital in bringing market offenders to justice.

SFC in Action, produced jointly by the SFC and Radio Television Hong Kong, will make its debut on TVB Jade during prime time on 1 May. The six-episode drama depicts how the SFC’s investigators manage to crack even the most intricate plots by perpetrators seeking unlawful gains.

“The SFC has employed a diversity of channels to promote understanding of our regulatory work. This drama gives realistic portraits of market misconduct that preys on innocent investors, even though the characters and plots are entirely fictitious,” Dr Eddy Fong, the SFC’s Chairman, said at the launch ceremony of the drama today.

“Orderly securities and futures markets are essential to the continuing development of Hong Kong as a world class financial centre. We hope the series will help to bring this message to the public and enhance understanding of the important role of the SFC as the regulator in maintaining a fair marketplace,” Dr Fong added.

The series will be aired from 8:00pm to 8:30pm on six consecutive Saturdays starting this week.
