Most people recognise that investing can be a means to expand wealth but they tend to forget that they must save enough money first to invest.

In his latest column, "Know Common Money Mistakes to Invest Wisely," Dr Wise reminds us to manage money prudently. He highlights some common money mistakes that people make:

  • Failing to budget: Budgeting is key to good money-management. Knowing your budget helps to keep your spending in check.
  • Spending as if there's no tomorrow: Advertisements and peer pressure push people to buy, often on impulse, ignoring the consequences of over-spending with credit cards and personal loans.
  • Not saving for contingencies: Saving for a rainy day is essential even when supplemented by sensible insurance coverage.
  • Not planning enough for retirement: With longer life expectancies, today's adults need more retirement funds than previous generations.
  • Not investing wisely: Aligning a portfolio with investment objectives and risk appetite, and understanding the features of an investment product and its associated risks are important to wise investing. People should also think very carefully before borrowing to invest.


"Be in control of your finances at all times," Dr Wise says. "Make the most of your hard-earned money and be smart when you plan, save and invest!"

Through his column, Dr Wise, a fictitious character created by the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC), shares with the public his wisdom on investing and his thoughts on regulatory issues.

Dr Wise’s column is published on the last working Tuesday of every month. The column is posted under the "Dr Wise" section of the SFC’s InvestEd website. Investors are welcome to send their feedback and questions to Dr Wise by email to InvestEd@sfc.hk.
