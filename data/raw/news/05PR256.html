The SFC is pleased to announce that its corporate website at www.sfc.hk is one of the recipients of the Gold Award in the Web Care Award 2005.

The Web Care Award 2005, organised by the Internet Professional Association, recognises user-friendly websites that can be easily accessed by everyone, including people with disabilities, with a view to “promoting no-barrier websites and building a digitally inclusive society” (Note 1).

In August 2005, the SFC introduced a text-only version of its corporate website, allowing visually impaired visitors to navigate and browse key information about the SFC. The graphical website has functions to enable users to retrieve information that is of interest to particular user groups, store their most frequently visited webpages, reformat a webpage for trouble-free printing and switch language instantly, etc. In addition, color contrast and table format have been fine-tuned and enhanced to facilitate browsing by the general public.

Mr Ian Johnston, Adviser to Chairman’s Office of the SFC, said: “The SFC is pleased to be recognised for its efforts to make the SFC website a useful portal of regulatory information for the market and the general public. We have been working hard to present our materials better so that our stakeholders can use them with ease. We will continue to improve the SFC website.”
