Reviewing regulatory developments in the 10 years since the return of Hong Kong to China, SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, Mr Martin Wheatley, said the development of the market in the past decade showed investors, issuers and intermediaries confidence in Hong Kong’s regulatory regime.

“Our close connection with the Mainland also offers opportunities for the future. In developing these opportunities, our quality regulatory environment complements other advantages Hong Kong has to offer: a critical mass of professionals, a favourable business environment, sound market infrastructure and strong international ties,” he said. “I am confident that, by capitalising on these advantages, Hong Kong will continue to play a unique role as a ‘platform’ bridging the Mainland and the international marketplace.”

The SFC today publishes an Information Note and a chart setting out the major market events and regulatory developments between 1997 and now. The note is available on the SFC website.
