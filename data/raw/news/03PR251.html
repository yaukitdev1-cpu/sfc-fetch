The SFC warmly welcomes the new appointment by the Financial Secretary of Mr Christopher Cheng as a Non-Executive Director to succeed Mr Henry Fan, whose term expires today, and the re-appointment of the Hon Jasper Tsang as a Non-Executive Director. 

“We depend a great deal on our Non-Executive Directors for their professional advice and invaluable insight. Their support is vital to the SFC’s work in enhancing the quality of the Hong Kong financial market following the implementation of the Securities and Futures Ordinance. I thank them all for their hard work and devotion,” said Mr Andrew Sheng, SFC Chairman. 

“I am particularly grateful to Mr Henry Fan for his exemplary contribution to the Commission affairs and the development of our market in the past eight years.  Mr Fan has unstintingly provided to the Commission the breadth and depth of his wide corporate and legal experience.  On behalf of the Board, I would also like to welcome Mr Christopher Cheng who will bring with him a wealth of professional expertise and public service experience, which will be of tremendous help to the SFC. I am also very pleased to be able to continue working with the Hon Jasper Tsang, whose independent views and LegCo experience have benefited the Commission so much,” Mr Sheng said. “All of us at the SFC look forwards to working closely with Mr Cheng, Mr Tsang and all the other Non-Executive Directors in the months ahead.”  

The Chief Executive has also appointed Dr York Liao to succeed Mr Fan as an ex-officio member of the Process Review Panel (PRP), which reviews the SFC’s internal operating procedures. The SFC welcomes the appointment. “We will continue co-operating fully with the PRP to enhance the accountability and transparency of the SFC’s operations,” Mr Sheng said. 

Mr Fan has been an SFC Non-Executive Director since November 1995. He is a member of the Commission’s Budget Committee, Remuneration Committee, Takeovers and Mergers Panel and Share Registrars’ Disciplinary Committee.  

Mr Tsang has been an SFC Non-Executive Director since November 2001. He is currently sitting on the Remuneration Committee. 

Dr Liao has been an SFC Non-Executive Director since May 2003. He is currently sitting on the Remuneration Committee.
