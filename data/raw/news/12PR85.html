To improve communication with the public and key stakeholders, the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has launched an upgraded corporate site following a comprehensive redesign.

The site has a fresh look and also delivers better online experience for target audiences in terms of navigation, functionality and information search.

"This ambitious redesign reflects our commitment to interact closely with external stakeholders and to maximise the transparency of our work,” said Mr Ashley Alder, the SFC’s Chief Executive Officer. “In providing more information and making it more readily accessible, we hope to help the financial industry address its compliance needs more efficiently while promoting among the broader public a better understanding of our regulatory functions and the workings of the markets.”

Highlighted below are some of the key enhancements:

  • Online library of codes and guidelines: This systematic and user-friendly rule book sub-site collates and presents regulatory materials in a readily searchable format. Users may now look up detailed information and compare different versions of a code or set of guidelines in just one or a few clicks.
  • Upgraded search: The newly installed Google Search Appliance is primed to generate more comprehensive and meaningful results.
  • Regulatory update and calendar: Updated periodically, this new section provides a comprehensive overview of the SFC’s latest regulatory changes and decisions, including the implementation of new or revised rules, the decisions and statements of the Takeovers Panel, and the SFC’s public consultations and conclusions.
  • Activity data and charts: This new section presents quantified regulatory activities in tables and charts. Updated quarterly, the activity data detail the SFC’s work in product authorization, enforcement, licensing, supervision of intermediaries, and listings and takeovers. 
  • Quick access and wish list: To allow users to land on the right page in just one click, the homepage now features direct access to a number of popular destinations, including the SFC Online Portal (for intermediaries), the public register of licensees, the investment product list and the investor education site. Separately, the “I want to” box on the homepage offers quick access to popular topics, such as how to apply for a licence and how to submit comments on a recent consultation.

In preparation for continual improvement, the SFC is seeking feedback from users via an online survey posted on the new site.

The SFC has also standardised, and in some cases updated, the electronic forms mainly for use by the financial industry. Users are welcome to access and download the new forms from the homepage (under Home > Forms) or from individual regulatory function sections (under Home > Regulatory functions).
