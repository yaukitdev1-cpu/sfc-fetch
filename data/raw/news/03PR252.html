The SFC today publishes the Autumn 2003 issue (No. 53) of the SFC Quarterly Bulletin.

The feature article of this issue, The Hong Kong Brokerage Industry – Challenges and Reform, is a speech delivered by SFC Chairman, Mr Andrew Sheng, at the Hong Kong Securities Institute Roundtable Luncheon.  Mr Sheng discussed the performance and structure of the Hong Kong brokerage industry and its major challenges, as well as regulatory responses of the SFC to bring about continuing reforms of the industry.

This issue also includes an article entitled Six-year Comparison of Business Activities Surveys on Investment Advisers, Commodity Trading Advisers and Exempt Investment Advisers under the pre-SFO Regime.  It highlights the major trends of operations of the three types of advisers based on the Business Activities Surveys conducted by the SFC during 1996-2001.  Another article, Development of Specialised Investment Funds in Hong Kong, reviews the special features of guaranteed funds, hedge funds, index funds, exchanged traded funds and real estate investment trusts.  It also summarises the recent developments and market presence of these specialised investment schemes in Hong Kong.   A third article, IPO Activities in Hong Kong, provides an analysis of IPO activities during 1997-2002.  The role of Hong Kong as a fund-raising centre is discussed.

The market update, Further Analysis of The Recent Rebound in Market Turnover, continues the discussion in the Summer issue and discusses additional factors underpinning the latest rebound in market turnover.  This article is primarily drawn from an SFC research paper of the same title, which is available on the SFC website at http://www.hksfc.org.hk/eng/statistics/html/researches.htm.
