In light of investor complaints about missing IPO refund cheques, the SFC today discussed with the Hong Kong Monetary Authority, the Commercial Crime Bureau of the Police, and the Federation of Share Registrars Limited how to help investors get their monies back safely in future IPOs.

The SFC is very concerned about recent reports that cheques issued to IPO applicants representing the return of excess or unsuccessful application monies had been stolen and cashed.

The SFC is pleased that the Police have arrested several suspects. The SFC will provide assistance to the Police’s investigations where required. However, because of the ongoing investigation, the SFC does not consider it appropriate yet to publicly discuss the details of the cases and their possible implications under the current IPO refund process.

The participants at today's meeting exchanged ideas on possible recommendations that might minimise the opportunity of theft. The SFC would like to stress that these recommendations would have to be further examined by the respective parties and discussed with market practitioners to determine if they are feasible for implementation. Another meeting would be held shortly to discuss the result of the deliberations.

As an immediate step, the SFC wishes to remind investors to take note of the process of returning monies and issuing the new share certificates in an IPO, which is set out in the prospectus.

As an investor, you should:
