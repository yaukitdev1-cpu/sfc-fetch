Amendments to the Securities and Futures (Levy) Order and the Securities and Futures (Investor Compensation – Levy) Rules (collectively the Statutory Rules) will come into effect tomorrow to facilitate the launch of new exchange-traded products with market-making facilities and to promote participation in the futures market.

A new approach of granting levy exemptions to holders of valid Securities Market Maker permits issued by the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited (SEHK) will replace the current practice of granting the SFC levy and investor compensation levy exemptions to market makers of defined products, namely Nasdaq-Amex Pilot Programme securities (Note 1) and Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs).

All Designated Market Makers (i.e. those holding valid market making permits) of the SEHK will be exempted from the payment of the SFC levy and investor compensation levy for their market making transactions on the SEHK after the amendments take effect.

This approach provides greater flexibility than the one adopted by the existing regime which requires legislative amendments each time a new product with market-making facilities was launched.

The amendments will also reduce the levies payable in respect of the trading of Mini-Hang Seng China Enterprises Index futures contracts (H-shares futures contracts) to one-fifth of the levy for the standard H-Shares futures contracts.

This reduction in levies will lower the overall transaction costs of trading these futures contracts and promote participation in the Mini H-Shares Index futures market.
