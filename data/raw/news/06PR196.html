The SFC has expanded its investor education efforts to all local universities and will introduce new credit-based courses and guest lectures in this academic year. Our efforts aim to help young people develop the right attitude towards personal financial management.

With the first-time collaboration with The Chinese University of Hong Kong and The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, SFC’s investor education has reached all eight local universities (Note 1).

Credit-based courses

The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK)

For the first time, the SFC has partnered with CUHK to launch a new general education course on Personal Financial Management for non-business-major students. Starting September 2006, the 13-session course covers extensive topics, such as fundamental concepts of stock and fund investments, corporate activities of listed companies, shareholder activism, scams and malpractices, warrant and option basics, specialised funds, bonds, structured products and personal financial planning. The course will also include visits to the SFC and HKEx’s Exhibition Hall. Students can obtain two credit units, subject to examination.

Lingnan University

The SFC will again jointly organise with Lingnan University two five-session courses with a revised curriculum:

Foundations of Stock Investing – The course starting September 2006 covers stock and warrant investments, and financial scams and malpractices. A visit to a broker firm and HKEx’s Exhibition Hall enables students to better understand stock dealing and settlement processes.

Get Financially Prepared for Your Future – To be offered in March 2007, the course will focus on personal financial planning, including MPF investments, investor psychology and how to achieve diversification and appropriate asset allocation in different products.

Students can earn 10 Integrated Learning Programme units for each course (Note 2).

Guest lectures

In this fall semester, SFC representatives will conduct the following guest lectures in various finance courses:

  • Getting Started in Stock Investing, and Market Malpractices, Misconceptions and Investor Protection at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University.
  • Market Malpractices: Case Studies and Lessons to Learn at City University of Hong Kong and Hong Kong Baptist University.
  • Understanding the Hong Kong Stock Market at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology.


Mr Martin Wheatley, SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, said: “Promoting preventive education for students is a key long-term strategy for enhancing the public’s financial literacy. This helps our young generation learn the essence of smart investing and personal financial management, thus benefiting their future. We are pleased to join with all local universities and will continue to work with them closely.”
