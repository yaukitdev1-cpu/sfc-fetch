The SFC today publishes the April issue of Dr Wise’s Column, Understand Capital Restructuring Before You Vote, on its InvestEd website at www.InvestEd.hk.

Share consolidations and share capital reductions are the most common capital restructuring. In this issue, Dr Wise explains how capital restructurings work and their effects on the number of issued shares, the par value of shares and the share price. Particularly, he clarifies that par value is a notional value that does not represent the real value of a share, and a change in par value following a capital restructuring does not necessarily mean that the share price will change accordingly.

Generally, a company needs to call a general meeting to obtain shareholders’ approval for a proposed capital restructuring. Dr Wise reminds investors to carefully read the circular containing details and reasons of the proposed capital restructuring, and attend the general meeting if necessary to find out more from the company’s management before voting for or against it.

Dr Wise is a fictitious character. Through his column, he talks to investors about the key issues of investing and regulatory thoughts.

Dr Wise’s Column is published on the last working Tuesday of every month. From time to time, additional articles in the form of special issues may be published on topical matters. The column is posted under the “Features & Dr Wise” section of the InvestEd website. Investors are welcome to send their feedback and questions to Dr Wise by email to InvestEd@sfc.hk.
