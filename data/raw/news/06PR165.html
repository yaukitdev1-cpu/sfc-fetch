The SFC today publishes the July issue of Dr Wise’s Column, Privatisations – Your Vote Can Make a Difference, on its InvestEd website at www.InvestEd.hk.

In this issue, Dr Wise focuses on the voting requirements under the Hong Kong Code on Takeovers and Mergers relating to privatisation of companies by way of a scheme of arrangement.

Dr Wise reminds shareholders facing a privatisation proposal which is conducted by way of a scheme of arrangement to carefully consider the terms of the scheme and to exercise their voting right accordingly at the shareholders’ meeting held to vote on the scheme. Shareholders must understand that whether a scheme can become effective depends not only on the number of votes approving it but also on the number of votes disapproving it.

Dr Wise is a fictitious character. Through his column, he talks to investors about the key issues of investing and regulatory thoughts.

Dr Wise’s Column is published on the last working Tuesday of every month. From time to time, additional articles in the form of special issues may be published on topical matters. The column is posted under the “Features & Dr Wise” section of the InvestEd website. Investors are welcome to send their feedback and questions to Dr Wise by email to InvestEd@sfc.hk.
