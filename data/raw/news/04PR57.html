Mr Peter Au-Yang, Chairman of the IEAC, Executive Director and Chief Operating Officer of the SFC, said: "I welcome the eight new members and three re-appointed members. They will bring their professional expertise and valuable experience to the IEAC."

Mr Au-Yang also paid tribute to outgoing members for their valuable contribution. They are The Honourable Albert Ho Chun Yan, Mr Edward Chow, Professor Lui Yu Hon, Dr Saimond Ip, Mr Roger Luk, Ms Edith Ngan and Mr Wilfred Wong.

The IEAC was established under section 8 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance to advise the SFC on its investor education work. It plays a crucial role in identifying areas that the SFC should study in order to further the interests of investors, and aspects of the securities and futures industry where investor education is desirable.

During the past year, the IEAC advised the SFC on the focus of and information that should be collected in its Retail Investor Survey 2003. Subsequent to the Survey, the Committee also commented on the findings and recommended future investor education strategies.

Besides, the IEAC gave valuable advice on the setting up of two fake scam websites and the Smart Investor Awards to raise the public’s awareness of boiler room scams and bogus websites.

In the year ahead, the SFC will continue to rely on the IEAC as the think-tank in formulating its investor education initiatives. The SFC will consult the IEAC on how to raise investors’ awareness of issues that they should note in interpreting research reports, and the feasibility of forming an investor educators group comprising market professionals to extend the reach of SFC’s educational messages to the investing public.
