The SFC has published an update of the Outline of Part XV of the Securities and Futures Ordinance to assist the market in complying with the disclosure of interests requirements.

The revised Outline of Part XV addresses a number of enquiries received from the market since the publication of the Outline in March 2003.

Additional comments cover the disclosure obligations arising in the case of placings and top-ups, the exemption from aggregation of interests of controlled corporations, the interaction of the wholly owned group exemption with the exemptions under the Disclosure of Interests - Securities Borrowing and Lending Rules, and a number of other detailed issues that have been raised over the last four months.

Since the implementation of the SFO in April, Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited has experienced problems in reading disclosure of interests (DI) forms filed by fax. This has sometimes led to delay in releasing information onto the DI database on HKEx’s website.

In the Outline, it is requested that if a form is to be filed by fax, the form should be printed out on four A4 pages (instead of two pages) for faxing so that it is more legible.

The updated Outline of Part XV is available on the SFC website under "Bills, Legislation and Codes" – "Securities and Futures Ordinance" – "Part XV – Disclosure of Interests".
