The SFC today releases a draft Code on Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs) for public consultation. The draft Code proposes standards and rules for the authorisation and regulation of REITs that are offered to the Hong Kong public.

A REIT is a collective investment scheme that owns and manages income-generating real estate, such as shopping malls, offices and residential properties.   It typically focuses on property investments and pays out its taxable income to scheme holders in the form of dividends.  Detailed disclosures of the operations and its underlying properties are provided to scheme members on a regular basis.  

After years of evolvement, REITs have gained recognition in major financial markets such as Australia and the US.  Industry practitioners have earlier requested the SFC to consider introducing REITS in Hong Kong. The SFC also believes that this is an appropriate time to introduce REITs to allow investors access to a broader range of investment products. 

Given the unique features and investment characteristics of REITs, the SFC proposes to formulate a separate code to regulate such product.  The draft Code has taken into consideration the regulatory experience of REITs in overseas jurisdictions and is designed to have a clearly defined, self-contained format that will provide both operational clarity and the flexibility for future expansion as the market evolves. Key proposals in the Code are set out in Note 1.   

Mrs Alexa Lam, SFC’s Executive Director of Intermediaries and Investment Products, said: “The proposed introduction of the REITs Code is one of the initiatives in the SFC’s continuous efforts to broaden the choice of investment products available to the investing public and to further reinforce Hong Kong’s leading position as an international financial hub.   Through REITs, retail investors may invest in large-scale income-generating real estate in a cost effective manner, with a relatively transparent and well-defined investment strategy.”

The one-month consultation will end on 9 April 2003.  Following the consultation, the SFC will prepare a consultation conclusions document to summarize the views expressed by the respondents and the SFC’s policy response and set out the final Code.

Note:

1. Key proposals in the draft Code on REITs

  • Proper management experience and expertise;
  • Clear description of a scheme’s investment policy and objective(s);
  • Segregation of management and safekeeping of assets;
  • Independent valuation for the assets of a scheme on a regular basis, and as required under specific circumstances;
  • Transparent and convenient exit mechanism – listing or annual redemptions for unlisted REITs;
  • Adequate and timely disclosure of financial reports and scheme activities; and
  • Voting by unit-holders on connected party transactions


As REITs gain acceptance among the Hong Kong investors, the Code can be expanded or modified at a later stage to accommodate broader product varieties.  The SFC will also review and modify the Code on a regular basis to ensure that the rules are kept up to date with the development of the REITs market in Hong Kong.
