The Basel Committee on Banking Supervision has issued a paper entitled The application of Basel II to trading activities and the treatment of double default effects, which was prepared by a joint working group of the Basel Committee and the International Organization of Securities Commissions (IOSCO).

The paper sets out the capital requirements for banks’ exposures to certain trading-related activities, including counterparty credit risk, and for the treatment of double default effects, or the risk that both a borrower and guarantor default on the same obligation. It aims to bring the capital treatment of trading activities and double default effects abreast of the Basel Committee’s revised capital framework for banking organisations, also known as "Basel II".

Mr Jaime Caruana, Chairman of the Basel Committee and Governor of the Banco de Espana, said: "The rules on the application of Basel II to trading activities and the treatment of double default effects would significantly increase the risk-sensitivity of regulatory capital requirements for these specific exposures."

SFC Chairman, Mr Andrew Sheng, who is Chairman of the IOSCO Technical Committee, said: "IOSCO fully endorses these new rules, which represent a significant step forward in the regulation of capital requirements for trading activities. We are looking forward to continuing our cooperation with the Basel Committee during their implementation."

For details, please see the attached joint press release issued by the Basel Committee on Banking Supervision and IOSCO on 18 July 2005.
