The SFC today releases a Consultations Conclusions paper dealing with proposed amendments to the Codes on Takeovers and Mergers and Share Repurchases and the procedures for hearings under these Codes.

Two submissions were received during the consultation period which ended on 14 December 2007, which generally welcomed the proposals.

Under the proposals which promote fairness and transparency, disciplinary proceedings before the Panel will be held in public and chaired by an experienced litigation counsel or solicitor or a retired judge.

For the first time the SFC is introducing detailed procedures for disciplinary and non-disciplinary hearings under the Codes to facilitate fair, efficient and timely decision-making.

The revised Codes will be published in the Gazette in due course. The procedural rules and the amendments to the Codes will become effective on 1 April 2008.

The Consultation Conclusions Paper is available on the SFC website at www.sfc.hk. The revisions to the Codes will be posted to subscribers to the Codes and published on the SFC website as soon as practicable.
