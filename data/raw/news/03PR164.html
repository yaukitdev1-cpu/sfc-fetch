The SFC has for the first time produced a documentary-drama, “Know the SFC”, to explain to the public the work of the SFC.

The video series comprises nine episodes, and aims to explain the SFC’s regulatory role, objectives, functions and how it carries out its work.  The series also highlights some significant improvements of the regulatory framework under the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

The nine episodes, each running for around five minutes, will feature the following themes:

1.        The Art of Financial Regulation

2.        Tackling Market Manipulation

3.        Penalty on Insider Dealers

4.        Licensing: The Gatekeeper

5.        Regulatory Partnership with Intermediaries

6.        Disciplinary Actions and Investor Compensation

7.        Fair Dealing in Takeovers

8.        Fund Authorisations

9.        How Complaints are Handled

SFC Chairman, Mr Andrew Sheng, said: “Only investors who understand how the regulatory regime works will have confidence in the market.  I believe that enhancing public understanding of SFC’s work will lead to more effective regulation of the market and greater investor confidence.”

The series is in Cantonese. The SFC will launch it on Cable TV on 21 July 2003. VCDs will be available free of charge to the public from 1 August.

After the broadcast of each episode on Cable TV, the episode can be viewed on the SFC-operated Electronic Investor Resources Centre (www .hkeirc.org).

At a ceremony today to announce the video, Mr Sheng thanked members of the Investor Education Advisory Committee for supporting the idea. He also thanked Cable TV for broadcasting the series on its channels.
