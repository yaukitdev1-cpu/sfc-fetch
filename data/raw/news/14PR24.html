The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the re-appointment by the Financial Secretary of Mrs Alexa Lam as Deputy Chief Executive Officer and Executive Director of Investment Products, International and China for a one-year term effective from 1 March 2014.

The SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, Mr Ashley Alder, said, “I’m pleased with the Financial Secretary’s decision to re-appoint Alexa. I look forward to continuing working closely with her during her new term.”
