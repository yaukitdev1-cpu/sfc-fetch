The SFC welcomes the appointments by the Financial Secretary of Mr Peter Au-Yang as an Executive Director of the SFC, assuming the role of Chief Operating Officer (COO), and Dr York Liao, JP as a Non-Executive Director (NED) of the Commission.

Mr Au-Yang was previously a senior investment banker with 18 years’ corporate finance experience with The Hongkong and Shanghai Banking Corporation Limited (HSBC). He served as HSBC’s Co-Head – Investment Banking, Asia Pacific from January 2001 to April 2002. He has dealt with a wide range of corporate finance projects and has in-depth knowledge of regulatory issues.

Mr Au-Yang attained his Bachelor of Science Degree in Business Studies with First Class Honours from the University of Bradford, England in 1982 and was awarded a Master of Science Degree in Accounting & Finance by the London School of Economics and Political Science, England in 1983.

The COO position has been vacant since the former Deputy Chairman/COO Mrs Laura Cha left the Commission in early 2001. As the COO, Mr Au-Yang will oversee Corporate Communications, Investor Education and Communications, Commission Secretariat, Finance and Administration, Human Resources / Training and Development and Information Technology. He will also co-ordinate cross-divisional functions and communications, lead the implementation of the SFC’s corporate initiatives and strategies, and assist the Chairman in the development and maintenance of external relations.

Dr Liao is the Managing Director of Winbridge Company Limited and was involved in the business of liquid crystal display panels. He also sits on the Council of Advisors on Innovation and Technology and the University Grants Committee. As an NED, Dr Liao will contribute to the policy development of the SFC and bring his independent views and insight to the management of the Commission.

Dr Liao received his Bachelor of Science Degree in Physics from the California Institute of Technology in 1967 and was awarded a Master of Arts Degree and a PhD in Applied Physics from the Harvard University in 1968 and 1973 respectively.

SFC Chairman Mr Andrew Sheng said: "I am pleased to welcome Mr Au-Yang as our new COO and Dr Liao as a NED. I am confident that with his professional management expertise and extensive experience in the corporate finance field, Mr Au-Yang will contribute significantly to strengthening the SFC’s internal operations as well as enhancing our external relationship with stakeholders. I also look forward to working closely with Dr Liao, whose invaluable experience in public services and broad vision will certainly benefit the Commission in its policy formulation and enhancement of our corporate governance."
