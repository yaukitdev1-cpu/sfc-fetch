What are the risks in margin trading? Are SFC authorised funds sure-win products? Why can’t physical share certificates be traded immediately? Let Carl tell you.

Carl is a fictitious character created by the SFC for its new investor education booklet, Get Ready to Invest. This bilingual booklet traces Carl’s experience in trading stocks, funds, warrants, futures and bonds, as well as how he selected his old school-friend, Kate, as his broker. He has had mixed success in investment and investors can learn how to avoid Carl’s blunders.

Carl’s experience shows that investment is not only a buy-low-and-sell-high strategy. Investors have to understand the features of financial products, learn how to manage their investments and know how to protect their own rights.

Feature Articles

The booklet is supplemented by two new series of feature articles on the SFC-operated Electronic Investor Resources Centre (www.hkeirc.org).

Sharing the title of the booklet, the Get Ready to Invest series includes more extensive discussion on the issues covered in the booklet, such as discretionary services, ways to safekeep shares and takeovers.

For more sophisticated investors, the SFC also launches the Futures and Options series. It explains the features of futures and options, their trading mechanisms, the risks involved and the differences between these two derivatives.

As with all other SFC educational publications, Get Ready to Invest is available to the public for free at the SFC’s office and the Consumer Advice Centres of the Consumer Council. The public may also access the electronic version of the booklet on the "Advice to Investors - Investor Education Programmes" section of the SFC website. Licensed intermediaries are also welcome to request copies for distribution by calling the SFC’s Investor Education and Communications Department at 2842 7711.
