The SFC today publishes the October issue of the SFC Enforcement Reporter, summarising the SFC’s enforcement action in the month of September 2004 and discussing the compliance issues highlighted by recent cases.

In September, the SFC successfully prosecuted two companies and 17 persons. Among them, one company and its director were convicted of providing false and misleading information, in the first prosecution under Dual Filing. Other entities were prosecuted for market manipulation, cold calling, failing to attend SFC investigation interviews, short selling of securities, aiding and abetting another person to conduct regulated activities whilst not licensed, unlicensed investment advising, and breaching the disclosure of interests law.

The SFC disciplined eight licensees during the month for inadequate supervision, internal control failings, and improper account opening procedures.

Since 1 April 2004, the SFC has successfully prosecuted 41 entities. We have withdrawn summonses issued against eight entities resulting in their acquittals and a further five entities were acquitted after trial. The SFC has also disciplined 35 licensees for various regulatory breaches and entered into settlements with voluntary payments with two licensees. Disciplinary proceedings have been taken against nine licensees, which were concluded with no formal sanction imposed, although six of them received private warnings. Such proceedings were also commenced and discontinued against three deemed licensees who left their firms before the conclusion of the action.
