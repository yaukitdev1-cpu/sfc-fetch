The SFC today publishes a revised Guidance Note for Short Selling Reporting and Stock Lending Record Keeping Requirements. 

The Guidance Note was published by the SFC in August 2000 to clarify the Commission’s policies and positions in the application of the short selling provisions in the Securities Ordinance and the Securities (Stock Lending) Rules to help the industry comply with the law.

In June 2002, an Addendum was published to address queries and concerns raised by industry participants since the publication of the Guidance Note in 2000.  

The revised Guidance Note published today consolidates and replaces the 2000 Guidance Note and the Addendum.  In addition, new guidance is given on the application of relief from short selling regulations introduced under the Securities (Miscellaneous)(Amendment) Rules 2002, which came into effect on 15 November 2002.

The Rules provide extended exemptions from section 80 of the Securities Ordinance (which prohibits naked short selling) to market making transactions on the exchanges and introduce alternative methods of complying with the covered short selling reporting requirements in section 80B of the Securities Ordinance (note 1).

The publication of the revised Guidance Note reflects the SFC’s continued effort to work with industry participants to address their concerns and to respond to market developments. 

The revised Guidance Note is available at the SFC office and accessible on the SFC’s website under “Bills, Legislation and Codes – Regulatory Handbook”.  Prior to commencement of the Securities and Futures Ordinance, the revised Guidance Note will be amended with references to current law substituted by the relevant new provisions.
