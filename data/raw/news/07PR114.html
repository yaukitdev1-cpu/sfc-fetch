More effective dialogue and co-operation internationally between and among regulators and market participants is needed in managing the risks in our highly complex financial markets, according to Mr Martin Wheatley, SFC’s Chief Executive Officer.

“Everybody who is engaged with the financial system is watching out for the risks,” he said. “We all share a responsibility in exercising discipline to ensure that risks are not excessive.”

In a keynote address to a conference, Mr Wheatley said risk management was more challenging in the current environment given the complexity of financial instruments and trading strategies. Illiquidity could magnify the risk of these instruments and strategies and embedded leverage compounded the difficulty in managing the risks, he said.

“The various international efforts to set international best practices and standards, better regulation, dialogue with industry, as well as market initiatives on hedge funds and the management of counterparty risk are efforts in the right direction,” Mr Wheatley said.

Mr Wheatley’s speech, “Systemic risk: Who’s watching the baby?”, is attached.
