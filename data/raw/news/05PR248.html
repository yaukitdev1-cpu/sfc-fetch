The SFC today publishes the October issue of Dr Wise's Column, Protecting Your Interests Starts with Asking the Right Questions, at its Electronic Investor Resources Centre (eIRC) at www.eirc.hk.

In this issue, Dr Wise reminds investors that different investment products have different pros and cons, and may be suitable for investors with different investment objectives, risk profiles and other personal circumstances. Given the increasing complexity of the investment products available in the market, Dr Wise further reminds investors to fully understand the products, including their features, costs, charges and risks, before deciding whether to make the investment. This is particularly important when investors are dealing with products with features designed to keep their money locked-up for a long time.

With a view to protecting their own interests and finding a suitable investment product, Dr Wise also encourages investors to ask more questions when assessing a product recommendation made by intermediaries, including:

  • Why is the recommended product suitable to you, based on your personal circumstances (including your investment objectives, investment horizon and attitude to risk), and the product's features and risks?
  • Is the recommendation confined to products offered by specific providers? If so, why only those providers?
  • How much commission will the intermediary receive from the product provider?
  • If the recommendation is to switch from one product to another, what are the costs you might incur and how much commission will the intermediary receive if you switch?
  • What are the alternatives available and why are they not being recommended?
  • Have you been given all the relevant information about the recommendation?


Dr Wise is a fictitious character. He represents the collective wisdom and knowledge of market specialists and experts at the SFC. Dr Wise, through his column, talks to investors about the key issues of investing and regulatory thoughts.

Dr Wise's Column is published on the last working Tuesday of every month. From time to time, additional articles may be published on topical issues. The column is posted under the "Features & Dr Wise" section of the eIRC. Investors are welcome to send their feedback and questions to Dr Wise by email at investor.info@sfc.hk.
