The SFC today publishes the Winter 2005 issue (No.62) of the SFC Quarterly Bulletin.

Trading of Mainland Stocks and HSI Constituent Stocks in Hong Kong, the UK and US compares the trading activities of the Mainland stocks and HSI constituent stocks in Hong Kong and in major overseas markets. It is observed that trading activities of most stocks are concentrated in Hong Kong.

Another article, Trading of the 31 Mainland Companies which Issued Both A-shares and H-shares, examines the trading activities of the Mainland companies on both A-shares and H-shares in the Mainland and in Hong Kong respectively. It is noted that whilst A-shares have been trading at premiums over their H-share counterparts, such premiums have declined significantly in recent years. Trading in Hong Kong is more active, although this is not directly comparable because the two markets are segregated.

A third article, Alternative Measures of the Size of the Stock Market, benchmarks the size of the Hong Kong stock market against the GDP and analyses its position against major overseas markets.

This issue includes a speech, Hedge Funds – Friend or Foe, which was presented by the SFC Chairman, Mr Martin Wheatley, at a conference in Hong Kong. It addresses the importance of hedge funds in Hong Kong as being a major source of liquidity and discusses the regulatory policy implications of their evolving role in the financial system.

This issue also includes the Review of the Securities Market and the Financial Review of the Securities Industry. The former reviews the performance of the local and regional stock markets during 2005, whilst the latter analyses the financial performance of the securities industry over the same period. The reports found that:

  • Hong Kong’s securities market continued to be strong in 2005; and
  • total income of the securities industry increased by 15% in 2005 over 2004. The increase was consistent with the positive market sentiment and increased funds raising activities during the year.
