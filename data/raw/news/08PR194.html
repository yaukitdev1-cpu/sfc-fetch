The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) will hold Investor Day 2009 jointly with the Open University of Hong Kong (OUHK) to help equip investors with the necessary knowledge to rethink their investment strategies in the volatile markets.

Entitled "Riding out the financial tsunami," Investor Day will be held on the OUHK campus on 10 January (Note 1). The full-day investor education event will feature forum discussions of the macro-economic overview and risks analysis of 2009, investing at times of adversity as well as lessons learnt from the financial tsunami and the way forward.

Professionals from the financial sector will share their expertise and views with the investing public. They include Mr George S K Leung, a seasoned economist; Mr Eddie Lam, a veteran investment consultant; Mr David Y T Lui, a senior fund manager; Professor Y H Lui, Director, Li Ka Shing Institute of Professional and Continuing Education, OUHK; Mr Karl Lung, President, the Hong Kong Society of Financial Analysts (HKSFA); and Mr Steve Chiu, Vice President, the Institute of Financial Planners of Hong Kong (IFPHK).

"Amid the changing economic and financial landscape, investors should reposition themselves and consider carefully their investment strategies to suit their own circumstances," said the SFC's Chief Executive Officer Mr Martin Wheatley. "We hope that this investor education initiative will provide investors with knowledge that helps them achieve this."

As the supporting organisations of Investor Day, HKSFA and IFPHK will set up counters at the event venue to offer advice on financial planning and provide analysis to investors.
