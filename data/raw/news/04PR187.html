The SFC is inviting secondary school teachers to enrol in a new series of investor education workshops.

Teachers workshops are part of the SFC's initiatives to assist secondary school teachers of Economics, Commerce, Accounting and related subjects to better understand the latest market developments and investment products so as to facilitate their teaching work.

A total of 11 workshops covering five different themes will be held from 15 November to 16 December 2004. There will be a new intermediate module on Funds focusing on investment advisory services, fund distribution channels and the dealing process. Participating teachers will have the opportunity to visit a fund house to learn more about fund management work.

Besides, the advanced module on Stocks has been expanded to include a new session on using a listed company’s annual reports and other corporate information as an investment appraisal tool. (Note 1)

Since 2000, the SFC has organised 71 teachers workshops reaching over 3,200 participants. A "Teachers Corner" has been set up on the SFC website to provide easy access to the PowerPoint presentation materials, case studies and exercises used in the workshops.

Invitation letters for enrolment have been sent to all secondary schools. The Education and Manpower Bureau is also supportive of SFC’s teachers workshops and has included the enrolment information on its website (www.emb.gov.hk) under the "Training Calendar" subsection (course code: CDI020040683). Teachers can also visit the "Teachers Corner" on the SFC website or contact the Investor Education and Communications Department at 2842 7711 to enrol.
