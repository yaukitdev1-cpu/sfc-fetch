An SFC delegation participated in the 29th Annual Conference of the International Organization of Securities Commissions (IOSCO) in Amman, Jordan from 17 to 20 May. The Conference was opened by His Majesty King Abdullah II. More than 500 senior executives representing regulators of more than 100 jurisdictions attended the Conference. (Note 1)

During the Conference,

  • SFC Chairman, Mr Andrew Sheng, was appointed Chairman of the IOSCO Technical Committee for a two-year term.

In October 2003, Mr Sheng was appointed Interim Chairman of the Technical Committee after the former Chairman, Mr David Knott of the Australian Securities and Investments Commission, had stepped down before expiry of his term. (Note 2)

  • The SFC, in collaboration with the Tourism Board and Brand Hong Kong Management Office of the Information Services Department of the Hong Kong Government, showcased Hong Kong as an international financial hub, a conference and exhibition centre, and a unique tourist destination. Hong Kong will host the Annual Conference in 2006.

An exhibition booth was set up with poster display, videocast and souvenir giveaway to promote the attractiveness and advantages of Hong Kong.

  • The Shanghai Stock Exchange (SSE) also took the opportunity to promote its markets to the international financial community. Highlighting its products and services as well as its latest developments, the SSE’s booth drew attention of participants from around the world.


Speaking from Amman, Mr Sheng said: "I am very honoured to be appointed Chairman of the Technical Committee and appreciate the recognition by IOSCO members of the work of the SFC. We will continue to participate in and contribute to the work of IOSCO in raising the standards of international securities regulation."

"The Conference in Amman gives us a very good opportunity to enhance the awareness and raise the profile of Hong Kong among the international regulators in the run-up to the IOSCO Annual Conference 2006," he said.

IOSCO has issued a Final Communique to give an account of the achievements of the Conference. The Final Communique (English version only) is attached to this press release for your reference.

Notes to Editor:

1. IOSCO is the primary forum for international co-operation among securities regulators and is recognised as the international standard-setter for the securities sector.

2. The Technical Committee is formed under the Executive Committee of IOSCO. It is made up of 15 agencies that regulate some of the world’s larger, more developed and internationalised markets. Its objective is to review major regulatory issues related to international securities and futures transactions and to co-ordinate practical responses to these concerns. The work of the Technical Committee is divided into the following five major functional subject areas:

  • Multinational Disclosure and Accounting;
  • Regulation of Secondary Markets;
  • Regulation of Market Intermediaries;
  • Enforcement and the Exchange of Information; and
  • Investment Management.
