The SFC welcomes the further liberalisation measures concerning the financial services sector under the Supplement IV to the Mainland and Hong Kong Closer Economic Partnership Arrangement (CEPA) signed today in Hong Kong.

Under the latest CEPA supplement, qualified fund management companies in the Mainland are allowed to set up subsidiaries in Hong Kong. Together with other prior commitments under CEPA, Mainland securities and futures companies and fund management companies can now participate in the Hong Kong market through their subsidiaries.

“The increased participation of Mainland intermediaries in the Hong Kong market will help broaden our intermediary base and increase employment opportunities for local financial professionals. This further liberalisation measure will also complement the Qualified Domestic Institutional Investors Scheme announced by the China Securities Regulatory Commission (CSRC) earlier this month,” said Mr Martin Wheatley, Chief Executive Officer of the SFC.

The SFC will coordinate with the CSRC on the implementation details of this commitment.
