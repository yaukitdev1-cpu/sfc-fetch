The SFC is pleased to announce the appointment of seven members and re-appointment of four members to its IEAC for a term of one year.

New members

The Honourable Ho Chun Yan Albert- Member of the Legislative Council
Ms Lin Yoke Seetoh- Chairman of Hong Kong Investment Funds Association
Mr Wilfred Wong- Chairman of Hong Kong Stockbrokers Association Limited
Mr Robert Chow- Media commentator
Mr Howard Sou- Chief Curriculum Development Officer (Technology Education) of Education Manpower Bureau
Mr Roger Luk- Managing Director and Deputy Chief Executive of Hang Seng Bank Limited
Dr Saimond Ip- Executive Director of Hong Kong Pearl River Delta Foundation


Re-appointed members

Mr Lawrence Fok- Deputy Chief Operating Officer of Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited
Mr Li Kai Ming- Deputy Chief Executive of Consumer Council
Professor Lui Yu Hon- Director, Li Ka Shing Institute of Professional & Continuing Education of The Open University of Hong Kong
Mr Edward Chow- Council Member of Hong Kong Society of Accountants, Deputy Chairman of Hong Kong Institute of Directors


Alexa Lam, Chairman of the IEAC and Executive Director of the SFC, said: “I welcome the seven new members and four re-appointed members, who will bring their expertise and valuable experience to the IEAC. This year, the IEAC have representatives from two new constituents, “Education Authority” and “Banks”, to enhance collaboration. With more representatives from different constituents in the society, I believe that the IEAC could carry out its missions more effectively for the interests of the public.”
 
Mrs Lam also paid tribute to outgoing members, The Honourable Sin Chung Kai, Mr Frederick Grede, Mr Shek Kang Chuen, Mr Lennon W. L. Chan and Ms Cynthia Lam, for their valuable contribution.

The IEAC was established in November 1998 under section 6 of the Securities and Futures Commission Ordinance (to be replaced by section 8 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance effective 1 April 2003) to advise the SFC on its investor education work. It plays a crucial role in identifying areas that the SFC should study in order to further the interests of investors, and aspects of the securities and futures industry where investor education is desirable.

During the past year, the IEAC advised the SFC on investor protection and education initiatives related to hedge funds. It also gave valuable advice on the issue concerning fees and charges of securities services, and recommended simplification of the names of charges and the use of examples to explain to retail investors how and when they will be charged.

Furthermore, the IEAC proposed production of video dramas to help the general public understand SFC’s regulatory work. Production of this video series is now underway and VCDs will be available for distribution in mid-2003.

In the year ahead, the SFC will continue its effort in investor education, focusing on explaining investors’ rights and protection under the Securities and Futures Ordinance and facilitating public understanding of the SFC’s regulatory functions.
