The SFC today publishes the December issue of SFC Alert, a newsletter for both investors and intermediaries.

The Government has announced its target to bring the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO) into operation on 1 April 2003. This issue of SFC Alert carries an SFO special.

A new chapter, the cover story, explains how the SFC prepares itself and the industry for the new regime. It also reports the signing of a new Memorandum of Understanding between the SFC and the Hong Kong Monetary Authority to help ensure a level playing field for banks and brokers to conduct their securities business. The 37 sets of subsidiary legislation gazetted so far are listed for readers' reference.

Separately, The new law and you takes a look at the Ordinance from the investors' perspective. It highlights the key initiatives in the SFO that aim to protect investors' interests and defend their rights.

There are two articles on funds.  Getting ready for hedge funds provides an update on the latest regulatory development in relation to retail hedge funds, including the publication of the disclosure guidelines for periodic reports. The SFC has reminded intermediaries of the conduct expected of them when marketing hedge funds and continues to educate investors about the product.

Fund questions answered is about a new set of Frequently Asked Questions the SFC has published on its website to help market participants understand more about the Code on Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds, hence facilitating compliance.

To attract more talented people to join the securities and futures industry, the SFC has introduced exemptions from recognised industry qualification and local regulatory framework paper requirements. Easier entry to industry has the details.

With the launch in November of the SFC Enforcement Reporter, which is a monthly summary of prosecution and disciplinary cases, the SFC Alert will be published every two months. The next issue of SFC Alert will be published in mid-February 2003. The SFC looks forward to your continuing support of the publications.

Copies of SFC Alert are available at the SFC's office. This latest issue and all previous issues are also available on the Commission's website at www.hksfc.org.hk.
