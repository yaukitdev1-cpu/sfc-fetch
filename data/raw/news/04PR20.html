Members of the public may receive an SFC Smart Investor Award if they report to the SFC suspicious scam websites (e.g. fraudulent copycat websites) or boiler rooms targeting Hong Kong investors.  

In view of the increasing incidence of financial scams, in particular online fraud, the SFC launches the Smart Investor Awards to raise the public’s awareness of how financial scams (Note 1) operate so that investors can better protect themselves.   

Any person holding a Hong Kong Identity Card and aged 18 or above is encouraged to report to the SFC and the Police if he or she comes across a suspected scam website or boiler room. The SFC will then decide if the case should be included in the SFC’s Alert List (Note 2). If the case is listed, the person who reported it will receive the Smart Investor Award and an SFC corporate gift. The rules of the Smart Investor Awards are attached for your reference.

Fake scam websites

In tandem with the Smart Investor Awards, the SFC has for the first time created two fake scam websites to educate investors on the typical pitfalls:

  • BiWelle Enterprise claims to be among the top five pharmaceutical enterprises in the US and an expert in influenza vaccines. It offers “too-good-to-be-true” investment opportunities - buying the company’s pre-IPO stocks at a 50% discount. If investors are tempted by the offer and click on the “Invest Now” icon, they will get a “You could get scammed” warning, followed by educational messages about this type of scam.
  • Maxwelle & Copemann is a consultant luring investors to buy overseas financial products. It claims to have a professional management team. When investors click to contact the company, they will be led to SFC messages explaining tactics used in boiler room fraud. In real life situations, boiler room operators usually disappear after receiving investors’ cash.


Both fake scam websites and details of the rules of the Smart Investor Awards can be found under the “Advice to Investors” - “Investor Alert” sub-section on the SFC website.

Notes to Editor:

1. Typical financial scams may include:

Scams websites – These websites offer unrealistically high-return investment opportunities such as pre-IPO shares at huge discounts or guaranteed funds with multiple returns to lure investors. In fact, the investment opportunities and companies do not exist.

Fraudulent copycat websites – These are another form of scam websites which imitate the websites of reputable financial institutions. They are not authorised by or related to the relevant financial institutions in any way. The websites may claim to provide investment services and invite investors to disclose their personal information.

Boiler rooms – Unlicensed professional-sounding salespersons cold call and use high-pressure sales pitches to urge investors to invest. The boiler room operators usually disappear after receiving investors’ cash.

2. The Alert List has been set up on the SFC’s website since November 2000 to alert investors to unlicensed overseas companies which might be boiler rooms or which might have failed to comply with SFC’s licensing requirements in Hong Kong.

3. “Talk on Wise Investing” is an SFC radio segment series comprising 15 five-minute episodes on different themes.  They are broadcast in the Commercial Radio One programme “Stockaeologist” at around 11 am every Saturday.   On completion of the series broadcast on 13 March 2004, all segments will be available under the “Studio” section of the SFC-operated Electronic Investor Resources Centre (eIRC) at www.hkeirc.org.

Smart Investor Awards


Smart Investor Awards are awarded to members of the public who report to the SFC  financial scams that target Hong Kong investors. The purpose of these awards is to increase the awareness of the investing public in Hong Kong of  the modus operandi of various financial scams (in particular online scams) so that investors can better protect themselves.
 
Who can participate?

Any person holding a Hong Kong Identity Card and aged 18 or above is eligible to participate in the Awards. Employees of the SFC and their family members are not eligible.

How to participate in the Awards?

1. You may report to the SFC any suspicious financial scams that aim at swindling Hong Kong investors.   Financial scams may be in one of the following forms:

Scam websites - where the scam operators may offer investment opportunities through their websites to investors with unrealistically high returns at little or no risk. The investment opportunities may range from a pre-IPO subscription of shares at a substantially discounted price to a fund which promises a guaranteed return that may double your investments in a few months’ time. (You may refer to the “Scam Websites” section on the SFC website for how a typical scam website works.)

Fraudulent copycat websites - where the fraudsters imitate the websites of well-known financial institutions when in fact the websites are not authorised by or related to the relevant financial institutions in any way. The fraudsters may claim to provide investment, banking services etc. Investors who are taken in may part with their money and/or disclose personal information such as Personal Identification Numbers (PINs).  (For more details, please refer to the “Fraudulent Copycat Websites” section on the SFC website.)

Boiler rooms - where unlicensed professional-sounding salespersons employed by boiler rooms may cold call and use high pressure sales tactics to urge potential investors to buy stocks / other investment instruments that they recommend.  Once investors part with their money, they are most unlikely to get it back. (Please refer to the “Boiler Room Operations” section on the SFC website for more details.)

2. You should send us details of the suspicious scams (for example, the names of companies and persons involved, their contact addresses, and any other relevant information), together with your full name and contact details. You should mark “Smart Investor Awards” on the correspondence and send it to the Investor Education and Communications Department of the SFC:

(i) 8/F, Chater House, 8 Connaught Road Central, Hong Kong (By post) or

(ii) 2524 3718 (By fax) or

(iii) investor.info@hksfc.org.hk (By email)

Criterion for granting the Awards

In order to be awarded the prize, the report must lead to the scam being added to the SFC website’s Alert List, a list of primarily unlicensed overseas companies which have come to the attention of the SFC. The decision of the SFC shall be binding.

Prizes

As a token of appreciation, the SFC will present a corporate gift to each Award winner. You will be notified after the case reported has been included in the SFC’s Alert List.

Personal Data

Please note our policy regarding the use of personal data in relation to the Smart Investor Awards:

(a) Personal data submitted to the SFC in the course of reporting suspicious financial schemes will only be used, disclosed or transferred for purposes relating to the report (e.g. for investigating the matters in the report) or where required or permitted by law;

(b) An entrant for the Smart Investor Awards must inform the SFC in his/her written submission, if he/she would like to keep his/her identity confidential or if he/she does not want his/her personal data being used for purposes related to the reported suspicious scheme.  In such a case, the SFC may be unable to effectively investigate the suspicious scheme;

(c) Personal data provided to the SFC will be retained for such period as may be necessary for the proper discharge of the SFC’s functions;

(d) Any person who wishes to request access to and/or correction of his/her personal data held by the SFC, may do so in writing addressed to the Data Privacy Officer, Securities and Futures Commission, 8th Floor, Chater House, 8 Connaught Road Central, Hong Kong.  All data access requests should be made using the specified Data Access Request Form.
