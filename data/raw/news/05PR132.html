The SFC today publishes the May issue of Dr Wise’s Column, Know a Fund’s Asset Mix.

In this issue, Dr Wise explains that each investment fund has its own characteristics and may perform differently from another, even if they have similar names and fall under similar fund categories.  Therefore, investors should look beyond a fund’s name.

Dr Wise also reminds investors to read the offering document carefully to find out the factors that may affect the return of a fund.  It is essential that investors know a fund’s asset allocation strategy because different allocations generate different risk profiles.  After buying a fund, investors should monitor their investment and study any circulars and notices received, especially those relating to significant changes that will impact their investment.        

Dr Wise is a fictitious character. He represents the collective wisdom and knowledge of market specialists and experts at the SFC. Dr Wise, through his column, talks to investors about the key issues of investing and regulatory thoughts.
 
Dr Wise’s Column is published on the last Tuesday of every month.  From time to time, additional articles may be published on topical issues.  The column is posted under the “Features & Dr Wise” section of the SFC-operated Electronic Investor Resources Centre at www.eirc.hk.  Investors are welcome to send their feedback and questions to Dr Wise by email at investor.info@sfc.hk.
