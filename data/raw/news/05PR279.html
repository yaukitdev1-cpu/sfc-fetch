For the first time, the SFC has organised an open competition inviting investors to share their personal experiences in investing and the lessons learnt.

“My Investment Story” competition is jointly organised with Radio 5 of the Radio Television Hong Kong (RTHK) and The Salvation Army. It aims to help investors establish a proper attitude towards investing and draw their attention to the risks associated with investing and other issues.

Contestants are invited to narrate their successful or unsuccessful experiences in investing in any Hong Kong stocks (Category 1) or any warrants, SFC-regulated investment products or a portfolio of SFC-regulated investment products (Category 2). Their stories should be able to demonstrate the importance of product knowledge, information and advice, risk management and attitude towards investing in making investment decisions.

Entries can be submitted in English or Chinese, and in written or audio recording format. The rules of the competition and entry forms are available at the SFC office and on SFC’s investor education portal, the Electronic Investor Resources Centre (eIRC) located at www.eirc.hk (Note 1). Submissions must be made by 16 January 2006.

Adjudicators of the competition are Dr Bill Kwok, Ms Connie Lau, and Professor Raymond So, who are members of the SFC Investor Education Advisory Committee, and Mr Karl Lung, a member of the SFC Public Shareholders Group.

Cash prizes totalling $14,000 will be offered to the top three winners in each category. 14 other entries that are short-listed will each receive an SFC corporate souvenir. Results of the competition will be announced in mid-February 2006.

The best 10 winning stories will be adapted into Cantonese radio dramas for broadcast on the “Programme for the Elderly” on RTHK Radio 5, and published in newspapers (Note 2).

Mr Ian Johnston, Adviser to Chairman’s Office of the SFC, said: “Investment behaviour of investors is often dominated by their emotions. While some investors are very confident in their own judgement, others may follow the herd. This competition is a new investor education initiative of the SFC to provide opportunities for investors to learn directly from others’ successful and unsuccessful experiences.”
