The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has granted final authorisation to the first Taiwan exchange-traded fund (ETF), Polaris Taiwan Top 50 Tracker Fund, for offering in Hong Kong (Note 1).

The cross-listing of the first Taiwan ETF will be done via a feeder fund, Polaris Taiwan Top 50 Tracker Fund (H.K.), to be listed on The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong on 19 August 2009.

“The SFC is committed to developing Hong Kong as an ETF hub in Asia by offering a wide range of investment choices to investors. We welcome more Taiwan ETFs to apply for cross-listing in Hong Kong,” said Mrs Alexa Lam, the SFC’s Deputy Chief Executive Officer and Executive Director of Policy, China and Investment Products.

End

Notes:

1. Taiwan Financial Supervisory Commission has approved three Hong Kong ETFs, namely, W.I.S.E CSI 300 China Tracker, Hang Seng Index ETF and Hang Seng H-Share Index ETF for offering in Taiwan.
2. Please also refer to SFC press releases dated 22 May 2009 and 21 July 2009.
