SFC Chairman Mr Andrew Sheng was asked by the media for his views on the accounting profession after the opening ceremony of the World Congress of Accountants today. In response to media enquiries on his remarks, the SFC issues the following statements:

1. The SFC will work with the Government and all relevant parties to enhance the accounting and auditing standards in Hong Kong.

2. Mr Sheng said that the IOSCO (International Organisation of Securities Commissions) had recently endorsed certain principles on auditor oversight, where the auditor oversight body should operate in the public interest.

3. Mr Sheng was asked for his views on whether the Government should set up an independent regulator to oversee the accountants. The SFC understands that the Government is monitoring the international developments. Mr Sheng expressed his view that if the Government considered it appropriate to follow the IOSCO standards, which recommend that there be an auditor oversight body, he would personally support.
