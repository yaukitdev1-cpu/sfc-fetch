Having heard the appeals on 8 April 2005, Deputy High Court Judge Anthony To today dismissed Ms Tsang Wai Lin’s appeal against conviction.

Tsang had earlier been convicted and sentenced in relation to having carried on a business in leveraged foreign exchange trading. She was fined $60,000 after trial.
