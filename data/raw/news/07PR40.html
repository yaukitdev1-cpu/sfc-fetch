The SFC has recently taken some steps which will facilitate further market development, Chief Executive Officer Mr Martin Wheatley says in the latest issue of the SFC Alert published today.

The issue reports on initiatives taken to facilitate the listing of overseas companies in Hong Kong and to promote the growth of the futures and options markets. There are also highlights from a speech by Mr Eddy Fong, SFC Chairman, on the SFC's role in enhancing the effectiveness and efficiency of the Hong Kong securities market.

The “Investor focus” section features the investor education theme for 2007 – “Know Your Risk” - and the activities the SFC organised during the Investor Education Month in January. This investor corner is complemented by an article highlighting the importance of investors replying promptly to circularisation requests.

The issue also notes the Hong Kong Council of Social Service's award to the SFC of the status of Caring Organisation for the SFC's good corporate citizenship.

The SFC Alert is a bi-monthly newsletter for both investors and intermediaries. Copies of the current issue of SFC Alert are available at the SFC office. All issues are available on the SFC website.
