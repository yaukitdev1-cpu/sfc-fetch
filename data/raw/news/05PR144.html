The SFC today publishes the June issue of Dr Wise’s Column, Have You Gambled Your Life Away?

In this issue, Dr Wise reminds investors that leveraged forex trading is not for financially unsophisticated investors.

Besides, Dr Wise advises investors to deal with SFC licensed intermediaries only and avoid unregulated offshore traders.

Investors should not trade in partnership with their account executives. They should also avoid authorising their account executives to trade on their behalf. However, if that is really necessary, it should only be for closing out a specified open position. Investors must note that they are responsible for any losses made on trades conducted by their authorised agents.

Investors may find out more about forex trading in Dr Wise’s March issue, Look Before You Lock. The article explains that "locking" leveraged forex positions that have incurred a trading loss cannot reverse the loss. On the other hand, investors incur additional commissions in unwinding locked positions. Furthermore, carrying locked positions may incur substantial interest expenses.
