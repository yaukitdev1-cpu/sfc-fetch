Dr Wise today explains to investors the access to online information packs about listing applicants at a much earlier stage of the listing process.

The SFC today publishes the December issue of Dr Wise’s Column, “Earlier Access to IPO Information”, on its InvestEd website at www.InvestEd.hk.

From January 2008, information packs of listing applicants, including real estate investment trusts and other investment funds, will be available on the HKEx website before the issue of the prospectuses, i.e. before the offer period starts. This will address the difference between the time given to retail investors to study a listing applicant and the time given to institutional investors.

Investors are reminded that an information pack is not a prospectus or an offering document. It is not a complete document and is subject to change, and investors should study and rely only on the registered prospectus or offering circular in making investment decisions.

Dr Wise is a fictitious character. Through his column, he talks to investors about the key issues of investing and regulatory thoughts.

Dr Wise’s Column is published on the last working Tuesday of every month. From time to time, additional articles in the form of special issues may be published on topical matters. The column is posted under the “Features & Dr Wise” section of the InvestEd website. Investors are welcome to send their feedback and questions to Dr Wise by email to InvestEd@sfc.hk.
