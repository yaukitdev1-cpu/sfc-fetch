The first of a series of infomercials/videos will debut on local television tomorrow (Note 1) to mark the Securities and Futures Commission’s (SFC) latest investor education campaign entitled “Know how to invest wisely”.

The SFC video campaign aims to promote the do’s and don’ts of investing.

Short dramas (Note 2) in the series featuring five-minute episodes will touch on a number of topical issues, including the need for proper handling of discretionary accounts, the risks of leveraging, areas to note in takeovers, investing in structured products and investment-linked assurance schemes. The videos also feature well-known market professionals (Note 3) commenting on the topics under discussion.

“The campaign is the latest initiative reinforcing the SFC’s investor education theme “Be Smart, Ask First,” said Mr Martin Wheatley, the SFC’s Chief Executive Officer. “Investors are encouraged to be more careful when dealing with their investments. It is critical that they ask the right questions before making investment decisions to avoid any potential pitfalls.”

End

The videos will also be available in the “Video” section of the SFC’s InvestEd website at www.InvestEd.hk from Monday. The one-minute abridged version of the video will also be screened on Cable TV, Now Business News Channel and other media outlets from October onward.
2. The synopses and photos (episode one, two, three, four and five) of the five video dramas are attached for your reference.
3. Market professionals interviewed in the videos include Mr Kenny Lee, Permanent Honorary President of Hong Kong Securities Association; Mr Ben Kwong, an experienced securities industry practitioner; Mr Karl Lung, a member of the SFC’s Takeovers and Mergers Panel; Mr Andrew Fung, Executive Board Member of Treasury Markets Association; and Mrs Francine Fu, President of the Institute of Financial Planners of Hong Kong.
