The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the re-appointment by the Financial Secretary of Ms Christina Choi as Executive Director (Investment Products) for a term of three years effective from 1 August 2019 (Note 1).

Mr Ashley Alder, the SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, said: "Christina has made valuable contribution to the SFC over many years.  I am confident that she will continue to bring the same dedication, energy and commitment to the work of the SFC during her new term."

End

Note:

  1. Ms Choi was first appointed to her current position in 2016.
