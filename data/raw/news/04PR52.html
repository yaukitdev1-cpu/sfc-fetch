The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X} for hawking of futures contracts.

An SFC investigation found that between November and December 2002, {X} had made unsolicited calls inducing others to enter into agreements for trading in commodity contracts on commodities exchanges in Japan.

{X}, who is a licensed representative of Celestial Commodities Limited, pleaded guilty today before Ms Bina Chainrai at Eastern Magistracy to one summons. {X} was fined $2,000 and ordered to pay costs of $12,996 to the SFC.
