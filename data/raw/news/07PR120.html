Starting from this issue on, the new look Enforcement Reporter will discuss in detail our thinking and approach behind enforcement actions to enhance transparency.

The Enforcement Reporter, published today, has been redesigned in format and content, to explain the regulatory benefits of our actions, help build stronger compliance practices and attitudes and encourage constructive debate.

The July issue highlights our first court action against a former listed company director for misconduct and reminds listed company directors of their duty to make themselves familiar with the relevant rules. We also discuss two decisions by the Securities and Futures Appeals Tribunal concerning financial resources breaches and locking.

In response to market feedback, the SFC also explains its new compliance advice letters which will replace warning letters.

Copies of the latest issue of the Enforcement Reporter, which highlights key enforcement outcomes and issues in the previous two months, are available on the SFC website under “Speeches, Publications & Consultations” – “Publications”.

The public are able to subscribe to the newsletter by registering for the Update Alert service on the SFC website. They will then be able to receive the newsletter via email.

The public are also invited to provide feedback to improve the Enforcement Reporter. Comments can be emailed to enfreporter@sfc.hk.
