The SFC welcomes the re-appointment by the Financial Secretary of Mrs Alexa Lam as Executive Director (Intermediaries and Investment Products) for two years from 1 March 2003 to 28 February 2005.

Mr Andrew Sheng, SFC Chairman, said the re-appointment was recognition of Mrs Lam’s contribution to the SFC and the Hong Kong markets.

"Financial intermediaries are operating under a difficult business environment. This makes the work of supervision of intermediaries more challenging than ever before. Mrs Lam will continue to ensure that the SFC team will administer the regulations flexibly but without compromising investor protection," Mr Sheng said.

"We will continue to facilitate the development of new investment products, which will in turn help industry practitioners to diversify and offer better services to investors," he said.

Mrs Lam joined the Commission in October 1998. She was appointed Chief Counsel in 1999, and Chief Counsel and Executive Director in March 2001. In December 2001, she was re-designated to head the Intermediaries and Investment Products Division.

Prior to joining the SFC, Mrs Lam taught in the Faculty of Law, University of Hong Kong, and was in private practice in New York and Hong Kong specialising in corporate commercial work and China investments.
