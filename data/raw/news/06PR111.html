The SFC has issued a list of Questions and Answers for the information of clients of Whole Win Securities Limited.

The SFC yesterday issued a restriction notice on Whole Win Securities (Note 1). The SFC is continuing its inquiry into the matter, and has sent a team of SFC staff to the office of Whole Win Securities.

The SFC this morning received 152 investor enquiries.

The SFC will issue further announcements when appropriate.
