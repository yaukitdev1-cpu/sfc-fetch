The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announces the appointment of Mr Lee Kam Wing, Bruno and Mr Pan San Kong, Terry as a member and an alternate member respectively of the Investor Education Advisory Committee (IEAC) with effect from 1 November 2012. Mr Pan replaces Ms Lam Po Yee who stepped down from the committee on 16 October 2012. Mr Lee and Mr Pan represent the Hong Kong Investment Funds Association (Note 1).

The SFC would like to thank Ms Lam for her contribution to the work of the IEAC.

End

Note:

  1. Full member list is available on the SFC website (www.sfc.hk).
