The SFC today publishes the Spring 2003 issue (No. 51) of the SFC Quarterly Bulletin.

The feature article of this issue, Investors and the New Securities and Futures Ordinance, is a speech delivered by SFC Chairman, Mr Andrew Sheng.  It recapitulates the key objectives of the new SFO in ensuring that the securities regulatory framework will fit and facilitate Hong Kong’s continuing status as a modern, efficient and competitive international financial centre.

This issue also includes an article entitled Development of the Exchange-traded Fund Market in Hong Kong.  It discusses the benefits and uses of exchange-traded funds (ETFs).  It also gives an overview of the developments of ETFs and the initiatives taken to facilitate development of the ETF market in Hong Kong.  Another article entitled Six-year Comparison of Business Activities Surveys on Securities Dealers discusses the major trends of securities dealers.  This is based on the Business Activities Surveys conducted by the SFC during 1996-2001.

The market update, Turnover of the Stock Market – A Macro-Economic Perspective, summarises the possible impacts of various macroeconomic aspects such as flow of capital and loss of wealth on market turnover.  The full paper is obtainable from the SFC website (http://www.hksfc.org.hk/eng/statistics/html/index/index0.html).
