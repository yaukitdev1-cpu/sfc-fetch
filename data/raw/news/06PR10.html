The SFC today publishes the January issue of Dr Wise’s Column, Before You Invest, Ask the Right Questions, on its InvestEd website at www.InvestEd.hk.

In this issue, Dr Wise points out that several surveys in the UK, Australia and Hong Kong all indicate a worrying lack of financial or investment knowledge among the general pubic. In order to improve the financial understanding of retail investors, it is important for all parties – regulators, market operators, investment advisers, brokers and educators – to work together. The SFC has been doing its part through different kinds of investor education programmes.

Dr Wise also reminds investors to take responsibility to ask the right questions – and understand the answers – before they commit their money. Investors must learn all that they can about an investment product or service before making an investment or taking up an investment adviser’s recommendation. This is crucial in helping investors safeguard their interests and make informed choices. There are also a few questions in this issue of Dr Wise’s Column that investors may attempt to test their financial knowledge.

Dr Wise is a fictitious character. He represents the collective wisdom and knowledge of market specialists and experts at the SFC. Dr Wise, through his column, talks to investors about the key issues of investing and regulatory thoughts.

Dr Wise’s Column is published on the last working Tuesday of every month. From time to time, additional articles may be published on topical issues. The column is posted under the “Features & Dr Wise” section of the InvestEd website. Investors are welcome to send their feedback and questions to Dr Wise by email at InvestEd@sfc.hk.
