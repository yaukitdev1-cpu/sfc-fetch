The SFC successfully prosecuted two companies and five people in August for failure to disclose interests in securities, cold calling, misleading the SFC and unlicensed dealing.

In the unlicensed dealing case, a DBS Bank staff member was convicted for accepting clients’ telephone orders to trade in securities for five months without an SFC licence or a Hong Kong Monetary Authority registration. This was the second prosecution against a bank staff member for performing regulated activities without a licence or registration.

Further, in August, the SFC disciplined one licensee for failing to verify the accuracy of the Financial Resources Rules returns and supervisory failures.

Apart from the prosecutions and disciplinary actions, the SFC issued a restriction notice prohibiting a broker, Wing Yip Company Limited, from dealing in securities, disposing or dealing with any client assets without the SFC’s prior consent. Upon an SFC application, an administrator was appointed by the Court to administer the property of the broker and that of its clients.

The SFC also obtained a High Court interim Mareva injunction against two responsible officers of another broker, Tiffit Securities (Hong Kong) Limited, restraining them from removing any of their assets from Hong Kong, or dealing with or diminishing the value of any of their assets, whether within or outside Hong Kong.
