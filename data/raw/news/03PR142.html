The SFC welcomes the announcement by the Government today regarding the gazettal tomorrow of the Companies (Amendment) Bill 2003 and its intention to introduce the Bill before the Legislative Council on 25 June 2003.

Mr Ashley Alder, SFC’s Executive Director of Corporate Finance, said: "The prospectus-related provisions of the Bill will facilitate public offers of shares and bonds and represent the first step towards bringing the prospectus regime more in line with international market practices."

"The proposals for amending the prospectus-related provisions have been developed by the SFC, working with the Administration in close consultation with market participants, including members of the Working Group on Debt Market and Securities Offering Issues sub-group. This dialogue with the market is fundamental to ensuring the appropriateness of the proposals and we wish to thank all those who contributed for their invaluable input," Mr Alder said.

A joint consultation paper was issued by the Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau and the SFC in March 2003 seeking public comments on the prospectus-related proposals. Submissions received from stakeholders indicated considerable support for the proposals and the majority of comments received have been taken into consideration in preparing the Bill for gazettal.

The Bill is the second phase of a three-phase approach to update the prospectus regime to facilitate public offers of shares and debentures. The third phase involves a comprehensive review of the securities offering regime by the SFC. "We will continue our dialogue with market practitioners in order to develop proposals for reform," Mr Alder said.

Notes:

The Working Group on Debt Market was established in December 2001 to pursue initiatives for the development of the retail debt market and reports to the Financial Market Development Task Force, which is chaired by the Secretary for Financial Services and the Treasury. The Securities Offering Issues sub-group was set up under the Securities and Futures Market Development Working Group of the Task Force.
