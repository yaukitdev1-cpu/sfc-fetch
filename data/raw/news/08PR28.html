More Mainland fund management companies are expected to establish operations in Hong Kong under the CEPA arrangement which would spur the growth of Hong Kong’s fund management industry, according to the SFC’s Chief Executive Officer Mr Martin Wheatley.

Speaking today at The Lipper Fund Awards 2008 luncheon in Hong Kong, Mr Wheatley said the fund management industries of Hong Kong and the Mainland would benefit from closer ties with one another.

“This will not only enable Mainland fund houses to widen the scope of their activities, but also increase the size of the fund management industry in Hong Kong. Equally importantly, our industry players will gain hands-on experience in dealing with Mainland investment rules and regulations, as well as serving the investment needs of the Mainland,” said Mr Wheatley.

“I believe this is fundamental to our success in playing a valuable role in the development of asset management business in China,” Mr Wheatley added.

Mr Wheatley said the SFC would continue to work closely with its Mainland counterparts to help facilitate the development of financial products for QDII scheme.

Hong Kong’s fund management industry reached a new milestone in 2007, with the sales of retail funds hitting a record of US$45.5 billion at the end of 2007- an increase of 87.1% compared to the year before.

The SFC authorised 257 new retail funds in 2007, bringing the total number of SFC-authorised funds to a new record of 2,077 at the end of January this year.
