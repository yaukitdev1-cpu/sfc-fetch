The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today published an investor education article explaining key features and risks related to latest Real Estate Investment Trust (REIT) products.

The article titled “Understanding the latest REITs in Hong Kong” highlighted, among other issues, important risks and features of REITs investing in real estate located in Mainland China and of REITs denominated and traded in renminbi.

As for REITs traded and settled in renminbi, investors should take into account the additional risk factors associated with renminbi fluctuation and convertibility.

Investors are urged to study offering documents and consult, where appropriate, professional advisers before making any investment decision in relation to REITs.
