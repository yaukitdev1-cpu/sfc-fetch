The Court of Final Appeal (CFA) has ordered Mr Lee Ming-tee to pay the costs incurred by the SFC in relation to Mr Lee's unsuccessful attempt to stay the criminal proceedings against him.

Mr Lee had obtained a stay of those proceedings from the Court of First Instance, based mainly upon allegations that the SFC had terminated a disciplinary inquiry into the prosecution's expert witness in order to protect the witness's status and standing as an expert in Mr Lee's criminal trial.

The prosecution appealed against the stay to the CFA, which permitted the SFC to intervene in the proceedings in order to defend itself against the very serious allegations made against it on Mr Lee's behalf.

In its judgment handed down on 22 August 2003 the CFA set aside the permanent stay of the criminal proceedings and remitted the matter to the Court of First Instance for Mr Lee to stand trial. The judgment of the CFA made it clear that the SFC had not terminated its disciplinary inquiry in order to preserve the credibility of the expert witness. The CFA asked all parties to make submissions on costs.

After considering the parties' submissions, the CFA today ordered Mr Lee to pay the SFC's costs in both the Court of First Instance and the CFA. Mr Lee was also ordered to pay the prosecution's costs.
