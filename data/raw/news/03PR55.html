The SFC has gazetted a number of codes, guidelines and forms etc that will become effective upon implementation of the Securities and Futures Ordinance on 1 April 2003.

These items are part of a considerable body of regulatory codes and guidelines that are to be published by the SFC in the run up to 1 April.

Many SFC codes and guidelines are being updated to rationalise their contents with the SFO. New items have also been prepared to assist the market in understanding and complying with the regulatory requirements where applicable.

Most of the revisions do not involve policy changes. Where substantive changes are made, they generally have been made in response to market requests or following consultation with the market.

The four items that are gazetted today are:

Guidelines and Codes published under section 399, SFO

1. Guidelines on the Regulation of Automated Trading Services

The SFC issued the Guidelines on the Regulation of Automated Trading Services in February 2002 before the enactment of the SFO. With the exception of substituting references to the draft Securities and Futures Bill with the corresponding SFO provisions and transferring the guidelines relating to transitional arrangements relating to automated trading services to an appendix in the Guidelines, no other major changes have been made to the Guidelines now being issued. The Guidelines are issued under sections 95(6) and 399(1) of the SFO. (Section 95(6) requires the SFC to publish guidelines relating to the authorisation of automated trading services.)

2. Guidance Note on Short Selling Reporting and Stock Lending Record Keeping Requirements

3. Code of Conduct for Share Registrars

These are re-makes of the existing version. Only minimal changes have been effected in order to extend their application to registered institutions to ensure a level playing field and to rationalise them with the provisions of the SFO. 

Others

4. Notice of approved futures contracts under section 20(2)(a) of the SFO

Section 20(2)(a) prohibits the trading of futures contracts on a recognised futures market unless the futures contracts are approved, whether generally or in a particular case, by the SFC in a notice in the Gazette. The notice gazetted covers all types of futures contracts currently trading on the Hong Kong Futures Exchange.

The various items can be accessed through hyperlinks on the SFC website to the Gazette (please click on the links embedded in the electronic version of this press release, which is located in What’s New under the SFO section). They will be fully available on the SFC website from 1 April 2003.
