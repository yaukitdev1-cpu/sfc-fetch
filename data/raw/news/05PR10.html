"Know What You Pay For" is the theme of a new SFC investor education campaign to complement the release of the Guidelines on Disclosure of Fees and Charges Relating to Securities Services (Note 1).

To help investors understand the various kinds of fees and charges of dealing and holding securities traded on the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong (SEHK), the SFC has published an investor leaflet Know What You Pay For and launched an on-line game Figure Your Fees on the Electronic Investor Resources Centre (eIRC) at www.eirc.hk.

Know What You Pay For

This leaflet explains the nature of common fees and charges under the six standardised categories which the SFC recommends intermediaries to adopt in disclosing their fee structure to clients. There are also illustrations and tips to help investors calculate, interpret and compare the fees of securities services providers.

The leaflet is also complemented by a series of articles, which provide detailed explanation of common fees and charges. The articles are available under the section of "Features & Dr Wise" - "Dealing with Intermediaries" - "Fees and Charges" on the eIRC.

As with all other SFC investor publications, the leaflet is available free at the SFC office and the Consumer Advice Centres of the Consumer Council. The electronic version is available under the section of "Publications" - "Dealing with Intermediaries" on the eIRC.

Figure Your Fees

The interactive game on the eIRC helps you find out if you understand the fees and charges. You are provided with a sample fee schedule (which shows the fees typically charged by an intermediary) and are asked to identify or calculate the applicable fees and charges for different transactions and investment portfolios. If you give a wrong answer, a window will pop up to explain the fees applicable to the transaction or the amount you should pay.

This game is available under the section of "Game Zone" - "Finding Facts" on the eIRC. Comments and suggestions on the leaflet and the on-line game are most welcome and can be sent to eirc@sfc.hk.

Notes to Editor:

1. The SFC released the Guidelines on Disclosure of Fees and Charges Relating to Securities Services in November 2004. It recommends disclosure of fees and charges concerning securities traded on the SEHK under six standardised categories to clients to enhance price transparency and comparability. The six categories are trade-related services; scrip handling and settlement-related services; information services; account maintenance; nominee services and corporate actions; as well as financing charges and others. The Guidelines have been effective since 1 January 2005.
