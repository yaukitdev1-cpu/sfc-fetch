The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the appointment and re-appointment by the Financial Secretary of members to the SFC Advisory Committee for two years from today 1 June 2011 to 31 May 2013.

“I warmly welcome the five new members of the Advisory Committee, whose expertise and experience in their respective fields will be a valuable asset to the Advisory Committee. I look forward to the continued support and advice of the seven re-appointed members,” said Dr Eddy Fong, the SFC’s Chairman.

“I would also like to express my appreciation of the insights and the invaluable contribution of the five retiring members in the past years,” Dr Fong added.
