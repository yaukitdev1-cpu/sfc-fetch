The SFC today publishes the April issue of SFC Alert, a bi-monthly newsletter for both investors and intermediaries.

On the front page, SFC proposals to address analyst conflicts of interest invites the public to comment on the proposals. 

Inside, in the regular Senior management interview column, Chief Counsel Mr Andrew Young introduces the work of the Legal Services Division and its challenges ahead.

Roadmap for listing reform outlines the Government’s consultation conclusions on the proposals to enhance regulation of listing, and the role of the SFC in the reform.

After 10 years of budgeted deficits, the SFC has estimated a surplus in the current financial year.  $4 million surplus projected for 2004-05 has more information.

Safer means for IPO cheques refund covers the measures that are put in place following investors’ complaints about missing refund cheques.

Another regular column, Investor Focus, teaches investors how to judge whether a derivative warrant is expensive and the factors that may affect its price.

The April issue of SFC Alert is the 23nd issue of the newsletter, which was first published in October 2001. Copies of the current issue of SFC Alert are available at the SFC office. All issues are available on the SFC website.
