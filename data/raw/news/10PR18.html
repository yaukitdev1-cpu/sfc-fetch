The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) is positively reviewing the many responses to its recent consultation aimed at enhancing investor protection, said Mr Martin Wheatley, the SFC’s Chief Executive Officer.

Speaking at the StructuredRetailProducts.com conference in London, Mr Wheatley shared with over 250 participants the reform initiatives that the SFC is advancing to address concerns arising from the latest financial crisis.

“We have received many well-thought out, detailed and constructive responses to our proposals. We are now reviewing this feedback and all the opinions will be considered carefully,” said Mr Wheatley.

Proposals contained in the consultation, which has just closed, include a wide range of measures spanning the three key stages of an investment life-cycle - pre-sale, sale, and post-sale - such as the introduction of key facts statements and a limited cooling-off period for certain investors.

“This is an important exercise that will help shape the way investment business is conducted in the retail market, and ultimately help re-build investor confidence in the industry. We will continue to actively engage the industry in discussions so that any changes undertaken will come together as a balanced approach to achieving our regulatory objectives,” Mr Wheatley added.

During the conference, Mr Wheatley discussed, with overseas participants, a wide range of global issues facing regulators worldwide and international reform initiatives emerging in the regulatory context.
