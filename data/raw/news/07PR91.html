The SFC is placing more emphasis on interacting with the industry on market development, Chief Executive Officer, Mr Martin Wheatley, says in today's issue of the SFC Alert.

Creating a better market by streamlining processes and removing obstacles to innovation has become a more important focus of the SFC. The SFC Alert reports on several of these initiatives: proposed amendments to the position limits of Hang Seng Index and H-share Index futures and options; simplified authorisation of UCITS III funds; and a review of the marketing guidelines of funds.

Market practitioners are invited to send comments on how the SFC may further facilitate market development to the CEO's mailbox: ceo@sfc.hk.

This SFC Alert issue also reports on the exchange of side letters between the SFC and the China Securities Regulatory Commission to strengthen enforcement co-operation, and the agreement between the SFC and the China Banking Regulatory Commission to establish an information sharing platform regarding the QDII business of Mainland commercial banks.

The SFC Alert is a bi-monthly newsletter for both investors and intermediaries. Copies of the current issue of SFC Alert are available at the SFC office. All issues are available on the SFC website.
