The SFC is pleased to announce the appointment of members to the Dual Filing Advisory Group for a term of one year from 1 April 2006 to 31 March 2007.

The SFC welcomes Mr Raymond Lee, Mr Gavin Nesbitt and Mr Terry Ng as new members of the Advisory Group. The SFC also welcomes the re-appointment of Mr Ashley Alder, Mr Dennis Cassidy, Mr David Cheung, Mr W Gage McAfee, Mr Mike Scales, Mr James Siu, Mr Frank J Slevin, Mr Carlson Tong and Mr V-Nee Yeh.

The SFC would like to thank Ms Teresa Ko, Mr Peter P W Tse and Mr Tony Tsoi, who have stepped down from the Advisory Group, for their valuable contribution over the years.

The Advisory Group is established under section 8 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance. Members include investors, directors of listed companies and fund managers as well as market practitioners, including investment bankers, legal advisers and accountants. Since its establishment in May 2003, the Advisory Group has provided valuable advice to the SFC on treatment of cases under the Dual Filing regime and the relevant regulatory provisions, as well as on related policy issues. A full list of the members of the Advisory Group is provided in Note 1.

The SFC very much appreciates the efforts of the members of the Advisory Group in performing the functions of the body effectively and transparently. The SFC is confident that with their expertise and significant experience in the financial and investment sector, the members will continue to make valuable contributions to the Advisory Group.
