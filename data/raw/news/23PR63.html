The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the appointments and re-appointments by the Financial Secretary of members to the SFC Advisory Committee for a two-year term from 1 June 2023 to 31 May 2025.

“I would like to extend a warm welcome to all the new members.  I am certain the SFC would benefit from the valuable advice and insight of new and re-appointed members on issues related to the SFC’s policy and market regulations,” said Mr Tim Lui, the SFC’s Chairman.

“I would also like to take this opportunity to express my appreciation to the outgoing members for their contribution to the SFC during their term,” Mr Lui added.
