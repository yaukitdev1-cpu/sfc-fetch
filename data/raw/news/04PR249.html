The SFC today publishes the December issue of SFC Alert, a bi-monthly newsletter for both investors and intermediaries.

Co-operation with Philippine and Thai regulators reports on two key initiatives to strengthen co-operation between the SFC and other regulators in the region.

Inside, in the regular column Senior management interview, Mrs Cindy Fung, Director of Human Resources, Training and Development, talks about SFC’s human resources policies and practices. In November, the Association of Chartered Certified Accountants awarded the statuses of Approved Continuing Professional Development Employer and Accredited Employer to the SFC. The SFC also won the Labour Department’s Good People Management Award 2004 in December.

Besides, two prestigious awards were awarded to the SFC’s Annual Report 2003-2004. For details, please refer to Quarterly results which also reports the SFC’s financial position as at the end of September 2004.

Guidelines on analyst conflicts of interest explains major features of the new guidelines to help firms and analysts to remove, reduce and manage the conflicts.

Review of Takeovers and Repurchases Codes highlights some key proposals to amend the Codes to ensure continued fair treatment for shareholders.

The regular Investor Focus column advises investors on matters to pay attention to when investing in concept stocks.

Fees & charges outlines the new SFC guidelines which will enable investors to compare securities-related fees and charges among banks and brokers easier.

The December issue of SFC Alert is the 27th issue of the newsletter, which was first published in October 2001. Copies of the current issue of SFC Alert are available at the SFC office. All issues are available on the SFC website.
