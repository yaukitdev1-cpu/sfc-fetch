The SFC has successfully prosecuted Ms Xu Li for failing to comply with notices issued by SFC's investigators requiring attendance at interviews.

Four notices under Section 33(4) of the Securities and Futures Commission Ordinance were issued to Xu requiring her to attend interviews with investigators in connection with two investigations into market manipulation and short selling respectively.  However, Xu without reasonable excuse failed to attend the interviews.

Xu pleaded guilty to four summonses today before Magistrate Mr Ian Candy at Eastern Magistracy.  Xu was fined a total of $40,000 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $3,000 to the SFC.
