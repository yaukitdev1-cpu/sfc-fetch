Stock exchange participant brokers recorded a 5% increase in net profits in the first quarter of 2007 when compared with the previous quarter, due to the continuing buoyant turnover, a review in the latest issue of the SFC Quarterly Bulletin shows.

The Financial Review of the Securities Industry is one of the articles in the Spring 2007 issue (No.67) of the SFC Quarterly Bulletin, which is published today. The issue also contains an article, Review of the Securities Market, and an information note, IPO Activities during 2006 and their Performance.

The issue also includes three speeches delivered earlier by SFC’s Chairman Mr Eddy Fong, Chief Executive Officer Mr Martin Wheatley and Executive Director of the Intermediaries and Investment Products Division Mrs Alexa Lam on different occasions (Note 1).

The Bulletin can be subscribed for at $400 per year or downloaded free of charge from the SFC website under “Speeches & Publications” – “Publications”. The public are welcome to sign up on the SFC website Update Alert service to get email notice of new releases of the Bulletin.

1. Presenting his speech “Positioning Hong Kong in a Competitive Global Marketplace” at a Hong Kong Securities Institute luncheon, Mr Fong stated the necessity to reinforce Hong Kong’s competitive edge by capitalising on the Mainland connection and enhancing the quality of the market. Mr Wheatley told a group of senior executives from the financial community in Australia in his speech “Opportunities in Hong Kong and China” that Hong Kong’s capital market acted as a conduit for funds investing both in and out of the Mainland. Mrs Lam talked about the SFC’s ongoing initiatives to facilitate development of the fund industry while upholding investor protection and the reputation of Hong Kong as an international financial centre when she delivered her speech “Regulation & Market Development”.
