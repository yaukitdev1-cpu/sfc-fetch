The Mainland, Taiwan and Hong Kong markets share a common vision for a Greater China market and to this end Hong Kong can play a pivotal role, according to Mrs Alexa Lam, the SFC’s Deputy Chief Executive Officer and Executive Director, Policy, China and Investment Products.

Speaking at the opening ceremony of the First Asia Conference on Financial Engineering, Mrs Lam noted that Hong Kong can play a facilitating role in strengthening the co-operation and development of the financial markets across the Straits.

Mrs Lam said: “Hong Kong has always maintained close ties with Taiwan in the financial arena. Currently 54 Taiwanese enterprises are listed in Hong Kong, with market capitalisation totalling HK$334.9 billion. Moreover, in the area of asset management, Hong Kong fund managers are active in managing a number of offshore Taiwanese funds.”

As Taiwan ushers into a new era, some Taiwanese fund managers are actively looking for co-operation opportunities in Hong Kong that will enable them to launch investment products related to Mainland stocks.

Mrs Lam added: “To create an investment environment that is mutually beneficial to investors and the fund industry in Taiwan and Hong Kong, we are conducting a preliminary study into the establishment of a mechanism for the mutual recognition of ETFs. With such an arrangement in place, Hong Kong-stock ETFs can be traded in Taiwan, and Taiwan-stock ETFs can be listed in the Hong Kong market.”

Mrs Lam believes the Greater China markets will develop into a major momentum in the new economy in Asia.
