The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X} under Part XV of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

In January 2006, {X}, a director of China State Construction International Holdings Limited, sold 400,000 shares in China Overseas Land & Investment Limited. China Overseas is an associated corporation of China State Construction. {X} failed his duty to disclose the disposal to the Stock Exchange and China State within three business days. As a result, {X} contravened the notification requirements under Part XV of the SFO.

{X} today pleaded guilty to two summonses before Mr Garry Tallentire, a Principal Magistrate at Eastern Magistracy. {X} was fined $8,000 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $7,276 to the SFC.
