The SFC will from today publish a regularly updated calendar of outstanding prosecutions.

The Calendar is posted on the SFC website under “Press Releases, Publications & Speeches” – “Press Releases” – “Enforcement Actions”. It contains information about prosecutions by the SFC including the date, court location, the offences with reference to sections of the law (and a general description), the number of summonses issued, the status (whether a case is for plea taking, pre-trial hearing or trial), and the names of the defendants in cases set for pre-trial hearing or trial (Note 1).

The calendar is for reference only. It is updated every two weeks. The SFC will endeavour to update it whenever necessary between the regular updates (Note 2). However, members of the public who are interested in particular cases may wish to confirm the relevant information with the courts.

An SFC spokesman said publication of the calendar increased the transparency of the enforcement work of the SFC, and the information would be useful for media who might be interested in covering those court cases.

“As can be seen from the current calendar the SFC is active, on a number of fronts, in bringing market misconduct to the attention of the Courts,” the spokesman said.

As a standard practice, the SFC will issue a press release summarising a prosecution upon its successful conclusion.

Ends

Notes:

1. The identity of defendants in cases set for plea taking cannot be published prior to the court appearance.

2. The SFC will not necessarily issue a press release when an update is made to the calendar. Please also note that the calendar does not include prosecutions of securities offences by the other authorities such as the Police or ICAC.
