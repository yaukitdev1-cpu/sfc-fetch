The SFC today launches more investor education materials to help investors learn how to deal in and monitor their investments in hedge funds.     

Two new feature articles have been posted on the SFC-operated Electronic Investor Resources Centre (eIRC) at www .hkeirc.org.

Dealing in Hedge Funds explains:

  • the minimum subscription thresholds for different types of hedge funds;
  • common fees and charges involved, especially performance fees which are usually  charged by hedge funds;
  • fund valuation requirements and the ways to get fund prices; and
  • the possible liquidity constraints when redeeming from a hedge fund.


Monitor Your Hedge Funds explains:

  • the importance for investors to monitor their investments in hedge funds;
  • the basic reporting requirements for hedge funds;
  • how to interpret the information disclosed in hedge fund reports, particularly the management commentary, portfolio review and some performance and risk measures disclosed in quarterly reports; and
  • the issues to note when comparing the performance figures of different hedge funds. 


In monitoring their hedge funds, investors are reminded that funds authorised by the SFC must issue regular reports to their holders on at least a quarterly basis in accordance with the Guidelines on Hedge Funds Reporting Requirement.

The SFC has previously published four articles - Hedge Fund Basics, Hedge Fund Strategies, Regulation on Hedge Funds and How to Read a Hedge Fund Prospectus - which introduce to investors the characteristics and risks of hedge funds, the SFC’s authorisation regime and the key information disclosed in fund prospectuses.  All articles are available on the eIRC. 

These articles complement the investor leaflets “How Much Do You Know About Hedge Funds?” and “Ten Questions to Ask Before Investing in a Fund”, which were published last year by the SFC.  Copies of the investor leaflets are available free of charge at the SFC office and the 11 Consumer Advice Centres of the Consumer Council.

The SFC’s Investor Hotline at 2840 9333 remains open to provide educational information on hedge funds.
