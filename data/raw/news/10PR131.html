The Eastern Magistracy today convicted after trial Mr {X} of giving misleading information to the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) in his application for a licence to carry on regulated activities.

{X} was fined $10,000 and ordered to pay the SFC’s investigation costs of $10,825.

The SFC found that {X} tried to mislead the SFC in explaining that he had been dismissed by his previous employer because of carelessness when his former employer in fact dismissed him for serious misconduct contrary to section 383 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

In its role to protect investors, the SFC only grants licences to persons who demonstrate the required level of integrity and honesty as well as expertise to perform regulated activities.
