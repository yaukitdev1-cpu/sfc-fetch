The SFC welcomes the findings contained in the Process Review Panel's third Annual Report, which is published by the Government today.

The SFC is pleased to note the Panel's conclusion that, in general, adequate safeguards and checks and balances are in place in the SFC's internal procedures. The Panel reviewed 51 completed cases and SFC procedures covering various areas of SFC work. It found that the decisions and actions of the SFC in carrying out its regulatory functions adhered to its established internal procedures.

The SFC welcomes the valuable suggestions made by the Panel for improving its procedures. The SFC also thanks the industry practitioners for their comments.

An SFC spokesman said: "The SFC is pleased to report that it has already adopted a number of specific recommendations of the Panel. The Commission looks forward to continuing its co-operation with the Panel in its fourth year of work."
