The SFC today publishes the December issue of SFC Alert, a bi-monthly newsletter for both investors and intermediaries.

The regular Investor Focus column reminds investors to watch out for bogus websites to avoid losses amid a new style of Internet fraud. 

Readers are also invited to read a new article entitled Fraudulent copycat websites on the SFC website (Note 1).  The article has been written in light of recent suspected website scams and supplements the advice made by the SFC on 10 December 2003. The article describes in detail common features of fraudulent websites and sets out the steps for investors to avoid becoming victims.

On the front page of the SFC Alert, the lead story Surveys on research activities reports on the findings of two surveys the SFC conducted earlier as part of its efforts to address the issue of conflicts of interest of analysts.

Inside, Enhancing fees transparency discusses the proposed guidelines on disclosure of fees and charges in securities dealing.  The guidelines aim to promote fair competition, price transparency and fees comparability.

More opportunities under CEPA outlines the agreement reached between the SFC and China Securities Regulatory Commission regarding the securities and futures industry.

Due to careful spending and increased income on the back of a higher market turnover, the financial position of the SFC has reversed from a deficit in April – June to a surplus in July – September 2003. On page three, $24.4 m surplus in Q2 has more details.

The December issue of SFC Alert is the 21st issue of the newsletter, which was first published in October 2001. Copies of the current issue of SFC Alert are available at the SFC office. All issues are available on the SFC website.
