The SFC welcomes the appointment by the Financial Secretary of Mr Paul Kennedy as the Executive Director / Chief Operating Officer of the SFC.

SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, Mr Martin Wheatley, said: “I am pleased to welcome Mr Paul Kennedy as our new Chief Operating Officer. With his professional management expertise and extensive operational experience in the financial field, I am confident Mr Kennedy will contribute a lot to further strengthening the SFC’s internal operations. With Mr Kennedy’s appointment, we now have a full team of Executive Directors that would ensure that we shall continue to carry out our regulatory mission effectively.”
