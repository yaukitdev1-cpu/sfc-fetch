The latest Dr Wise’s Column reminds investors to be wary of an increasingly common scam in which stock prices are “pumped” up on the Internet or in spam emails.

The SFC today publishes the March issue of Dr Wise’s Column, “Pump and Dump” Schemes, on its InvestEd website at www.InvestEd.hk.

“Pump and dump” schemes are a form of Internet financial fraud that is becoming more common. Usually, fraudsters “pump” up the price of a small counter with false rumours and then “dump”, i.e. sell, the stock. Fraudsters make use of chat room messages, postings on bulletin boards, weblogs and spam emails to induce investors to buy stocks with favourable news. Most of the time investors do not know the identities of these people, who may use pseudonyms, and cannot verify the authenticity and accuracy of the information.

Investors should always check information carefully and not get taken in by wild claims of new products or future profits, especially when they are looking for low-priced stocks in a volatile market.

Dr Wise is a fictitious character. Through his column, he talks to investors about the key issues of investing and regulatory thoughts.
