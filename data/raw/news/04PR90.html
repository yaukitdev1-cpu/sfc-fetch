The SFC today publishes the May issue of the SFC Enforcement Reporter, summarising the SFC's enforcement action in the month of April 2004 and discussing the compliance issues highlighted by recent cases.

In April, the SFC successfully prosecuted three companies and five people for defrauding the SFC in Financial Resources Rules returns, failure to make timely and accurate disclosure of interests, unlicensed margin financing, and cold-calling. Among them, one person was sentenced to three months’ imprisonment for defrauding the SFC. He was the first one to be jailed for such an offence.    
The SFC disciplined six licensees during the month for internal control failures, lack of professional knowledge, allowing a third party to operate a client’s account without written authorisation, and losing client agreements.

We have continued to publish figures on acquittals and disciplinary proceedings that have been concluded without sanction. For the first month of the financial year 2004-2005, the SFC has successfully prosecuted eight entities. We withdrew all summonses issued against three persons, but otherwise there was no acquittal.  The SFC has also disciplined six licensees for regulatory breaches. Disciplinary proceedings have been taken against four licensees, which were eventually discontinued with no formal sanction imposed. Such proceedings were also commenced and discontinued against one deemed licensee who left his firm before the conclusion of the action.
