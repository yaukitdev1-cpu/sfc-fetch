The new regime set out under the Guidelines for Sponsors and Compliance Advisers (Sponsor Guidelines) will become effective on 1 January 2007 (Note 1). The SFC would like to remind existing intermediaries licensed or registered for Type 6 regulated activity that do not intend to act as sponsors or compliance advisers to indicate their intentions to the SFC by tomorrow (6 October 2006). They may complete and return the reply slip attached to a circular that was sent to them last month (Note 2).

Intermediaries that intend to act as sponsors or compliance advisers from 1 January 2007 should submit the required information for the SFC’s assessment as soon as practicable.
