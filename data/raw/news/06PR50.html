The SFC is pleased to report that the Investor Education Advisory Committee (IEAC) has been actively advising the SFC on several key investor education initiatives over the past year.

The IEAC advised the SFC on the scope of its various retail investor surveys covering retail participation, level of financial understanding, and investment behaviour and practices. Members also gave valuable comments on the theme “Before you Invest, Ask the Right Questions” and other activities for the SFC’s investor education work in 2006. Some showed particular support by attending the ceremony in January to launch the Investor Education Month and the renamed InvestEd website.

Outside of the regular committee meetings, members have been making use of their networks to assist the SFC in disseminating its investor education messages. For instance, members acted as adjudicators of the SFC’s first “My Investment Story” competition and gave investing tips to the public in investor forums and radio programmes.

In the year ahead, the SFC will look to the IEAC for advice on a range of matters including how to raise investors’ understanding of derivative warrants and help them guard against mis-selling of financial products.

The SFC is pleased to announce the appointment of four members and re-appointment of seven members to the IEAC for a term of one year from 1 April 2006 to 31 March 2007.

Mr Peter Au-Yang, SFC Executive Director and Chief Operating Officer, who chairs the IEAC, said: “I welcome the new members who will bring professional expertise and valuable insights to the Committee. Also, I thank the re-appointed members for continuing to share with us their expert views.”

Mr Au-Yang also paid tribute to outgoing members for their valuable contribution. They are Dr Bill Kwok, Mr Ben Kwong, The Honourable Li Kwok Ying and Mr Joseph Pang.

Established under section 8 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance, the IEAC plays a crucial role in identifying areas that the SFC should study in order to further the interests of investors, and aspects of the securities and futures industry where investor education is desirable.
