The SFC has been approving subordinated loans or loan facilities obtained by licensed brokers, to enable them to meet the minimum liquid capital requirement under the Financial Resources Rules (FRR). This helps facilitate brokers’ business operations amid Initial Public Offerings (IPOs), without compromising investor protection and market integrity.

Over the past two weeks, the Commission has approved subordinated loans or loan facilities totalling $1.5 billion.  It has managed to approve all of applications, each taking, on average, less than four business days to process. 

Mrs Alexa Lam, SFC's Executive Director of Intermediaries and Investment Products, said: "While it is the responsibility of the practitioners to comply with the financial resources requirements, the SFC works with the industry to facilitate their business in IPO activities without compromising investor protection."
