The SFC welcomes the State Administration of Foreign Exchange’s announcement of a pilot scheme to allow Mainland individuals to invest in securities listed on the Hong Kong stock market through specific channels.

“We believe this initiative will benefit the long term development of both the Mainland and Hong Kong markets, and further strengthen Hong Kong’s status as an international financial centre. The SFC will work closely with the Mainland authorities on any regulatory matters that may arise for investor protection,” SFC Chairman, Mr Eddy Fong, said.
