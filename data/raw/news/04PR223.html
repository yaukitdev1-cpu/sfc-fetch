The SFC is pleased to announce that it has signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with the Philippine Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) (Note 1).

The MOU establishes a framework for mutual assistance and facilitates the exchange of information between the SFC and the SEC to enable better regulation and enforcement of the securities laws and regulations of Hong Kong and the Philippines (Note 2).

The MOU was signed today by Mr Andrew Sheng, Chairman of the SFC, and Mrs Fe B. Barin, Chairperson of the SEC.  The signing took place in Singapore when they both attended the IOSCO Asia Pacific Regional Committee meeting  (Note 3).

Mr Sheng said: “This Memorandum opens a new chapter in our working relationship with the SEC.  With this Memorandum our respective abilities in market protection and law enforcement are greatly enhanced.  I am delighted with the closer and enhanced co-operation between our two Commissions.”

Mrs Barin said:   “Vast changes in technology have blurred the distinctions between borders, particularly in terms of regulating the securities market and enforcing securities law.  We anticipate that with the signing of the Memorandum of Understanding between our Commissions, the effectiveness of the enforcement of our respective laws will be greatly enhanced and will strengthen the confidence of investors in the Philippine market.”

Ends

Notes:      

1. SEC is the Philippine capital markets regulator.

2. A copy of the MOU can be found on the SFC website under “About the SFC” – “Cross-Border Co-operation”.  Including this MOU, the SFC has signed 34 MOUs or similar co-operative agreements with securities and futures regulators around the world. 

3. The Asia Pacific Regional Committee (APRC) is one of the Regional Committees of the International Organization of Securities Commissions (IOSCO).  It meets once a year to discuss specific regional issues.  This year’s APRC meeting is held in Singapore and hosted by the Monetary Authority of Singapore.
