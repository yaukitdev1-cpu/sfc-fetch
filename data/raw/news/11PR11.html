Consultation conclusions published today by the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) show respondents welcomed the draft Guidelines on Disclosure of Inside Information the SFC issued earlier, along with the Government’s proposals to impose on listed corporations a statutory obligation to disclose price-sensitive information.

When the Government publishes the relevant Bill (Note 1), the SFC will issue a revised draft of the Guidelines incorporating the text of the bill. The final Guidelines will be published when the legislation is settled.

Two sets of consultation conclusions were released today:

  • conclusions to an SFC consultation paper issued in March 2010 (Note 2), summarising the comments received from the public, the regulator’s responses to such comments and the amendments to be made to the draft Guidelines; and
  • the Government conclusions paper (Note 3), setting out policy responses to the proposals.


“We support the proposed legislation, which if implemented, will be a major step forward in cultivating a continuous disclosure culture among listed corporations,” said Mr Martin Wheatley, the SFC’s Chief Executive Officer. “We will continue to work closely with the Government on the legislative amendments and related measures.”

The SFC received 19 written submissions, mainly from listed corporations, professional associations and market practitioners. The Guidelines provide guidance on the interpretation of inside information, a term used in the legislation to mean price-sensitive information, and explain the application of safe harbours. Respondents generally found the Guidelines helpful and practicable with comments seeking further guidance or clarification on specific matters.

Notes:

1. The Government is preparing a bill to codify the requirements on listed corporations to disclose price-sensitive information. The bill is expected to be gazetted and introduced to the Legislative Council in the 2010/11 legislative session.
2. On 29 March 2010, the SFC issued a “Consultation Paper on Draft Guidelines on Disclosure of Inside Information” for a three-month consultation, which ended on 28 June 2010.
3. The Government’s “Proposed Statutory Codification of Certain Requirements to Disclose Price Sensitive Information by Listed Corporations – Consultation Conclusions” is available on its website at www.fstb.gov.hk.
