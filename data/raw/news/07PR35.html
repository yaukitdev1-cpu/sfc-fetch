The International Organization of Securities Commissions (IOSCO) is inviting comments on new principles which aim to ensure that hedge funds' financial instruments are appropriately valued.

In releasing its consultation paper, IOSCO said that the principles sought to ensure in particular that valuations were not distorted to the disadvantage of fund investors. It was extremely important that hedge funds adopt and operate robust portfolio valuation policies and procedures, it said.

The Principles for the Valuation of Hedge Fund Portfolios emphasize the importance of clear written procedures which are consistently operated and regularly reviewed, and which provide for an appropriate degree of independence to deliver effective checks and controls.

The principles are meant to be a practical tool for hedge fund managers and governing bodies, and those involved in the valuation process. They may also be helpful for institutional and sophisticated investors and their representatives.

SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, Mr Martin Wheatley, encouraged industry practitioners and investors to submit their comments to IOSCO by the closing date of 21 June 2007.

“The SFC believes that this subject warrants the close attention of the market. The release of the consultation paper provides a topical backdrop for an upcoming IOSCO panel discussion on the regulatory issues concerning hedge funds, which is expected to attract wide international interest,” Mr Wheatley said.

Mr Wheatley will chair a public panel on “Hedge Funds - New Regulatory Challenges” on 12 April, during the IOSCO Annual Conference in Mumbai, India. The panel will also feature senior speakers from the public and private sectors internationally.

As a leading hedge fund management centre in Asia, Hong Kong has participated actively in the discussion of the principles. The SFC’s effort is led by Mrs Alexa Lam, Executive Director of Intermediaries and Investment Products, who is a member of a high-level industry-regulator working group to study the issue.

The IOSCO Consultation Report on Principles for the Valuation of Hedge Fund Portfolios is available on the IOSCO website (www.iosco.org). A final paper is expected in the autumn of 2007.

Meanwhile, IOSCO’s Technical Committee has also published its current Work Program, which contains an outline of the priorities in the short term. It invites the international financial services community to comment on the program both in respect of prioritisation and relevance. The document is also available on the IOSCO website.
