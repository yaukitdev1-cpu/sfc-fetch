The SFC today publishes a research paper on the listing of Taiwanese companies in Hong Kong.

The paper noted 54 Taiwanese companies were listed in Hong Kong as of the end of March 2008. 10 IPOs were launched by Taiwanese companies during January 2007-March 2008, raising a total of HK$20.6 billion.

While Hong Kong provides a sound regulatory environment, international visibility, and a broad institutional and international investor base, specific technical reasons such as investment restrictions have contributed to the number of Taiwanese listings in Hong Kong. However, market participants believe Taiwanese companies listed in Hong Kong can benefit from international exposure, strong linkage with the Mainland and higher valuations.

Hong Kong can capitalise on these advantages to strengthen its position by continuing to attract more listings of Taiwanese and overseas companies, thereby enhancing its role as an international financial centre, the paper said.

The research paper is available on the SFC website under the section “Research and Statistics”.
