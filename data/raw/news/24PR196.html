The Securities and Futures Commission’s (SFC) Chairman Dr Kelvin Wong and Chief Executive Officer Ms Julia Leung held a high-level meeting with the Minister of the National Financial Regulatory Administration (NFRA) Mr Li Yunze in Hong Kong yesterday.

The two sides gave an update on the current landscape of the Mainland and Hong Kong financial markets, respectively. They also exchanged views on various topics including strengthening cross-boundary regulatory cooperation, as well as consolidating and enhancing Hong Kong's status as an international financial centre.
