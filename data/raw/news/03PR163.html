The SFC proposes to amend the price stabilizing rules to facilitate global offerings of securities.

The SFC releases today for public consultation a proposed amendment to the Securities and Futures (Price Stabilizing) Rules (PS Rules) in relation to the recognition of the UK Price Stabilizing Rules.

The PS Rules prescribe the circumstances in which price stabilizing action may be taken in connection with public offerings and regulate such action. This facilitates capital raising by corporations within an open, transparent and accountable framework.

The PS Rules are to a large extent commensurate with international standards so as to facilitate global offerings of securities conducted in Hong Kong and other markets. Having regards to the similarity in the regulatory framework for securities transactions and mechanisms for securities offerings, the PS Rules have been modelled closely on the UK Price Stabilizing Rules issued by the Financial Services Authority (FSA) pursuant to the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000.

In order to facilitate global offerings, the PS Rules provides that stabilizing action conducted overseas for Hong Kong traded securities with a view to stabilizing the price of the securities on the overseas market and effected in accordance with the stabilization rules in recognised jurisdictions specified in Schedule 4 to the PS Rules will not for the purpose of the Securities and Futures Ordinance be regarded as market misconduct.

It has been the SFC’s intention to recognise certain overseas jurisdictions which provide similar regulatory safeguards against market manipulation for the purpose of PS Rules as soon as possible once the rules are made.  The Consultation Paper seeks to consult the public on the inclusion of UK as a recognised jurisdiction.

The SFC has also held discussions with the FSA regarding recognition by it of the PS Rules. The FSA is proposing to grant recognition of the PS Rules by making the necessary amendments to the UK Price Stabilizing Rules after a public consultation.
