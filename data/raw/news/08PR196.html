The Vice Chairman of the China Securities Regulatory Commission (CSRC), Mr Yao Gang, today visited the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) and held discussions with its Chairman, Mr Eddy Fong, on the latest developments in the capital markets and the related regulatory issues.

This was Mr Yao’s first visit to the SFC since he was appointed Vice Chairman of the CSRC.

Mr Yao also attended a welcome reception hosted by the SFC, where he met over 60 senior representatives from the Hong Kong financial industry.

Mr Yao stressed that strengthening exchange and co-operation between the Mainland and Hong Kong securities markets would facilitate an effective response to the challenges posed by the current global financial crisis. The CSRC will actively support the measures and policies adopted by Hong Kong towards this end, he said.

“In particular, the CSRC will continue to lend support to qualified Mainland enterprises who are considering a Hong Kong listing, to Mainland securities and futures institutions seeking to set up branch operations in Hong Kong, and to qualified Mainland enterprises hoping to achieve synergy through collaboration with Hong Kong listed companies.”

Mr Fong pointed out that the fast-growing Mainland economy is a strong buttress for the further development of Hong Kong’s capital market.

“The fallout of the US sub-prime crisis has impacted the global financial markets. The new financial landscape emerging from the turmoil has underlined the importance of co-operation between the Mainland and the SAR. Closer co-operation will assist us in tackling market risks. We will join hands in responding to the crises and challenges ahead, and our concerted efforts will ensure the prosperity and development of our capital markets.”

Both sides agreed to strengthen regulatory co-operation between the CSRC and the SFC, to introduce joint measures to cope with the current crisis and to promote the healthy development of the securities markets.
