Eminent speakers from around the world will participate in the plenary panel discussions at the 31st IOSCO Annual Conference, which will be held in Hong Kong on 5 - 8 June 2006.

Mr Michel Prada, Chairman, Autorité des marchés financiers, France, Mr Martin Wheatley, SFC’s Chairman, Mr Arthur Docters van Leeuwen, Chairman, The Netherlands Authority for the Financial Markets and Dr Doğan Cansizlar, Chairman & CEO, Capital Markets Board of Turkey will be moderators of the four panels.

In addition, we are honoured to have Mr Shang Fulin, Chairman of China Securities Regulatory Commission to be one of the expert panelists. Other distinguished regulators, practitioners and experts from around the world will be speaking on these panels (Note 2).

These influential leaders will discuss: (i) initial experiences in the implementation of International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS), (ii) regulation of hedge funds, (iii) transparency in the bond markets and (iv) challenges related to the implementation of the IOSCO principles and of the IOSCO MOU in emerging markets.

The IOSCO Annual Conference is hosted by the SFC and expected to draw hundreds of regulators and market practitioners from over 100 jurisdictions. IOSCO is a forum for member securities regulators to set priorities in launching initiatives and setting standards for securities regulation, keeping in view the three objectives of regulation: the protection of investors, ensuring that markets are fair, efficient and transparent, and the reduction of systemic risk.
