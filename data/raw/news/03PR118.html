The SFC today publishes the May issue of SFC Enforcement Reporter, summarising the Commission’s enforcement actions in the month of April 2003 and discussing the compliance issues highlighted by recent cases.

The SFC revoked the licence of a securities dealer’s representative for stealing over $21 million over a substantial period of time at his employer’s company.
 
Other notable cases included a concluding disciplinary action concerning manipulation of derivative warrants. A major shareholder of a securities firm surrendered her licences as a result of the SFC’s allegations that she had either actively assisted or turned a blind eye to the manipulative trading of derivative warrants.
 
The Takeovers and Mergers Executive of the SFC for the first time imposed cold shoulder orders on two executive directors of a company for its failure to consult the Executive on certain transactions and to issue a response document despite a ruling by the Takeovers Panel.
 
A securities company was severely reprimanded for inadequate internal controls following a string of disciplinary actions against its employees.
 
For the first month of the financial year 2003-04, the SFC has successfully prosecuted five people and firms, and disciplined 11 market intermediaries for various regulatory breaches.
