The SFC is pleased that it is a winner of the Good People Management Award 2004 organised by the Labour Department. 

The competition is particularly keen this year since altogether 374 participating organisations and companies took part in the award competition, almost doubling the number when it was last organised in 2001.

Mr Peter Au-Yang, SFC’s Executive Director and Chief Operating Officer, received the award from the Financial Secretary Mr Henry Tang at an award presentation ceremony today.

Mr Au-Yang said: “We are very pleased to be given the award. It shows recognition of the quality of SFC’s human resources policy, procedures and practices, which we would continue to enhance to the highest standards.”

As a winner, the SFC can display the award logo for a period of two years on SFC’s promotional materials to reflect the Commission’s achievements in good people management practices.
