The latest edition of the Enforcement Reporter published by the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) reports that two more individuals were jailed for insider dealing, marking the eighth and ninth insider dealing convictions and the third insider dealing case that resulted in imprisonment in the last 12 months.

On the Lehman Brothers Minibonds issue, this edition reports that the SFC, the Hong Kong Monetary Authority and 16 distributing banks have reached an agreement to repurchase Minibonds from eligible banking customers. Separately, the two brokers who had entered into agreements with the SFC earlier to repurchase Minibonds notified the SFC that they had completed their voluntary repurchases.

The Reporter gives an account of the Court of Appeal’s ruling in favour of an SFC appeal against a court approval to privatise PCCW Ltd. The Court of Appeal opined that clear vote manipulation had occurred and that share splitting was a form of vote manipulation.

Also reported was the SFC’s publication of the first announcement on high concentration of shareholdings. The announcement alerts the investing public to exercise extreme caution when dealing in such shares as their price might fluctuate substantially even if only a small number of shares is traded.

The Enforcement Reporter is a newsletter that highlights key enforcement outcomes and issues. It is available on the SFC website under “Speeches, Publications & Consultations” – “Publications”.

The public are invited to subscribe to the Enforcement Reporter by registering for the Update Alert service on the SFC website. They will then be able to receive the newsletter via email.
