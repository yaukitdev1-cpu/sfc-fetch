The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today announces in a consultation conclusions paper that, with immediate effect, certain routine takeovers-related announcements will no longer be required to be submitted to the Executive (Note 1) for comment prior to publication.

The changes (Note 2) came following a four-week public consultation on proposals to relax the current pre-vetting requirement of certain routine announcements issued under the Codes on Takeovers and Mergers and Share Repurchases (Codes) and to make miscellaneous amendments to the Codes.

"Respondents generally agreed with the proposed amendments, expressing the opinion that they represent a positive development that would reduce compliance burden potentially," said the SFC's Chief Executive Officer Mr Martin Wheatley.

Details of their comments and the SFC’s responses to those comments are in the conclusions paper (Note 3).

The SFC has also made amendments to the Codes in relation to real estate investment trusts. Please refer to the separate press release issued today for details.

Ends

Notes:

  1. The Executive refers to the Executive Director of the SFC's Corporate Finance Division or his delegate.
  2. Amendments to the Codes were gazetted today and set out in Appendix 2 to the consultation conclusions.
  3. A total of eight submissions were received from market participants during the consultation period.
