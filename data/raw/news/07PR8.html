The SFC today unveils the investor education theme for 2007: “Know Your Risk”.

Throughout 2007, the SFC will focus its investor education on encouraging the public to understand different aspects of “risk” in their investments, including market volatility, increasing complexity of products, scams and malpractices.

Officiating at the Investor Education 2007 Ceremony today, SFC Chairman, Mr Eddy Fong, said: “Volatility is a double-edged sword and investors must always keep in mind the risks they are exposing themselves to. Investors should not simply look at the nominally attractive return of investment products without seriously considering their suitability and the downside risk. They should also take steps to monitor their accounts and stay vigilant against misappropriation and unauthorised trading.”

“Investor education is a huge task and it requires long-term commitment. The SFC, Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited, financial intermediaries, industry bodies, media and schools each has a role to play to enhance the financial knowledge of our investors. I look forward to working closely with all of you to improve the financial literacy of Hong Kong people,” Mr Fong added (Note 1).

The SFC has also launched a new TV drama series “Wising up with Experience”, which for the first time is adapted from real-life stories, as part of its activities during January, the Investor Education Month in 2007 (Note 2). The series comprises five episodes, each for six minutes, covering malpractices such as misappropriation, mis-selling, cold calling, the importance of knowing your broker and misconceptions about derivative warrants (Note 3). The series will be screened on the Finance Channel of Cable TV from 22 to 26 January 2007. The stories will also be published in Hong Kong Economic Times on 20, and 22 to 25 January 2007.
