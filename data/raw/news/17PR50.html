The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the re-appointment by the Financial Secretary of Mr Albert Au Siu-cheung as a Non-Executive Director for a term of two years with effect from 26 May 2017 (Note 1).

"Mr Au’s advice and insights have greatly benefited the SFC over the past two years and I look forward to working with him in his new term," the SFC’s chairman Mr Carlson Tong said.

End

Note:

  1. Mr Au was first appointed as a Non-Executive Director of the SFC on 26 May 2015.
