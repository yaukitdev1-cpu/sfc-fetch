The SFC today issues a circular to inform fund management companies that real estate investment trusts (REITs) authorised by the SFC are permissible collective investment schemes for the purpose of funds authorised under Chapter 7 of the Code on Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds (the UT Code).

Under the UT Code, collective investment schemes are not allowed to invest in any type of real estate. Therefore, a scheme authorised under Chapter 7 of the UT Code may not invest in schemes that invest primarily in real estate, generally known as REITs.

In order to give market participants more flexibility, the SFC has reviewed this investment restriction and concluded that with immediate effect, schemes authorised under Chapter 7 of the UT Code may now invest in SFC-authorised REITs, subject to an overall limit of 10% of their total net asset value.

Mrs Alexa Lam, SFC’s Executive Director of Intermediaries and Investment Products, said: "Given that an SFC-authorised REIT is required to be listed on the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong, collective investment schemes that invest in it have an objective and transparent means for investing in and valuing their investments therein.

The circular is available at the office of the SFC and on the SFC website.
