The SFC today publishes the February issue of SFC Alert, a bi-monthly newsletter for both investors and market participants.

On the front page, Our priorities in 2006 covers the SFC’s key work areas this year: enforcement and investor education.

In the regular Senior Management Interview, Mr Leo Lee, Director of Finance and Administration, shares with readers how the SFC prepares its budget.

The SFC will in June host the 31st Annual Conference of the International Organization of Securities Commissions (IOSCO) in Hong Kong. Register for IOSCO Annual Conference 2006! invites interested parties to register before 15 March to enjoy a 15% discount off the standard registration fee. The following topics will be discussed at the conference’s public panels on 7 – 8 June:

1. International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) – initial experiences
2. Hedge funds – how far is it necessary to regulate?
3. Bond markets – should their transparency be enhanced?
4. Challenges related to the implementation of the IOSCO principles and of the IOSCO MOU in emerging securities markets.

The SFC is sending invitation to practitioners and other interested parties in Hong Kong and the Mainland to participate in the conference (Note 1).

Share margin financing reports the SFC’s plan to set a securities collateral re-pledging limit soon in order to better protect Hong Kong’s over 1.3 million investors. Meanwhile, according to the Business Activities Survey 2004/05, the securities industry saw its income increase for a second year.

In a two-page special, Investor education programmes 2006 outlines the SFC’s latest investor education initiatives and upcoming activities, which aim to encourage investors to ask the right questions about products, services and advisers’ recommendations.

Copies of the current issue of SFC Alert are available at the SFC office. All issues are available on the SFC website.
