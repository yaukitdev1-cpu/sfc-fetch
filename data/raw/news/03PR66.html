The SFC has gazetted a number of codes, guidelines and forms etc that will become effective upon implementation of the Securities and Futures Ordinance on 1 April 2003.

These items are part of a considerable body of regulatory codes and guidelines that are to be published by the SFC in the run up to 1 April.

Many SFC codes and guidelines are being updated to rationalise their contents with the SFO. New items have also been prepared to assist the market in understanding and complying with the regulatory requirements where applicable.

Most of the revisions do not involve policy changes. Where substantive changes are made, they generally have been made in response to market requests or following consultation with the market.

The three items that are gazetted today are:

Guidelines and Codes published under section 399, SFO

1. The Codes on Takeovers and Mergers and Share Repurchases

Changes have been made to bring the Codes into line with the provisions of the SFO. The changes include replacing various references to Ordinances that are now encompassed in the SFO and replacing the extracts from the old SFC (Fees) Rules with the relevant extracts from the revised Securities and Futures (Fees) Rules that were gazetted on 13 December 2002 (L.N. 228 of 2002). There are also changes to the membership structure of the Takeovers Appeal Committee.

2. Guidelines on Use of Offer Awareness and Summary Disclosure Materials in Offerings of Shares and Debentures Under the Companies Ordinance

The Guidelines replace the version of the same guidelines which were gazetted on 21 February 2003.  With the exception of substituting references to provisions of the Protection of Investors Ordinance and the Securities Ordinance with the corresponding SFO provisions, no other changes have been made to the Guidelines now being issued.  

Guidelines and Codes published under section 169(1), SFO

3. Code of Conduct for Corporate Finance Advisers

Changes have been made to the definition of corporate finance advisers to reflect the changes to the licensing regime under the SFO. They now include persons or entities who are licensed or registered under the SFO as licensed representatives, licensed corporations or registered institutions.  Clarification is also given on what "advising on corporate finance" entails under the SFO.

The various items can be accessed through hyperlinks on the SFC website to the Gazette (please click on the links embedded in the electronic version of this press release, which is located in What’s New under the SFO section).  They will be fully available on the SFC website from 1 April 2003.
