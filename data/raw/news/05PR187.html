The SFC issues the following update on the operation of the Dual Filing arrangements under the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

In the three months from April to June 2005, the SFC received, via the Stock Exchange, 23 listing applications.  This compares to 30 listing applications received in the previous quarter (Note 1). Of the 23 applications, we issued comments on 12 cases. Our average response time was seven working days.  In the previous quarter we commented on 19 cases of the 30 applications received.

As reported previously (Note 2), in the three months from October to December 2004 we commented on eight out of 18 applications received.  Of those eight cases, four cases lapsed due to the expiry of the six-month application period, one was rejected by the Stock Exchange, and three have been listed.

Conduct of sponsors

To protect investors, the Companies Ordinance requires prospectuses to be either in the English language with a Chinese translation or in the Chinese language with an English translation, unless an exemption is granted. We note with concern that certain sponsors failed to exercise due care and skill regarding the preparation of the Chinese translation of prospectuses, as required under the Corporate Finance Adviser Code of Conduct.

For example, in one case the listing applicant’s final prospectus included seven inconsistencies between the English and the Chinese versions as to certain significant information about the company. There were also other errors in the English prospectus.

In another case, the information about the profession of a director in the Chinese version of the final prospectus was different from that in the English version. This was an obvious inconsistency that should not be left unnoticed.

Mr Peter Au-Yang, SFC’s Executive Director of Corporate Finance, said:  “Sponsors are required to use all reasonable efforts to assist their clients in ensuring that public documents are prepared to the required standard and no relevant information has been omitted or withheld. This includes assistance in ensuring the accuracy of translations, which otherwise are liable to misrepresent important information to investors.  As SFC licensees, sponsors are expected to act professionally and diligently.”

The SFC issues this quarterly update as part of its efforts to ensure the transparency of the Dual Filing regime.
