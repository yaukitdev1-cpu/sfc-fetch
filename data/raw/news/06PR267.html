The SFC will kick off its investor education initiatives in the new year with a variety of activities in January, which will be designated as the Investor Education Month of 2007.

One of the highlights is a series of six seminars, hosted jointly by the SFC and the Li Ka Shing Institute of Professional and Continuing Education (LiPACE) of The Open University of Hong Kong (OUHK). The seminars will focus on the assessment of risks of investing. Speakers include prominent columnists, market commentators, practitioners and academics, such as Mr Cho Chi Ming, Ms Scarlett Pong, Ms Agnes Wu, Mr Patrick Wong, Mr Eric Wong and Professor Raymond So (Note 1).

Summaries of the seminars will be published in the Hong Kong Economic Journal in February 2007. Video excerpts of the seminars will be aired on the Business News Channel of now TV and on TVB Pearl as part of the OUHK’s Open for Learning programme in February and April 2007 respectively. More details will soon be announced at the “Education News” section on the SFC’s InvestEd website at www.InvestEd.hk.

The SFC has also produced a new TV drama series, which for the first time is adapted from real-life cases. The series will be screened on the Finance Channel of Cable TV in the week of 22 January 2007. Details will be announced at a launch ceremony next month.

Other on-going initiatives, such as Dr Wise’s Column, newspaper and magazine columns, workplace and school talks will continue to cover wide-ranging investment topics.

Mr Martin Wheatley, SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, said: “Knowledge is everyone’s best investment. By naming January as the Investor Education Month, we hope to draw investors’ attention to the importance of planning ahead. We look forward to their active participation in our activities during the month and for the rest of the year.”
