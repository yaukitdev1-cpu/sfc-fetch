The Court has sentenced {X} to suspended imprisonment for market manipulation.

On 17 June 2005, {X} was convicted after trial before Mr Anthony Yuen, a Magistrate at Eastern Magistracy, of intentionally creating a false or misleading appearance of active trading in the shares of EVI Education Asia Limited, contrary to sections 135 and 139 of the Securities Ordinance. Sentencing for {X} was adjourned to today (Note 1).

{X} was sentenced today to three months’ imprisonment for each of the two summonses to run concurrently but to be suspended for 18 months. {X} was ordered to pay investigation costs of $8,202 to the SFC.

An SFC investigation revealed that on 8 May 2002, {X}, with the intention of raising the market price of EVI shares, had bought and sold EVI shares via accounts held by him with two brokers and that such trades had led to no change in the beneficial ownership of the EVI shares.

In passing the suspended sentence, Mr Yuen said he had taken into account that {X} had already been held in custody for 17 days and he was remorseful.
