In response to media enquiries, the SFC publishes the following statistics on the transaction volume of brokers following the outbreak of the severe acute respiratory syndrome (SARS).

The SFC has analysed the SEHK transaction volume (Note 1) from March to May 2003 to assess the impact of SARS on the brokerage industry. We are very pleased to report that transaction volume has actually risen for all three participant categories.

There have been concerns that Category C participants may be badly affected. It is because their clients usually visit their offices in person to place orders and they are likely to have stopped this because of SARS. In fact, as liquidity improves, Category C participants also see an increase in transaction volume, in line with the market trend.

Alexa Lam, Executive Director of Intermediaries and Investment Products, said: "While we are happy to see how resilient the industry has been towards the SARS contingency, we are also aware of the compliance issues that may have arisen and will be flexible in addressing them. For instance, for brokers who, due to logistical constraints arising from taking necessary precautions against the disease, have not been able to comply in full with continuous training requirements, we will be happy to allow some flexibility."
