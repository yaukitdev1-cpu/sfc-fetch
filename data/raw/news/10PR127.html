The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announces that Mr Terry Pan San Kong becomes a member of the Investor Education Advisory Committee (IEAC) with immediate effect. He replaces Ms Lam Po Yee as representative of the Hong Kong Investment Funds Association who is stepping down from the committee on the same date (Note 1).

The SFC would like to thank Ms Lam for her contribution to the work of the IEAC.


End

1. Full membership list is available on the SFC website (www.sfc.hk).
