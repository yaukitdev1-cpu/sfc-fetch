The SFC today publishes the Summer 2004 issue (No. 56) of the SFC Quarterly Bulletin.

This issue of the SFC Quarterly Bulletin includes the SFC’s first Half-Yearly Financial Stability Report – the Securities Market and the Securities Industry.

The Half-Yearly Report analyses the financial performance of the securities industry in the first quarter of 2004.  It reveals that:

  • profitability of SEHK Participants has increased significantly from last year as a result of improved market conditions and effective cost control;
  • profitability of Category C SEHK Participants has continued to increase, with net profit showing a two-fold increase in the first quarter of 2004 compared to the last quarter of 2003; and
  • number of licensed representatives employed by Category C SEHK Participants has remained steady since 2003.


Since the preparation of this Quarterly Bulletin, the SFC has further analysed the financial performance of Category C SEHK Participants and will share the findings with the market shortly.

The Bulletin also contains an article entitled Profile of the Stock Options Market in Hong Kong, which analyses the major features of the stock options market.  This is the    second paper of a special research paper series which aims at providing information and analysis of exchange-traded securities and derivatives markets in Hong Kong.  The special research paper series is available on the SFC website at http: //www.hksfc.org.hk/eng/statistics/html/researches.htm .

Based on a fact-finding survey on corporate bond markets conducted by the IOSCO (International Organization of Securities Commissions), Transparency of Corporate Bond Markets finds that transparency of listed corporate bonds traded on exchanges is similar to that of other listed securities, and there is less transparency in the over-the-counter market.

Some commentators believe that IPOs which are highly over-subscribed and where the amounts of funds locked up are large may have a certain impact on market turnover.  The article Impact of IPOs on Market Turnover analyses the impact of IPOs on market turnover during the lockup period and the post-listing period.

Understanding Investors in the Hong Kong Listed Securities and Derivatives Markets discusses the profile and behaviour of stock and derivatives investors in Hong Kong.  This article is based on surveys conducted by HKEx, the SFC and the Hong Kong Investment Funds Association.

Fund Management Activities Survey 2003 summarises the findings of a survey on fund management activities in Hong Kong designed to enable a better understanding of the local fund management industry and to identify possible trends.

The Bulletin can be subscribed at $400 per year or downloaded from the SFC website under “Press Releases, Publications & Speeches” – “Publications”.  The public are welcome to sign up the SFC Website Update Email Alert service for the notification of new releases of the Bulletin.
