The SFC wishes to remind the market that the amendments to Schedule 5 to the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO) have come into effect today, following gazettal of the amendment notice on 11 November 2005, and discussion by the Legislative Council on 18 November 2005.

In brief, the amendments include: -

1) Extending the definition of “asset management” to include management of real estate investment trusts;

2) Amending the definition of “dealing in securities” to exclude dealings by approved money brokers where they represent authorised financial institutions; and

3) Amending the definitions of “advising on securities” and “advising on futures contracts” to exclude licensed asset managers where they give advice on their own funds.

For details of the amendments, please refer to the Consultation Conclusions on Proposed Amendments to Schedule 5 to the SFO available at the SFC office and on the SFC website under “Speeches, Publications & Consultations” – “Consultation Papers & Conclusions”.
