The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has published today a revised draft of the Guidelines on Disclosure of Inside Information, alongside the Government’s issuance of the Legislative Council Brief on the Securities and Futures (Amendment) Bill 2011 (Note 1).

The revised draft guidelines have reflected public comments the SFC received during the consultation (Note 2) and the relevant text of the bill. Once the final form of the bill is settled after its passage through the Legislative Council, the guidelines may need to be revised further. The final version of such guidelines then will be published in the Gazette and will take effect on the same day as the bill.

The revised draft guidelines provide guidance on the interpretation and application of the relevant provisions as set out in the bill. The guidelines are intended to assist listed corporations to comply with the proposed statutory obligations.

A clean and a marked-up copy of the revised draft guidelines are available on the SFC website at www.sfc.hk.

End

Notes:
1. A copy of the Legislative Council Brief (which contains the Securities and Futures (Amendment) Bill 2011) is available on the Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau’s website.
2. On 11 February 2011, the SFC issued a press release on the consultation conclusions on the draft Guidelines on disclosure of inside information.
