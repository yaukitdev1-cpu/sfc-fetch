The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X} for making unsolicited calls.

An SFC investigation found that in May and December 2004, {X}, a licensed representative of Hantec International Limited, had telephoned two persons who were not clients of Hantec. {X} had also offered to make agreements with the two persons with a view to inducing them to sell or purchase leveraged foreign exchange contracts at Hantec, contrary to section 174 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

{X} today pleaded guilty to two summonses before Mr Raymond Wong, a Magistrate at Eastern Magistracy. {X} was fined $10,000 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $12,144 to the SFC.
