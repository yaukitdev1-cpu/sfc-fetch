The Academic and Accreditation Advisory Committee (AAAC) of the SFC (Note 1) has decided to appoint the Hong Kong Securities Institute (HKSI) as an independent exam body to run the industry and local regulatory framework exams (Note 2), except those relating to leveraged foreign exchange trading.
 
The move is to maintain impartiality and even standard of exams, as well as to facilitate negotiation and development of mutual recognition of qualifications with both local and overseas counterparts.  
 
The leveraged foreign exchange trading exams at the moment are offered only by the Financial Services Development Centre of the Vocational Training Council (VTC).   These are stand-alone exams, pertaining to leveraged foreign exchange trading only. Recognising VTC’s experience and resources invested in the exams, the AAAC decided to allow the exams to continue.
 
The SFC is aware of the concerns raised by the industry as a result of the above arrangement, such as possible conflict of interest and high exam fees.  In this respect, both institutions are subject to fulfilment of the criteria determined by the AAAC for exam providers.  For example, in line with international best practice, the exam bodies will not run exam preparatory courses to avoid conflict of interest. They are required to be non-profit making and strive to keep the exam fees at a low level.  The AAAC may also withdraw the approval status with sufficient cause.
 
Meanwhile, the AAAC encouraged HKSI to work together with the Hong Kong Institute of Bankers in developing programmes/exams on financial planning with a view to helping intermediaries gain relevant skills and product knowledge to diversify their businesses and remain competitive.

The HKSI, which has been organising exam preparatory training courses solely or jointly with tertiary institutions, will wind down these preparatory training programmes by 31 August 2003. VTC currently does not provide preparatory training courses.

Tertiary institutions or professional bodies with track records in providing industry training should approach SFC’s Licensing Department if they are interested in providing the preparatory training programmes.

Ends

Notes:

1. The AAAC was established under section 6 of the Securities and Futures Commission Ordinance to advise the SFC on any policy matters regarding competence and continuous professional training requirements for the industry.  Members include industry representatives and academics.

2. In April 2001, the SFC introduced the competence requirements with the objective of ensuring that licensed persons are adequately equipped to carry out their functions and are aware of their legal responsibilities.  Applicants seeking to be licensed by the SFC may demonstrate competence by obtaining certain recognised industry qualification and by passing a local regulatory framework examination.

3. Locally administered exams that are recognised by AAAC include:

HKSI   -          

  • HKSI Foundation Programme Exam
  • HKSI Financial Market Principal Programme Exam
  • HKSI Diploma Programme Exam
  • Licensing Exam for Securities and Futures Intermediaries (scheduled to be launched in June 2003)


VTC    -          

  • Leveraged Foreign Exchange Trader’s Representative Exam
  • Leveraged Foreign Exchange Trader’s Responsible Director Exam
