A retail investor was today sentenced by the Eastern Magistracy to 160 hours of community services for manipulating the price of four derivative warrants.

The court also ordered {X} to pay investigation costs of $16,570 to the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC).

The court heard that {X} manipulated the price of the derivative warrants on Industrial and Commercial Bank of China Ltd, China Mobile Ltd, China Huiyuan Juice Group Ltd and Bank of China Ltd during the continuous trading session on nine trading days between 11 June and 29 July 2008 (Note 1).

{X} had earlier pleaded guilty to all nine charges.

End

Note:

1. The four derivative warrants involved were: KC-ICBC@EC0807A, RB-CMOB@EC0810B, HS-HYJG@EC0811A and UB-BOCL@EC0808B. For details, please see SFC press release dated 5 January 2010.
