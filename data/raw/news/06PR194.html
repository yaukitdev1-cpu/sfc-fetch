Profits of stock exchange participant brokers in the first half of 2006 increased, a review in latest issue of the SFC Quarterly Bulletin shows.

Profits of stock exchange participant brokers in the first half of 2006 amounted to approximately $7.4 billion, compared to $3.6 billion in the preceding 6 months. The total reported net profits of Category A, B and C brokers were 116%, 78% and 155% respectively higher than those for the second half of 2005.

The Half-Yearly Financial Review of the Securities Industry is one of the feature articles in the Summer 2006 issue (No.64) of the SFC Quarterly Bulletin, which is published today.

Another article, Review of the Securities Market, reviews the performance of major overseas markets during the second quarter of 2006. Underpinned by continuous capital inflow and bullish market sentiment, the HSI and HSCEI rose to a six-year high and an 8.5-year high respectively in early May. The indices retreated thereafter amid heightened rate hike concerns and corrections in commodities prices. The HSI and HSCEI closed 2.9% and 1.2% higher respectively during the quarter.
