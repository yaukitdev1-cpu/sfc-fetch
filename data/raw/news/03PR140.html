The Asian Roundtable on Corporate Governance, which was organised by the OECD in co-operation with the World Bank and regional partners, presented its White Paper on Corporate Governance in Asia in Tokyo, Japan today.

The Roundtable comprises international and Asian senior policy makers, regulators, and representatives from stock exchanges, private sector bodies, multilateral organisations and non-governmental institutions. The White Paper is a non-binding document reflecting the discussions and recommendations of Roundtable meetings that have taken place throughout Asia from 1999 to 2003.

The Companies Registry, the Hong Kong Society of Accountants (HKSA), Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited (HKEx) and the SFC were active participants at the Roundtable meetings. The HKSA, HKEx and the SFC hosted the second Roundtable on the Role of Disclosure in Strengthening Corporate Governance and Accountability in the summer of 2000.

The White Paper builds on the OECD Principles of Corporate Governance taking into account local issues faced in Asia. It focuses primarily on listed companies although it may serve as useful reference for governance of private companies and state owned enterprises. The White Paper represents a homegrown response to corporate governance issues in Asia utilising the general structure of the OECD Principles. In so doing, the White Paper reflects both the importance of coherence and convergence in international corporate governance standards and the need to reflect local conditions.

The key recommendations in the White Paper include: full convergence with international accounting and auditing standards, better protection of minority shareholders, stronger implementation and enforcement of laws and regulations and improved bank governance.

Mr Ashley Alder, SFC’s Executive Director of Corporate Finance, said: "The SFC welcomes the publication of the White Paper that provides an Asian perspective on corporate governance issues and suggests various strategies to address common issues. We look forward to continued collaboration with OECD and our colleagues in Asia as the Roundtable focuses on implementation issues."

An electronic copy of the White Paper on Corporate Governance in Asia is available for free at www.oecd.org/corporate.
