The latest Dr Wise’s Column discusses various ways that investors can find information about listed companies, in particular Main Board companies, under the new arrangement for corporate announcements.

The SFC today publishes the June issue of Dr Wise’s Column, “Finding Listed Company Information”, on its InvestEd website at www.InvestEd.hk.

Starting yesterday, instead of publishing announcements in newspapers, Main Board companies need only post their announcements on the HKEx website and eventually on their own websites.

Investors can keep updated on listed company information using free investor services provided by the HKEx Investment Service Centre at www.hkex.com.hk. They can also search and retrieve current and historical regulatory information regarding any listed company on the HKEx website. Full details of announcements are also available on the HKEx website.

Dr Wise is a fictitious character. Through his column, he talks to investors about the key issues of investing and regulatory thoughts.

Dr Wise’s Column is published on the last working Tuesday of every month. From time to time, additional articles in the form of special issues may be published on topical matters. The column is posted under the “Features & Dr Wise” section of the InvestEd website. Investors are welcome to send their feedback and questions to Dr Wise by email to InvestEd@sfc.hk.
