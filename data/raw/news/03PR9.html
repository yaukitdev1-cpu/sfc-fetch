In view of the impending target commencement of the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO) in April this year, the SFC issues a circular today to all registrants, exempt persons and licensed banks who are not exempt persons to explain certain key issues concerning their licences or registrations under the SFO. 

Issues discussed in the circular include the arrangements for migration to the new regime and clarification of certain licensing conditions under the SFO.  It also informs the intermediaries that some simplified migration forms have been designed for existing registrants or exempt persons to apply for their new licence or registration.

An SFC spokesman said: “The migration process is designed to be simple and easy. They are only required to provide certain essential information in the forms. In addition, to assist with their smooth transition to the new regime, the SFC will shortly be issuing the Guidelines on Transitional Arrangements in this regard.”

In the circular, the SFC also asks intermediaries to provide certain basic information for the purpose of preparing for the implementation of the new licensing regime under the SFO. 

A copy of this circular has been sent to all registrants and exempt persons through FinNet.  Licensed banks who are not exempt persons will receive this circular by post.
