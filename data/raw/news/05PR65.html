The SFC has been authorising UCITS III funds. To date, 468 UCITS III fund applications have been received and the SFC has authorised 353 UCITS III funds.

The SFC has formulated interim measures with the fund industry which include a fast track procedure to assist the processing of UCITS III fund applications that meet with all the necessary requirements and do not intend to have significant adjustments in their investment policies. The interim measures focus on the disclosure of the key features and associated risks of the relevant UCITS III funds.

The SFC understands that the market is always interested to be informed of the potential impact the new UCITS III Directives may have on the fund industry in Hong Kong. Hence, the SFC will update the market regularly on the UCITS III conversion progress in Hong Kong.
