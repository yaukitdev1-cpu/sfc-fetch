The SFC today publishes the October issue of SFC Alert, a bi-monthly newsletter for both investors and intermediaries.

Better regulation on margin financing reports on the SFC consultation on proposals to reduce the risks arising from securities margin financing activities. The public are invited to submit their views by 31 October. 

IOSCO consults on CRAs introduces the consultation by the International Organization of Securities Commissions on Code of Conduct Fundamentals for Credit Rating Agencies (CRAs).

Inside, in the regular Senior management interview, Mr Eric Cheng, Senior Director of Surveillance, Enforcement, talks about how the SFC monitors the market for unusual price or volume movements in stocks and derivatives. 

In September, we successfully prosecuted a listed company and its director for providing false or misleading information. First prosecution under Dual Filing provides more details.

Disciplinary process explained notes that the SFC has issued a pamphlet to explain in plain language its disciplinary process and describes the procedural rights of persons who are subject to disciplinary proceedings.

Robust regulation for hedge funds reiterates that hedge fund managers wishing to operate in Hong Kong must meet the same high regulatory standards expected of SFC licensed persons.

Another regular column, Investor Focus, explains the pros and cons in using the services of a  nominee company and reminds investors to consider carefully if such services are suitable for them.

The October issue of SFC Alert is the 26th issue of the newsletter, which was first published in October 2001. Copies of the current issue of SFC Alert are available at the SFC office. All issues are available on the SFC website.
