The SFC announces today the gazettal of the Companies Ordinance (Exemption of Companies and Prospectuses From Compliance with Provisions) (Amendment) Notice 2003. The Notice has been tabled before the Legislative Council and is subject to negative vetting.

The Notice is aimed at issuers of prospectuses relating to debentures (i.e. bonds, notes and other instruments creating or acknowledging a debt) and seeks to reduce their obligation to include in the prospectus specified information that the SFC considers to be irrelevant to investors in debentures and/or unduly burdensome for issuers of prospectuses. The SFC believes that this will help issuers to make their prospectuses more relevant and comprehensible to investors without compromising investor protection. This initiative forms part of a series of ongoing reforms to the legal and regulatory framework applying to offers of shares and debentures, which began with the gazettal on 21 February 2003 of three SFC guidelines to facilitate offers of shares and debentures.

The Notice was issued by the SFC on 24 March 2003 pursuant to its powers under sections 38A(2) and 342A(2) of the Companies Ordinance (CO) to exempt classes of prospectuses from compliance with those requirements in the Third Schedule to the CO which the SFC considers are unduly burdensome or irrelevant.

The Notice contains the following two new class exemptions:

1. All prospectuses relating to offers of debentures which are to be listed on the Main Board or Growth Enterprise Market (GEM) of The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited will be exempted from (i) those contents requirements in the Third Schedule to the CO that are the same as or similar to the content requirements under the Listing Rules applicable to debt securities (provided no waiver, modification or other dispensation has been granted from such requirements), and (ii) those contents requirements that the SFC considers irrelevant for the purposes of an investor in debentures making an informed investment decision and/or unduly burdensome for companies to comply with; and

2. All prospectuses relating to offers of debentures not proposed to be listed on the Main Board or GEM will be exempted in respect of certain contents requirements in the Third Schedule to the CO that the SFC considers irrelevant for the purposes of an investor in debentures making an informed investment decision and/or unduly burdensome for companies to comply with.

A summary of the exempted requirements is set out in the Appendix.

The SFC’s powers to exempt may be exercised either on a case-by-case basis pursuant to sections 38A(1) or 342A(1), or by way of a class exemption pursuant to sections 38A(2) and 342A(2) of the CO.

In recent years, in the context of offers of debentures (whether or not they are proposed to be listed), the SFC has granted on a case-by-case basis many of the exemptions described in items 1(ii) and 2 above on the grounds that either the information was irrelevant for the purposes of an investor in debentures making an informed investment decision or that the provision of the information was unduly burdensome to the issuer of the prospectus. Following a recent detailed review of the content requirements in the Third Schedule to the CO in the context of offers of debentures specifically, the SFC has concluded that a small number of additional requirements are irrelevant to investors and/or unduly burdensome for the company proposing to issue the debentures.

In the context of offers of debentures proposed to be listed, the SFC has concluded that it is unduly burdensome for companies to comply with two sets of the same or similar requirements (one in the CO and the other in the relevant Listing Rules) where they relate to the same subject matter. The SFC has therefore also exercised its exemption powers under sections 38A and 342A to issue the class exemption mentioned in item 1(i) above.

The Notice also introduces a new requirement that a prospectus shall include a statement identifying any one or more class exemptions in the Companies Ordinance (Exemption of Companies and Prospectuses From Compliance with Provisions) Notice that the issuer of the prospectus has relied upon. This information is designed to increase transparency as to compliance of the prospectus with the statutory content requirements.

Once the Notice becomes effective, an application to the SFC for an exemption from any or all of the requirements set out in the class exemptions will not be required for prospectuses that meet the conditions specified. The SFC is satisfied that this initiative will facilitate offers of debentures without compromising investor protection.

The gazetted Notice is available in Legal Supplement No. 2 to the Government Gazette as L.N. No. 85 of 2003 and can be viewed on the Government’s website at *www.info.gov.hk/pd/egazette/*. The Notice has been tabled before the Legislative Council and, subject to negative vetting, will take effect on 23 May 2003. The SFC will inform the market of any further development.
