The SFC today publishes the Consultation Conclusions on the Proposed Revisions to the Guidelines for Registered Persons Using the Internet to Collect Applications for Securities in an Initial Public Offering.

The Guidelines will be renamed as the Guidelines for Electronic Public Offerings and will be published in the Gazette under section 399 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance on 11 April 2003. 

The original guidelines were published by the SFC in July 2000 and set out the responsibilities of a person registered with the SFC who uses the Internet to collect applications from its clients in an initial public offering.

On 28 January 2003, the SFC published a consultation paper proposing  revisions which would provide guidance on other forms of electronic public offerings that have evolved in the past few years.

The SFC received four written responses.  Taking into account the comments received, several amendments were considered appropriate. The main amendment is a clarification on obtaining an issuer’s prior consent for hyperlinking to the electronic public offer documents on the issuer’s website. Drafting amendments were also made for clarification of the Guidelines. The Consultation Conclusions paper released today provides an analysis of the submissions and explains the Commission’s position.

Copies of the [Consultation Conclusions](/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/consultation/conclusion?refNo=03CP3) are available at the SFC office and on the SFC website.
