The SFC today publishes the August issue of *SFC Enforcement Reporter*, summarising the SFC’s enforcement action in the month of July 2003 and discussing the compliance issues highlighted by recent cases.

In July, the SFC successfully prosecuted nine persons for breaking securities laws – a record monthly figure so far in 2003-2004.

Another market manipulator was given suspended prison sentence – making him the fifth manipulator that the SFC has successfully prosecuted since April this year. The other prosecutions involved lying to the SFC, non-compliance with the Financial Resources Rules, illegal short selling, and disclosure of interests failures.

A broker and two of its directors were reprimanded for their roles in the transfer of shares in a stock which was suspended on the stock exchange. The SFC notes that dealing in suspended shares may jeopardise market integrity by, for example, possibly facilitating insider dealing.

Since April 2003, the SFC has successfully prosecuted 25 entities and disciplined 27 licensees for various regulatory breaches.
