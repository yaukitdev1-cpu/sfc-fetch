The SFC today publishes a guidance note to explain when interests in listed companies need to be disclosed under the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO).

The Outline of Part XV of the SFO gives extensive guidance on the situations in which a Disclosure of Interests (DI) notice will have to be filed under Part XV and related matters.

The SFC also announces that three items are published in the Gazette under Part XV. These are:

1. Forms prescribed for use under Part XV of the SFO;
2. Guidelines for the approval of corporations as approved lending agents; and
3. Recognised Places Notice.

The new regime for disclosure of interests in Part XV of the SFO will commence on 1 April 2003. Part XV seeks to modernise the regulatory regime on the disclosure of interests in securities, and provide investors with more complete, timely and better quality information so they can make informed investment decisions.

Part XV requires shareholders who have an interest in 5% or more of the shares of a listed corporation (substantial shareholders) to give notice of interests in voting shares of listed corporations in certain specified circumstances. It also requires a director or a chief executive, who is interested in the shares or debentures of a listed corporation (of which he is a director or chief executive) or any of its associated corporations, to give notice in certain specified circumstances.

*Outline of Part XV*

The 49-page Outline of Part XV explains the obligations of substantial shareholders and directors under Part XV, the time allowed for filing notices and the forms that must be used. Among other issues, the Outline explains that an initial notification (e.g. for those who are currently holding between 5% and the existing threshold of 10%) can be made within 10 business days of commencement of the SFO on 1 April, i.e. by 14 April, as opposed to the period of three business days normally allowed.

The Outline is indexed and adopts a Q&A style so as to be more user friendly. It also comments on related matters such as the extraterritorial effect of Part XV, offences, investigations by listed corporations, and registers of interests and short positions of substantial shareholders and directors.

The Outline is available on the SFC website under the SFO section.

*Prescribed forms*

Part XV requires that substantial shareholders and directors use forms specified by the SFC when giving notice of their interests. Two forms have been prepared for disclosures by all substantial shareholders, other than those who are directors of the listed corporation concerned, and four forms have been specified for use by directors. Each form has extensive notes containing directions and instructions on the completion of the form.

The forms have been prepared in two different formats. They can be downloaded in pdf format for printing out and completion in manuscript. Alternatively they can be downloaded in Microsoft Excel format for completion offline using the Excel program. Once a form has been completed using the Excel program it can be filed electronically with Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited (HKEx). Forms completed manually can be filed with HKEx by hand, by Post or by fax in the same way as at present under the Securities (Disclosure of Interests) Ordinance.

Part XV also requires every listed corporation to keep a register of the interests and short positions disclosed to them by substantial shareholders and a separate register for directors’ interests. Listed corporations have to send notice of the place where the registers are kept if they are not kept at the registered office. The SFC has specified forms of notice for this purpose.

The forms and the explanatory notes, in Chinese and in English, are now available under the SFO section of the SFC website. The DI forms will also shortly be available on a new site specially established by HKEx at [http://sdinotice.hkex.com.hk](http://sdinotice.hkex.com.hk). There will also be a hyperlink from the main HKEx website ([http://www.hkex.com.hk](http://www.hkex.com.hk)) to the new site for downloading DI forms and e-filing.

*Guidelines for the approval of corporations as approved lending agents*

The Securities and Futures (Disclosure of Interests – Securities Borrowing and Lending) Rules (SBL Rules) establish a simplified disclosure regime that will limit the disclosure obligations of certain classes of participants in the stock borrowing and lending market without undermining the integrity of the disclosure regime or compromising investor protection.

If certain conditions are satisfied, the SBL Rules provide:

(a) an exemption for substantial shareholders (who lend shares using the services of an approved lending agent);
(b) a simplified disclosure regime for approved lending agents, and their holding companies; and
(c) a disregard of the interests of regulated persons in shares borrowed and lent.

The Guidelines gazetted today set out the criteria that the SFC will take into account when deciding whether to grant an approval to a corporation to become an approved lending agent under the SBL Rules.

*Recognised Places Notice*

In the SFO and SBL Rules there are a number of exemptions for corporations or schemes that are regulated or established in a place outside Hong Kong. The Recognised Places Notice lists the 11 countries that have been recognised by the SFC for the purposes of these provisions of the SFO and the SBL Rules.

Copies of all the documents are available under the SFO section of the SFC website and the FinNet communication network.
