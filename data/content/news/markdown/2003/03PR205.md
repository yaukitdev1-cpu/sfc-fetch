The SFC today publishes for public consultation the Securities and Futures (Contracts Limits and Reportable Positions) (Amendment) Rules 2003 (the Amendment Rules) and proposed revisions to the Guidance Note on Position Limits and Large Open Position Reporting Requirements (the Guidance Note).

The Securities and Futures (Contracts Limits and Reportable Positions) Rules (the Rules) prescribe limits (position limits) on the number of futures contracts or options contracts that may be held or controlled by a person and require a person holding or controlling a reportable position to notify the recognised exchange company. Under section 7 of the Rules, Exchange Participants are permitted to apply the limits separately to their own accounts and to each of their client’s positions. But intermediaries who are not Exchange Participants are required to aggregate their own positions and all their clients’ positions in applying the prescribed limits.

During the public consultation of the Rules in 2002, industry participants commented that section 7 of the Rules creates an unlevel playing field and practical difficulties for non-Exchange Participants. The SFC undertook to review the provision after the commencement of the Rules.

To address market participants’ concerns, the Amendment Rules propose to extend the application of section 7 by allowing any person who holds or controls futures contracts or stock options contracts for other persons to apply the reporting requirements and the prescribed limits separately to his own account and to the positions of each of the other persons except where the person has discretion in respect of the futures or stock options contracts which he holds for other persons.

The Guidance Note which was issued in March 2003 is also revised to reflect the Amendment Rules. The SFC proposes to issue the revised Guidance Note under section 399 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.
