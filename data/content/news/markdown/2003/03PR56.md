The SFC publishes today new licensing application and notification forms to be used under the Securities and Futures Ordinance when it commences.

In drafting the new licensing forms, the SFC has strived to ensure that they are simple and their completion is not unnecessarily burdensome. Under the existing regime, there are numerous sets of forms for the various governing Ordinances. They have now been consolidated into one set for all the nine types of regulated activities (Note 1).

To ensure their clarity and user-friendliness, the forms have been drafted in consultation with a working group comprising industry practitioners. The SFC has also consulted various other parties, including broker associations and banks on the forms.

Further, to make it easier for existing licensees and exempt persons to transit to the new regime, the SFC has also drafted a set of abridged migration forms. These are simplified versions of the licensing forms and only request information which the SFC does not currently have.

New approaches which are welcomed by the industry have also been incorporated into the new forms. They include:


- Dispensing with the need by representatives to provide supporting documents such as copies of education certificates or employer’s reference letters. Instead, the applicants’ principals will be requested to endorse the relevant applications and the information stated in the application forms.
- Dispensing with the need by corporations to provide supporting documents such as copies of business registration certificates, board resolutions approving the applications and memorandum and articles of association.
- Simplifying the Annual Return to make it a "no change declaration" form. It no longer requests for information on the compliance with the continuous professional training requirement. Records of such training programmes will still have to be kept and be subject to inspection by Commission staff.

The forms (in English and Chinese) are available on the SFC website (www.hksfc.org.hk) under the section "Securities and Futures Ordinance – Forms Specified by the Commission". The forms will be gazetted this month.
