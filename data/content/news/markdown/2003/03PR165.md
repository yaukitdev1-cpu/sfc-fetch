The SFC issues the following update on the operation of the dual filing arrangements under the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

As the SFC reported in May, it had reviewed 27 new applications for listing on the stock exchange up to the end of April 2003 and had commented on 10 of them. Four of the cases had apparent fundamental problems. (Note 1)

Of the 17 applications that the SFC did not comment on, three have become listed.

Of the 10 applications that the SFC commented on, two have withdrawn or suspended their applications, two have not made any response, and one has made a response that did not address the comments thus the review process has been discontinued. Two of these five cases were initially identified as having apparent fundamental problems.

Of the other five cases, three have gone through two further rounds of comments, and two have gone through one further round. The average response time by applicants and their sponsors was 24 working days. The SFC’s average response time in giving second/third round comments was nine working days.

In May and June, the SFC received (via the Exchange) 15 new listing applications and commented on six, of which three had fundamental problems. The SFC’s average response time was six working days. The main issues are similar to those reported in May.
