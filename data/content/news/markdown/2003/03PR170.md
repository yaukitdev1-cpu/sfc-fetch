The SFC today publishes new performance pledges for licensing applications and will endeavour to handle applications more quickly and meet the pledges.

There are new pledges as well as improvements to existing onespledges. These are:




Acknowledging applications
- 2 business days (after receipt of applications)
Processing of applications:

 Representatives (provisional licence)
- 7 business days
 Representatives (normal licence)
- 8 weeks
 Representatives (responsible officer)
- 10 weeks
 Corporations
- 15 weeks
 Change of accreditation
- 7 business days


The performance pledges for provisional licence and change of accreditation are new, whereas those for normal representative licence and responsible officer (formerly dealing director and supervisory director) licence have been substantially reduced from 10 weeks and 15 weeks respectively.

The SFC is confident in handling applications faster owing to its streamlined procedures and an enhanced computer system. For example, applicants are no longer required to submit certain supporting documents such as educational certificates or employer references, and no duplicative information will be requested. The new arrangements have also enabled the change of accreditation to be processed within a shorter period.

Ms Lucinda Wong, SFC’s Director of Licensing, said: "In making these pledges, the Intermediaries and Investment Products Division takes into account the need to process an application promptly while observing the due process. Staff must ensure the fitness and properness of the applicant is carefully reviewed internally and vetted with the criminal investigatory or other relevant regulatory authorities, before making a decision."

The SFC proposes the new pledges as a service improvement despite its overwhelming workload. With the implementation of the SFO, the SFC is handling the migration of about 27,000 existing licenceslicensees to the new licensing regime using only existing resources.

It should also be noted that the SFC might not be able to meet the pledges in cases where the applicant submits incomplete documentation or in contentious cases. The latter may include those requiring the imposition of licensing conditions or where there is fitness and properness concern.

The performance pledges will be reviewed for further improvements after the two-year transitional period for the new licensing regime.

As in the past, the SFC will publish its performance statistics against the benchmarks in its annual report.

The performance pledges are published on the SFC website under the "About the SFC" section.
