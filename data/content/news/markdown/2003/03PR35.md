The SFC releases today the consultation conclusions on the proposed revisions to the Code of Conduct.

The SFC issued a consultation paper in November 2002. It had received 15 submissions from leveraged foreign exchange traders, local and international brokerage firms, legal firms, industry representative bodies and professional associations etc by the end of the one-month consultation period on 21 December 2002.

The submissions were carefully considered. Respondents in general acknowledged the need for the review.

Several changes to the original proposals have been made in light of the comments. The consultation conclusions document provides an analysis of the submissions and explains SFC’s views.

The Code as revised is to be made under section 169 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO). Overall, only minimal changes are introduced as the main purposes of this revision are to rationalise the Code with the SFO and its rules, and to have a uniform code of conduct applicable to all intermediaries and their representatives.

The revised Code will become effective on 1 April 2003, the target date for implementation of the SFO. The revised Code will be gazetted in March 2003.

Copies of the Consultation Conclusions are available at the SFC office, on the SFC website ([www.hksfc.org.hk](/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/consultation/conclusion?refNo=02CP27)) and the FinNet communication network.
