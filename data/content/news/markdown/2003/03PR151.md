The SFC has recently received a number of complaints over the performance of exotic warrants and considers it appropriate to issue a general investor advice.

The term "exotic warrant" describes a special class of derivative warrants with exotic features that are different from standard call or put warrants. Their names displayed on the Teletext carry a symbol "X" for easy identification.

Most of the complaints we received seem to have arisen from a lack of understanding of the product features, and the risk and return profile of exotic warrants. Investors are advised to read the listing documents of exotic warrants and understand the terms and conditions. This is to ensure that they are aware of each issue’s unique features and understand how it works before making an investment decision.

To help investors learn more about exotic warrants, the SFC has posted a new article on *Exotic Warrants* on its Electronic Investor Resources Centre (eIRC) at www.hkeirc.org. It explains:

- the features of some exotic warrants, such as capped warrants, locked-in return warrants, digital warrants and dividend accumulator warrants;
- the importance for investors to understand the pros and cons of an exotic warrant and the differences between an exotic warrant and a standard warrant; and
- the need to carefully use and interpret commonly used analytical tools, such as premium and gearing.

The SFC has previously published introductory articles on the characteristics of derivative warrants and the commonly used terms, the role of liquidity providers, and on how to choose and trade derivative warrants. All articles are available on the eIRC.

The SFC’s Investor Hotline at 2840 9333 remains open to provide educational information on derivative warrants, including exotic warrants.
