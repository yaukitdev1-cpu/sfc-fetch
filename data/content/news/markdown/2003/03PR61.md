The SFC issues this advice to investors in view of recent advertisements by securities dealers offering new brokerage commission rates and reward schemes to be introduced with effect from 1 April 2003.

While the SFC welcomes the offering by dealers of competitive commission rates, charges and reward schemes, it encourages investors to carefully ascertain the details of any such promotional offers by a dealer before trading through the dealer. In particular, investors must exercise caution to ensure that all relevant features of the promotional offer are disclosed in full, including the method of calculation, the period for which such promotional offer will be available, any minimum threshold of charges, any eligibility conditions or restrictions, and whether there are other hidden charges.

Should any dealer be found to have issued an advertisement that is false, disparaging, misleading or deceptive, the SFC will immediately undertake a review of that dealer's fitness and properness to remain registered and take appropriate disciplinary action.

Investors should notify the SFC on 2840 9333 if they feel that they may have been misled by dealers regarding new commission rates and charge or reward schemes.
