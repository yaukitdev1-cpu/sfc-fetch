The SFC today publishes the Summer 2003 issue (No. 52) of the *SFC Quarterly Bulletin*.

The feature article of this issue, *Asia: A Locomotive of the World Economy*, is a speech delivered by SFC Chairman, Mr Andrew Sheng at the German Economic Council National Conference.  Mr Sheng discussed the four key challenges to the sustained growth in Asia, namely demographics, governance, institutions and environment.

This issue also includes an article entitled *Performance Overview of Futures Brokers in 2001 and 2002*.  It provides a profile of the HKFE Participants and the recent trend of the futures brokerage industry.  The second article *Six-year Comparison of Business Activities Surveys on Commodity Dealers* discusses the major trends of commodities dealers’ (including HKFE and non-HKFE Participants) business activities.  This is based on the Business Activities Surveys conducted by the SFC during 1996-2001.  The third article *Analysis of Transaction Size Distribution on HKEx* gives an account on the size of stock trades on the Hong Kong stock market during 1999-2002. 

The market update, *Analysis of the Recent Rebound in Market Turnover*, summarises the attributing factors to the recent increase in turnover.  This article is primarily drawn from an SFC research paper of the same title, which is obtainable from the SFC website at http://www.hksfc.org.hk/eng/statistics/html/researches.htm.
