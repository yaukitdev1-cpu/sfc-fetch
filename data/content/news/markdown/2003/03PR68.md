The SFC today publishes the Consultation Conclusions on the Guidance Note on Position Limits and Large Open Position Reporting Requirements.  The Guidance Note will be published in the Gazette under section 399 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO) on 28 March 2003.  

The Securities and Futures (Contracts Limits and Reportable Positions) Rules, made under section 35(1) of the SFO, prescribe limits on the number of futures contracts or options contracts that may be held or controlled by a person and require a person holding or controlling a reportable position to notify the recognised exchange company.  The Rules will come into operation on 1 April 2003.

To help market participants better understand how the Rules are intended to operate in practice, the Guidance Note is published to explain compliance requirements of the Rules and clarify issues raised by the industry.  Since the Rules allow person authorised by the SFC or the recognised exchange company to hold or control positions in excess of the prescribed limits, the Guidance Note explains the application procedures for a person wishing to hold or control excess positions and the procedures for appealing against an SFC decision under the Rules.

The SFC received five responses to the consultation on the draft Guidance Note.  Taking into account the comments received, several amendments were considered appropriate. The Consultation Conclusions paper released today by the SFC provides an analysis of the submissions and explains the Commission’s position.

[Copies](/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/consultation/conclusion?refNo=03CP2) of the Consultation Conclusions are available at the SFC office and on the SFC website.
