2. Commencement of Part XV

The new regime for disclosure of interests in Part XV of the SFO will commence on 1 April 2003. Part XV seeks to modernise the regulatory regime on the disclosure of interests in securities, and provide investors with more complete, timely and better quality information so they can make informed investment decisions.

Part XV requires shareholders who have an interest in 5% or more of the shares of a listed corporation (substantial shareholders) to give notice of interests in voting shares of listed corporations in certain specified circumstances. It also requires a director or a chief executive, who is interested in the shares or debentures of a listed corporation (of which he is a director or chief executive) or any of its associated corporations, to give notice in certain specified circumstances.

3. Prescribed forms

Part XV requires that substantial shareholders and directors use forms specified by the SFC when giving notice of their interests. Two forms have been prepared for disclosures by all substantial shareholders, other than those who are directors of the listed corporation concerned, and four forms have been specified for use by directors. Each form has extensive notes containing directions and instructions on the completion of the form.

The forms have been prepared in two different formats. They can be downloaded in pdf format for printing out and completion in manuscript. Alternatively they can be downloaded in Microsoft Excel format for completion offline using the Excel program. Once a form has been completed using the Excel program it can be filed electronically with Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited (HKEx). Forms completed manually can be filed with HKEx by hand, by Post or by fax in the same way as at present under the Securities (Disclosure of Interests) Ordinance.
