The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X} a substantial shareholder of GreaterChina Technology Group Limited (GreaterChina), under the Securities (Disclosure of Interests) Ordinance.

{X} pleaded guilty to one summons relating to his failure to report to Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited within five business days from his disposal of 4,000,000 GreaterChina shares on 6 April 2000.  {X} did not notify HKEx of the disposal until 24 December 2001, a delay of more than 20 months.

Ms Polly Lo, a Magistrate at Western Magistracy, fined {X} $5,000, and order him to pay costs of $9,210 to the SFC.

An SFC spokesman reminds investors to check their shareholdings against the issued share capital of the stocks they hold to ensure that once their shareholding reaches the notification threshold they notify both HKEx and the listed company within the required time.  Similarly, such duty also arises whenever there is a whole figure change in the percentage level of the shareholding.  Investors are recommended to seek professional advice on their disclosure requirements.
