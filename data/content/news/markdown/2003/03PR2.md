The SFC today publishes the January issue of [*SFC Enforcement Reporter*](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/enforcement/jan_03.pdf), summarising the Commission’s enforcement actions in the month of December 2002 and discussing the compliance issues highlighted by those recent cases.
 
In December, the SFC successfully prosecuted two company directors for failing to disclose their dealings in their companies’ stocks, hence breaching the Securities (Disclosure of Interests) Ordinance.
 
During the same period, the SFC disciplined five intermediaries for various misconduct, including breaches of licensing condition, failings in the operation of discretionary accounts and violation of the Financial Resources Rules.
 
Since 1 April 2002, the SFC has successfully prosecuted 29 people and disciplined 57 entities.
 
The January issue of [*SFC Enforcement Reporter*](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/enforcement/jan_03.pdf) is the 3rd issue of the newsletter, copies of which are available at the SFC's office. This issue and all the previous issues are also available on the Commission's website at www.hksfc.org.hk, under the section “Press Releases, Publications & Speeches – Publications”.
 
The public are welcome to subscribe to the newsletter by registering for the SFC Website Update Email Alert service on the Commission’s homepage. They will then be able to receive the newsletter via email.
