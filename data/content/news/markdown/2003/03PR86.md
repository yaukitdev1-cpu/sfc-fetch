The SFC today issues a circular requesting licensed intermediaries to ensure that their contingency plans include measures specifically addressing issues that may arise from possible staff infection of atypical pneumonia.

The Commission takes a serious view of the potential disruption to intermediaries' operations resulting from their staff members' infection of atypical pneumonia. As part of the contingency plans for business operations, intermediaries are requested to ensure that the following measures are included:

1. Notify the SFC promptly if any their staff member is either suspected of having atypical pneumonia symptoms or has been diagnosed as having an atypical pneumonia infection; and

2. Devise policies and procedures to ensure business continuity or prevent substantial disruption to the operation of the firm in the event that any staff member is infected. These procedures may include (but are not limited to):


- arranging for a (reciprocal) back-up office at unaffected premises;
- arranging for certain staff members to work at compatible remote premises where feasible;
- arranging with counterparts or exchanges on contingency closing out of positions, clearing and settlement matters;
- encouraging clients to use telephone or online facilities in place of physical branch presence; and
- notifying clients of suspected staff infection and advising clients on the relevant contingency measures.

Intermediaries have been requested to remind their staff members to take the precautionary measures for the prevention of respiratory tract infections, as advised by the Department of Health. They are also requested to consider other measures such as permitting staff members to wear clothes that are easily washable and cleaning their office premises more frequently to ensure a healthier working environment.
