The SFC today publishes the October issue of *SFC Alert*, a bi-monthly newsletter for both investors and intermediaries.

The lead story, *HK to host IOSCO Annual Conference 2006,* reports on the selection of Hong Kong by the International Organization of Securities Commissions (IOSCO) as the host of its 31st Annual Conference in 2006. It also notes the appointment of SFC Chairman, Mr Andrew Sheng, as Interim Chairman of the IOSCO Technical Committee.

In September, the IOSCO released a Statement of Principles and a set of Core Measures to address conflicts of interest of sell-side securities analysts. The SFC will consider these and other factors when deciding how to regulate the sector. *SFC to develop regulatory approach on analysts* has more details.

Inside, *More boiler room tricks* warns investors of new boiler room scams in the United States. Some fraudsters claimed that their investment products had been authorised by an official agency, and invited investors to check the authenticity. However, investors were given phone numbers which would only reach the fraudsters, who would have no problem confirming the "authenticity".

Some brokers have reportedly allowed their cash clients to delay the payments for stock purchases for prolonged periods. *Prudent settlement urged for brokerages* reminds brokerages and investors of the risks that may be involved.

*Application for re-entry into the industry by persons with disciplinary records* discusses the factors the SFC would consider in processing applications for licences submitted by people with disciplinary records.

The regulator Investor Focus column highlights the pooling risk of margin accounts.

The [October issue of *SFC Alert*](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/newsletter/sep_oct03_eng_a4.pdf) is the 20th issue of the newsletter, which was first published in October 2001. Copies of the current issue of *SFC Alert* are available at the SFC office. All issues are available on the Commission's website at www.hksfc.org.hk.
