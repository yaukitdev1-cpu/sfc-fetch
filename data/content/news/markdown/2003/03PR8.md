In response to media enquiries of certain complaints reportedly made by a group of minority shareholders of a listed company, the SFC issues the following statements:

1. The SFC enforces the Takeovers Code in an impartial manner to ensure that all shareholders are treated even-handedly.

2. The SFC does not interfere with the terms of an offer, as long as they are made in accordance with the Takeovers Code and other applicable laws. Whether an offer price is “high” or “low” is not the issue under the Takeovers Code – a key SFC responsibility is to ensure all eligible shareholders are given the opportunity to consider the same offer.

3. Companies involved in an offer have to fully comply with the requirements of the Takeovers Code, so that shareholders are given sufficient information, advice and time to make informed decisions.

4. Aggrieved shareholders may seek a Takeovers Panel review of the decisions of the SFC Takeovers Executive.

5. The SFC will follow up on suspected non-compliance with the Takeovers Code. It will not hesitate to take regulatory action if investigations reveal wrongdoing. The SFC will seek assistance from its counterparts in other jurisdictions where necessary.

6. Shareholders or other parties in possession of information related to possible breaches of the Takeovers Code are encouraged to report to the SFC. Complaints will be treated in confidence.  The SFC in general does not publicly discuss individual complaints. 

7. Listed companies must disclose accurate information to the market in a timely manner. Company announcements made under the Listing Rules are presently a matter for the Stock Exchange. The “dual filing” arrangement under the Securities and Futures Ordinance, expected to become effective on 1 April 2003, will enable the SFC to more effectively investigate and take action against those making false or intentionally misleading announcements.
