The SFC has listed a catalogue of questions investors should consider before investing in a stock, in light of recent increased retail participation in the stock market, particularly in initial public offerings. The SFC urges investors to consider the following questions in five main areas so as to make informed investment decisions:


- *Business viability* – What business is the company in? What recent corporate moves has the company made? Do the moves enhance the profit of the company, improve its competitiveness, or suggest a change in its business?
- *Corporate governance* – Do you have confidence in the management’s integrity and experience? How frequently does the company raise funds and by what way? Are management changes frequent? Has the management kept shareholders and the investing public informed in a timely manner?
- *Financial health* – Does the company give a detailed review of its operations, financial results and prospects in the annual reports? Is the company delivering on these prospects as promised? Are there any warning signs, such as heavy debts, cashflow problems, qualified auditors’ reports, or unusual change of auditors?
- *Valuation* – Is the stock over- or under-valued? If so, what are the reasons? Seemingly cheap stocks are not necessarily good buys.
- *Trading pattern* – How actively has the stock been trading? Are there frequent unusual price or volume movements? Are there explanations for these movements?

All these questions and more are included in a new SFC leaflet, "Questions to Ask Before Investing in a Stock". The list is not exhaustive, but will help investors choose from the more than 1,000 companies listed on the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong.

As with all other SFC investor publications, the leaflet is available to the public for free at the SFC office and the Consumer Advice Centres of the Consumer Council. The public may also view and download the electronic version of the leaflet on the "Advice to Investors – Investor Education Programmes" section of the SFC website.

Licensed intermediaries are welcome to request copies for distribution by calling the SFC’s Investor Education and Communications Department at 2842 7711.
