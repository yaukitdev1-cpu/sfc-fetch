The SFC has submitted a report to the Secretary for Financial Services and the Treasury on the incident of the HKATS interruption yesterday.

The report outlines the cause of the incident as identified in a preliminary review of the problem by Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited (HKEx). The cause has been identified to be related to a software defect in handling complex combination of orders (the combo orders) under certain circumstances. It was the first time a software problem of this nature has occurred in Hong Kong.

HKEx and the vendor of the system, OM Technology, are continuing their analysis of the problem. OM is preparing a new software program, which HKEx will consider implementing upon successful testing. The SFC will closely monitor the testing and the installation of the program.

Before the program is installed, HKEx is considering alternatives including disabling the function of combo orders to avoid the recurrence of the problem. The SFC has requested an impact analysis before agreeing to this.

The SFC has received from HKEx a preliminary report on the incident. A detailed diagnosis is expected to follow as soon as possible.

The SFC notes that there are currently a number of system-related reviews being conducted by HKEx. These include the comprehensive review on HKATS system, IT quality review and the operational review. The SFC has been involved in the selection of the independent consultants and in monitoring the process of the reviews. The comprehensive review, conducted by KPMG, is expected to be completed by the first quarter this year.

Furthermore, HKEx is also preparing for the introduction of an upgraded version of the OM software.

In the light of the 29 January trading interruption, the SFC proposes the following additional action to address the current problem. The proposals are contained in the SFC’s report to the Administration today. They are:

1. The SFC will request HKEx to engage an independent technology expert acceptable to and accountable to the SFC to retest the HKATS software and to set up an HKEx Board committee to oversee the implementation of the KPMG recommendations and those of the independent technology expert.

2. The SFC will request that HKEx convey to the HKATS system vendor the SFC’s serious concerns about the recurrence of system incidents due to hidden system defects and secure its undertaking of an improvement of product quality and vendor supports.

3. The SFC will step up its supervision process on all HKEx system related enhancements, alterations and reviews, and will closely monitor their implementation.
