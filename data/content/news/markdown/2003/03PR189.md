The SFC today publishes the August issue of *SFC Alert*, a bi-monthly newsletter for both investors and intermediaries. 

The front page story, *An additional investment choice*, reports the release of the Code on Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs) in July.  With the Code in place, applications to launch REITs can be submitted to the SFC and investors will have an additional choice of investment products.

*Know the SFC* is about a new series of documentary-drama that gives an account of the work of the SFC. The series is available in VCD format and on the Electronic Investor Resources Centre (www.hkeirc.org.hk).

*HK remains a major fund management centre* reports that a survey conducted by the SFC has found that Hong Kong remains a major fund management centre in the Asia-Pacific region for attracting overseas funds for management. The survey results also show continuous growth in the amount of money sourced from Hong Kong and the total assets managed in Hong Kong.

By signing a Declaration on Co-operation and Supervision of Cross-Border Investment Management Activity (MoU) with the Australian Securities and Investments Commission (ASIC), the SFC’s co-operation with overseas regulators on fund management supervision is strengthened.  *MoU with ASIC* has the details.

*Quarterly results* gives an update on the financial position of the SFC during the period April to June 2003.

*Outline on disclosure of interests* reminds market participants of reading an updated Outline of Part XV of the Securities and Futures Ordinance, which will assist their compliance with the disclosure requirements of the Ordinance.

Meanwhile, we have introduced new performance pledges to provide efficient and quality service. Read *Faster processing of licence applications* for details.

The regular Investor Focus column discusses the characteristics of REITs to enhance investors’ understanding of the new investment product.

The August issue of SFC Alert is the 19th issue of the newsletter, which was first published in October 2001. Copies of the current issue of *SFC Alert* are available at the SFC office. All issues are  available on the Commission's website at [www.hksfc.org.hk](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/newsletter/jul_aug_eng.pdf).
