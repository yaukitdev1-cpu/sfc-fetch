SFC Chairman, Mr Andrew Sheng, spoke on corporate risk management at a luncheon of the Risk Management Association Hong Kong today.

In his speech, Mr Sheng identified common types of changes in the business environment that carried with them different levels of risk.

He discussed how crucial corporate governance was to success or failure of risk or crisis management. He also stressed that self-discipline and market disciplines were the areas that corporate managers should focus on.
