The SFC releases today the Consultation Conclusions on the proposed revisions to the Guidance Note.

The SFC issued a Consultation Paper in January 2003 and the consultation period ended on 22 February 2003. The SFC received five submissions from brokerage firms, a legal firm, an industry association and a professional body.

The submissions were carefully considered. Respondents in general acknowledged the need for the proposed revisions.

Clarifications to the original proposals have been made in light of the comments. The Consultation Conclusions provides an analysis of the submissions and explains SFC’s views.

The Guidance Note as revised is to be made under section 399 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO). Overall, only minimal changes are introduced as the main purposes of the revisions are to rationalise the Guidance Note with the SFO and its rules. In addition, the revisions will bring in the requirements and practical implications arising from the new anti-terrorist financing law and changes in the relevant anti-money laundering laws and ensure that associated entities of intermediaries are subject to same requirements.

The revised Guidance Note will become effective on 1 April 2003, the implementation date of the SFO. The revised Guidance Note will be gazetted on 28 March 2003.

Copies of the Consultation Conclusions are available at the SFC office, on the [SFC website](/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/consultation/conclusion?refNo=03CP1) and the FinNet communication network.
