This morning China Life issued a clarification announcement in the South China Morning Post, the Hong Kong Economic Times, and the Hong Kong Economic Journal. The announcement contains the identification codes or identification numbers of successful applicants made by electronic application instructions given to HKSCC and where 2,000 or 3,000 Hong Kong offer shares were conditionally allotted to them, as well as correcting certain information in the previous announcement made by China Life on 17 December 2003.

The full allocation lists are available on the websites of China Life, Computershare (the share registrar), and Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Ltd:

1. China Life: [www.chinalife.com.cn](http://www.chinalife.com.cn)
2. Computershare: [www.computershare.com.hk](http://www-uk.computershare.com/Investor/FormsCatalogue.asp?cc=HK&lang=en&bhjs=1&fla=1&theme=cpu) (under "Investors" - "Frequently Requested Forms" then click "downloadable forms")
3. HKEx: [www.hkex.com.hk](http://www.hkex.com.hk)

Investors may also call Computershare's hotline to check their allotment information. The numbers are (852) 2862 8555 and (852) 2862 8666. The hotline will be in operation from 9 am to 6.30 pm.

HKEx has announced that trading in China Life shares will commence on the Stock Exchange this morning.

Both the SFC and HKEx would like to remind investors and exchange participants to check the correct number of Hong Kong offer shares which were allotted to them and their clients before dealing in this stock.
