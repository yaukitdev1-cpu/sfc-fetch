The SFC publishes on its website today a set of Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) to help the market understand our regulatory requirements for the authorisation of REITs.  An Application Form and a set of checklists have also been prepared to assist REIT applicants to assemble relevant documents for the purpose of submitting applications.

FAQs

The FAQs are part of the SFC’s efforts to assist the industry in its understanding of the regulatory requirements and authorisation procedures for REITs based on the Code on REITs. 

In line with this, the FAQs introduce the authorisation procedures, criteria, and on-going compliance requirements under the Code on REITs, plus guidance for REIT applications.  The FAQs aim to help potential REIT applicants and investors understand the Code’s requirements for all REITs seeking SFC authorisation.

The FAQs have been posted on the SFC website. They will be updated regularly and interested parties are encouraged to check the website for new information.

Market participants are encouraged to review the FAQs to obtain a basic understanding of the procedures and requirements for REIT authoristion.  As the FAQs are by no means exhaustive, prospective REIT applicants are advised to contact the SFC for further information and clarification prior to their submission of formal applications.

Application Form & Checklists

The Application Form and checklists are also available on the SFC website.  An applicant is required to submit the Application Form and checklists to the SFC for REIT authorisation.  The Application Form facilitates an applicant’s description of the key characteristics and operators of a REIT.  The checklists contain a detailed list of relevant documents to be submitted to the SFC for the purpose of obtaining authorisation.  Such documents should help an applicant demonstrate the REIT’s compliance with the Code on REITs. 

The FAQs, the Application Form and checklists are available on the SFC website at www.hksfc.org.hk, under the section “Bills, Legislation and Codes – Regulatory Handbook – Volume 2, Part C”.
