The SFC has published a comparison table on certain requirements of the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO) to help intermediaries understand the changes from the existing regime.

The SFO is expected to come into effect on 1 April 2003. The SFC has been assisting the industry in the transition to the new regime through training lectures, workshops, publications, and resources materials on the website.

One of the most significant features of the SFO is the single licensing regime. This is a streamlined regulatory framework for the licensing or registration of firms and individuals engaged by licensed firms in carrying on any regulated activity. It replaces the current licensing requirements, which are spread across several ordinances.

Once a firm is licensed or registered, it is subject to ongoing operational requirements. The structure for these requirements is also different in the SFO as they are primarily contained in subsidiary legislation. The requirements have been modernised to improve investor protection and ease compliance.

A comparison table is published today to outline the differences between the current framework and the SFO in licensing and operational requirements (the latter including audited accounts and other reporting; client securities; client money; contract notes, statements of account and receipts; financial resources; and keeping of records).

A circular has been sent to SFC registered intermediaries and exempt persons through FinNet. The table is also available on the SFC website under "[Others: General Educational Materials](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/legislation/securities/others/lic_comparison_table_eng.pdf)" in the SFO section.

The SFC welcomes enquiries related to the SFO. They can be made by email to sfoenquiry@hksfc.org.hk.
