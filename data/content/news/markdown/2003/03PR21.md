The SFC today publishes the Winter 2002 issue (No. 50) of the *SFC Quarterly Bulletin*.

The feature article of this issue, *New Investor Compensation Arrangement*, gives an overview on the new and improved investor compensation arrangements upon the commencement of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

This issue includes the excerpts of two speeches, entitled *The Role of the Accounting Profession in Enhancing Corporate Governance* and *Structure of the Brokerage Industry* respectively, by SFC Chairman, Mr Andrew Sheng. 

Also included are the abridged versions of two research articles - *The Impact of Stock Consolidations and Capital Reductions on Equity Valuation: Preliminary Evidence for Hong Kong* contributed by Prof Paul B. McGuinness, and *Fund Management in Hong Kong and Singapore* contributed by Mr Matthew Harrison. 

Full versions of the above mentioned articles are all available at the SFC website. 

The market update, *Arbitrage Activities Between HSI Stocks and HSI-Related Derivatives Products*, discusses the significance of arbitrage activities to the local cash market turnover and the performance of major stock markets during 2002.
