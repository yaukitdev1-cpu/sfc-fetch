The SFC welcomes the publication of a report today by the Expert Group to Review the Operation of the Securities and Futures Market Regulatory Structure. The SFC will study the report carefully.

The SFC will co-operate fully with the Government and other relevant parties, in particular HKEx, to draw up a programme for implementing the recommendations for the transfer of listing functions. In particular, we will work towards a smooth and efficient transfer of responsibilities.  We will work with HKEx to effect the smooth transfer of HKEx Listing Division colleagues into the Commission.

The SFC will do its best to ensure that it will discharge its new responsibilities effectively and efficiently, in order to help strengthen Hong Kong’s competitiveness as an international financial centre and the premier capital formation centre for China.
