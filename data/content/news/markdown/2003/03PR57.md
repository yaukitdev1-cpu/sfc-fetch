The SFC today publishes two guidelines to help intermediaries migrate smoothly to a new licensing regime under the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

1.         Guidelines on Transitional Arrangements

The Guidelines explain the operation of the transitional provisions under the SFO that are generally applicable to existing registrants and exempt persons.  They also set out detailed procedures to guide existing intermediaries how to migrate to the new regime during the two-year transitional period following implementation of the SFO on 1 April 2003.

The major topics include:

- Special arrangements during the transitional period (e.g. a licensed individual only needs to complete the same number of Continuous Professional Training hours as required under the current regime regardless of the number of regulated activities he is deemed to have been licensed during the transitional period);
- Application for new licence or registration by existing intermediaries;
- New regulatory requirements under the SFO; and
- Comparison of the current and new licensing regimes.

2.         Guidelines on Waivers of Certain Licensing Fees

The SFC has previously announced that certain fee waivers will be made available to existing intermediaries during the transitional period so that they will not incur extra licensing costs over and above the amounts they are now paying. The Guidelines provide for and explain the various fee waivers.

Furthermore, the Guidelines provide for a discount on annual fees to intermediaries seeking to convert their licences under the new licensing regime in the first year of the transitional period. Intermediaries which migrate early can enjoy a 5% early bird discount on their annual fees till the end of the transitional period.

The SFC plans to gazette these two Guidelines under section 399(1) of the SFO on 21 March 2003.  They will be effective at the same time when the SFO commences.

The two Guidelines can be downloaded free of charge from the SFC’s website under “Licensing Related Matters” of the Intermediaries, Licensing and Investment Products section.  Printed copies are available for sale at the SFC’s office ($100 each for the Guidelines on Transitional Arrangements (Note 1) and $20 each for the Guidelines on Waivers of Certain Licensing Fees).
