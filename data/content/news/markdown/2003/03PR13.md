The SFC today publishes for public consultation a draft Guidance Note on Position Limits and Large Open Position Reporting Requirements.  The SFC proposes to issue the Guidance Note under section 399 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO) when it comes into operation.  

The SFC has made the Securities and Futures (Contracts Limits and Reportable Positions) Rules under section 35(1) of the SFO to (i) prescribe limits on the number of futures contracts or options contracts that may be held or controlled by a person and (ii) require a person holding or controlling a reportable position to notify the recognized exchange company.  The Rules shall come into operation when the SFO commences.

To help market participants better understand how the Rules operate in practice, the SFC proposes to issue the Guidance Note to explain compliance requirements of the Rules and clarify issues raised by the industry.  Since the Rules also provide that persons authorized by the SFC or the recognized exchange company could hold or control positions in excess of the prescribed limits, the Guidance Note describes the application procedures for a person wishing to hold or control excess positions and the procedures for appealing against an SFC decision under the Rules.
