The SFC today launches a new series of investor education materials on portfolio planning. 

The SFC has in the past educated the public on the basics of investment and features of different investment products. The latter range from stocks and bonds, to futures and other structured products such as equity-linked instruments (ELIs). Progressing from fundamental investment knowledge, investors who may be interested in a variety of products and have specific investment needs such as for retirement planning should recognise the importance and learn the skills of portfolio planning. It is particularly important in light of the increasing variety and complexity of investment products.

Portfolio planning refers to the process where a person decides how to allocate assets and manage risks. For example, retired people and parents planning the education funding for their children may wish to bear different levels of risks and must plan accordingly.

Investor leaflet and feature articles

A new leaflet entitled “Portfolio Planning - Managing *Your* Investments to Suit *You*” explains the steps in the portfolio planning process. It reminds investors to be clear about their own investment objectives, to understand the nature and risk of any investment offers, how they can manage risks, and the importance of rebalancing their portfolio where appropriate.

The leaflet is supplemented by on-line educational articles on the SFC-operated Electronic Investor Resources Centre (eIRC) discussing analysis and risk management techniques. There are also questions and answers in the articles to make them more user-friendly and easy to understand.

*Fundamental Analysis* introduces commonly used tools – such as earnings per share, price/earning ratio, dividends and book value – to access the “fundamentals” of listed companies by looking at the companies’ financial performance.* *

*Technical Analysis* describes beginner concepts of charting to help study stock price movements and identify trends. Some technical terms are also explained.

*Risk Management* examines the various risks of investing and some of the ways, such as diversification and hedging, to control risk exposure.
 
*Investing in Emerging Industries* highlights the risk of “herd” investing and issues investors should consider before buying shares in start-up companies.

Hyperlinks to other websites
 
To help investors conduct their own research, the eIRC provides hyperlinks to other websites specialised in portfolio planning, fundamental analysis and technical analysis.  These hyperlinks are available at the “[eLibrary/Investment Analysis](http://www.eirc.hk/eirc/html/EN/elibrary/index.htm%20%20)” section.
