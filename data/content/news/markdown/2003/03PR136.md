The Securities and Futures Commission is pleased to announce the Shareholders Group membership for 2003-2004. The Commission has appointed 13 members to the Group for a one-year term, effective 17 June 2003.

Three new members have been appointed. They are Mr Albert Jinghan Cheng, Mr David Cheung and Mr Richard Mak.

The Shareholders Group is a standing committee under section 8 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance. The Group’s members include representatives of retail and institutional investors, professionals, academics, prominent advocates of investors’ rights, and the current Chairman of the Consumer Council. Since its formal establishment in June 2002 the Group has held seven meetings covering a wide range of topics. Views gathered from the Group have assisted the Corporate Finance and other divisions of the SFC in considering issues relating to shareholders’ interests and on the formulation of regulatory proposals.

"All members of the Shareholders Group have given valuable input on the topics we discussed over the last year. The Commission very much appreciates their generous support in giving us their time and sharing of their insights. We wish to thank especially Mr Mark Taylor, Prof Judy Tsui and Mr V-Nee Yeh who are retiring from the Group," said Mr Ashley Alder, SFC’s Executive Director of Corporate Finance and Chairman of the Group.
