In view of media enquiries to follow up certain newspaper reports today on listed investment companies, the SFC is pleased to issue the following advice to investors as part of its ongoing educational efforts.

Listed investment companies are companies listed on the Main Board under Chapter 21 of the Stock Exchange’s Listing Rules. These companies are investment vehicles whose primary objectives are making investments in listed and unlisted securities. They do not have any other active business. As of 30 September 2003, there were 26 investment companies listed on the Main Board.

Investors should note that investment companies listed under Chapter 21 are subject to different listing and disclosure requirements as compared to other listings (e.g. no track record and minimum market capitalisation requirements). Shares in most of the currently listed investment companies are not actively traded and their portfolios of listed investments generally consist of inactive stocks. Besides, many of them tend to trade either substantially above or below their reported net asset value (NAV).

Before investing in a listed investment company, investors are advised to:


- Read the relevant corporate announcements carefully to learn more about the company’s background.
- Compare the NAV and share price. A large deviation between them, especially for a prolonged period, warrants particular attention.
- Note the size of the company by making reference to its reported NAV. Relatively high and fixed operating expenses like salary, rents and management fees may substantially drag down investment returns for a less well-capitalised investment company.
- Check the proportion of listed securities, unlisted securities and other investments in the investment portfolio.
- Find out the valuation basis, in particular for the company’s holding in unlisted securities.

To help investors learn more about listed investment companies, the SFC has posted a new article on *Listed Investment Companies* on its website under the "Advice to Investors/Questions Corner" section.

Retail investors can call the SFC’s Investor Hotline at 2840 9333 for educational information on listed investment companies.
