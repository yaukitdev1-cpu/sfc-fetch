The SFC publishes a Licensing Information Booklet providing useful information concerning licensing and related requirements under the Securities and Futures Ordinance. Some of the topics covered in the Booklet are as follows:-


- Types of regulated activities,
- Scope of the licensing net and related exemptions,
- Procedures of applying for a licence or registration,
- Basic approval criteria,
- On-going obligations of licensed persons and registered institutions, and
- Licensing requirements in relation to the provision of financial information on the Internet or on-line trading services.

The Booklet can be downloaded free of charge from the SFC website at http: //www.hksfc.org.hk (Note 1). Printed copies will soon be available for sale at $50 each at the SFC office.

The SFC would like to remind intermediaries that apart from the Booklet, licensing related information is also available through the SFC Infoline (telephone number: 2840 9393). A reference guide to the Infoline is appended to the Booklet to assist users in obtaining the required licensing information or documents, e.g. licence application forms and notification forms.

It is believed that the Booklet, together with the Infoline and other supplementary information such as *Answers to the Frequently Asked Questions* posted on the SFC website, could deal with most enquiries on licensing matters. Intermediaries, in particular their compliance personnel, should hence make good use of the materials available.
