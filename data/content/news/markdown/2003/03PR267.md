The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X} under the Securities Ordinance for acting as an investment representative of TG Holborn (HK) Limited without being registered.

{X} joined TG Holborn, a registered investment adviser, as a consultant in March 2001. Without having any registration with SFC, {X} approached two clients in about June 2001 and promoted certain investment products concerning securities. Subsequent to the clients’ subscriptions of these investment products, {X} was remunerated by TG Holborn.

{X} pleaded guilty today before Mr Anthony Yuen, a magistrate at Western Magistracy. {X} was fined $1,000 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $3,000 to the SFC.

An SFC spokesman said that any person who intended to perform any of the functions of a registered investment adviser must first be registered with the SFC. The public are reminded that regulated activities must only be carried out by licensed personnel, and that the public should check to ensure that the persons with whom they deal are licensed.
