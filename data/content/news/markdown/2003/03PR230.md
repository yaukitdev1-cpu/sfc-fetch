SFC Chairman, Mr Andrew Sheng, today gave a speech on the above subject at the inaugural meeting of the International Investment Bank Regional General Counsel Group, Hong Kong (Note 1).

Mr Sheng discussed the role of the consultation process in shaping the law, and particularly referred to the promulgation of the Securities and Futures Ordinance. He addressed several key questions: *Why do we consult? Who do we consult? What do we consult on?* and *How do we consult?*

"We are very conscious of the importance of transparency in our securities and futures regulatory regime and we recognise the key role of public consultation as being fundamental to regulatory transparency. Effective consultation helps us, the regulator to communicate our policy goals and enhance the understanding of our objectives," Mr Sheng said.

"At the same time, with the market’s feedback on our regulatory proposals, we are made aware of the expectations and needs of market participants and investors. This improves our decision-making process, helps to ensure that our proposals meet the needs of the market and the investing public and enhances our accountability," he said.

"The SFC relies on the active co-operation of market participants to ensure the quality of the rules, codes and guidelines that are eventually adopted," Mr Sheng said.

The SFC has prepared a summary of its consultation procedures, which is posted on the SFC website under "Bills, Legislation and Codes" - "Regulatory Handbook" under Volume 1, Regulatory Philosophy.
