The SFC has gazetted a number of codes, guidelines and forms etc that will become effective upon implementation of the Securities and Futures Ordinance on 1 April 2003.

These items are part of a considerable body of regulatory codes and guidelines that are published by the SFC in the run up to 1 April.

Many SFC codes and guidelines are being updated to rationalise their contents with the SFO. New items have also been prepared to assist the market in understanding and complying with the regulatory requirements where applicable.

Most of the revisions do not involve policy changes. Where substantive changes are made, they generally have been made in response to market requests or following consultation with the market.

The 9 items that are gazetted today and include in this press release are:

Guidelines and Codes published under section 399, SFO

1. *Code on Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds*

2. *Code on Investment-Linked Assurance Schemes*

3. *Code on Pooled Retirement Funds*

4. *Code on Immigration-Linked Investment Schemes*

5. *SFC Code on MPF Products*

Changes have been made mainly to bring these Codes into line with the provisions of the SFO.  The changes include replacing various references to Ordinances that are now encompassed in the SFO and reflecting the new authorisation requirements relating to "approved persons" as set out in sections 104(2) and 105(2) of the SFO.

6. *Fund Manager Code of Conduct*

Changes introduced are primarily to rationalise the revised Code with the provisions of the SFO and to result in a code of conduct applicable to all persons licensed by or registered with the SFC whose business involves the discretionary management of collective investment schemes.

7. *Guidance Note for Persons Advertising or Offering Collective Investment Schemes on the Internet*

The Note replaces the version of the same Note which was published in May 2001.  With the exception of substituting references to provisions of the Protection of Investors Ordinance and the Securities Ordinance with the corresponding SFO provisions, no other material changes have been made to the Guidelines now being issued.  

8. *Guidance Note Issued by the Securities and Futures Commission on Prevention of Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing*

Changes were made to rationalise the Guidance Note with the SFO and its rules, to bring in the requirements and practical implications arising from the new anti-terrorist financing law and changes in the relevant anti-money laundering laws, and to ensure that associated entities of intermediaries are subject to same requirements.  Clarification of some of the existing provisions on customer due diligence and further guidance in identifying suspicious transactions and detecting terrorist financing were also added.  (Please also see related SFC press release issued on 24 March 2003.  The release is available on the SFC website.)

9.* Guidance Note on Position Limits and Large Open Position Reporting Requirements*

The Guidance Note explains the SFC's policy intent on the application of the Securities and Futures (Contracts Limits and Reportable Positions) Rules.

The Guidance Note is published to explain compliance requirements of the Rules and clarify issues raised by the industry.  (Please see related SFC press release issued on 24 March 2003.  The release is available on the SFC website.)

The various items can be accessed through hyperlinks on the SFC website to the Gazette (please click on the links embedded in the electronic version of this press release, which is located in What’s New under the SFO section).  They will be fully available on the SFC website from 1 April 2003.
