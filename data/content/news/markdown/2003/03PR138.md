The SFC today publishes the June issue of *SFC Enforcement Reporter*, summarising the Commission’s enforcement actions in the month of May 2003 and discussing the compliance issues highlighted by recent cases.

Five market manipulators have been convicted recently, including two brothers who conspired to defraud investors by manipulating the shares of Gay Giano International Group Ltd. It is a record of convictions in such a short period of time. The other three were prosecuted for intentionally creating false markets in the shares of Yeebo (International Holdings) Ltd, China Development Corporation Ltd and Pioneer Global Group Ltd. Together with other law enforcement agencies, the SFC is committed to clamping down on market crimes. It also appears that the courts will not hesitate to impose jail terms on market manipulators, as evidenced in the prevalence of custodial and suspended custodial sentences.

In May, the SFC also successfully prosecuted a person for breaching the Securities (Disclosure of Interests) Ordinance and a securities firm and its director for failure to notify the Commission of its liquid capital deficiency.

On disciplinary actions, five licensees were suspended for periods ranging from four weeks to 12 months as a result of front running, failure to act on possible illegal trading activities, exercising clients’ rights without consent and improper handling of client accounts. One firm and a licensed representative were also reprimanded for other misconduct.

Since April 2003, the SFC has successfully prosecuted 11 entities and disciplined 18 licensees for various regulatory breaches.
