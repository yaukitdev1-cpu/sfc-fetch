The SFC announces that the Companies Ordinance (Exemption of Companies and Prospectuses From Compliance with Provisions) (Amendment) Notice 2003 came into operation today.

The Notice was issued by the SFC on 24 March 2003 pursuant to its powers under sections 38A(2) and 342A(2) of the Companies Ordinance (CO) to exempt classes of prospectuses from compliance with those contents requirements in the Third Schedule to the CO which the SFC considers are unduly burdensome or irrelevant. The Notice was published on 28 March 2003 in Legal Supplement No. 2 to the Government Gazette as L.N. No. 85 of 2003 and can be viewed on the Government’s website at [*www.info.gov.hk/pd/egazette/*](http://www.gld.gov.hk/cgi-bin/gld/egazette/gazettefiles.cgi?lang=e&year=2003&month=3&day=28&vol=7&no=13&header=0&acurrentpage=10&df=0&agree=1&gaz_type=ls2). The Notice, which was tabled before the Legislative Council and subjected to negative vetting, became effective on 23 May 2003.

The Notice contains two new class exemptions: (1) prospectuses relating to offers of listed and unlisted debentures will be exempted from those contents requirements that the SFC considers are irrelevant for the purposes of making an informed investment decision and/or unduly burdensome for companies to comply with; and (2) prospectuses relating to offers of listed debentures will be exempted from those contents requirements that are the same as or similar to those in the Listing Rules applicable to debt securities (provided no waiver has been granted from such requirements).

The Notice also introduces a new requirement that a prospectus shall include a statement identifying any of the class exemptions that the issuer of the prospectus has relied upon. This information is designed to increase transparency as to compliance of the prospectus with the statutory content requirements.

With immediate effect, an application to the SFC for an exemption from any or all of the requirements set out in the class exemptions will not be required for prospectuses that meet the conditions specified.

Mr Ashley Alder, SFC’s Executive Director of Corporate Finance, said: "This initiative will facilitate offers of bonds and other debt securities and forms part of the first phase of a three-phase approach to update the prospectus regime to facilitate public offers of shares and debentures, commencing with the issue of SFC guidelines on offer awareness materials, programme offerings and registration formalities in February 2003."

The second phase kicked off with the release of a joint consultation paper by the SFC and the FSTB in March 2003 seeking public comments on proposed amendments to the CO aiming to provide a statutory basis for the SFC guidelines and to make other significant improvements to the prospectus regime. The third phase involves a comprehensive review of the securities offering regime by the SFC.

For full details of the exemptions, please refer to the Notice. For details of the background and rationale for granting the exemptions, please refer to the [SFC’s press release of 28 March 2003](/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/news-and-announcements/news/doc?refNo=03PR76) which is available on the SFC website.
