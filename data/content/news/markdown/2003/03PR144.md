The SFC today publishes the June issue of *SFC Alert*, a bi-monthly newsletter for both investors and intermediaries.

The lead article, *Strengthening regulation of intermediaries,* explains the consultative proposals on enhancing regulation of sponsors and independent financial advisers. The public are invited to comment before 31 July 2003.

Inside, *Creating a better business environment for brokers* describes the wide-ranging measures a working group has suggested to improve the business environment of the brokerage industry, as well as the progress so far.

Meanwhile, *Brokerages’ business improves amid SARS* shows that the business turnover of brokers has increased despite the outbreak of the atypical pneumonia.

*Proper procedures confirmed* reports on the findings of the Process Review Panel that checks and balances were in place in the SFC.

The Government and the SFC are jointly consulting on proposals to allow the SFC to initiate derivative actions. *Proposal to empower SFC to bring derivative actions* gives some details.

The regular Investor Focus column discusses how dealings between investors and their intermediaries have changed under the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

The June issue of *SFC Alert* is the 18th issue of the newsletter, which was first published in October 2001. This is also the last issue published out of the existing SFC office in The Landmark. The SFC will move to its new premises in Chater House on 30 June 2003.

Copies of the [current issue](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/newsletter/may_jun_eng_a4.pdf) of *SFC Alert* are available at the SFC office. All issues are available on the Commission's website at www.hksfc.org.hk.
