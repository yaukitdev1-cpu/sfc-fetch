The SFC warmly welcomes the re-appointment by the Acting Financial Secretary of Mr Raymond Kwok as a Non-Executive Director of the SFC.
 
“We feel indebted to our Non-Executive Directors for their independent views and  active participation in the Commission’s affairs,” said SFC Chairman, Mr Andrew Sheng. “All of us at the Commission will continue to work closely with Mr Kwok, who has made invaluable contribution to the SFC.”
 
Mr Kwok has been an SFC Non-Executive Director since 1998. He is also the Deputy Chairman of the SFC’s internal Budget Committee, and a member of the Audit Committee and the Remuneration Committee.
