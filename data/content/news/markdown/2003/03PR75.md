The SFC will step up its review of promotional materials and advertisements issued by intermediaries following the lapse of the Stock Exchange’s minimum commission rule on 1 April.

In view of certain recent advertisements by securities dealers offering lower brokerage commission rates and reward schemes, the SFC last week issued an alert to urge investors to carefully ascertain the details of any such promotional offers.

The SFC wishes to reiterate that promotional materials and advertisements must accurately disclose the relevant information in full.

Alexa Lam, SFC’s Executive Director of Intermediaries and Investment Products, said the SFC would not tolerate dissemination of false or misleading information by registered intermediaries to the investing public.

"Should any intermediary be found to have issued an advertisement that is false, disparaging, misleading or deceptive, the SFC will immediately undertake a review of that intermediary’s fitness and properness to remain registered and take appropriate disciplinary action," she said.

The SFC attaches great importance to intermediaries’ observance of the Code of Conduct regarding the provision of full and accurate information to their clients and the investing public. Intermediaries who fail to adhere to the Code will be disciplined.
