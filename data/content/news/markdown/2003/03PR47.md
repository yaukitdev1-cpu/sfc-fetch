The SFC today publishes the March issue of *SFC Enforcement Reporter*, summarising the Commission’s enforcement actions in the month of February 2003 and discussing the compliance issues highlighted by recent cases.

February saw two insider dealers ordered to pay more than $8 million in disgorgement of profits, penalties and costs. The Insider Dealing Tribunal has earlier identified the pair as insider dealers concerning the shares of a company of which they were previously directors.

An unauthorised advertisement on a spa resort led to a $50,000 fine on a company for breach of the Protection of Investors Ordinance. Also, during the month, eight intermediaries were disciplined, including one who tried to mislead the SFC in a market manipulation investigation.

Since 1 April 2002, the SFC has successfully prosecuted 30 people and firms and disciplined 70 entities.

The March issue of [*SFC Enforcement Reporter*](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/enforcement/march_03_eng.pdf) is the 5th issue of the newsletter, copies of which are available at the SFC's office. All issues are also available on the SFC website under the section "[Press Releases, Publications & Speeches – Publications](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/speeches/public/enforcement/enforcement.html%20%20)".

The public are welcome to subscribe to the newsletter by registering for the SFC Website Update Email Alert service on the Commission’s homepage. They will then be able to receive the newsletter via email.
