The SFC today publishes the February issue of *SFC Alert*, a bi-monthly newsletter for both investors and intermediaries.

In the main story *New regulatory framework, Better quality of market*, SFC Chairman Mr Andrew Sheng outlines the priorities of the Commission’s work in 2003. Implementing the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO) will be the SFC’s top priority, while toughening enforcement activities and relocating the SFC’s office are two other important tasks.

*Budget deficit contained* reports on the latest financial performance of the Commission. According to the SFC’s third Quarterly Report for the financial year 2002-2003, the SFC has successfully reduced operating cost, hence keeping the accumulated deficit for nine months to $35 million, well below the projected deficit of $87 million in the approved budget.

*Commitment to partnership* stresses that opportunities remain in the financial services sector despite the difficult economic environment, and the SFC continues to help the brokerage industry.

Inside, *Hand in hand* describes a new Memorandum of Understanding signed between the SFC and the Stock Exchange to provide for the dual filing arrangement.

*Cold shoulder order* reports on an order by the Takeovers and Mergers Executive to deny a person’s access to the securities market for his breach of the Takeovers Code.

Still on enforcement, the SFC has filed a bankruptcy petition against a broker. *No hesitation* summarises the SFC’s action to protect investors’ interests.

In *Compliance reminder*, the SFC reiterates the importance for fund managers and intermediaries to observe the law when marketing funds. It also encourages investors to check if the products they plan to invest are authorised.

An investor education article, *Trading a stock with two codes*, advises investors of the points to note during parallel trading, which occurs when a listed company announces a share split or consolidation.

The regular column *SFO updates* continues to provide latest news on the implementation of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

The February issue of *SFC Alert* is the 16th issue of the newsletter, which was first published in October 2001.

Copies of the current issue of [*SFC Alert*](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/newsletter/jan_feb03_eng_a4.pdf) are available at the SFC's office. All issues are also available on the Commission's website at www.hksfc.org.hk.
