The SFC today releases the Consultation Conclusions on a Proposed Amendment to the Securities and Futures (Price Stabilizing) Rules in relation to Recognition of Overseas Rules.

The proposed amendment was released to the public for a one-month consultation on 18 July 2003. The proposal was to amend the Securities and Futures (Price Stabilizing) Rules (PS Rules) to include the United Kingdom (UK) as a recognised jurisdiction for the purposes of section 15 of the PS Rules.

In order to facilitate global offerings, section 15 of the PS Rules provides that stabilizing action conducted overseas for Hong Kong traded securities with a view to stabilizing the price of securities on the overseas market and effected in accordance with the stabilization rules in recognised jurisdictions specified in Schedule 4 to the Rules will not for the purpose of the Securities and Futures Ordinance be regarded as market misconduct.

Two submissions were received and they were supportive of the recognition of the UK rules. There were a few other comments that did not fall within the scope of the subject consultation. The Consultation Conclusions explain our policy viewpoints on these other comments.

In July this year, the UK Financial Services Authority (FSA) proposed to grant recognition of Hong Kong’s PS Rules by making the necessary amendments to the UK Price Stabilizing Rules. It is expected that the FSA will recognise the PS Rules in the near future, following the procedures necessary in the UK for rules amendments.

The PS Rules amendment is subject to negative vetting by the Legislative Council and will become effective in early February 2004.

Copies of the Consultation Conclusions are available at the SFC office and on the SFC website.
