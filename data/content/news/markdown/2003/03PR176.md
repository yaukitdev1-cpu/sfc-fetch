The VCD containing the full version of the SFC’s new documentary-drama, "Know the SFC", is now available to the public free of charge.

An abridged version of the video series is being shown on Cable TV. It contains nine episodes, each lasting about five minutes (Note 1). The original full version, now available in VCD format, is slightly longer with some episodes lasting up to seven minutes.

"Know the SFC" introduces the SFC’s regulatory principles and daily work, such as licensing and supervising stockbrokers, tackling market manipulation and insider dealing, regulation of takeovers, fund authorisations and complaints handling. The series also highlights some significant improvements of the regulatory framework under the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

The series is in Cantonese and complemented by a Chinese language leaflet that contains the synopsis of each episode.

The public can request for free copies of the VCD and the leaflet at the SFC office. Copies of the VCD and the leaflet have been sent to each licensed corporation. Intermediaries are encouraged to show the video at their offices. Those who wish to receive additional copies for distribution to their staff and clients may contact our Investor Education and Communications Department at 2842 7711.

The full version of the series can be viewed on the SFC-operated Electronic Investor Resources Centre (www.hkeirc.org).
