The SFC today publishes [a report by the independent Audit Committee on an investigation into certain anonymous allegations about personnel related matters of the SFC](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/audit_committee_report.pdf).

The SFC welcomes and endorses the report and will implement the recommendations of the Audit Committee in full.

The report, entitled “Report of the Audit Committee of the Securities and Futures Commission on its investigation into certain allegations concerning the Commission contained in a series of anonymous documents circulated in the community during the period from July 2002 to January 2003”, is available at the SFC office and on the SFC website.
