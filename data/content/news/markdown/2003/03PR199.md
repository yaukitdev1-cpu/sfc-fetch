The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X} under the Securities Ordinance for acting as an investment representative of TG Holborn (HK) Limited without being registered. {X} pleaded guilty before Mr T S Jenkins at Western Magistracy today. {X} was fined $3,500 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $5,000 to the SFC. 

An SFC spokesman reminds the public that a person needs to be licensed before he is qualified to conduct activities regulated by the SFC.

Before the Securities and Futures Ordinance became effective on 1 April 2003, a person needed to be registered with the SFC to conduct the regulated activities.
