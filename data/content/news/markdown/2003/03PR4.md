The SFC welcomes Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited’s (HKEx) confirmation of its earlier decision that minimum commission rates will cease to have effect from 1 April 2003.

The SFC believes the liberalisation of commission rates is in the best interests of the public and the Hong Kong market as a whole.

The SFC will actively participate in the tripartite forum as proposed by Mr Frederick Ma, the Secretary for Financial Services and the Treasury, and will work closely with the Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau and HKEx to help brokers strengthen their competitiveness in the current business environment.
