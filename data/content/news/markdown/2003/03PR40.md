The SFC welcomes the Financial Secretary’s plan to strengthen the financial services industry in Hong Kong.

The SFC will fully co-operate with and assist the Government in order to achieve the objectives of the proposals outlined by the Financial Secretary, Mr Antony Leung.

Mr Leung said that Hong Kong must move up the value chain to attract quality companies to list in Hong Kong and investors to participate in the market.

SFC Chairman, Mr Andrew Sheng, said the SFC had been working closely with the Administration to implement the action plan on upgrading governance for listed companies.

"I am confident that the implementation of the Securities and Futures Ordinance on 1 April will further the objective of improving the quality of the stock market," he said.

The SFC is also pleased that the Financial Secretary has proposed exempting offshore funds from profits tax, and exempting the fixed stamp duty on subscriptions to and redemption of unit trusts domiciled in Hong Kong.

"As noted in the Budget speech, the SFC will this week announce a consultation on the authorisation of Real Estate Investment Trusts for public sale in Hong Kong. The SFC will continue to facilitate the development of the fund industry, in line with the Government’s objective to reinforce the status of Hong Kong as an international financial centre," Mr Sheng said.

In addition to the publication of guidelines last month to facilitate the public issuance of equities and bonds, the SFC is also working with the Administration on proposed Companies Ordinance amendments on prospectus requirements.
