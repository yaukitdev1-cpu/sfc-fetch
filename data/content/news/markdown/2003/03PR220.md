The SFC today has issued a circular to licensed corporations to alert them of the perpetration of fraud by walk-in clients.

The SFC has recently received a number of reports regarding walk-in clients using false identity cards and/or dishonoured cheques or a faked cashier order to open accounts at licensed corporations. Once payment for the transactions is rejected, the licensed corporation is left to bear the cost of the purchase.

The circular urges licensed corporations to observe the "Know Your Clients" Rules and implement effective control procedures to protect their operations from fraud or dishonest acts and avoid suffering unnecessary financial losses.

The circular is available on the SFC website under the section of *Bills, Legislation and Codes -* *Codes, Guidelines and Circulars*.
