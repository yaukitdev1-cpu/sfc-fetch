The SFC welcomes today’s announcement by Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited of the appointment of Mr Paul Chow Man Yiu as its Chief Executive.

The appointment was approved by the Commission today. It will take effect on 1 May 2003.

Mr Chow will succeed Mr K C Kwong, who will step down on 15 April 2003. The SFC has approved HKEx's appointment of Mr Frederick Grede as the Acting Chief Executive from 16 to 30 April 2003.
