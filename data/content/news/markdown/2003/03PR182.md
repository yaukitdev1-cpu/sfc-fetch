SFC Chairman, Mr Andrew Sheng, spoke on the Hong Kong brokerage industry at a luncheon of the Hong Kong Securities Institute today.

Mr Sheng said that since the SFC had taken over the full supervision of the brokerage industry in March 2000, it had studied the risks inherent in the industry and was managing these risks closely in close co-operation with the industry.

Two main types of risk posed the greatest challenges, namely the integrity risk of misappropriation of client assets within firms and the pooling risk arising from a structural aspect of the Hong Kong industry in relation to provision of margin financing for investors. In his speech, Mr Sheng discussed the various initiatives that sought to address those issues.

Mr Sheng added that the business model of the brokerage industry worldwide was beginning to change with demographics, technology and consumer needs. Customers nowadays wanted convenient and safe one-stop solutions, which paved the way for the financial planning industry, he said.

"It offers brokers an excellent opportunity to enter the industry by providing integrated solutions, better services and a wider range of products to clients. The brokerage industry can work with Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Ltd and the other infrastructure providers to see how such services can be provided efficiently," he said.

Mr Sheng pledged the support of the Commission in working with the industry to look after the interest of investors.
