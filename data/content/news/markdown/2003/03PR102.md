The SFC today publishes the April issue of *SFC Alert*, a bi-monthly newsletter for both investors and intermediaries.

The Securities and Futures Ordinance came into force on 1 April 2003. The main story, *SFO – effective now*, informs readers of the latest initiatives to help intermediaries and investors understand the law.

Also on page one is an article discussing the SFC’s finances. It is reported that the deficit of the Commission for 2002-03 is projected to be $59 million, half of the original estimated deficit of $117.6 million. The SFC will continue its cost control measures.

*New office* announces the relocation of the SFC office to the Chater House in July. The SFC will achieve 10% rental savings.

Inside, *Securities offering made easier* describes the efforts of the Government and the SFC to facilitate the offering of shares and debentures.

*Real estate funds for more choices* explains the background and asks the public to comment on the draft code on Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs).

The SFC has obtained a bankruptcy order on a broker. See article entitled *Licensees must observe fiduciary duties.*

The regular Investor Focus column takes a look at fees and charges on securities transactions.

The April issue of *SFC Alert* is the 17th issue of the newsletter, which was first published in October 2001.

Copies of the current issue of [*SFC Alert*](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/newsletter/mar_apr03_eng_a4.pdf) are available at the SFC's office. All issues are available on the Commission's website.
