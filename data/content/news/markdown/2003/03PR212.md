The SFC today publishes the Consultation Conclusions on Proposals for a Scripless Securities Market following a Scripless Consultation Paper published last year.

HKEx will also publish a consultation paper on the scripless model in early October 2003, which largely follows the same split register model as that outlined in the Consultation Conclusions and adds operational details.

Major proposals in the SFC’s Consultation Paper are summarised as follows:


Listed companies' registers of members would comprise two parts: a Central Clearing and Settlement System (CCASS) register and an Issuer register.

(a) The CCASS register. All CCASS Participants would appear on the CCASS register and enjoy the benefits of being registered shareholders. Share certificates held by CCASS would be cancelled and the CCASS register would be scripless.

(b) The Issuer register. The Issuer register would include both scripless and certificated holdings. Holders of share certificates would be able to deliver certificates to share registrars for cancellation and creation of scripless holdings.

Shareholders would have three main options for scripless holdings.

(i) Hold as beneficial owners through a broker, custodian or another nominee and not have their names appear on the register,

(ii) Be registered shareholders on the CCASS register or on the Issuer register and have full control of their holdings, or

(iii) Be registered shareholders similar to (ii) above but who give a broker or custodian an authority to make transfers for ease of trading or for pledging purposes.


A total of 12 submissions of comments on the above proposals were received from different practitioners, including international brokerage firms, custodians, securities and banking industry representative bodies, and legal and professional associations. The overall tone of the comments was positive and commentators generally supported the proposed scripless model. Please see Appendix 2 of the Consultation Conclusions for the list of comments and SFC’s relevant responses.

The stakeholders on the project including HKEx, the Federation of Share Registrars, the SFC and the various working groups have been working closely on the project and will take these comments into consideration in refining the working model and the technical details.

The consultation conclusions have been posted on the SFC website and hard copies will be available at the SFC's offices.
