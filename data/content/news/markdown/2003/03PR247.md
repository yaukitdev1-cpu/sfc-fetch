The SFC is pleased that its Annual Report for the financial year 1 April 2002 to 31 March 2003 has been given the Diamond Award in the Public Sector/Not-for-Profit Organisations category of the Hong Kong Society of Accountants Best Corporate Governance Disclosure Awards 2003.

SFC Executive Director and Chief Operating Officer, Mr Peter Au Yang, made some brief remarks today at the HKSA's press conference to announce the awards. Enclosed please find Mr Au Yang's statement for your reference.

The Annual Report is entitled "New Regulatory Framework, Better Quality of Market; Enforce the Law, Protect Investors." It is available on the SFC website.

*** * * * * ***
Remarks by SFC Executive Director and Chief Operating Officer,
 Mr Peter Au Yang,
**at the HKSA Best Corporate Governance Disclosure Awards 2003**
**Result Announcement**


Ladies and Gentlemen,

The HKSA Best Corporate Governance Disclosure Award is a great honour to the Securities and Futures Commission. I thank the Hong Kong Society of Accountants and the panel of Judges.

I am very pleased today to represent the Commission at this press conference.

As a regulator in Hong Kong promoting, amongst other things, corporate governance here, it is obviously important that we practise what we preach.  Our corporate governance disclosure in our Annual Report is a follow through of our corporate governance practices.

My colleagues and I are sincerely grateful for the Judges’ comments on our Annual Report’s disclosure of corporate governance structure, the activities of the Board and Committees, and other matters. The Judges also note that the SFC seeks to engage stakeholders in the development of regulatory policies. In this connection, I am pleased to report that we have recently published our Consultation Procedures on the website. Although the SFC is only statutorily required to consult the public and the market on rules we propose to make under the Securities and Futures Ordinance, we have set higher standards for ourselves and consistently exceed the requirement by consulting on proposed non-statutory codes and guidelines as well.

Every year we aim to make improvements to the Annual Report, so that it will become more informative, relevant, and comprehensible to readers. In doing so, we bear in mind our readers include market practitioners and professionals, as well as the general investors. We are pleased that our efforts in producing an Annual Report that tells a simple yet complete story are appreciated by the HKSA panel of experts and professionals. We hope that the general investing public find the Annual Report as useful and interesting as the Judges find it worthy of the Award.

As an open, transparent and accountable financial market regulator, the SFC believes information must be not only accurate and easy to understand, but also timely. Our Annual Report is published within 45 days of the period end, and so are our quarterly financial reports.

We look forward to sharing with and learning from the market inspirations to further improve the standard of annual reports in Hong Kong in the years to come.

Thank you.

11 November 2003
