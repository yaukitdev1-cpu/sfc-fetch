The SFC today publishes the October issue of *SFC Enforcement Reporter*, summarising the SFC’s enforcement action in the month of September 2003 and discussing the compliance issues highlighted by recent cases.

In September, the SFC successfully prosecuted five people for breaking securities laws, including two persons who were fined for not showing up in SFC investigation interviews.

The SFC disciplined five entities during the month. A director of a securities margin financier had his approval as a director revoked and his representative licence suspended for 14 months, and a related securities firm was severely reprimanded for failings in handling trading records and conflicts of interest.

Since April 2003, the SFC has successfully prosecuted 33 entities and disciplined 44 licensees for various regulatory breaches.

Copies of the October issue of *SFC Enforcement Reporter* are available at the SFC's office. All issues are also available on the SFC website under "Press Releases, Publications & Speeches" – "Publications".

The public are welcome to subscribe to the newsletter by registering for the [SFC Website Update Email Alert service](https://www.sfc.hk/sfcUpdateAlert/jsp/EN/update_alert.jsp) on the SFC's homepage. They will then be able to receive the newsletter via email.
