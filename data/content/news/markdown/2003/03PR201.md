The SFC today publishes the September issue of *SFC Enforcement Reporter*, summarising the SFC’s enforcement action in the month of August 2003 and discussing the compliance issues highlighted by recent cases.

In August, the SFC successfully prosecuted three persons for breaking securities laws. One of them involved non-compliance with the Financial Resources Rules. Since April this year, the SFC has successfully prosecuted three people for providing false or misleading information to it and six people for breaches of the Financial Resources Rules.

In August, the SFC disciplined 12 intermediaries – a record monthly figure for 2003-04 so far. An account executive’s licence was suspended for one week for failure to inquire into suspicious trades. Four brokerages, some together with their management, were reprimanded for failing to put in place adequate internal controls or failing to manage and supervise properly. 

Since April 2003, the SFC has successfully prosecuted 28 entities and disciplined 39 licensees for various regulatory breaches.

Copies of the September issue of *SFC Enforcement Reporter* are available at the SFC's office. All issues are also available on the SFC website under “Press Releases, Publications & Speeches” – “Publications”.
