The SFC welcomes the appointments by the Financial Secretary of Mr Roger Luk and Mr Eddy Fong as new members of the SFC Advisory Committee and the re-appointments of nine existing members. All members will serve a two-year term beginning 1 June 2003.

Established under section 7 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance, the Advisory Committee’s membership also includes SFC Chairman and two Executive Directors appointed by the Commission. It advises the SFC on matters of policy regarding any of its regulatory objectives and functions.

SFC Chairman Mr Andrew Sheng welcomes all members. "I am confident that both Mr Luk and Mr Fong will bring their professional expertise and valuable experience in the financial services sector to the Advisory Committee. I am also very pleased to be able to continue working with all re-appointed members, whose insight and independent views have been guiding the SFC in its policy formulation," Mr Sheng said.

Mr Sheng also paid tribute to Mr Didier Balme, Mr Brett Goodin and Ms Christina Hui, who have retired from the Committee, for their contribution to the Advisory Committee and the SFC.
