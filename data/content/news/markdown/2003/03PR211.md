SFC Chairman, Mr Andrew Sheng, yesterday spoke on *The Stewardship of Corporations in 21st Century* at the "Agenda for the 21st Century Corporation - Revitalizing the Corporation" International Conference in Kuala Lumpur organised by the Malaysian Association for the Institute of Chartered Secretaries and Administrators.

In his speech, Mr Sheng stressed the stewards of companies had to abide by their fiduciary duties to their stakeholders and therefore the society they operated in. And at the heart of stewardship was public trust as a defining issue, he said.

The speech is available on the SFC website.
