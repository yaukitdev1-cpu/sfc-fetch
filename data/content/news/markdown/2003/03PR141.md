The SFC issues the following statement today on certain recent actions by the Independent Commission Against Corruption (ICAC):

The SFC has been assisting the ICAC in the investigation of certain listed companies and listed company personnel. It will continue that co-operation.

In addition, the SFC is also examining the disclosure, conduct and other aspects of certain transactions and activities involving those parties.

The SFC works closely with the ICAC and the Commercial Crime Bureau of the Police by providing expert evidence and other assistance where required. In the year ended 31 March 2003, the SFC referred 47 cases to the Police and ICAC for further investigation and action.

The SFC considers action against corporate misconduct as an enforcement priority in order to maintain and enhance the quality of the Hong Kong market. The reputation of the many could be damaged by the bad few.

The SFC is constrained by law from discussing individual cases or investigations. It is currently pursuing 49 investigations and inspections into corporate and market misconduct and various potential crimes under the SFO and its predecessor Ordinances. These involve companies, sponsors, brokers and other market participants. The SFC is liaising with other law enforcement agencies, in Hong Kong and the Mainland, on a number of these cases. In addition, there are a number of completed investigations that will proceed to court in the next few months.
