The SFC today launches a new series of investor education materials on bonds.   They aim to help retail investors understand bonds and the issues they should consider when dealing in bonds.  

Investor leaflet

A new leaflet entitled “Getting into Bonds” explains the basic features and risks of bonds, how to evaluate the merit of bonds, and where to get information.

Copies of the investor leaflets are available free of charge at the SFC office and the 11 Consumer Advice Centres of the Consumer Council.  Investors may also seek to obtain the leaflets through their brokers, who may call the SFC’s Investor Education and Communications Department at 2842 7711 to request for copies.

Feature articles

On-line education resources on bonds can also be found at the “Features” section of the SFC-operated Electronic Investor Resources Centre (eIRC) at www.hkeirc.org.

*Bonds ABC* explains the language of bonds. It also highlights the differences between direct investment in bond and buying units in a bond fund, and the different rights of a bondholder vis-à-vis a shareholder.

*Choosing a Bond* provides a step-by-step guide to investors on selection of bonds, starting from goal setting to comparing different bonds.

*Finding Information on Bonds* explains where investors can get the information about the features and terms and conditions of a bond, its latest quotes and yield figures.  
 
*Buying and Trading Bonds* discusses the various means of buying bonds, and issues regarding market liquidity, settlement and transaction costs in bond investment. 
 
The SFC’s Investor Hotline at 2840 9333 remains open to provide educational information on bonds.
