The Li Ka Shing Institute of Professional and Continuing Education (LiPACE) of The Open University of Hong Kong (OUHK), the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) and Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited (HKEx) have partnered to launch a series of investor education lectures for the public free of charge.

This is the first time ever that the OUHK, the SFC and HKEx have joined hands to offer investor education lectures.  The series is sponsored by the Wu Jieh Yee Charitable Foundation and supported by The Hong Kong Investment Funds Association (HKIFA), The Hong Kong Capital Market Association (HKCMA) and the Mandatory Provident Fund Schemes Authority (MPFA).

Entitled “Tai Ning Lectures – Investor Education Series”, the initiative aims to help the public cultivate a healthy attitude towards investing and learn more about investment analysis, risk management and different investment products.
