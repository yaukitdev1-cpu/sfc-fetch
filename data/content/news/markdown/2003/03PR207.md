The Financial Secretary has announced the re-appointment by the Chief Executive of Mr Andrew Sheng as the Chairman of the SFC for a period of two years, from 1 October 2003 to 30 September 2005. In accepting the re-appointment, Mr Sheng expressed his sincere thanks to the Chief Executive for the confidence shown in him and the recognition of his work. Mr Sheng also felt honoured to be able to continue to serve the community.

Mr Sheng said: "I am very grateful that the Chief Executive has re-appointed me to this position. This is a time of enormous opportunity for Hong Kong. The key issue we must address in building our reputation as an international financial centre is market quality. I look forward to working with the Government, Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited and the wider financial community towards that end."

"I am also grateful for the hard work, dedication and support of my colleagues over the years. Together with all staff of the Commission, we will continue to discharge the SFC’s regulatory duties in a fair, transparent and accountable manner," Mr Sheng said.
