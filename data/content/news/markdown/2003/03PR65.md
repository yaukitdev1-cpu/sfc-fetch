SFC’s Director of Corporate Finance, Mr Laurence Li, made a speech today on the practical issues and coordination between the SFC and Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited in administering the dual filing requirements under the Securities and Futures Ordinance. He was speaking at a seminar organised by the Chamber of Hong Kong Listed Companies Limited.  A copy of his [speech](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/speeches/03/ll030321_chklc.pdf) (Chinese Only) is attached and also available on the SFC website.
