The SFC issues the following update on the operation of the Dual Filing arrangements under the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

As reported previously (Note 1), the SFC received, via the Stock Exchange, a total of 42 applications for listing in the four months from March to June, and commented on 16 of these, again via the Exchange.

In seven cases the applicants improved their disclosure sufficiently and we did not comment further.

Of the other nine applicants, three did not respond to our comments, two withdrew their applications, and one lapsed. After consulting its Dual Filing Advisory Group, the SFC indicated to the Exchange its intention to object to listing applications in two cases because disclosure remained materially deficient. Only one of the 16 cases remains under review.

Of the 26 applications on which the SFC did not provide comments, 11 are now listed or have been approved for listing.

For the three months from July to September, the SFC received 28 applications and provided comments on 13 cases. Our average response time is seven working days.

The SFC issues this update as part of its effort to ensure transparency of the Dual Filing regime.

Mr Ashley Alder, SFC’s Executive Director of Corporate Finance, said: "In Dual Filing, our emphasis is on the quality of disclosure by listing applicants and listed companies. This is not merit regulation or 'picking winners'. The focus is on material and meaningful information disclosure to enable investors to make an informed decision on whether or not to invest. We will strive to work as transparently as possible and will continue to operate a constructive and reasoned approach to disclosure regulation to assist the market. I am pleased that the regime has been operating smoothly and effectively in the first six months."

"We have reported to our SFC Advisory Committee, the Shareholders’ Group, and the Dual Filing Advisory Group. Members have endorsed our approach as the right way to administer disclosure-based regulation. We are grateful for their support and further guidance," he said.
