SFC Chairman, Mr Andrew Sheng, today made a statement to the media. The following is the full statement:


 ***Remarks by SFC Chairman to the Media on the***

Publication of the Audit Committee Report on

Independent Investigation into Certain Anonymous Allegations




Good afternoon,

Thank you for coming at short notice.

I am pleased to inform you that the independent Audit Committee of the SFC has completed a report on certain anonymous allegations concerning the Commission’s personnel matters.  The Commission has decided that in the interest of transparency and accountability, the full report of the Audit Committee will be published.

Full findings and observations of the Audit Committee are available in the report, which will be made available after this session and will appear on our website shortly.

Most of you are probably familiar with the background. In July last year, the SFC received anonymous letters making allegations about certain personnel matters, primarily directed against me.  The Commission responded promptly and asked the Audit Committee to look into the matter. The Audit Committee was mandated to engage an independent person to assist it in the completion of its work if it felt the need and in due course PwC was appointed for this purpose.   I was not involved in the work of the independent inquiry and where appropriate did not take part in board meetings to avoid conflicts.

I am pleased that the independent Audit Committee, based on a review conducted by independent consultants and its own analysis, has not found any suggestion of impropriety or bad faith on my part.

In particular, the Audit Committee reports that it has found no evidence to support the allegation that I deliberately contrived with an executive to facilitate evasion of tax.

It was most unfortunate that the integrity and professionalism of some of the staff, present and past, were impugned in what appeared to be a personal attack against me.  This has been a difficult time for all staff of the Commission, and I am grateful for their understanding, support and patience during the inquiry. Because of the need to preserve the confidentiality and objectivity of the inquiry, we have not been able to discuss details with the public or the media until now.

I have always believed in a meritocracy, irrespective of age, gender, nationality, colour or religion. We believe in due process and procedures.

I welcome open and frank criticisms, but malicious and anonymous letters deserve contempt.  

As the report shows, staff morale has been affected primarily because of our determination to ensure that the SFC operates with efficiency under tight budget conditions in order to meet the expectations of the market.  We had to make difficult personnel decisions with which a few of those affected may  have disagreed.  But such tough management decisions have to be made from time to time.  As Chairman, I accept full responsibility for these decisions.  The SFC has accepted all the recommendations of the Audit Committee Report, which aim further to enhance the transparency and impartiality of the SFC’s internal systems and processes.  We are taking immediate action to implement these recommendations in full.  Work has in fact begun on some aspects of the proposals.

I am looking forward to the results of our search for a Chief Operating Officer. The COO will further strengthen the management of the Commission’s internal affairs, and prepare it for the major challenges brought by the Securities and Futures Ordinance which comes into force in a month’s time.

Hong Kong is going through a trying period, and so is the SFC. With this matter behind us, let us focus our energy and resources on how to improve the quality of the Hong Kong market and better protect investors.

I have been asked from time to time about my service with the Commission. My terms are determined by the Chief Executive of the Hong Kong SAR.  For as long as I am in my current position, I will do my very best to serve the public of Hong Kong.

Finally, I would like to thank the members of the Audit Committee – Mr Brian Stevenson, Ms Anna Wu and Mr Raymond Kwok – for completing an arduous task. The report has been submitted to the Financial Secretary.

Andrew Sheng

Chairman, SFC

28 February 2003
