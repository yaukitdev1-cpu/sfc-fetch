On 4 December 1997, a Magistrate at Western Magistracy ruled that the Securities and Futures Commission was time barred in bringing summons against Mr Fok Hiu-tong and New Chuan Kong Investments Limited (previously known as Jialing Finance Company Limited) for offences relating to unlicensed leveraged foreign exchange trading which were triable both on indictment and summarily. This decision was based on an interpretation of Section 67 of the Leveraged Foreign Exchange Trading Ordinance that the SFC could only bring summons in relation to any offences under the Ordinance within 3 years of its commission, or within 12 months of the first discovery thereof, whichever period expires first. The SFC appealed this decision by way of case stated.

In her decision today, Mrs Justice V Bokhary, a Judge of the Court of First Instance of the High Court, allowed the SFC appeal, ruling Section 67 of the Ordinance applies to offences triable only summarily.

Mr Mark Dickens, the Executive Director of the SFC's Enforcement Division, commenting on the ruling said this was an important decision affecting the operation of his division and was pleased with the result.
