The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it had publicly reprimanded {X}, who was registered as a securities dealer of Y.F. Chan Securities Limited (YFChan) under the Securities Ordinance (SO).

The SFC conducted an inquiry under section 56 of the SO which revealed that, during the period March 1998 to January 1999, {X} failed to properly discharge her function of managing and supervising the dealing business of YFChan and that such failure on her part resulted in YFChan’s:

1. repeated failures to maintain the level of liquid capital required by section 6(1) of the Financial Resources Rules and, in some cases, despite undertakings to the SFC to ensure compliance with section 6 of the Financial Resources Rules;
2. repeated failures to notify the SFC of falls in, and reasons for falls in, its liquid capital below the levels specified by section 19 of the Financial Resources Rules;
3. failure to have in place adequate internal control procedures to protect its operations from financial loss, thereby enabling a single director to authorize drawings to himself and to do so without recording clearly the nature and purpose of such drawings;
4. failure to conduct its business honestly and fairly, in that it window-dressed its financial position so that it could report to the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited that it had complied with section 6(1) of the Financial Resources Rules.


The SFC concluded that {X} was guilty of misconduct and that her fitness and properness had been impugned. However, as {X} had resigned as a dealing director before the issue of the SFC’s decision, and as the terms of her registration were such that it lapsed upon her ceasing to be a dealing director, the SFC accordingly decided to reprimand {X} under section 56(2) of the SO and to make the reprimand public. Had {X} remained a registrant, the SFC would have been minded to revoke her registration.
