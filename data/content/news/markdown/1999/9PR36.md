The Securities and Futures Commission announced that on 13 April 1999 it had successfully prosecuted {X} for short selling securities on the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited in May and June 1998.


{X} pleaded guilty before Ms Polly Lo at Western Magistracy to 2 summonses in relation to her short selling of securities in contravention of Section 80 of the Securities Ordinance. Section 80(1) of the Securities Ordinance prohibits the sale of securities when the seller does not have a presently exercisable and unconditional right to sell.


{X} was fined a total of $4,000 and was ordered to pay investigation costs of $5,558 to the SFC.
