The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) notes that the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited (SEHK) has issued a press release today to clarify the GEM Listing Rules with regard to the GEM moratorium requirements applicable to Management Shareholders. 

The GEM Listing Rules currently provide that initial management shareholders are subject to restrictions to dispose of their interest in GEM companies for two years after listing.

In considering whether the GEM moratorium period applicable to management shareholders ought to be reduced to six months, the Commission takes into account that one of the important elements in analysing a GEM investment is the identity of the key management and their commitment to the company. Public investors would expect the initial management shareholders to show commitment to the company. However, reducing the moratorium period to six months may allow initial management shareholders to sell out, even before the issue of the first annual report after listing.

The Commission notes that certain markets such as the Nasdaq do not impose any disposal restrictions on management shareholders of listed companies. There are differences in the two markets, especially taking into account the prevalence of civil actions made by investors against listed companies and their management in the US. For these reasons, the listing requirements in Hong Kong are not directly comparable with other markets.

Since the GEM is still a new market, there will be a period of adjustment of the regulatory framework before the full rules are finalized. Thus, under Rule 13.17(5) of the GEM Listing Rules, the SEHK has the power to exercise its discretion to waive the disposal restriction. In other words, the SEHK has the flexibility to shorten the two years moratorium period to six months or other period if it considers that it is desirable to do so. Such power is exercisable by the GEM Listing Committee. The sponsors and initial management shareholders would have to justify to the GEM Listing Committee their level of commitment to the company post-listing for any waiver to be exercised.
