The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today announces that, pursuant to the SFC (Fees) (Amendment) Rules 1999, a new application fee category will be introduced on 3 August 1999. Under the new category individuals advising on securities incidental to the marketing of Mandatory Provident Fund (MPF) Schemes only will be able to apply for registration as investment representatives under the Securities Ordinance at a reduced fee of $400 each. The $400 fee compares with the $1,850 fee applicable to a normal investment representative. There is no corresponding change to other categories of registration.

Investment representatives registered under the reduced fee category may only give advice incidental to the marketing of MPF schemes, and their registration will be valid for one year or until 14 days upon the commencement date for contribution to the MPF schemes (i.e. 1 December 2000 as it currently stands), whichever is earlier.

It is envisaged that a large number of individuals will participate in the promotion of MPF schemes from the beginning of year 2000 (when approval for individual MPF schemes will be granted by the MPF Schemes Authority) to December 2000 (when all employers and employees in Hong Kong are expected to start contribution into any of their selected approved MPF schemes). These intermediaries must be registered with the SFC if they wish to give advice on securities, such as the underlying unit trusts of a MPF scheme. In addition to meeting the fit and proper criteria otherwise applied to persons registered with the SFC, these individuals must pass a MPF intermediaries examination recognized by the MPF Schemes Authority before registration would be granted.

Persons interested in obtaining details of the registration requirements and procedures under this new fee category may refer to the booklet "Registration Guidelines For Intermediaries advising on securities incidental to the marketing of Mandatory Provident Fund Schemes only" distributed free of charge at the SFC Licensing Counter. Enquiries can also be made by calling the MPF Licensing Hotline at 2840-9595 or by e-mail to mpfenq@hksfc.org.hk.
