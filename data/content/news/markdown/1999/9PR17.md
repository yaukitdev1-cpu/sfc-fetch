The Securities and Futures Commission announced today that it had suspended the registration of Mr Fu Yuen-Hung (Fu) for 10 months as a registered securities dealer's representative under the Securities Ordinance for misconduct, with effect from 1 January 1999.

The action stemmed from an investigation in which the SFC found that from May to September 1996, Fu had:

(a)   traded through an account of a relative at Solid King Securities Limited (Solid King) in direct contravention of Solid King's policy that prohibited share dealings by its employees;

(b) used this account to conduct trades in a manner which advantaged himself over other clients of Solid King.
