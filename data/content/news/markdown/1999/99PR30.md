The Securities and Futures Commission welcomes the appointment by the Financial Secretary of Mr Paul Bailey as Executive Director of Enforcement.

SFC Acting Chairman Mrs Laura Cha said: “Mr Bailey's appointment is a recognition of his experience from his long years of service at the Commission. I am particularly pleased that the Financial Secretary has made the appointment from among the existing staff of the Commission."

"Mr Bailey has the respect of the staff as well as that of the market. He will continue to uphold the Commission's enforcement policy," she said.

Formerly with the Office of the Commissioner for Securities, Mr Bailey has been with the SFC since its inception in May 1989. He has been Senior Director of Enforcement since 1995.

Mr Bailey will succeed Mr Mark Dickens, who will replace Mr David White as head of the Supervision of Markets Division from 1 April 1999. The Financial Secretary has also extended Mr Dickens' appointment by 15 months to expire on 31 March 2002.

Mrs Cha said: "Mr Dickens has done an outstanding job in the two years as head of the Enforcement Division. Under Mr Dickens' stewardship of the division, the Commission has discharged its statutory duty of investigation without fear or favour and, as evidenced in several recent court decisions, acted entirely in accordance with the powers vested in it by the law."

"With the market reform moving rapidly forward, I believe Mr Dickens is the most suitable person to take on the greater responsibilities of the position. His extensive experience in the Commission will be particularly useful in implementing the various market reform programmes underway," she said.

Mr Dickens joined the Commission in March 1991. He was Senior Director of Corporate Finance before his appointment as Executive Director of Enforcement on 1 January 1997.

On behalf of the Commission, Mrs Cha also thanked Mr White for helping to ensure a smooth transition and wish him all the best in his future pursuits.
