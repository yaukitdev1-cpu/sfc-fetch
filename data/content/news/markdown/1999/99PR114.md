The Takeovers and Mergers Panel yesterday released its decision in relation to an application by Cobra Technologies Corp. (“Cobra”) for a review of the Executive’s ruling of 19 October 1999 that Cobra had triggered a mandatory general offer obligation under Rule 26 of the Code on Takeovers and Mergers as a result of Cobra’s acquisition of 10.25% of the shares of the Company and the rights, title and interest of UBS AG in certain financing and security documents.

The Panel decided in a meeting held on 5 November 1999 that the ruling of the Executive should be upheld and that Cobra is under an obligation to make a general offer for all the shares of the Company not already owned by Cobra or parties acting in concert with it. The general offer is to be made at a price of not less than HK$0.32 per share and comparable offers extended for any convertibles, warrants and options issued by the Company. The Panel also upheld the Executive’s ruling that Mr. Tse Sui Luen, a director and shareholder of the Company, and Cobra are acting in concert in respect of control of the Company.
