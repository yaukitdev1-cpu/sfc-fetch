The Securities and Futures Commission welcomes and fully supports the initiatives in the SAR Chief Executive’s Policy Address which would maintain and enhance Hong Kong’s status as an international financial centre through the provision of an appropriate economic and legal environment for an open, fair and efficient market.

SFC Chairman Mr Andrew Sheng said: “Maintaining an open, fair and efficient market is vital to strengthening the confidence of local and international investors in Hong Kong as an international financial centre. The SFC will work closely with the merged Exchange, the Administration, and the financial community to implement the various measures proposed in the Policy Address.”
