The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it had successfully prosecuted {X} for contravention of the Leveraged Foreign Exchange Trading Ordinance (LFETO).


{X} pleaded guilty before Ms Pang Ip Tsui-Yuk at Western Magistracy to one summons in relation to holding himself out as carrying on an unlicensed business of leveraged foreign exchange trading as an agent of a Macau-based company. He was fined $8,000 and ordered to pay costs of $11,132 to the SFC.


The SFC's investigation found that between 1 March 1997 and 20 May 1998 (the period), {X}, as an agent of Tse's International Investment (Macau) Limited (Tse's Macau), introduced clients to trade leveraged foreign exchange contracts through Tse's Macau and provided administration support to these clients' trading. During the period, {X} and Tse's Macau were not licensed with the SFC in any capacity.
