The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) and the Putonghua Channel of Radio Television Hong Kong (RTHK) have jointly organised a competition on investor education themes, which will begin on 18 October 1999.

The objective of this competition is to enhance the audience’s understanding of the securities regulations and market practices, and the vital role they must play to protect their interests when investing. There are attractive cash prizes for the winners, as well as book prizes for all entrants.

Contestants in the competition will guess a fictional investor’s choice in different life scenarios, which cover the account opening process, discovery of unauthorised transactions on account, margin and warrants trading, on-line dealing, conditional cash offers and short selling. Finalists in each round will also enter a quiz in order to win the grand prize. In addition, there is a slogan competition for the general audience.

Beginning Monday, 18 October 1999, the competition will air on RTHK’s Putonghua Channel at frequency AM621 and FM 100.9 (for Happy Valley residents), and on the Internet at its Website [http://www.rthk.org.hk](http://www.rthk.org.hk) between 9 p.m. and 9:30 p.m. during *Putonghua Newsline*. The competition will end on 12 November 1999.

This new investor education initiative follows a positive response to the various series of radio segments sponsored by the SFC for airing on RTHK’s Radio 1 (Cantonese) and Putonghua Channels in 1998 and the early part of 1999.

In another step to make further use of the Internet medium to explain to the investing public their rights and responsibilities within the current regulatory regime, the SFC has launched audio programmes on its Website. With the courtesy of the RTHK, the following radio series are now available on the SFC’s Website, located at [http://www.rthk.org.hk](http://www.rthk.org.hk):

- SFC Academy (Putonghua): A series of 20 three-minute episodes. Through the lively dialogue of a university student and his professor, it aims to cover the role and function of the SFC and self-regulatory organisations, advice to investors in situations like new share offerings and rights issues subscription, and matters to consider when dealing with brokers.
- SFC Theatre - The Careless-Lee Family (Putonghua): A series of eight 15-minute dramas to illustrate common problems experienced by investors. Each story has a different theme, covering different types of investment activities and products, such as stocks, funds, leveraged forex trading, investment-linked insurance plans and the use of services like discretionary accounts and margin accounts.
- SFC Theatre – Be a Smart Investor (Cantonese): A series of ten 15-minute segments, featuring a short drama based on issues that investors may encounter, followed by SFC’s advice on matters investors should consider when investing. Topics include short selling, insider dealing, risks of margin accounts and trading of derivative warrants, investment solicitation on the Internet.
