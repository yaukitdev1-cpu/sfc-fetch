The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that on the 31 August, 1999 it had successfully prosecuted {X} for acting as a dealer&rsquo;s representative of Tung Tai Securities Co., Limited (Tung Tai) without being registered.


{X} pleaded guilty before Ms Polly Lo at Western Magistracy. {X} was fined $3,000 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $21,000 to the SFC.


The SFC investigation revealed that during the period between 14 April 1998 and 15 August 1998, {X} introduced clients to Tung Tai, assisted his clients to complete the account opening documentation, received trading instructions from his clients and relayed the orders to the registered persons of Tung Tai for execution in return for remuneration from Tung Tai. During the investigation period, {X} was not registered with the SFC in any capacity.
