The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it had successfully prosecuted Wong Yin Ling (Wong) for short selling securities on the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited in February and March 1998.

Wong pleaded guilty before Ms Pang Ip Tsui-yuk at Western Magistracy to 18 summonses in relation to her short selling of securities in contravention of Section 80 of the Securities Ordinance. Section 80(1) of the Securities Ordinance prohibits the sale of securities when the seller does not have a presently exercisable and unconditional right to sell.

Wong was fined a total of $36,000 and was ordered to pay investigation costs of $22,913 to the SFC.
