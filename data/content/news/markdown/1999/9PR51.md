The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it had publicly reprimanded {X}, a registered dealer's representative of Grand Onward Securities Limited under the Securities Ordinance.

The reprimand stems from an investigation in which the SFC found that during October 1993 to January 1994,{X} has used his wife's account with Tai Shing Stock Investment Company (Tai Shing) as his own nominee account.

As a result of {X}'s failure to disclose his interest to Tai Shing, Tai Shing was unable to comply with Code 12.2 of the Code of Conduct for Persons Registered with the SFC, which required a broker to write to the other broker to seek its prior written consent before dealing in securities for that second broker's employee. In addition, it also resulted in Grand Onward being unable to monitor the trading of its employee.
