The Securities and Futures Commission announced that it had successfully prosecuted {X} for aiding and abetting an employee of C.A. Pacific Securities Limited to act as its unregistered representative during the period 1 October 1997 to 16 January 1998.


{X} was convicted after trial before Mr A.B.bin Wahab at Western Magistracy on 11 June 1999. He was fined $2,000 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $5,734 to the SFC.
