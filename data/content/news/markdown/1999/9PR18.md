The Securities and Futures Commission announced today that it had successfully prosecuted Cornucopia International Trading Inc (CITI), {X1} and {X2} for holding themselves out as investment advisers without being registered. The SFC had also successfully prosecuted CITI for holding itself out as carrying on a business of advising other persons concerning the purchase and sale of futures contracts without being registered. Both {X1} and {X2}, as directors of CITI, were also successfully prosecuted for consenting to or conniving at such aforesaid holding out by CITI.


The prosecutions stemmed from an investigation by the SFC which found that, from on or about 26 August 1996 to 8 August 1997, CITI (through {X1} and {X2}) had been soliciting customers for participation in an unregistered investment programme by the name of Kwong Fai 88 Investment Program (KF 88). KF 88 purported to invest in 44 different items of securities, currencies, precious metals, commodities and indices. The investigation also found that none of CITI, {X1}, {X2} and KF 88 were registered with the SFC in any capacity.


CITI, {X1} and {X2}pleaded guilty to a total of 6 summonses before Mr. Y.T. Chan at South Kowloon Magistracy on 22 February 1999. They were fined $6,000 each, and ordered to pay costs totalling $21,000 to the SFC.
