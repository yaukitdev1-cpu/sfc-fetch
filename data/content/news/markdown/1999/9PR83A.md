The SFC announced that it had successfully prosecuted {X}, the Managing Director and Dealing Director of Ever-Long Securities Company Limited (Ever-Long) for aiding and abetting two former staff for acting as dealer representatives of Ever-Long whilst unregistered.


{X} pleaded guilty to 2 summonses before Mrs Pang Ip Tsui Yuk at Western Magistracy on 10 August 1999. {X} was fined $5,000 and ordered to pay costs $10,918 to the SFC for each summons.


An investigation by the SFC found that between 27 August 1997 and 20 February 1998, Ever-Long remunerated one staff for handling clients' orders, and the other for recruiting clients and handling clients' orders. {X} was aware that both were not registered with the SFC in any capacity during the time of their activities.
