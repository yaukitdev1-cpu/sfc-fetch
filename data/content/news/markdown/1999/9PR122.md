The Securities and Futures Commission announced today that it had publicly reprimanded {X}, a dealer's representative of Phillip Securities (HK) Limited (Phillip Securities), for issuing a web page on the internet aimed at recruiting persons to introduce clients to him to deal in securities with a view to increasing his client base and therefore his commission payments. These "introducing agents' were offered remuneration for such services. 

The web page was issued without the knowledge or consent of Phillip Securities and was deleted before {X} was able to recruit any introducing agents.

{X}'s actions could have resulted in a breach by him, and the persons he intended to recruit, of the registration provisions of the Securities Ordinance. The Commission considers this conduct as adversely affecting {X}'s fitness and properness resulting in the public reprimand. The decision took into account the fact that no introducing agents were recruited, {X}'s immediate action to cancel the web page and {X}'s cooperation in the inquiry.

Commenting on this action, Mr Paul Bailey, Executive Director of Enforcement, said "This is the first disciplinary case by the Commission relating to the Internet. It is illegal in Hong Kong for a person not registered with the Commission to receive remuneration for passing orders to deal in securities from a third party to a registered dealer or dealer's representative. Therefore, members of the public are advised to take care when seeking employment in the securities industry through the internet, or through any other medium, to ensure that they become properly registered with the Commission. If members of the public require advice as to what activities may require registration, they may telephone the Commission’s hotline 2840 9333 for advice. The public are also welcome to report any suspicious advertisements, web sites, or chatrooms pertaining to the securities and futures market in Hong Kong on the internet to the hotline. All such reports will be treated with the utmost confidence."
