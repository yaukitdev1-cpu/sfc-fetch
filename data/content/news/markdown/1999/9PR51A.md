The SFC announced today that it had publicly reprimanded {X}, who is registered as a securities dealer's representative under the Securities Ordinance (SO). She was accredited to Amsteel Securities (H.K.) Limited during the period 15 August 1996 to 17 May 1998 (the relevant period).

The SFC conducted an inquiry under section 56 of the SO which revealed that during the relevant period, {X} had failed both to disclose her interest in a client's accounts to her then employer and to notify the SFC of changes in personal circumstances on a timely basis.

On this basis the SFC concluded that {X}'s fitness and properness as a registered person has been impugned.
