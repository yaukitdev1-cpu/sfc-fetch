The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) is working closely with The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Ltd (SEHK) and The Hong Kong Futures Exchange Ltd (HKFE) to ensure the smooth transfer of regulatory responsibilities for the 700 members of the two Exchanges. The transfer follows the successful vote for merger in September 1999.

The SFC, Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Ltd (HKEC), the SEHK and HKFE are committed to ensuring that there will be no reduction in investor protection and no increase in the regulatory burden on intermediaries, during and after the transitional period.

SFC takes on increased regulatory responsibilities as a result of the transfer of front line regulatory responsibility from SEHK and HKFE. To ensure adequate investor protection, there will be a need for the SFC to increase its staff to cope with the additional work. It is currently estimated that approximately 50 new staff will be recruited in the course of the next 12 months as the transfer of responsibilities takes place and in the light of market development. The SFC recognizes the depth of talent and experience present in the staff currently employed with the two Exchanges and will focus its recruitment efforts on those staff.

A circular for recruitment of staff is today posted on the bulletin boards of the two Exchanges. All Exchange staff are welcome to apply for appropriate posts ranging from Senior Manager grade to Assistant Manager grade.

The SFC and the Senior Executives of the two Exchanges are also discussing a secondment program from the Exchanges to the SFC. Secondments would complement the recruitment program to assist in the smooth transfer of regulatory responsibilities.
