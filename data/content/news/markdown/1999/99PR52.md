The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today launches a new publicity campaign which comprises a TV Announcement in the Public Interest (API), in English and Chinese, a Cantonese radio API and a bi-lingual poster. 

This campaign is part of the SFC’s overall efforts to educate investors on their vital role of protecting themselves – that they should understand their investment and the operation of the markets when investing.

In this campaign, different sports are employed as the analogy to communicate the message that ‘learning the rules of the game’ is just as important in investing as in sports.

The TV API, in English and Chinese, features an energy-charged substitute player dashing in rain for a heroic rugby "try", only to discover, from the shocked silence of the rowdy crowd, that he is in a soccer game. Beginning 1 June 1999, this TV API will be screened on both the Chinese and English channels of TVB and ATV, and on all Cable TV channels.

In the radio API, a boxing player loses the match even when his opponent is down, simply because the protagonist has violated the rules of the game by fighting with Thai boxing kicks. The voice talent is a professional sport commentator to add amusement to this radio announcement. The radio API will be aired on local radio stations, also starting 1 June 1999.

The bi-lingual poster, also along the same creative platform, shows a table tennis player photographed mid-serve. However, rather than a table-tennis ball, he has tossed up a badminton shuttlecock. These posters will be displayed in Government’s poster boxes, on public notice boards of libraries, district offices, universities, public and private property developments and public transportation systems.
