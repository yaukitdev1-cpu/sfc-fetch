The Securities and Futures Commission announced today that it had revoked the registration of Mr Leung Wai Ting, Demon (Leung) in respect of his registration as a dealer's representative under the Securities Ordinance.

The revocation stems from an inquiry in which the SFC found that Leung had, whilst a dealer's representative of Ng Yat Hon trading as Ling Co, misappropriated approximately $3 million that his clients had given him to purchase shares on their behalf. It was also found that Leung had fabricated statements of accounts and issued them to clients so as to mislead the clients as to the state of their accounts.

An SFC spokesman said that the gross abuse of trust warranted the revocation of Leung's registration.
