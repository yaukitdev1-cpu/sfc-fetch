The Securities and Futures Commission today published a Guidance Note on Internet Regulation to clarify its regulatory approach on various Internet activities under the existing legislation.

The SFC considers that the fundamental principles of regulation are not premised on the use of a particular medium of communication or delivery, whether it is paper-based or electronic.

For example, the SFC's licensing requirements are applicable to all securities, futures and leveraged foreign exchange dealing, trading and advisory businesses in Hong Kong, regardless of the communication or delivery medium used.

The SFC expects that the use of Internet will have a significant impact on the development of the businesses and encourages the legitimate use of the Internet.

As a general principle, the SFC will not seek to regulate Internet activities that originate outside Hong Kong, provided that such activities are not detrimental to the interests of the investing public in Hong Kong.

In addition to compliance with the registration requirements and applicable SFC codes and guidelines, the SFC expects registered persons to put in place additional operational measures for conducting businesses over the Internet:

- Computer systems must have sufficient operational integrity, and address issues of security, reliability, capacity and contingency;
- Suitably qualified personnel and adequate operational resources to handle Internet communications and transactions;
- Written procedures, including those to handle contingency situations;
- Provide clients with adequate information about Internet transactions, including risks involved;
- Records of all communications including e-mail and transactions are created and maintained without being susceptible to manipulation, and accessible to the SFC upon request.
Similarly, the issue of advertisements or documents on securities, investment arrangements and advisory services is subject to SFC regulation irrespective of the medium used. The SFC, however, will not seek to apply the regulatory requirements to activities that are not targeted at people residing in Hong Kong.

The Guidance Note lists the factors in considering whether an activity is targeted at people residing in Hong Kong. For example, the use of “push? technology such as spamming to a particular group of persons whom the service provider knows reside in Hong Kong may trigger regulatory concern.

In respect of offers of securities or investment arrangements, the SFC will generally permit the distribution of electronic prospectuses provided that the relevant legislative requirements have been met. Paper copies should be prepared and made available to investors as well.

The SFC considers that issuers should at present accept only paper application forms for subscription of shares and debentures. An intermediary or fund manager should only accept electronic application for collective investment schemes from investors who already maintain an account with him.

The Guidance Note also states that the SFC will apply the general anti-fraud and anti-manipulation provisions of the relevant ordinances in its enforcement actions over Internet activities.

The SFC published the Guidance Note after consultation with certain interested parties including the exchanges, broker associations, and professional bodies. It does not have the force of law but failure to follow may reflect adversely on the fitness and properness of registered persons.

The SFC may update the Guidance Note in accordance with the upcoming composite Securities and Futures Bill and in light of development of Internet technology.

The SFC would also like to take this opportunity to remind investors that there are certain risks associated with Internet trading. The Investor Education section in the SFC Website currently provides some advice on dealing with investment solicitation and trading on the Internet. Further efforts will be made to expand the section and educate the investors through other channels.

The Guidance Note is available free of charge at the SFC. It can also be found on the SFC Website at http://www.hksfc.org.hk.
