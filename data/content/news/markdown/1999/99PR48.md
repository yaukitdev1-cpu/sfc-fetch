The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today issues the SFC Code on MPF Products which sets out its requirements for authorization of MPF products under the Mandatory Provident Fund system (MPF system). 

Under the current legislative framework, the Mandatory Provident Fund Authority (MPFA) is the primary regulator for the MPF system. It is responsible for the overall administration of the MPF system including the regulation of master trust schemes, industry schemes and pooled investment funds. The SFC is involved in the regulation of these products only to the extent that their offering documents and marketing materials are required to be authorized by the SFC prior to their issue or publication in Hong Kong. In addition, the investment managers of these products are also required to be approved by the SFC.

The English version of the Code can be found on the SFC Website at http://www. hksfc.org.hk . Printed copies of the Code, including the Chinese version, are expected to be available on sale in the next few weeks at the SFC office at 12/F, Edinburgh Tower, The Landmark, Central, Hong Kong.
