The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today (Monday, 5 July 1999) issued an Overview Guide to the proposed composite Securities and Futures Bill. This publication is intended as an aid to explain the legislative reform, which is in turn an integral part of the Financial Secretary's three-pronged initiatives to reform the securities and futures markets. The Government intends to introduce the Bill in the Legislative Council by the end of 1999.

"The Bill is intended to provide a thoroughly efficient and modern securities and future regulatory regime, under which our financial services industry can remain competitive in the globalised market," said Mrs Laura Cha, Acting Chairman of the SFC.

The Guide principally provides an overview of the proposed law and sets out the rationale for the new legislation and what it intends to achieve. In addition, the SFC has also made available on its website at <http: // www . hksfc.org.hk> a series of guides on the major proposals in the draft bill to supplement the Overview Guide. These Guides cover individual areas such as the objectives, powers and functions of the Commission, the proposed Market Misconduct Tribunal, a streamlined licensing regime for market intermediaries, regulation of electronic trading, etc.

"We very much welcome the comments of market participants on the issues raised in the Overview Guide and any of the individual Guides. We will continue to work closely with the market to ensure that the regulatory regime of the securities and futures markets in Hong Kong retain its world-class standards and standing," added Mrs Cha.

Comments on the issues raised in the Overview Guide or the individual Guides should be sent to The Commission Secretary, Securities and Futures Commission, 12/F, Edinburgh Tower, The Landmark, Central, Hong Kong by 6 August 1999.
