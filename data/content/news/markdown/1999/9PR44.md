The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it had publicly reprimanded {X}, who is registered as a securities dealer of Mayfair Securities Limited under the Securities Ordinance (SO).

The SFC conducted an inquiry under section 56 of the SO which revealed that during September 1996 to April 1997, {X} had breached Clause 12.2 of the Commission's Code of Conduct in that he had knowingly dealt in securities for an employee of another registered person without having first sought the written consent of that other registered person.

A spokesman of the SFC said that the above matters impugned {X}'s fitness and properness as a registered person.
