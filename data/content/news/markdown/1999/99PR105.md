China Securities Regulatory Commission (CSRC) and Hong Kong Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) signed here today the Letters of Exchange regarding regulatory co-operation between CSRC and SFC for Mainland companies which will be listed on the Growth Enterprise Market (GEM) of the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited (SEHK).

Mr. Andrew Sheng, Chairman of SFC, said at the signing ceremony that “SFC attaches great importance to GEM as a financing market for growth enterprises in the Mainland and fully recognises the need for mutual consultation and assistance with CSRC as a means of improving effectiveness in administering and enforcing our respective relevant laws, regulations and rules with regard to the GEM. The signing of the Letters of Exchange between CSRC and SFC today has provided the regulatory framework governing issues related to Mainland enterprises to be listed on GEM. SFC is committed to creating an efficient, fair and transparent market for GEM and enhancing investor confidence.”

Mr. Zhou Zhengqing, Chairman of CSRC, said on the same occasion that “CSRC supports the establishment and the development of GEM. On 13th October, CSRC published the “Guidelines on the Vetting and Regulation of Applications for Listing on the GEM in Hong Kong by Mainland Enterprises”, which has been approved by the State Council. The Guidelines contains simplified provisions aimed at enhancing the efficiency in CSRC’s vetting and regulation of such applications. The signing of the Letters of Exchange regarding GEM today is conducive to further strengthening the regulatory co-operation between CSRC and SFC, to promoting the healthy development of GEM in Hong Kong, and to promoting the operations in accordance to relevant rules and regulations by domestically listed companies in China.”

CSRC and SFC are parties to the Memorandum of Regulatory Co-operation (MORC) signed on 19th June 1993, which provides the framework for regulatory co-operation in the securities sector between Hong Kong and the Mainland. In the Letters of Exchange signed today, CSRC and SFC reaffirmed all the relevant provisions of the MORC and provided for further steps specifically related to GEM as supplementary arrangements between CSRC and SFC within the MORC framework. Such steps include providing assistance in terms of supervision, inspection and investigation required for the enforcement of the regulation of the GEM. Such assistance may include investigation of interests in the securities of Mainland GEM Issuers and supervision and inspection of sponsors, financial advisers and other intermediaries.

GEM is the newly established market by the SEHK catering for small and medium-sized growth companies which may not have any profit record. On 15th September 1999, SEHK announced the list of approved GEM sponsors, which includes 28 sponsors and 2 co-sponsors. It is understood that a number of applications for listing on GEM have already been received and are currently under review.
