The Securities Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it had publicly reprimanded {X}, who was registered as a securities dealer's representative of Prudential-Bache Securities (HK) Limited under the Securities Ordinance (SO).

The SFC conducted an inquiry under S.56 of the SO which revealed that during December 1997 to July 1998, {X} had engaged in short selling, and on 9 March 1999 he was fined $38,000 in Western Magistracy in respect of 19 representative summonses.

On this basis, the SFC concluded that {X} had been guilty of misconduct and his fitness and properness as a registered person has been impugned.
