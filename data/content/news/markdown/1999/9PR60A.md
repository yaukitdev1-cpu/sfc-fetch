The SFC announced today that it had publicly reprimanded {X}, who is registered as a securities dealer’s representative of Lau Shing Chiu trading as Tai Fung & Co under the Securities Ordinance (SO).

The reprimand stemmed from an inquiry conducted by the SFC under Section 56 of the SO in which it was found that {X}:

(a) had allowed a client’s account to be operated by a third party without proper authorisation; and

(b) when executing a sale of securities on 28 August 1998, had failed to ensure that a client had sufficient shares to settle the sale prior to the execution of the sale order resulting in a settlement failure.

A spokesman of the SFC said that {X}’s conduct impugned his fitness and properness as a registered person and warranted a public reprimand.
