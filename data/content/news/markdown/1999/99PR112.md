The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced the gazetting today of amendments to its Code of Conduct for Persons Registered with the SFC (Code). 

A new paragraph 3 is added to Schedule 1 of the Code. The amendments require registered persons to ask their clients to sign a separate risk disclosure statement in relation to securities traded on the Growth Enterprise Market (GEM) before they trade for them. GEM will soon be launched by the SEHK. The requirement for a separate risk disclosure statement is to promote the awareness of investors to differentiate the securities traded on GEM from those of the Main Board, and in particular highlighting the possibility of higher risk nature of the securities in GEM and the special features of the operation of GEM, such as the principal means of information dissemination on GEM will be publication on the internet website operated by SEHK.

Copies of the Code are available for sale at the SFC counter at 12/F., Edinburgh Tower, The Landmark, 15 Queen’s Road Central, Hong Kong at $20 each. The Code is also available from the SFC website ([http://www.hksfc.org.hk](http://www.hksfc.org.hk)).
