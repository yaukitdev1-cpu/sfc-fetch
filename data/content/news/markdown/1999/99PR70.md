The Securities and Futures Commission welcomes today's announcement by the Government of the proposed supervisory framework for the new Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited.

 

Striking the right balance of regulatory responsibilities between SFC and the new Exchange and ensuring effective regulation are vital parts of the proposals. The SFC will continue to work closely with SEHK, HKFE and HKSCC on all aspects of the proposed merger, particularly in the area of regulatory responsibilities. We shall ensure that arrangements are quickly put in place to transfer responsibilities where appropriate and to avoid any uncertainty or regulatory gaps.

 

The streamlined regulatory framework announced by the Government today will take several months to put in place, with full implementation expected by mid-2000. There will be close consultation with the Exchanges and Clearing Houses and the markets during the implementation phase.

 

The SFC recognises the importance of the continued smooth operation of the markets during the transitional period and the significant role to be played in that regard by the staff of SEHK, HKFE and HKSCC.

 

The SFC appreciates the significant experience and understanding of regulatory issues held by staff at the Exchanges and Clearing Houses. The SFC will work closely with the management of both Exchanges and Clearing Houses to organise the transition of the relevant regulatory functions, and to ensure that their staff are kept well informed of developments.
