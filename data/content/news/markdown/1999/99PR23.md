In his Budget Speech today, The Financial Secretary, Mr Donald Tsang, announced the Government's intention that The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong (SEHK) and the Hong Kong Futures Exchange (HKFE) and their associated clearing houses should demutualise and merge and that a resulting holding company would be formed and thereafter list on the Stock Exchange.

The Securities and Futures Commission fully supports the Government in this major reform of Hong Kong's securities and futures markets. Mr Andrew Sheng, SFC Chairman, said, "In the face of global competition, we need to consolidate and upgrade our competitive capacity. The SFC believes that through implementation of this reform Hong Kong's market infrastructure will be well placed to meet the competitive challenges of the new millenium."

The SFC has advised the Government in preparing for this important reform of Hong Kong's securities and futures market. The SFC will discharge its statutory responsibilities by continuing to be closely involved in its implementation. In overseeing the implementation of the demutualisations of the Exchanges and the listing of NewCo, the SFC will seek to ensure that the existing market structure continues to operate effectively during the period of transition, that the procedures to implement reform are fair and transparent and conducted in accordance with all applicable regulatory requirements, and that the new market structure fully reflects the important public role to be performed by NewCo. The SFC will also discuss with the Government the legislative changes required to implement the reforms and any proposed rationalisation of the regulatory function between NewCo and the SFC under the new market structure.

The SFC also welcomes the establishment of the Steering Committee on the Enhancement of the Financial Infrastructure in Hong Kong and the appointment by The Financial Secretary of Mr Andrew Sheng as its Chairman. The Steering Committee is tasked to identify and examine the key issues in implementing the proposals to set up a single clearing arrangement for stocks, futures and options, to enhance the financial technology architecture to facilitate straight through processing and to move towards a secure, scripless securities market. Action plans on the above will have to be drawn up and concrete recommendations will be submitted to the Financial Secretary by September 1999.

The SFC looks forward to working closely with the Government, SEHK, HKFE, Hong Kong Securities Clearing Company and the securities community in ensuring that the major reforms announced today are successfully carried through and that Hong Kong's securities and futures markets move forward into the new millenium in a position of strength and with a renewed sense of purpose.
