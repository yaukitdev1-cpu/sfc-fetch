The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it had publicly reprimanded {X}, who is registered as a securities dealer's representative of Corporate Brokers Limited (CBL) under the Securities Ordinance (SO).

The SFC conducted an inquiry under section 56 of the SO which revealed that during June 1995 to January 1997 {X} had :

a) facilitated a breach of Code 12.2 of the Commission's "Code of Conduct for Persons Registered with the Securities and Futures Commission" in that he knowingly dealt in securities for an employee of another registered person without having taken steps to secure the written consent of that other registered person; and

b) failed to make arrangements for an oral authorisation given by a client to be reduced into writing thereby exposing his employer to financial risk.

On this basis the SFC concluded that {X}'s fitness and properness as a registered person has been impugned.
