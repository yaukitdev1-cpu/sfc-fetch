The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) congratulates the Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited (HKEC) on the approval for the merger from the members of the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong and the Hong Kong Futures Exchange.

"Today will be remembered as one of the most significant milestones in the development of Hong Kong's securities and futures markets. Members of both exchanges have demonstrated that they are ready to face the challenges in unity and to take advantage of the opportunities in the new Millennium," said the Chairman of SFC, Mr Andrew Sheng.

In the last few months, the SFC has worked closely with the Government, the two Exchanges and HKSCC, as well as the professional advisers on regulatory and structural issues, both to address members' concerns and to prepare for implementing the merger upon members' affirmative vote. Mr Sheng said, "the process has demonstrated the unity of purpose and collective efforts by all parties in carrying out the market reform to improve Hong Kong's overall competitiveness and to consolidate Hong Kong's premier status as an international financial centre."

He added that with the merger, Hong Kong would be even better positioned to play its role as the major fund-raising and risk management center for the Mainland.

The SFC also sends its congratulations to the respective Chairmen and Council/Board members of the Exchanges and Clearing Houses, the HKEC, their management and staff, the Financial Services Bureau and Department of Justice, and all who worked so hard to achieve this merger. "This is a great day for the Hong Kong securities and futures industry," remarked Mr Sheng.
