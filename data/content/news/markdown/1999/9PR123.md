The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it had publicly reprimanded {X}, who during the period between December 1995 and August 1998 (the relevant period) was registered as a dealer and investment adviser of Core Pacific Securities International Limited (Core Pacific) under the Securities Ordinance. As {X} had resigned from Core Pacific the terms of his registration resulted in him no longer being registered with the SFC.

The action arose from an inquiry that found that during the relevant period, {X}

a. executed unauthorised trades in a client’s account;
b. allowed clients to trade in certain shares and warrants in circumstances that suggested the integrity of the market was being compromised without making inquiry as to the propriety of such trades; and
c. failed to obtain a written authorisation from a client in order to operate the account on a discretionary basis.


The SFC concluded that {X} was guilty of misconduct and that his fitness and properness had been impugned. The SFC accordingly decided to reprimand {X} under section 56(2) of the SO and to make the reprimand public.
