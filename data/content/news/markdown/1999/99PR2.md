The Securities and Futures Commission today announced three new additions to its website:

- a series of eight investor education stories recently published in a leading Chinese language language daily;
- a searchable database of changes to the status of licensed persons and authorised investment products in the most recent month; and
- the full text of the Codes on Takeovers and Mergers and Share Repurchases.
The investor education series is a collection of eight articles containing fictional accounts of investor experiences with different financial intermediaries, investment products and scenarios. Each article also contains "SFC Comment" on the issues investors should consider when investing in similar circumstances. The stories enhance the website’s existing Investor Education section, which contains a set of six independent guides to investing, Frequently Asked Questions on specific issues, and a previously published series of stories in the same paper.

The searchable database of monthly changes to the status of licensed persons and authorised investment products enhances the Registry database introduced on the SFC website earlier this year. The Registry includes a database of all licensed persons and authorised investment products, and is updated monthly.

The addition to the website of the Codes on Takeovers and Mergers and Share Repurchases provides a useful resource to corporate finance and other market practitioners. The website also contains the following codes:

- Code on Unit Trust and Mutual Funds
- Code on Pooled Retirement Funds
- Code on Investment-linked Assurance Schemes
- Code of Conduct for Persons Registered with the SFC
- Fund Manager Code of Conduct
In addition to this new content, a wealth of other information can be found on the SFC website, including a dedicated section on the Year 2000 computer problem, press releases, speeches, consultation papers, investor education materials, licensing information, and links to other websites. The site currently receives over 8,000 hits per day.

The SFC website is located at [http://www.hksfc.org.hk](http://www.sfc.hk/).
