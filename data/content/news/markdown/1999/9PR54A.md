The SFC announced today that it had publicly reprimanded {X}, a registered securities dealer's representative under the Securities Ordinance (SO).

The reprimand stemmed from an inquiry under section 56 of the SO in which it was found that during the period April to November 1998, {X} had:

(a) knowingly allowed a nominee account to be opened with his principal, thereby exposing his principal to risk has there been any dispute in regards to trades booked to that account; and

(b) failed to ensure a client had sufficient shares to settle her sales orders prior to their execution.

A spokesman of the SFC said that the above matters impugned {X}'s fitness and properness as a registered person.
