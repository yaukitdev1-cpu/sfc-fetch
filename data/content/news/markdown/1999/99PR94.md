The Securities and Futures Commission has today issued a consultation paper on a review of the Hong Kong Code on Takeovers and Mergers. The public is invited to comment on the proposed amendments set out in the paper.

The Code was first introduced in 1975, and was thoroughly reviewed between 1989 and 1991, resulting in the 1992 version. The Code was again reviewed in 1997 and a consultation paper was issued in February 1998 resulting in the current version being published in August 1998. The policy of the SFC is to keep the Code under regular review.

This review considered a number of issues raised by public respondents during the consultation in 1997 and issues that have arisen from the day-to-day administration of the Code. The review also took into account relevant amendments to the City Code on Takeovers and Mergers made by the Takeover Panel in London in 1998.

The review has been carried out by the Special Adviser to the SFC, Mr Christopher de Boer, former Chairman of the Takeovers and Mergers Panel in Hong Kong, in conjunction with the Corporate Finance Division of the SFC. The proposed amendments have been endorsed by the Takeovers Panel and the Commission.

Mr. David PR Stannard, Executive Director of Corporate Finance Division, said "This is part of the continuing review aims to refine the Code to keep up with the market. Public comments collected during the last consultation have been useful to reflect market and commercial viewpoints. The SFC intends to conduct similar reviews on a regular basis."

Some of the principal areas reviewed and changes recommended are as follows:

Timing of posting of the offeree board circular and the minimum period for an offer to remain open

Rules 8 and 15 will be amended so that the 14 day period in which the offeree company must post its circular may only be extended if the offeror agrees and that the first closing date will remain day 21 if the offer document and offeree board circular are posted on the same day, or combined in a composite document, or day 28 if the offeree board circular is posted later.

*Technical issue arising from the revision or extension of an offer resolved*

Under the amended Rules 15 and 19, offers must be closed by 9:30 am on a closing date and must contain a condition that acceptances must be received by 4 pm on the preceding day. On the first closing dates mentioned above the offers must close by 9:30 am on days 22 or 29 and acceptances must be received by 4 pm on days 21 and 28 respectively.

*Statement of material change in a company’s financial or trading position needs to be reported on*

Under the amended Rule 10, a statement of material change in a company’s financial or trading position needs to be reported on by the auditor and financial adviser and, in respect of the negative statement required to be made, the directors of the company must provide evidence to the Executive of the steps they have taken to support that statement.

*Cash offer or cash alternative at the highest price paid during offer period must be made under a non-cash offer*

Under the amended Rule 23, if an offeror purchases shares for cash at any price (not just at above the offer price) during the offer period, it must provide a cash offer or cash alternative at the highest price during the offer period.

*Significant asset or profit test under the chain principle clarified*

It is proposed that the significant asset or profit test be 60% of the relative value of the companies in a chain.

*Disclosure under Rule 33 is no longer required*

As there is duplication between Securities (Disclosure of Interests) Ordinance and Rule 33, the Panel has proposed to delete Rule 33.
