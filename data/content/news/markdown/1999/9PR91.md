The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced that on 13 September 1999 it had successfully prosecuted {X1}, {X2}, {X3} and {X4}, who are presently or were previously employed by TMT Financial Services Limited, for the unauthorised issue of promotional documents in relation to certain investment arrangements which invited members of the public to subscribe for unit-linked insurance policies offered by a scheme provider and which guaranteed returns.


{X1} and {X2} were fined a total of $7,000 by Mr. H. P. Chau at Western Magistracy in relation to four summonses for breaching Section 4 of the Protection of Investors Ordinance (PIO). {X3} and {X4} were fined a total of $6,000 in relation to two summonses for breaching the same section. Section 4 of the PIO prohibits any person from issuing an advertisement inviting the public to participate in an investment arrangement unless previously authorised by the SFC. The four defendants were ordered to pay costs totalling $1,500 to the SFC.


An investigation launched by the SFC found that the above four defendants had distributed flyers that invited members of the public to participate in certain investment arrangements. They had placed the flyers in letterboxes of the residents in the Discovery Bay area and had also faxed the information to potential clients. {X1} and {X2} had additionally distributed the promotional materials to SFC officers responding to the flyers campaign. These flyers and promotional materials had not been authorised by the SFC.
