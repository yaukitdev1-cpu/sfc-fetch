As part of its proactive Investor Education programme, the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has sponsored the Exploration Theatre Company to produce an interactive drama entitled “Don’t guess when you invest!” The drama will be performed at 50 secondary schools.

The focus of this education campaign, which targets students in the upper forms, is to foster a correct attitude in investing among future investors by promoting understanding of the risks and rewards of the financial markets.

Interactive drama for schools emphasises interaction between the stage performers and the students. By combining an investment game with performance of situation dramas, the activity aims to illustrate to the students issues that people should consider when deciding to invest, and the common mistakes made by novice investors in safeguarding their interests. After each drama, an SFC representative will also conduct a brief Q & A session.

The school tour has started on 13 September 1999 and will run until 22 October 1999. Each drama will be watched by 400 to 600 upper form students.

“Launching preventive education in schools is an effective way to improve financial literacy of Hong Kong people. This interactive drama campaign is the latest in a variety of investor education initiatives by the SFC. The Commission will further enhance its investor education programme in the months ahead through all available media including radio, television, print and the Internet,” an SFC spokesman said.

Note to Editor:

Exploration Theatre Company was founded in June 1982. From 1989 onwards, it has become a professional theatre company supported by the Hong Kong Arts Development Council (previously the Council For Performing Arts). The Chairman of the Board is Mr. Chung King Fai, and the Artistic Director is Mr Karley Ng.

Apart from regular public performances, Exploration Theatre Company also conducts school touring projects in primary and secondary schools for different government organisations on subjects related to the daily lives of the students. Interactive dramas were introduced by Exploration Theatre Company in 1991.

Two photographs taken during the debut performance at one of the schools on 13 September 1999 have been boxed at the Government Information Services Department for collection.
