The Securities and Futures Commission announces that payments are being made out from the Unified Exchange Compensation Fund to 58 clients of Foreground Securities Co. Ltd. (Foreground) who suffered pecuniary losses as a result of the default of Foreground in March 1998 and whose claims were allowed or partially allowed by the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited. The Stock Exchange has allowed their claims to the extent of $25,865,002.76. The Stock Exchange considers Foreground to be an exceptional case and has decided, in consultation with the SFC, that the per claimant limit of $150,000 will apply. Under this arrangement, this tranche of payment out of the Compensation Fund in respect of the default of Foreground will be $5,491,613.78. Other than these 58 claimants, the claims of 2 claimants who failed to substantiate their claims were disallowed by the Stock Exchange and one claim is still awaiting determination by the Stock Exchange, as further information is required.

After the Stock Exchange has determined all the claims, it will prorate the $8 million (the compensation limit under section 109 of the Securities Ordinance) based on the total amount of all allowed claims. Should the apportioned amount of compensation be greater than the payment made to a particular claimant on this occasion, the Stock Exchange will notify the claimant who will then be able to receive the balance from the Compensation Fund.

Investors should take note that the Stock Exchange has decided that the $150,000 per claimant limit compensation arrangement will not automatically apply to other new cases, which will have to be considered on their individual merits, taking into account, among other things, the financial position of the Compensation Fund.
