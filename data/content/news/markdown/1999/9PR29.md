The Securities and Futures Commission announced that it had successfully prosecuted {X1} and {X2} for acting as dealer's representatives of Amber Global Limited (AGL) whilst unregistered.

{X1} and {X2} today pleaded guilty before Ms Suzanna Remedios at Western Magistracy. {X1} and {X2} were fined $2,000 each and ordered to pay costs totalling $8,000 to the SFC.

The SFC investigation revealed that during the period 1 April to 30 July 1998, AGL acted as an agent in Hong Kong for Amber Securities Corporation (ASC), a company registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission of the United States as a securities broker. Sales staff of AGL, including {X1} and {X2}, were engaged in recruiting clients in Hong Kong to open accounts with ASC and then receiving securities trading instructions for onward transmission to ASC. AGL, {X1} and {X2} were not registered with the SFC in any capacity.
