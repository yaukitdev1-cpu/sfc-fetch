A new series of investor education radio segments entitled "SFC Theatre : Be a Smart Investor", which is sponsored by the Securities and Futures Commission, will begin airing this Saturday on RTHK Radio 1 and Radio 5 Cantonese channels.

This radio series is the latest in a variety of investor education initiatives by the SFC. There are ten 15-minute segments, each featuring a short drama based on issues that investors may encounter, followed by SFC advice on matters investors should consider when investing.

In 1998, similar education radio programmes launched by the SFC on RTHK’s Putonghua Channel received positive response.

Topics in the current series include legislation on illegal short selling, risks involved in opening margin accounts, and investment solicitation on the Internet.

The producer, RTHK, has invited popular television artiste Ms Nancy Sit to be the voice of the main character in the present drama series.

Beginning 23 January, the series will run for 10 consecutive weeks in the "SAR Era" programme, which is broadcast every Saturday morning on Radio 1 (FM92.6-94.4) and Radio 5 (AM783) simultaneously. The debut segment will start at 8:45am and the subsequent episodes at 9.45am.

Mr Andrew Sheng, Chairman of the SFC, said: "We all invest for our future, which is a good thing. It is therefore very important to be a smart investor - who knows the investment, the intermediary, the risks, the rights and responsibilities of an investor, and monitors the investments. The Commission will further enhance its investor education programme in the months ahead through different media. The market works best when each investor makes decisions wisely and takes personal responsibility for investment decisions."
