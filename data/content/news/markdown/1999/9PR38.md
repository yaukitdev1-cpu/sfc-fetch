The Securities and Futures Commission announced today that it had publicly reprimanded {X}, a registered securities dealer's representative under the Securities Ordinance (SO).

The SFC conducted an inquiry under section 56 of the SO which revealed that in 1993 and 1995, {X} had signed as a witness on prospective clients' account opening documentation even though he was not present when the prospective clients signed the documentation. Further, it was revealed that {X} had allowed an unauthorized person to operate a client's account, thus exposing the account holder to risks and liabilities it had not chosen to assume and exposing his employer to potential liability.

An SFC spokesman said that {X}'s conduct impugned his fitness and properness as a registered person and warranted a public reprimand.
