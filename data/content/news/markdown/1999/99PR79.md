The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has recently worked with the public libraries of the Provisional Urban Council and the Provisional Regional Council to extend their collection of reference materials related to the regulatory regime of the securities and futures markets. 

With effect from 2 August 1999, a special collection entitled "Securities and Futures Commission Collection", which contains rules, codes, consultation papers and investor guides published by the SFC, as well as SFC’s own annual reports and other publications, will be available for public reference at the following central libraries:

City Hall Library
Kowloon Central Library
Sha Tin Central Library
Tsuen Wan Central Library
Tun Mun Central Library
Hong Kong Central Library (opening date to be announced by Provisional Urban Council)

"Making various SFC publications available for general public reference is one of SFC’s education efforts to advance public understanding of the financial markets," said the SFC spokesperson.
