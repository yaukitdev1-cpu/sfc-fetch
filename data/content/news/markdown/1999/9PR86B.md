The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it had successfully prosecuted {X} trading as Vincent Management Company (VMC) for carrying on a business of dealing in securities whilst unregistered.


{X} pleaded guilty before Ms Pang Ip Tsui-Yuk at Western Magistracy. {X} was fined $10,000 and ordered to pay costs of $13,400 to the SFC.


The SFC investigation revealed that during the period 13 May 1997 to 31 March 1998, staff of VMC arranged clients to open securities trading accounts with Vermont Securities Company Limited (Vermont) and to trade through such accounts in return for commission payment from Vermont. Neither {X} nor VMC have been registered with the SFC as a securities dealer.
