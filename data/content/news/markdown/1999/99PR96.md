The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today published the August 1999 progress report on the status of Year 2000 readiness in the securities and futures industries. A copy of the report is attached. 

Past SFC monthly progress reports can be found in the Year 2000 section of the SFC website at http: //www.hksfc.org.hk. The Year 2000 section of the SFC website also includes SFC letters to intermediaries, guidance notes, recommended methodologies, testing scenarios, reporting forms, and links to other Year 2000 resources on the Internet.
