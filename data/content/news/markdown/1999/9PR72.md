The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it had publicly reprimanded {X}, a dealer's representative.

The reprimand stemmed from an investigation in which the SFC found that {X} , whilst employed by OSK Asia Securities Limited (OSK):

- had knowingly opened and used nominee accounts for trading in OSK for a period of four years;
- had failed to monitor his trading to ensure he did not short sell securities; and
- had facilitated another staff of OSK to use a nominee account for trading in OSK.
 {X} is no longer employed by OSK.

A spokesman of the SFC said that {X} 's conduct impugned his fitness and properness to be registered and warranted a public reprimand.
