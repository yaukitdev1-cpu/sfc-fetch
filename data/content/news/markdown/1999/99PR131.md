As part of its Investor Education programme, the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has sponsored Radio Television Hong Kong (RTHK) to produce a ten-part, half-hour-per-episode television series, for screening on TVB Jade.

These magazine programmes, *Investment Challenge 2000*, feature major changes and developments in the financial markets and the economy of Hong Kong over the last century, upcoming reforms in the marketplace and regulatory structure, and how technology advances will affect the way investments are conducted.

The latest series of programmes follows the success of two educational TV series produced in conjunction with RTHK in October 1996 and February 1997 respectively.

The series will be broadcast in Cantonese on TVB Jade Channel at 7:30 pm for ten consecutive Sunday evenings starting 26 December 1999. In addition, the programme provides interactive features such as chat boards and quiz competition on RTHK's Website ([http://www.rthk.org.hk](http://www.rthk.org.hk)).

At the launching ceremony of the television series hosted by RTHK today, the SFC spokesman said: "The securities and futures markets will face many changes arising from globalisation of financial markets and advances in information technology. It is hoped that the RTHK programme can help explain these issues to the public, and encourage them to be informed investors."
