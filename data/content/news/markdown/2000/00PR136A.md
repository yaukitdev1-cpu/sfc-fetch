The SFC announced today that it had publicly reprimanded {X}, a registered dealer's representative under the Securities Ordinance.

The reprimand stemmed from an inquiry covering the period from May 1999 to February 2000 when {X} was a registered dealer's representative of Tung Tai Securities Company Limited (Tung Tai) and Phillip Securities (HK) Limited (Phillip).

The investigation found that {X} had operated a securities trading account with an outside broker during her employment with Tung Tai and Phillip without first seeking their approval in breach of their staff dealing policies. {X} also failed to notify the outside broker of her employment with Tung Tai and Phillip, which resulted in the outside broker failing to seek the written consent of Tung Tai and Phillip before dealing in securities on her behalf in accordance with clause 12.2 of the Code of Conduct for Persons Registered with the Securities and Futures Commission.

{X}'s conduct meant that Tung Tai and Phillip were unable to properly monitor her trading.
