The Securities and Futures Commission today published a summary survey report on the business activities of securities and futures intermediaries and exempt persons during 1999. It is an annual exercise conducted since 1996. 

The summary survey report includes findings on primary business activity, market share, client profile, custody of client assets, human resources profile, financial performance and business development.

Some of the major findings of the 1999 survey include :

- Similar to the position of 1998, 81% of securities dealers and 87% of commodity dealers respondents cited stockbroking and futures/option contracts broking respectively as their primary business;
- In 1999, securities and futures intermediaries recorded a 47% growth in total gross operating income which was mainly contributed to by the increase in stock broking and proprietary trading income under the recovering market conditions. Exempt dealers were noted to have increased their share in the securities market in 1999;
- The number of active clients participating in the securities and futures markets increased by 29% in 1999. Most of these new clients were retail clients in the stock and unit trust / mutual funds markets; and
- Registered securities and futures firms employed about 22,652 persons in 1999. The general economic recovery during 1999 has seen a reversal in the previous trend of reduced headcount.
Information collected in the survey enables the SFC to maintain an up to date profile of market intermediaries which is valuable to the SFC when it formulates policies for the industry. 

The SFC wishes to thank those intermediaries who had responded to the survey.

Interested parties may obtain a copy of the report from the SFC office or download it from http://www.hksfc.org.hk.
