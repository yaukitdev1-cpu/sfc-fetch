The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it had publicly reprimanded {X}, a registered dealer of Tai Wah Securities Limited under the Securities Ordinance. 

The reprimand stems from an investigation in which the SFC found that in November 1999 {X} opened an account with Young Champion Securities Limited (Young Champion) and represented himself as a merchant when he was employed by a registered person. As a result, Young Champion did not know that it had to seek {X}'s employer's consent for {X} to trade through the account as required under C12.2 of the Code of Conduct For Persons Registered with the Commission until July this year when it was informed by the Commission about {X}'s employment position.

Further, it was found that {X}'s securities trading through the Young Champion account was in breach of one of his employers' internal policy which prohibited its staff from dealing in securities. It was also found that {X} had breached the internal policy of a subsequent employer which allowed its staff to trade through outside brokerages provided they seek its written consent. It was found that {X} had never informed any of his employers between November 1999 and August 2000 that he had opened an account with Young Champion.

As a result of {X}'s failure to disclose his account with Young Champion to his employers, his employers were unable to properly monitor his trading.
