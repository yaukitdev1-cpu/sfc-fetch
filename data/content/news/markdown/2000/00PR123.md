The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it had publicly reprimanded {X}, a dealer's representative of Asia Financial (Securities) Limited (AF) under the Securities Ordinance.

The reprimand stems from an investigation in which the SFC found that on 4 May 1998, {X} arranged a private placement in the shares of GKC Holdings Limited (GKC) for AF's clients to subscribe at a discount price of $0.95 per share. When obtaining approval for the transaction from his employer, he informed his employer that the placement price was $1.18 per share. His action misled his employer about the price of the placement. The SFC concluded {X}'s conduct impugned his fitness and properness and decided to publicly reprimand him.
