The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it has revoked the registration of Mr Man Kin Hung (Man), a dealer’s representative under the Securities Ordinance.

The revocation stems from an inquiry which revealed that from the end of 1997 to May or June 2000, whilst employed by Daily Growth Investment Company Limited (Daily Growth), Man had:

- opened a margin account in a client’s name without that client’s knowledge and consent;
- conducted unauthorized trading through a number of clients’ accounts;
- used the credit facilities of clients’ margin accounts to engage in unauthorized trading because his own account was already in debt;
- credited his own account with profits made from buying and selling stocks through clients’ accounts; and
- withheld monthly statements from clients.
In light of Man’s conduct the SFC concluded that Man was guilty of serious misconduct and has failed to meet the continuing fitness and properness requirements for a registered dealer’s representative. Therefore the SFC decided to revoke Man’s registration.
