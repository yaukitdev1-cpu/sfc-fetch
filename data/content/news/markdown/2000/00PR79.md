The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it has publicly reprimanded {X}.

The Commission's action stemmed from an inquiry into the activities of {X} during the period September 1996 to April 1998 when he was the head of private banking at ING Barings Capital Markets Ltd (ING Barings).

The Commission found that {X} had failed to make proper enquiry into the status of 5 OTC shares traded in the United States before agreeing to sign 6 letters of comfort in respect of each of those shares. The letters {X} signed represented amongst other things that each of the accountholders had clear title.

It transpired that in fact certain of these shares were subject to restrictions as to their resale. The shares were later disposed of through a private banking account at ING Barings that {X} had earlier opened for a person he knew to be an employee of another registered person. {X} had opened the account without first seeking the prior written consent of that other registered person contrary to regulatory requirements and ING Baring's internal guidelines. He had not ensured that orders for this account were only accepted from authorized parties only.

{X} had admitted he had been negligent in providing the letters of comfort and in ignoring the requirements of ING Barings.

In deciding to publicly reprimand {X}, the Commission took into account that {X} had voluntarily surrendered his registration and has undertaken not to reapply for registration for a period of four months.

{X} had since left ING Barings.
