The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it had publicly reprimanded {X} under the Securities Ordinance.

The reprimand stemmed from an investigation into the unusual trading activities of the shares of Chi Cheung Investment Company Limited (Chi Cheung) between July and September 1998. At the time {X} was a director of EAA Securities Limited (EAA) and registered as a dealing director with the SFC. During its investigation the SFC identified a number of clients of EAA who had traded heavily in Chi Cheung shares. Further investigation revealed that the trading had in fact been carried out by only one client and that {X} had arranged for this client's trades to be booked to accounts of {X}'s friends and relatives at EAA. These accounts were therefore nominee accounts.

{X} had a duty to act with due skill, care and diligence in the best interests of his clients and the integrity of the market. The SFC found that by his actions {X} had seriously breached this duty. As {X} had left EAA and is no longer registered with the SFC, the SFC has decided to reprimand {X}.
