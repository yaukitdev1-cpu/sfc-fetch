1. The public subscription of Tom.com closed yesterday (23 February) at noon. Due to the large number of subscribers handing in their forms during that morning, there were disorderly scenes at the receiving banks.
2. Maintaining a fair and orderly subscription process is the responsibility of the sponsor, the receiving bank and the company concerned. They should have made better arrangements for distribution and acceptance of application forms.
3. SFC is not involved in every detailed aspect of the subscription process as it involves certain commercial decisions. However, the company concerned, sponsor and receiving bank must ensure that the subscription process is fair and orderly, including aspects of crowd control and related matters.
4. SFC is disappointed that the sponsor, receiving bank and company concerned, despite our advice through the SEHK, have not prepared sufficient subscription forms in a timely manner and set up proper arrangements for receiving the forms. The company, sponsor and receiving bank should have been aware of the investor interest in the IPO, particularly in view of the publicity running up to the IPO. SFC has asked the sponsor to submit a report on the case.
5. SFC is working with the SEHK to ensure that in future the sponsor and the company take the necessary measures to ensure a fair and orderly subscription process. Publication of a specific guidance on the subject is being considered.
6. Ultimately, the development of a financial infrastructure for electronic subscription at IPOs will be speeded up. This would obviate the need to queue up for forms and to hand in the forms.
