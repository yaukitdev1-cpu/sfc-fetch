The SFC announced today that it had reprimanded {X} as a dealer's representative registered under the Securities Ordinance (SO) and as a commodity dealer's representative registered under the Commodities Trading Ordinance (CTO). 

The reprimand stemmed from an inquiry which revealed that during November to December 1998 {X} had:-

- signed as a witness to his client's signature on the account opening documentation although he was not present when his client signed the documentation;
- failed to take reasonable steps to establish the true and full identity, financial situation, investment experience and investment objectives of his client;
- allowed a third party to operate his client's account without obtaining a written authorization from his client; and
- allowed an employee of another registered person to deal in securities for his client without having first sought the written consent of that other registered person.
{X}'s conduct impugned his fitness and properness as a registered person under both the SO and the CTO. As a result of the above findings, the SFC has decided to publicly reprimand him.
