The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today welcomed the arrival of Mr Zhou Xiaochuan, the Chairman of the China Securities Regulatory Commission (CSRC), for a two-day visit in Hong Kong.

Chairman Zhou attended the 26th meeting of the Memorandum of Regulatory Co-operation (MORC) held at the Stock Exchange this morning. Also present at the meeting were SFC Chairman Mr Andrew Sheng and the Chief Executive of the Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Mr Kwong Ki-chi. MORC was first established in 1993 among the CSRC, the SFC, the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong, Shanghai Stock Exchange and Shenzhen Stock Exchange to enhance co-operation among securities regulators in the Mainland and Hong Kong.

This is the first official visit of Chairman Zhou to Hong Kong. He visited the SFC, where he was briefed on the latest securities market development in Hong Kong.

In welcoming Chairman Zhou, Mr Sheng said, "It’s a valuable opportunity for us to exchange views and share experience regarding the development and regulation of the securities markets in both places.”

“Co-operation in both markets is important for the further development of the financial sector of China as a whole,” Mr Sheng said.
