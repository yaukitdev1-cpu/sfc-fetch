The Securities and Futures Commission announces that the revised Hong Kong Code on Takeovers and Mergers (the Takeovers Code) have been gazetted today. The revised Takeovers Code will become effective on 1 April 2000.

The Takeovers Code was first introduced in 1975, and was thoroughly reviewed between 1989 and 1991, resulting in the 1992 version. The Takeovers Code was revised in August 1998. It was reviewed again in 1999 and a consultation paper was issued in September 1999 The policy of the SFC is to keep the Takeovers Code under regular review.

This review considered a number of issues raised by public respondents during the consultation in 1998 and issues that have arisen from the day-to-day administration of the Takeovers Code. The review also took into account relevant amendments to the City Code on Takeovers and Mergers made by the Takeover Panel in London in 1998.

A total of 11 submissions were received from the public during the consultation. The submissions were reviewed and refinements to the proposed amendments were adopted by the Takeovers Panel and approved by the Commission.

Mr. David Stannard, Executive Director of SFC’s Corporate Finance Division, said "The SFC aims to refine the Takeovers Code to keep up with market practice and development. Public comments collected during the consultation are useful and they helped refine the Takeovers Code."

Some of the principal areas revised are as follows:

Timing of posting of the offeree board circular and the minimum period for an offer to remain open

Rules 8 and 15 have been amended so that the 14 day period in which the offeree company must post its circular may only be extended if the offeror agrees and that the first closing date will remain day 21 if the offer document and offeree board circular are posted on the same day, or combined in a composite document, or day 28 if the offeree board circular is posted later.

*Technical issue arising from the revision or extension of an offer resolved*

Under the new Rules 15 and 19, offers must be closed by 9:30 am on a closing date and must contain a condition that acceptances must be received by 4 pm on the preceding day. On the first closing dates mentioned above the offers must close by 9:30 am on days 22 or 29 and acceptances must be received by 4 pm on days 21 and 28 respectively.

*Statement of material change in a company’s financial or trading position needs to be reported on*

The new Rule 10 provides that a statement of material change in a company’s financial or trading position needs to be reported on by the auditor and financial adviser and, in respect of the negative statement required to be made, the directors of the company must provide evidence to the Executive of the steps they have taken to support that statement.

*Cash offer or cash alternative at the highest price paid during offer period must be made under a non-cash offer*

Under the new Rule 23, if an offeror purchases shares for cash at any price (not just at above the offer price) during the offer period, it must provide a cash offer or cash alternative at the highest price during the offer period.

*Significant asset or profit test under the chain principle clarified*

The significant asset or profit test has been set at 60% of the relative value of the companies in a chain under the revised Note 8 to Rule 26.1.

*Disclosure under Rule 33 is no longer required*

Rule 33 has been deleted as there is duplication between Securities (Disclosure of Interests) Ordinance and Rule 33.

All other proposed amendments set out in the consultation paper have been adopted.

The revised Takeovers Code will apply to takeover and merger transactions with effect from 1 April 2000 other than those transactions which have already been announced prior to 1 April 2000. When in doubt, market participants should consult the Takeovers Executive in the first instance.
