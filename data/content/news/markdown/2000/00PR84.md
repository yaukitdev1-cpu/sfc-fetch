The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) launches a new publicity campaign using TV/radio “Announcements in the Public Interest (API)” and a bilingual poster.

The campaign aims to bring out two key messages:

- It is important to be an informed and educated investor; and
- The availability of the Electronic Investor Resources Centre (eIRC) for investors to acquire knowledge on investing and to conduct research on their investments.
In May 1998, the SFC aired a Chinese and an English TV API using the theme of “sheep” to educate the public not to follow the crowd when investing. As this API has earned a very high recall rate, it is now re-mixed with a new voice-over to reflect the current market sentiment. Beginning 10 July 2000, the revised Sheep TV API will be aired on both the English and Chinese channels of TVB and ATV, and on Cable TV channels.

Supporting the re-mixed Sheep TV API is a Cantonese radio API with a similar voice-over, which will be aired on local radio stations, also starting 10 July 2000.

The bi-lingual poster, also along the same creative platform, features a “sheep” asking itself six questions “www – where, what, why, which, who and when” on hot investment items. These posters will be displayed in Government’s poster boxes, on public notice boards of libraries, district offices, universities, public and private property developments and public transportation systems.
