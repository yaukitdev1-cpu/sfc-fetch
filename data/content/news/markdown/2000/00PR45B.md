The SFC announced today that it had publicly reprimanded {X}, a dealer's representative under the SO.

The action stemmed from an inquiry into {X}'s conduct whilst registered as a dealer's representative of Cheung's Securities Brokers Limited (Cheung's). The inquiry found that during the period March 1997 to December 1997, {X} had knowingly breached the registration requirements of the SO by facilitating unregistered dealing by persons not registered with the SFC.

As a result of the above findings, the SFC concluded that {X}'s fitness and properness had been impugned and decided to publicly reprimand him. In deciding on the public reprimand, the SFC took into consideration that {X} had pleaded guilty at the North Kowloon Magistrate's Court to various breaches of the registration requirements under the SO and had been fined.
