The SFC announced today that it had publicly reprimanded {X}, a dealer's representative under the CTO. 

The action stemmed from an inquiry into {X}'s conduct whilst registered as a dealer's representative accredited to Lee Fung Hong (International) Futures Limited (LFH) and employed as its marketing manager. The inquiry found that during the period of January 1997 to December 1997, {X} had:

- acted as a dealer's representative of Cheung's Securities Brokers Limited (Cheung's), a sister company of LFH, while not registered as a dealer's representative under the SO; and
- knowingly breached the registration requirements of the SO and CTO by facilitating unregistered dealing by persons not registered with the SFC.

As a result of the above findings, the SFC concluded that {X}'s fitness and properness had been impugned and decided to publicly reprimand him. In deciding on the public reprimand, the SFC took into consideration that {X} had pleaded guilty at the North Kowloon Magistrate's Court to various breaches of the registration requirements under the SO and CTO and had been fined.
