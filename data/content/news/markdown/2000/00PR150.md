The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it has publicly reprimanded {X}, a dealer's representative registered under the Securities Ordinance.

The reprimand stems from an inquiry which revealed that during September to November 1997, {X} operated an account maintained with CA Pacific Securities Limited (CA Pacific) without written authorization. In operating the account {X} allowed a staff member of CA Pacific to use the account to book a purchase of warrants without the prior knowledge of the account holder. Also, during part of the same period {X} has aided and abetted one of the employees of CA Pacific to act as a dealer's representative of CA Pacific whilst unregistered, for which proceedings had been concluded in court. The Commission views {X}'s conduct most seriously as he placed the account holder at unnecessary risk and these events occurred whilst {X}'s registration as a dealer's representative of CA Pacific was suspended by the SFC.

In deciding to publicly reprimand {X} the Commission took into account that {X} has agreed to surrender his registration as a dealer's representative and has undertaken not to reapply for any registration for a period of 2 years and {X} had not derived any pecuniary benefit from his conduct.
