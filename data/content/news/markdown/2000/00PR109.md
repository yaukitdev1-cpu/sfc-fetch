The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it had successfully prosecuted both Central China Enterprises Ltd. (Central China) and {X}, a director of Central China, under the Securities (Disclosure of Interests) Ordinance. Although {X} acquired 2 million Central China shares on 27 October 1999 as a result of exercising a share option he did not report this change in his shareholding to the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Ltd. until 10 January 2000. Central China was aware of {X} exercising the option but did not make an entry in the Register of Directors&rsquo; Interests, which it is obliged to maintain, until 10 January 2000.


Ms. Amanda Chan, a Magistrate at Western Magistracy, fined {X} $4,000 and Central China $1,000, respectively and they were also ordered to pay costs totalling $18,488 to the SFC.
