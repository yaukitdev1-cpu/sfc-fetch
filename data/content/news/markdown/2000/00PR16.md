The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes today’s announcement by the Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Ltd. (HKEC) of the appointment of Mr Kwong Ki-chi and Mr Alec Tsui as the Chief Executive and Chief Operating Officer respectively. The appointments were approved by the Commission today.

As technology is playing an increasingly important part in the activities of exchanges all over the world, the background of Mr Kwong in this area would be most useful in his role to lead the HKEC in the face of global challenges, said Mrs Laura M Cha, Acting Chairman of the SFC.

At the same time, the significant experience of Mr Tsui in the industry and the Stock Exchange would be a tremendous asset to Mr Kwong in moving HKEC to be one of the top exchanges in the world, added Mrs Cha.

The Commission looks forward to working closely with Mr Kwong and Mr Tsui to fully implement the securities and futures market reforms, and to the successful establishment of the HKEC under the Exchanges and Clearing Houses (Merger) Ordinance, said Mrs Cha.

The SFC has worked closely with the Government, the two exchanges and the clearing houses on the formation of HKEC and is committed to working with the Government in implementing reforms announced in the 1999 Budget Speech.
