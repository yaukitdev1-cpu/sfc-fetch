The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announces today that it has reprimanded {X}, a registered dealer representative of Lehin Securities Limited (Lehin), and Lehin, a dealer registered under the Securities Ordinance (SO), upon conclusion of inquiries conducted under section 56 of the SO.

{X} is reprimanded for:

- mishandling the operation of two client accounts in breach of provisions of *Code of Conduct for Persons Registered with the Securities and Futures Commission*; and
- failing to observe Lehin's company policy which requires an account executive to have obtained a client's written authorisation before allowing a third party to operate the client's account.
Lehin, on the other hand, is reprimanded for its:

- breach of guidelines contained in *Management, Supervision and Internal Control Guidelines for Persons Registered with or Licensed by the SFC* in that it failed to exercise proper supervision over {X}'s activities;
- delay in delivering contract notes to a client within the period prescribed under section 75(1)(a) of the SO; and
- failure to deliver contract notes directly to the client.
