The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it had revoked the registrations of Ms Ao Pou Ting Surita (Ao) and Ms Wong Suet Kam (Wong) as dealer's representatives under the Securities Ordinance.

The revocations stem from an inquiry in which the SFC found that Ao and Wong had, whilst dealer's representatives of Grand Fortune Securities & Investment Company Limited (Grand Fortune):

- used clients' securities without authority and provided documentation to clients that they knew to be false and misleading to mislead those clients as to the state of their accounts at Grand Fortune; and
- opened and operated trading accounts in clients' names without their knowledge or consent.

A SFC spokesman said that Ao's and Wong's gross abuse of their clients' trust warrants the revocation of their registrations.
