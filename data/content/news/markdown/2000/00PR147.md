The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomed the announcement today by the Secretary for Financial Services of the establishment of an independent Process Review Panel to review the Commission's internal operations.

Mr Mark Dickens, Acting Chairman of the SFC, said that the Commission was committed to enhancing its accountability and the transparency of its operations for the benefit of the public and market participants alike and was confident that the Commission would be shown to exercise its regulatory functions in a reasonable and consistent manner.

Mr Dickens noted that the Commission was already subject to various checks and balances designed to ensure fairness and observance of due process. However, statutory secrecy provisions restricted the Commission's ability to inform the public fully of its activities. This made it difficult for the public to judge whether the Commission had acted fairly in any particular case. Mr Dickens felt sure that the work of the PRP would bridge any gap in this regard.
