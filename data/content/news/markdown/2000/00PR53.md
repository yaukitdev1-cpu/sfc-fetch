The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) is delighted to learn that the Board of Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited (HKEx) has endorsed a proposal to remove the rules which fix the respective minimum brokerage commission rates for stock and futures transactions with effect from 1 April, 2002 (the Proposal).

“It has always been the policy of the SFC to encourage measures by the industry to make Hong Kong a cost effective place to conduct businesses,” said the SFC spokesman.

The Proposal together with the reduction of the rate of stamp duty on stock transactions by 10 per cent which was announced in the last Budget Speech will certainly enhance the competitiveness of the Hong Kong markets, added the spokesman.

The Commission will consider the Proposal as soon as possible, concluded the spokesman. Under the Stock Exchanges Unification Ordinance, any proposal that may lead to amendment of the rules has to be sent to the Commission for approval before it is going to take effect.
