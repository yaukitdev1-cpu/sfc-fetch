The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) and the Hong Kong Securities Institute (HKSI) have today jointly organised a professional development seminar on the topic of "How to Advise Investors on Hi-Tech Stocks" for members of the HKSI.

Speakers for the panel are:

- Mr. Lennon Chan, Deputy Managing Director, Tai Fook Securities Group Company Limited
- Mr. Cho Chi Ming, Hong Kong Economic Journal
- Dr. Matthew K O Lee, Head of Department and Associate Professor, Department of Information Systems, City University of Hong Kong
"Many investors rely on their brokers for advice in making investment decisions. Securities professionals are therefore charged with a duty of due care to provide investors with correct information and to explain to them the risk of trading stocks, particularly in relation to the new technology sectors," said Mr. Andrew Sheng, chairman of the SFC at the opening of the seminar. 

The three speakers, in their presentations, discussed the following issues:

- Classification of Technology and Net stocks
- How to identify true High-technology counters
- Pros and cons of different valuation methods for Net stocks
- Effect of announcing ‘DotCom’ and e-commerce initiatives on share prices
- Risks inherent in trading ‘New Economy’ shares
- Usefulness of Technology Stock Indices
All speakers concluded that the Net stocks are still an immature market, characterised by irrational pricing and extreme price fluctuations. Moreover, both the Technology and Internet sectors will see consolidation, convergence and elimination. It is therefore very important for the regulators, market professionals and the media to remind investors of the risks and devote greater efforts to investor education.
