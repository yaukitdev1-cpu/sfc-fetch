The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today launches new booklets and pamphlets, in English and Chinese, for retail investors. 

As part of SFC’s investor education programme, the SFC has produced free investor brochures since mid 1996. All investor publications have been highly popular, with over 250,000 copies distributed. The new publications are a result of revamp of the existing booklets to include additional advice on new market developments and rule changes, such as online trading, GEM stocks and share margin financing.

The titles of these new publications are:

*A Smart Investor*

What Every Stock Investor Should Know

What Every Fund Investor Should Know

When to Pursue a Complaint

*A Smart Investor* discusses the golden rules of investing, i.e. know the investment products, the level of risks and the financial professionals that sell those products. There are also tips on how to monitor investments and the danger signals that investors should watch out for.

*What Every Stock Investor Should Know* aims to help the investing public understand some basic issues in stock investments, such as initial public offerings, rights issues, takeovers, and as shareholders, their rights to vote and access to company information.

*What Every Fund Investor Should Know* explains how the SFC authorises and supervises funds as well as the circumstances that would lead to suspension of dealing, mergers and terminations. It also examines the factors that investors should consider when selecting a fund.

*When to Pursue a Complaint* is about what investors should do when they encounter malpractice or suspicious activities in the financial markets.

While the publications may give useful guidance on certain specific issues, investors should seek professional advice where appropriate before making their investment decisions.

All publications are available to the public free at the SFC, the Investor Service Centre and Depository Service Counters Hall of Hongkong Clearing and all 12 Consumer Advice Centres of the Consumer Council.

Contents of the new publications can also be found in the Investor Education section of the SFC website at http://www.hksfc.org.hk.
