The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the re-appointment by the Financial Secretary of Mr Paul Bailey as Executive Director of Enforcement.

" I am very pleased that the Financial Secretary has re-appointed Mr Bailey to head our Enforcement Division. The re-appointment is a recognition of Mr Bailey's hard work and valuable experience," said SFC Chairman Mr Andrew Sheng.

"Mr Bailey will continue to execute the Commission's enforcement policy fairly and squarely,'' Mr Sheng said.

Mr Bailey's new term of appointment will be from 1 October 2000 to 30 September 2001.

Formerly with the Office of the Commissioner for Securities and Commodities Trading, Mr Bailey has been with the SFC since its inception in May 1989. He was Senior Director of Enforcement from 1995 to 1999. He became Executive Director of Enforcement in April 1999.
