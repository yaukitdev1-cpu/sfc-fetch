The law relating to the regulation of securities margin finance activities will come into effect on 12 June 2000. 

Under the new law, any entity that carries on a business of providing securities margin finance must be registered as a securities margin financier. In addition, any individual, who performs functions on behalf of a securities margin financier, is required to be registered as a securities margin financier's representative.

There are some exceptions to the new licensing requirements, including those who are registered or exempt dealers under the Securities Ordinance and authorized institutions under the Banking Ordinance. Registered and exempt dealers must however comply with new provisions that have similar effect as those that are applicable to securities margin financiers.

Finance companies that have been providing securities margin finance prior to the commencement of the legislation may continue to do so only if they have lodged applications to be registered as securities margin financiers within 30 days from the 12 June 2000 and then until their applications are determined.

An applicant applying for registration as a securities margin financier must

a. be a company;
b. have its sole business in securities margin financing;
c. have at least one director who has applied to or is approved by the SFC to be responsible for the supervision of its business;
d. maintain minimum capital requirements - issued and paid-up capital of HK$10 million and liquid capital of HK$3 million or 5% of the total liabilities, whichever is greater at all times; and
e. otherwise be fit and proper to carry on the business.


An individual applying to be a securities margin financier's representative or a director applying to be approved for the purpose of supervising the business is required, among others, to have the necessary educational and/or relevant experience and must otherwise be fit and proper.

The Licensing Department of the Securities and Futures Commission has issued a licensing information booklet, which details the application procedures. The Commission has also published guidelines to help the public to understand how it applies the fit and proper test: The Fit And Proper Criteria for Persons Engaging in Securities Margin Finance.

The [licensing information booklet](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/intermediaries/supervision/licinfbo.pdf), the [Fit and Proper Criteria](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/intermediaries/supervision/f&pcsmf.pdf) and the application forms are available at the Office of the Securities & Futures Commission, 12th Floor, Edinburgh Tower, The Landmark, 15 Queen's Road Central, Hong Kong. In addition, updated information concerning securities margin finance is also available in the Commission’s website http: //www.hksfc.org.hk.
