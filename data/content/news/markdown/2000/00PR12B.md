The SFC announced today that it had publicly reprimanded {X}, who is registered as a dealer's representative of Mr Chan Hing Sum Sam, trading as Promising Securities Company (Promising).

The SFC conducted an inquiry under section 56 of the Securities Ordinance which revealed that during September 1998, {X} was negligent in his duties in that he allowed a share trading account to be opened but failed to take reasonable steps to establish the full and true identity of the client contrary to clause 5.1 of the Commission's "Code of Conduct for Persons Registered with the Securities and Futures Commission". In addition, {X} allowed an unauthorized person to operate this account, thereby exposed both the account holder to risks and liabilities he may not have chosen to assume and Promising to potential liabilities in the event of dispute or default.

On this basis the SFC concluded that {X}'s fitness and properness as a registered person has been impugned and a public reprimand was appropriate.
