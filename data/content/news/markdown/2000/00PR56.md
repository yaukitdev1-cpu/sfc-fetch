Miss Vivien Chen (Chen) was successfully prosecuted at Western Magistracy on 26 April 2000 for providing false or misleading representations in her application to become registered with the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC). Chen was sentenced to four months imprisonment, suspended for 2 years.

The offence came to light following an investigation commenced by the SFC and concluded by the Crime Section of Waterfront Police Station. The conviction of Chen related to her providing a false statement to the SFC that she had no criminal conviction. This statement was made by her in her application to become a registered investment representative under the Securities Ordinance.
