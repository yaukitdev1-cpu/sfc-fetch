The Chairman of the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC), Mr Andrew Sheng gave a speech on "Regulatory Environment and Market Reforms" at a seminar held in Beijing today. The seminar entitled "New Horizon of Capital Raising" is jointly organised by the Hong Kong Trade Development Council, the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, the SFC, the Hong Kong Monetary Authority and the Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing.
