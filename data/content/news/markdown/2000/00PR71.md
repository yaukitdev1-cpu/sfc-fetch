The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it had reprimanded {X} as a dealer's representative registered under the Securities Ordinance and as a commodity dealer's representative registered under the Commodities Trading Ordinance. 

The reprimand stems from an inquiry which revealed that on 12 January 1998 {X}:

- permitted 2 clients to open accounts with his employer, Get Nice Investment Limited (Get Nice), without properly completing account opening procedures; and
- took orders from a third party who had not been authorized by the clients in writing to operate their accounts.

In reaching this decision the Commission took into consideration that {X} had admitted he had been reckless in carrying out his duties and had expressed regret for his conduct.
