The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it had revoked the registration of Ms Ho Yin Mui Fenny (Ho) as a dealer’s representative under the Securities Ordinance.

The revocation stems from inquiries in which the SFC found that Ho had, whilst a dealer’s representative of Prosperous Securities Limited (Prosperous):

- used clients’ assets and securities without their authority and provided false documentation to clients to mislead those clients as to the state of their accounts at Prosperous; and
- conducted trades through clients’ accounts without their knowledge or consent.

A spokesman of SFC said that Ho’s gross abuse of her clients’ trust warrants the revocation of her registration.
