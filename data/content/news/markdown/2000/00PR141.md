The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today informed the public about the registration status of the 9 securities margin financiers whose applications have been submitted within 30 days from the enactment date of the Securities (Margin Financing) (Amendment) Ordinance 2000 (12 June 2000). These financiers have all been granted registration as securities margin financiers as at 31 October 2000.

In addition to these 9 existing businesses, the Commission received one new business application which has also been approved.

Only registered financiers are permitted to carry on securities margin finance business unless they fall within one of the exempt categories of business. Securities dealers and Authorized Institutions are amongst those who may provide securities margin finance.

The SFC is aware that a number of finance companies which used to provide securities margin financing but have not applied as securities margin financiers before 13 July 2000 have ceased these activities.

Investors should be cautious in dealing with persons who provide margin financing to them. Should they deal with persons who are not lawfully allowed to provide such service, they will not be afforded the level of protection expected under the new regime.

A full list of registered securities margin financiers is attached.
