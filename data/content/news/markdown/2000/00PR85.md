The Securities and Futures Commission today announced the names of financiers that have been providing securities margin finance immediately prior to the commencement date of the Securities (Margin Financing)(Amendment) Ordinance 2000 (12 June 2000) and who have submitted applications within 30 days from that date to be registered as securities margin financiers. Under the transitional arrangement of the law, these financiers may continue to provide securities margin financing until their applications are determined.

Whilst the above-mentioned financiers are permitted by law to carry on securities margin financing during the interim, members of the investing public should be aware that their applications may not be approved. A financier whose application has been refused must cease business within 14 days or such period as specified by the Commission. Investors should exercise caution when dealing with these financiers, as they may not be able to comply with all the regulatory requirements and therefore may not be able to afford investors the level of protection expected under the new regime.

Any financier whose name does not appear in the attached list is not permitted to carry on securities margin finance business unless they are included in one of the excepted categories of business. Securities dealers and Authorized Institutions are amongst those who may provided securities margin finance.

The Commission will from time to time publish the names of those financiers whose applications have either been refused or approved to ensure members of the investing public are fully informed of the registration status of the financiers they are dealing with.

Information concerning the registration status of a financier may also be accessed via the Commission's Web-site at [http: //www.hksfc.org.hk](http://www.sfc.hk/) or alternatively through the inspection of the public register maintained at the Office of the Securities and Futures Commission 12th Floor, Edinburgh Tower, The Landmark, 15 Queen's Road Central Hong Kong.
