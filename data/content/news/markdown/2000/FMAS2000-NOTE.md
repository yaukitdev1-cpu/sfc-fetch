Figures quoted in the Summary Findings of the Fund Management Activities Survey 1999 (FMAS 1999) were adjusted in June 2001 as a number of respondents had subsequently reported that errors had been made in their returns. Please refer to the [Adjusted Summary of the Aggregate Figures of FMAS 1999](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/consult/fmas2000-attachment.pdf). For background and the unadjusted version, see Press Release of FMAS 1999 and Summary Findings of FMAS 1999 issued on 4 July 2000.
