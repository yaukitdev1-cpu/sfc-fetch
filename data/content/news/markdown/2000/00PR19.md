The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the decision of the Government to publish the new Securities and Futures Bill in April as a White Bill. The news was announced in the Budget Speech today by the Financial Secretary, Mr Donald Tsang.

“As stated in his Budget Speech, the Financial Secretary said that we need to press on with our efforts to improve Hong Kong’s own regulatory regime and standards of corporate governance. To achieve these, we have imminent need of the new Securities and Futures Bill. Market consultation through the White Bill will help significantly,” said the Chairman of the SFC, Mr Andrew Sheng.

The SFC also applauds the Government’s proposal to reduce the rate of stamp duty on stock transactions by 10 per cent, from 0.25 per cent to 0.225 per cent.

Mr Sheng said, "In order to enhance the competitiveness of the Hong Kong Exchange and Clearing Company (HKEx) and for our market to remain attractive, the cutting of stamp duty is very helpful."

In his Budget Speech today, the Financial Secretary also endorsed the recommendations on financial infrastructure made by the Steering Committee on the Enhancement of the Financial Infrastructure (SCEFI) which was formed 12 months ago, and chaired by the Chairman of the SFC.

The SCEFI has recommended the establishment of an open and secure electronic network that will allow all securities and derivatives transactions to be processed straight through. The newly established HKEx will need to complete the critical aspects of this new network within two years.

“The SFC is committed to delivering its part of the modern financial infrastructure on time, as recommended in the SCEFI Report,” said Mr Sheng. The SFC will work closely with HKEx and the securities industry to expedite the infrastructure development.

The SFC has worked closely with the Government, the two exchanges and the clearing houses and the securities community in the past twelve months in ensuring that the major reforms announced in last year’s Budget Speech are successfully carried through. The Commission looks forward to working with the Government in implementing reforms announced in this year’s Budget Speech.
