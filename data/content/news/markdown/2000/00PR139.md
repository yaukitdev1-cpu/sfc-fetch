The SFC announced today that its Internet web site would warn investors about unregistered stockbrokers who appear to be operating in Hong Kong or claim to be operating from Hong Kong.

The SFC has recently received a number of complaints and inquiries from investors who have received unsolicited calls from stockbrokers who claim to have offices in Hong Kong. In fact, these firms only used the facilities of Hong Kong business centres or company secretarial firms to make it appear that they operated from Hong Kong and really operated from foreign countries.

A firm that carries on the business of dealing in securities in Hong Kong or that claims that it does must be registered with the SFC as a securities dealer. None of the firms inquired or complained about were registered.

Investors should be careful about dealing with stockbrokers that are not registered with the SFC. It is generally illegal for a firm to deal in securities in Hong Kong or to claim that it does when it is not registered. If an investor deals with an unregistered stockbroker, he will not have the full protection of the Securities Ordinance.

Investors are urged to ascertain whether a stockbroker is registered with the SFC by visiting the Registry section on the SFC web site.

To help investors make more informed choices about who they deal with when trading shares, the SFC has set up a new Investor Alert sub-section within the Investor Education section on its Internet web site to list names of firms who are not registered but who, according to information available to the SFC, claim that they are dealing in securities in Hong Kong. Investors can visit the list by going to the SFC web site at http: //www.hksfc.org.hk.

The SFC will update the list when it learns of new firms.

The SFC encourages investors who believe that they have been approached by an unregistered stockbroker or who have been cold called to contact it by emailing the details to investor.info@hksfc.org.hk, or call the Investor Hotline at 2840-9333.

The SFC will take appropriate enforcement action against those in Hong Kong who are unregistered and who deal in securities or claim they do or who make cold calls and those who knowingly assist them. The SFC will co-operate with its international counterparts in relation to those outside Hong Kong who claim to operate from Hong Kong.
