The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it has reprimanded {X}, a dealer registered under the Securities Ordinance and sole proprietor of New Union Securities Company (New Union).

The reprimand stems from an inquiry concerning the misappropriation of client assets by an employee of New Union during January 1996 to September 1999, which revealed that the misappropriation was partly attributable to deficiencies in the internal controls and procedures of New Union.

The reprimand takes into account {X}'s engagement of a firm of accountants to review New Union's operations and his undertaking to implement the accountants' recommendations, a number of which have already been adopted to improve the internal control systems of New Union.
