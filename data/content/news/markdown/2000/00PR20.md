The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) wishes to remind all firms engaged in the business of providing share margin financing that the new Securities (Margin Financing) (Amendment) Bill 1999 (Bill) is scheduled to be presented in the Legislative Council on March 15 for the Second and Third Reading. Subject to the approval of the Legislative Council, the Bill as amended by the Committee Stage Amendments proposed by the Administration will be enacted on that date. 

The Bill will bring firms who provide share margin finance under the supervision of the SFC. As the Bill will impose certain regulatory requirements on firms who are engaged in share margin financing, such firms are strongly urged to review the new regulations to gauge the effect the Bill will have on their businesses and to ensure compliance with the new requirements.

SFC also notes that there have been recent reports of aggressive margin lending. In this regard both registrants and share margin financing firms are reminded that prudent credit policy should be adopted and enforced. Proper risk management systems must also be established to monitor the companies’ risk positions. This is necessary to safeguard the interests of the firms’ clients and that of the general investing public. This is particularly important in view of recent volatility in the market. SFC will monitor the situation closely and may review specific situations where appropriate. The SFC will take all possible measures to ensure that the interest of the general investing public is protected.
