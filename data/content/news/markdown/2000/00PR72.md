The Securities (Margin Financing) (Amendment) Ordinance 2000 and revised Financial Resources Rules came into effect on 12 June 2000.

In this connection, the Commission has taken the following initiatives to assist the market participants in preparing for compliance with the new rules and regulations:

a. The Commission has held a number of briefing sessions for market participants and given presentations to other interested parties such as members of the Hong Kong Society of Accountants. A copy of the presentation materials has been posted to the Commission’s website for use by those who have not been able to attend the briefing sessions.
b. The revised Financial Resources Rules, as amended by the Legislative Council on 31 May 2000, have been posted to the Commission’s website to facilitate reference and downloading.
c. The Commission has received a number of enquiries from the market on the Securities (Margin Financing) (Amendment) Ordinance 2000 and the revised Financial Resources Rules in the past two months. A number of the more frequently asked questions and their answers have been summarized and posted to the Commission’s website.
d. The revised Financial Resources Rules have introduced a stock concentration adjustment on related securities which requires identification of cross shareholding amongst listed companies. 


The Commission has identified such groups of related securities in Hong Kong for the purpose of revised Financial Resources Rules and posted the same to the Commission’s website. This list will be updated on a regular basis and intermediaries are reminded to check for revisions from time to time.

e. The Commission is mindful of the need for systems enhancement to support the revised Financial Resources Rules. We shall shortly be posting a set of test financial data and the model liquid capital computation to the Commission’s website so that interested parties can use the data to check their own systems.


To access the information described above, please visit our website www.hksfc.org.hk and click on the icon Updates for Intermediaries. The Commission welcomes any further enquiries on the revised Financial Resources Rules. Enquiries should be addressed directly to the Director of the Intermediaries Supervision Department (Fax No. 2526-5304).
