The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today issues a [Guidance Note for Short Selling Reporting and Stock Lending Record Keeping Requirements](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/legislation/regulatory/codes_guide/ss%20guidance-e-printout-aug00.pdf) (Guidance Note). The Guidance Note aims to clarify the legal requirements applicable to short sellers, brokers and stock lenders under the Securities (Amendment) Ordinance 2000 (Amendment Ordinance) and Securities (Stock Lending) Rules 2000 (Rules) which came into operation on 3 July 2000. It also sets out the SFC’s expectations with respect to the requirements. The Guidance Note does not have the force of law but is intended to help the industry to establish procedures that may be needed to put in place in practice to comply with the Amendment Ordinance and the Rules.

With the support of the Government, the SFC will provide the industry a grace period until 3 October 2000 to allow participants to have adequate time to establish proper procedures and build in systems before reporting and record-keeping requirements under the Amendment Ordinance and the Rules will be fully enforced. During this transitional period, the SFC will take action, in the case of failure to comply with the requirements, by way of issuing a warning letter. It should be noted that the grace period is only applicable to the reporting and record-keeping requirements under the Amendment Ordinance and the Rules. However, section 80 of the Securities Ordinance including the new penalties will be strictly enforced. Members of the industry are urged to put in place measures to ensure compliance with the requirements before the end of the grace period.
