The SFC announced today that it had publicly reprimanded {X1} and {X2}, both dealing directors of Sun On Tat Securities Company Limited (Sun On Tat) under the Securities Ordinance (SO). 

The action stemmed from an inquiry covering the period February 1996 to August 1999 in which the SFC found that during that period a person at Sun On Tat had taken client orders whilst being not registered with the SFC. {X1} was responsible for the day to day operation of Sun On Tat and knew of the person's lack of registration but allowed the person to operate in this matter. {X2} admitted he was negligent in that he knew of that the person was unregistered but took no steps to stop the person taking client orders.

The SFC concluded that the conduct of {X1} and {X2} impugned their fitness and properness. Accordingly the SFC decided to publicly reprimand them.
