Mr So Wing Hang (So) was successfully prosecuted at Western Magistracy on 14 June 2000 for providing false or misleading representations in his application to become registered with the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC). So was sentenced to four months imprisonment, suspended for two years.

The offence came to light following an investigation commenced by the SFC and concluded by the Crime Section of Waterfront Police Station. The conviction of So related to his providing a false statement to the SFC that he had no criminal conviction. This statement was made by him in his application to become a registered investment representative under the Securities Ordinance.
