The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it has already requested the Joint Global Coordinators in relation to the MTRC share offer to provide a report by Central Registration on the duplicate share certificates incident. 

In response to media enquiry, SFC Chairman Mr Andrew Sheng said, “I expect that the report will identify the causes of the incident and hope that it will point to steps that can be taken to prevent recurrence of a similar incident in the future.”

“We have also stressed to Central Registration the importance of explaining the incident clearly to the investors and the press and responding to their queries, in order to avoid market confusion.”

On the issue of regulation of share registrars, the SFC will consult the market participants and discuss with the Government on the way forward.
