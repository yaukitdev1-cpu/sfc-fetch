The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it had publicly reprimanded {X1} and {X2} under the Leveraged Foreign Exchange Trading Ordinance for misconduct.

The SFC's action stemmed from an investigation in which it was found that between April and July 1998 the above persons, whilst being the representatives of a leveraged foreign exchange trader licensed with the SFC, had acted as agents for an unlicensed Macau-based entity to carry on a business of leveraged foreign exchange trading.
