The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it had publicly reprimanded {X}, a dealer's representative of Winfull Securities Limited (Winfull) under the Securities Ordinance.

The reprimand stems from an inquiry which revealed that in handling the settlement of client sales on 28 August 1998, {X} had contrary to his company's settlement policy without authority used shares of Winfull's cash clients to cover settlement shortfalls of other clients, thereby exposing cash clients to unnecessary risk.

As a result, the Commission concluded that {X}'s conduct warrants a public reprimand.
