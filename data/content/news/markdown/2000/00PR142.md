The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) published today the latest issue of the [Quarterly Bulletin](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/bulletin/jul-sep00.pdf). The Bulletin has been revamped to include a larger variety of articles and a new section on Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) on Compliance. 

The lead article of this issue, *“Market Segmentation and the Pricing of Different Categories of Stock in Mainland-Incorporated Companies”* is a preliminary study that uses recent international research experience to examine differential pricing between Mainland’s ‘domestic’ A share and ‘foreign’ B and H shares. The article notes that the ‘domestic’ A share typically commands a premium over comparable ‘foreign’ B and H share prices although they offer the same stream of entitlements and voting rights. Moreover, A share markets appear to be more liquid and less volatile than the designated ‘foreign’ investor share markets.

The second article, *“Regulatory Issues Relating to On-line Securities Trading in the Asia Pacific Region”* discusses the challenges securities regulators face in the New Economy.

FAQ on Compliance is a new section that clarifies some of the problems frequently encountered by intermediaries in complying with SFC’s rules and guidelines. In this issue, answers are provided to frequently asked questions about the Client Identity Rule and the Short Selling Guideline.

Interested parties may subscribe the Quarterly Bulletin at $500 per annum or download it from our website (http://www.hksfc.org.hk).
