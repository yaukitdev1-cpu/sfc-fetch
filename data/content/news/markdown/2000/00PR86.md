The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) and the Monetary Authority of Macau (AMCM) signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) in Macau today.

The purpose of the MOU is to enhance the protection of investors and to promote the integrity of the financial markets of Hong Kong and Macau by providing a framework for mutual assistance and the exchange of information to the extent permitted by the laws and practices of the respective jurisdictions.

The signatories to the MOU were Mr Andrew Sheng, Chairman of the SFC, and Mr Anselmo Teng, Chairman of the Monetary Authority of Macau. The AMCM is responsible for policy development and regulation in relation to the monetary, financial, foreign exchange, and insurance markets of Macau.

While signing the MOU, Mr Andrew Sheng said, “We welcome the MOU as it provides a framework for co-operation between the AMCM and the Commission which is becoming increasingly important as cross-border activities between the financial sectors in both economies increase. The Commission looks forward to closely working with the AMCM in future on matters of mutual interest.”

Mr. Anselmo Teng said, "Owing to the close economic linkage between Hong Kong and Macau, it is the responsibility of the financial regulators of both sides to establish an effective mechanism under which the supervision of cross-border activities can be enhanced. The signing of the MOU is timely and we are confident that the MOU will allow better protection of the interests of investors from both Hong Kong and Macau.”

Including this MOU with the AMCM, the SFC has signed a total of thirty-four co-operative arrangements with securities regulators around the world including those of North America, South America, Europe, Asia Pacific and Africa.
