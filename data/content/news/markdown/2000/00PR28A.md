The SFC announced today that it had suspended the registration of Mr Mok Yik Shing (Mok), a dealer's representative of Whole Win Securities Limited, for one month under the Securities Ordinance (SO). 

The SFC's action stemmed from an inquiry which revealed that Mok:-

(a)    had between February to March 1998 failed to make proper enquiry to ensure his client had the securities in the account or had made the necessary borrowing arrangement before executing sell orders on the client's behalf; and

(b)    had concealed his trading through another broker from his employer during the period 12 June 1996 to 9 February 1999 in contravention of its internal policy on staff dealing.

The SFC concluded that Mok's conduct had impugned his fitness and properness to be registered.
