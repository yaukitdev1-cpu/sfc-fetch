The Securities and Futures Commission has published a draft Guidance Note for Persons Advertising or Offering Collective Investment Schemes on the Internet (the CIS Internet Guidance Note). The Commission is seeking the views of market participants and interested parties on the draft.

The CIS Internet Guidance Note aims to clarify the SFC’s expectations about the marketing and offering of collective investment schemes (CIS) on the Internet. The Guidance Note does not impose any new rules.

Mr Andrew Procter, SFC’s Executive Director of Intermediaries and Investment Products, said: “The Internet is becoming a popular advertising and distribution channel for CIS. The SFC wants to encourage investor choice and recognises the need to provide clear guidance to the industry. We hope that the Guidance Note will facilitate market development.”

“The Guidance Note aims to ensure that investors who invest over the Internet have access to all the information they need to make an informed choice,” he said.

The SFC welcomes comments sent in by 31 December 2000. The consultation draft is available on the SFC website at http://www.hksfc.org.hk.
