The SFC announced today that it had publicly reprimanded {X}, a registered dealer's representative of Chief Commodities Limited (Chief Commodities) and Chief Securities Limited (Chief Securities).

The reprimand stems from an investigation in which the SFC found that {X} had allowed an employee of another broker to operate securities and commodities accounts in that employee's name at Chief Securities and Chief Commodities, during the period March 1996 to September 1999, without first having obtained the written consent from the other broker - as required by his employer. {X}'s failure meant that the other broker was unable to properly monitor its employee's trading.
