The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has today published a Guidance Note on the Application of the Electronic Transactions Ordinance to Contract Notes. With the enactment of the Electronic Transactions Ordinance, it is now possible for dealers to issue electronic contract notes. 

The general uncertainty in the securities and futures industry as to whether contract notes can be issued in electronic form was raised as an issue in the SFC’s On-line Trading Working Group. In response, the SFC in consultation with the members of the On-line Trading Working Group, HKEx and the Collector of Stamp Revenue drafted the Guidance Note which clarifies the instances in which contract notes may be issued in electronic form.

“The popularity of on-line trading is growing rapidly in Hong Kong. Through discussions with the members of the On-line Trading Working Group and other parties, the SFC is closely monitoring developments in this area. The Guidance Note summarises the requirements for dealers who wish to issue contract notes in electronic form. We encourage the move towards a paperless work flow and would like to assist dealers where existing legislation may be unclear,” said Mr. Mark Dickens, Executive Director of the Supervision of Markets Division.

“We will continue to publish guidance in relation to our efforts to educate investors on their rights and responsibilities and industry practitioners as to new developments in the market,” he added.

The key points of the Guidance Note are:

- Contract notes are acceptable in electronic form for most transactions in securities, futures and leveraged foreign exchange trading.
- Dealers must remember that the client must consent to the contract note being in electronic form.
- When delivering or retaining original and copies, dealers must ensure that the provisions of the ETO and the other relevant ordinances are complied with.
Dealers who wish to issue electronic contract notes should familiarise themselves with the Guidance Note and the relevant legislation to ensure compliance with the law.
