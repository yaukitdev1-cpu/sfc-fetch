The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has today launched the SFC brochure – The New Regulatory Framework. The brochure contains two parts – an information guide to the new Securities and Futures Bill and an introduction to the SFC. 

“We hope that the Bill will create a more transparent, integrated and firmer regulatory framework to consolidate Hong Kong’s position as an international financial centre. Since the Bill is a large document, we thought a layman’s guide to it might be helpful,” said SFC Chairman Mr Andrew Sheng.

“At the same time, the implementation of the Bill requires a transparent and accountable regulator so we thought an explanation of what the SFC is and does might be useful,” Mr Sheng said.

“The brochure is designed to promote greater understanding of who we are, what our mission and objectives are, and how we intend to regulate the market under the new Bill. I sincerely hope that the fundamental message that comes across is that the SFC is committed to ensuring a transparent and vibrant securities and futures market and that, we wish to work in close partnership with all market players and institutions to achieve this,” Mr Sheng said.

Interested parties may obtain a copy of the brochure from the SFC office or download it from http://www.hksfc.org.hk.
