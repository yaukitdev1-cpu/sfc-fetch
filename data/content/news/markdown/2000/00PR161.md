The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today issues the revised [Fit and Proper Criteria](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/legislation/regulatory/codes_guide/2000fit.pdf), the [Guidance Note on Competence](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/legislation/regulatory/codes_guide/GN-competence%20-%20English.pdf) and the [Guidance Note on Continuous Professional Training](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/legislation/regulatory/codes_guide/GN-CPT-English.pdf) (CPT) to provide guidance to the industry on the new competence and CPT requirement for applicants and licensees.

The SFC believes that the new competence and CPT requirements will affirm the professional standard of financial intermediaries in Hong Kong and further enhance Hong Kong’s competitive position as an international financial centre. The starting date for the new requirements is 1 April 2001.

Background

In the June 1999 consultation paper on “Review of Licensing Regime”, the SFC proposed the setting of competence standards and the establishment of a CPT requirement to ensure that applicants and licensees possess the necessary technical skill and knowledge to perform regulated activities.

In March 2000, the SFC issued two consultation papers on Competence and CPT, detailing its general expectations of applicants and licensees in these two areas. The proposals were well received by the industry and interested parties. Consultation Conclusions on these two papers were published in July 2000.

The Revised Fit and Proper Criteria

The Fit and Proper Criteria have now been revised to incorporate the new competence and CPT requirements. Applicants for a licence and licensees are expected to demonstrate that they comply with the applicable criteria.

Guidance Notes on Competence and CPT

To supplement the Fit and Proper Criteria and to provide more detailed guidance on how the SFC will administer the competence and CPT requirements, two Guidance Notes on Competence and CPT are now issued.

A corporate applicant must be competent to perform the regulated activities efficiently and in particular to manage its business risk. The SFC will assess its competence with reference to its organisational structure, risk management system and personnel.

An individual’s competence will be assessed with regard to his education, industry qualification and work experience. All licence applicants are expected to demonstrate that they have a basic understanding of the market and the services they are to perform by either passing an approved industry based examination or having relevant working experience. Also, they have to pass an approved local regulatory framework examination to demonstrate a basic understanding of the local legislative and regulatory environment.

Both corporate and individual licensees are expected to comply with the CPT requirements. Corporate licensees are obliged to design and implement a continuous education programme best suited to the needs of its licensed directors and representatives. All existing individual licensees are required to achieve 5 CPT hours per calendar year.

Implementation date

All applicants who submit their applications on or after 1 April 2001 will be subject to the new competence requirement. Similarly, all licensees must demonstrate compliance with the CPT requirement one year after 1 April 2001.

Existing licensees must remain competent at all times but will not be required to demonstrate competence again by obtaining the recognised industry qualification and passing the local regulatory framework examination.

Academic and Accreditation Advisory Committee (“AAAC”)

The SFC has established the AAAC with members from both the industry and academic institutions. The AAAC will approve industry qualification and local regulatory framework examinations for meeting the competence standard and endorse training suitable for meeting the CPT requirement. Interested parties are invited to submit courses for consideration by the AAAC.

The SFC will, of course, continue to recognize suitable qualifications obtained prior to 1 April 2001 or upon successful completion of courses commenced prior to 1 April 2001. Regard will be given to the Fit and Proper Criteria as they were prior to 1 April 2001 to ensure that no applicant is unfairly disadvantaged by the change.

Copies of the Revised Fit and Proper Criteria and the Guidance Notes are available for collection at the SFC Office or can be downloaded from the SFC’s web site at http: //www.hksfc.org.hk.
