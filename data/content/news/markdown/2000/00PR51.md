The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) and the Comissao do Mercado de Valores Mobiliarios (CMVM) signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) during the International Organisation of Securities Commissions Annual Conference in Sydney on 16 May 2000. The CMVM, or the Portugal Securities Market Commission, is a public body who regulates and supervises the securities and derivatives markets in Portugal.

The MOU establishes a framework for mutual assistance and facilitates the exchange of information between the two authorities. Furthermore, both the SFC and the CMVM possess statutory powers to provide investigatory assistance to overseas securities regulators and this MOU also documents their intention to co-operate for the protection of investors and preservation of market integrity.

The MOU was signed by Mr Andrew L T Sheng, Chairman of the SFC, and Mr Fernando Teixeira dos Santos, President of CMVM in Sydney.

“As an international financial centre, Hong Kong recognizes the increasing international activity between financial markets. This Memorandum establishes a regulatory link between the Hong Kong and Portugal securities markets and will enhance the two authorities’ ability to ensure market integrity and law enforcement. The Commission is most delighted to work with our Portugal counterpart in the future,” said Mr. Sheng.

In commenting on CMVM’s latest MOU, CMVM Chairman Teixeira dos Santos stated: “This MOU continues the CMVM's efforts to establish cooperative arrangements with foreign authorities. As internationalisation continues to define world markets, each authority needs cooperation in order to achieve an effective regulatory and enforcement program. Indeed, cooperative arrangements, such as the one undertaken today, have become cornerstones in meeting the twin mandates of investor protection and market integrity.”

Including this MOU with the CMVM, the SFC has signed a total of thirty-three cooperative arrangements with securities regulators around the world including those of the United States, Europe and Asia-Pacific region.
