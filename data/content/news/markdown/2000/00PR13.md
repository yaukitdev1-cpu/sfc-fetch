The Securities and Futures Commission announced that it had successfully prosecuted Chan Yuk Ping (Chan) and {X} for acting as dealer&rsquo;s representatives of Sanfull Securities Limited (Sanfull) whilst unregistered.


Chan and {X} pleaded guilty before Ms Polly Lo at Western Magistracy on 22 February 2000. Chan and {X} were fined $5,000 each and ordered to pay costs totalling $16,740 to the SFC.


An investigation by the SFC found that Chan and {X} handled securities trading transactions for Sanfull&rsquo;s clients whilst they were not registered in any capacity with the SFC during the period between 1 November 1998 to 16 March 1999 and 1 August 1998 to 16 March 1999 respectively. They were remunerated by Sanfull via Cosmo Horizon Limited for these unregistered activities.
