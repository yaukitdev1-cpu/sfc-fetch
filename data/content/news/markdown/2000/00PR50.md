The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) makes the following statement in response to a press query concerning the proposed merger between Cable & Wireless HKT Limited (CWHKT) and Pacific Century CyberWorks Limited (PCCW). So far the SFC, as the Takeover Executive under the Takeovers Code, is not aware of any fact which either itself or together with the surrounding circumstances would lead the SFC to conclude that Cable &Wireless plc (C&W) and PCCW are acting in concert. Accordingly, there is no reason why C&W is not entitled to vote in CWHKT's shareholders meeting in relation to the merger under the Takeovers Code.
