The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has today published a *Consultation Paper on the Regulation of On-line Trading of Securities and Futures*.

In June 1999, the Commission published an initial Guidance Note on Internet Regulation (Guidance Note). In recognition of the importance of the continued development of Hong Kong as a premier financial centre and the impact of on-line trading upon the financial services industry, the SFC subsequently set up an On-line Trading Working Group (Working Group) to study the growth of the on-line trading market, identify any impediments to its progress and make recommendations to facilitate its development in Hong Kong. The resulting Consultation Paper, released today, sets out the regulatory issues identified by the Working Group and contains a number of proposals to expand the Guidance Note.

“We expect the popularity of on-line trading will continue to grow rapidly in Hong Kong. The SFC has already issued a number of guidance notes, circulars and guidelines for the industry on this topic. The proposals in this paper are generally related to the clarification and enhancement of existing guidance,” said Mr Mark Dickens, Executive Director of the Supervision of Markets Division.

In particular the Consultation Paper addresses issues of investor protection, operational standards and the new challenges ahead. The proposals include:

- minimum record keeping as well as suitability requirements;
- methods to ensure that system integrity is properly addressed, and possibly, requiring independent certifications of systems;
- minimum standards for adequate system, security, reliability, capacity and contingency planning;
- stressing the importance of information technology competence and adequate human resources; and
- standards for the operation of chat rooms by registered firms.
The SFC welcomes comments from industry practitioners and members of the investing public on the proposals in the Consultation Paper and specifically invites comments on the desirability of independent certifications of systems.
