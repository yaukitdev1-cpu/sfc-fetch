A new series of investor education radio segments entitled “Basics of Smart Investing”, which is sponsored by the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC), will begin airing on 19 September 2000 (Tuesday) on Commercial Radio 1 Cantonese channel.

There are sixteen 3-minute segments, which through lively chit-chat with the audience, aim to help retail investors understand the importance of knowing their investments and the risks they may face when investing.

Topics in the current series include initial public offerings, on-line trading, unlicensed dealing, order dispute, unauthorised transactions on trading account, pooling arrangement in margin financing, derivative warrants, consolidation of shares, share splits, how to read company announcements and annual reports.

Beginning 19 September, the segments will air at around 2:20 pm on each Tuesday and Friday, in the “Crossirade” programme, which is broadcast from 2 – 3 pm, Monday to Friday, on Commercial Radio 1 (FM88.1 – 89.5). The programme hosts, Mr Lennon Chan Wing Luk, Mr Tsang Chi Ying and Ms Chan Wai Yee, are also voices for the radio segments.

Since 1998, the SFC has launched similar education radio programmes on Radio Television Hong Kong’s Cantonese and Putonghua channels. Positive response was received, and some of the radio programmes are now available on the SFC’s web site, located at http : // www . hksfc . org . hk/.

“Knowledge is not only power when it comes to investment – it’s investor’s safety net. That is why the SFC puts so much emphasis on investor education,” an SFC spokesman said.
