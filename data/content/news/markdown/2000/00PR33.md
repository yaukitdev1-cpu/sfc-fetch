On Monday 27 March 2000, the Takeovers and Mergers Panel ruled that share repurchases by a company are acquisitions of voting rights under the Takeovers and Mergers Code (“Code”). The Panel ruled that in consequence share repurchases give rise to disqualifying transactions under the terms of Whitewash Guidance Note to the Code. As a result, share repurchases may invalidate a waiver granted by the Takeovers Executive from an obligation under the Code to make a general offer for the shares of a listed company (a “whitewash waiver”). 

The Panel wished to take this opportunity to remind practitioners and parties seeking a whitewash waiver of the very clear requirements of the Code for prior consultation with the Executive and the need for full disclosure of all relevant facts when making a whitewash application.

The Takeovers Executive made a referral to the Panel following an announcement by Regent on 15 March 2000, that it had agreed to buy Interman Holdings Limited, an internet investment company, from Regent's chairman, Mr. James Mellon. If the sale goes ahead, the consideration of HK$634,536,596 will be settled by Regent issuing 226,620,213 new shares to Mr. Mellon at $2.80 per share thereby increasing the aggregate shareholding of Mr. Mellon and his concert parties in Regent from 22.5% to 37.8%. As a result, under the Code, Mr. Mellon will be obliged to make a general offer for all the remaining shares in Regent unless the Takeovers Executive grants him a whitewash waiver. No application for a waiver has yet been made.

On 17 March 2000, the Company purchased 3,559,000 of its own shares on market, amounting to 0.38% of its issued share capital. These purchases increased the aggregate shareholding of Mr. Mellon and parties acting in concert with him by 0.2% from 22.5% to 22.7%.

In respect of Regent, the Panel noted that in the event that an application for a whitewash waiver was made, the Takeovers Executive has discretion over whether or not to grant such waiver having regard to all the relevant circumstances including the share repurchases by Regent.
