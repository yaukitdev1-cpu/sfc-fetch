An anonymous email was sent to the HKFE, the SFC, the Government and the media on 6 January 2000 regarding the problems encountered by the options pricing system of the open outcry system (HSI Options).

On 4 January 2000, the HKFE reported to the SFC that the options pricing system of the open outcry system (HSI Options) had encountered some problems. The wrong prices were found as a result of incorrect values stored in the HKFE’s databases. The wrong prices resulted from the incorrect calculation of expiration dates to trading days of each series of option traded. Therefore, the duration of options were incorrectly calculated.

The minor incident involving the incorrect calculation of the indicative HSI 33 Options prices via the TIPS, which are normally used by the traders as a reference price to facilitate trading, was reported to the SFC which in turn reported the incident to the Financial Sector Emergency Coordination Center.

The incident was not considered to be material, as it did not affect the normal trading operations of the HKFE. Furthermore, the HKFE had put in place backup procedures to calculate the theoretical prices and at the same time investigated into the problem. A solution was found soon after.

The incident was quickly corrected (before the opening of the second session) and in no way did it ever affect the normal trading of the HSI 33 Options products.

At the outset, it was unascertainable if the incident was Y2K related. Once a solution to the problem was found and the incident corrected, it was found to be a Y2K related incident.
