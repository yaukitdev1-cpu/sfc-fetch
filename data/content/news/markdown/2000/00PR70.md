The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that on 13 June 2000 it had successfully prosecuted Insinger Secretaries (HK) Ltd (Insinger) for acting as a Dealer without being registered.

An authorized representative of Insinger pleaded guilty before Ms Polly Lo at Western Magistracy. Insinger was fined $7,500 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $4,127 to the SFC.

The SFC investigation revealed that during the period between 16 March to 22 April 1999 Insinger had received trading instructions from one of its clients and relayed the securities trading orders to a broker for execution in return for remuneration from the said client. During the investigation period, Insinger, its directors and employees were not registered with the SFC in any capacity.
