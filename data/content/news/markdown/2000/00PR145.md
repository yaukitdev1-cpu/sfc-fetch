Mr. Zhou Xiaochuan, Chairman of the China Securities Regulatory Commission (CSRC) today attended a seminar jointly organized by the Securities and Futures Commission of Hong Kong and Hong Kong Securities Institute.  As the keynote speaker, Chairman Zhou introduced to the audience the latest developments of China’s capital markets and some of the major tasks of the CSRC.

Chairman Zhou said that in a bid to promote the development of the mainland capital markets, the CSRC is now working towards the following directions:

1. To promote and perfect relevant laws and regulations;
2. To improve market transparency and standards of disclosure;
3. To enhance investor protection;
4. To step up the regulatory framework and the monitoring systems of the Shanghai and Shenzhen Stock Exchanges;
5. To further open up and internationalize China’s securities market;
6. To prepare for the establishment of a Venture Board in Shenzhen;
7. To develop institutional investors;
8. To facilitate securities trading on the Internet;
9. To engage in vigorous training of securities practitioners and active promotion for the development of financial institutions;
10. To further promote investor education programmes;
11. To strengthen the communications between the CSRC and the government departments; and
12. To strengthen the monitoring role of the media and public opinions.


“I am very delighted to have been able to meet and exchange views with the securities practitioners in the Hong Kong SAR, and also to discuss the status and recent developments of the mainland capital markets from a macro-perspective. It is my hope that the talk today will be conducive to the better understanding of the operations of the mainland markets as well as forging stronger ties between the markets on the mainland and the Hong Kong SAR, ” noted Chairman Zhou after the talk.
