The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has today posted on its web-site a circular on the [Provision of Financial Information on the Internet – Licensing Requirements.](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/general/general/press_release/00/00pr78_attach.pdf) The circular reminds internet web-site operators who provide financial information or investment advice that they must observe relevant licensing requirements.

"Web-sites offering financial information and advice, targeted at Hong Kong investors, are becoming more common. This web-based financial information industry raises some important regulatory and investor protection issues. The SFC wishes to encourage market innovation without compromising investor protection. Providing financial services through the internet offers great opportunities and we are happy to meet with industry to answer any regulatory questions that may arise", said Mr Andrew Procter, Executive Director of the SFC.

The Circular describes the licensing requirements for those who provide financial information or advice through internet web-sites. It also outlines the SFC's general view on specific issues pertaining to the licensing of these advisers. The issues include:

i. General Regulatory Approach;

ii. Remuneration (in respect of advisory activities);

iii. Periodical Publication Exemption;

iv. Investment or Commodity Trading Advising;

v. Hyperlinks to Other Financial Web-sites;

vi. Provision of Analytical Tools;

vii. Provision of Electronic Trading or Advisory Platforms; and

viii. Advisory Services Provided by Overseas Web-sites.

Persons engaged in the provision of financial information or investment advice via internet web-sites are advised to review the Circular which is posted under the "What's New" and "Updates for Intermediaries" sections of the SFC Web-site at [http:// www.hksfc.org.hk](http://www.sfc.hk/). 

 They are also welcomed to call the Licensing Hotline at 2840 9393.
