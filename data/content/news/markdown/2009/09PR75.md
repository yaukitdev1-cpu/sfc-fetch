The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the appointment and re- appointment by the Financial Secretary of members to the SFC Advisory Committee for two years from 1 June 2009 to 31 May 2011.

“I warmly welcome new members of the Advisory Committee, whose expertise and experience in their respective fields will be a valuable asset to the Advisory Committee. I also look forward to the continued support and advice of the re-appointed members,” said Mr Eddy Fong, the SFC’s Chairman.

“I would also like to take the opportunity to thank the five retiring members for their invaluable insights and contribution to the SFC in the past years,” Mr Fong added.
