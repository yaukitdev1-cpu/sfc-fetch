The latest edition of the [Enforcement Reporter](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/enforcement/09/jan_09.pdf) published by the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) shows that the regulator completed 85% of its investigations during 2008 within seven months, reflecting further enhancement of investigation efficiency. This was achieved despite the larger number and increased complexity of investigations handled. This edition also addresses some general misconceptions about what an SFC investigation involves.

The previous Reporter highlighted the commencement of three separate proceedings in the High Court seeking orders against nine current and former company directors for alleged misconduct as well as a number of important market misconduct proceedings arising from our investigations. Recently, the SFC obtained a disqualification order of five years against a former company director. This was the second time that the SFC had obtained a disqualification order.

The Reporter also gives an account of recent disciplinary cases, drawing attention to the importance of fair treatment to clients and adequate controls and supervision.

The Enforcement Reporter is a newsletter that highlights key enforcement outcomes and issues. It is available on the SFC website under “Speeches, Publications & Consultations – Publications”.

The public are invited to subscribe to the Enforcement Reporter by registering for the Update Alert service on the SFC website. They will then be able to receive the newsletter via email.

The SFC welcomes public feedback to improve the Enforcement Reporter. Comments can be sent to [enfreporter@sfc.hk](mailto:%20enfreporter@sfc.hk).
