The Securities and Futures Commission’s (SFC) first-ever photo competition attracted more than one thousand entries from the public with the winners being announced today (Note 1).

The photo competition, which is part of the SFC’s investor education campaign “Be Smart, Ask First”, highlights the importance of asking the right questions before investing. Launched in March, contestants were invited to submit photos and captions which answered the question – “Why am I investing?”

 “Building on the success of the photo competition, we will roll out more investor education initiatives to raise investors’ awareness of the importance of doing homework and asking the right questions before investing,” said Mr Martin Wheatley, Chief Executive Officer of the SFC.

The top three winners were presented with cash prizes at a ceremony held at the Maritime Square in Tsing Yi today. (Notes 2 and 3)

The overall winner Mr Ho Chung Ip took home a cash prize of $8,000, with the first runner-up, Ms Chow Oi Ling, and second runner-up, Ms Pang Mei Ching, receiving $5,000 and $2,000 respectively. In addition, five merit prize winners each received a digital camera worth $1,500 and 20 consolation prize winners each received shopping coupons worth $500.

The public is welcome to view the photos of the 28 finalists at an exhibition at the Maritime Square which runs through Sunday (28 June 2009).

As part of the SFC’s effort to broaden its reach to the community, a series of investor education activities are also being held at the Maritime Square over the weekend.

Today Mr Eddie Lam, an experienced investment advisor, discussed the importance of developing a proper investment attitude before today’s award ceremony.

Tomorrow a children’s singing contest will be held on the theme song for SFC’s TV advert – “Ask Me”. Mrs Francine Fu, President of the Institute of Financial Planners of Hong Kong, will share with parents some key financial planning considerations for their children’s future.
