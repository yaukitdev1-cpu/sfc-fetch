The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) encourages the finance industry to increase collaboration in contributing to the continued opening up of Mainland capital markets.

Speaking today at the Taifook Securities Investor Conference 2009, Mrs Alexa Lam, the SFC’s Deputy Chief Executive Officer and Executive Director, Policy, China and Investment Products, addressed a group of institutional investors and corporate representatives from the region on Hong Kong’s strategic value to the Mainland.

“Hong Kong’s value lies in its position as an international offshore market, a place where the Mainland can conduct capital market experiments in an environment that operates according to international rules while providing business models that, once honed, can be seamlessly adopted on the Mainland,” said Mrs Lam.

“For Hong Kong, the Mainland is an unparalleled source of support and resources, providing a strong bulwark for continued development and innovation,” she added.

Mrs Lam highlighted the key role of the SFC in mapping and executing plans ahead:


- Internationalisation of the RMB – the SFC will work closely with other local and Mainland regulators to develop Hong Kong into an RMB offshore center.
- The Greater Pearl River Delta Development Plan – the SFC is exploring areas in which our qualified securities dealers may broaden their business opportunities in the region.
- Closer ties with Taiwan market through ETF development – to integrate Taiwan into the greater China trading platform, the SFC is working on a Memorandum of Understanding with Taiwan to enable cross-listings of ETFs in Taiwan and Hong Kong.
- GEM Board on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange – as the Shenzhen Stock Exchange rolls out its milestone GEM program, the SFC looks forward to sharing its experience and will encourage its intermediaries to provide relevant support.
