The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has today launched an on-line portal ([https://portal.sfc.hk/](https://portal.sfc.hk/sfcportal/signin)), which will streamline its licensing processes and procedures and enhance communication efficiency between intermediaries and the SFC.

The first phase of this 30-month project will offer more than 36,000 licensees, registrants and associated entities (Note 1) the option of submitting their licensing notifications and annual returns electronically. In the first quarter of 2010 this will be enhanced with the facility to accept on-line payments for annual fees and various other charges. Using the portal, users can also view and update their profile information, at any time, and receive messages from the SFC.

When the second phase of the portal is operational in the first half of 2011, users will be able to carry out virtually all licensing processes and procedures, including the submission of licensing applications, via the Internet. At that time, the use of paper forms will largely be eliminated.

"We are committed to streamlining our systems and procedures to make them more user friendly and efficient,” said the SFC’s Chief Executive Officer Mr Martin Wheatley. “This multi-phase project, when completed, will result in the SFC having world-class systems for use by our licensees.”

A pilot run of the portal, involving approximately 1,600 users, was launched on 14 August 2009 and has generated helpful feedback from users. The portal has undergone some minor modification and enhancement during that period and is operating satisfactorily.

Between now and the end of October 2009, all remaining users will receive letters inviting them to use the portal. These letters will provide user names, passwords and usage instructions.
