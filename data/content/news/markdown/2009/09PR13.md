In its third quarterly report of 2008-09, released today, the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has highlighted various initiatives undertaken in response to the ongoing financial crisis, including continuing action to tackle market misconduct.

The SFC’s [Quarterly Report](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/quartely/oct_dec_08.pdf) for October to December 2008 describes how the regulator has commenced multiple investigations into alleged mis-selling of Lehman Brothers Minibonds “from the top down” so that complaints are looked into collectively rather than case by case. The conclusion of the first such investigation into a brokerage, which was announced in January, reflected the merits of this approach. It also highlights the benefits to investors of a negotiated settlement, given that the SFC has no legal powers to order compensation.

In late December, the SFC submitted a report to the Financial Secretary on “Issues raised by the Lehmans Minibond crisis”. This report has recommended a review of the current regulatory framework and proposes a number of improvements which could be made to enhance investor protection.

On the enforcement front, the SFC obtained the second disqualification order from the High Court to ban a former director of a delisted company from acting as a company director or being involved in the management of any company for five years, without court approval.

With investor education, focus was placed on reminding investors to consider the most basic factors, along with ongoing efforts to raise awareness for risks.

The latest Quarterly Report is available on the SFC website at www.sfc.hk.
