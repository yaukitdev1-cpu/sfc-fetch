Robust regulation and sound management practices will help restore investor confidence in the fund industry, according to Mrs Alexa Lam, the Deputy Chief Executive Officer and Executive Director, Policy, China and Investment Products, of the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC).

Speaking today at the Hong Kong Investment Funds Association luncheon, Mrs Lam highlighted the importance of adequate risk disclosures, proper suitability advice and investor education in regaining confidence that has been heavily battered in the recent market turmoil.

“Now is the time to show investors that you understand their concerns, that you are willing to walk the extra mile to give them the information that makes it easier for them to understand,” Mrs Lam told the audience.

Mrs Lam also called on fund managers to be responsive to market demand under the current economic climate.

“Fund managers can provide value by offering products that help investors capture opportunities arising from changing market conditions,” Mrs Lam explained. “It is important for Hong Kong as an international financial centre to continue to offer a broad and deep range of investment products.

An inverse / short exchange-traded fund is one of the new products that the SFC is processing. The SFC is also studying a bond fund that seeks to take advantage of the current steep discount in the pricing of investment and non-investment grade corporate bonds.

Mrs Lam pointed out that the SFC has also authorised more than 80 new funds since mid-September. She called for a constructive and two-way dialogue between the regulator and industry players to help rebuild market confidence.
