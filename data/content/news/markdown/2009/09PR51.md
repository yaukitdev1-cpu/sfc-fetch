The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) and the State Securities Commission of Viet Nam (SSC) (Note 1) today signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) to strengthen regulatory co-operation between the two regulators.

The MOU establishes a framework for mutual assistance and facilitates the exchange of information between the SFC and the SSC. The enforcement of the securities laws and regulations in Hong Kong and Viet Nam will benefit from the closer co-operation made possible by the MOU.

The MOU was signed by Mr Eddy Fong, Chairman of the SFC, and Mr Vu Bang, Chairman of the SSC. The signing took place in Hong Kong to coincide with a visit by Prime Minister Nguyen Tan Dung of Viet Nam, who was also present at the ceremony.

Mr Fong said: “This Memorandum opens a new chapter in our working relationship with the SSC. With this Memorandum our respective abilities to protect our markets and enforce our laws will be enhanced. I am delighted with the formal establishment of co-operation between the SFC and SSC under the MOU.”

Mr Vu said: “The MOU lifts the co-operation relations between the two regulators up to a higher layer of development towards the sustainable development of our two securities markets in the future.”
