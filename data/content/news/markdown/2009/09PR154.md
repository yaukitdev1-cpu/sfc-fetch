The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today publishes a user friendly, plain language guide to help the investing public better understand how the securities and futures markets are regulated and the SFC’s role in the overall regulatory structure.

“One of the key missions of the SFC is investor education -- to enhance the public’s understanding of the markets and the risks of investing. This brochure will complement our investor education efforts by providing a straightforward summary of our current regulatory regime,” said Mr Martin Wheatley, the Chief Executive Officer of the SFC.

The brochure gives a high level overview of how regulators, market players and investors can play their respective part in contributing to a healthy investment environment.

“The SFC Guide is a publication designed to facilitate informed discussion. As well as being helpful to those who wish to better understand what we do, we hope that this publication will provide useful background to our recently released consultation paper on proposals to enhance investor protection. We encourage members of the public to write to us and share their views,” Mr Wheatley added.

The brochure will be distributed at SFC investor outreach activities and public libraries. Both the [consultation paper](/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/consultation/doc?refNo=09CP3) and the [SFC Guide](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/sfc_guide/WhoWeAreWhatWeDo.pdf) are available on the SFC website (www.sfc.hk) and the InvestEd website (www.InvestEd.hk).
