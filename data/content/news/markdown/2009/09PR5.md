This month, in the approach to the Lunar New Year, Dr Wise advises investors about investing in gold, a historical favourite with many Asian investors.

In his latest column entitled, [“Is gold a safe bet?”](http://www.invested.hk/invested/en/html/section/dr_wise/2009articles/gold_jan09.html) Dr Wise explains to retail investors some risks associated with trading bullion.

The bullion market is self regulated and designed mainly for wholesalers or industry participants, who buy or sell the metal to meet business needs. To participate in the bullion market, retail investors will have to deal through bullion investment companies, usually by investing relatively small sums to conduct leveraged trading. Although bullion trading has been operating smoothly over many years, Dr Wise says, retail investors should be aware that bullion investment companies are not regulated and that leveraged trading involves high risks.

Dr Wise reminds investors that to get exposure to gold, they may also consider various regulated investment products, e.g. gold futures contracts, gold exchange-traded funds and gold passbooks. Depending on their product nature, the degree and scope of the risks involved in these other gold investments will vary. “Before picking gold, as with any other investment, find out as much as you can about the possible risks,” Dr Wise says. “Be honest with your personal circumstances and risk appetite. Sometimes all that glitters is not gold.”
