The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today publishes the [Report on the Securities and Futures Commission’s 2008 Annual Review of the Exchange’s Performance in its Regulation of Listing Matters](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/surveys/09/09PR49_sehk_eng.pdf) (Note 1).

The SFC is of the view that the operational procedures and decision-making processes reviewed were appropriate to enable the Exchange to discharge its statutory obligation to maintain an orderly, informed and fair market in 2007.

Mr Brian Ho, SFC’s Executive Director of Corporate Finance, said: “We are satisfied that the Exchange has taken steps to address the recommendation in our 2007 report. We find that the Exchange’s performance has improved in the past four years. At the same time, we identified certain areas where we recommend that the Exchange should continue its efforts to enhance its performance.”

The SFC will continue to discharge its statutory responsibility to supervise, monitor and regulate the activities carried on by the Exchange.

End

Notes:

1. In March 2004, the Government published its Consultation Conclusions on Proposals to Enhance the Regulation of Listing. Among other matters, the Government recommended that the SFC prepares annual reports on its reviews regarding the Exchange’s performance of its listing functions. This is the SFC’s fourth report following the Government’s recommendation. The report is available on the SFC website.
