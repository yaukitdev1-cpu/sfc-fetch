In the current volatile markets, now more than ever, investors should do their homework before investing, Dr Wise says. It is also important that investors regularly review the performance of their investment portfolio to check changes to their risk exposure.

In his latest column entitled “[Dr Wise’s guide to investment homework](http://www.invested.hk/invested/en/html/section/dr_wise/2009articles/homework_apr09.html),” Dr Wise tells investors where to look up investment information to improve and update their investment knowledge.

In today’s electronic age, investors have easy access to local and overseas websites offering information on both product knowledge and market specific advice. This allows investors to efficiently conduct their own research and to monitor the exposure of their investment portfolio. They can start with the SFC’s investor portal, the [InvestEd website](http://www.invested.hk/invested/en/html/section/index.html) ([www.InvestEd.hk](http://www.invested.hk/invested/en/html/section/index.html)), which provides up-to-date educational information about financial products and regulations. The site also offers easy access to other useful websites via links.

For information on listed companies or products traded on the local exchanges, e.g., derivative warrants, callable bull and bear contracts, investors can check the [HKExnews](http://www.hkexnews.hk/index.htm) website. The trading information of products can be found on the websites of their issuers as well.

“When considering new investments, do not take an intermediary’s recommendations at face value,” Dr Wise says. “Arm yourself with at least a minimum level of knowledge. Find out enough to ask the right questions and assess if an investment product is suitable for your particular circumstances.”

Through his column, Dr Wise, a fictitious character created by the SFC, shares with the public his wisdom on investing and his thoughts on regulatory issues.

Dr Wise’s column is published on the last working Tuesday of every month. The column is posted under the “Dr Wise” section of the SFC’s InvestEd website. Investors are welcome to send their feedback and questions to Dr Wise by email to [InvestEd@sfc.hk](mailto:%20InvestEd@sfc.hk).
