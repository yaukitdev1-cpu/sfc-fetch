In its first quarterly report of 2009-10, released today, the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) highlights the progress achieved in its enforcement actions, along with the initiatives taken to further foster growth of Hong Kong’s financial market.

In terms of enforcement actions, the SFC’s [Quarterly Report](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/quartely/apr_jun_09.pdf) for April to June 2009 covers the agreement with a second brokerage to repurchase Minibonds from its clients at a price equal to the principal amount invested. The report also describes the SFC’s first intervention in court proceedings regarding the proposed delisting and privatisation of a listed company and the subsequent court ruling at the appellate level.

During the period, the SFC also secured court clarification crucial to its regulatory work. In May, a judgment delivered by the Court of Appeal allowed the SFC to re-impose an injunction against certain defendants for alleged insider dealing.

Meanwhile, the SFC exchanged with the Taiwan Financial Supervisory Commission a Side Letter, which paved the way for the first Hong Kong exchange-traded funds (ETFs) to be approved for offering in Taiwan and the first Taiwan ETF to list in Hong Kong.

The SFC analysed the implications of a renminbi internationalisation proposal on the Mainland and Hong Kong, and reviewed Hong Kong-related policies in the Mainland’s current 11th Five-year Plan as part of its efforts to capitalise on the Mainland's integration with the world economy.

The report also describes the SFC’s continued use of the mass media and interactive approaches for its investor education programmes. Initiatives launched included the first photo competition and the third Investment Story Competition, which were designed to promote a healthy investment attitude and the importance of understanding risks before investing.

The latest Quarterly Report is available on the SFC website at [www.sfc.hk](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/).
