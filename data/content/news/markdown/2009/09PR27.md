Investors are invited to consider the important question of why they invest and then portray this as a picture in the Securities and Futures Commission’s (SFC) photo competition.

This competition is part of the SFC’s investor education campaign -- “Be Smart, Ask First” -- that highlights the importance of asking the right questions before investing.

“The title of the photo competition, ‘Why am I investing’, is first of the five elementary questions that the SFC suggests investors ask before investing their hard earned money,” said Mr Martin Wheatley, the SFC’s Chief Executive Officer.

“Only when investors have addressed this key question will they be able to consider how best they can achieve their investment objective. We hope that our photo competition will be a fun way to reinforce this key message,” he added.

Entries are accepted via the InvestEd website starting from today until 30 April 2009. Participants can win attractive prizes by capturing their thoughts in a photo. Sing Tao Daily, Headline Daily and PC Market are media partners in this event.
