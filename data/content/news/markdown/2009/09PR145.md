The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has recently identified a number of websites of bogus organizations claiming to be Hong Kong’s securities or financial services regulator.

The SFC warns investors not to rely on any statements or representations from the fake regulators as none of these websites contain any legitimate material.

These bogus regulatory websites are often set up by fraudsters to deceive unwary investors into thinking that certain unauthorised brokers or banks are supervised by a statutory regulator.

These websites are sophisticated and often contain up-to-date finance news to create a false impression of authenticity.

The following fake websites were identified by the SFC and added to our Alert List on our investor portal InvestEd ([http://www.invested.hk/InvestEdAlertList/jsp/en/AlertList.jsp](http://www.invested.hk/InvestEdAlertList/jsp/en/AlertList.jsp)) (Note1).


- Financial Services Regulator of Hong Kong (www.fsrhk.org);
- Hong Kong Financial Securities Board (www.hkfsb.org);
- Hong Kong Financial Services Authority (www.hkfsa.org); and
- Hong Kong Financial Trading Authority (www.hkfta.org)

None of the websites are or were hosted in Hong Kong and some of them have already been shut down following our action.

Investors who want to verify authenticity of a regulator can check if it is a member of the International Organization of Securities Commissions ([www.iosco.org](http://www.iosco.org)).


End

Notes:

1. The Alert List is a list of entities which have come to the attention of the SFC because they are unlicensed in Hong Kong and are believed to be, or to have been, targeting Hong Kong investors or claim to have an association with Hong Kong.
