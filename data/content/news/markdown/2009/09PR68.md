The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the appointment by the Financial Secretary of Mr Wong Kai–man to succeed Dr York Liao as Non-Executive Director for a term of two years, with effect from 26 May 2009 to 25 May 2011.

“Mr Wong is a senior professional in the accounting field and I am sure he will bring with him a wealth of experience and expertise for the benefit of the Board as well as the securities industry. I am delighted to have him on the Board and look forward to working closely with him,” said the SFC’s Chairman, Mr Eddy Fong.

“I would also like to take this opportunity to thank Dr York Liao for his contribution to the SFC over the past six years. We have benefited tremendously from the advice and insights of Dr Liao over the years,“ Mr Fong added.
