The latest edition of the [Enforcement Reporter](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/enforcement/09/apr_09.pdf) published by the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) reports that the SFC has secured seven criminal convictions in three insider dealing cases within nine months. Three persons were sent to jail immediately in two of the cases. The immediate jail sentences, which were handed down for the first time for insider dealing, reflect the seriousness of insider dealing and mark a new chapter in the SFC’s programme in tackling market misconduct.

The Reporter gives an account of the conclusion of two Lehman Brothers Minibonds investigations. By agreements made under the Securities and Futures Ordinance, two firms offered to purchase from eligible clients all outstanding Minibonds bought through them, resulting in a triple-win resolution for investors, the firms and the SFC.

Also reported is the SFC’s decision to apply to intervene in court proceedings regarding proposals to de-list and privatise PCCW.

As an update on previous reports that the SFC commenced proceedings in the High Court seeking orders against company directors for alleged misconduct, this issue notes that a disqualification order of six years was obtained against a former company director. This was the third time that the SFC had obtained a disqualification order.

This issue also covers the Securities and Futures Appeals Tribunal’s confirmation of the first SFC decision to revoke the licence of a securities firm.

The Enforcement Reporter is a newsletter that highlights key enforcement outcomes and issues. It is available on the SFC website under “Speeches, Publications & Consultations” – “Publications”.
