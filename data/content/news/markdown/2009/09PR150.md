The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the re-appointment by the Chief Executive of Mr Eddy Fong as Chairman for a two-year term effective from 20 October 2009.

“We look forward to continue working with Mr Fong to further strengthen Hong Kong’s financial regulatory regime and enhance Hong Kong’s status as an international financial centre,” said Mr Martin Wheatley, the SFC’s Chief Executive Officer.

The SFC also welcomes the re-appointment of the Hon Chan Kam-lam and appointment of Mr Lawrence Lee Kam-hung by the Financial Secretary as Non-Executive Directors for a term of two years, effective from 15 November 2009.

“We would like to express our gratitude to outgoing Non-Executive Director Mr Christopher Cheng for his dedication and support to the SFC during the past six years,” added Mr Wheatley.

Mr Cheng’s current term expires on 14 November 2009.
