The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) is pleased to note that the Process Review Panel for the SFC (Panel) concluded in its latest Annual Report that the SFC had generally followed its internal procedures and complied with operational guidelines.

The Panel, whose Annual Report is published by the Government today, reviewed 55 cases in 2008-09 and assessed if the standard procedures laid down in the operational manuals were complied with where necessary.

The SFC notes that some of the Panel's recommendations had already been fully or partially addressed as the SFC constantly upgrades its internal procedures to enhance the effectiveness and efficiency of regulation.

The SFC looks forward to continuing its co-operation with the Panel this year.
