The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) invites the public to share their investment experiences by taking part in a story-telling competition aimed at promoting a healthy investment attitude and highlighting the importance of risk management before making investment decisions.

The “Investment Story Competition 2009” is jointly organised by the SFC and its media partners, Commercial Radio One, Hong Kong Economic Journal and Cable TV. Contestants will be asked to recount their own valuable experience, or that of their friends and families, in investing in any SFC-regulated investment products.

To encourage broader participation in this year’s competition, apart from the Public Group open to the general public, full-time students are invited to take part in a newly created Student Group.

The factors taken into account in judging contestants’ entries will include the significance of product knowledge, investment attitude and risk management in their investing experiences, and how investor education has shaped their own experiences.

Cash prizes totalling $16,000 will be offered to the top three winners of each group. Six merit prizes will also be awarded (Note 1). Entries can be submitted in English or Chinese, in written or recorded video format. Rules of the competition and entry forms are available at the SFC office and on the SFC InvestEd website at [www.InvestEd.hk](http://www.invested.hk/invested/en/html/section/index.html). Submissions must be made by 27 July 2009.

A series of trailers will be aired on Commercial Radio One for eight weeks from 2 June to promote the competition.

Adjudicators of the competition are Mr Chan King Cheung, Chief Editor, Hong Kong Economic Journal; Mr Lennon Chan Wing Luk, a consultant and programme host of Commercial Radio; Mr Ronald Chiu, Executive Director of i-Cable News Ltd and Member of the SFC Investor Education Advisory Committee; and Mr Wong Siu Fun, Chief Executive of the Hong Kong Securities Institute.

Winning entries will be published in the Hong Kong Economic Journal, Cable TV and the InvestEd website. Selected winning entries will be edited and adapted into dramas to be broadcast in the “CrossTrade” programme on Commercial Radio One (FM 88.1 - 89.5).
