The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) and the Hong Kong Society of Financial Analysts (HKSFA) jointly invite the public to participate in an investor education event on the economic outlook of Hong Kong and the Mainland for the second half of 2009.

The full day event consists of seminars of different topics – the economic outlook of Hong Kong and the Mainland, and the prospect of Hong Kong-listed property developers and banks. Members of the HKSFA will be on hand to share their views on these topics with the investing public.

"Before making any investment decision, investors should always try to gain a better understanding of key macro-economic trends. These seminars highlight the importance of knowledge in these issues for making informed decisions. We are very pleased to partner with the HKSFA in hosting the event," the SFC's Chief Executive Officer, Mr Martin Wheatley said.

Those who are interested in the event can enrol online via the [HKSFA website](http://www.hksfa.org/event_zh.asp?id=98) (www.hksfa.org) or contact the HKSFA on 2521 2453 for details. Enrolment is on a first-come-first-served basis.
