The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) is pleased to announce new appointments and re-appointments to the following committees with effect from 1 April 2009:


- Academic and Accreditation Advisory Committee
- Committee on Real Estate Investment Trusts
- Public Shareholders Group
- SFC (HKEC Listing) Committee
- Share Registrars’ Disciplinary Committee
- Takeovers and Mergers Panel
- Takeovers Appeal Committee

The SFC welcomes the new committee members whose expertise and significant experience in their respective fields will contribute to the effective performance of committee functions. It also expresses gratitude to outgoing members for their valuable contributions over the years.

End
