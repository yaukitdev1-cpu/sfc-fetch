The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) will launch its first ever interactive TV game show aimed at promoting better understanding of investment products, securities markets and the rights of investors. This programme has been developed in response to SFC surveys which consistently show that the vast majority of the public identify TV as the primary source of financial information.

 "SFC special: Outsmart" -- jointly produced by the SFC and TVB -- will feature scores of celebrities as contestants. The programme will be broadcast on TVB’s Jade channel Monday to Friday between 10:30 pm and 11:00 pm from 16 March to 27 March.

"This programme is part of SFC's on-going investor education initiatives and I hope that investors will benefit from the various issues discussed in the game show," the SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, Mr Martin Wheatley said.

The game show is made up of two parts: a question and answer session and a case study session.

Apart from having celebrity contestants competing in the show, spectators will have opportunities to test their own investment knowledge and will be invited to provide answers to the questions.

Professionals from the securities industry and members of the academia on hand will explain the correct answers to the various issues raised in the show.
