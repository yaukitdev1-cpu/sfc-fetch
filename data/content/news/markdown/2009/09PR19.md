The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today issued a [consultation paper](/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/consultation/doc?refNo=09CP1) proposing legislative amendments to facilitate remote access to Hong Kong’s futures markets by overseas industry participants.

It is proposed that the definition of “dealing in futures contracts” (Note 1) under the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO) be amended so that overseas players who are participants of a Hong Kong-based futures market (Note 2) do not need to be licensed by the SFC, although this will be subject to a number of limitations. They will not be able to establish a place of business in Hong Kong, conduct any dealing activities locally, serve Hong Kong clients, or market their services to the Hong Kong public.

The SFC believes the proposal has the potential to benefit Hong Kong’s futures markets by enlarging their investor base, increasing liquidity, and facilitating market development. At the same time, the proposed restrictions will ensure that local investors continue to be served by Hong Kong-based brokers.

The SFC does not propose to introduce remote participation in the securities market at this stage. Based on experience gained in the futures market, the SFC will consider carefully whether to introduce remote participation in the securities market at a future date. A separate consultation exercise will be conducted at that time.

The SFC welcomes interested parties to submit comments on the proposal by 31 March 2009.

The [Consultation Paper](/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/consultation/doc?refNo=09CP1) is available on the SFC website at www.sfc.hk.


End


Notes:

1. The definition of “dealing in futures contracts” is set out in Part 2 of Schedule 5 to the SFO.
2. Hong Kong-based futures market refers to a futures market that is operated in Hong Kong by a recognised exchange company (i.e., a company recognised under section 19(2) of the SFO) or by a provider of automated trading services authorised under Part III of the SFO.
