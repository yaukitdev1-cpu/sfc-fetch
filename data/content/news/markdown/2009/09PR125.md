A video that captures the foolish mistakes of a youthful investor won top prize in the Public Category of this year’s Investment Story Competition organised by the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) and its media partners.

For the first time in the competition’s three-year history, contestants were given the option of submitting their entries either in writing (English or Chinese) or on video. Besides members of the general public, students also were invited to join the competition via the Student Category.

The champion entry, produced by Mr Chan Ka Ho, Mr Lam Hiu Fung and Mr Yau Kwun San, tells of how a university student makes a foolish investment which ends up losing him his government-funded student loan. The episode highlights three common pitfalls: 1) not understanding the investment; 2) succumbing to herd instinct; and 3) investing beyond one’s means.

Set against the backdrop of frenzied speculation in the stock market, the video describes the student making a bet on a much-hyped stock, armed with nothing but its stock code, solely based on the recommendation of an acquaintance. For capital, he uses a student loan meant for his tuition. However, the stock price fell nearly costing him his prized education.

Winners of both categories of the competition shared their investment stories at an award presentation ceremony hosted by SFC Chairman Mr Eddy Fong today (Note 1).

“The winning entries demonstrate that good investment and product knowledge, a proper attitude towards investing and understanding one’s own risk tolerance level are important attributes to sound investment decisions,” Mr Fong said. “Equally important, investors should ask more questions about any investment recommendations by intermediaries to ensure the products are suitable for them.”

Media partners Commercial Radio One (CR1), Hong Kong Economic Journal (HKEJ) and Cable TV will broadcast or publish the winning entries from mid-September. The 12 winning entries were selected out of a total of nearly 100 (Notes 2 and 3).
