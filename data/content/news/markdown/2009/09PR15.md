The Takeovers Panel ruled on 20 August, 2008 that in the event that proposed acquisitions by the members of the Fung family of a 5% interest and by China Life Insurance Co, Ltd of a 10% interest in Wing Hang Bank Ltd, from BNY International Financing Corporation were to proceed this would trigger an obligation on the part of the Fung family to make a general offer for Wing Hang Bank under Rule 26.1 of the Code on Takeovers and Mergers.

The Executive referred the matter to the Panel as there were particularly novel, important or difficult points at issue. The Panel met on 20 August 2008 to consider the matter but it deferred the publication of its decision at the request of the parties due to the confidential and price-sensitive nature of the matter at the time.

A [copy of the Panel’s decision](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/cfd/mergers/panel/021a%20-%20Panel%20Decision_Wing%20Hang%20Bank.pdf) can be found in the “Prospectuses, Takeovers & Mergers” – “Takeovers and Mergers” – “Panel and Executive Decisions / Statements” section of the SFC Website at [http://www.sfc.hk](http://www.sfc.hk).
