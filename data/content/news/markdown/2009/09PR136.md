The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the re-appointment by the Financial Secretary of Mr Mark Steward as Executive Director of Enforcement for another three-year term effective from 25 September (Friday).

“Since joining the SFC in September 2006, Mark has been tireless in leading the Enforcement Division to combat market misconducts. He is a valuable asset to the Commission and I am certain that the SFC will continue to benefit from Mark’s commitment and professional knowledge,” the SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, Mr Martin Wheatley said.

SFC’s Chief Operating Officer, Mr Paul Kennedy’s current term of appointment has been extended for nine months to 15 July 2010.

“Paul has made significant contribution to the SFC in the past three years. I would like to thank Paul for his hard work and I look forward to working with him in his remaining term,” Mr Wheatley said.
