The latest edition of the [Enforcement Reporter](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/enforcement/09/dec_09.pdf) published by the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) reports that four people were sent to jail by the District Court for conspiring to manipulate the market, marking the first indictable prosecution for false trading. This was also the largest market manipulation case brought before a court in Hong Kong.

The Reporter also notes the jailing of a former investment banker for seven years, the longest term ever imposed on a person found guilty of insider dealing and the maximum sentence available to the trial judge. He is the 10th insider dealer that the SFC has prosecuted within 14 months and the sixth person jailed since the first conviction for insider dealing in July 2008.

This issue also reports that the sixth and seventh disqualification orders were obtained against two former company directors, who were banned from being company directors for six and seven years respectively.

Separately, the Reporter gives an account of how the SFC continues to make full use of its civil powers.

As an update on the Lehman Brothers Minibond issue, the Reporter notes that 99% of those who responded had accepted the repurchase offer made by 16 distributing banks to eligible customers.

The Enforcement Reporter is a newsletter that highlights key enforcement outcomes and issues. It is available on the SFC website under “Speeches, Publications & Consultations” – “Publications”.

The public are invited to subscribe to the Enforcement Reporter by registering for the Update Alert service on the SFC website. They will then be able to receive the newsletter via email.
