The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) will launch a new series of TV programme to help the investing public equip themselves with financial and investment product knowledge.

“The programme, entitled ‘Wealth Management: Be Smart, Ask First’, is the SFC’s latest investor education initiative. Market conditions are rapidly changing and it is more important than ever to understand what you invest in. We believe television is an effective means to bring this important message to a large audience of investors,” said Mr Martin Wheatley, the SFC’s Chief Executive Officer.

The 13-week programme, which will be broadcast on now TV Business Channel starting from 5 August 2009, features interviews with academic and industry experts. The programme will also run short messages explaining the regulatory work of the SFC (Note 1).

Building on last year’s success, the SFC will further explore a wide spectrum of topical issues in this second series of TV programme.

Investors will be able to find out more from the programme about IPO investing and the associated risks, bond investment, risks of investing in a volatile market, understanding features and risks of structured products, shareholders’ rights in takeovers and important issues in fund investments.
