The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the re-appointment by the Financial Secretary of two Executive Directors for another three-year term effective from tomorrow (28 August 2009).

They are Mr Brian Ho, Executive Director of Corporate Finance and Mr Keith Lui, Executive Director of Supervision of Markets.

“I am pleased with the Financial Secretary’s decision to re-appoint Mr Ho and Mr Lui. Both of them have been with the SFC for many years and their vast experience and professional knowledge are valuable assets to the SFC. I look forward to working closely with them in their new term,” the SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, Mr Martin Wheatley said.
