The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) will kick off 2010 with a series of investor education initiatives in January, designated as the Investor Education Month.

The importance of asking the appropriate questions for smart investing under the prevailing investment environment will continue to be the focus under our investor education theme: “Be Smart, Ask First”.

Mr Martin Wheatley, the SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, said: “The New Year is a good time for investors to review their investment portfolios and plan ahead. Amidst growing concerns of asset price bubbles forming in emerging markets and recent volatility in the global financial markets, it is important that investors do their homework, ask the right questions and evaluate risks to make informed investment decisions.”

The following events in the Investor Education Month are part of the SFC’s continuous efforts to disseminate investor education messages to the public:


-  A new radio talk show, “Invest Wisely” to be aired from 28 January to 6 May 2010 on Commercial Radio 1. With industry professionals and market practitioners as guest speakers, the 30-minute programme will address topical issues including securities trading, fund investing, risk management, shareholders’ rights and responsibilities.
-  The final of “Third Investment Triathlon”, a financial knowledge quiz, to be held on 30 January 2010 at Chiang Chen Studio Theatre of the Hong Kong Polytechnic University. An edited version of the event will be aired on Cable TV’s Live News Channel at 10:00 pm on the same day (Notes 1 and 2).
-  Investor Day 2010, entitled “Market Outlook and Investment Attitude for 2010”, to be held on 16 January. The event is jointly organised by the SFC and the Open University of Hong Kong (Note 3).
-  A new series of "Education Tips by Dr Wise" radio segments to be broadcast on Metro Finance from 14 December 2009 to 13 March 2010.

The SFC will also continue its advertising campaign echoing the theme “Be Smart, Ask First” in various media channels, including commercials and an infomercial series on TVs, public buses and other outdoor media.
