The fund industry can only win clients’ trust by putting their money to work in the most favourable manner while treating all fairly, said Mrs Alexa Lam, the Deputy Chief Executive Officer and Executive Director, Policy, China and Investment Products, of the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC).

Speaking today at the Lipper Fund Awards ceremony, Mrs Lam said the global financial turmoil highlighted the importance of fund managers’ fiduciary duty to investors, who count on their expertise and professionalism to navigate the current financial storm.

“When investors invest in SFC-authorised funds, they entrust their money to the discretionary management, within defined parameters, of professional fund managers,” Mrs Lam told the gathering of fund management professionals. “Fund managers have a commendable tradition to exercise all honest endeavours and care in putting clients' money to work in a most favourable manner, and to treat clients fairly.”

The Hong Kong fund industry has exhibited resilience in these challenging times, Mrs Lam noted. Firms licensed for asset management increased 17% in 2008 from 2007, and number of authorised unit trusts and mutual funds expanded 6.3% year on year in January 2009.

Mrs Lam stressed the merits of a strong partnership between the regulator and the industry in restoring confidence in the market. “The SFC is ready to listen, engage and consult. Our door is always open when guidance is needed with regard to any of the SFC rules,” she said.
