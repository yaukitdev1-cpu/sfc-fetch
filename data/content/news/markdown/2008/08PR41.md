The SFC has established a regulatory co-operation and information sharing arrangement with the China Insurance Regulatory Commission (CIRC). Under this arrangement, the two regulators will be able to provide regulatory assistance to each other, including exchanging information regarding their respective licensees.

The Regulatory Co-operation Agreement of Understanding was signed today in Beijing by Mr Eddy Fong, the Chairman of the SFC and Mr Li Kemu, Vice-Chairman of the CIRC.

Mr Fong said: “The signing of the agreement of understanding marks another milestone in the journey of closer co-operation between the Mainland and Hong Kong regulators. With the establishment of the information sharing and co-operation framework between the SFC and the CIRC, Hong Kong will be able to play a more active role in facilitating Mainland insurance companies conducting their investment and asset management activities outside the Mainland using Hong Kong as a platform.”

Mr Li said: “This is the first regulatory co-operation agreement on the use of insurance funds of the Mainland outside the Mainland. It covers key areas including the scope and effect, sharing of information, use of information and system of communication etc under the agreement while stipulating the respective rights and obligations of the CIRC and SFC in the regulatory co-operation. It will certainly contribute to the development of promoting the use of insurance funds of the Mainland outside the Mainland.”
