Investors are warned of the potential risks inherent in an on-line securities trading system, despite rapid advances in technology and the efforts by operators to reduce risks to a minimum.

In the latest column - [“Risks of On-line Securities Trading”](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/features/doctor/features_87_226_355.htm) - Dr Wise reminds investors of some potential risks, including temporary breakdown of electronic systems and consequential delays in orders, as well as the risks of identity theft and theft of passwords.

Investors should know what alternative means they have to place their orders in case of system delays or failures. They should also contact their brokerage immediately whenever they experience technical difficulty while trading securities on-line. In particular, they should check with their brokerage straight away when they have doubts about any changes made to earlier on-line instructions.

“To avoid unauthorised access to your account, you should take care of your password, avoid logging onto your account via public computer terminals and change your password regularly,” Dr Wise said.

Dr Wise is a fictitious character. Through his column, he talks to investors about the key issues of investing and regulatory thoughts.

Dr Wise’s Column is published on the last working Tuesday of every month. From time to time, additional articles in the form of special issues may be published on topical matters. The column is posted under the [“Features & Dr Wise”](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/features/index.htm) section of the SFC’s InvestEd website.
