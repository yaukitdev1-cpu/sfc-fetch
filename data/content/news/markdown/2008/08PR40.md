Fraudsters are using more sophisticated tricks to make their investment scams appear attractive to unwary investors, Dr Wise warns today.

In the latest column - “[Investment Scams Become More Sophisticated](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/features/doctor/features_87_226_353.htm)” - Dr Wise informs investors about some new tricks used by fraudsters to make their investment scams more credible, such as using fake brokers’ licences and creating fake corporate websites.

Dr Wise also reminded investors to check the identity of any suspected person or company against independent sources and ensure that checks are made with the proper authorities. The SFC warns investors, where it becomes aware of suspicious individuals or companies, through its [Alert List](http://www.invested.hk/InvestEdAlertList/jsp/EN/AlertList.jsp) on the InvestEd website at www.InvestEd.hk.

Investors should ignore cold calls and report all suspicious activities to the appropriate authorities. “Above all, never give away your personal records or send any money until you’re absolutely certain the operation is legitimate,” Dr Wise said.

Dr Wise is a fictitious character. Through his column, he talks to investors about the key issues of investing and regulatory thoughts.

Dr Wise’s Column is published on the last working Tuesday of every month. From time to time, additional articles in the form of special issues may be published on topical matters.
