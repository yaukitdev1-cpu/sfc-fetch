Dr Wise today explains to investors how they can be certain of the safe custody of the shares they bought and to effectively monitor the portfolio of their shares.

In the latest column - [*“Benefits of Using Investor Participant Account and Stock Segregated Account”*](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/features/doctor/features_87_226_352.htm) - Dr Wise reminded investors that these two accounts provide them with different levels of control over the stocks they own.

Investors, who hold stocks in their personal accounts under the Central Clearing and Settlement System (“CCASS”), known as IP Accounts, have full control of the stocks as IP accounts are direct custody accounts.

“This means that only the investors, not their broker nor anybody else, can authorize the transfer of shares out of their account,” Dr Wise said.

An alternative - Stock Segregated Account, also known as SSA - is a formal sub-account of the brokers’ own account with CCASS.

“The direct ownership of stocks held by CCASS therefore rest with your broker, rather than yourself, but the amounts held on your behalf are separately identified,” Dr Wise said.

An SSA enables investors to track changes in their accounts more frequently through daily statements of their SSA balances instead of just a monthly statement, Dr Wise added.
