The SFC welcomes the new measures announced by the China Securities Regulatory Commission (CSRC) today to allow qualified fund management companies in the Mainland to set up operations in Hong Kong under the Mainland and Hong Kong Closer Economic Partnership Arrangement (CEPA).

We believe the new measures will bring more Mainland fund management companies to Hong Kong (Note 1).

“The new measures represent a milestone for Mainland fund management companies, providing them with a platform to familiarise themselves with international investment and regulatory practices,” said Mr Eddy Fong, the Chairman of the SFC.

“Both fund industries in Hong Kong and Mainland will also benefit from increased opportunities to strengthen their co-operation,” he added.

The SFC will work closely with the CSRC to ensure a smooth implementation of the measures under Supplement IV of the CEPA in Hong Kong (Note 2).

Mainland fund management companies are welcome to meet us to discuss their business plans and internal control procedures (Note 3).

Ends

Notes:

1. Under the prior commitments under CEPA, Mainland securities and futures companies are already allowed to participate in the Hong Kong market through their subsidiaries.

2. Supplement IV to CEPA was signed on 29 June 2007. For details, please refer to the website of Trade and Industry Department of the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region ([www.tid.gov.hk](http://www.tid.gov.hk)).

3. Applicants for SFC licences are required to satisfy the same regulatory requirements that are applicable to other licence applicants in Hong Kong.
