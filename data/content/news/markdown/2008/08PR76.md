The [SFC Bulletin](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/newsletter/08/may08.pdf) published today highlights some recent initiatives we have taken to promote market development and efficiency.

To enable Hong Kong to play a more active role in the development of Islamic finance, the SFC has entered into a Memorandum of Understanding with the Dubai Financial Services Authority for co-operation on capacity building and human capital development in Islamic finance. We have also signed with the China Insurance Regulatory Commission a co-operation agreement - the first regulatory co-operation agreement on the use of the Mainland’s insurance funds outside the Mainland.

We have issued a joint consultation paper with the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong to allow electronic prospectuses to be an alternative to paper prospectuses when issuing paper application forms for public offers.

The Bulletin also notes the releases of consultation conclusions of electronic submission of financial returns and disclosure of interests notices, initiatives which aim to facilitate efficient handling of data and to improve the timeliness of information dissemination to the public.

The SFC Bulletin is a bi-monthly newsletter for intermediaries and market practitioners available on the SFC website.

Interested parties can subscribe to the newsletter by registering for the [Update Alert service](https://www.sfc.hk/sfcUpdateAlert/jsp/EN/update_alert.jsp) on the SFC website.
