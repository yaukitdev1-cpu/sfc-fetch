The SFC and The Hong Kong Society of Financial Analysts (HKSFA) invite the public to participate in a series of investor education seminars on investment analysis.


The speakers of the seminars will mainly discuss techniques for conducting company analysis when investing in the stock market, with emphasis on property, insurance, resources and energy stocks. The seminars will also cover the nature and suitability of funds for investors, and trends in the Hong Kong and mainland China stock markets.


Mr Martin Wheatley, SFC’s Chief Executive Officer said, “Before making any investment decision, investors should always try to gain a better understanding of the investment product. Therefore, it is important for investors to develop a set of skills so that they can carry out their own company analysis before making their stock selections. We are very pleased to partner with the HKSFA in hosting these seminars.”

The public can now enrol on-line at the HKSFA website (www.hksfa.org) or contact the HKSFA at 2521 2543 for details. Enrolment is on a first-come-first-served basis.
