The SFC today releases a Consultation Conclusions paper on Electronic Submission of Financial Returns.

The Consultation paper, published in November 2007, proposed amending the Securities and Futures (Financial Resources) Rules, or the FRRs, to make it mandatory for all licensed corporations to submit their financial returns electronically.

The SFC received eight written submissions to the Consultation paper, mostly from licensed corporations, which generally supported the proposal. Most submissions are related to technical questions regarding the electronic submission procedures.

The FRR amendments will be introduced into the Legislative Council and subject to the negative vetting process, the new measures are expected to come into effect in the fourth quarter of 2008.

The SFC will provide training to licensed corporations and allow testing of the new system before the FRR amendments become effective.

The [Consultation Conclusions paper](/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/consultation/conclusion?refNo=07CP4) is available on the SFC website at www.sfc.hk.
