The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) will launch an investor education radio programme to promote better understanding of risks and other issues associated with investing.

Drawing on the Dr Wise columns, the programme, which will be broadcast on Metro Finance Radio (FM 104) from 20 November 2008 until mid-February 2009, will examine a broad range of topical issues (Note 1).

These include the importance of asking relevant questions and understanding risk factors before making any investment decisions, the duties of investment advisors, boiler room operations and other investment scams.

The radio programme will also feature an interview with the SFC’s Director of External Relations. Separately, Metro Radio will air a 30-second spot highlighting some useful advice rendered by Dr Wise in his columns (Note 2).

 “Our Dr Wise column has been an integral part of SFC’s investor education programme for many years. We believe that the latest initiative will allow more investors to benefit from Dr Wise’s advice at this time of increased market volatility,” the SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, Mr Martin Wheatley said.
