While brokerage failures are not common in our market, investors need to be aware of the procedures for making claims and the amount of compensation available should this situation arise.

In the latest column - [“Investors’ compensation claims”](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/features/doctor/features_87_226_356.htm) - Dr Wise reminds investors that while they can seek claims from the Investor Compensation Fund, they must have realistic expectations about the level of compensation they will receive.

Investors can claim a maximum compensation of $150,000 for securities products and another $150,000 for futures products, regardless of the number of securities and futures accounts - individual or joint – they have with the brokerage, Dr Wise said.

Dr Wise points out that responding to requests by the court-appointed administrator of the failed brokerage for confirmation of account records is no substitute for filing claims with the Investor Compensation Company.

Investors must therefore file claims for compensation with the Securities and Futures Commission or the Investor Compensation Company before the prescribed deadline as late filings may be rejected.
