The Eastern Magistracy today granted an application by the Department of Justice to transfer to the District Court the third prosecution this year for insider dealing following an investigation by the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC).

Defendant Mr Du Jun faces a total of 10 charges of insider dealing (Note 1).

Du will appear for plea in the District Court on 23 September 2008.

His bail was extended on the same terms as those imposed on his first court appearance on 11 July 2008.
