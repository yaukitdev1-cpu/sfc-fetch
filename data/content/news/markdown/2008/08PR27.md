The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the re-appointment by the Financial Secretary of Mrs Alexa Lam (Note 1) as Executive Director (Policy, China and Investment Products) with a new title of Deputy Chief Executive Officer of the SFC for three years from 1 March 2008 to 28 February 2011.

Mrs Lam’s new role covers the development of the overall policy for the SFC as a whole, with a particular emphasis on Mainland China, as well as the regulation and approval of investment products.

Mr Martin Wheatley, the SFC Chief Executive Officer, said: “Alexa has played an important role in helping to develop Hong Kong as an international financial centre through her work with the fund management industry of Hong Kong and especially in the development of products whether authorised funds or new products such as REITS, and in attracting hedge funds managers to set up in Hong Kong. Alexa has also been instrumental in facilitating the development of the Mainland QDII scheme via investment tools available in Hong Kong, as well as facilitating the implementation of CEPA arrangements at different stages which have brought benefits to both the Mainland and Hong Kong markets. The SFC recognises how important the growing interaction with the Mainland capital markets will be for Hong Kong’s future growth. This new role will give a clearer focus to the SFC’s efforts in developing Hong Kong’s market and the ties with the Mainland.”

Mr Eddy Fong, the SFC Chairman, said: “Over the years, Alexa has made valuable contributions to the work of the SFC. In particular, Alexa has been working closely with the industry to implement many of the reforms that enhance intermediaries’ standards which in turn help improve the quality of the Hong Kong market and strengthen investor confidence. Alexa has also played an important role in facilitating market development by introducing greater flexibility for intermediaries to set up businesses in Hong Kong without compromising investor protection. I look forward to Alexa’s continued dedication and professionalism to meet the challenges and opportunities ahead of us, in particular to continue to work closely with the market and our Mainland counterparts.”

Notes:

1. Mrs Lam joined the SFC in October 1998. She was appointed Chief Counsel in 1999, and Chief Counsel and Executive Director in March 2001. In December 2001, she was redesignated as Executive Director of the Intermediaries and Investment Products Division.
