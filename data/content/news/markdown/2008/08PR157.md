The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today welcomes the re-appointment of Mr Martin Wheatley as its Chief Executive Officer for another three-year term from 1 October 2008 to 30 September 2011.

“Martin has made invaluable contributions to the SFC in the past three years and I am very pleased to see him continue to take the helm as the Chief Executive Officer at the Commission,” said the SFC’s Chairman Mr Eddy Fong.

“In today’s highly volatile environment, we are particularly pleased to have someone with Martin’s depth of experience and professional knowledge to play a leading role. I look forward to working closely with him in his new term.”

Mr Wheatley has been with the Commission since June 2005 and played various key roles before his current appointment as the Chief Executive Officer on 23 June 2006.
