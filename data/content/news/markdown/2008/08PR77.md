The [Enforcement Reporter](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/enforcement/08/may_08.pdf), published today, features the SFC’s approach to insider dealing using the full scope of the Securities and Futures Ordinance, including injunctions and now the first criminal prosecution.

The latest issue also highlights the suspension of the licence of a firm engaged in leveraged foreign exchange trading, and the convictions of a listed company and its chairman for making a false or misleading statement to the SFC.

The Enforcement Reporter is a newsletter which highlights key enforcement outcomes and issues. It is available on the SFC website under [“Speeches, Publications & Consultations” – “Publications”](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/speeches/public/enforcement/enforcement.html).

The public are invited to subscribe to the Enforcement Reporter by registering for the [Update Alert service](https://www.sfc.hk/sfcUpdateAlert/jsp/EN/update_alert.jsp) on the SFC website. They will then be able to receive the newsletter via email.

The public are welcome to provide feedback to improve the Enforcement Reporter. Comments can be sent to [enfreporter@sfc.hk](mailto:%20enfreporter@sfc.hk).
