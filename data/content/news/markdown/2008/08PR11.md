The [Enforcement Reporter](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/enforcement/08/jan_08.pdf), published today, features the SFC’s recent success in securing the first-ever immediate custodial sentence and in obtaining court orders to freeze assets and profits in on-going insider dealing investigations.

The latest issue also highlights the SFC’s improved efficiency in investigations, and a new approach to resolve outstanding disciplinary proceeding against licensed firms.

The Enforcement Reporter is a newsletter which highlights key enforcement outcomes and issues. It is available on the SFC website under “Speeches, Publications & Consultations” – “Publications”.

The public are invited to subscribe to the Enforcement Reporter by registering for the [Update Alert](https://www.sfc.hk/sfcUpdateAlert/jsp/EN/update_alert.jsp) service on the SFC website. They will then be able to receive the newsletter via email.

The public are welcome to provide feedback to improve the Enforcement Reporter. Comments can be sent to [enfreporter@sfc.hk](mailto:%20enfreporter@sfc.hk).
