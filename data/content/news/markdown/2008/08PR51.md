The SFC is pleased to announce the new appointments and re-appointments to the following committees with effect from 1 April 2008:


- Academic and Accreditation Advisory Committee
- Committee on Investment-Linked Assurance and Pooled Retirement Funds
- Committee on Unit Trusts
- Dual Filing Advisory Group
- Investor Compensation Fund Committee
- Investor Education Advisory Committee
- Securities Compensation Fund Committee
- SFC (HKEC Listing) Committee
- Takeovers and Mergers Panel, Takeovers Appeal Committee and Nominations Committee

The SFC would like to welcome all new members and to thank outgoing members for their valuable contributions over the years.
