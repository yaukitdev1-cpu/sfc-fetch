Hong Kong should play a wider and more proactive role in working with the Mainland to bring mutual benefits to the asset management industry.

That’s the message from Mrs Alexa Lam, the SFC’s Executive Director of Intermediaries and Investment Products, while speaking at a seminar organised by the Hong Kong Securities Institute today.

Citing the QDII scheme as an example, Mrs Lam said the scheme is not just for channelling excess capital overseas.

“In the Mainland’s grander scheme of opening its capital market and growing it into a deep and matured market, Hong Kong should seek to play a wider and more proactive role, and not just that of a recipient of Mainland funds.”

Mrs Lam said Hong Kong should work with the Mainland to help their financial intermediaries build up the necessary skills which in turn will benefit our own market.

“This will ensure that they become integral and long term players in our market and that eventually they could, just like any of the asset managers licensed here, manage SFC authorised funds and accept investment mandates from overseas investors,” she said.

Building a critical mass of Mainland financial intermediaries and players is vital for Hong Kong to be truly global and competitive, according to Mrs Lam.

The SFC will continue to streamline the processes and remove unnecessary regulatory impediments where appropriate, she added.

Mrs Lam’s speech, “[Positioning Hong Kong as an Asset Management Hub](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/speeches/08/acl_20080116.pdf)”, is attached.
