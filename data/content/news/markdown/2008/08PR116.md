With adverts promoting retail funds becoming more visible in the city, some investors may find it difficult to screen the multiple messages and select funds that meet their investment objective and risk tolerance level.

In the latest column entitled, “[Proper way of choosing retail funds](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/features/doctor/features_87_226_357.htm)”, Dr Wise cautions investors to read the entire advert and do their own research to learn more about the fund and, if necessary, seek advice from a professional, such as a licensed investment adviser, before making any decision.

Dr Wise explains that although a retail fund advert is required to be accurate, with limited space, it can only highlight key features of the product without going into all of the details.

“With any advert, look beyond the eye-catching slogan and attractive graphics to read the fine print, footnotes and the warning statements,” Dr Wise says.

He also reminds investors that when analysing the retail fund featured in an advert, they should always look up information about the specific market or industry segment the fund features.

Investors should not hesitate to ask the fund management company offering the fund for all relevant marketing materials ranging from fund fact sheets to the offering document to gain better understanding of important issues, such as risk factors, Dr Wise says.
