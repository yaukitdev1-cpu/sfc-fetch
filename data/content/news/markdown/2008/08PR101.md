The SFC has today gazetted the Advertising Guidelines Applicable to Collective Investment Schemes Authorized under the Product Codes (revised Advertising Guidelines) and the revisions to the following product codes, which will take effect from 1 August 2008:

- the Code on Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds
- the Code on Investment-Linked Assurance Schemes
- the Code on Pooled Retirement Funds
- the Code on MPF Products
 (collectively, the “Product Codes”)

The revisions are to implement the streamlining measures set out in the Consultation Conclusions on the Proposed Streamlining of the Pre-vetting of Notices and Advertisements of Relevant Authorized Collective Investment Schemes published on 26 June 2008 (Note 1).

When the revisions to the Product Codes and the revised Advertising Guidelines take effect, the SFC will cease to pre-vet eligible notices and advertisements. Advertisement issuers may continue to follow the current advertising guidelines included in the respective Product Codes until 31 December 2008, but must comply with the revised Advertising Guidelines from 1 January 2009.

The Gazette is available at the Government Publications Centre and accessible on the website of the Government Logistics Department. The revised Product Codes and the revised Advertising Guidelines will also be published shortly on the SFC website under “Legislation and Regulatory Handbook” – “Regulatory Handbook” – “Code, Guidelines and Circulars”.
