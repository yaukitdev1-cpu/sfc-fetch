The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has today issued a [circular](http://www.sfc.hk/sfcRegulatoryHandbook/EN/displayFileServlet?docno=H532) informing the industry of a phased approach to implementing revised advertising guidelines for marketing materials of collective investment schemes (CIS) authorised by the SFC.

The revised advertising guidelines enhance disclosure in CIS marketing materials, codify regulatory practices and facilitate post-vetting of marketing materials. All new marketing materials will be required to comply with these new guidelines with effect from 1 January 2009.

In order to accommodate practical difficulties faced by the industry in updating existing marketing materials, the SFC has agreed to adopt administrative measures to allow the updating of these materials over a period up to 31 March 2009. Where individual issuers continue to have difficulties, they will need to discuss this with the SFC at the earliest opportunity in accordance with procedures set out in the circular.

“These new guidelines will enhance investor protection while the phased approach addresses industry concerns without compromising investors’ interests,” said the SFC’s Acting Chief Executive Officer, Mr Paul Kennedy. “This pragmatic approach represents a win-win arrangement for both the industry and the investor,” he added.

The SFC will maintain active surveillance of marketing materials to ensure compliance with the approach adopted in implementing the revised advertising guidelines and will take action to ensure investors’ interests are safeguarded in the event of compliance failure.
