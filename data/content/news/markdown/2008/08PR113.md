The SFC will bring its investor education to commuters who travel daily on public transport by airing a series of videos aboard 1,600 public buses.

The videos -- to be broadcast from tomorrow until 30 September 2008 -- were inspired by real-life experiences of some investors. They aim to provide the public with some practical advice to help them make investment decisions.

The 18 segments feature advice on investment in stocks, funds and structured products, such as warrants and equity-linked instruments. They also discuss risks of margin trading, how to filter investment advice offered by market commentators and investment advisors, as well as the benefits of opening Investor Participant accounts.

The video broadcast is part of the SFC’s continuing efforts to equip investors with the required knowledge to make informed investment decisions and to manage risks.

The videos will also be aired on Cable TV starting from 28 July 2008 and are available in the [“Studio”](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/studio/tvc2008.htm) section of the SFC’s InvestEd website at [www.InvestEd.hk](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/index.htm).
