Good corporate governance benefits companies in both good times and bad as it gives investors the confidence to commit their money and even pay a premium for their shares, said Mr Eddy Fong, Chairman of the Securities and Futures Commission.

Speaking today at the First Annual Listed Companies Conference organised by the Chamber of Hong Kong Listed Companies, Mr Fong said the fact that Hong Kong has been able to provide a “reputable and trusted platform” for investors from around the world is a testimony to the city’s track records in corporate governance.

“Investors will invest in companies with good corporate governance and will be attracted to markets that provide significant numbers of investment opportunities with high governance standards,” he said.

Mr Fong pointed out that a governance structure is just a base for companies to build on. “The continued success of your companies depends on finding the governance structure that best meets the needs of your individual companies and the expectations of your investors,” he added.
