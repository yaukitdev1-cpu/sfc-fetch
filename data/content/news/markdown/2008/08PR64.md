Investors must fully understand the risks of structured products before they make any investment decisions, as many of these products are high risk with complex features.

In the latest column - “[Structured products: understand first, then invest.](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/features/doctor/features_87_226_354.htm)” - Dr Wise reminds investors to find out the specific terms and risks of structured products from their offering documents and consider whether or not a particular structured product is suitable for them after taking account of their own personal circumstances.

Most structured products are not capital protected, Dr Wise said. Before investing, investors should fully understand the risk-return profile of a particular structured product - they may suffer a capital loss if the price of the underlying asset moves against their view.

“You should also take the initiative to ask questions to safeguard your interests. You must make sure that you fully understand the investment you are going to make and the more questions you ask the more likely it is that you will be able to determine whether or not it is suitable for you,” Dr Wise said.
