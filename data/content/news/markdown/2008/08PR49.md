Hong Kong should proactively engage with the Mainland market as it enters the next stage of development, according to Mrs Alexa Lam, the SFC's Executive Director of the Policy, China and Investment Products Division and Deputy Chief Executive Officer.

Speaking today at a luncheon of the Hong Kong Investment Funds Association, Mrs Lam said Hong Kong must integrate further with the Mainland market as it has now reached a critical juncture.

“We are a developed market within the Chinese developing market. In fact, we are developed offshore market that is really onshore. No other developing market in the world has the luxury of having within its political boundaries an international market, which is a strategic ally and in which it can experiment with new initiatives and imbibe experience and opportunities that international markets and investors can offer,” Mrs Lam said.

“Given Hong Kong’s own experience, we are in a good position to share our knowledge and expertise with the Mainland market so that together we stand stronger and taller as we weather market uncertainties that lie ahead.”

Mrs Lam stressed that Hong Kong should engage with all relevant Mainland players to help them build capabilities similar to those which have made the Hong Kong market an international success.
