The continuing reform of China’s capital market offers ample opportunities for both Hong Kong and Shanghai to tap the country’s growing demands for financial services, according to the SFC Chairman, Mr Eddy Fong.

Speaking at a conference today in Australia organised by the Australian Securities and Investments Commission, Mr Fong said Shanghai and Hong Kong can play complementary roles in meeting those needs.

“It is not a zero sum game,” Mr Fong told the conference. “Shanghai has a huge domestic market to serve while Hong Kong can continue to play its role as a bridge with the rest of the world for companies that wish to have an international profile and presence.”

Hong Kong needs to continually challenge itself to stay competitive and to identify and build on its niche and role in the development of China’s capital markets, Mr Fong added.

Mr Fong pointed out the Mainland’s stock market was now the second largest in the world after the United States, with a market capitalisation of US$4.3 trillion. Mainland companies accounted for 51% of Hong Kong’s market capitalisation and 48% of its market turnover at the end of last year.
