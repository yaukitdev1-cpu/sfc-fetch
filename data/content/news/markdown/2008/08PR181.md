The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) will launch an investor education programme on television to promote better understanding of how the financial market operates and of risks associated with various investment products.

The 10-week programme, which will be broadcast on now TV Business Channel from 5 November, comprises a series of interviews with representatives from the SFC, academia and the securities industry (Note 1).

The programme will explore a broad spectrum of topical issues. These include scams and boiler room operation, benefits of opening Investor Accounts in the Central Clearing and Settlement System (CCASS) when trading securities, analysing and managing market risks, and different ways of selecting investment funds.

“This programme is one of our latest investor education initiatives and we hope it will help investors better understand investment risks amidst the current market volatility,” the SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, Mr Martin Wheatley said.
