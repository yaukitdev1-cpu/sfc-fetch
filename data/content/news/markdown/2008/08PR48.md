The [March Issue of the Takeovers Bulletin](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/cfd/mergers/takeovers_bulletin/08/mar_08.pdf) published today contains a Practice Note on the exempt system for fund managers and principal traders under the Codes on Takeovers and Mergers and Share Repurchases in response to market practitioners’ requests for practical guidance.

In view of two recent disciplinary cases, the Bulletin reiterates the importance of compliance with two rules under the Codes. Rule 3.6 and Rule 31.3 set out respectively the requirement to make an announcement immediately after any acquisition of voting rights that gives rise to an obligation to make a mandatory offer, and the restriction on the acquisition of offeree company shares at a price higher than the offer price in the six months after an offer has closed. 

Copies of all issues of the Takeovers Bulletin are available on the SFC website under [“Speeches, Publications & Consultations” – “Publications”](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/speeches/public/public.html).
