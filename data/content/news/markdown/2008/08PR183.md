The “Investment Triathlon”- a cross-media financial knowledge quiz-organised by the Securities & Futures Commission (SFC) will enter the second round tomorrow.

The first round of the competition drew 565 entries, with over 75% of them having answered the questions correctly. The 16 contestants who are selected to take part in the second round will compete in pairs in the “Investment Era” programme of Radio Television Hong Kong (RTHK). The programme will be broadcast every Saturday morning for eight consecutive weeks from 8 November and 27 December.

Eight or more winners of the second round will proceed to the final round of the competition which will be aired on Cable TV in January 2009 (Note 1 and 2).

The SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, Mr Martin Wheatley, said: “The quiz provides radio listeners with an excellent opportunity to enhance their investment knowledge as they listen to contestants battling it out.”

The Investment Triathlon is an investor education activity organised by the SFC (Note 3). Through this programme, the SFC hopes to help investors understand more about financial investments and their risks.
