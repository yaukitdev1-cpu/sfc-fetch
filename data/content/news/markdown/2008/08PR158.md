Hong Kong is caught up in a financial tsunami that is hurting markets worldwide and exposing investments to unpredictable risks, Dr Wise says in his [latest column](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/features/doctor/features_87_226_359.htm).

Given highly volatile market conditions, Dr Wise advises investors to exercise extreme caution and to critically assess their own circumstances and risk tolerance level before investing.

“Can I afford to hold the investment long term if the market goes against me, or would I have to sell at a loss? What is the maximum I can afford in terms of losses? These are some basic questions that investors must ask themselves,” Dr Wise says.


Dr Wise also draws investors’ attention to structured products. Since these instruments have underlying assets linked to different parties, including the issuer, the guarantor, the arranger, the trustee and their counterparties, investors can be exposed to a number of indirect risks arising from the performance and financial conditions of each of these related entities.

“Ask questions exhaustively to ensure that the salesperson fully explains financial products to you,” Dr Wise advises investors. After making the investment, investors should also keep up to date with its status and consider changes in the underlying risk factors which might occur, including credit and liquidity risks.
