The June Issue of the [Takeovers Bulletin](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/cfd/mergers/takeovers_bulletin/08/jun_08.pdf) published today contains two Practice Notes that clarify the application of certain provisions of the Codes on Takeovers and Mergers and Share Repurchases relating to whitewash transactions and announcements by listed issuers on suspension in trading of their shares.

The Bulletin also contains an update on the activities of the Takeovers Team in the six months ended 31 March 2008.

Market practitioners are also provided with a compliance reminder about special deals and of the impending introduction of a new rule on securities borrowing and lending and dealings by connected discretionary fund managers and principal traders.

The Takeovers Bulletin is a series of periodic newsletters which contain short informative articles and information relating to takeovers in Hong Kong as well as Practice Notes on relevant issues under the Codes.

Copies of all issues of the Takeovers Bulletin are available on the SFC website under “Speeches, Publications & Consultations” – “Publications”.

Members of the public who wish to receive the Takeover Bulletin via e-mail may register for the [Update Alert service](https://www.sfc.hk/sfcUpdateAlert/jsp/EN/update_alert.jsp) and select “Takeovers and Mergers” on the SFC website.
