The [SFC Bulletin](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/newsletter/08/jan08.pdf) published today highlights some practical steps we have taken to reduce compliance burden of the industry and introduce flexibility to better meet market needs.

These include a new measure to issue a single annual waiver, from certain disclosure requirements, to issuers of structured products rather than the present practice of issuers having to obtain a waiver in respect of each individual product issued.

We also report changes to the position limits regime and a pilot scheme requiring listing applicants to post information online prior to issuing the IPO prospectuses.

The SFC Bulletin is a bi-monthly newsletter for intermediaries and market practitioners available on the SFC website.

Interested parties can subscribe to the newsletter by registering for the [Update Alert service](https://www.sfc.hk/sfcUpdateAlert/jsp/EN/update_alert.jsp) on the SFC website.
