The [SFC Bulletin](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/newsletter/08/mar08.pdf) published today highlights some initiatives to provide market practitioners with practical guidance and help them comply with the relevant codes and guidelines in conducting their business.

These initiatives include clarification of market units which may be repurchased by SFC-authorised real estate investment trusts (REITs) and a new set of REITs-related Frequently Asked Questions.

We have also issued a circular to licensed corporations to remind them on the importance of prudent risk management and to draw their attention to some deficiencies that some firms may have in this area.

The Bulletin also includes the SFC’s annual review of the Exchange’s performance in its listing functions and our third Quarterly Report for the financial year 2007-2008 which highlights the various measures taken to help promote market development and to address market needs during October and December 2007.

The SFC Bulletin is a bi-monthly newsletter for intermediaries and market practitioners available on the SFC website.

Interested parties can subscribe to the newsletter by registering for the [Update Alert service](https://www.sfc.hk/sfcUpdateAlert/jsp/EN/update_alert.jsp) on the SFC website.
