The SFC today releases a Consultation Conclusions Paper in relation to certain proposed amendments to the Codes on Takeovers and Mergers and Share Repurchases. One of the amendments will help plug potential loopholes in privatisations and delistings.

A new provision will be added to the Takeovers Code to clarify that the voting threshold and other requirements of the Takeovers Code which are applicable to a privatisation will also apply to a transaction involving a disposal of assets and/or operations by a listed company coupled with the possible delisting of that company.

Various proposed amendments to the Codes in relation to securities borrowing and lending and dealings by connected discretionary fund managers and principal traders will also be adopted. These amendments will help to enhance fair treatment of shareholders and to meet market needs for greater clarity.

Nine submissions including one which represented 11 financial institutions were received during the consultation period.

The amendments to the Codes will become effective on 1 August 2008.

The [Consultation Conclusions Paper](/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/consultation/conclusion?refNo=07CP2) is available on the SFC website at www.sfc.hk.
