The SFC will review the Code on Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds (UT Code) to attract more funds and talent into Hong Kong, according to the SFC’s Executive Director of the Policy, China and Investment Products Division and Deputy Chief Executive Officer, Mrs Alexa Lam.

Speaking today at the Fund Forum Asia 2008, Mrs Lam said the revamp aims to provide greater flexibilities to meet market growth and innovation so that the Hong Kong market stays competitive in the region.

“Our retail fund platform has attracted not just local investors but also investors from the region. As mainland liquidity seeks investment products overseas and mainland managers seek skills outside the mainland border, we have the ability to become the fund supermarket in Asia, serving both the mainland and other parts of this region,” said Mrs Lam.

“We must therefore revamp the UT Code to make it easier for funds and talent to congregate in Hong Kong,” she added.

Mrs Lam envisaged that the new Code will contain guiding principles rather than prescriptive measures, but stressed that the fundamental investor protection tenets will remain.
