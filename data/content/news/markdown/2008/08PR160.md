The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) invites investors to participate in this year’s cross-media financial knowledge quiz, the “Investment Triathlon”.

As one of the SFC’s investor education activities for the general public, this competition aims to help investors understand financial investments and their associated risks.

 “This is the second year we are organising the quiz following the positive response we received last year. We hope to help more investors understand the risks involved in various investment products and enhance their market knowledge,” the SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, Mr Martin Wheatley said.

The “Investment Triathlon” consists of three rounds which will take place through three separate media channels.

In the first round, which runs for three consecutive Tuesdays starting 14 October, the public will be invited to answer questions published in the Hong Kong Economic Times (HKET) and the SFC’s InvestEd website at www.InvestEd.hk.

In the second round, 16 contestants will pair up and compete in the “Investment Era” programme of Radio Television Hong Kong (RTHK) between 8 November and 27 December 2008 (Note 1). Eight or more winners of this round will proceed to the final which will be aired on Cable TV in January 2009.

Winners will receive awards including cash prizes totalling $45,000 (Note 2).

The SFC would like to thank Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Ltd, the Hong Kong Institute of Directors, Hong Kong Stockbrokers Association Ltd and the Institute of Securities Dealers Ltd for their support and sponsorship of the prizes.

The [rules of the Investment Triathlon](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/general/general/press_release/08/08pr160_rules.pdf) are attached for reference. Investors can call the SFC at 2840 9222 or email to [InvestEd@sfc.hk](mailto:%20InvestEd@sfc.hk) for enquiries.
