The SFC today presented the 2008/09 budget to the Legislative Council Panel on Financial Affairs.

The estimated revenue for the year ending 31 March 2008 is expected to reach $2,478 million as a result of robust market activities. Operating expenditure is expected to be $605 million, marginally above the approved estimates for the year, leaving a surplus for the year of $1,873 million.

The SFC is forecasting income of $2,776 million for 2008/09 while operating expenditure is expected to increase to $780 million, mainly attributable to rise in personnel expenses.

“Certain increases in personnel are needed to cater for the increasing workload brought about by both higher volumes and increasing complexity of the market activities that we regulate,” the SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, Mr Martin Wheatley said.

Mr Wheatley also pointed out that the a disciplined approach had been applied to budgeting and cost control over the years but it was now necessary to take into account the significant market growth in recent years and to allow the SFC to recover from certain cost control measures put in place during the 2001 – 2004 recessionary period.

“In the past twelve months we have noted capacity problems in some areas and are also concerned to ensure that we remain competitive in the labour market,” Mr Wheatley said.

“As the economy and in particular the securities market has taken off in the last two years, the increased workload and pressure on our resources have highlighted the need to address these issues,” he added.

Mr Wheatley said the SFC will continue to tightly control its expenditure to ensure that costs stay within budget commitments.

The SFC has not requested any appropriation from the Legislative Council for 16 years in a row.
