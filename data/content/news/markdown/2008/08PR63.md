The SFC has today gazetted the [Securities and Futures (Financial Resources) (Amendment) Rules 2008](http://www.gld.gov.hk/cgi-bin/gld/egazette/gazettefiles.cgi?lang=e&year=2008&month=4&day=25&vol=12&no=17&gn=92&header=1&acurrentpage=12&df=1&nt=s2&agree=1&gaz_type=ls2&part=1&newfile=1&pid) (Amendment Rules). The amendments are to implement the proposal of requiring all licensed corporations to submit their financial returns to the SFC electronically.

Under the new electronic submission regime, licensed corporations can enjoy more flexibility in preparing and amending their returns. The built-in arithmetic checking function also helps reduce clerical and data entry errors.

The proposal was set out in the Consultation Conclusions paper on Electronic Submission of Financial Returns published on 13 March 2008.

The Amendment Rules will be tabled at the Legislative Council (LegCo) for negative vetting on 30 April 2008. Subject to the expiry of LegCo’s negative vetting period on 18 June 2008, the Amendment Rules will come into effect on 1 November 2008.

The Gazette is accessible on the website of the Government Logistics Department. The Amendment Rules are also accessible via the SFC website under “Legislation and Regulatory Handbook” – “Legislation” – “[Legislative Amendments](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/legislation/securities/amendment/amendment.html)”.

The SFC will start to provide training to licensed corporations and allow testing of the new system in the third quarter of 2008.
