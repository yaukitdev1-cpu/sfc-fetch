The SFC is inviting the public to share their investment experiences with others by taking part in a story-telling competition aimed at promoting a healthy investment attitude and emphasising the importance of understanding the risks before making investment decisions.

The “My Investment Story” competition is jointly organised by the SFC and its media partners -- Commercial Radio 1 and Sing Tao Daily. Contestants will be asked to recount successful or unsuccessful experiences in investing in Hong Kong stocks or warrants, or any SFC-regulated investment products.

The factors taken into account in judging contestants entries will include the significance of the key lessons they learnt from their investing experiences and how investor education has shaped their own experiences.

Entries can be submitted in English or Chinese, in written or recorded audio format. The [rules of the competition](http://www.invested.hk/invested/doc/EN/game/Rules_story_ENG.pdf) and [entry forms](http://www.invested.hk/invested/doc/EN/game/Entry%20form_story_ENG.pdf) are available at the SFC office and on the SFC InvestEd website at [www.InvestEd.hk](http://www.invested.hk/invested/en/html/section/index.html). Submissions must be received by 30 June 2008.

A series of trailers will be aired on Commercial Radio 1 for four weeks from June 2 to promote this story-telling competition.

Adjudicators of the competition are Ms Barbara Shiu, Member of the SFC Investor Education Advisory Committee; Dr Billy Mak Sui Choi, Associate Professor of the Department of Finance and Decision Sciences, Hong Kong Baptist University; Mr Siu Sai Wo, Chief Editor of Sing Tao Daily; and Mr Lennon Chan Wing Luk, a consultant and programme host of Commercial Radio.

Cash prizes totalling $10,000 will be offered to the top three entries. Seven merit prizes will also be awarded.

All winning entries will be published in the Investment Weekly Magazine of Sing Tao Daily and on the InvestEd website. Winners will also have a chance to share their investment experiences with Mr Lennon Chan in his “Crosstrade” programme on Commercial Radio 1 (FM 88.1 - 89.5).
