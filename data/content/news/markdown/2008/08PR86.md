The Eastern Magistracy convicted Mr Yu Ka Tak, a former business manager of Sincere Bullion Ltd., of carrying on leveraged foreign exchange trading without a license.

Yu was found guilty on 6 June of three counts of unlicensed dealings and was fined $18,000. The Court also ordered Yu to pay total investigation costs of $41,116 to the SFC.

The SFC commenced criminal proceedings against Yu following investigations which revealed that between May and December 2004 Yu introduced three people to open accounts with a company based and operated in Macau, Tse’s International Investment (Macau) Limited (also known as Tse’s Investimento Internacional (Macau) Limitada) (Tse’s Macau).

Yu handled the account opening procedures and placed orders trading leveraged foreign exchange contracts for the three accounts with Tse’s Macau. Yu was required to have a license issued by the SFC to engage in leveraged foreign exchange dealing which he did not have. These acts contravened section 114 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.
