The SFC today successfully prosecuted {X} for market manipulation.

The SFC alleged:

- {X} placed single board lot orders for shares in CEC International Holdings Limited, Longlife Group Holdings Limited and Frankie Dominion International Limited near market close between 1 August and 19 October 2006;

- he did so with an illegal intention to fix higher closing prices (a person with a genuine intention to buy shares will normally want to buy them at the lowest possible market price rather than inflate the price above the prevailing market price); and

- {X} made a small profit.

The court took into account the fact that {X} pleaded guilty at the first opportunity and only made a small profit. He was sentenced to two months&rsquo; imprisonment, suspended for two years and ordered to pay investigation costs of $13,058 to the SFC.
