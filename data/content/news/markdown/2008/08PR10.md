The SFC will significantly increase its resources for investor education in the coming year after positive public response to a string of recent initiatives.

Speaking at the closing ceremony of the Investment Triathlon today, the SFC’s Chief Executive Officer Martin Wheatley said investor education plays a crucial role in developing a healthy financial market.

“We firmly believe investor education can empower investors by giving them the necessary tools in making informed investment decisions at a time when securities market is becoming more volatile, and financial innovations create an ever-expanding list of sophisticated financial products,” said Mr. Wheatley.

As part of the SFC’s new investor education initiatives for the coming year, Mr Wheatley said: “We will conduct extensive surveys to gauge investors' understanding of market and product risks.”

Investment Triathlon is the first-ever financial knowledge quiz organised by the SFC. It drew over 600 contestants when it kicked off in mid-September.

After two rounds of competitions, 10 finalists competed at the final round, held at the Wei Hing Theater of the City University of Hong Kong today.

The champion, Ms Wong Wai-yee, took home a cash prize of HK$30,000, with the first runner-up, Mr Chan Ching-kit, and second runner-up, Ms Tam Wai-ling, receiving HK$10,000 and HK$5,000 respectively.

In addition, the 10 finalists will be invited to dine with Mr Arthur Shek, associate publisher and head of research of the Hong Kong Economic Times.

The SFC is grateful for the support of the media, where many investors look to for information and knowledge, and for the support of organizations and individuals participating in this event.

Cable TV will air an edited version of the event (Note 1).
