The Takeovers Panel today issued a decision on the application of the Takeovers Code in respect of purchases of shares for cash by the Lehman Group during the offer period of the
proposed privatization of CITIC International Financial Holdings Limited announced in June 2008.

A copy of the Panel’s decision can be found in the [“Prospectuses, Takeovers & Mergers” – “Takeovers and Mergers” – “Panel and Executive Decisions / Statements”](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/cfd/mergers/panel/paneldecision/paneldecision.html) section of the SFC Website at [http://www.sfc.hk](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/).
