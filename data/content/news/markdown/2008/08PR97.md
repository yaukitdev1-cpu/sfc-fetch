The SFC today publishes a Consultation Conclusions paper on the proposal to streamline the pre-vetting of notices and advertisements of collective investment schemes authorized by the SFC under the four Product Codes (Note 1).


The SFC received 16 written submissions during the consultation period from various market practitioners, legal professionals and industry associations. Respondents were generally supportive of the main proposals of the Consultation Paper (Note 2):

- to remove pre-vetting for the majority of notices as well as advertisements falling within the exemptions under the Securities and Futures Ordinance (Note 3).
- to establish a common set of advertising guidelines for all schemes authorized under the four Product Codes (Note 4).
The SFC will implement the proposal to streamline the pre-vetting of notices and advertisements by revising the Product Codes and the advertising guidelines. In particular, the SFC will further streamline the pre-vetting of insignificant scheme changes and the related revisions to the offering documents.

Mrs Alexa Lam, the SFC’s Deputy Chief Executive Officer and Executive Director of Policy, China and Investment Products, said: “This streamlined approach will create efficiency for the fast growing fund industry in Hong Kong.”

The revisions to the Product Codes and the revised Advertising Guidelines will be gazetted and their effective date will be no later than 1 August 2008. The SFC will then concurrently cease pre-vetting the eligible notices and advertisements. Advertisement issuers are permitted to continue to follow the current Advertising Guidelines included in the respective Product Codes until 31 December 2008, but they must comply with the revised Advertising Guidelines from 1 January 2009.

The [Consultation Conclusions](/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/consultation/conclusion?refNo=08CP1) are available at the SFC office and on the SFC website at www.sfc.hk.

Notes:

1. The four Product Codes are the Code on Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds, the Code on Investment-Linked Assurance Schemes, the Code on Pooled Retirement Funds and the Code on MPF Products.

2. On 31 January 2008, the SFC issued a Consultation Paper on the Proposed Streamlining of the Pre-vetting of Notices and Advertisements of Relevant Authorized Collective Investment Schemes. The consultation period ended on 29 February 2008.

3. Under section 103 of the SFO, it is unlawful to offer any collective investment schemes to the public in Hong Kong, unless it is Authorized by the SFC, or is exempted under the SFO.

4. Advertising Guidelines Applicable to Collective Investment Schemes Authorized under the Product Codes (revised Advertising Guidelines).
