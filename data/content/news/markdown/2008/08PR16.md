The SFC today issued a circular to managers of SFC-authorised real estate investment trusts (REITs) to clarify that REITs may purchase their own units on the The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong subject to similar requirements to those applicable to listed companies under the Exchange’s Listing Rules.

The SFC also issued a new set of FAQs (Frequently Asked Questions) on its website to provide guidance on other practical matters concerning REITs, including:


- “Top-up placings”
- Secondary offerings
- Adoption of incentive plans
- Valuation requirements
- Interim/annual reports and results announcements
- Annual general meetings
- Format of accountants’ reports

This guidance is issued as part of the SFC’s initiative to give informative and practical guidance to market practitioners and the industry on matters of interest and to facilitate capital raising for REITs.

The [circular](http://www.sfc.hk/sfcRegulatoryHandbook/EN/displayFileServlet?docno=H486) and the [FAQs](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/faqs/products/REITs_FAQ_Eng-final_310108_link.pdf) are available on the SFC website under “Intermediaries, Licensing and Investment Products” – “Investment Products Related Matters” – “Circulars” and “FAQs” respectively.
