“Be Smart, Ask First” is the theme of the latest investor education campaign by the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC), as it kicks off 2009 with a number of new initiatives. As in previous years January is nominated as the Investor Education Month and will see a number of investor education activities.

This theme highlights the importance of asking the right questions before investing, according to the SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, Mr Martin Wheatley.

“The New Year is always a good time for investors to formulate or review their investment strategies. This exercise will prove particularly valuable in today’s volatile market,” he said.

“Through a series of investor education initiatives, we hope to reinforce the important message that investors must ask the right questions and do their homework before investing. Knowing and understanding risks is a prerequisite to making informed investment decisions," Mr Wheatley added.

To increase the audience for this investor education message, a commercial echoing the new theme “Be Smart, Ask First” and featuring a catchy theme song will be launched in mid-January. This will be part of a year-long campaign using various media platforms and will be one of the major investor education activities for 2009.

Other events to be rolled out throughout the Investor Education Month include:


- Investor Day entitled "Riding out the financial tsunami” jointly organised with the Open University of Hong Kong on 10 January (Note 1);
- the finale of “Investment Triathlon”, a cross-media financial knowledge quiz, to be held on 18 January at Chiang Chen Studio Theatre of the Hong Kong Polytechnic University. Cable TV will air an edited version of the event (Note 2);
- a radio programme entitled “Understanding financial knowledge” to be aired on 7 January on RTHK Radio 1. Through this chat show topical issues such as counterparty risks and boiler room scams will be addressed; and
- a TV programme to be broadcast on TVB HD Jade Channel, in which industry professionals will make live appearance to share their knowledge in financial products and analyse the associated risks.

The interview programme running on now Business News Channel since early November has been well received and will be extended by five episodes into February (Note 3).

The SFC will also continue to update its investor education leaflets and publish new series to help investors equip themselves with useful and current financial knowledge.
