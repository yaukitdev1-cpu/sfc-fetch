The SFC today releases a Consultation Conclusions paper on the proposal to make electronic submission of disclosure of interests notices mandatory.

12 written submissions were received during the consultation period from a wide spectrum of respondents which, on the whole, supported the proposal.

Under the proposal, electronic submission of disclosure of interests notices to the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong (SEHK) will become mandatory. The SEHK will in turn provide electronic copies of the notices to the listed corporations. The current practice of updating the disclosure of interests section on the website maintained by Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited (HKEx) at about 5pm on every business day will be retained.

The listed corporations’ obligation to keep and maintain registers of interests and short positions shall remain. The SFC will continue to work with the market on the proposal to lessen specific requirements on the maintenance of these registers.

The SFC aims to implement the proposal as soon as possible and will work with the Government and the market on the implementation details.

The [Consultation Conclusions paper](/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/consultation/conclusion?refNo=07CP5) is available on the SFC website at www.sfc.hk.
