A [survey](https://www.sfc.hk/-/media/EN/files/COM/Reports-and-surveys/AWMAS-2021_final_e.pdf) published today by the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) found that the asset and wealth management business (Note 1) in Hong Kong recorded a 2% year-on-year increase in assets under management (AUM) to $35,546 billion (US$4,558 billion) (Note 2) as at the end of 2021. Net fund inflows during the year reached $2,152 billion (US$277 billion), up 6% from 2020. The AUM of the asset management and fund advisory business conducted by licensed corporations and registered institutions increased 8% to $25,888 billion (US$3,320 billion).


&ldquo;The survey findings underscore the core strengths of the asset and wealth management industry in the fast-changing, challenging environment,&rdquo; said Ms Christina Choi, the SFC&rsquo;s Executive Director of Investment Products. &ldquo;The SFC will continue to support the development of Hong Kong as a premier global asset and wealth management centre and preferred fund domicile.&rdquo;


Other highlights of the survey (Note 3) include:



- Assets held under trusts increased 5% to $4,719 billion (US$605 billion).

- The AUM of the private banking and private wealth management business decreased 6% to $10,583 billion (US$1,357 billion). Respondents reported that the decrease was mainly due to changes in the values of underlying investments held by clients.

- Non-Hong Kong investors remained a major source of funding for the asset and wealth management business, accounting for 65% of AUM.

- The total number of staff in the asset and wealth management business increased 12% to 54,003.

Notes:
