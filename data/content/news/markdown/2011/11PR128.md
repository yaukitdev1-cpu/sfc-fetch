The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has launched a new television advertisement -- Invest prudently Signing means responsibility” -- reminding investors the important questions they should ask before signing up for an investment (Note 1).

“While we have introduced new rules to raise standards of selling practices, it is equally crucial for investors to understand their rights and recognise their responsibility,” said Mr Ashley Alder, the SFC’s Chief Executive Officer.

“To tie in with the enhanced investor protection measures, this TV advert campaign calls upon investors to consider carefully before signing any documents -- an act of acknowledging responsibility for their investment decisions,” he added.

The new TV advert encourages investors to find out the basis of their intermediary’s assessment of their risk profile, enquire about the rationale of the investment recommendation and understand the associated downside risk.

The new TV advert is a sequel to the SFC’s previous TV advert featuring Canto-pop song “Ask Me”. Building on the success of the previous advert, the song is re-arranged as the theme music for the new advert, which will be broadcast on various TV stations and on-line media from today (Notes 2 and 3).
