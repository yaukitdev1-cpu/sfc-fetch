The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that the class exemption notice (Note 1) that implements the proposals to streamline requirements for property valuation disclosures in prospectuses will take effect on 1 January 2012.

Implementation will result in more focused and relevant information to be disclosed in prospectuses and a more cost-effective process for listing applicants.

The proposals were subject of a public consultation, the [conclusions](/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/consultation/conclusion?refNo=10CP7) of which were published on 20 October 2011.

End

Note:

1. The class exemption notice refers to the Companies Ordinance (Exemption of Companies and Prospectuses from Compliance with Provisions) (Amendment) Notice 2011.
