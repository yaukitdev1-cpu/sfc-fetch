The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the re-appointment by the Financial Secretary of Mr Wong Kai-man as a Non-Executive Director for a term of two years from 26 May 2011.

“Mr Wong’s insights and expertise have greatly benefited the SFC over the past years. I look forward to working with him again in his new term,” the SFC Chairman, Dr Eddy Fong said.

Mr Wong was first appointed to the SFC as a Non-Executive Director in May 2009.
