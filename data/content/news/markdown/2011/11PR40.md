The Eastern Magistracy today convicted Mr {X} of four counts of issuing advertisements promoting an unlicensed business of advising on securities and carrying on regulated activities when he was not licensed by the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) to do so.

{X}, who pleaded guilty today, was fined a total of $5,000 and was ordered to pay the SFC&rsquo;s investigation costs.

From April to May 2010, whilst unlicensed, {X} carried on a business of advising on securities and issued advertisements on the internet promoting his service.

The SFC reminds investors not to deal with unlicensed firms or people in order to protect their own interests. Investors can check if a firm or person is licensed on the SFC website ([www.sfc.hk](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/)).
