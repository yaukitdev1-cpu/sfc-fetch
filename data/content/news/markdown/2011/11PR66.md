In the light of the departure of the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) today, and given that the process of appointing the next CEO is still in progress, the Securities and Futures Commission announces that Mrs Alexa Lam, Deputy CEO and Executive Director, (or in her absence another Executive Director) will act as CEO until the appointment of the next CEO by the Chief Executive (Note 1).

‪End

Note:

1. Mrs Lam will be on leave from 9 to 13 June 2011. During her absence, Mr Brian Ho, Executive Director, will act as CEO.
