The Court of Final Appeal today dismissed an application for leave to appeal made by Sammy Ma Hon Kit and his wife Cordelia Tso Kin Wah against their convictions for insider dealing in shares of Egana Jewellery & Pearls Ltd (Note 1).

Today’s decision follows an earlier ruling by the Court of Appeal rejecting their appeals against convictions (Note 2).

The decision of the Court of Final Appeal marks the end of the first indictable insider dealing prosecution since insider dealing was made a criminal offence under the Securities and Futures Ordinance in 2003.

The case led to a total of five convictions for insider dealing with one defendant’s conviction being overturned by the Court of Appeal.
