The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the re-appointment by the HKSAR Chief Executive of Dr Eddy Fong as Non-Executive Chairman effective from 20 October 2011 to 19 October 2012.

The Commission looks forward to the continued leader ship of Dr Fong to further strengthen Hong Kong’s financial regulatory regime and to enhance Hong Kong’s status as an international financial centre as well as the leading offshore renminbi centre.

The SFC also welcomes the re-appointment of the Hon Chan Kam-lam and of Mr Lawrence Lee Kam-hung by the Financial Secretary as Non-Executive Directors for a term of two years, effective from 15 November 2011.

The Commission appreciates the contribution of Mr Chan and Mr Lee and looks forward to continue working with them in their new term.
