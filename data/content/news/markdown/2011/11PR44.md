The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) issued today [a consultation paper](/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/consultation/doc?refNo=11CP2) proposing amendments to the list of exchanges specified in the Securities and Futures (Financial Resources) Rules.

The proposed amendments aim to update the list of specified exchanges so as to facilitate market development and alleviate the compliance burden of licensed corporations. Under the proposal, four futures exchanges (including the newly-authorised Hong Kong Mercantile Exchange) will be added to the list and the names of some of the exchanges will be updated.

“This is a step taken by the SFC to facilitate futures trading by licensed corporations,” said Mr Martin Wheatley, the SFC’s Chief Executive Officer. “We will continue to monitor market developments and review our regulatory requirements where appropriate.”

The public is invited to submit comments to the SFC on or before 3 June 2011. Comments may be submitted online via the SFC website ([www.sfc.hk](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/)), by email to frrconsultation@sfc.hk, by post or by fax to 2523 4598.
