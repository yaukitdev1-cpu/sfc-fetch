The latest edition of the [Enforcement Reporter](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/enforcement/11/jan_11.pdf) published by the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) reports that the SFC successfully obtained further orders from the High Court to disqualify three company directors, showing that directors who breach their obligations, commit misconduct or keep bad news to themselves have no place in Hong Kong.

Also, recent decisions of courts and tribunal show continuing support for the SFC’s enforcement action against insider dealing. In particular, an insider was jailed upon a sentence review by the SFC.

This issue reports that the Court of Appeal has unanimously allowed the SFC’s appeal against a decision of the Securities and Futures Appeals Tribunal and ruled that criminal procedures are not applicable to disciplinary proceedings.

The Reporter also gives an account of how dishonest behaviour is punished and why the SFC only allows people who act with integrity and honesty to be and remain licensed.

The Enforcement Reporter is a newsletter that highlights key enforcement outcomes and issues. It is available on the [SFC website](http://www.sfc.hk/web/EN/regulatory-functions/enforcement/enforcement-actions/enforcement-reporter-(publication).html).

The public are invited to subscribe to the Enforcement Reporter by registering for the [subscription](https://www.sfc.hk/CampaignHelper/campaignForm.jsp?lang=EN) service on the SFC website. They will then be notified by email to retrieve the publication.

The SFC welcomes public feedback to improve the Enforcement Reporter. Comments can be sent to [enfreporter@sfc.hk](mailto:enfreporter@sfc.hk).
