The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the appointment by the Financial Secretary of Mr Carlson Tong as a Non-Executive Director and the re-appointment of Mrs Alexa Lam as Executive Director (Policy, China and Investment Products) and Deputy Chief Executive Officer.

Mr Tong’s appointment for a two-year term takes effect on 1 April, 2011, while Mrs Lam has been re-appointed for a term of three years, with effect from 1 March, 2011.

“We look forward to working with Mr Tong, whose expertise and experience in the financial services field will benefit the SFC and Hong Kong’s financial market,” the SFC’s Chairman, Dr Eddy Fong said.

The SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, Mr Martin Wheatley said: “I am pleased with the Financial Secretary’s decision to re-appoint Mrs Lam. I am certain that the SFC will continue to benefit from her dedication, wealth of experience, as well as professional knowledge.”
