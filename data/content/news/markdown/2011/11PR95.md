The latest [Quarterly Report](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/quartely/apr_jun_11.pdf) of the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) released today highlighted key regulatory enhancements made for Hong Kong’s securities market in the period of April to June 2011.

Following the transfer of regulation of public offers of structured products in the form of shares or debentures from the regime of the Companies Ordinance to that of the Securities and Futures Ordinance, advertisements and offer documents for all structured products that are publicly offered must be authorized by the SFC, unless otherwise exempted by the SFO.

To enhance the transparency of short positions, the SFC launched a consultation on the proposed subsidiary legislation that will give effect to the new short-position reporting regime.

Since June, credit rating agencies and their rating analysts providing credit rating services in Hong Kong have come under the SFC’s regulation, requiring them to be licensed for a new Type 10 regulated activity and to comply with the relevant code of conduct and other legal and regulatory requirements.

Other progress made in June includes the SFC's plan to proceed with the proposals to extend the conflict of interest requirements governing research analysts to research reports on real estate investment trusts (REITs) and listing applicants, and the publication of a revised draft guidelines on disclosure of inside information, which will take effect when the relevant bill is passed at the Legislative Council.

The report notes the SFC's authorization of the first renminbi-denominated REIT for Hong Kong listing and a renminbi-denominated fund. It also gives an account of the prosecutions and disciplinary actions taken against various types of misconduct.

The SFC focused its investor education effort on reminding the public of two enhanced investor protection measures implemented as of June, ie, the product key facts statements for SFC-authorized investment products and the mandatory disclosure of sales-related information by product distributors. Various investor education materials also were prepared to help investors understand renminbi REITs.

To further enhance transparency of the SFC's work, a quarterly overview of key data of regulatory activities has been introduced to the report. In addition, a new, [e-book](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/QRapril-june2011/EN/index.html) format of the report makes it easier to search for information. It is available under “Speeches, Publications & Consultations” – “Publications” of the SFC website at [www.sfc.hk](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/).
