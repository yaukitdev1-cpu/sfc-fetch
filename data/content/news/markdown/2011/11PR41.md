The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) will license and regulate credit rating agencies (CRAs) and their rating analysts from 1 June 2011, the date when amendments (Note 1) to the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO) come into effect.

The creation of a regulatory regime for CRAs follows a public consultation exercise that was conducted by the SFC in the second half of 2010 (Note 2). In anticipation of the upcoming regulation, both CRAs and their rating analysts intending to provide credit rating services on or after 1 June 2011 may start today to submit applications to the SFC for a Type 10 licence. To assist the industry make the transition, the SFC has issued a circular highlighting certain licensing requirements, including:


- the timetable for the submission of licence applications;
- competence requirements for rating analyst applicants; and
- criteria for exemption from regulatory examinations for existing rating analysts.

“We believe that the licensing and regulation of credit rating agencies in Hong Kong is in the public interest and will bring Hong Kong’s regulatory regime in line with international developments in this area,” said Mr Martin Wheatley, the SFC’s Chief Executive Officer. “We also encourage credit rating agencies to talk to us if they need further clarification of our licensing requirements and the manner we implement them.”

In addition, the SFC has gazetted today revised application forms and the Code of Conduct for Persons Providing Credit Rating Services (CRA Code) (Note 3). A new series of [frequently asked questions](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/faqs/licensing/faq-lic-18.pdf) related to the upcoming CRA regulation is published on the SFC website.
