The High Court has varied the interim order made by Madam Justice Kwan on 4 August 2009 in relation to the proceedings commenced by the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) involving allegations of market misconduct by Ms Du Juan, and her husband, Mr Wong Kwong Yu, former chairman of GOME Electrical Appliances Holding Ltd (GOME) (Note 1).

Following undertakings to the Court by Shinning Crown Holdings Inc (Shinning Crown) and Shine Group Ltd (Shine Group), the SFC consented to the discharge of the interim order made against Du.

The undertakings ensure that HK$1,655,167,000 in GOME shares as represented by the share certificates that have been deposited with the Court by Shinning Crown and Shine Group in compliance with Madam Justice Kwan’s interim orders will also irrevocably and unconditionally be used and applied to meet any liability of Du, if such liability is imposed by the Court in these proceedings.

The variation of the interim order has no effect on the freezing order against Wong.
