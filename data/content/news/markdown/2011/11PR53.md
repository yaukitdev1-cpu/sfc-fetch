In the latest issue of [InvestEd Intelligence](http://www.invested.hk/invested/en/html/section/focus/may11/sounding.html), the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) advises investors to choose carefully their investment advisers and get the most out of them in the course of investing.

The “Choosing Wisely” column in the bimonthly offers a check list as a practical tool towards using the service of an investment adviser. Besides pointing out an investor’s rights, the check list reminds readers of what is most often overlooked.

As stipulated in the code of conduct for intermediaries, all licensed persons advising on securities and futures must know their clients. On their part, investors must make sure that investment advisers understand their needs and explain the products to them thoroughly.

“While strengthening regulation of the markets, the SFC also aims to promulgate a responsible investing attitude among Hong Kong people,” said Mr Martin Wheatley, the SFC’s Chief Executive Officer. “We want to remind investors to go back to the basics. Before even making the investment decision, investors should consider carefully their own circumstances, ask questions, and choose investment advisers wisely.”

Penning this issue’s “Sounding Board” column, Senior Director of Intermediaries Supervision Mr Stephen Po noted that the SFC has been developing additional regulatory measures to improve the transparency of information provided to investors and to upgrade the selling practices of intermediaries.

This issue also discusses what investors should note when diversifying their investments overseas and explains why trading of derivative warrants ends before they expire.

The “IE Activities Planner” details a number of investor education initiatives, a highlight of which is an upcoming series of investor education seminars in mid-June, to be jointly organised by the SFC and the Hong Kong Society of Financial Analysts. Topics to be covered will include renminbi products and how to invest in an inflationary climate.

InvestEd Intelligence is a bimonthly newsletter for investors that provides a diverse range of educational information about investing. Topical market issues, product development information and regulatory updates are among topics to be covered regularly. The newsletter is published at the SFC’s InvestEd site on the 20th day of every odd month, or the day after, if the 20th falls on a Saturday or a public holiday.
