In a set of [consultation conclusions](/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/consultation/conclusion?refNo=10CP6) released today, the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced that it will proceed with the proposals to refine the requirements for evidencing whether a person qualifies as a high-net-worth professional investor under the Securities and Futures (Professional Investor) Rules (Professional Investor Rules) (Note 1).

Taking a principles-based approach, the SFC’s [proposals](/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/news-and-announcements/news/doc?refNo=10PR118) will allow firms to use appropriate methods to prove that an investor qualifies as a high-net-worth professional investor. To enable firms that so wish to continue with existing practices, the current methods for proving that investors qualify as professional investors will be preserved.

The SFC has received 16 written submissions from market participants and professional bodies expressing general support of the proposals in its consultation (Note 2) launched on 4 October 2010.

End

Notes:

1. Changes will be made to the indicative draft of the proposed amendments to the Professional Investor Rules to implement the proposals. The revised amendments will be gazetted following review by the Department of Justice.

2. On 4 October 2010, the SFC launched a one-month consultation to invite comments on a “Consultation Paper on the Evidential Requirements under the Securities and Futures (Professional Investor) Rules”.
