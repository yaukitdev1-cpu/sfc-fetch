The Securities and Futures Commission’s (SFC) financial knowledge quiz, InvestEd Arena, concluded today with Mr Chan Che Wang beating five other contestants in the Final Challenge (Note 1).

Under the theme of “Know how new rules protect you as an investor”, the three-part quiz (Note 2) seeks to raise awareness of new investor protection measures in relation to the regulation and sale of investment products. In addition, the quiz also highlights the risks of financial investing.

“Investor protection is fundamental to maintaining investor confidence in the financial market. It is important for investors to familiarise themselves with these new obligations required of issuers and intermediaries so that they know how to exercise their rights and ask the right questions before making any investment decisions,” said the SFC’s Chairman, Dr Eddy Fong.

The package of investor protection measures governing the sale of investment products, unveiled in May last year, are implemented in phases. Some of the measures are already in place while others are set to come into effect in June including disclosure of monetary and non-monetary benefits by intermediaries, and investor characterisation. To help investors stay abreast of these measures, a series of investor education programmes are being conducted in the print and electronic media.

Sponsored by the SFC, the champion Mr Chan Che Wang was awarded a cash prize of $30,000, with the first runner-up, Mr Suen Kwok Wing, and second runner-up, Mr Li Tat Wai, receiving $10,000 and $5,000, respectively.

The SFC is thankful for the support of media partners and all the supporting organisations (Note 3 & 4).
