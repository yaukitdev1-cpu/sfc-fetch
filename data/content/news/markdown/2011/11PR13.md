In its [Quarterly Report for October to December 2010](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/quartely/oct_dec_10.pdf) released today, the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) summed up regulatory changes to enhance market efficiency.

Jointly with the Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Ltd, the SFC published in November the consultation conclusions on “mixed media offer” for listings. Subject to negative vetting by the Legislative Council, the new measure would allow companies seeking to list shares or debentures on the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Ltd to distribute paper application forms without an accompanying print prospectus, as long as the prospectus is available on line.

Separately, the report noted that the regulatory oversight for credit rating agencies (CRAs), pending legislative amendment, is expected to be completed in April with a view to licensing CRAs and their rating analysts operating in Hong Kong by June this year.

During the reporting period, the SFC worked closely with the industry to prepare participants for the requirements under the revised codes relating to investment products.

In line with efforts to provide investors more choices, the SFC authorized the first exchange-traded fund managed by a Hong Kong asset management subsidiary of a Korean investment and securities group, and renminbi products, including two funds and the prospectuses of two bonds for retail investors.

In December, the Financial Services and Treasury Bureau gave the green light to the plan to establish the Investor Education Council (IEC). Subject to legislative amendments, the IEC will be set up as a company wholly owned and fully funded by the SFC. Meanwhile, the SFC’s investor education initiatives reminded investors of counterparty risks of synthetic exchange-traded funds among other topics.

In terms of enforcement actions, the report highlighted prosecutions against those who breached their duties as directors of listed companies and against solicitors involved in insider dealing using non-public information obtained through their work.

The Quarterly Report is available on the [SFC website](http://www.sfc.hk/web/EN/published-resources/corporate-publications/quarterly-reports.html).
