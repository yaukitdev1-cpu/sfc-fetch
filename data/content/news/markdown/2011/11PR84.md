In the latest issue of [InvestEd Intelligence](http://www.invested.hk/invested/en/html/section/focus/jul11/sounding.html), the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) provides a number of pointers for investing in initial public offerings and other listings that are new to local investors in terms of their domicile, the form of the security or their listing methods, etc.

The “Market Focus” column highlights that investing in a foreign company listed in Hong Kong may expose investors to more uncertainties, such as, overseas tax obligations and exchange fluctuations of the investment.

The article also stresses that investors should evaluate the basic fundamentals and risk factors of a new listing, regardless of its nature and form, by using the prospectus.

“Robust development of the securities market opens up more options for investors,” said the SFC’s Acting Chief Executive Officer Mrs Alexa Lam. “However, the diversity seen in the market also increases its complexity. Therefore, investors must fully understand their rights and obligations as well as the risk factors associated with a new listing before subscribing.”

Penning this issue’s “Sounding Board” column, Executive Director of Supervision of Markets Mr Keith Lui discusses the importance of short selling and how the recently proposed reporting requirement will further enhance the monitoring of the short-selling activities.

The bimonthly also reminds investors to make use of the last wave of investor protection measures regarding product key facts statements (KFS) as part of the enhanced product disclosure and the investor characterisation requirement for intermediaries. In addition, Dr Wise explains in his column how the SFC oversees intermediaries further to its licensing regime, providing an overview on its work in respect of ongoing supervision and monitoring.

The “IE Activities Planner” invites readers to participate in “ATM,” an interactive quiz show to be aired on now TV from now through mid-September. The show will feature a total of 20 questions related to investor education. In addition, the 2011 edition of [“How to Read KFS” booklet](http://www.invested.hk/invested/en/images/publications/kfs.pdf) is published today. Investors are encouraged to download an e-copy from the SFC’s InvestEd website ([www.InvestEd.hk](http://www.invested.hk/invested/en/html/section/index.html)).

InvestEd Intelligence is a bimonthly newsletter for investors, which provides a diverse range of educational information about investing. Topical market issues, product development information and regulatory updates are among topics to be covered regularly. The newsletter is published at the SFC’s InvestEd site on the 20th day of every odd month, or the business day after, if the 20th falls on a Saturday or a public holiday.
