The Eastern Magistracy today fined Eminent Investments (Asia Pacific) Limited (Eminent) and its director {X} $5,000 and $3,000 after both pleaded guilty to holding out as carrying on a business in regulated activities without having a licence to do so issued by the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC).

The SFC alleged that Eminent and {X} were not licensed by the SFC in any capacity yet in or about October 2009, {X} established a website stating that Eminent provided, among other things, &ldquo;investment banking and investment advisory and capital raising&rdquo; services to emerging growth to multinational companies. The SFC alleged that, by doing so, Eminent held itself out as carrying on a business in regulated activities, namely advising on securities and advising on corporate finance. {X} was convicted of the same charge.

At the time of detection, the SFC believes that Eminent had not attracted any customers or clients. The SFC&rsquo;s swift action has nipped in the bud any damage or harm to the investing public.

The SFC reminds investors to check the SFC&rsquo;s Public Register of Licensed Persons and Registered Institutions on the SFC website [(www.sfc.hk)](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/) to ensure that people who provide investment advice are licensed.
