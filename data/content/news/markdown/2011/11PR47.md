The Eastern Magistracy today convicted Mr {X} of one count of issuing an advertisement promoting an unlicensed business of advising on futures contracts when he was not licensed by the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) to do so.

{X}, who pleaded guilty today, was fined $1,500 and was ordered to pay the SFC&rsquo;s investigation costs.

In or around March 2010, whilst unlicensed, {X} advertised on the internet promoting his service of advising on futures contracts.

The SFC reminds investors not to deal with unlicensed firms or people in order to protect their own interests. Investors can check if a firm or person is licensed on the SFC website ([www.sfc.hk](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/)).
