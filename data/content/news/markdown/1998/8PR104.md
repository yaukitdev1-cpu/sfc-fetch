The Securities and Futures Commission announced today that it had revoked the registration of Ms Chung Chui Fung, Yvonne (Chung) in respect of her registration as a commodity dealer's representative under the Commodities Trading Ordinance.

The revocation stems from an inquiry in which the SFC found that over a period from September 1997 to September 1998, Chung had, whilst a commodity dealer's representative, used her employer's funds and margin funds from her employer's clients to cover her own trading losses on the stock and futures market.

An SFC spokesman said that the gross abuse of trust warrants the revocation of Chung's registration.
