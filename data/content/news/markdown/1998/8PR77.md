The Securities and Futures Commission announced today that it had signed Memoranda of Understanding (MOUs) with the Finansinspektionen of Sweden and Financial Services Board of South Africa on 12 and 15 September 1998 respectively in Nairobi, Kenya. The three organisations were in Nairobi to attend the annual general meeting of the International Organisation of Securities Commissions. 

The Finansinspektionen (FI) is the Swedish supervisory which regulates the insurance, credit, and securities markets. The Financial Services Board of South Africa (FSB) has regulatory responsibility for the activities of financial institutions (excluding banks) and those offering financial services in South Africa. It also advises the Minister of Finance on matters relating to such institutions and financial services.

These MOUs establish a framework for mutual assistance and facilitate the exchange of information between the SFC and these two authorities so that they may effectively perform their respective statutory duties.

SFC Chairman Anthony Neoh said: "We are delighted to be able to formalise and enhance our working relationship with our counterparts in Sweden and South Africa. These Memoranda establish co-operative arrangements which enhance our respective abilities in market protection and law enforcement."

Mr Claes Norgren, Director-General of the FI, said: "We are very pleased to sign this MOU with the Securities and Futures Commission and to enhance the co-operation between the authorities."

With the signature of these two MOUs, the total number of MOUs and similar co-operative arrangements entered into between the SFC and overseas regulators has reached 32.
