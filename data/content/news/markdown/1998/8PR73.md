The Securities and Futures Commission welcomes the announcement today by the Financial Secretary of new measures aiming to tighten market discipline and strengthen confidence in the securities and futures markets. The SFC supports the Government's proposals and will work closely with the Government and market bodies to implement these measures.

The SFC will work with the Exchanges and Clearing Houses to implement the measures put forward by the Government. Some of these measures can be implemented in the short term while others will require approval by the boards of the each of the market bodies and the SFC before they can be implemented. The SFC will work expeditiously to complete the programme as soon as practicable.

The draft composite Securities and Futures Bill, drafted in 1996, increases the penalties for illegal short selling. In light of recent market activities, the SFC now proposes to further strengthen the maximum penalty of $10,000 and 6 months' imprisonment to $100,000 and 2 years' imprisonment.

In addition, the SFC will assist in drafting amendments to legislation which would make unreported short selling activities, as well as false reporting to the SFC and exchanges, a criminal offence.
