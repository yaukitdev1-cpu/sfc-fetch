The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) and The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited (the Exchange) today issue a report on the conclusions reached from a consultation paper on large-scale, multi-jurisdictional initial public offerings (IPOs) involving both a public subscription tranche and a placing tranche. A copy of the joint announcement outlining the results of the consultation is attached. The announcement will appear in local papers tomorrow.

The consultation paper, issued on 25 June 1997, was the result of a review of the Joint Policy Statement on Offer Mechanisms by the SFC and the Exchange.

Mrs. Laura Cha, Deputy Chairman and Executive Director of the SFC said, "These new procedures for offer mechanisms will ensure a fair, orderly, and efficient market in IPOs for institutional and retail investors alike. We thank the industry for its response to the consultation."

Mr Lawrence Fok, Executive Director, Listing Division of the Exchange said, "the new procedures were developed by consulting with a range of market participants. They provide greater certainty in multi-jurisdictional offers and balance the interest of retail and institutional investors."

Copies of the Consultation Conclusions document are available from the SFC and the Exchange. The document is also available on the SFC website at http: //www.hksfc.org.hk and the Exchange website at [http://www.sehk.com.hk.](http://www.sehk.com.hk/)

**Joint Policy Statement**

Offer Mechanisms


On 25 June 1997 the SFC and the Exchange issued a consultation paper on offer mechanisms in initial public offerings ("IPOs") involving large scale multi-jurisdictional offers with both a subscription tranche and a placing tranche. The SFC and the Exchange have considered the responses to the consultation and have published a report on the conclusions reached. Copies of the report may be obtained from either organisation. This announcement summarises our conclusions.

Allocation of shares

**(a) The subscription tranche**

The following formula should be used to calculate the minimum allocation of shares to the subscription tranche:

(i) an initial allocation of 10% of the shares offered in the IPO;

(ii) a clawback mechanism that increases the number of shares in the subscription tranche to 30% when the total demand for shares in such tranche is 15 times but less than 50 times the initial allocation;

(iii) a clawback mechanism that increases the number of shares in the subscription tranche to 40% when the total demand for shares in such tranche is 50 times but less than 100 times the initial allocation; and

(iv) a clawback mechanism that increases the number of shares in the subscription tranche to 50% when the total demand for shares in such tranche is 100 times or more the initial allocation.

**(b) Eligibility to apply for shares in the subscription tranche and the placing tranche**

As set out in the joint announcement by the SFC and the Exchange dated 22 May 1997, the subscription tranche should be divided into two equal pools: Pool A and Pool B. The total allocation of shares to the subscription tranche should be calculated using the above formula with reference to the total demand for shares in Pool A and Pool B.

Shares in Pool A should be allocated to applicants each applying for up to HK$5 million worth of shares. Shares in Pool B should be allocated to applicants each applying for over HK$5 million worth of shares. Where demand in one pool is less than the shares allocated to that pool the surplus should be allocated to the other pool. Investors may not apply for more than the total number of shares originally allocated to each pool.

Investors may apply in either the placing tranche or the subscription tranche.

Issuers, their directors, sponsors and underwriters are required to take reasonable steps to identify and reject applications in the subscription tranche from investors who receive shares in the placing tranche. Multiple applications within each pool or between pools will be rejected.

(c) Over-allocation of shares

Over-allocation of shares by underwriters will be restricted to the extent provided under the over-allotment option, but need not be divided between the subscription tranche and the placing tranche.

(d) Announcement of indications of interest in the placing tranche

Issuers will be required to disclose the level of indications of interest for shares in the placing tranche before trading in the shares commences. Disclosure may consist of either a numerical or qualitative description.

The Exchange Listing Rules will be amended as appropriate for the above requirements. In the meantime these requirements shall take immediate effect. Issuers who consider their special circumstances warrant a deviation from these requirements should contact the Exchange at the earliest opportunity.

Other conclusions

**(a) Exchange's discretion on the use of placing**

The Exchange will retain its discretion under Listing Rule 7.10 to disallow the use of placing in IPOs.

(b) Stabilisation

The SFC will consider the merits of enacting rules under Section 137 of the Securities Ordinance concerning stabilisation activities.

(c) Underwriters' discretion on the allocation of shares in the placing tranche

No additional disclosure will be required of underwriters on their discretion in allocating shares in the placing tranche.

(d) Flexibility in adjusting the price, size and timing of an offer during the subscription period

The SFC and the Exchange invite market participants to provide detailed proposals for consideration in respect of flexibility in adjusting the price, size and timing of an offer during the subscription period.
