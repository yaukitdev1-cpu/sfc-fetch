The Securities and Futures Commission welcomes the tax reductions announced by the Financial Secretary, the Honourable Donald Tsang, in his Budget Speech today.

Mr Anthony Neoh, Chairman of the SFC, said he was pleased with the positive messages in the Speech and pledged that the SFC will work with the Government in reviewing the regulatory system in the wake of recent market volatility.

"The Commission stands ready to give every assistance to the Government to enable it to complete its review in March," he said.

On tax concessions, Mr Neoh said the reduction in stamp duty on stock transactions from 0.3% to 0.25% would further enhance the competitiveness of the Hong Kong securities market.

"It has always been the Commission's wish to see lower stock transaction costs in Hong Kong. In this regard, the Commission has proposed a reduction of its share of the transaction levy from 0.006% to 0.004%, effective 1 April," he said.

"I would also join the Financial Secretary in calling on the stockbroking industry to come up with proposals to further reduce the overall transaction costs in Hong Kong," he added.

Mr Neoh said he was also particularly pleased that the Financial Secretary had proposed a reduction in the ad valorem capital duty charged under the Companies Ordinance from 0.3% to 0.1% and that a cap be imposed at $30,000 per case.

"The concession would substantially lower the cost of capital raising activities," he said.
