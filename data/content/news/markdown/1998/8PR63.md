The Securities and Futures Commission announced today that it had publicly reprimanded {X} in respect of his registration as a dealer's representative under the Securities Ordinance.

The reprimand stems from an investigation in which the SFC found that, in August 1997, {X} had failed to reveal his employment with a member of the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited (SEHK) that was also a registered securities dealer when opening an account with another securities dealer that was also an SEHK member.

An SFC spokesman said that the Commission's finding impugned {X}'s efficiency, fairness and reliability and his fitness and properness to be a dealer's representative. The Commission therefore decided to publicly reprimand {X}.
