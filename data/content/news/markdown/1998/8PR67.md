The Securities and Futures Commission announced today that {X} had pleaded guilty before Mr P C White at Western Magistracy on 3 August 1998 of providing false and misleading representations in her application to become registered with the SFC. She was fined $3,000.


The offence came to light following an investigation initiated by the SFC and concluded by the Criminal Investigation Department of Waterfront Police Station.


In her application to become a dealer's representative under the Securities Ordinance accredited to Tang Ping Kong Limited, {X} claimed that she had no criminal conviction when such was not the case.
