The Securities and Futures Commission is pleased that the Special Administrative Region Government has accepted its recommendation to reduce its share of the transaction levy on securities trading to 0.004% of the consideration of a transaction, from 0.006% at present.

Mr Anthony Neoh, Chairman of the Commission, said the SFC was always mindful of the need to reduce dealing costs in Hong Kong in order to maintain the competitiveness of the market.

"The reduction by one-third of the Commission's share demonstrates its commitment to contributing to a low transaction cost environment in Hong Kong," Mr Neoh said.

The Securities and Futures Commission (Levy) (Securities) (Amendment) Order 1998 is gazetted by the Government today and will bring the reduction into effect on 1 April 1998 to coincide with the commencement of SFC's 1998-99 financial year.

The transaction levy was last changed in 1994, when the total rate was reduced from 0.02% to the present level of 0.013%, which is split between the SFC and the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong in the ratio of 6 to 7.

Apart from levy on market transactions, the SFC's revenue sources may comprise fees and charges for services provided to market participants, and an annual recurrent grant from the Government. However, the SFC has requested the Government not to provide the annual grant since 1993.
