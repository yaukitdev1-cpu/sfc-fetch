The Securities and Futures Commission and the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong will make a joint announcement on the use of plain language in prospectuses and application forms in local newspapers tomorrow. A copy of the announcement is attached for your reference.


JOINT ANNOUNCEMENT
ON
THE USE OF PLAIN LANGUAGE IN PROSPECTUSES
AND APPLICATION FORMS






New listing applicants and market practitioners should note the following:-

15th April 1998

The Exchange will not comment on any draft prospectuses and application forms, submitted with Forms Al on or after this date to the Exchange, unless they are submitted in plain language format.

31st July 1998

All prospectuses and application forms shall be issued in plain language format after this date.

On 12th January 1998, the Working Group on Plain Language announced proposals to improve the format, language and content of prospectuses and application forms. The Working Group has identified problems common in prospectuses and application forms and has suggested improvements in the way information could be presented by creating a plain language sample prospectus and a sample application form for adoption by the market. Booklets on "How to create a clear Prospectus" (Chinese and English versions) and sample application forms are available from the SFC and the Exchange.

In order to carry out such proposals, the Exchange shall return without comment A1 Forms and accompanying draft prospectuses and draft application forms which are submitted on or after 15th April 1998 to the relevant new listing applicants' sponsors, if they are not drafted in plain language format. The Exchange will not approve any such draft prospectuses and application forms.

The SFC and the Exchange shall require all prospectuses and application forms which are issued after 31st July 1998 to adopt the layout, format and style as prescribed by the Working Group.

For the avoidance of doubt, the Working Group intends to adopt such plain language sample prospectuses and sample application forms primarily for new equity issues to be listed on the Exchange. Listed issuers who intend to list other securities on the Exchange, or whose securities are not listed on the Exchange but intend to distribute their prospectuses to the Hong Kong public, are also encouraged to adopt the samples as they reflect standards acceptable in Hong Kong.
