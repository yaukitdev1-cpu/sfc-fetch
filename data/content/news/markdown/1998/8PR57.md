The Securities and Futures Commission welcomes the appointment by the Chief Executive of Mr Andrew Sheng Len-tao as its Chairman from 1 October 1998. 

Mr Anthony Neoh, whose term as Chairman has been extended by the Chief Executive by three months to 30 September 1998, said he would ensure a smooth transition of the Chairmanship in the meantime.

Mr Neoh said: "Mr Andrew Sheng has been working closely with us in his capacity as Deputy Chief Executive of the Hong Kong Monetary Authority. Therefore, not only will he be able to assimilate very quickly into the work of the Commission, but his regional and international experience will take the Commission to a new stage of development."

Mr Neoh also said he was confident that all Commission staff would give their full support to the new Chairman and continue to do their best in discharging the responsibilities of the SFC.
