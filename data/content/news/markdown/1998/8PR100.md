The Securities and Futures Commission welcomes the passage of the Securities (Amendment) Bill 1998 by the Legislative Council today. This will enable the previously announced compensation fund arrangements for clients of C.A. Pacific Securities to proceed.

The SFC expects to receive the first batch of claim determination notices from The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong (SEHK) by the end of November. After proper vetting and other essential procedures have been completed, the first batch of payments will be made to clients of C.A. Pacific Securities.

The SFC will work closely with SEHK in applying the compensation arrangements to the cases of Forluxe Securities Ltd., Foreground Securities Co. Ltd., and Chark Fung Securities Co. Ltd.

Investors should take note that the current compensation arrangement will not automatically apply to other new cases. They will have to be considered on their individual merits, taking into account, among other things, the financial position of the Compensation Fund.

Investors should also be aware of the risks of share margin financing, especially where provided by unregulated share margin finance companies. While measures have been proposed to bring these companies under the SFC's regulatory supervision, investors should exercise appropriate caution when opening share margin accounts, and should not open such accounts if they have no intention of using them.

The SFC also reminds the public that the comment period for the SFC's Consultation Paper on New Investor Compensation Arrangements for Hong Kong ends on 30 November 1998.
