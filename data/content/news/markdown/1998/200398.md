The Securities and Futures Commission today announces that the consultation period on the review of the Hong Kong Code on Takeovers and Mergers has been extended by one week to 27 March at the request of market practitioners. Several submissions have been received so far. A small number of copies of the Consultation Paper on a Review of the Hong Kong Code of Takeovers and Mergers, published last month, are still available at the SFC.
