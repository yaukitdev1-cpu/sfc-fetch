The Securities and Futures Commission announced today that it had successfully prosecuted Mr Kwong Wai Tong (Kwong) and {X} for short selling securities on the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited during the period 8 January 1997 to 26 March 1997.
Kwong and {X} both pleaded guilty before Ms Polly Lo at Western Magistracy to a total of 74 summonses in relation to their short selling of securities in contravention of Section 80 of the Securities Ordinance. Section 80(1) of the Securities Ordinance which prohibits the sale of securities when the seller does not have a presently exercisable and unconditional right to sell them. Kwong (68 summonses) and {X} (6 summonses) were fined $ 34,000 and $ 6,000 respectively. They were also ordered to pay costs totalling $ 17,794 to the SFC.
