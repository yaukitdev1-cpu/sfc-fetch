The Securities and Futures Commission announced today that it had successfully prosecuted {X}, a former director of Billion International Holdings Limited (Billion) under the Securities (Disclosure of Interests) Ordinance for failing to report on two occasions in March 1997 his interest in the disposal of a total of 75 million shares in Billion through Ming Kee Investments Limited (Ming Kee), a company which he controlled. Ming Kee was also prosecuted for failing to report these disposals as a substantial shareholder of Billion.


In regard to these breaches, Ms Polly Lo at Western Magistracy, fined both {X} and Ming Kee $10,000 for their breaches. They were also ordered to pay costs totalling $23,427 to the SFC.
