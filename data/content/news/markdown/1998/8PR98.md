The Securities and Futures Commission is pleased to announce the appointment of Professor Stephen Yan-leung Cheung as head of its newly established Research Unit. Prof. Cheung's appointment begins on 1 January 1999.

Prof. Cheung is a Professor of Finance of the Department of Economics and Finance, Faculty of Business of the City University of Hong Kong. He will be spending a six-month sabbatical at the SFC.

Prof. Cheung, also a founding Director of the Hong Kong Securities Institute, would make valuable contributions to the SFC with his extensive consultancy and research background, SFC Acting Chairman Mrs Laura Cha said.

Welcoming the appointment, Mrs Cha said: "We expect Prof. Cheung to enhance our research capability, and the appointment signals greater collaboration in the future between the financial and academic sectors in Hong Kong."

Reporting to the Chairman's Office, the Research Unit has been created through the redeployment of existing posts in other divisions.

Prof. Cheung said: "I am honoured to be appointed head of the Research Unit. I look forward to contributing to the work of the SFC and to strengthening the ties between the universities and the regulatory bodies in Hong Kong."

Prof. Cheung holds a B.Sc degree with honours from the Chinese University of Hong Kong, a doctorate degree in Statistics from the University of Paris VI, France, and a doctorate degree in Finance from the University of Strathclyde, UK.

He has held teaching and research positions in Europe and Asia. Before he joined the City University of Hong Kong in 1992, he was Head of the Department of Finance and Decision Sciences at the Hong Kong Baptist University.

Prof Cheung is the Chairman of the Professional Education Committee of the Hong Kong Securities Institute. Previously he has served as a project co-ordinator for the Consumer Council to examine the Hong Kong banking sector. He has also done consultancy work for the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong on stock options.
