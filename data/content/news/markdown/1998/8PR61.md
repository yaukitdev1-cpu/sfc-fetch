The Securities and Futures Commission announces today that a new Putonghua radio drama series, "SFC Theatre: The Careless-Lee Family", will begin airing on 16 July on RTHK.

This radio programme is the latest in a variety of investor education initiatives by the SFC. It comprises eight 15-minute episodes and aims to illustrate common problems experienced by investors and explain how investors can safeguard their interests in similar situations.

Each story has a different theme, and the series will cover different types of investment activities and products, such as stocks, funds, leveraged foreign exchange trading, investment-linked insurance plans, as well as the use of services like discretionary accounts and margin accounts.

These eight stories chronicle the investment experiences of a fictional Lee family and their friends, who often carelessly fail to do their homework before investing or monitor their investments.

The programme, sponsored by the SFC, is produced by RTHK which has invited popular artistes and Canto-pop singers to be the voices. They include Miss Gigi Leung, Ms Wendy Yu, Mr Joe Chan, and Mr Eason Chan.

Beginning Thursday 16 July, the series, which in Putonghua is called "Wulong Jiazu", will air on RTHK's Putonghua Channel at frequency AM621 at the following times:

Every Thursday between 9pm and 9.30pm during *Putonghua Newsline*

Repeat every Friday between noon and 1pm during *621 Exchange Mall*

This new series follows a positive response to a similar SFC drama on the Putonghua Channel in March and April entitled "SFC Academy" or "Zhengjiang Xuetong". The last series consisted of 20 three-minute episodes covering the role and functions of the SFC, advice to investors in situations like new share offering and rights issue subscription, and matters to consider when dealing with brokers.

Mr Anthony Neoh, Chairman of the SFC, said: "The Commission's latest radio series should help the investing public recognise and protect themselves against market malpractice. The Commission will further enhance its investor education programme in the months ahead through all available media including radio, television, print and the Internet."

The SFC has previously sponsored investor education television series in Cantonese. Announcements in the Public Interest (APIs) carrying investor education messages are currently broadcast on both the Cantonese and English channels of TVB and ATV.
