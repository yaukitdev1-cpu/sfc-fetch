The Securities and Futures Commission announced today that it had publicly reprimanded {X} in respect of his registration as a dealing director under the Securities Ordinance.

The reprimand stems from an inquiry in which the SFC found that in September 1997, {X}, a dealing director with Harvester (Securities) Company Ltd, had improperly signed as a witness to a client's signature on a client agreement and a margin agreement when he had in fact not been present when the client had signed the agreements.

While the SFC accepts that {X} has co-operated fully in its inquiry and that he has acknowledged his error, an SFC spokesman said that its findings impugned {X}'s efficiency, fairness and reliability. The SFC therefore decided to publicly reprimand him.
