The Securities and Futures Commission welcomes the publication by the International Organisation of Securities Commissions (IOSCO) of a consultative paper on risk management and control guidance, and invites the public to comment on the paper.The paper was released by IOSCO recently and was prepared by its Technical Committee, which is chaired by the Chairman of the SFC, Mr Anthony Neoh.

The objective of the paper is to promote domestic and international risk management and control structure awareness for securities firms and regulators. It provides information to be considered by firms and supervisors, and describes elements of a risk management and control system grouped under five categories - control environment, nature and scope of controls, implementation, verification, and reporting.

This is the first time that IOSCO has provided guidance to supervisors in the area of risk management and controls applicable to the general management of a securities firm. The paper provides benchmarks by which both securities firms and their supervisors may assess risk management and control systems. It represents another positive step forward in the area of supervision of internationally active securities firms.

Mr Neoh encouraged Hong Kong intermediaries to provide their comments on the paper.

"The Commission will examine its codes and guidelines in light of the IOSCO recommendations and identify policies and procedures that may be appropriate for the Hong Kong market," he said.

"In the meantime, registered persons must meet the requirements set out in the Management, Supervision and Internal Control Guidelines that were issued last year by the Commission," he added.

The paper (English version only) can be found on the IOSCO Web site ([http://www.iosco.org](http://www.iosco.org/)) and the SFC Web site (http://www.hksfc.org.hk).  Interested parties may send the comments directly to the IOSCO General Secretariat or through the SFC (Attention: Secretary to the Commission). All comments should reach IOSCO by 15 May, 1998.
