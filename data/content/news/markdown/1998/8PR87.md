The Securities and Futures Commission announces today that the appointment of Mr David White as an Executive Director of the SFC has been extended by three months to 31 March 1999.

The extension was made by the Acting Financial Secretary, under delegated powers of the Chief Executive of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.

The three-month extension is by mutual agreement and would help ensure a smooth transition. Mr White's term was originally due to end at the end of this year.

SFC Chairman, Mr Andrew Sheng, said: "I would like to pay tribute to Mr White for his service to the Hong Kong markets and the Commission since he joined the Commission in May 1995. As Executive Director of Supervision of Markets, he has made significant contributions to the development of the market systems, which stood firmly during the recent financial turmoil."

The SFC has commissioned an executive recruitment consultant to find a suitable replacement for the position.
