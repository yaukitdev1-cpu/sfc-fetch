The Securities and Futures Commission today announces the launch of its Chinese Web site. 

Both Chinese and English versions of the SFC Web site feature a searchable “Registry? of over 21,000 SFC-licensed securities, futures and leveraged foreign exchange intermediaries as well as over 1,400 SFC-authorised investment products. The Registry, updated on the third working day of each month, allows members of the public to check whether an intermediary is licensed, or if an investment product has been authorised with the SFC.

The site also features an Investor Education section, including the SFC's six independent guides for investors. These guides cover such topics as: issues to consider when investing in stocks, mutual funds or other financial products; how to choose your broker or adviser; the importance of monitoring your investments; and when to pursue a complaint.

The Investor Education section also includes a facility for e-mail enquiries and complaints, as well as a series of investor stories illustrating common problems faced by investors.

A wealth of other information on the SFC Web site includes consultation papers, codes and guidelines, licensing information, press releases, speeches, and links to other Web sites.

An SFC spokesman said, “The addition of a Chinese-language Web site now makes SFC information available on-line to Chinese speakers in Hong Kong and overseas. Market practitioners and the investing public alike should find the site a valuable resource of up-to-date information on Hong Kong's securities and futures markets.?

The address of the SFC website is [http://www.hksfc.org.hk](http://www.sfc.hk/). The English Web Site was launched in 1996.
