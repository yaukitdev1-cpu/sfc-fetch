The SFC deeply regrets the fact that Morgan Stanley Asia Ltd (MSAL) has seen fit to issue their press release this afternoon, following the SFC's public reprimand of the firm today. 

The SFC's reprimand of MSAL is the result of a year long process, during which MSAL has had full opportunity to address the Commission, which included two meetings with their officers. Yesterday, the Commission served, pursuant to Section 56(4) of the Securities Ordinance, a full and reasoned decision on MSAL.

MSAL had an opportunity to comment on the SFC's press release before it was issued. MSAL would have had full opportunity to apply for the Judicial Review if they were aggrieved by the SFC's decision. MSAL did not indicate that it intended to publicly dispute the SFC's decision. Had it done so, the SFC's public release would have been much more detailed.

The Commission regrets that MSAL's attempt at public exoneration was made without reference to the Commission's written decision which was 14 pages in length, which fully discussed the evidence gathered by the SFC, MSAL's representations and the reasons for the SFC reaching the conclusions it did.

In its press release, MSAL states that "there were no regulatory guidelines for a situation similar to the one Morgan Stanley and its client were facing".

On the contrary, the SFC has taken similar action against other registrants in similar matters.

Contrary to MSAL's assertion that the Commission is retrospectively imposing regulatory standards on MSAL, the Commission has concluded after careful consideration that MSAL knew what was required of it as a registered investment adviser and failed to meet such requirements.
