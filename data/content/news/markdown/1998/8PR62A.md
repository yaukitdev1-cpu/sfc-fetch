On 24 June 1998, the SFC successfully prosecuted {X1}, {X2} and Ping On Securities Limited (Ping On) for aiding and abetting three employees of Ping On for unregistered dealing during the period 1 December 1996 to 31 March 1997 (the relevant period). The SFC also successfully prosecuted {X3}, {X4} and {X5} for short selling of securities on the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited during the relevant period.


{X1}, and {X2}, both dealing directors of Ping On, and Ping On each pleaded guilty before Mr P C White at Western Magistracy to three summonses in relation to aiding and abetting three employees of Ping On who were not registered with the Commission, to perform the functions of a dealer's representative. They were each fined $3,000 and ordered to pay costs totalling $30,000 to the SFC.


{X3}, a dealer's representative of Ping On, and {X4} and {X5}, both clients of Ping On, each pleaded guilty before Mr P C White at Western Magistracy to two summonses of short selling of securities in contravention of section 80 of the Securities Ordinance which prohibits the sale of securities when the seller does not have a presently exerciable and unconditional right to sell. {X3} was ordered to serve 80 hours of community service and ordered to pay costs of $5,000 to the SFC. {X4} was fined $2,000 and ordered to pay costs of $2,000 to the SFC. Mr P C White took into account {X5}'s old age and previous good record and discharged her unconditionally.
