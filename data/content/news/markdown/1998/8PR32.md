The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong, the Securities and Futures Commission, and the Hong Kong Trade Development Council are pleased to announce that they will jointly organise a securities market seminar on 7 May, 1998 in Dalian, Liaoning.

The Seminar on the Securities Market of Hong Kong follows on the success of a similar event in Chengdu last September.

The Dalian seminar aims to introduce the Hong Kong securities market and its capital raising function to mainland enterprises in the region.

The event is co-organised by the Dalian Municipal People's Government and is expected to be attended by 250 people including senior management of enterprises in the northeast provinces, PRC market professionals, and government officials.

Senior representatives of the Hong Kong SAR Government, Dalian Government, and the organisers have been invited to give keynote addresses. Stock Exchange executives, market practitioners and experts from Hong Kong will also speak on a wide range of topics. (A tentative programme is attached for your reference.)

The seminar will be conducted in Putonghua at the Shangri-La Hotel, Dalian from 9.00am to 4.45pm.

For further information please contact Ms Betty To of the Stock Exchange at 2840 3807.

 

Notes to Editor:

The organisers welcome coverage of the event by Hong Kong news media. However, media representatives are required to obtain the approval of the Dalian Government in addition to approvals by any other relevant mainland authorities to report in the mainland.

Media representatives who wish to cover the event must indicate their interest by completing and returning the attached reply slip in Chinese by ***4pm, Tuesday 14 April, 1998***. Your request will be forwarded to the Dalian Government and you will be informed of the result as soon as possible.

Please note that the organisers will not be responsible for any other reporting approval applications that may be necessary. Media covering the event will be required to make their own travel and accommodation arrangements.

Further details on media coverage will be released in due course.
