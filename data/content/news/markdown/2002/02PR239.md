The SFC today launches a new series of investor education materials on hedge funds.  This move is to help investors understand more about the product following the authorisation of three hedge funds for the retail market. 

Investor leaflet

The SFC has published a new leaflet entitled “Ten Questions to Ask Before Investing in a Fund” to help investors examine the characteristics and suitability of a product before making an investment decision.  It includes a checklist of questions investors should ask in general before investing in a fund, and in particular before investing in hedge funds.

This leaflet complements the one entitled “How Much Do You Know About Hedge Funds?”, which was published earlier by the SFC.

Open letter to investors

Alexa Lam, SFC's Executive Director of Intermediaries and Investment Products, has issued an open letter to investors to explain the major features of the hedge funds reporting requirements.  The letter is available on the SFC website.

Mrs Lam explains that one of the most effective ways for the Commission to protect investors is to help them to protect themselves. Investors must learn to identify key information in a hedge fund prospectus.

New feature articles

Two additional articles have been posted on the SFC-operated Electronic Investor Resources Centre (eIRC) at www .hkeirc.org.

*Regulation on Hedge Funds* explains in simple terms the SFC’s authorisation regime, requirements on hedge fund managers’ qualifications and the information that should be disclosed to investors.

*How to Read a Hedge Fund Prospectus* explains why it is important for investors to read the prospectus and provides a step-by-step guide to help them find key facts from the prospectus.

In September, the SFC published two articles entitled *Hedge Fund Basics* and *Hedge Fund Strategies*, which introduced to investors the characteristics and risks of hedge funds.  All articles can be found on the eIRC.  More education materials on dealings in hedge funds and how to read hedge fund reports will be released in due course.

Copies of the investor leaflets are available free of charge at the SFC office and the 12 Consumer Advice Centres of the Consumer Council.  Registered persons are strongly encouraged to obtain the leaflets for distribution to their clients by calling the SFC’s Investor Education and Communications Department at 2842-7711.
