Members of the public who know their rights and understand their responsibilities when investing in stocks will be collecting cash prizes totaling $7,500 in a new radio quiz competition.

Winners in the three-times-a-week competition will each get a prize of $500. Runners-up will get a handsome SFC corporate briefcase.

The competition is jointly organised by the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) and Radio Television Hong Kong (RTHK) to strengthen investors' knowledge relating to investing in stocks. It is the latest of the SFC's investor education initiatives to increase investors' awareness of their rights and obligations.

Beginning Monday, 25 March 2002, the competition will run for five weeks in the "Investment Life Show" section of the "Bucket of Gold" programme on the Cantonese speaking RTHK Radio 1 (FM 92.6 or 94.4).

The "Investment Life Show" section is aired every Monday, Wednesday and Friday between 12:30 - 1:00 pm. It is simultaneously broadcast on RTHK's website at [http://www.rthk.org.hk](http://www.rthk.org.hk/). The competition will end on 26 April 2002.

In each segment of the "Investment Life Show", there will be a brief story about stock trading. Some of the topics will include:

- The importance of transaction documents;
- Risk of pooling in margin accounts;
- What to watch out for when using discretionary services and in short selling;
- How to avoid abuses like misappropriation and unauthorised transactions; and
- Issues to consider in corporate activities of listed companies, such as initial public offerings, takeovers, rights issues.
The audience can participate by calling RTHK's telephone at 187 2311 after the segment. Two contestants will be given the opportunity to answer questions. The one with more correct answers wins $500. The runner-up takes home a SFC briefcase that is not for sale. 

"The competition represents an interactive way to alert investors to the risks they take, and inform them of their rights and obligations when investing," an SFC spokesman said.

Investors are encouraged to provide the SFC (email: investor.info@hksfc.org.hk or telephone 2840 9333) with their views and feedback on this competition.
