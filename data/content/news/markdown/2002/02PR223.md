The SFC today publishes the Autumn 2002 issue of the SFC Quarterly Bulletin.

The feature article of this issue, Tackling Market Misconduct, gives an overview on the new market misconduct regime under the Securities and Futures Ordinance. The article is the abridged version of a more detailed paper entitled Fairer Markets: the SFO and More Effective Market Misconduct Laws, which can be found on the SFC website.

This issue also includes a speech entitled Post-Enron Impact on Regulation of Financial Markets by SFC Chairman, Mr Andrew Sheng. The article on Profiling Our Licencees provides an up-to-date review on licensed firms and individual licencees. The market update, Turnover Behaviour of the Hong Kong Stock Market, discusses the recent shrinkage in local market turnover and the performance of major stock markets during the third quarter of 2002.

To better serve the needs of our readers, the Quarterly Bulletin has strengthened the Statistics section, providing data on the international and domestic securities markets, as well as statistics on registrants and investment products regulated by the Commission. The statistics are also downloadable from the Research Papers and Statistics section on the website (
http: //www.hksfc.org.hk/eng/statistics/html/index/index1.html), which has been revamped to provide more useful market and industry statistics to market participants. This section also contains a newly launched research paper series. Currently, there are two research papers, namely Turnover Behaviour of the Hong Kong Stock Market and Revenue and Cost Trends of Global Stock Exchanges and HKEx.

As the Quarterly Bulletin enters its 10th year, its subscription fee will be reduced from $500 to $400 per annum.
