The Securities and Futures Commission releases today the Consultation Conclusions on the draft Securities and Futures (Insurance) Rules to be made under section 116(5) of the Securities and Futures Ordinance. 

On 26 June 2002, the SFC issued a Consultation Document on the draft Rules for a one-month public consultation.

As proposed in the Consultation Document, all corporations licensed or applying to be licensed to carry out securities dealing, futures dealing or securities margin financing activities will be required to take out and maintain insurance against specified risks (in relation to infidelity events, errors and omissions, etc), subject to exceptions applicable to certain licensed corporations.

The SFC received a total of nine submissions responding to the consultation. The respondents generally supported the proposed Rules on insurance requirements, and they also raised certain comments and enquiries in relation to the new insurance scheme. Details of the respondents' comments and the SFC's responses are set out in the Consultation Conclusions.

Having carefully considered the comments received and consulted with its adviser on related matters, the SFC has made certain amendments to the draft Rules. A copy of the revised draft Rules is also attached to the Consultation Conclusions.

Among the amendments, the commencement date of the draft Rules is now revised to 1 April 2003, which is later than the expected commencement of the principal ordinance. This is proposed in order to coincide with the expiry of the Brokers' Fidelity Insurance (BFI) Scheme currently arranged by the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited for its participants. As mentioned in the Consultation Document, the new insurance scheme would replace the BFI Scheme upon implementation.

In order to assist with the administration of the new insurance scheme, the SFC will issue detailed guidelines to the licensed corporations concerned upon finalization of the scheme.

Copies of the Consultation Conclusions are available at the SFC's office and on the SFC's website at www.hksfc.org.hk.
