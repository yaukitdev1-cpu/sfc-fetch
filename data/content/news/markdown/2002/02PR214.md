The SFC today publishes a new monthly summary of its enforcement action. The SFC Enforcement Reporter also raises compliance issues highlighted by recent SFC enforcement action that market participants should address to help ensure the integrity of Hong Kong markets.

The publication is part of SFC's efforts to increase the transparency of its enforcement work and strengthen investor confidence in the SFC's determination and ability to fight market crimes and misconduct.

Mr Alan Linning, Executive Director of Enforcement, said: "We hope the SFC Enforcement Reporter, written in plain language, will increase the public's awareness of the SFC's enforcement action. The newsletter will also serve to remind market participants of the standard of conduct that is expected of them."

In addition to the case summary, the SFC Enforcement Reporter discusses trends of malpractice and provides useful statistics on prosecution and disciplinary actions.

The debut issue of the SFC Enforcement Reporter records that in October 2002, the SFC won the appeals lodged by two market manipulators against immediate prison sentences, and had six people convicted for breaking securities laws. We also disciplined three intermediaries for regulatory breaches.

Since 1 April 2002, the SFC has successfully prosecuted 24 people and firms and disciplined 44 entities.

Copies of the SFC Enforcement Reporter are available at the SFC's office and on the Commission's website at www.hksfc.org.hk under the section "Press Releases, Publications & Speeches - Publications". The public are welcome to subscribe to the newsletter by clicking on this icon [](https://www.sfc.hk/sfcUpdateAlert/jsp/EN/update_alert.jsp)on the SFC homepage and registering for the SFC Website Update Email Alert service. They will then be able to receive the newsletter via email.

SFC intermediaries will receive the SFC Enforcement Reporter through FinNet.
