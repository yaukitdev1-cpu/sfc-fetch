The Securities and Futures Commission today kicks off a new series of investor education workshops for secondary school teachers.

The workshops are part of the SFC's initiative to help secondary school teachers of Economics, Commerce and related subjects understand more about the latest market developments and investment products so as to facilitate their teaching work.

A total of 10 workshops will be held until 3 December 2002. They are divided into three modules - stocks, funds, and options & futures. Please see details in the appendix.

More than 450 teachers have already enrolled in the workshops. They will receive for free a teaching kit to facilitate discussions in class and other SFC investor education materials.

Since 2000, the SFC has organised workshops for secondary school teachers to encourage them to share with their students the key concepts of investing. A total of 29 workshops on stocks, funds and futures were held in the past two years, reaching 1,180 participants. A "Teachers' Corner" has also been set up on the SFC website to provide an easy access to the PowerPoint presentation materials, case studies and exercises previously used in the workshops.

The workshops are still open for enrolment. Interested teachers can visit the Teachers' Corner on the SFC website or contact the Investor Education and Communications Department at 2842-7711.
