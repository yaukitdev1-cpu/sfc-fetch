The Securities and Futures Commission has recognised more industry qualifications and professional institutions, making it easier for market practitioners to meet the SFC's licensing requirements.

The SFC is pleased to announce that its Academic and Accreditation Advisory Committee (AAAC) has approved two more Recognised Industry Qualifications (RIQ):

- Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) designation - A reputable designation that has become popular among securities and futures practitioners. Such recognition will allow qualified CFA applicants to obtain their licenses without further examination on their industry knowledge.
- Leveraged foreign exchange examination programmes offered by the Vocational Training Council's Financial Services Department Centre - This is the first approved RIQ under the leveraged foreign exchange category that has been long awaited by the market.
RIQ is part of the competence requirement for persons applying to become SFC registrants. Other approved local RIQs include the following examinations offered by the Hong Kong Securities Institute:

- Diploma Programme Examination
- Financial Market Principal Programme Examination
- Foundation Programme Examination
The AAAC has also approved four more Recognised Institutions to provide Continuous Professional Training (CPT) for SFC registrants, bringing the total number to 12. The increased number of Recognised Institutions will help to make CPT compliance more convenient for licensees, who must complete a minimum of five CPT hours per calendar year in enhancing their technical knowledge and professional expertise. The four new Recognised Institutions are:

- Hong Kong Investment Funds Association
- Department of Accountancy of the Hong Kong Polytechnic University
- School of Professional Education and Executive Development (SPEED) of the Hong Kong Polytechnic University
- School of Continuing Education of the Hong Kong Baptist University
For a complete list of Recognised Institutions and further information on Competence and CPT, please refer to the "Frequently Asked Questions on Competence" and "Frequently Asked Questions on CPT" posted on the Commission's website at www.hksfc.org.hk, under the "Licensing, Intermediaries and Investment Products - Licensing Related Matters" section.
