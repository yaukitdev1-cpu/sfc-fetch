The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) will broadcast Episode 4 of "Invest with Your Heads Up", entitled "Over-trust Breeds Betrayal" on RoadShow Media on 5 June 2002 (Wednesday). 

In this episode, Mrs Wong (played by Tsang Wai Wan) tries not to let her husband know about her stock investments. She therefore asks her broker, Mr Zhou (played by Mak Tsz Wan), to keep all transaction documents for her.

One day, at Mr Zhou's apartment, Mrs Wong finds some statements of her account that are different from those given to her by Mr Zhou. Worst still, those statements show some speculative stocks that she has never invested in. These stocks have been bought without her authority. Mr Zhou explains that he bought the stock for Mrs Wong in light of the company's good prospects, but he has forgotten to tell her. He asks Mrs Wong not to report his act to the management of the brokerage or the SFC. Mrs Wong agrees to cover up for him.

The key messages for retail investors are:

- Your account executive should obtain explicit and express authority from you in advance of every trade, unless discretionary authority is granted;
- Do not use 'hold-mail' arrangement unless you really need it;
- Never allow contract notes and statements of accounts to be delivered to you by your account executive, this may create an opportunity for subsequent changes or even falsification by dishonest account executives who wish to hide unauthorized trades made in your account;
- Any unauthorized trades found in the account should be brought to the attention of the management of the brokerage immediately. Never accept the wrongdoer's plea to cover up his misconduct.

 "Invest with Your Heads Up" is a new 12-episode investor video series produced by the SFC, which aims to explain stock trading and settlement procedures, as well as measures investors should take to protect their own interests when dealing in stocks.

Airing has begun on 15 May 2002 on RoadShow Media on 2,400 buses of the KMB and Citybus and 200 public light buses for 12 consecutive Wednesdays, with an estimated 3.5 million passenger journeys every day. After broadcast, each episode will be made available on the SFC-operated Electronic Investor Resources Centre, located at www.hkeirc.org.
