The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) releases today the Consultation Conclusions on the draft Securities and Futures (Associated Entities) Rules. 


On 22 March 2002, the SFC issued a Consultation Paper to solicit comments on the draft Rules.
 To improve investor protection, section 164 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance (No.5 of 2002) prescribes the types of persons that are allowed to receive or hold in Hong Kong client assets. These persons include an "associated entity of an intermediary" - a term that would cover most nominees that hold client assets of intermediaries.


In general, the Securities and Futures (Associated Entities) Rules prescribe for the purposes of section 165 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance the particulars that an associated entity of an intermediary must provide to the SFC when it becomes or ceases to be an associated entity. A notice is also required when there is a change in any such particulars.


During the one-month consultation period, three submissions were received. All submissions supported the Rules. The Consultation Conclusions provide an analysis of the several amendments to the Rules and explain our perspectives in making these changes.


The Rules will become effective on the day appointed for the commencement of Part VI of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

Copies of the Consultation Conclusions are available at the SFC's office and on the SFC's website at www.hksfc.org.hk.
