The IOSCO Technical Committee has set up a high level sub-committee to coordinate and focus its response to the securities regulatory issues highlighted by the bankruptcy of Enron and other high-profile business failures around the world.

The sub-committee, called the Chair's Committee, comprises Chairs and senior representatives of securities regulators from the following jurisdictions: Brazil, Canada, France, Germany, Hong Kong, Italy, Japan, Mexico, the Netherlands, Portugal, United Kingdom and the United States. It is chaired by Mr David Brown, Chairman of the Technical Committee.

The first meeting was convened on 20 March 2002. The group will continue to meet over the coming months to provide a forum for identifying and examining strategic issues of common interest to securities regulators. This will ensure that national regulators are able to benefit from the experiences of others. It will also assist in identifying new issues for study and re-prioritising IOSCO's work plan. The group will also be the focal point for collaboration with other international bodies where that may be required.

IOSCO is a worldwide forum for securities regulators that promotes co-operation and high standards of regulation in order to maintain fair, efficient and sound markets. There are 110 ordinary and associate members that are securities regulators plus a further 61 affiliate members that are stock exchanges, international organizations and other similar entities. The Hong Kong Securities and Futures Commission is a member of the Technical Committee, which consists of 16 securities regulators from developed markets.

Further information on IOSCO is available on its website at [www.iosco.org](http://www.iosco.org/).
