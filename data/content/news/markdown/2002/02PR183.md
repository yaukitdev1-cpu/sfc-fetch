The Securities and Futures Commission has partnered with the General Education Unit of The University of Hong Kong (HKU) to launch a course on personal financial management for university students.

This is the first time ever that the SFC has joined hands with a tertiary education institution to offer academic course.

Entitled "Financial Intelligence (FQ) - The Art of Personal Financial Management and Investment", the course is part of the SFC's investor education initiative to help university students cultivate a healthy attitude towards investing and money management. The course also meets the objective of the General Education Unit, HKU, to provide the undergraduates with whole-person education.

Prominent speakers and market practitioners will speak on the following topics in the seven sessions of the course:

1. 1Introduction to Personal Financial Management
(By Mr Arthur Shek Kang Chuen, Deputy Publisher and Head of Research, Hong Kong Economic Times)
2. Ways to Become a Smart Investor
(By Mr Chan Wing Luk, Market Commentator)
3. Financial Management and Our Lives
 (By Mr Cho Chi Ming, Columnist, Hong Kong Economic Journal)
4. Essence of Funds Investing
 (By Ms Tina So, Director, Investment Products, SFC)
5. Professional Ethics and Quality of Market Practitioners
(By Mr Alec Tsui, Chairman, Hong Kong Securities Institute)
6. Market Pitfalls and Investors' Responsibilities
(By Mr Eric Cheng, Senior Director, Enforcement, SFC)
7. The Art of Financial Regulation (in English)
(By Mr Andrew Sheng, Chairman, SFC)


The course will begin on 26 September 2002 for seven consecutive Thursdays (except 24 October 2002). Subject to seats availability, the last session by Mr Sheng, scheduled on 14 November 2002, will be open to the public. The SFC will announce details of Mr Sheng's session nearer the date.

All current HKU students can enrol in the course. They can contact the General Education Unit of HKU at 2975-8843 to enquire about the enrolment details.

The SFC will also explore the possibilities of working with HKU and other universities and colleges to introduce various investor educational programmes to students.
