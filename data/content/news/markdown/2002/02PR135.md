The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today releases the consultation conclusion on the draft Securities and Futures (Short Selling Exemption and Stock Lending) Rules as proposed in the consultation paper released on 24 May 2002. 

Sections 170 and 171 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance repeat the existing naked short selling prohibition in section 80 and the reporting requirements for "covered" short selling orders in section 80B of the Securities Ordinance respectively.

The proposed exemptions for naked short selling amalgamate the current exemptions in Rule 17 of the Securities (Miscellaneous) Rules and address inconsistencies under the current law to enable equal treatment for all categories of market makers (including short selling in the performance of market making obligations and hedging of market making positions). No new policy changes have been incorporated. The exemptions have been proposed as incentives to market makers who help to provide liquidity to the markets.

There are no exemptions from the "covered" short selling reporting requirements under the existing law. The proposed exemptions are to enable the timely execution of transactions and will apply to sellers placing short selling orders pursuant to a stock borrowing and lending agreement as defined in the Securities and Futures Ordinance if they comply with alterative reporting requirements specified in the Rules.

The SFC received five responses. Most of the comments received were in relation to the application of the exemptions. Taking into account these comments, several amendments to the draft rules were made for the purposes of clarifying of the intended application of the exemptions. In addition, the draft Rules have been renamed to better reflect the substance of the rules. A Consultation Conclusion released today by the SFC provides an analysis of the submissions and explains the Commission's perspectives.

The Rules will become effective on the day appointed for the commencement of Part VII of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

Copies of the Consultation Conclusion are available at the SFC's office and on the SFC's website at www.hksfc.org.hk.
