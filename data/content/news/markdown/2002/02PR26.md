The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today releases for public consultation certain proposed amendments to the current Financial Resources Rules (FRR) which will: (1) help margin financing intermediaries better manage their credit risks; and (2) provide better investor protection. 

"Under the current market condition, it is particularly important for firms to manage their lending and liquidity risks. This is key to the firms' own financial health and investor protection," said Mrs Alexa Lam, SFC Executive Director of Intermediaries and Investment Products.

In essence, the draft amendments introduce two new measures to address the most risky and imprudent practices noted amongst margin financing intermediaries, namely the acceptance as collateral for margin loans a large quantity of illiquid stocks; and re-pledging the liquid stocks from "inactive" margin clients to obtain bank loans to fund the firms' own working capital.

The SFC proposes to amend the FRR to require margin financing intermediaries to:


- apply a 90% "illiquid collateral" haircut (or discount ratio) on illiquid stocks and warrants (as defined in the consultation paper with a number of criteria) which are pledged as margin collateral; and
- effectively self-finance at least 50% of the total amount lent to margin clients.

In the formulation of the proposed amendments, the SFC has already consulted market participants and incorporated their comments, where appropriate.

The FRR are made under section 28 of the Securities and Futures Commission Ordinance (Cap. 24). Rules made by the SFC are subject to negative vetting by the Legislative Council. The SFC must also consult the Financial Secretary before promulgating the FRR or any changes. In addition, the SFC now releases the draft amendments to the FRR for public consultation.

The SFC has used the FinNet communication network to send copies of the consultation document to all registered dealers and securities margin financiers. In addition, copies of the consultation document are available free of charge at the SFC's office and on the SFC's Internet website at [http://www.hksfc.org.hk.](/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/consultation/doc?refNo=02CP5)
