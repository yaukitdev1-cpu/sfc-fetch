The Securities and Futures Commission releases today the Consultation Conclusions on the draft Securities and Futures (Fees) Rules to be made under section 395 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance. 

The draft Rules require and provide for payment of fees to the SFC and prescribe fees, including fees in respect of applications, takeovers and mergers, things done by the SFC in the performance of its functions and other matters with regard to which provision is made under the SFO.

On 30 July 2002, the SFC issued a Consultation Document on the draft Rules for public consultation. This followed a public consultation in March 2002 on the proposed licensing fees under the new licensing regime.

The SFC received a total of two submissions responding to the consultation in relation to the fees in respect of investment products. With the adoption of a single notion of "collective investment scheme" in Part IV of the Ordinance, it is intended to streamline and rationalize the fee structures in order to ensure that there is a level playing field for all collective investment schemes in respect of the fees required to be paid to the SFC, regardless of whether they are structured in the form of a trust, corporation or policy contract. The respondents sought clarification on various aspects of the new fee structure. Details of the respondents' comments and the SFC's responses are set out in the Consultation Conclusions.

After the completion of the consultation exercise and considering the comments, it is considered that no amendment to the substance of the draft Rules is necessary. The draft Rules have however been refined to improve drafting where necessary.

Copies of the Consultation Conclusions are available at the SFC's office and on the SFC's website at www.hksfc.org.hk.
