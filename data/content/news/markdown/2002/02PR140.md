The Securities and Futures Commission today publishes the July issue of *SFC Alert*, the SFC's monthly newsletter for both investors and intermediaries. 

In this issue's cover story *Clamping down on misgovernance*, Mr Alan Linning, the SFC's Executive Director of Enforcement, names corporate misgovernance as one of the SFC's major enforcement areas for the coming year. He also explains the other two enforcement priorities, namely serious misconduct by intermediaries and general market misconduct including market manipulation and insider dealing.

To promote investor protection, *Investors beware of high-rate deposit offers* highlights the risks associated with high-rate deposit schemes, usually targeted by broker firms at clients who hold large cash balances in their accounts. Meanwhile, *Old tricks, new firms* reminds investors of two typical types of scam emails and calls, as well as the precautionary procedures they should take upon receipt of these messages.

The Financial Market Development Task Force has earlier announced certain recommendations to facilitate the development of Hong Kong's financial markets. *Bagging the quick wins* explains the Task Force's "quick win" proposals, and the SFC's role in implementing these proposals.

*Here's the money* outlines the key findings of the SFC's Fund Management Activities Survey 2001. The findings confirm Hong Kong's position as a leading fund centre in the region.

*Curbing Risks* discusses the SFC's current financial regulatory framework for intermediaries and puts the SFC's recent formation of a review group in perspective.

In *Tapping the China market*, the SFC updates intermediaries on the requirements for the foreign shareholder of joint venture securities companies in the Mainland.

Locally, intermediaries may be pleased to know that the SFC has recently introduced more flexible client identification procedure for online account opening. *A/C opening made easier* explains the new procedure, which became effective on 28 June 2002.

 Last but not least, this issue continues with its regular column of updates on matters relating to the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

The July issue of *SFC Alert* is the tenth issue of the newsletter, which was first published in October 2001.

Copies of SFC Alert are available at the SFC's office. This latest issue and all previous issues are also available on the Commission's website at www.hksfc.org.hk.
