The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) is pleased to announce the appointments of members of the Takeovers and Mergers Panel and the Takeovers Appeal Committee for the year 1 April 2002 to 31 March 2003 (please see attached list). 

The SFC welcomes Mr. Malcolm Brown and Mr. John Lees as new members of the Panel. The SFC also welcomes the re-appointment of Mr. Kevin Westley as Chairman and Mr. Clifton Chiu and Mr. Henry Fan as Deputy Chairmen of the Panel.

Mr. Mark Dickens and Mr. Andrew Procter have stepped down from the Panel. This followed the recent amendments to the Takeovers and Mergers Code (effective on 1 February 2002) that now provides that no Executive Directors of the SFC may be members of the Panel.

The Panel and Appeal Committee are voluntary bodies which owe their success to the dedication and expertise of their members. The SFC is confident that the significant experience of members in takeovers and mergers and the securities and financial markets will ensure the continued success of the Panel and Appeal Committee.
