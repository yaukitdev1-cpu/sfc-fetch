The SFC today publishes for public consultation proposed revisions to the existing Code of Conduct for Persons Registered with the SFC.  The Code, as revised, forms part of the proposed amendments necessary for transition to the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO).

The main purposes of the revisions are: -

(a) to rationalise the Code with the SFO and its rules; and 

(b) to have a uniform code of conduct applicable to all intermediaries (including authorized institutions registered with the SFC and licensed leveraged foreign exchange traders) and their representatives.

As such, only minimal changes are proposed in the consultation document.

Since the revised Code will also apply to licensed leveraged foreign exchange traders and their representatives, we propose to include specific conduct requirements for leveraged foreign exchange trading in the new Schedule 6 of the revised Code for easy reference.  These specific requirements are basically adopted from the existing conduct of business requirements under the Leveraged Foreign Exchange Trading (Books, Contract Notes and Conduct of Business) Rules and the Conduct of Business Guidelines for License Holders under the Leveraged Foreign Exchange Trading Ordinance. 

The SFC has sent copies of a consultation document to intermediaries via the FinNet communication network.  In addition, the public may obtain copies of the consultation document free of charge at the SFC’s office and on its website at [www.hksfc.org.hk](/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/consultation/doc?refNo=02CP27).
