The SFC announces today that it has successfully prosecuted {X} of Winsor Properties Holdings Limited, under the Securities (Disclosure of Interests) Ordinance.

{X} pleaded guilty in relation to two summonses regarding his failure to report to the Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited (HKEx) and Winsor Properties within five business days following his acquisition of 447,000 shares of Winsor Properties on 3 September 2001. {X} did not notify the HKEx and Winsor Properties of his interest until 15 November 2001.

Ms Polly Lo, a Magistrate at Western Magistracy, fined {X} a total of $10,000 and ordered him to pay costs of $17,516 to the SFC.
