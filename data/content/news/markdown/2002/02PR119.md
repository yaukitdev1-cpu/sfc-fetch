Two persons convicted of market manipulation were today given sentences of community service. 

Mr Peter White, a Magistrate at Western Magistracy, sentenced {X1} and {X2} to 160 hours and 96 hours of community service respectively for manipulating shares of Grand Field Group Holdings (Grand Field). {X1} and {X2} were also ordered to pay the SFC's investigation costs of $36,556 each.

On 10 June 2002, {X1} and {X2} each pleaded guilty before Mr White to one summons of intentionally creating a false and misleading appearance of active trading in Grand Field's shares during the period from 20 March 2000 to 7 June 2000. Sentencing for both {X1} and {X2} was adjourned until today, pending their background reports.

During sentencing, the Magistrate commented that he noted the respective financial positions of both {X1} and {X2}, and {X2}'s transactions were being financed by {X1}. Accordingly, the Magistrate considered {X1} had played a more significant part in the conspiracy than {X2}, and this was reflected in the sentencing.

An SFC spokesman said the SFC was encouraged that the Court viewed the offence as a serious matter.

For more details of the case, please refer to a press release issued by the SFC on 10 June 2002. The release is available on the SFC website www.hksfc.org.hk.
