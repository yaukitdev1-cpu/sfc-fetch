The SFC today issues the following decision on behalf of the Takeovers and Mergers Panel.

"On 31 October 2002 the Takeovers Panel affirmed a ruling of the Executive that a voluntary offer for all the shares in ICN by Koffman Securities Limited was an offer for the purposes of the Takeovers Code and that it should be properly responded to in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Takeovers Code.

The background to this matter is that on 5 September 2002 Koffman announced its intention to make a voluntary offer for all the issued shares in ICN. Shortly thereafter ICN advised the Executive that it did not consider the offer to be in accordance with the Takeovers Code. Given this, ICN stated that it did not propose to take any action in response to the Koffman offer until a number of matters had been investigated fully and a number of alleged breaches addressed.

The Executive ruled that the announcement of the offer was an announcement of the terms of an offer that triggered various obligations under the Takeovers Code. Given this, the Executive confirmed that a refusal by ICN to respond to the Koffman offer would not be in the interests of shareholders and would be in breach of the Takeovers Code. ICN applied to the Panel to review the Executive's ruling."

A copy of the Panel's decision is attached. A copy of the decision and the relevant Takeovers Code provisions can be found on the SFC Website at http:// www.hksfc.org.hk.
