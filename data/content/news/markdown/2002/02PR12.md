The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today invites comment on proposals for a scripless securities market. The proposals outline a model for the electronic issuance and transfer of shares and have been made following the Report of the Financial Secretary's Steering Committee on the Enhancement of the Financial Infrastructure. The proposed model will help to reduce the risk and inefficiencies of the current paper-based transfer system.

"A Consultation Paper on Proposals for a Scripless Securities Market" has been released for a three-month consultation. Major proposals in the consultation paper are summarised as follows:

Listed companies' registers of members would comprise two parts: a Central Clearing and Settlement System (CCASS) register and an Issuer register.

(a) The CCASS register. All CCASS Participants would appear on the CCASS register and enjoy the benefits of being registered shareholders. Share certificates held by CCASS would be cancelled and the CCASS register would be scripless.

(b) The Issuer register. The Issuer register would include both scripless and certificated holdings. Holders of share certificates would be able to deliver certificates to share registrars for cancellation and creation of scripless holdings.

Shareholders would have three main options for scripless holdings.

(i) Hold as beneficial owners through a broker, custodian or other nominee and not have their names appear on the register,

(ii) Be registered shareholders on the CCASS register or on the Issuer register and have full control of their holdings, or

(iii) Be registered shareholders similar to (ii) above but who give a broker or custodian an authority to make transfers for ease of trading or for pledging purposes.

The SFC will set up a working group to set the technical details and standards for the implementation of the proposals.

The consultation paper has been posted on the SFC website: http://www.hksfc.org.hk and hard copies will be available at the SFC's offices after 8 February 2002.
