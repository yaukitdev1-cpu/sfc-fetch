The Securities and Futures Commission releases today the consultation conclusions on the Securities and Futures (Levy) Order and the Securities and Futures (Levy) Rules. 

On 3 April 2002, the Securities and Futures Commission issued a consultation paper to solicit public comments on the draft Securities and Futures (Levy) Order and the draft Securities and Futures (Levy) Rules which it is proposed should be made by the Chief Executive in Council under section 394(1) and (5) respectively of the Securities and Futures Ordinance (No.5 of 2002) when it commences.

Section 394(1) of the Securities and Futures Ordinance empowers the Chief Executive in Council to specify, by Order, the rates of levy payable on sales and purchases of securities and futures contracts. Section 394(5) of the Ordinance empowers the Chief Executive in Council to make Rules for the payment of such levies and charges for any late payments, and for matters concerning the keeping, examination and audit of the accounts of the exchange companies relating to the collection and payment to the SFC of such levies.

The draft Order has been prepared having regard to the existing Securities and Futures Commission (Levy)(Securities) and (Levy)(Futures Contracts) Orders, which were made, respectively, under section 52(1) and (2) of the Securities and Futures Commission Ordinance (Cap. 24). The draft Rules have been prepared having regard to the existing Securities and Futures Commission (Levy) Rules, which were made under section 52(8) of the Securities and Futures Commission Ordinance. No new policy changes have been incorporated in the draft Order and Rules, which are intended simply to replicate the existing provisions.

The consultation period lasted until 30 April 2002.

One written submission and two verbal enquires were received. There were no substantive comments. No major change has been made to the substance of the original draft Rules and the draft Order. However, certain amendments have been made to the consultation draft of the Order and Rules with a view to improving the drafting of the Order and Rules.

Copies of the Consultation Conclusions are available at the SFC's office and on the SFC's websites at http://www.hksfc.org.hk.
