The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today publishes for public consultation a draft of the Securities and Futures (Short Selling Exemption and Stock Lending) Rules which the Commission proposes to make under section 397 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance (No. 5 of 2002). 

Sections 170 and 171 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance repeat the existing naked short selling prohibition in section 80 and the reporting requirements for "covered" short selling orders in section 80B of the Securities Ordinance respectively.

Based on the discussion with industry participants, the SFC proposes exemptions under the draft Rules to facilitate market making transactions, reduce compliance burdens on market and enable more timely execution of transactions.

Section 170 Exemptions

The proposed exemptions for naked short selling amalgamate the current exemptions in Rule 17 of the Securities (Miscellaneous) Rules and address inconsistencies under the current law to enable equal treatment for all categories of market makers. Under the draft Rules, the current exemptions will be extended to all market makers and liquidity providers registered with the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong and the Hong Kong Futures Exchange, to enable them to short sell securities in performing their market making obligations as well as to short sell underlying securities of products for which they act as market makers / liquidity providers for hedging purposes. No new policy changes have been incorporated.

Section 171 Exemptions

There are no exemptions from the "covered" short selling reporting requirements under the existing law. The proposed exemptions are to enable the timely execution of transactions whilst preserving a proper audit trail for short selling transactions. They will apply to sellers placing short selling orders pursuant to a stock borrowing and lending agreement as defined in the Securities and Futures Ordinance if they comply with alternative reporting requirements specified in the draft Rules.
