The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) releases today the Consultation Conclusions on the draft Securities and Futures (Registration of Commission Disciplinary Orders) Rules (the draft Rules) as proposed in the corresponding consultation paper released on 27 March 2002. 

The draft Rules provide for the manner in which applications may be made to register orders of the SFC in the Court of First Instance. It is proposed that essentially identical rules will be made by the Chief Justice to provide for registration of orders of the Securities and Futures Appeals Tribunal (SFAT) and the Market Misconduct Tribunal (MMT).

The orders of the SFC, SFAT and MMT, on registration, will be regarded for all purposes as orders of the Court of First Instance.

The draft Rules prescribe that on application to the Court of First Instance in respect of orders made by the Commission for the purposes of sections 194(5) and 196(5) of the Ordinance there must be produced to the Registrar of the High Court the order, in duplicate, and notice in writing requesting that the order be registered.

The draft Rules are procedural and merely implement the policy in the Ordinance that was considered in detail by the Legislative Council Bills Committee.

The SFC received one submission during the one-month consultation period. The details are set out in the Consultation Conclusions. The draft Rules are subject to negative vetting by the Legislative Council and will become effective on the day appointed for the commencement of Part IX of the Ordinance.

Copies of the Consultation Conclusions are available at the SFC's office and on the SFC's website at www.hksfc.org.hk and the FinNet communication network.
