The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the appointment by the Financial Secretary of Dr Eden Woon and Mr Samuel Poon to the Advisory Committee of the SFC for a period of one year from 1 June 2002 to 31 May 2003.

"I welcome Dr Woon and Mr Poon, who will bring their professional expertise and valuable experience to the Advisory Committee," said Mr Andrew Sheng, Chairman of the SFC.

Mr Sheng also thanked Mr Tai Tak Hing and Mr Louis Wolfe, who have retired from the Committee, for their valuable contribution.

The Advisory Committee is set up pursuant to section 10 of the Securities and Futures Commission Ordinance to advise the SFC on any matter of policy regarding the performance of its functions.
