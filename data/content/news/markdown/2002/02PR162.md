The Securities and Futures Commission warmly welcomes the re-appointment by the Financial Secretary, Mr Antony Leung, of Mr Raymond P L Kwok as a Non-Executive Director of the SFC. 

"We feel indebted to our Non-Executive Directors for their invaluable insight and active participation in the Commission's affairs," said SFC Chairman, Mr Andrew Sheng. "All of us at the Commission will continue to work closely with Mr Kwok."

Mr Kwok has been an SFC Non-Executive Director since 1998. He is also the Deputy Chairman of the SFC's internal Budget Committee.
