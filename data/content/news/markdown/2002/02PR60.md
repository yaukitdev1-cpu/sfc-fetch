A recently launched investor knowledge radio quiz will be extended for three weeks in light of overwhelming response.

Last month, the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) launched the competition on Radio Television Hong Kong (RTHK). It was due to end on 26 April 2002, but the joint organizers have decided to extend it until 17 May. The competition is broadcast in the "Investment Life Show" section of the "Bucket of Gold" programme on the Cantonese speaking RTHK Radio 1 (FM 92.6 or 94.4). "The Investment Life Show" is aired every Monday, Wednesday and Friday between 12.30 to 1 pm.

Cash prizes will continue to be given to winners of the competition. After a brief story in each segment of the "Investment Life Show", audience can call the RTHK's number at 187 2311 to enter the competition. Two callers will be chosen to answer questions. Apart from issues related to stock investing, the extended competition will also test investors' knowledge on funds and warrants.

The contestant with more correct answers wins $500 and the runner-up wins a SFC corporate briefcase. With the extension, total cash prizes will amount to $12,000.

The SFC welcomes investors to take initiatives to find out more about their rights and obligations in investing. The SFC-operated Electronic Investor Resources Centre (eIRC) at www.hkeirc.org offers valuable resources on topics of investing and investor protection.
