The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today releases the Consultation Conclusions on the draft Securities and Futures (Leveraged Foreign Exchange Trading - Exemption) Rules. 

The draft Rules were put out for public consultation on 5 June 2002 with the consultation period ended on 26 June 2002. The Rules prescribe, for the purposes of the definition of "leveraged foreign exchange trading" in Part 2 of Schedule 5 to the Securities and Futures Ordinance, that certain acts performed by any person belonging to three qualifying classes are to be excluded from the definition. The three classes are:

- corporations that carry on any form of leveraged foreign exchange trading but whose principal business is not in leveraged foreign exchange spot transactions or the average principal amount in such transactions is not less than $7.8 million; and the corporation or its sole holding corporation or partnership has a qualifying credit rating;
- licensed persons who are performing an act in connection with the sale, purchase or transfer of currency warrants that are listed or are to be listed, and clients of the licensed persons undertaking such transactions through the licensed persons; and
- issuers of such currency warrants and corporations belonging to the same group of companies as the issuers who are undertaking the above transactions through licensed persons, or undertaking intra-group purchases, sales or transfers of such currency warrants.
The first qualifying class replicates that of the existing Foreign Exchange Trading (Exemption) Rules prescribed under the Leveraged Foreign Exchange Trading Ordinance, while the latter qualifying classes constitute a new policy initiative. The aim is to facilitate the development of the listed currency warrant market by removing unnecessary technical obstacles, while not compromising investor protection.

Two written submissions were received from legal professionals and they have been posted on the SFC's website at www.hksfc.org.hk. Two other market practitioners have also informally commented on the draft Rules. The respondents generally welcome the proposed rules. The Consultation Conclusions provide an analysis of the submissions and explain the SFC's perspectives. No material changes to the draft Rules are required.

The Rules will become effective on the day appointed for the commencement of Part V of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

Copies of the Consultation Conclusions are available at the SFC's office and on the SFC's website at www.hksfc.org.hk and the FinNet communication network.
