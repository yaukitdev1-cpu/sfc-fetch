The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today releases the Consultation Conclusion on the draft Securities and Futures (Licensed Persons And Registered Institutions) Rules.

The Rules were put out for a one month public consultation on 16 November 2001 and they specify:

- the particulars to be entered in the register of licensed persons and registered institutions, including history of disciplinary actions;
- the circumstances when certificates of licence or registration need to be returned to the SFC, such as the termination of licensed representatives' accreditation and cessation of corporations' business in regulated activities; and
- the matters and changes that are to be promptly notified to the SFC.
A total of seven submissions from market participants and other organisations were received by the SFC during the consultation period. These submissions have been published on the SFC's website at www.hksfc.org.hk.

Taking into account the comments received, the Rules will be further refined to, among others:

- require the public register to include information of a firm's complaint channel instead of the details of an assigned complaints officer as originally proposed;
- treat registered institutions and licensed persons alike, where feasible, in relation to reportable changes.
The Consultation Conclusion provides an analysis of the submissions and explains the SFC's perspectives.

The Rules will become effective on the day appointed for the commencement of Part V of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.
