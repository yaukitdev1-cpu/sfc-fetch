The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) releases today the Consultation Conclusions on the above draft subsidiary legislation.

On 28 March 2002, the SFC issued a consultation paper to solicit comments on the draft subsidiary legislation relating to the new investor compensation arrangements to be put in place under Parts III and XII of the Securities and Futures Ordinance. The draft subsidiary legislation comprises rules to be made by the Chief Executive in Council and rules to be made by the SFC under the Ordinance. The consultation period ended on 25 April 2002. A total of 6 submissions were received. There were no substantive comments. No major change has been made to the substance of the original draft Rules and the draft Order set out in the consultation paper. However, the draft Securities and Futures (Investor Compensation - Levy) Rules have been amended to achieve consistency with the existing practice so that no changes will be made to the existing levies and rates.

The Securities and Futures (Investor Compensation - Levy) Rules, previously named the Securities and Futures (Investor Compensation) Rules, provide for details about the funding arrangements of the new compensation fund, namely a 0.002% compensation fund levy on securities transactions (subject to several exceptions) and a $0.5 levy on each futures contract ($0.2 for each Mini-Hang Seng Index futures contract and stock futures contract) payable by both the buyer and the seller.

The Securities and Futures (Investor Compensation - Compensation Limits) Rules provide that $150,000 is the maximum amount of compensation that may be paid to a person making a claim from the investor compensation fund.

The Securities and Futures (Transfer of Functions - Investor Compensation Company) Order prescribes the functions to be transferred to the Investor Compensation Company.

The Securities and Futures (Investor Compensation - Miscellaneous) Rules has been retitled the Securities and Futures (Investor Compensation - Claims) Rules. The rules prescribe the persons who may make a claim for compensation from the investor compensation fund established under section 236 of the Ordinance, the manner in which a claim is to be made and how such a claim will be assessed and paid and other miscellaneous matters.

Copies of the Consultation Conclusions are available at the SFC's office and on the SFC's websites at www.hksfc.org.hk.
