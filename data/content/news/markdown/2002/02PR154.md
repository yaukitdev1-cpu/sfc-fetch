The Securities and Futures Commission releases today the Consultation Conclusions on the Proposed Licensing Fees under the Securities and Futures (Fees) Rules. 

On 8 March 2002, the SFC issued a Consultation Document on the proposed licensing fees for a one-month public consultation.

In the Consultation Document, it is proposed that licence application and annual fees will be reduced by 3% in general. Furthermore, an additional discount of 5% on annual fees for the two-year transitional period will be offered to intermediaries who apply to migrate to the new regime in the first year of the transitional period. In other words, intermediaries may enjoy up to 8% savings in annual fees during the transitional period.

In response to the consultation, a total of three submissions were received from the industry. The respondents generally welcomed the SFC's proposal to reduce licensing fees. In their submissions, they mainly sought clarification on issues concerning the need to be licenced for a particular regulated activity under specific circumstances after the new regime commences. Details of the respondents' comments and enquiries as well as the SFC's responses are set out in the Consultation Conclusions.

Having considered the comments received, the SFC decided to adopt almost all of the fees as set out in the Consultation Document, except that:

1. certain additional fee waivers are to be added so that during the transitional period, existing licensees or exempt persons will not incur extra licensing costs over and above the amounts they are currently required to pay under the existing regime; and

2. two fees payable for issuing confirmation letters in relation to a person's licensing status or other relevant matters are to be removed.

The proposed licensing fees will form part of the draft Securities and Futures (Fees) Rules which will be released for public consultation shortly.

Copies of the Consultation Conclusions are available at the SFC's office and on the SFC's website at [www.hksfc.org.hk](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/consult/e_conclusions_%20licensing_fees.pdf%20%20).
