The Securities and Futures Commission today publishes the September issue of SFC Alert, the SFC's monthly newsletter for both investors and intermediaries. 

This issue's cover story Hedge funds disclosure - have your say discusses the proposed Hedge Funds Reporting Guidelines and invites investors and market participants to comment on them. The proposals aim to enhance the transparency of hedge funds so as to allow investors to make informed investment decisions. Meanwhile, Understanding hedge funds explains the basics of hedge funds and offers advice to investors.

Stronger links with the CCB leads page 3 with the latest co-operation between the SFC and the Hong Kong Police's Commercial Crime Bureau (CCB). Locally, strengthening co-operation with the CCB will help in our battle against corporate misgovernance. On the international front, Behind bars tells how co-operation with the Financial Services Authority and the police in the UK has led to the imprisonment of a boiler room operator who set up its head office in Hong Kong to cheat overseas investors.

In this issue's "Investor Focus" column, Adjusting the tap explains the role of liquidity providers for derivative warrants and how the system works.

Licensed to deal contains survey results on the types of regulated activities exempt dealers and investment advisers (non authorized institutions) intend to operate after the Securities and Futures Ordinance comes into effect.

For intermediaries, Training aids provides an update on the new qualifications and recognized institutions the SFC has recently approved, while It's good to talk describes how the SFC gathers industry opinions for improving the regulation of the market.

The September issue of SFC Alert is the 12th issue of the newsletter, which was first published in October 2001.

Copies of SFC Alert are available at the SFC's office. This latest issue and all previous issues are also available on the Commission's website at www.hksfc.org.hk.
