The Securities and Futures Commission will broadcast the final episode of "Invest with Your Heads Up", entitled "Too Good to Be True" on RoadShow Media on 31 July 2002 (Wednesday). 

In this episode, Paul (played by Yau Bill) and his broker, Tracy (played by Chan Yan Yan), chat about a recent hot IPO stock that is over-subscribed. As Paul fails to get any of the allotted shares, Tracy offers to obtain some shares for him through other channels and asks him to privately buy the shares from her. Paul finds the deal too attractive to resist and therefore accepts Tracy's offer.

When Paul calls the brokerage to sell the IPO shares on the first day of listing, he is shocked to know that Tracy has disappeared and the shares are in fact never bought.

The key messages for retail investors are:

- Never enter into any private deals with your broker. It would be difficult for you to seek redress if you encounter problems later.
- Be careful if your broker offers to buy stocks for you at a discount off the market.
- Always trade in a proper way through a licensed brokerage firm.
"Invest with Your Heads Up" is a 12-episode investor video series produced by the SFC, which aims to explain stock trading and settlement procedures, as well as measures investors should take to protect their own interests when dealing in stocks. 

Airing began on 15 May 2002 on RoadShow Media on 2,400 buses of the KMB and Citybus and 200 public light buses for 12 consecutive Wednesdays.

The entire series is now available on the SFC-operated Electronic Investor Resources Centre, located at www.hkeirc.org.
