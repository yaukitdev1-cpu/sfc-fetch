The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today publishes an Addendum to the Guidance Note for Short Selling Reporting and Stock Lending Record Keeping Requirements. 

The Guidance Note was published by the SFC in August 2000 to clarify the application of the short selling provisions in the Securities Ordinance and the Securities (Stock Lending) Rules on certain types of transactions or in certain situations.

Since the commencement of the Securities (Amendment) Ordinance 2000 and the Securities (Stock Lending) Rules - which introduced the short selling reporting and stock lending record keeping requirements respectively - and the publication of the Guidance Note, the industry has raised additional queries in relation to the application of the short selling provisions in the legislation. In the light of these queries, the Commission has come to the view that it would be appropriate to provide additional guidance to the market.

The Addendum aims to address the queries raised by industry participants. It clarifies the application of the relevant short selling provisions in specific situations so that industry participants have comfort that they will not be in breach of those provisions in those circumstances. In addition, in response to the concerns of stock lenders, the Addendum stresses that the Commission does not intend to create a criminal offence for breaches of the Stock Lending Rules. It is hoped that this confirmation will encourage more stock lending activity in the Hong Kong markets.

The SFC will continue to work with industry participants and revise the Guidance Note in response to market developments and to reflect legislative changes.

Copies of the Addendum are available at the SFC's office and on the SFC's website at www.hksfc.org.hk.
