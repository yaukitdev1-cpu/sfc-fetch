The SFC issues this statement in response to media enquiries today:

- Mr Alan Linning, Executive Director, and Mr Eric Cheng, Senior Director, both of the Enforcement Division of the SFC, visited the China Securities Regulatory Commission (CSRC) this week.
- This was Mr Linning's first visit to the CSRC in Beijing since he took up office. Mr Linning is also the first Executive Director of SFC's Enforcement Division to visit the CSRC.
- The visit has been arranged for a long time as a courtesy call. They were to visit the CSRC in early September but the trip had been postponed.
- During the meetings between Mr Linning and CSRC officials, they sought to better understand the work of each other and to explore ways to further strengthen co-operation, and talked about recent topics of mutual interest.
- We are pleased that the visit has achieved satisfactory results and thank the CSRC for its hospitality.
