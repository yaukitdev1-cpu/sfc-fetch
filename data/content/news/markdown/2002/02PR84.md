The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) will broadcast Episode 2 of "Invest with Your Heads Up", entitled "Misery of Sharing an Account" on RoadShow Media on 22 May 2002 (Wednesday). 

In this episode, Mrs Wong trades stocks using the account of her friend, Jason. One day, Mrs Wong is delighted to see her stock rise sharply in price, but to her surprise, the account executive, Mr Zhou, tells her that Jason has already sold that stock. Even worse, Jason denies he has ever bought that stock for her. Mrs Wong feels helpless and does not know what to do.

The key messages for retail investors are:

- Never trade stocks through another person's account. Set up your own account for trading;
- Appointing a stock brokerage is not a light decision. Deal with licensed brokerages and account executives only;
- Not all brokerages offer the same service. Shop around to find out more about their scope of services, fees and charges and settlement procedures before making the selection;
- Read the client agreement and make sure you understand the terms and conditions as well as the obligations of an account holder under the agreement before you sign on it.

"Invest with Your Heads Up" is a new 12-episode investor video series produced by the SFC, which aims to explain stock trading and settlement procedures, as well as measures investors should take to protect their own interests when dealing in stocks.

Airing has begun on 15 May 2002 on RoadShow Media on 2,400 buses of the KMB and Citybus and 200 public light buses for 12 consecutive Wednesdays, with an estimated 3.5 million passenger journeys every day. After broadcast, each episode will be made available on the SFC-operated Electronic Investor Resources Centre, located at www.hkeirc.org.

Investors are welcome to contact the SFC (by email: investor.info@hksfc.org.hk or by phone: 2840-9333) if they have any feedback or enquiries on the programme.
