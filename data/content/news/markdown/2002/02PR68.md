The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today releases the Consultation Conclusion on the draft Securities and Futures (Recognized Counterparty) Rules as proposed in a consultation paper published on 30 November 2001. 

The Rules provide, in addition to authorized institutions and licensed leveraged foreign exchange traders as stipulated under the Securities and Futures Ordinance, four other categories of institutions to be recognized counterparty:

1. Corporations licensed in Hong Kong for dealing in futures contracts that deal in currency futures contracts on specified futures exchanges;

2. Futures dealers regulated in specified jurisdictions that deal in currency futures contracts on specified futures exchanges and have shareholders' funds of at least HK$30,000,000 or its equivalence;

3. Banks which are incorporated in specified jurisdictions and are regulated by specified bank regulators; and

4. Corporations that have issued debt instruments which continue to attract a qualifying credit rating.

The SFC can also grant relief where particular circumstances and anomalous cases do not come within the main categories but it is still appropriate to grant recognition as a recognized counterparty.

During the one-month consultation period, the SFC received a total of six responses from leveraged foreign exchange traders, professional associations and academics. The submissions were carefully considered and amendments were made to the original draft Rules. We have further consulted industry participants for the proposed amendments. The amendments received a high level of acceptance and constituted the basis for the revised Rules.

The Consultation Conclusion provides an analysis of the submissions and explains the Commission's perspectives.

The Rules are subject to negative vetting by the Legislative Council and will become effective on the day appointed for the commencement of Part XVI of the Ordinance.

Copies of the Consultation Conclusion are available at the SFC's office, on the SFC's website (www.hksfc.org.hk) and the FinNet communication network.
