The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) will broadcast Episode 7 of "Invest with Your Heads Up", entitled "Care to Pay" on RoadShow Media on 26 June 2002 (Wednesday). 

In this episode, Fai (played by Fung Kwok) tells his broker, Tracy (played by Chan Yan Yan) that he finds it a hassle to bring her a cheque for settlement every time he buys a stock. Tracy then suggests Fai to deposit money into her personal bank account, and she will then settle the transactions with the brokerage on his behalf. Fai accepts Tracy's suggestion, but this turns out to be a nightmare.

One day, Fai receives a call from the brokerage, urging him to settle a buy transaction executed one week ago. Fai claims that that he has already deposited the money into Tracy's bank account and reminded her to settle the trade for him. However, the brokerage tells him that the settlement money is not received.

The key messages for retail investors are:

- Make all payments to the brokerage directly. Never deposit money directly into the bank account of your account executive. It is also unwise to give blank signed cheques to anybody;
- If you have to deposit money into the brokerage's bank account for settlement, remember to write on the deposit slip your trading account number and name together with your signature before passing the slip to the brokerage. Keep a copy of the deposit slip for your own record;
- Be careful when your account executive suggests a change in settlement method, whereby cheques are to be made out in the name of the account executive, instead of the brokerage.
"Invest with Your Heads Up" is a new 12-episode investor video series produced by the SFC, which aims to explain stock trading and settlement procedures, as well as measures investors should take to protect their own interests when dealing in stocks. 

Airing began on 15 May 2002 on RoadShow Media on 2,400 buses of the KMB and Citybus and 200 public light buses for 12 consecutive Wednesdays. After broadcast, each episode will be made available on the SFC-operated Electronic Investor Resources Centre, located at www.hkeirc.org.
