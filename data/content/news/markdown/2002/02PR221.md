The SFC today issues a circular to all registered investment advisers on cold calling.

The circular has been prompted by a number of complaints to the SFC's Complaints Control Committee (Note 1) against registered investment advisers for cold calling to sell funds and financial advisory or planning services. The Committee considers it appropriate to remind registered investment advisers that while conducting business, they should comply with the provisions governing hawking of securities in section 74(1) of the Securities Ordinance.

Hawking of securities is in general prohibited to protect the investing public from:

- using an intermediary without fully understanding its background and registration status;
- believing in what a stranger brags about the investment opportunities;
- opening an account without following the proper procedures;
- recklessly giving personal details and money to a stranger;
- buying a stock without fully understanding the background of the stock issuer; and
- falling in the facade put up by the stranger, such as the seemingly professional analyst reports and company brochures.
A copy of the circular is available on the SFC website at www.hksfc.org.hk under the section "Intermediaries, Licensing and Investment Products - Licensing Related Matters - What's New".
