The Securities and Futures Commission welcomes the appointment today by the Financial Secretary of the three members of the expert group to review the regulatory structure.

Mr Alan Cameron, Deputy Chairman of the Sydney Futures Exchange Corporation Limited and formerly Chairman of the Australian Securities and Investments Commission, leads the group. The other members are Dr Raymond Ch'ien and Mr Peter Clarke.

"The three members bring with them a wealth of knowledge and expertise in international regulatory practices as well as Hong Kong's local conditions," said Mr Andrew Sheng, Chairman of the SFC.

"We look forward to working with the Expert Group on this very important review," he said.
