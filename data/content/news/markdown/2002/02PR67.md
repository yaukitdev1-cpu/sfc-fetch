The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today releases the Consultation Conclusion on the draft Securities and Futures (Contracts Limits and Reportable Positions) Rules as proposed in the consultation paper released on 28 March 2002. 

The Rules set out the limits on the number of specified futures contracts and specified stock options contracts which may be held or controlled by any person in any one contract month or expiry month. The Rules also provide that the limits apply unless a person has an express authorization from a recognized exchange company and require a person holding or controlling a reportable position in such contracts to notify a recognized exchange company under its rules.

The SFC received two responses. A Consultation Conclusion released today by the SFC provides an analysis of the submissions and explains the Commission's perspectives.

The Rules will become effective on the day appointed for the commencement of Part III of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

Copies of the Consultation Conclusion are available at the SFC's office and on the SFC's website at www.hksfc.org.hk.
