The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) will broadcast Episode 10 of "Invest with Your Heads Up", entitled "Contract Notes = Statements?" on RoadShow Media on 17 July 2002 (Wednesday). 

In this episode, Mr Chu (played by Mak Cheung Ching) thinks that there is no need to check account statements because he receives a contract note for every transaction and the information in it is always correct. Besides, he finds it a pain reading long statements given that he trades frequently. He therefore throws them away whenever he receives them from his broker firm.

One day, when Mr Chu places an order to sell a stock, he is shocked to know that the stock is no longer in his account!

The key messages for retail investors are:

- After a trade has been executed, your brokerage must issue a contract note to you, listing out details of the transaction, on the next trading day.
 - Your brokerage also has to provide you with a regular statement of account at least once a month. A statement contains trading and settlement details during the statement period, cash balances and a full list of shares held in your account on the statement date. It serves as a proof of ownership of your shares and cash held at the brokerage.
- Contract notes and statements are different documents and serve different purposes for investors. Contract notes provide details of individual transactions only. You still need to check your statements in order to keep track of the activities in your account.
- Ensure contract notes and statements are issued on time. Keep these documents and verify the information in them, as they are official records of your transactions.
- If you spot any discrepancies in your transaction documents, contact the management of the brokerage immediately for clarification.

"Invest with Your Heads Up" is a new 12-episode investor video series produced by the SFC, which aims to explain stock trading and settlement procedures, as well as measures investors should take to protect their own interests when dealing in stocks.

Airing began on 15 May 2002 on RoadShow Media on 2,400 buses of the KMB and Citybus and 200 public light buses for 12 consecutive Wednesdays. After broadcast, each episode will be made available on the SFC-operated Electronic Investor Resources Centre, located at www.hkeirc.org.
