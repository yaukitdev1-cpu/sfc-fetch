The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today publishes the Spring 2002 issue of the *SFC Quarterly Bulletin.*

The feature article of this issue, *"The Securities and Futures Ordinance, 2002 - Continuity and Change"* gives an overview of the newly enacted Securities and Futures Ordinance. It illustrates how the existing law is continued and what new features have been included.

This issue includes a speech entitled *The Art of Financial Regulation* by SFC Chairman Mr Andrew Sheng. The article on *The Securities Clearing and Settlement System* gives an overview of clearing and settlement process and provides a comprehensive framework to risk management. The market update, *Consensus Recovery Restores Investor Confidence*, discusses on the performance of major stock markets during the first quarter of 2002. Statistics on the securities markets and the domestic industry are also provided.

Interested parties may subscribe to the *Bulletin* at $500 per annum; academic, students and Hong Kong Securities Institute members are entitled to a special discount of 20%. The *Bulletin* may also be downloaded free of charge from our website at www.hksfc.org.hk.
