The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) is pleased that the Special Administrative Region Government has accepted its recommendation to reduce the SFC levy on options on stock futures contracts trading to $0.2 per contract per side, from $1.0 per contract per side at present. Separately, the Commission has also made rules to reduce the compensation fund levy on trading of the same to $0.1 per contract per side, from $0.5 per contract per side. 

The reductions are to bring the levies payable on the trading of options on stock futures contracts in line with those payable on stock futures contracts which are their underlying. Stock futures contracts have been subject to a reduced SFC levy of $0.2 per contract per side and compensation fund levy of $0.1 per contract per side since 16 November 2001 as a result of a reduction in contract size. The options on stock futures contracts currently trading on the Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited are the Options on Korean Stock Futures, Options on Taiwanese Stock Futures, Options on US Stock Futures and Options on Japanese Stock Futures.

The Securities and Futures Commission (Levy) (Futures Contracts) (Amendment) (No.3) Order 2001(Commencement) Notice 2002 will be gazetted by the Government on 8 March 2002 to bring the levy reductions into effect.
