The Securities and Futures Commission welcomes the findings contained in the Process Review Panel's first Annual Report, which was published today.

The SFC considers that the Report contains a comprehensive review of the fairness and reasonableness of its operating procedures and a full assessment of whether those procedures had in fact been adhered to by the Commission in a cross-section of cases selected at random by Panel members.

The SFC is pleased to note that, with few exceptions, its internal procedures were generally found to contain adequate checks and balances to ensure fairness of treatment to those regulated by the Commission. The Panel found that the Commission had adhered to those procedures in the 43 cases that it had reviewed.

The SFC welcomes the helpful suggestions made for improving its operating procedures.

An SFC spokesman said: "We are pleased to report that the majority of those suggestions have already been adopted. In particular, a number of licensing and inspection procedures have been streamlined and standardised, thereby helping to engender an improved business environment while simultaneously encouraging and facilitating regulatory compliance."

From the outset the SFC has welcomed the establishment of the Panel as an independent body to complement existing checks and balances on the performance of the SFC's functions.

The Commission believes that the Panel provides an important means of enhancing the Commission's transparency and public accountability, and has been pleased to work with Panel members to that end over the last year.

As the Panel proceeds to the next stage of its work, the Commission looks forward to continuing its co-operation with the Panel during the next 12 months.
