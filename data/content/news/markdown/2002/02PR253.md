The SFC plans to produce a video series to explain to the public its regulatory work and would like to invite production houses to submit proposals. 

The SFC is responsible to the public for the performance of its functions.  It is important that the public understands the SFC’s regulatory role, objectives and functions and how it carries out its work. 

To this end, the SFC intends to produce a video series to explain its regulatory functions, covering its work in the making of rules, formulating market development policies, licensing and supervision of intermediaries, authorisation of collective investment schemes, market surveillance, investigation of market misconduct, regulation of takeovers of public companies, handling of investors’ complaints and market contingency planning.  The series will also highlight some significant changes under the new Securities and Futures Ordinance which is expected to commence on 1 April 2003. 

The SFC now invites production houses to submit proposals on providing the necessary design and production services.  The details are set out in the Request for Proposals for Production of the Video Series on SFC’s Work, available on the “Employment & Miscellaneous -- Request for Proposals” section of the SFC website.  Interested companies can send their proposals to the SFC on or before 6 January 2003.
