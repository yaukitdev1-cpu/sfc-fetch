The Securities and Futures Commission has introduced a new e-mail alert service on its corporate website at www.hksfc.org.hk.

Investors and other users who have signed up for this free service will be notified of any updates made to the parts of the website in which they have indicated interest.

The e-mail alert service presently is available for the sections on investor education, licensing matters, and the new Securities and Futures Ordinance. More sections will be included in the future.

The SFC believes the use of the service is a convenient and timely way of getting the latest news on specific matters. It represents an initiative of the SFC to encourage the use of its corporate website, which contains useful information for investors, intermediaries, other market participants and interested persons.

Visitors may click on this icon [](https://www.sfc.hk/sfcUpdateAlert/jsp/EN/update_alert.jsp)on the home page or anywhere within the SFC website to sign up for the service.
