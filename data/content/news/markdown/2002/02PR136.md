The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) releases today the Consultation Conclusions on the draft Securities and Futures (Disclosure of Interests - Exclusions) Regulation as proposed in the corresponding consultation paper released on 16 May 2002. 

Part XV of the Securities and Futures Ordinance requires the disclosure of interests in securities by persons who are interested in 5% or more of the relevant share capital of a listed corporation and by directors and chief executives of listed corporations. Under section 376 of the Ordinance the Chief Executive in Council may make regulations covering a range of matters including prescribing interests to be disregarded for the purposes of Part XV of the Ordinance.

The SFC received a single submission during the consultation period. The details are set out in the Consultation Conclusions. In the light of the submission the SFC proposes to create an additional exemption for intermediaries who enter into transactions in Exchange traded stock futures contracts and stock options contracts for their clients. The SFC considers that the exemption can be compared with the existing exemption in Part XV of the Ordinance for an intermediary who acquires an interest in shares of a listed corporation as an agent for a client. The exemption can be justified on a similar basis - that the intermediary does not acquire an economic interest in the underlying shares if the intermediary, to all intents and purposes, entered into the stock futures or options contract for a client.

The Regulation is subject to negative vetting by the Legislative Council and will become effective on the day appointed for the commencement of Part XV of the Ordinance.

Copies of the Consultation Conclusions are available at the SFC's office and on the SFC's website at [www.hksfc.org.hk](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/consult/doi_exclusions_consult_concl_eng.pdf%20%20) and the FinNet communication network.
