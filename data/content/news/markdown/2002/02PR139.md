The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announces today that it has successfully prosecuted {X} for contravening the Protection of Investors Ordinance. 

{X} issued three advertisements in a local Chinese newspaper inviting the acquisition of shares of a locally listed company. The three advertisements, which had not been authorized by the SFC, were published between 23 January 2002 and 25 January 2002.

{X} pleaded guilty to three summonses before Ms Polly Lo at Western Magistracy today. {X} was fined $3,000 for each summons and ordered to pay costs totalling $9,053 to the SFC.
