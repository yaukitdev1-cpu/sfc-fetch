The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today publishes for public consultation a draft of the Securities and Futures (Miscellaneous) Rules (the draft Rules) which the SFC proposes to make under section 397 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance (5 of 2002) when it commences. 

Several sections of the draft Rules have previously been the subject of public consultation and the Commission invites comments only on sections 4 and 7 of the draft Rules.

Section 4 of the draft Rules requires each intermediary to maintain a register of complaints received by the intermediary concerning its conduct or the conduct of its employees and to make this register available for inspection by its clients or an interested member of the investing public during office hours. This requirement is introduced in the interests of investor protection and transparency and would help to ensure that client complaints are being addressed.

Section 179 of the Ordinance gives a person, who the SFC has authorized, the power to inquire into suspected crimes or misconduct in a corporation that is or was listed. In the course of such an inquiry, the authorised person can compel an "auditor" to produce documents in the nature of audit working papers and compel the "auditor" to explain documents that have been produced. In section 7 of the draft Rules the SFC proposes to widen the category of persons considered to be "auditors" for the purpose of section 179 of the Ordinance. The term "auditor" in section 179 will cover:

- former auditors;
- auditors appointed under foreign legislation; and
- people employed or engaged by an auditor for the purposes of an audit, whether or not they are "auditors" themselves.
