The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) will broadcast Episode 9 of "Invest with Your Heads Up", entitled "Keep Your Personal Information to Yourself" on RoadShow Media on 10 July 2002 (Wednesday). 

In this episode, Paul (played by Yau Bill) gives a xerox copy of his ID card to his colleague, Jason (played by Ng Wai Yip), to arrange for membership of a fitness centre at a discounted admission fee.

Three months later, Paul receives a call from a brokerage, requesting him to settle a large outstanding balance. Paul then finds out that Jason has used his identity to open an account at the brokerage and by forging his signature, traded actively on the account, and resulted in huge losses.

The key messages for retail investors are:

- There are no good reasons for a new client to have a third party opening an account on his/her behalf. SFC licensed brokerages are obligated under the "know-your-client" rule to clearly establish the true and full identity of a new client, his/her financial situation, investment objectives and experience.

- Don't let anyone use your identity or lend your account to a third party to trade. If you knowingly allow third parties to operate your account, you may be liable for any losses suffered. Even worse, you may attract criminal liability if your account has been used for market manipulation.

- Report to the brokerage immediately and seek its explanation when you discover that an account has been opened by another person who misuses your identity.

"Invest with Your Heads Up" is a new 12-episode investor video series produced by the SFC, which aims to explain stock trading and settlement procedures, as well as measures investors should take to protect their own interests when dealing in stocks.

Airing began on 15 May 2002 on RoadShow Media on 2,400 buses of the KMB and Citybus and 200 public light buses for 12 consecutive Wednesdays. After broadcast, each episode will be made available on the SFC-operated Electronic Investor Resources Centre, located at www.hkeirc.org.

Investors are welcome to contact the SFC (by email: investor.info@hksfc.org.hk or by phone: 2840-9333) if they have any feedback or enquiries on the programme.
