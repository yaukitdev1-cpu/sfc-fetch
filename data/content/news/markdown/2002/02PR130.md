The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today releases the Consultation Conclusions on the draft Securities and Futures (Price Stabilizing) Rules.

The draft Rules were put to the market and the public for consultation on 8 February 2002. They prescribe the circumstances where price stabilizing actions may be taken to facilitate securities offerings where shares or debentures are offered to the public in Hong Kong.

During the one-month consultation period, a total of seven submissions were received from industry practitioners, legal professionals, and other interested parties. The submissions were carefully considered and amendments were made to the original draft Rules. The Consultation Conclusions provide an analysis of the amendments to the draft Rules and explain our perspectives in making these changes.

The key features of the Rules are summarized as follow:

- The Rules will be available to offers of shares or debentures to the public where the offer size exceeds HK$100 million (originally proposed to be HK$200 million).
- Both primary offers and secondary offers will be eligible provided that the relevant documentation requirements are met. (In the original proposal only primary offers are eligible.) In particular, placing and top-up transactions will be eligible so long as the offer is wholly for corporate fund raising.
- Prior disclosure of intended stabilizing actions should be made in communications issued to the public.
- A stabilizing manager has to be appointed to oversee the stabilizing actions, to keep records of such transactions, and to make them available for inspection by the issuer and the SFC.
- Stabilizing bids for shares will be subject to upper limits, which should not be higher than the public offer price.
- The stabilizing period will run from the commencement of trading following the issue of the relevant offer document and fixing of the offer price, and will end on 30 days after the earlier of the closing day or the commencement of trading.
- After the stabilizing period, disclosure of certain information on the stabilizing transactions undertaken has to be made.
The Rules will put Hong Kong on a par with other international markets in allowing underwriters of securities offering to take price stabilizing actions to reduce short-term price fluctuations resulting from the sudden increase in supply of the securities in the market, thereby promoting the orderliness and efficiency of the Hong Kong market. However, investors must be aware that there is no guarantee that there would be price stabilization or that any stabilizing actions, once taken, would continue.

The Rules are subject to negative vetting by the Legislative Council and will become effective on the same day Parts XIII and XIV of the Ordinance come into operation.

Copies of the Consultation Conclusions are available at the SFC's office, on the SFC's website ([www.hksfc.org.hk](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/speeches/public/consult/consult.html%20%20)) and through the FinNet communication network.
