The Securities and Futures Commission has received an investigation report from Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited (HKEx) on the HKATS trading disruption on 28 May 2002.

The SFC believes the recommendations in the investigation report would significantly reduce the likelihood of the recurrence of the problem. It will work closely with HKEx to address and implement the recommendations.

The SFC is pleased that HKEx has committed to improving its system stability and robustness, which is crucial for Hong Kong to continue to enjoy its status as an international financial centre.

The SFC will continue to actively monitor and advise HKEx as it embarks on its information technology improvement and a comprehensive review of HKATS and the other mission critical systems.

The SFC will oversee HKEx's implementation plan for the improvement measures. For system changes, the SFC will review the implementation plan, follow up on the progress and review the results. For policy and procedure changes, it will ensure that they are in the interest of and transparent to the investing public.

The SFC also welcomes HKEx's improvements in the procedures for the provision of timely and accurate information to the market if there is a trading interruption.
