The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today releases the Consultation Conclusion on the draft Securities and Futures (Leveraged Foreign Exchange Trading - Arbitration) Rules as proposed in the consultation paper released on 28 March 2002. 

The Rules prescribe the procedure for the settlement by arbitration of any dispute between a client and a licensed corporation in relation to leveraged foreign exchange trading.

The SFC received two comments, both of which were supportive. A Consultation Conclusion released today by the SFC provides an analysis of the submissions and explains the Commission's perspectives.

The Rules are subject to negative vetting by the Legislative Council and will become effective on the day appointed for the commencement of Part V of the Ordinance.

Copies of the Consultation Conclusion are available at the SFC's office, on the SFC's website www.hksfc.org.hk and the FinNet communication network.
