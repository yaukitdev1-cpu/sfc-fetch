The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announces today that it has reprimanded {X}, a dealer's representative registered under the Securities Ordinance. 

The reprimand follows an inquiry covering the period from May 2001 to June 2001 which revealed that {X} had failed to comply with his employer's internal policies in that:

- he did not request a new client with a cash account to make appropriate cash deposit before he executed orders for that client; and
he did not request the same client to sign on the order tickets when the client placed the orders in person. The SFC concluded that {X}'s conduct fell short of the standard of a registered representative and his fitness and properness to remain as a registered person had been impugned.

An SFC spokesman said: "A registered person should comply with all regulatory requirements applicable to the conduct of its business activities so as to promote the best interests of clients and the integrity of the market. Any internal policies implemented and maintained by a registered person for the proper performance of its business activities should also be observed by its staff. Failure to comply with these internal policies will be regarded seriously by the SFC."
