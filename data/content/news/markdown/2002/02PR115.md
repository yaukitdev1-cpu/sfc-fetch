The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today publishes the June 2002 issue of SFC Alert, the SFC's monthly newsletter for both investors and intermediaries.

This issue's cover story Investors taking ownership recommends that investors open a Stock Segregated Account with Statement Service in the Central Clearing and Settlement System (CCASS). The account enables investors to keep track of their shareholdings and stock movements in CCASS. If investors want to have full control of their shareholdings, they are encouraged to open an Investor Account in their own names. Characteristics and benefits of both accounts are explained in detail in the story.

To prepare the market for the implementation of the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO), the SFC has written a feature article, A fine balance, to highlight the efficiency gains of the new single licensing regime under the SFO. It explains how the new regime reduces compliance costs for intermediaries while giving investors better protection with increased transparency.

Other articles of interest include Unregistered dealing in cyberspace, which reports on the SFC's successful prosecution of an unlicensed on-line broker which breached both the Securities Ordinance and the Protection of Investors Ordinance.

Expanding horizons welcomes the mutual recognition of fund managers by the SFC and the Australian Securities and Investment Commission (ASIC). The article discusses the advantages that the agreement will bring to Hong Kong investors, fund managers and the local fund market as a whole.

Finally, there are articles on the latest SFO updates and an introduction to FinNet, the financial services network that connects Hong Kong's regulators and market intermediaries.

The June issue of SFC Alert is the ninth issue of the newsletter, which was first published in October 2001.

Copies of SFC Alert are available at the SFC's office. This latest issue and all previous issues are also available on the Commission's website at www.hksfc.org.hk.
