The SFC welcomes the appointment by the Financial Secretary of Mr Brett P Goodin as a member of the SFC Advisory Committee from 1 November 2002 to 31 May 2003. 

"I welcome Mr Goodin, who will bring his professional expertise and valuable experience to the Advisory Committee," said Mr Andrew Sheng, Chairman of the SFC.

Mr Sheng also paid tribute to Mr Keith Ferguson, who has retired from the Committee, for his valuable contribution.

The Advisory Committee is set up pursuant to section 10 of the Securities and Futures Commission Ordinance to advise the SFC on any policy matter regarding the performance of any of its functions.
