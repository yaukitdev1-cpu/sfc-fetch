The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) will broadcast Episode 8 of "Invest with Your Heads Up", entitled "Genuine or Forged?" on RoadShow Media on 3 July 2002 (Wednesday). 

In this episode, Paul (played by Yau Bill) shows to his colleague, Mr Chu (played by Mak Cheung Ching) contract notes for his profit-making transactions. Mr Chu notices that they are typewritten, unlike the computer-generated ones that he usually receives. Paul thinks that typewritten contract notes are all right as he sometimes receives hand-written ones, and his account executive also attributes such a change of format to the recent breakdown of the brokerage's computer system.

A few days later, Paul is shocked to know that all the typewritten and hand-written contract notes that he has received are in fact forged by his account executive.

The key messages for retail investors are:

- Contract notes and statements of account issued by brokerages are generally printed by computer. Be wary if they contain hand-written amendments that are not satisfactorily explained;

- Ensure contract notes and statements are issued timely;

- Keep your transaction documents and verify the information in them;

- Check with the management of the brokerage and not the account executive if the transaction documents that you receive are different in format from what you usually receive.

"Invest with Your Heads Up" is a new 12-episode investor video series produced by the SFC, which aims to explain stock trading and settlement procedures, as well as measures investors should take to protect their own interests when dealing in stocks.

Airing began on 15 May 2002 on RoadShow Media on 2,400 buses of the KMB and Citybus and 200 public light buses for 12 consecutive Wednesdays. After broadcast, each episode will be made available on the SFC-operated Electronic Investor Resources Centre, located at [www.hkeirc.org](http://www.eirc.hk/eirc/html/EN/index.htm).
