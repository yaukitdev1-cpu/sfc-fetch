SFC Chairman Mr Andrew Sheng will speak on The Art of Financial Regulation in the last session of a personal financial management course co-organised by the General Education Unit of The University of Hong Kong (HKU) and the SFC.

The lecture will be open to the public and the media. No registration is required.

Details of the lecture are as follows:

Date : 14 November 2002 (Thursday)

Time : 5:30 pm - 7:00 pm

Venue : Rayson Huang Theatre, HKU
(Seating capacity: 290. Members of the public will be admitted on a first-come-first-served basis. Limited seats have been reserved for representatives of the media.)

Language : English

Entitled "Financial Intelligence (FQ) - The Art of Personal Financial Management and Investment", the course comprises seven lectures by prominent market practitioners and SFC speakers. It has been running since 26 September 2002. This is the first time that the SFC has joined hands with a tertiary education institution to offer an academic course.
