The SFC will seek to ensure that Hong Kong's fund industry remains at the forefront of international fund management.

Ms Tina So, SFC's Director of Investment Products, said that in order to achieve this objective, Hong Kong needs to have an efficient, effective and pragmatic regulatory regime.

"We intend to work closely with the industry and the investing public in formulating timely responses to new policy initiatives as well as addressing market concerns," she said.

"The changes in global investment markets, the gradual opening up and de-regulation of the Mainland economy poses great challenges and opportunities to Hong Kong. The Commission believes that a strong three-way partnership between the regulator, investors, and the industry is essential towards achieving a robust and enterprising investment market," Ms So said.

Speaking at a press briefing today - the first since joining the SFC in July - Ms So said the Investment Products Department would focus on putting forward appropriate policy for new products and continuing to improve the codes and guidelines that govern the fund industry's best practices.

The Department's objectives are to:

- Facilitate wider product choice for investment and industry development;
- Ensure efficient processing of fund applications while upholding the principle of investor protection;
- Broaden international regulatory co-operation and recognition;
- Contribute to policy initiatives aimed at developing the fund management industry in Hong Kong;
- Work with the CSRC on funds related policy issues; and
- Reinforce investor education and communication
To widen product choice for investors, the SFC in the fourth quarter of 2002 aims to:

(a) complete the review of comments received from the recent public consultation on hedge funds disclosure requirements; and
(b) consult the public on the concept and viability of introducing Real Estate Investment Funds (REITs) in Hong Kong. Public consultation on the authorisation guidelines for REITs will follow in the first half of 2003 if response to the concept consultation is supportive.

Other projects currently under study include the facilitation of exchange traded funds (ETFs) and best practices for the marketing of funds.

Ms So said: "The primary objective of the Commission is to protect investor interests and uphold the integrity of the market. By doing this, we will be able to maintain public confidence, which is the cornerstone of a progressive and sustainable financial market."
