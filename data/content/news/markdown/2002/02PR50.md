The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has today published the Consultation Conclusion on the Proposed New Investor Compensation Arrangements. 

On 7 March 2001, the SFC issued a paper to solicit comments on proposals for new investor compensation arrangements for Hong Kong. A total of 12 submissions were received. Most comments were supportive of the proposed new compensation arrangements. The framework for the new compensation arrangements has been provided in Parts III and XII of the Securities and Futures Ordinance. Proposed related subsidiary legislation will be set out in a consultation paper to be issued by the SFC shortly.

Copies of the Consultation Conclusion are available at the SFC's office and on the SFC's website at http:// www.hksfc.org.hk .
