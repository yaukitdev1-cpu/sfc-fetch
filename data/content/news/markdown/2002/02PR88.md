The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) will broadcast Episode 3 of "Invest with Your Heads Up", entitled "At Your Discretion", on RoadShow Media on 29 May 2002 (Wednesday). 

In this episode, Mrs Chu (played by Lai Tsz Shan) introduces her brother, Fai (played by Fung Kwok) to her broker, Tracy (played by Chan Yan Yan) to set up a stock account. Apart from signing the client agreement, Tracy also suggests Fai to sign an authorization letter to grant her discretionary authority to trade for him. Tracy's reasoning is that this arrangement will enable her to watch the market for him and therefore will not miss any good investment opportunities. Fai is not sure if he should accept Tracy's offer.

The key messages for retail investors are:

- If you prefer to rely on your broker's expertise, you may open a discretionary account, whereby you give your broker the authority to act on his or her initiative without first seeking your instruction;
- If you choose to open a discretionary account, grant the authority to your broker in writing, and ensure that the account is designated as a "discretionary account" in the client agreement;
- The discretionary authority should be re-confirmed at least on an annual basis;
- Notify the broker firm in writing if you wish to cancel the discretionary arrangement;
- Do not give discretionary authority without seriously considering whether this arrangement is necessary or suitable for you.
"Invest with Your Heads Up" is a new 12-episode investor video series produced by the SFC, which aims to explain stock trading and settlement procedures, as well as measures investors should take to protect their own interests when dealing in stocks. 

Airing has begun on 15 May 2002 on RoadShow Media on 2,400 buses of the KMB and Citybus and 200 public light buses for 12 consecutive Wednesdays, with an estimated 3.5 million passenger journeys every day. After broadcast, each episode will be made available on the SFC-operated Electronic Investor Resources Centre, located at www.hkeirc.org.
