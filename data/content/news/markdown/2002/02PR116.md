The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) will broadcast Episode 6 of "Invest with Your Heads Up", entitled "Don't Ignore The Auditor's Circular" on RoadShow Media on 19 June 2002 (Wednesday). 

In this episode, Fai (played by Fung Kwok) receives a letter from an audit firm, requesting him to confirm his stock holdings and cash balance at a brokerage. Fai notices that a stock, the certificates of which were given to his broker Tracy (played by Chan Yan Yan) the week before for safe custody, is missing. Tracy explains that the brokerage has kept those certificates in its safe at the office premises, instead of depositing them with CCASS (Central Clearing And Settlement System). Hence, Fai's statement of account will not show those shares. Tracy also urges Fai to confirm to the auditor that the information on the statement is correct.

The key messages for retail investors are:

- When the SFC or independent auditors request you to confirm your holdings and account balances (a process called "circularization"), you should check the information against your records carefully and promptly report to the designated independent party named on the confirmation request, even if no discrepancies are noted;
- To ensure independency of the circularization exercise, it is not necessary to first contact your brokerage or the account executive if you spot any discrepancies;
- Be alert when your monthly statement does not show all your share holdings, and your broker gives you an excuse that some of your shares are held physically in the brokerage's office. A statement of account should show a complete list of stocks held in the account as at the statement date, no matter the stocks were bought on the market, or physically deposited with the brokerage for safekeeping;
Your brokerage should always issue a receipt for stock certificates received for safe custody. The brokerage usually deposits them with CCASS. After verifying the authenticity of the certificates with the registrar, CCASS will re-register them in the name of HKSCC (Nominees) Limited and entrust them to its Depository. "Invest with Your Heads Up" is a new 12-episode investor video series produced by the SFC, which aims to explain stock trading and settlement procedures, as well as measures investors should take to protect their own interests when dealing in stocks.

Airing began on 15 May 2002 on RoadShow Media on 2,400 buses of the KMB and Citybus and 200 public light buses for 12 consecutive Wednesdays. After broadcast, each episode will be made available on the SFC-operated Electronic Investor Resources Centre, located at www.hkeirc.org.
