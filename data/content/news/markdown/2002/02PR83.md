The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announces that the Hedge Funds Guidelines as contained in Chapter 8.7 of the Code on Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds (Code) have been published in the Gazette and become effective today. 

Since the release of the Guidelines, the SFC has received many enquiries from the industry on the application procedures and requirements for authorisation. Most of these enquiries are centred on two main areas, which we would like to clarify:

1. Eligibility for authorisation and supporting documents:

Applicants should refer to the basic requirements for fund authorisation in the Code with particular attention to the specific requirements set out in Chapter 8.7. To facilitate market practitioners to prepare supporting documents for their applications, the SFC has prepared an application checklist, which can be downloaded from the SFC website ([www.hksfc.org.hk](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/forms/products/products.html)) under Part C of Volume 2 of the Regulatory Handbook in the "Bills, Legislation & Codes" section.

2. Procedures for processing hedge fund applications:

The SFC will process hedge fund applications on a timely basis and in much the same way as other fund applications. However, the timing for authorization will depend largely on the readiness of the submission for authorization such as the quality of the application, and the disclosure standard of the offering and related documents.

As the Guidelines are new to the market, the SFC believes there may still be various aspects relating to the authorization and marketing of the products that market practitioners may wish to enquire. Market practitioners are encouraged to contact the the SFC's Investment Product Department by calling 2840-9259 on any questions about the application of the Guidelines.

The SFC also plans to launch a series of investor education programmes on hedge funds in the near future. Details will be announced nearer the time of their launch. If investors have general enquiries about funds and other investment products, please call the Investor Hotline at 2840-9333.
