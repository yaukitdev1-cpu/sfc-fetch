The SFC today publishes the November issue of *SFC Alert*, the SFC's monthly newsletter for both investors and intermediaries. 

This issue's cover story reports that the SFC’s accumulated deficit for the six months ended 30 September amounted to $20 million, 66% lower than the projected deficit. This was in part due to stringent control of expenditure, which totalled $197 million for the half year, representing a 6.4% drop from a year earlier.

In *Professionals must act professionally,* the SFC stresses that market professionals and intermediaries play a key role in promoting good corporate governance. They should do their jobs according to accepted professional standards or face possible discipline.

The enforcement news of *Keep your promises* urges intermediaries to observe their undertakings to the SFC. Otherwise, serious consequences might follow. In October, the SFC reprimanded a broker for breaching its undertakings to the SFC. In this issue, the SFC also announces the publication of a new monthly enforcement newsletter, the *SFC Enforcement Reporter*.

*Tape it for your own good* reminds SFC licensees to record clients’ telephone order instructions; such requirements protect the interests of an intermediary and its clients.

*Not for amateurs* is an investor education article explaining the differences between closing and locking a position in commodity futures trading. On more general aspects of investment, the SFC has produced VCDs and CD-ROMs to educate the public. *Watch, click, play… and learn* has the details.

Maintaining close dialogues with the market and the public has always been one of the SFC’s priorities. *Strengthen ties* reports that the SFC has begun a monthly forum with the broker community.

The November issue of *SFC Alert* is the 14th issue of the newsletter, which was first published in October 2001.

Copies of *SFC Alert* are available at the SFC's office. This latest issue and all previous issues are also available on the Commission's website at www.hksfc.org.hk.
