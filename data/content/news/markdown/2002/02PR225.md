SFC Chairman Mr Andrew Sheng spoke on the *Structure of the Brokerage Industry* at a Hong Kong Institute of Bankers Luncheon yesterday (13 November).

Mr Sheng took the view that there were great opportunities for banks and brokers to work together to improve the overall quality of service to the investor community. He said that in almost all developed markets, retail brokers had worked closely with banks in improving retail service – many had moved to become boutique financial planners, working with financial supermarkets that provided the custody and payment services.

"I envisage that this will happen in Hong Kong and I believe that the co-operation, not just competition, between banks and brokers would benefit everyone," Mr Sheng said.

In his speech, Mr Sheng also gave an overview of Hong Kong’s brokerage industry and pointed out that the consolidation of the industry was a worldwide phenomenon. He said that while the SFC was working to refine its regulatory approach on brokers’ capital and financial requirements, the Commission was extremely conscious to minimise the regulatory burden on the industry.

He added that the SFC and the Hong Kong Monetary Authority were working closely to ensure an open market with a level playing field for all financial intermediaries, including banks and brokers.
