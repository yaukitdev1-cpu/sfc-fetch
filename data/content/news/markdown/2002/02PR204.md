The Securities and Futures Commission today publishes the October issue of SFC Alert, the SFC's monthly newsletter for both investors and intermediaries. 

SFC Chairman Mr Andrew Sheng in this issue's cover story Fighting corporate misgovernance discusses the regulation of listed companies and the SFC's enforcement work against corporate misconduct.

Broker consolidation continues is an analysis of the trend of broker consolidation. Latest figures on cessation of business are available.

In Setting standards for funds, Ms Tina So, our recently appointed Director of Investment Products, explains the department's key guiding principles for policies for the development of Hong Kong's funds industry, and outlines projects in the pipeline.

Investment Linked Assurance Scheme (ILAS) is the focus of Leveraging up can hurt in this issue's "Investor Focus" column. The article highlights to investors the risks of taking out a loan to invest in ILAS.

Internet policing mobilized reports on SFC's participation in the Internet Surf Day, a regional campaign launched by International Organisations of Securities Commissions (IOSCO) to combat crime conducted over the Internet.

Another enforcement story Log your interests reminds directors of listed companies of their obligations to disclose their dealings in equity-linked instruments.

In Manipulators lose appeals, the SFC welcomes the Court's decision to dismiss appeals by two market manipulators against immediate custodial sentences imposed on them.

The October issue of SFC Alert is the anniversary issue of the newsletter, which was first published in October 2001.

Copies of SFC Alert are available at the SFC's office. This latest issue and all previous issues are also available on the Commission's website at www.hksfc.org.hk.
