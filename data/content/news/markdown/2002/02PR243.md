The SFC today publishes the December issue of *SFC Enforcement Reporter*, summarising the Commission’s enforcement actions in the month of November 2002 and discussing the compliance issues highlighted by those recent cases.

In November, the SFC won the appeal lodged by a substantial shareholder of a company who had been convicted of contravening the Securities (Disclosure of Interests) Ordinance. In addition, the SFC had three other people convicted for breaking securities laws and disciplined nine intermediaries for various misconduct.

As regard prosecution, the SFC noted that from recent judgments, it appeared that market manipulators who were convicted after pleading not guilty could expect to be jailed.

Since 1 April 2002, the SFC has successfully prosecuted 27 people and disciplined 52 entities.

The December issue of *SFC Enforcement Reporter* is the 2nd issue of the newsletter, copies of which are available at the SFC's office. This issue and the previous issue are also available on the Commission's website at www.hksfc.org.hk, under the section “Press Releases, Publications & Speeches – Publications”.
 
The public are welcome to subscribe to the newsletter by registering for the SFC Website Update Email Alert service on the Commission’s homepage. They will then be able to receive the newsletter via email.
