The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) will broadcast Episode 5 of "Invest with Your Heads Up", entitled "Pooling Risk of Margin Accounts" on RoadShow Media on 12 June 2002 (Wednesday). 

In this episode, Mrs Wong (played by Tsang Wai Wan) runs into her friend, Joyce (played by Chan Ka Man). Joyce shares with Mrs Wong her painful experience in trading a penny stock. Joyce decides to change her investment strategy by buying blue clips on margin so as to magnify returns. Mrs Wong does not support Joyce's idea as she thinks margin trading is risky.

The key messages for retail investors are:

Know the risks of margin trading:
a. Your loss will be magnified if your shares decline in value;
b. You may have to put up additional cash or shares to meet margin calls;
c. You may be forced to sell your shares if you fail to meet the margin calls;- You should also take into account the risk of "pooling", which is a feature of shares margin financing in Hong Kong, whereby margin clients' shares are pooled and re-pledged to banks to secure the brokerage's own lines of credit. In the event that the brokerage becomes insolvent, the banks, as first creditors to the pledged shares, could liquidate the shares to compensate their losses. Investors may not get back their shares if the brokerage's available assets fall short of the creditors' claims;
- Your brokerage may ask for your written authority to pledge your shares before it provides margin account services to you. Once signed, your brokerage will have the right to pledge your shares, whether you have traded on margin or not;
- Do not open a margin account if you have no intention of using such a credit facility.
"Invest with Your Heads Up" is a new 12-episode investor video series produced by the SFC, which aims to explain stock trading and settlement procedures, as well as measures investors should take to protect their own interests when dealing in stocks. 

Airing began on 15 May 2002 on RoadShow Media on 2,400 buses of the KMB and Citybus and 200 public light buses for 12 consecutive Wednesdays, with an estimated 3.5 million passenger journeys every day. After broadcast, each episode will be made available on the SFC-operated Electronic Investor Resources Centre, located at www.hkeirc.org.
