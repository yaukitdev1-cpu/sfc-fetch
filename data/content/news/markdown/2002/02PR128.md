The Securities and Futures Commission is taking action to protect investors' interests after finding that Prosperous Securities Limited has failed to operate with sufficient liquid capital.

The failure of Prosperous Securities to meet the minimum liquid capital requirement under the Financial Resources Rules was detected by SFC staff this week. The SFC has since been in contact with the owners and the management of Prosperous Securities. The owners have been requested to stay in Hong Kong or otherwise remain in contact with the SFC.

The management of Prosperous Securities have informed the SFC that there has not been any misappropriation of assets and all client assets are properly segregated. The SFC has begun a reconciliation exercise to check if client assets are properly protected.

SFC's Executive Director of Intermediaries and Investment Products, Alexa Lam, said: "The SFC places great importance on the financial health and integrity of brokers. Our broker monitoring and vigilance programme is designed to detect financial and integrity problems and the few recent cases show that by and large the system works."

She said the SFC would step up monitoring of brokers where necessary.

Following the SFC's intervention, Prosperous Securities has today voluntarily suspended its trading on the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong.

For the time being, the firm will not be allowed to execute any trades for clients (whether buy or sell).

Under SFC's close monitoring, the firm has made arrangements for the orderly return of money and stocks to investors upon request. Investors may call the SFC investor hotline at 2840-9333 for enquiries. There have been no complaints that requests for stock withdrawal cannot be met.

Clients are called upon to withdraw stocks from the firm or request it to transfer their shares to their designated brokers through the Central Clearing and Settlement System (CCASS).

In addition, clients are advised to exercise caution in their settlement with Prosperous Securities. For instance, clients who have bought securities through the broker in the last two days are advised to pay against delivery of scrip.

The SFC will continue to follow up on the matter and monitor the situation. It will take all necessary measures to protect the interests of investors.

Prosperous Securities has undertaken to fully co-operate with the SFC and provide all necessary information and documents upon request.

The FRRs are to ensure brokers have sufficient funds to operate their business and meet liabilities. The SFC carries out a vigilance programme, which seeks to ensure intermediaries comply with all regulatory requirements including financial resources.

The SFC has also recently tightened certain financial requirements in relation to margin lending activities of intermediaries. These will become effective on 1 October 2002 following the support of the Legislative Council members.

For the longer term, an SFC working group has been formed to advise on a future financial regulatory regime for intermediaries that is most suitable for Hong Kong. The system will seek to ensure that brokers have sufficient capital and risk management structure to buffer them against the risks of their business, and that investors do not stand to lose as a result of any such risks. The working group comprises mainly industry practitioners and other outside interested parties.

The SFC believes that for the greatest protection, investors need to work with the regulator as partners. Investors should exercise care and vigilance in the protection of their assets. If investors have any queries concerning the security of their assets, they should immediately call the SFC investor hotline at 2840-9333.

The SFC also wishes to take this opportunity to remind investors to consider opening an Investor Account in CCASS. An Investor Account is opened in the own name of the investor, and gives the investor full control of stock movements in the account.
