The SFC today publishes trial versions of the forms which the SFC proposes to prescribe for use by persons giving notice under Part XV of the Securities and Futures Ordinance when it commences.  

Part XV of the Ordinance requires the disclosure of interests in securities by persons who are interested in 5% or more of the relevant share capital of a listed corporation and by directors and chief executives of listed corporations. 

The trial forms are available in English and in Chinese, and in two formats. They can either be downloaded in PDF Format for manual completion. Alternatively they can be downloaded in Microsoft Excel Format and then completed using MS Excel 97 or above. There are detailed notes containing directions and instructions explaining how the each form should be completed. Two forms are for use by substantial shareholders and four forms are for use by directors and chief executives:

Form 1     -  Individual Substantial Shareholder Notice

Form 2     -  Corporate Substantial Shareholder Notice

Form 3A -  Director’s/Chief Executive’s Notice  - interests in shares of listed corporation

Form 3B  -  Director’s/Chief Executive’s Notice  - interests in shares of associated corporation

Form 3C -  Director’s/Chief Executive’s Notice  - interests in debentures of listed corporation

Form 3D  -  Director’s/Chief Executive’s Notice  - interests in debentures of associated corporation

A new system for electronic filing of the forms with the Stock Exchange is being developed. Further details of the new e-filing system will be announced in early 2003.

The trial forms are not final although material changes are not expected when they are finalised and gazetted.

Copies of the trial forms and notes are available on the SFC’s website at the following address: http: //www.hksfc.org.hk/eng/bills/html/disclosure.htm
