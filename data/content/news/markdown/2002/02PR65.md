The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) releases today the Consultation Conclusions on the draft Securities and Futures (Information) Rules. 

On 19 March 2002, the SFC issued a consultation paper to solicit comments on the Rules.

In general, the Rules prescribe the information required to be submitted to the SFC under section 110(1) of the Securities and Futures Ordinance (No. 5 of 2002) by an issuer of certain certificates of deposit or commercial paper issues. Such information covers only the basic terms and conditions of the issues.

During the one-month consultation period, two submissions were received. One submission supported the Rules while the other made some suggestions (as discussed in the Consultation Conclusions). After studying the comments received, the SFC considers that no changes need to be made to the Rules.

The Rules will become effective on the day appointed for the commencement of Part IV of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

Copies of the Consultation Conclusions are available at the SFC's office and on the SFC's website at www.hksfc.org.hk.
