The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today released for public consultation the draft Securities and Futures (Keeping of Records) Rules (draft Rules). The rules are to be made under the Securities and Futures Bill (Bill) when the Bill is enacted.

Unlike the Securities Ordinance and the Commodities Trading Ordinance, the Bill does not contain any detailed record keeping requirements for intermediaries and their associated entities. It merely provides the SFC with the necessary rule-making power under clause 147 to prescribe requirements in the subsidiary legislation. This approach is consistent with modern securities legislation in order that regulators can respond efficiently to changing market conditions and practices.

While there are controls already built into the legislative system whereby any rules made by the SFC must be subject to negative vetting by the Legislative Council, the SFC now releases the draft Rules for public consultation.

In essence, the draft Rules require intermediaries and their associated entities to keep records containing sufficient details to explain their business activities and operations and account for their client assets.

In the formulation of these draft Rules, the SFC has already consulted market participants and incorporated their comments into the draft Rules, where appropriate.

The SFC has used the FinNet communication network to send copies of the consultation document to all intermediaries. In addition, copies of the consultation document are available free of charge at the SFC's office and can also be downloaded from the SFC's Internet website at [http://www.hksfc.org.hk](/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/consultation/doc?refNo=02CP5).
