The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has today published for public consultation a draft of the Securities and Futures (Levy) Order ("the draft Order") and the Securities and Futures (Levy) Rules ("the draft Rules") which it is proposed should be made by the Chief Executive in Council under section 394(1) and (5), respectively, of the Securities and Futures Ordinance (No. 5 of 2002) when it commences. 

Section 394(1) of the Ordinance empowers the Chief Executive in Council to specify, by Order, the rates of levy payable on sales and purchases of securities and futures contracts. Section 394(5) of the Ordinance empowers the Chief Executive in Council to make Rules for the payment of such levies and charges for any late payments, and for matters concerning the keeping, examination and audit of the accounts of the exchange companies relating to the collection and payment to the SFC of such levies.

The draft Order has been prepared having regard to the existing Securities and Futures Commission (Levy)(Securities) and (Levy)(Futures Contracts) Orders, which are made, respectively, under section 52(1) and (2) of the Securities and Futures Commission Ordinance (Cap. 24). The draft Rules have been prepared having regard to the existing Securities and Futures Commission (Levy) Rules, which are made under section 52(8) of the that Ordinance. No new policy changes have been incorporated in the draft Order and Rules, which are intended simply to replicate the existing provisions.

The SFC welcomes comments from industry practitioners and members of the investing public on the draft Order and the draft Rules.
