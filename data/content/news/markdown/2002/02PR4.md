The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today announced that it had suspended the registration of Mr Wong Chung Kun (Wong), a registered dealer's representative, under section 56 (2) of the Securities Ordinance for a period of 1 month.

The SFC's action stemmed from an inquiry into the conduct of Wong which revealed that during the period May 1999 to June 2001, when Wong was employed by Yuanta Brokerage Company Limited and later Core Pacific-Yamaichi Brokerage Company Limited, Wong obtained commission payments from his employer to which he was not entitled by conducting trading through two accounts that, unknown to his employer, he had an interest in. In so doing, Wong also breached the staff dealing policies of his employer and prevented it from monitoring his trading activities.

The SFC found that Wong's fitness and properness have been impugned because his ability to act as a securities dealer's representative fairly and honestly has been called into question.

An SFC spokesman said: "Wong deceived his employer to benefit himself. Deceit is unacceptable conduct for a registered person because it contravenes the most basic principles of the Code of Conduct. A suspension is therefore the appropriate penalty."
