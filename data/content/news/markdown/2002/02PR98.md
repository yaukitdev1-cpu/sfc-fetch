The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today releases the Consultation Conclusions on the draft Securities and Futures (Professional Investor) Rules.

The Rules were put out for a one-month public consultation on 1 February 2002 and they proposed expanding the definition of "professional investor" in the Securities and Futures Ordinance to include also the following classes of persons:

- trustee companies having been entrusted with total assets of not less than HK$40 million;
- high net worth individuals having a portfolio of not less than HK$16 million; and
corporations or partnerships having either a portfolio of not less than HK$16 million or total assets of not less than HK$40 million.The proposed expanded definition of "professional investor" however will only apply to sections 103, 174 and 175 of the Ordinance. In brief, clause 103 prohibits the issuance of unauthorized advertisements, invitations or documents relating to investments. Clause 174 essentially prohibits cold calling and clause 175 generally prohibits the offer of securities not accompanied by an offering document containing relevant information. These prohibitions imposed upon intermediaries can be dispensed with in the case of "professional investors". The reasoning is that these persons should be sufficiently sophisticated that certain investor protection requirements imposed upon intermediaries could be disapplied. Releasing intermediaries from compliance with such requirements when dealing with such persons will facilitate the provision of investment services to these clients.

A total of 10 submissions were received from industry practitioners, legal professionals and other interested parties. All the submissions with the exception of two, where consent for publication had not been obtained, have been published on the SFC's website at www.hksfc.org.hk.

In response to the feedback, the SFC has decided, among others, to take the following approach:

· reducing the portfolio threshold for individuals, corporations or partnerships (to become professional investors) to HK$8 million, in line with that in the Code of Conduct;
· including as professional investors, corporations which act solely as investment holding companies and are wholly-owned by individuals who are professional investors in their own right;
 · clarifying that firms can rely on several statements issued by different custodians in aggregating their clients' portfolio values;
· extending the definition of custodians to also include authorized institutions, licensed corporations and their overseas equivalents provided their businesses involve holding client assets; and
· aligning the Code of Conduct definition for professional investors with that of the legislation.

The Consultation Conclusions provides an analysis of the submissions and explains the SFC's perspectives.

The Rules will become effective on the day appointed for the commencement of Part V of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

Copies of the Consultation Conclusions are available at the SFC's office and on the SFC's website and the FinNet communication network.
