The Securities and Futures Commission today publishes the August issue of SFC Alert, the SFC's monthly newsletter for both investors and intermediaries.

This issue provides some useful tips to intermediaries. The cover story *Watch out for scams* highlights a recurring scam performed by fraudsters who pose as walk-in clients. The story urges stockbrokers to strengthen their internal control systems in order to protect themselves against such fraudulent activities.

For investors, *A lever pulls two ways* featured in a new "Investor Focus" column explains the characteristics of equity-linked instruments (ELIs). It advises investors of the risks and factors to consider when investing in listed ELIs.

"Investor Focus" will become a regular column of the SFC Alert, providing practical advice to investors on how to invest wisely and to protect their own interests.

Meanwhile, *Investor advice over multiple channels* informs the public of the availability of the entire series of the "Invest with Your Heads Up" investor video drama on the SFC-operated eIRC (the Electronic Investor Resources Centre).

That the SFC will not hesitate to crack down on market irregularities is the theme of *Serious sentencing safeguards market integrity* and *No hiding place*. The successful prosecution of two persons in the Gay Giano market manipulation case and the sanctions imposed on two SFC registrants who have committed impropriety, as reported in the two articles, demonstrate the SFC's commitment to ensuring the integrity of the Hong Kong market.

Turning in a good report covers the latest SFC quarterly report, which shows that the Commission has kept its deficit in check.

As usual, *SFO updates* briefs readers on the latest progress of the consultation exercise relating to the subsidiary legislation of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

The August issue of SFC Alert is the 11th issue of the newsletter, which was first published in October 2001.

Copies of SFC Alert are available at the SFC's office. This latest issue and all previous issues are also available on the Commission's website at [www.hksfc.org.hk](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/newsletter/aug(eng)a4.pdf%20%20).
