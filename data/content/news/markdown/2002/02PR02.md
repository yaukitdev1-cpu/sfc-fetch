The SFC announced today that under the Securities (Disclosure of Interests) Ordinance (SDIO), it had successfully prosecuted {X} of Wah Fu International Holdings Limited* (Wah Fu).

{X} was appointed director of Wah Fu on 1 February 2001 and on that day {X} had interests in 169,549,439 shares of Wah Fu.

Pursuant to S. 28 of SDIO, {X} should have notified the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited (SEHK) of his interests in Wah Fu shares on or before 7 February 2001. But the SEHK only received {X}'s written notifications on 20 March 2001, a delay of 35 days.

Ms Polly Lo, a Magistrate at Western Magistracy, fined {X} $5,000 and ordered him to pay costs of $9,432 to the SFC.

* Renamed Quam Limited on 14 November 2001.
