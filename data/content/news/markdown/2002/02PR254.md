The SFC has published new licensing related guidelines to facilitate entry to the securities and futures industry.

In April 2001, the SFC introduced the competence requirements with the objective of ensuring that licensed persons are adequately equipped to carry out their functions and are aware of their legal responsibilities.  Applicants seeking to be licensed by the SFC may demonstrate competence by obtaining certain recognised industry qualification and by passing a local regulatory framework examination.

The SFC believes that the requirements may be modified to allow greater flexibility for new entrants without compromising investor protection.  To this end, the SFC has today published the Guidelines for Exemptions from the Recognised Industry Qualification and Local Regulatory Framework Paper Requirements (“the Guidelines”).

The Guidelines set out the circumstances under which an applicant may be considered for licensing without the recognised industry qualification or taking the applicable local regulatory framework paper.  Factors that will be taken into consideration include the applicant’s level of academic achievement, years of relevant work experience, scope of responsibilities, and whether there is sufficient compliance support in place.

Requirements for representatives seeking additional licence of different product categories are relaxed.  Previously they have to take the applicable regulatory framework paper but may now apply for exemption under the Guidelines.

However, all individual applicants seeking exemption from the local regulatory framework paper have to fulfill, among other things, additional Continuous Professional Training hours in the specific area.

For details, please refer to the Guidelines which can be found in the sub-section of “Frequently Asked Questions on Competence” under the “Intermediaries, Licensing and Investment Products – Licensing Related Matters” section of the SFC website at www.hksfc.org.hk.
