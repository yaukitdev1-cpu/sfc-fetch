The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has today published for public consultation a draft of the Securities and Futures (Leveraged Foreign Exchange Trading - Arbitration) Rules ("the draft Rules") which the Securities and Futures Commission proposes to make under section 118(2) of the Securities and Futures Ordinance (No. 5of 2002) ("the Ordinance") when it commences. 

Part V of the Ordinance provides for the licensing and registration of individuals and corporations who carry on activities regulated by the Commission ("regulated activities"), including trading in leveraged foreign exchange contracts. It imposes a general prohibition on carrying on a business in a regulated activity unless licensed under section 116 or 117 or registered under section 119. The Commission may make a license granted under section 116 or 117 subject to such reasonable conditions that the Commission may impose.

Section 118 of the Ordinance stipulates conditions to which a license for certain regulated activities is subject. Section 118(1)(b) provides that it is condition of a license for carrying on trading in leveraged foreign exchange contracts that a client may have any dispute regarding or touching upon any matter concerning such trading settled by arbitration in accordance with rules made under section 118(2).

The draft has been prepared having regard to the existing Leveraged Foreign Exchange Trading (Arbitration) Rules under the Leveraged Foreign Exchange Trading Ordinance (Cap. 451). No new policy changes have been incorporated into the draft Rules which are intended to largely replicate the requirements of the existing provisions. However, the draft Rules have been modernized and made more user-friendly.
