Staff training by securities and futures licensees has become easier with the removal of a notification requirement.

Today, the Securities and Futures Commission is pleased to announce the removal of the requirement with immediate effect. The SFC hopes this may alleviate the administrative burden of the industry.

Since the introduction of Continuous Professional Training (CPT) in 2001, corporate licensees had been required to notify the SFC of their internal training sessions and submit the relevant training materials at least five working days in advance. This was designed to ensure a high quality of training. After a year of monitoring, the SFC is satisfied with the standard of training and that the requirement can be removed without compromising the standard.

Nevertheless, corporate licensees should continue to keep proper training and attendance records for each training session, and report status of CPT compliance in their Annual Returns to the SFC.

The SFC may also request for training information in its annual Business Activities Survey, or at any other appropriate time.

Further information about the CPT may be found in the "Frequently Asked Questions on CPT" posted on the SFC website at [www.hksfc.org.hk](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/newsletter/aug(eng)a4.pdf).
