The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announces today that it has publicly reprimanded {X}, a registered dealer's representative under the Securities Ordinance (SO). During the material time, {X} was an account executive of HT Securities Limited (HT) responsible for taking orders from clients. 

The reprimand stemmed from an inquiry in which the SFC found that {X} had, on 1 and 2 March 2001, conducted personal trades in one of his client's account at HT. These personal trades incurred losses of around HK$200,000 which {X} was unable to settle. HT instituted legal proceedings seeking to recover the losses from that client. {X}'s personal trades via one of his client's account not only seriously jeopardised the interests of his client and HT, but also gave rise to a problem of conflict of interests, which every registered person should try his/her best to avoid.

The SFC concluded that {X}'s conduct called into question his fitness and properness to be a dealer's representative under the SO and has decided to reprimand him. In reaching this decision the SFC has taken into account the following factors:-

- {X} has effectively been out of the industry for almost four months;
- {X} undertakes to the SFC to repay HT such losses incurred from his personal trades, and
- in the event HT's client loses in the litigation and is ordered to compensate HT, {X} undertakes to compensate that client in return.
