The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today releases the Consultation Conclusion on the draft Securities and Futures (Contract Notes, Statements of Account and Receipts) Rules as proposed in a consultation paper published on 28 September 2001. 

The Rules are intended primarily to:

- apply to all intermediaries (and where applicable, their associated entities) that enter into contracts with or on behalf of their clients in the conduct of the intermediaries' regulated activities for which they are licensed or registered, or receive and hold client assets;
- rationalize the existing requirements found in three different ordinances (i.e., sections 75 and 121Z of the Securities Ordinance, section 45A of the Commodities and Trading Ordinance, section 4 of the Leveraged Foreign Exchange Trading (Books, Contract Notes and Conduct of Business) Rules), the Code of Conduct for Persons Registered with the SFC, and the Fund Manager Code of Conduct;
- avoid duplicative documentation where two intermediaries are involved in a transaction with a client;
- allow for consolidation of contract notes with statements of account, where appropriate, in view of the single licence regime;
- permit reporting of average pricing in contract notes for dealings in securities and futures contracts; and
- relax existing disclosure requirements in relation to securities margin financing.
During the one-month consultation period, the SFC received a total of twenty-two responses from practitioners including fund management firms, international brokerage firms, legal firms, industry representative bodies, professional associations and industry regulators. One of the submissions consisted of a survey of the members of the commentator organization. Sessions were held with industry participants and their legal advisors during and after the consultation period to discuss their comments. The overall tone of the comments was positive. Commentators generally welcomed the proposed Rules. The submissions were carefully considered and several revisions to the original draft Rules were considered appropriate. 

The Consultation Conclusion provides an analysis of the submissions and explains the Commission's perspectives.

The Rules are subject to negative vetting by the Legislative Council and will become effective on the day appointed for the commencement of Part VI of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

Copies of the Consultation Conclusion are available at the SFC's office, on the SFC's website (www.hksfc.org.hk) and the FinNet communication network.
