The Securities and Futures Commission is bringing forward the effective date of new measures to facilitate regulated short selling.

The SFC today gazettes the Securities (Miscellaneous)(Amendment) Rules 2002 which introduce new exemptions for sections 80 and 80B of the Securities Ordinance (Note 1). The Rules, if passed by the Legislative Council, will become effective on 15 November 2002.

Section 80 prohibits uncovered short selling. Exemptions have been given to certain categories of market makers. The Rules extend the current exemptions from section 80 to all categories of market makers registered with the Stock Exchange and the Futures Exchange in the performance of market making obligations and hedging of market making positions. The exemptions will make it easier for market makers to perform their functions, which will in turn help provide liquidity to the markets.

Section 80B sets out reporting and record keeping requirements for covered short selling transactions. The Rules introduce alternative means for sellers placing short selling orders pursuant to a stock borrowing and lending agreement to comply with section 80B, to enable timely execution of transactions.

These exemptions are identical to those to be made under the Securities and Futures Ordinance which have been released for public consultation and considered by the relevant Legislative Council Subcommittee in July 2002 (Note 2).

The SFC is committed to the further development of the Hong Kong markets and has worked closely with the Government and Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited to expedite the implementation of the exemptions. The Rules made under the existing legislation are brought into operation prior to the commencement of the Securities and Futures Ordinance so that industry participants can benefit from the exemptions as soon as possible.


To complement the commencement of the Rules, the SFC will issue a revised Guidance Note on Short Selling Reporting and Stock Lending Record Keeping Requirements later this year. The Guidance Note will explain the SFC's position and expectations in respect of the Rules.
