The Securities and Futures Commission welcomes the publication today of the [Report of the Panel of Inquiry on the Penny Stocks](http://www.info.gov.hk/info/pennystock-e.htm) Incident.

The SFC fully supports the recommendations of the Report. The Report has vindicated the conduct of the SFC in the incident.

The SFC will work closely with the Administration, Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited, and other relevant parties on efforts to improve the quality of the securities market in Hong Kong.

Mr Andrew Sheng, Chairman of the SFC, said: "I would like to thank the Panel for having given the SFC the opportunity to explain the issues and present the facts on the incident."

"The SFC fully agrees with the Panel's conclusions and recommendations that we must press ahead with reform measures and look forward," he said. "In doing so, we will make sure that the market and the public are engaged as early as possible to identify the key issues and build consensus."

Mr Sheng said: "The Panel's Report has clearly set out the facts. The SFC and I personally feel deeply about the incident and its effects on the public. We will be on guard to avoid the recurrence of similar incidents in the future."
