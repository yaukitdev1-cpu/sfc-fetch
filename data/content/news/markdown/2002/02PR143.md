The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today releases for public consultation the draft Securities and Futures (Intermediary Information) Rules. The rules are to be made under section 397 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO) when it comes into effect.

Under the existing Securities Ordinance and Commodities Trading Ordinance, licensing application forms constitute part of the subsidiary legislation. In addition, the current Securities and Futures Commission Ordinance also prescribes in subsidiary legislation a specific form for filing of annual returns by licensed persons. This arrangement has proved to be inflexible and is not consistent with modern securities legislation.

The SFO dispenses with this arrangement. Instead, it provides for the SFC to make Rules prescribing the information that is to be provided in making licensing related applications and filing annual returns.

The draft Rules set out:

- the information the SFC may ask from a person making an application under Part V of the SFO, which pertain to licensing matters (the information will be requested in forms specified by the SFC);
- the changes that are required to be notified by licensed persons, their substantial shareholders and registered institutions;
- the information that is required to be contained in annual returns to be submitted by licensed persons (again, the information will be requested in forms specified by the SFC); and
- the particulars to be entered in the public register and issues relating to the correction of errors in the register.
