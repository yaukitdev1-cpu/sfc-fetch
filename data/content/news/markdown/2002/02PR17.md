The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announces today that it has suspended the registration of Mr Wong Ming Leuk (Wong), a securities dealer's representative, for a period of 12 months pursuant to section 56(2) of the Securities Ordinance (SO).

The SFC's action stemmed from an inquiry into Wong's conduct as a securities dealer's representative in which the SFC found that Wong:

- failed to act in the best interest of five of his clients by allowing a third party to conduct personal trades in the accounts of those clients;
- failed to take reasonable steps to ascertain the investment background of his clients and explain to them the contents of the client agreements;
signed account opening documentation without properly witnessing the clients' signatures; and
 breached internal control guidelines of his former employer. Wong has since left the industry.

The SFC found that Wong was guilty of misconduct and his fitness and properness to remain registered were called into question. The SFC has therefore decided to suspend Wong's registration.

An SFC spokesman reminds registrants that they should strictly adhere to the *Know Your Client Rule*. Registrants should not allow third parties to operate their clients' accounts unless written authorisation is obtained from the clients. Such conduct constitutes a serious breach and registrants guilty of such conduct will be penalised by the SFC.
