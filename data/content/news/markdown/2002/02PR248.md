The SFC published on its website today a set of Frequently Asked Questions to help the fund industry understand our regulatory requirements. To further facilitate compliance, the SFC has also published our standardised forms to assist fund managers in reporting ongoing compliance matters to the SFC.

FAQs

Since September this year, the SFC has been streamlining its fund authorisation procedures. Its Investment Products Department has actively engaged the fund industry and the investing public in open dialogue. 

In line with this, the FAQs are part of the SFC’s on-going efforts to improve communications with market participants and increase the transparency of its fund authorisation work.

The FAQs introduce the fund authorisation procedures and criteria, and on-going compliance issues under the Code on Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds. They aim to help existing participants, potential fund applicants, and investors understand the Code’s requirements for funds seeking SFC authorisation.

The FAQs have been posted on the SFC website. They will be updated regularly and interested parties are encouraged to check the website often for new information.

Standardised compliance forms

At the same time, standardised on-going compliance forms on matters such as mergers of funds or pricing errors are now available on the SFC website.  These standardised forms should help fund managers prepare their submission of  information to the SFC when necessary. 

Ms Tina So, Director of Investment Products, said: “We hope that market participants will use the FAQs as the first port of call to obtain a basic understanding of our procedures and requirements.  The FAQs are not designed to be exhaustive, so prospective fund applicants are encouraged to contact us for further information and clarification regarding their application.”

The FAQs and compliance forms are available on the SFC website at www.hksfc.org.hk, under the section “Bills, Legislation and Codes – Regulatory Handbook – Volume 2, Part C”.
