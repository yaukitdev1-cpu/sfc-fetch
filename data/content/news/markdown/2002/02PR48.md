The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today publishes a derivation table on its website to facilitate market participants' understanding of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

The Ordinance, enacted on 13 March 2002, is gazetted by the Government today. It consolidates and modernises 10 existing ordinances regulating the securities and futures markets.

The derivation table indicates where from the current legislation the various sections of the new Ordinance have been derived. The numbers of the equivalent clauses of the Blue Bill (including clauses added during the Committee Stage of Legislative Council deliberations) are also given in the table.

The derivation table is available in the "What's New" section of the SFC website at [www.hksfc.org.hk](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/whatsnew/whatsnew.html%20%20). (Chinese version will be available soon.)

The SFC website also provides a link to the text of the Ordinance on the relevant Government website.
