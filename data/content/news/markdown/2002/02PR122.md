The Securities and Futures Commission is pleased to have received a report on the review of remuneration of senior executives in statutory and other bodies, which was conducted by the Hay Group Limited.

The SFC welcomes the recommendations in the report and will study them carefully. The Board of Directors of the SFC and the Remuneration Committee will be invited to consider the recommendations.

An SFC spokesman said the existing remuneration practices of the SFC were generally in line with the recommendations.

The pay levels of the staff under review - the Chairman, the Executive Directors (4) and Senior Directors (8) - are all in line with the respective recommended ranges of remuneration.

"As a public body, the SFC considers that accountability is vital to maintaining the confidence of investors and other market participants in the SFC," he said. "The SFC has fully co-operated in the review and will consider the recommendations and make improvements where appropriate."

The Commission determines the pay levels and pay awards of the staff (with the exception of the Executive Directors) on the advice of the Remuneration Committee.

The Remuneration Committee is chaired by a Non-Executive Director (presently Mr Daniel R Fung). It reviews proposals on compensation structure and reports on pay levels and pay trends, and recommends any necessary adjustments.

The pay packages of Executive Directors, including the Chairman, are determined by the HKSAR Government.

The SFC has since last year exercised stringent control in personnel costs and other expenditures. In addition to hiring and wage freeze, the payment of a performance-based discretionary "variable pay" to staff was suspended in 2001-2002.
