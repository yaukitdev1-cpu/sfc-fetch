The SFC today launches a publicity campaign through TV and radio Announcement of Public Interests (API) to remind the public to make investment decisions based on fact, and not rumours.

The TV API, to be broadcasted in English, Cantonese and Putonghua, uses the concept of "human ears" to convey the message "*When you Invest, Bank on Facts, not Rumour*s".

Airing begins today, 25 February, on all major Hong Kong television stations, as well as the Chinese and InfoNews channels of Phoenix Satellite TV.

Using similar creative themes, the radio API urges investors to "do their homework" before making investment decisions. The radio API also starts airing today on all major Hong Kong radio stations in both Cantonese and Putonghua.

As part of the SFC's initiatives to encourage investors to learn more about their investments and improve their market knowledge, the SFC also announces the launch of its revamped Electronic Investor Resources Centre (eIRC), at www.hkeirc.org.

With the vast array of educational information and new interactive features, the re-designed eIRC strives to make learning an enjoyable and interesting experience.

Initially launched in June 2000, the eIRC is a one-stop website which provide investors with investment and education information, and helps them carry out research on their investments.

The eIRC has five main sections:

**Game Zone** - Play and learn. Improve financial literacy by answering quizzes, matching market jargons, and reading company announcements, fund prospectuses and transaction documents. Investor can also take an investment journey to learn to make informed investment decisions.

**Studio** - Watch SFC's TV Announcements of Public Interests (APIs) or tune in to the radio segments on subjects of investor interests.

**Monthly Focus** - Receive news through email about topical issues in the market, investor alert update as well as latest regulatory information.

**eLibrary** - Carry out research through links to over 550 websites of regulators, market institutions and market information, with special focus on the Hong Kong and China markets.

**Features** - Find key facts on areas of special focus and topical interest.

"Visiting the eIRC is a cost effective and accessible way for investors to get hold of investor education material. We hope that more and more investors would take advantage of the eIRC to educate and equip themselves," a SFC spokesman said.
