The Securities and Futures Commission today issues the following decision on behalf of the Takeovers and Mergers Panel.

"On 24 June 2002 the Takeovers Panel ruled that Mr Chee Ying Cheung and Asia Time Investments Limited were not acting in concert in relation to the acquisition of control of CMB. The full written decision of the Panel was issued today.

The background to this matter is that on 20 April 2002, Yu Ming Investment Management Limited on behalf of Asia Time (a newly-created private company jointly owned by Mr Peter Fung Yiu Fai and Mr Tony Fung Wing Cheung), approached CMB with a voluntary conditional cash offer for its shares.

On 30 May 2002, CMB complained to the Executive that Mr Chee (who with his wife held around 9.8% shareholding in CMB) and Asia Time were acting in concert. It followed, CMB argued, that the offer price should be increased to match the highest price paid for purchases of CMB shares in the market by Mr Chee in accordance with Rule 24.1 of the Takeovers Code.

The Executive referred this matter to the Panel for its ruling as it considered there were particularly novel, important and difficult points at issue in this case.

On the basis of the evidence before it the Panel ruled that there was no concert party relationship between Mr Chee and Asia Time as the Panel was not persuaded that an agreement or understanding existed between Mr Chee and Yu Ming or Asia Time actively to co-operate to obtain control of CMB within the meaning of the Takeovers Code. In view of this, the Panel took the view that it was not necessary for it to consider the implications under the Takeovers Code of any purchases by Mr Chee."
