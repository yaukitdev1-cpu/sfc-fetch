The Securities and Futures Commission today publishes for public consultation a draft of the Securities and Futures (Amendment of Schedule 8) Order. The SFC intends to recommend to the Chief Executive in Council to make the draft Order under section 234 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance (No. 5 of 2002) when it commences. 

Under section 217 of the Ordinance, a person aggrieved by a "specified decision" (i.e. decisions listed in Part 2 of Schedule 8 to the Ordinance) may apply to the Securities and Futures Appeals Tribunal (SFAT) for a review of the decision. The SFAT is an independent, full-time body chaired by a judge, which may review, upon application, the merits of the decision afresh. The purpose of the draft Order is to add to the list certain decisions so that they will become "specified decisions" and thus subject to review by the SFAT.

Section 232(2) of the Ordinance sets out the time at which "specified decisions", except those listed in Division 5 of Part 3 of Schedule 8, are to take effect. It is also proposed that the draft Order will add to that list certain "specified decisions" which should take effect immediately for the better protection of the interest of the investing public.
