A new section dedicated to the recently enacted Securities and Futures Ordinance has been created on the website of the Securities and Futures Commission. 

With just a click on an icon, the public can access a vast amount of essential information on this significant piece of legislation.

The new section offers comprehensive resources relating to the Securities and Futures Ordinance such as:

- the latest news and updates on the implementation of the Ordinance, including when available news of industry education before its commencement;
- a link to the full text of the Ordinance;
- various consultation and conclusions papers on the subsidiary legislation and progress of consultations;
- a derivation table which indicates from where in the current legislation the various sections of the Ordinance have been derived; and
- related press releases and circulars.
More features, including an automatic update alert service, will be introduced later.

The SFC believes that the new section, with its "one-stop shop" structure, will enhance the public's understanding of the Ordinance.

An SFC spokesman said: "We welcome suggestions to improve this section and on any other matters which may help the industry comply with the Ordinance and related rules and regulations when they become effective."

To access the section, click on this shortcut [](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/legislation/securities/securities.html%20%20)on the SFC homepage ([www.hksfc.org.hk](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/legislation/securities/securities.html%20%20)) or click the section of "[Bills, Legislation & Codes](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/legislation/securities/securities.html%20%20)".

The Securities and Futures Ordinance was enacted on 13 March and gazetted on 28 March. It consolidates and modernises 10 existing ordinances regulating the securities and futures markets which were written over the last 25 years. The SFC is consulting the public on a considerable body of subsidiary legislation. It is hoped that the new regulatory regime can become effective by the end of this year, when all the necessary subsidiary legislation is in place.
