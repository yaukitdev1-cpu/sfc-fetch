The Securities and Futures Commission will broadcast Episode 11 of "Invest with Your Heads Up", entitled "Borrowed but Never Returned" on RoadShow Media on 24 July 2002 (Wednesday). 

In this episode, Mrs Wong (played by Tsang Wai Wan) is asked by her broker, Mr Zhou (played by Mak Tsz Wan), to allow him to conduct personal trades in her account because he is not allowed to trade stocks at the brokerage that he works for. Mr Zhou offers to share with her 20 per cent of the profits made and takes full responsibility for any losses incurred. Mrs Wong finds the arrangement irresistible and agrees to it.

A month later, Mrs Wong is shocked to know that Mr Zhou has disappeared and her account is left with a sizeable debit balance.

The key messages for retail investors are:

- Never "lend your account" to your broker, i.e. to allow your broker to conduct his personal trades in your account. As the account holder, you are fully responsible for any liabilities incurred in the account.
- Be careful if your broker promises you quick and easy profits, or agrees to share in any losses in your account.
- If you spot any unauthorized trades in your account, report to the management of the brokerage and the SFC immediately.
"Invest with Your Heads Up" is a new 12-episode investor video series produced by the SFC, which aims to explain stock trading and settlement procedures, as well as measures investors should take to protect their own interests when dealing in stocks. 

Airing began on 15 May 2002 on RoadShow Media on 2,400 buses of the KMB and Citybus and 200 public light buses for 12 consecutive Wednesdays. After broadcast, each episode will be made available on the SFC-operated Electronic Investor Resources Centre, located at www.hkeirc.org.
