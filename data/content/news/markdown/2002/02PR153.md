Chairman of the Securities and Futures Commission, Mr. Andrew Sheng, today made the following statements at a press conference:

1. The Government, the SFC and Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited (HKEx) consider that an effective delisting mechanism is important for promoting good corporate governance, protecting investors and safeguarding Hong Kong's position as an international financial centre.

2. We wish to stress that the delisting proposals announced by HKEx last Thursday are at the consultation stage, not the decision stage. We are pleased that HKEx is clarifying any misunderstandings and refining the consultation proposals. As in the past, the SFC will work closely with HKEx on this matter.

3. The SFC wishes to emphasise that it has been closely monitoring the financial positions of brokers. As of close of business last Friday, there was no indication that brokers had been materially affected. Furthermore, there was no indication that banks had called in loans to brokers.

4. The SFC will watch very closely the market behaviour. We will be alert if anyone tries to take advantage of the situation to manipulate the market.

5. Investors are urged to exercise caution in times of market volatility.
