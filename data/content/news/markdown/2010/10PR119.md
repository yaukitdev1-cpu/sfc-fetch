The Securities and Futures Commission’s (SFC) package of measures to strengthen the regulation of the sale of investment products has been well-received, with industry participants embracing them positively, said Mr Martin Wheatley, the Chief Executive Officer of the SFC (Note 1).

Speaking today at the fourth annual conference of the Hong Kong Investment Funds Association, Mr Wheatley reiterated that in shaping new regulations, the SFC will continue to adopt a balanced approach and engage various stakeholders through consultation and active communication.

“Good regulation needs to balance investor protection and market development, and implementing these regulations requires efforts from both market participants and regulators,” he said.

Mr Wheatley observed some issuers voluntarily began producing Key Facts Statement (KFS) -- a summary of a product’s key features and risks -- even before the requirement of KFS became mandatory in June.

In today’s speech, Mr Wheatley also pointed out that it is crucial for product issuers to treat investors fairly and not lose sight of investors’ needs.

“To have a sustainable market, there must be a business case for both sides of the bargain. In that sense, a product must be ‘fair’. A product’s design and payout that is skewed in favour of one side will not stand the test of time,” he said.

In this light, he urged issuers contemplating to launch investment products denominated in renminbi (RMB) to ensure that the products are capable of generating genuinely RMB-related returns to investors.

Mr Wheatley’s [speech](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/speeches/10/Martin_20101004.pdf) is available on the SFC’s website ([www.sfc.hk](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/index.html)).
