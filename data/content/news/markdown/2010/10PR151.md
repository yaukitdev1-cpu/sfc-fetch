The Takeovers and Mergers Panel (Panel) has ruled that there was insufficient evidence to suggest that Merdeka Commodities Limited (MCL) and its sole shareholder, Mr Lai Wing Hung, were acting in concert with CCT Telecom Holdings Limited and its wholly-owned subsidiary, Manistar Enterprises Limited (Manistar), in an acquisition by MCL of 13.14% of the voting rights in Merdeka Resources Holdings Limited (MRH) from Manistar (Notes 1 & 2). The Panel also considered that, in light of the circumstances of the case, any presumption that the parties are acting in concert under the Takeovers Code as a result of the acquisition is rebutted. Accordingly, a mandatory general offer obligation had not been triggered as a result of the acquisition.

On 25 November 2010, the Takeovers Executive received an application for a rebuttal of the presumption that the parties were acting in concert as a result of the acquisition. The matter was referred to the Panel as there were particularly novel, important or difficult points at issue. The Panel met on 9 December 2010 to consider the referral.

A copy of the [Panel’s decision](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/cfd/mergers/panel/014g%20-%20Panel%20Decision-Merdeka%20(Final).pdf) can be found in the “Prospectuses, Takeovers & Mergers” – “Takeovers and Mergers” – “Panel and Executive Decisions / Statements” section of the Securities and Futures Commission’s (SFC) website at [www.sfc.hk](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/index.html).
