A clear majority of industry participants and industry-related parties polled in a recent survey has expressed satisfaction with the work of the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC).

When polled by The Nielsen Company in the SFC-commissioned Industry Participants Survey 2009/10, 76% of the 586 randomly sampled respondents indicated they were either “satisfied” or “very satisfied” with the regulator’s overall work. This was the first in a cohesive and inter-related series of staggered surveys targeting different stakeholder groups to gain insights for corporate planning.

In addition, 73% of respondents expressed satisfaction with both the SFC’s efficiency and staff quality. These aspects were deemed respectively to be the primary and secondary driver of satisfaction. Clarity of communication was the third aspect in the survey’s “satisfaction framework.” Each aspect was further defined by attributes so that respondents could rate their level of satisfaction for each attribute on a scale of one to five (See appendices overleaf).

“The survey is a way for us to identify possible gaps between the industry’s experience with us versus their expectations and whether such gaps can be closed realistically,” said the SFC’s Chief Executive Officer Mr Martin Wheatley. “While we are encouraged to see a high approval rate for our work, we will not be complacent. Going forward, we shall use the findings as indicators to prioritise our work and allocate our resources towards upgrading our work.”

To obtain findings that are focused and actionable, only those who had dealt with the SFC in their normal course of business were sampled. They included: licensed corporations regulated by the SFC, law firms and accounting firms serving the securities and futures industry, as well as listed companies. Out of a random sample of 2,581 decision makers and functional heads at such entities, 22.7% or 586 gave valid responses to questionnaires either on line or by phone.

A [report](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/surveys/10/survey_210610.pdf) of the key findings of the survey is available on the SFC website (www.sfc.hk).
