Hong Kong is well placed to benefit from the internationalisation of Renminbi (RMB) with its world-class capabilities and a talented and resourceful workforce, according to Mrs Alexa Lam, the Deputy Chief Executive Officer and Executive Director, Policy, China and Investment Products, of the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC).

Speaking today at the International Swaps and Derivatives Association Equity Derivatives Conference 2010, Mrs Lam said that Hong Kong can contribute to the introduction of RMB-denominated investment products, a role that has been recognised by the Central Government.

“We have clear support and encouragement to enter uncharted waters of RMB-related business, and we are able to do this with a new perspective,” said Mrs Lam.

“On the supply side, Hong Kong should look into widening the availability of RMB-denominated investment products. On the demand side, we need to work on allowing a broader group of investors to invest in these RMB-denominated products,” she added.

Mrs Lam noted that a gradual approach should be adopted in developing Hong Kong’s RMB-related businesses, and stressed that “we need to be mindful of the long-term objectives and specific needs of the underlying Mainland economy”.

She further pointed out the need for more RMB-denominated financial and investment products for risk hedging and value accretion purposes, whereas on the demand side, “it is only natural to expand the investor base to include institutions”.

Mrs Lam encouraged market participants to keep their focus on innovation, adding that the current regulatory reforms aim to achieve “a balance between investor protection and providing sufficient scope for the market to continue to develop”.
