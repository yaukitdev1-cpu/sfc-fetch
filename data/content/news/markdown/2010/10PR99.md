In its [Quarterly Report for April to June 2010](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/quartely/apr_jun_10.pdf) released today, the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) describes how it made headway to implement measures to enhance investor protection while scoring more success in enforcement actions.

During the quarter, a consolidated SFC handbook of revised product codes and a new code for unlisted structured investment products were introduced to enhance product transparency and to standardise disclosure requirements for retail investment products in different forms. In parallel, measures were introduced to enhance intermediary conduct and selling practices relating to the sale of retail investment products.

A mass-media campaign was launched in June to give investors a clear understanding of the new measures to enhance their protection.

In terms of enforcement actions, the report recounts successful prosecutions against market manipulation, resulting in penalties for offending intermediaries and investors alike. The report also describes the first instance the SFC sought a court order to exclude an entity from trading in the Hong Kong market.

During the quarter, the SFC authorised the first exchange-traded fund (ETF) managed by the Hong Kong asset management subsidiary of a Mainland insurance group, reaffirming Hong Kong’s position as the second-largest ETF market in Asia with 62 ETFs listed.

In line with efforts to promote public understanding of the SFC’s regulatory work, the first-ever television drama, SFC in Action was launched.

The report also describes the publication of the findings to a survey in which 76% of participating industry respondents indicated that they were either “satisfied” or “very satisfied” with the SFC’s work in terms of efficiency, staff quality and clarity of communication.

The Quarterly Report is available under “Speeches, Publications & Consultations” – “Publications” of the SFC website at www.sfc.hk.
