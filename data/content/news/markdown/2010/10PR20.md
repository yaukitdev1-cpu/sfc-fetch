The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) supports the measures proposed by the Financial Secretary, Mr John Tsang, to strengthen the competitiveness of Hong Kong’s financial services industry.

Extending the stamp duty concession to cover exchange traded funds (ETFs) that track indices comprising not more than 40 percent of Hong Kong stocks will help promote Hong Kong’s position as an international financial centre and the development of the ETF market.

“The SFC will continue to work closely with the government and other market participants to implement the initiatives to facilitate the growth of the asset management business in Hong Kong,” the SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, Mr Martin Wheatley said.

The SFC will also work together with the government on the consultation on proposals to establish an Investor Education Council and a Financial Dispute Resolution Centre, aiming at enhancing investor protection.
