As an initiative to step up investor education, the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has launched today [InvestEd Intelligence](http://www.invested.hk/invested/en/html/section/focus/sep10/sounding.html), a bimonthly e-newsletter with a diverse range of educational information about investing.

The new publication is an expanded platform for the SFC to convey messages of interest to investors. Topical market issues, product development information and regulatory updates are among topics to be covered regularly.

“InvestEd Intelligence reflects the SFC’s holistic and comprehensive approach to investor education,” says Mr Martin Wheatley, the SFC’s Chief Executive Officer. “Using this augmented channel, we believe that we can disseminate investor education messages more effectively.”

The inaugural issue uncovers risks intrinsic to products denominated in renminbi, a controlled currency, cautions investors against falling for online “investment tips” from possibly unlicensed advisers and describes how excess rights are allocated.

Adopting a more interactive approach, InvestEd Intelligence welcomes questions from readers and deploys easy navigation and shared tools to add fun to learning. A calendar further notifies the public of upcoming investor education activities, such as, the “Hard Facts about Investing” radio interviews and the “Know your Protection” video series.

InvestEd Intelligence is published at the [SFC’s InvestEd site](http://www.invested.hk/invested/en/html/section/index.html) on the 20th day of every odd month, or the day after, if the 20th falls on a Saturday or a public holiday.
