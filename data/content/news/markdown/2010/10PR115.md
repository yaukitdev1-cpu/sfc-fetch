To enhance the integrity and objectivity of pre-deal research reports, the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has begun today a two-month [public consultation](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/speeches/consult/consult.html) on proposals to expand the scope of the present requirements governing conflicts of interest for analysts so that not only research reports on listed securities but also those on initial public offerings (IPOs) are covered.

The current conflicts-of-interest requirements apply when analysts prepare research reports on listed securities (Note 1). The SFC believes that these requirements also should apply to IPO-related research reports. The SFC also proposes to promote equal access to information by requiring that only information included in the prospectus or listing document be disclosed to analysts.

"We believe that the proposed amendments will address concerns about inequality of information,” said Mr Martin Wheatley, the SFC's Chief Executive Officer. “If adopted, the amendments will help ensure material information about a company is not selectively disclosed to analysts prior to its listing.”

The SFC also proposes to apply these conflicts-of-interest requirements to analysts preparing research reports on real estate investment trusts that are applying for listing and those that are listed.

Until 30 November 2010, comments to the SFC can be submitted online via the SFC website, by e-mail to [cfdconsult@sfc.hk](mailto:%20cfdconsult@sfc.hk), by post or by fax 2810 5385.
