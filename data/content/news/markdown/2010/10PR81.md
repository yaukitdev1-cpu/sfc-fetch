The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the re-appointment by the Financial Secretary of Mrs Angelina Lee Pui Ling as Non-Executive Director for a term of two years from 1 August 2010 to 31 July 2012.

 “I look forward to continue working with Mrs Lee, whose insights and advices have greatly benefited the SFC over the past four years. I am certain that Mrs Lee will continue to contribute to the SFC and the Hong Kong financial market through her wealth of experience and expertise,” the SFC Chairman, Dr Eddy Fong said.

Mrs Lee was first appointed to the SFC as a Non-Executive Director on 1 August 2006.
