The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has today begun a four-week [consultation](/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/consultation/openFile?refNo=10CP3) to solicit public comments on proposals to amend the Codes on Takeovers and Mergers and Share Repurchases (Codes) so that certain routine announcements will no longer be required to be submitted to the Executive (Note 1) for comments prior to publication.

"The proposals are an example of our effort to reduce the cost and burden of compliance for the industry without compromising investor protection and market integrity," said the SFC's Chief Executive Officer Mr Martin Wheatley. "If adopted, they will help promote self-discipline among parties issuing announcements and market practitioners."

The proposals and miscellaneous amendments to the Codes are formulated in consultation with the Takeovers Panel.

Until 19 May 2010, comments to the SFC can be submitted online via the SFC website, by e-mail to [cfdconsult@sfc.hk](mailto:cfdconsult@sfc.hk), by post or by fax 2810 5385.
