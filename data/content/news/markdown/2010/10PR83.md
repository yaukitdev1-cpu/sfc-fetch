The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) launched today two plain-language publications to help investors better understand product Key Facts Statements (KFS) and post-sale cooling-off arrangement, two of the SFC’s latest measures to enhance investor protection (Notes 1 and 2).

“We believe the package of investor protection measures released in late May will strengthen the regulatory regime governing the sale of investment products. Investors are encouraged to familiarise themselves with the new measures and they will find these user-friendly publications great tools to understand two of the key measures,” said the SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, Mr Martin Wheatley.

“The two publications are part of our latest investor education initiatives to raise public awareness of the new investor protection measures,” added Mr Wheatley.

The package of investor education programmes, under the theme of “Know how the new rules protect you as an investor”, also includes articles published on the SFC’s investor portal, the InvestEd website ([www.InvestEd.hk](http://www.invested.hk/invested/en/html/section/index.html)). Other initiatives being planned are short Dr Wise radio segments, radio and TV interview programmes as well as short videos to be broadcast on multiple media channels.

The two publications launched today will be distributed at SFC investor outreach activities, public libraries and via intermediaries upon request. The [electronic versions](http://www.invested.hk/invested/en/html/section/publication/publication.html) are available on the InvestEd website.

End

Notes:

1. Product KFS is a summary of the key features and risks of investment products. Cooling-off arrangement is the requirement for issuers to provide a post-sale “unwind” right for applicable unlisted structured investment products. For details of the measures, please refer to SFC [press release](/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/news-and-announcements/news/doc?refNo=10PR58) dated 28 May 2010.
2. The product KFS and post-sale cooling-off arrangement for unlisted structured investment products with a term of more than one year have come into effect since 25 June 2010. Some other measures, such as restriction of offering gifts to promote a specific product, disclosure of monetary and non-monetary benefits and disclosure of sales-related information by product distributors, will be implemented in phases in the coming months.
