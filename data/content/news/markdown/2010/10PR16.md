The Securities and Futures Commission’s (SFC) Investment Triathlon concluded today with Mr Chan Che Wang beating seven other contestants to clinch the first prize after a hotly contested Final Challenge.

The financial knowledge quiz, in its third year of running, aims to help investors understand their rights and responsibilities and the risks of investing.

“There are growing concerns over the asset price bubbles as liquidity continues to flow into the emerging markets. Investors should exercise caution and regularly review their portfolio according to changing market conditions and their own risk tolerance level,” Dr Eddy Fong, Chairman of the SFC, said today at the prize presentation ceremony.

“It is important for investors to fully understand the underlying risks by raising the proper questions,” Dr Fong added.

This year’s quiz has attracted over 600 entries. After two rounds of competition, eight finalists competed at today’s Final Challenge (Note 1).

The champion Mr Chan Che Wang took home a cash prize of $30,000, with the first runner-up, Ms Tam Hoi Ying, and second runner-up, Ms Lai Yin Hing, receiving $10,000 and $5,000 respectively. The newly created $5,000 cash prize for best performing team was shared between Mr Chan Che Wang and Ms Lai Yin Hing (Note 2).

The SFC is grateful for the support of the prize sponsors, media partners (Note 3) and all the supporting organisations.

Cable TV – one of our media partners of the event – will air an edited version of the Final Challenge of the Investment Triathlon (Note 4).
