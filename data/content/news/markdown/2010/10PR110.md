The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has adhered to established internal procedures and complied with the operational guidelines in its decisions and actions generally, according to the Process Review Panel for the SFC (PRP).

The PRP annual report published today covers a comprehensive review of the SFC’s work in a number of areas. The report notes that the SFC has responded positively to the observations and recommendations made by the PRP.

The SFC will continue to support the review work of the PRP.
