The latest edition of the [Enforcement Reporter](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/enforcement/10/apr_10.pdf) published by the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) reports that the SFC successfully obtained further orders from the High Court to disqualify five company directors, marking the first time that directors were disqualified for failing to ensure timely disclosure of material information to shareholders.

For the first time, the High Court also directed a listed company to commence civil proceedings against three former directors to seek compensation for the loss and damage suffered as a result of their misconduct.

This issue further chronicles how the SFC continues to make full use of its civil powers. An urgent interim order was obtained from the High Court to freeze initial public offering (IPO) proceeds of up to $997 million. The action stemmed from the SFC’s concerns about the false or misleading disclosure in the related IPO prospectus. The SFC also directed that trading in the shares of the company be suspended.

This issue reports that more than 25,000 investors of Lehman Brothers(LB)-related products recovered more than $5.4 billion within 12 months, following agreements reached with 19 distributors and a non-distributor of LB Minibonds, and a resolution reached with two banks concerning their sale of certain LB-related structured products. These speedy outcomes are results of the top-down investigation approach adopted by the SFC.

The Reporter also gives an account of the disciplinary actions taken by the SFC following a recent determination by the Market Misconduct Tribunal.

The Enforcement Reporter is a newsletter that highlights key enforcement outcomes and issues. It is available on the SFC website under “Speeches, Publications & Consultations” – “Publications”.

The public are invited to subscribe to the Enforcement Reporter by registering for the Update Alert service on the SFC website. They will then be able to receive the newsletter via email.
