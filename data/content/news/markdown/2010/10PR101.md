When making an investment decision, investors should be rational and not be distracted by the incentive on offer.

In his latest column, “[No More Gifts to Take Your Mind off the Product,](http://www.invested.hk/invested/en/html/section/dr_wise/2010articles/gifts_aug10.html)” Dr Wise discusses the new measure of the ban on offer of gifts to promote specific investment products.

As one of the new measures introduced by the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) to enhance investor protection, intermediaries will be barred from using gifts to promote specific investment products from 4 September 2010. Without these promotions distracting them, investors could focus on a product’s features and risks and hence make an informed decision.

However, discounts on fees and charges to lower the cost of investment are still allowed. Gifts offered to promote an image, build client relationships and/or for purposes other than specific investment product promotion also may continue.

“Base your investment decisions on whether the transaction matches your investment objective and risk appetite, and if it is suitable for you in terms of your personal circumstances,” says Dr Wise. “Never make any commitment for the incentive on offer.”

Through his column, Dr Wise, a fictitious character created by the SFC, shares with the public his wisdom on investing and his thoughts on regulatory issues.

Starting September 2010, Dr Wise’s article will be published once in two months under InvestEd Intelligence, a new bimonthly e-newsletter of the SFC’s InvestEd website, in addition to the “Dr Wise” section of the [InvestEd website](http://www.invested.hk/invested/en/html/section/index.html). Investors are welcome to send their feedback and questions to Dr Wise by email to [InvestEd@sfc.hk](mailto:InvestEd@sfc.hk).
