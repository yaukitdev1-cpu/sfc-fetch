The Takeovers and Mergers Panel (Panel) has upheld the Executive’s ruling that Rich Legend International Limited (Rich Legend) must proceed with its conditional voluntary general offers (Offers) to acquire the outstanding shares, convertible bonds and share options of Zhongyu Gas Holdings Limited.

The matter arose as a result of an application made on 6 May 2010, on behalf of Rich Legend and its parent company, China Gas Holdings Limited, requesting the Panel to review the Executive’s ruling. On 13 May 2010 the Panel met to consider the matter and upheld the Executive’s decision not to confirm that Rich Legend may invoke certain conditions under Note 2 to Rule 30.1 of the Code on Takeovers and Mergers and accordingly Rich Legend must proceed with the Offers.

A copy of the [Panel’s decision](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/cfd/mergers/panel/Decision%20-%20Zhongyu%20Gas%20(Eng).pdf) can be found in the “Prospectuses, Takeovers & Mergers” – “Takeovers and Mergers” – “Panel and Executive Decisions / Statements” section of the SFC website at www.sfc.hk.
