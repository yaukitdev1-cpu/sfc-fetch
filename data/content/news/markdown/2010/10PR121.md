The Eastern Magistracy today fined Mr {X} and a company under his control, Hong Kong Business Agency Group Limited (HKBA), a total of $10,000 after they pleaded guilty to two offences of unlicensed dealing in securities.

Magistrate Amanda Jane Woodcock also ordered them to pay investigation costs of $25,000 to the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC).

The prosecution followed an investigation by the SFC which found that {X} and HKBA, who were not licensed by the SFC in any capacity, made offers to the general public to sell Hong Kong listed shares held by clients. The offers were made through advertisements on an internet website and made also to a potential buyer between 18 February 2010 and 26 March 2010, even though the offers did not exist (Note 1).
