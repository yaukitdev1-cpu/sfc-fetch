The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today announced the resignation of Mr Paul Kennedy, Executive Director and Chief Operating Officer, with effect from 7 April 2010.

Mr Kennedy will be leaving to pursue other interests.

"During the past three years, Paul has made significant contributions to the work of the SFC and played an important role in advancing a number of major initiatives. I want to thank him for his support and wish him every success in his future endeavours," the SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, Mr Martin Wheatley, said.

Mr Kennedy was first appointed to the position in October 2006.
