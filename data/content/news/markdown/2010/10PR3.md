The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has begun today a two-month consultation to solicit public comments on [proposals to extend the Codes on Takeovers and Mergers and Share Repurchases (Codes) to real estate investment trusts (REITs) and to apply the market misconduct and disclosure of interests provisions in the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO) to listed collective investment schemes (CISs)](/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/consultation/openFile?refNo=10CP1).

The SFC believes that, given the development of REIT markets in Hong Kong and other parts of Asia and rising interests in mergers and acquisitions of REITs, this is an appropriate time to review the regulatory regime governing takeovers and mergers of REITs with a view to providing better protection for minority unitholders. "We believe that the proposals represent a step forward in defining a regulatory infrastructure that better protects the interests of investors and assists the further development of Hong Kong's REIT market," said Mr Martin Wheatley, the SFC's Chief Executive Officer.

The first part of the proposals calls for amendments to the REIT Code including aligning the control structure of REIT with that of listed companies, and to the Codes including introducing a set of REIT Guidance Notes to the Codes (Note 1).

The second part seeks to clarify and enhance the regulation of market conduct regarding dealings in listed CIS by applying Parts XIII to XV of the SFO (Note 2) to them. While Parts XIII and XIV deal with market misconduct, Part XV stipulates disclosure of interests requirements for listed companies.

Until 8 March 2010, comments to the SFC can be submitted online via the SFC website, by e-mail to reitsconsult@sfc.hk, by post or by fax 2877 0318.
