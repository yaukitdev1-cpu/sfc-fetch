The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the appointment by the Financial Secretary of Mr Anderson Chow Ka-ming, SC and Professor Leonard Cheng Kwok-hon as Non-Executive Directors for a term of two years, with effect from 1 January 2011.

“We look forward to working with Mr Chow and Professor Cheng. I am certain their expertise and experience will benefit the SFC and Hong Kong’s financial market,” the SFC’s Chairman, Dr Eddy Fong said.

“We would also like to express our gratitude to Mr Kenneth Kwok Hing-wai, SC, Professor Liu Pak-wai and Mr Zhang Shengman for their invaluable advice and contribution during their time with the Commission,” Dr Fong added.

Their term of appointment will expire on 31 December 2010.
