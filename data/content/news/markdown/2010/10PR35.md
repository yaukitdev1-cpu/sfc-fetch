The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) is pleased to announce new appointments and re-appointments to the following committees with effect from 1 April 2010:


- Academic and Accreditation Advisory Committee
- Committee on Unit Trusts
- Committee on Investment-Linked Assurance and Pooled Retirement Funds
- Disciplinary Chair Committee
- Investor Compensation Fund Committee
- Investor Education Advisory Committee
- Nominations Committee
- Securities Compensation Fund Committee
- SFC Dual Filing Advisory Group
- SFC (HKEC Listing) Committee
- Takeovers Appeal Committee
- Takeovers and Mergers Panel

The SFC welcomes the new committee members who will bring with them significant expertise and experience to the effective functioning of the respective committees. It is also grateful to outgoing members for their service and contributions to the committees over the years.
