The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announces the gazettal of the [SFC Handbook](http://www.sfc.hk/sfcRegulatoryHandbook/EN/displayFileServlet?docno=H584) for Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds, Investment-Linked Assurance Schemes and Unlisted Structured Investment Products (Handbook) today.

The introduction of the Handbook is one of the measures set out in the [Consultation Conclusions](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/consult/q3c_consultation.pdf) on Proposals to Enhance Protection for the Investing Public published on 28 May 2010. The Handbook includes revised product codes for unit trusts and mutual funds and for investment-linked assurance schemes as well as a new product code for unlisted structured investment products.

The Handbook has become effective today and different transitional arrangements apply to different products under the applicable codes.

To provide additional guidance to the industry, the SFC has made available on its website [frequently asked questions](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/faqs/products/products.html) and the SFC’s answers in connection with application procedures, together with [forms and checklists](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/forms/products/products.html) applicable to the respective investment products subject to the Handbook.
