The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today announced the establishment of the Products Advisory Committee (PAC).

In the [Consultation Conclusions](/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/consultation/conclusion?refNo=09CP3) on Proposals to Enhance Protection for the Investing Public published in May 2010, the SFC introduced a new [SFC Handbook](http://www.sfc.hk/sfcRegulatoryHandbook/EN/displayFileServlet?docno=H584) for Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds, Investment-Linked Assurance Schemes and Unlisted Structured Investment Products (Handbook) that covers a wide range of investment products offered to the public. With a view to rationalising the regulation of products being offered to the public, it was concluded in the same Consultation Conclusions paper that the PAC should be established.

The PAC replaces the existing Committee on Unit Trusts and Committee on Investment-Linked Assurance and Pooled Retirement Funds. The PAC is advisory in nature and it may be consulted on a wide range of matters pertaining to the Handbook, the [SFC Code on MPF Products](http://www.sfc.hk/sfcRegulatoryHandbook/EN/displayFileServlet?docno=H582) and the [Code on Pooled Retirement Funds](http://www.sfc.hk/sfcRegulatoryHandbook/EN/displayFileServlet?docno=H583), including overall market environment, industry practices and novel product features. Depending on the issues under consideration, the SFC may convene a meeting of all or any sub-group of the PAC members with the relevant experience or background.

The PAC comprises representation from the industry, professionals, academics and other stakeholders with market knowledge and expertise. The SFC welcomes the 29 members (Note 1) appointed to the PAC from 10 August 2010 to 31 March 2012.

A [full membership list](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/aboutsfc/structure/regulatory/regulatory.html) is available on the SFC website ([www.sfc.hk](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/index.html)).
