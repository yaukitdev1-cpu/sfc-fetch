Hong Kong, both as part of China and as an international financial centre, is set to play an important role in the renminbi (RMB) internationalisation process, according to Mrs Alexa Lam, the Deputy Chief Executive Officer and Executive Director, Policy, China and Investment Products, of the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC).

Speaking today at the 19th Annual Global Investment Funds Forum in Luxembourg, Mrs Lam said that Hong Kong, which boasts a mature and open market and a world-class legal system, is uniquely placed to become the premier offshore RMB centre.

“Through Hong Kong, the world’s investors now have a brand new investible currency. For asset managers, they now have the opportunity to create a new range of RMB products using the Hong Kong platform,” she said.

She also reminded asset managers that when creating new products, they must use “not just their skills and capacity for innovation, but also their commitment to excellence and their willingness to put clients’ interests first”.
