Dr Wise has taken to his column to raise awareness for a package of new measures to enhance investor protection introduced by the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) and to be implemented in phases.

In his latest column, “[Know How New Rules Protect You When Buying Investment Products](http://www.invested.hk/invested/en/html/section/dr_wise/2010articles/newrules_jun10.html),” Dr Wise walks investors through two of the measures that have taken effect first, namely, the:


- Product Key Fact Statements (KFS), this explains in plain language an investment product’s key features and risks and will be given to investors along with the offering document at the pre-sale stage;
- Cooling-off period, a pre-determined timeframe after an order has been placed during which investors have the right to unwind their investment in unlisted structured investment products.

While investors can make use of a KFS to get an overview of a product, Dr Wise cautions against relying solely on it to make investment decisions and that investors should read the offering document. He also reminds investors that they should not make hasty investment decisions, thinking they may exit from their purchase commitment during a cooling-off period. As certain fees and a market-value adjustment will have to be taken into account, investors are unlikely to receive a full refund on their invested amount.

“Now that the SFC has new measures to enhance investor protection, it is important that you find out about your rights,” says Dr Wise.

Through his column, Dr Wise, a fictitious character created by the SFC, shares with the public his wisdom on investing and his thoughts on regulatory issues.

Dr Wise’s column is published on the last working Tuesday of every month. The column is posted under the “Dr Wise” section of the SFC’s [InvestEd](http://www.invested.hk/invested/en/html/section/index.html) website. Investors are welcome to send their feedback and questions to Dr Wise by email to [InvestEd@sfc.hk](mailto:invested@sfc.hk).
