The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today filed an application to the Eastern Magistracy requesting Deputy Magistrate Mr Thomas Chan to state a case for a review to the High Court following his decision on 20 January 2010 to acquit Mr Tsoi Bun, a futures trader, of five price rigging charges (Note 1).
