The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the re-appointment by the Financial Secretary of Dr Kelvin Wong Tin-yau as a Non-Executive Director for a term of two years effective from 20 October 2014.


"Dr Wong has provided invaluable guidance to the SFC in the past two years and I look forward to continue working closely with him in his new term," said Mr Carlson Tong, the SFC&rsquo;s Chairman.
