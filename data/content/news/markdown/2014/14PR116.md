The Court of Final Appeal has granted leave to Pacific Sun Advisors Limited and its director {X} (the Applicants) to appeal a decision of the Court of First Instance in relation to issuing advertisements to promote a collective investment scheme. The two points of law to be determined by the Court of Final Appeal are:



- whether for section 103(3)(k) of the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO) to be applicable, it must be seen from the advertisement, invitation or document itself, that it is, by its terms, confined to professional investors to the exclusion of other members of the investing public; and

- whether the carrying out of a screening process to ensure that all investors investing in the collective investment scheme, are professional investors is irrelevant.


The Applicants were convicted on 10 June 2014 on four charges of issuing advertisements that offered interests in a collective investment scheme without the authorization of the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC). The Applicants have subsequently appealed these convictions. The Court of Final Appeal has stayed these appeals pending its determination of the two points of law (Notes 1 & 2).


The appeal is scheduled to be heard before the Court of Final Appeal on 5 March 2015.
