The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) is pleased to announce new appointments and re-appointments to the following committees with effect from 1 April 2014:



- Academic and Accreditation Advisory Committee

- Disciplinary Chair Committee

- Investor Compensation Fund Committee

- Nominations Committee

- Products Advisory Committee

- Securities Compensation Fund Committee

- SFC Dual Filing Advisory Group

- SFC (HKEC Listing) Committee

- Takeovers and Mergers Panel

- Takeovers Appeal Committee


The SFC welcomes all new committee members and expresses its gratitude to outgoing members for their service.
