The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the re-appointment by the Financial Secretary of Professor Leonard Cheng Kwok-hon as a Non-Executive Director for a term of two years effective from 1 January 2015.


"Professor Cheng has made a valuable contribution to the SFC and I look forward to continue working closely with him in his new term," said Mr Carlson Tong, the SFC&rsquo;s Chairman.
