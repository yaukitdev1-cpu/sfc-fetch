The Eastern Magistrates&rsquo; Court today convicted {X1} and {X2}, former dealers of Vermont Securities Company Limited (Vermont), of dealing in securities without a licence (Notes 1 & 2).


Both pleaded guilty and was fined $1,500 and $1,000 respectively. The Court also ordered them to pay the Securities and Futures Commission&rsquo;s (SFC) investigation costs.


The SFC alleged that {X1} and {X2}, whilst unlicensed, directly handled securities trading order instructions from customers of Vermont and confirmed execution of trades with customers from May 2011 to April 2012 and from May 2011 to September 2011 respectively.


The SFC reminds investors to check the SFC&rsquo;s Public Register of Licensed Persons and Registered Institution on the SFC website ([www.sfc.hk](http://www.sfc.hk/)) to ensure that people who handle their trades are licensed.


End
