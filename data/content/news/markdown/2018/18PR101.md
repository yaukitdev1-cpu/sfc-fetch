The Court of Final Appeal today dismissed the appeal of Moody&rsquo;s Investors Service Hong Kong Limited (Moody&rsquo;s) in relation to the Securities and Futures Commission&rsquo;s (SFC) disciplinary action concerning a special comment report Moody&rsquo;s published in 2011 (Notes 1 & 2). 


The Court will deliver reasons later.


This brings to an end Moody&rsquo;s challenge on the SFC&rsquo;s jurisdiction to take disciplinary action against it in this matter (Note 3).


End


Notes:



- Moody&rsquo;s is licensed under the Securities and Futures Ordinance to carry on business in Type 10 (providing credit rating services) regulated activity.

- The panel of judges at today&rsquo;s Court of Final Appeal hearing comprised The Hon Ribeiro PJ, The Hon Tang PJ, The Hon Fok PJ, The Hon Bokhary NPJ and Lord Neuberger of Abbotsbury NPJ.

- For further details of the case, please refer to the SFC&rsquo;s press releases dated [5 April 2016](https://www.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/news-and-announcements/news/doc?refNo=16PR34), [8 June 2017](https://www.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/news-and-announcements/news/doc?refNo=17PR77), [17 October 2017](https://www.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/news-and-announcements/news/doc?refNo=17PR132), and [7 February 2018](https://www.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/news-and-announcements/news/enforcement-news/doc?refNo=18PR11).
