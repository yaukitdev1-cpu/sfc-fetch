The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the appointment by the Financial Secretary of Ms Agnes Chan Sui-kuen and the re-appointment of Mr Edward Cheng Wai-sun as Non-Executive Directors for a term of two years. 


The term of Ms Chan will begin on 20 October 2018 and Mr Cheng&rsquo;s term will start on 1 January 2019 (Note 1).


"On behalf of the board, I would like to extend a warm welcome to Ms Chan and I am certain that the SFC will continue to benefit from Mr Cheng&rsquo;s valuable counsel," said Mr Carlson Tong, the SFC&rsquo;s chairman.


"I would also like to express our gratitude to outgoing Non-Executive Director Dr Kelvin Wong Tin-yau for his valuable contributions to the SFC&rsquo;s overall development over the past six years," Mr Tong added (Note 2).
