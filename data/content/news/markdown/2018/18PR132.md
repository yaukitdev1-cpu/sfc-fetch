The China Securities Regulatory Commission (CSRC) and the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) have entered into a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) regarding the cooperation and exchange of information in connection with the supervision and oversight of regulated entities of the CSRC or the SFC that operate on a cross-boundary basis in Hong Kong and Mainland (Cross-Boundary Regulated Entities).


The MoU facilitates the CSRC and the SFC to cooperate with each other in the interest of fulfilling their respective mandates, particularly in the areas of investor protection, promoting the integrity and financial prudence of Cross-Boundary Regulated Entities, fostering fairness of markets, reducing systemic risk and maintaining financial stability.
