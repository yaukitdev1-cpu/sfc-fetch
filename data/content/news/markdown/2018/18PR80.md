The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the appointment by the Financial Secretary of Mr James C Lin and the re-appointment of Dr William Wong Ming-fung, SC as Non-Executive Directors for a term of two years with effect from 1 August 2018 (Note 1).


"I would like to extend a warm welcome to Mr Lin and look forward to continuing to work with Dr Wong," said Mr Carlson Tong, the SFC&rsquo;s chairman.


"I would like to express our gratitude to outgoing Non-Executive Director Ms Teresa Ko Yuk-yin for her energetic and valuable contributions to the SFC&rsquo;s overall development over the past six years," Mr Tong added (Note 2).
