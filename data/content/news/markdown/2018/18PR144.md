The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today released a [report](https://www.sfc.hk/web/EN/files/ER/PDF/17%20-%202018%20review%20report%20(English).pdf) on its review of The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited&rsquo;s (SEHK) performance in its regulation of listing matters.


The report summarises the findings and recommendations of the SFC&rsquo;s review, which covered 2016 and 2017. It also identifies certain areas for SEHK to enhance its performance.


The report has been published on the SFC website.
