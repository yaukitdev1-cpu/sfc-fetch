The Securities and Futures Commission ("SFC") is pleased to announce that at a meeting of the Technical Committee of the International Organisation of Securities Commissions ("IOSCO") held on Monday (16 September 1996), in Montreal, Canada, its Chairman, Mr Anthony Neoh, QC, was elected Chairman of the Technical Committee.

The Technical Committee comprises representatives from the regulatory authorities of the sixteen most advanced markets and is IOSCO's key policy forum for considering ways of facilitating cross-border securities transactions and in particular developing internationally recognised regulatory requirements.

Mr Neoh, who is attending the 21st Annual Conference of IOSCO in Montreal, said: "I am deeply honoured to be elected Chairman and will continue the good work of the past Chairmen of the Committee."

"The support of the members in electing me reflects their confidence in Hong Kong's ability to remain an international financial centre and our continued ability to operate effectively in the international arena both in the run-up to and after the change of sovereignty," added Mr Neoh.
