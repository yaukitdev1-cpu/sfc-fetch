The Securities and Futures Commission ("SFC") today announced that the High Court has sanctioned a compromise between Canwell Forex International Limited ("Canwell") and Silver Bound Capital Limited ("Silver Bound") in relation to High Court Action No. A11686 of 1995. 

The terms of the compromise involve Silver Bound making available a sum of HK$6.6 million to be used for the payment in full of all amounts due to Canwell's margin clients as well as the costs of the SFC and the administrator/receiver ad litem.

The Administrator, Mr Stephen Caswell, has begun the process of returning client deposits and assets to Canwell's clients.

Commenting on the compromise, Mr Michael Wu, Deputy Chairman of the SFC, noted that it enabled the SFC to discharge its statutory duties through the total satisfaction of those interests that it is charged to protect. Mr Wu also took the opportunity to remind registered intermediaries of the SFC's commitment to stamping out investor abuse. He pointed out that the SFC will not hesitate to use its powers to pursue investors' interests.
