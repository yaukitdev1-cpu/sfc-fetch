The Securities and Futures Commission (“SFC") announced today that it had signed a Declaration of Cooperation on Investment Management with the Luxembourg Monetary Institute (“LMI").

The purpose of the Declaration is to enhance the protection of investors and strengthen the cooperation between the SFC and the LMI. The Declaration provides for regular exchange of information between the SFC and the LMI on investment fund managers, investment advisers, and other persons involved in the fund management industry.

In the case of Luxembourg funds authorised in Hong Kong, the LMI will obtain a special report from the funds' auditors to ensure compliance with Hong Kong requirements. Of the 1,327 unit trusts and mutual funds authorised in Hong Kong (as at 30 November 1996) , 39% were domiciled in Luxembourg.

Commenting on the Declaration, Mr Anthony Neoh, Chairman of the SFC, said, “This agreement demonstrates the SFC’s ongoing commitment to regulatory cooperation on investment management business and represents another important link in the network of agreements signed by the SFC with regulatory bodies worldwide. The supervision of fund management is increasingly a global exercise and it is important that an investor’s choice of domicile does not compromise investor protection."

This is the third declaration on joint regulation of investment management signed by the SFC. Previous declarations have been signed with the Securities and Exchange Commission of the United States and the Investment Management Regulatory Organisation of the United Kingdom.

In addition to the Declaration with the LMI, the SFC also has Memoranda of Understanding on regulatory supervision with the securities and futures regulators in Australia, British Columbia, China, France, Indonesia, Malaysia, New Zealand, Spain, Taipei, Thailand, the United Kingdom, and the United States.
