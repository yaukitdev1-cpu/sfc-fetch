The Chairman of the US Securities and Exchange Commission ("SEC"), Mr Arthur Levitt, Jr, today visited the Securities and Futures Commission ("SFC").

SFC Chairman, Mr Anthony Neoh, held discussions with Mr Levitt over co-operation between the SEC and the SFC and other matters of mutual interest. Mr Levitt, together with other officials of the SEC, also met with senior SFC directors and executives.

Mr Levitt and Mr Neoh reiterated during the meeting each other's commitment to strengthening co-operation between the two authorities.

Note to Editor: The SFC and the SEC entered into a Memorandum of Understanding in Washington DC in October last year. The MOU, signed by Mr Neoh and Mr Levitt, provides for regulatory co-operation and enhances existing information sharing arrangements between the Hong Kong and the US authorities. In addition to the MOU, the SFC and the SEC also signed a Declaration on Co-operation and Supervision of Cross-Border Investment Management Activity. The Declaration is intended to establish procedures for authorities to assist each other in oversight of investment advisers and fund managers, investment companies and commodity trading advisers doing business in Hong Kong and the US.
