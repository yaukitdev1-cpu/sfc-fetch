The Securities & Futures Commission ("SFC") today launches four new publications designed for both prospective and existing retail investors as a step-by-step guide to investment.The booklets, in English and Chinese, are part of the SFC Investor Education Programme and discuss some of the key issues people should consider when deciding to invest.

Those issues include the risks and rewards of investing, the importance of "doing your homework" when selecting an investment or choosing a broker or financial adviser, and what the SFC can do to help investors who encounter market malpractice by intermediaries.

The booklets also contain lists of publications available to the investing public from the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong, the Hong Kong Futures Exchange, the Hong Kong Investment Funds Association, the Hong Kong Federation of Insurers and the Hong Kong Securities Clearing Company, as well as contact details for these organisations.

The titles of the four publications are:

**Step 1: Your First Moves Towards Investing**

Step 2: Choosing Your Broker or Financial Adviser

Step 3: The Importance of Monitoring Your Investments

Step 4: When to Pursue a Complaint

All four publications are available to the public free of charge from the SFC and all 16 offices of the Consumer Council.
