In a speech today at the Asian Fund Management conference in Singapore, SFC Executive Director Laura Cha discussed the factors behind Hong Kong's position as the leading investment management centre in Asia outside of Japan. Major opportunities for industry growth include the introduction of the Mandatory Provident Fund and the development of the fund management industry in China.

The factors behind Hong Kong's position as a leading fund management centre include Hong Kong's open markets, absence of exchange controls, developed market infrastructure and a system of clear rules fairly administered.

"In the SFC, we have worked hard to create the right environment to allow the industry to prosper while offering adequate investor protection -- a very important balance," said Mrs. Cha.
