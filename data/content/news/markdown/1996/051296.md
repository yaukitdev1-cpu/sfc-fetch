The Securities and Futures Commission ("SFC") approved the appointment of Mr Alec Tsui Yiu Wa as the new Chief Executive of the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong at a board meeting today.

"The SFC welcomes Mr Tsui’s appointment and fully endorses the view of Dr Edgar Cheng, the Chairman of the Stock Exchange, that the Hong Kong market was fortunate to have a person of such quality and experience to lead the Stock Exchange forward," said Mr Anthony Neoh, Chairman of the SFC.

The SFC board also took the opportunity to record its deep appreciation of Mr Paul Chow Man Yiu’s leadership and contributions to the Hong Kong market during his tenure as Chief Executive of the Exchange.

Mr Neoh added that the SFC was confident that Mr Tsui would uphold the high standards set by Mr Chow and was looking forward to continuing its close working relationship with Mr Tsui.

"Mr Tsui’s leadership and management skills will assist the Exchange in meeting the challenges ahead and in ensuring that the Exchange fully exploits the opportunities that lie ahead," Mr Neoh said.
