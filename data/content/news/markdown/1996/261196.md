The Securities and Futures Commission (“SFC") today announced the introduction of the SFC Infoline -- a 24-hour automated telephone system which provides information on SFC requirements for license applicants, ongoing requirements for licensed persons, and general SFC matters. Callers can listen to the Infoline’s recorded messages in either Cantonese or English, receive information by return fax, or speak to an SFC representative during office hours regarding specific questions. The SFC Infoline number is **2840-9393**.

The SFC Infoline complements the SFC Hotline, which is designed for use by the investing public for enquiries or complaints about registered persons and authorised funds. The SFC Hotline number is **2840-9333**.

An SFC spokesman said, "Collectively, the SFC Infoline, Hotline, publications and Website on the Internet represent the Commission’s commitment to providing regulatory services and information through multiple channels to both the investing public and market practitioners. The SFC Infoline should prove to be an especially useful resource to first-time license applicants as well as to market practitioners with questions about licensing and ongoing requirements."
