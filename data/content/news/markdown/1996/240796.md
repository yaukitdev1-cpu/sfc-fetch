By an order of the Supreme Court of Hong Kong dated 24 July 1996, the Official Receiver has been appointed the trustee in bankruptcy to deal with the financial affairs of Cheung Cheong Woon. The order was made following an application by the Securities and Futures Commission under section 46 of the Securities and Futures Commission Ordinance.

Investors' claims can be dealt with in an orderly manner pursuant to proceedings under the Bankruptcy Ordinance following the granting of the receiving order.
