The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) is pleased to announce new appointments and re-appointments to the following committees with effect from 1 April 2012:  Academic and Accreditation Advisory Committee Disciplinary Chair Committee Investor Compensation Fund Committee Investor Education Advisory Committee Nominations Committee Products Advisory Committee Securities Compensation Fund Committee SFC Dual Filing Advisory Group SFC (HKEC Listing) Committee Takeovers and Mergers Panel Takeovers Appeal Committee  The SFC welcomes new committee members whose significant expertise and experience will contribute to the effective functioning of the respective committees. It is also grateful to outgoing members for their services to the committees over the years.  End  Note: 1. Please see the attached for membership changes to SFC committees effective from 1 April 2012. [Full member lists](http://www.sfc.hk/web/EN/about-the-sfc/organisational-chart/external-committees/) are available on the SFC website ([www.sfc.hk](http://www.sfc.hk)).
