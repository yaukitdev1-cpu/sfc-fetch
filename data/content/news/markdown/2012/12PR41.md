The Court of First Instance today refused an application by Mr X, the director of Billion Global Asset Management Limited, for leave to appeal to the Court of Final Appeal.


On 6 September 2011, Mr X was convicted of two charges of holding out he was carrying on an asset management business and also issuing a related advertisement, both without a licence. Mr X&rsquo;s convictions were upheld by the Court of First Instance on 14 March 2012.


In dismissing Mr X&rsquo;s application today, the Court of First Instance said Mr X&rsquo;s proposed appeal raised no point of law of great and general importance.
