The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the appointment by the Financial Secretary of Ms Teresa Ko Yuk Yin as Non-Executive Director for a term of two years, with effect from 1 August 2012.

“We look forward to working with Ms Ko. I am certain that the SFC and the Hong Kong financial market will benefit from her expertise and insight,” the SFC’s Chairman, Dr Eddy Fong said.

“We would also like to express our gratitude to outgoing Non-Executive Director Mrs Angelina Lee for her invaluable advice and contribution during her time with the SFC in the past six years,” Dr Fong added.

Mrs Lee was first appointed to the SFC as Non-Executive Director on 1 August 2006. Her current term expires on 31 July 2012.
