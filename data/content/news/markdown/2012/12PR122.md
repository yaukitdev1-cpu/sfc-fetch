The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) is pleased to announce that Mr Carlson Tong, Chairman of the SFC, has been appointed as a member of the Nominations Committee. 


The SFC also announces that Mr Chan Kin Keung, Anthony, SC has stepped down as a member of the Disciplinary Chair Committee (DCC).  The SFC would like to thank Mr Chan for his contribution to the work of the DCC.


End


Notes:



- Full membership list is available on the SFC website ([http://www.sfc.hk/](http://www.sfc.hk/)).

- The primary role of the Nominations Committee is to nominate members of the Takeovers and Mergers Panel, the Takeovers Appeal Committee and the Disciplinary Chair Committee.

- Members of the Disciplinary Chair Committee are nominated on the basis that they are duly experienced and legally qualified persons. Their role is to act as Chairman of the Takeovers and Mergers Panel in disciplinary proceedings under the Codes on Takeovers and Mergers or of the Takeovers Appeal Committee on a case-by-case basis.
