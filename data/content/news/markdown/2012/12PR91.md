The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the re-appointment by the Financial Secretary of three Executive Directors for another three-year term. 


They are Mr Brian Ho, Executive Director of Corporate Finance; Mr Keith Lui, Executive Director of Supervision of Markets; and Mr Mark Steward, Executive Director of Enforcement (Note 1).


"I am pleased with the Financial Secretary&rsquo;s decision to re-appoint Brian, Keith and Mark," the SFC&rsquo;s Chief Executive Officer, Mr Ashley Alder said.


"They have made significant contributions over the years to advance the SFC&rsquo;s regulatory objectives, and their expertise and vast experience are valuable assets to the SFC. I look forward to continuing working with them closely in their new term," he added.
