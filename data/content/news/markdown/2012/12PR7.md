The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has extended the functionality of the SFC Online Portal ([https://portal.sfc.hk](https://portal.sfc.hk)) to facilitate electronic submission of licence applications and other licensing-related communications.

The new features further enhance communication efficiency between licensed intermediaries and the SFC. Licensed persons and licence applicants are now able to utilize the Internet to submit to the SFC, and manage, all types of licensing applications, notifications, annual returns and payments (Note 1 and 2).

“We are committed to the continuous improvement of our systems and procedures to make them more efficient, convenient and user-friendly, and the enhanced functionality of the SFC Online Portal is an important part of this ongoing process,” said the SFC’s Chief Executive Officer Mr Ashley Alder.

The enhanced Portal functions are now available to the public. Any person without a Portal account may open a self-registered account to make use of the new application facilities.

End

Notes:
1. The SFC Online Portal was first launched on 30 September 2009, allowing the electronic submission of notifications and annual returns.
2. In April 2010, the Portal was enhanced to facilitate the electronic payment of annual fees and other changes.
