The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today announced the implementation of the Government&rsquo;s grant scheme to subsidise the setting up of open-ended fund companies (OFCs) and real estate investment trusts (REITs) in Hong Kong (Note 1).


For OFCs successfully incorporated in or re-domiciled to Hong Kong and SFC-authorised REITs successfully listed on the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited, the scheme covers 70% of eligible expenses paid to Hong Kong-based service providers, subject to a cap of $1 million per OFC and $8 million per REIT.


&ldquo;By encouraging a broader range of investment vehicles, the grant scheme will reinforce Hong Kong&rsquo;s role as a leading capital raising venue and its status as an international asset and wealth management centre,&rdquo; said Mr Ashley Alder, the SFC&rsquo;s Chief Executive Officer.


The scheme will operate for three years and is open for applications starting today on a first-come-first-served basis. Details, including the eligibility criteria and application process, are set out in the [Attachment](https://www.sfc.hk/-/media/EN/files/ER/PDF/OFC-and-REIT-Grant-Scheme--PR-AttachmentEN.pdf).


The SFC also published [frequently asked questions](https://www.sfc.hk/-/media/files/PCIP/FAQ-PDFS/FAQ-on-OFC-and-REIT-Grant-Scheme__20210510.pdf) to provide guidance to the industry together with the grant application forms.


Investment managers are welcome to consult the SFC about the scheme before making an application (Note 2).
