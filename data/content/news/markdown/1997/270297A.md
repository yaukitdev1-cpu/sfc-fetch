Section 80(1) of the Securities Ordinance prohibits the sale of securities when the seller does not have a presently exercisable and unconditional right to sell. {X1} (1 summons), {X2} (1 summons), {X3} (1 summons), {X4} (2 summonses), {X5} (3 summonses), HY Chan (6 summonses) and Mak (28 summonses) were fined $5,000, $4,000, $3,000, $5,000, $6,000, $12,000 and $84,000 respectively.
