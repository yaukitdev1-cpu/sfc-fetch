As a result of the withdrawal by Mr Wong Kwok Lau (Wong) of his appeal lodged with the Securities and Futures Appeals Panel, the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that Wong's registration as securities dealer's representative had been suspended for a period of 18 months effective 16 July 1997.

The action resulted from an inquiry initiated by the SFC under section 56 of the Securities Ordinance in relation to Wong's trading malpractices through his wife's securities trading account maintained with Cheer Pearl Investment Limited (Cheer Pearl) during the period April 1993 to March 1994, whilst Wong was employed as floor trader of Paul Securities Limited (Paul Securities).

The investigation revealed that Wong had been guilty of misconduct in that :

a. he actively participated in trading malpractices that contributed to him gaining profits of approximately $100,000 through his wife's account maintained with Cheer Pearl;
b. these profits were gained at the expense of clients of Paul Securities and where buying insurance was involved, also denied the clients of other brokers best execution; and
c. he concealed such trading from his employer, Paul Securities.


As a result, the SFC concluded that Wong was guilty of misconduct and his acts and failures were prejudicial to the interests of the investing public.
