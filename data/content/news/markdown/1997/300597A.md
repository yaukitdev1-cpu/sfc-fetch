The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that the Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) signed between the SFC and the Ontario Securities Commission (OSC) had become effective.

The MOU was signed in Mexico City on 25 February 1997 during a meeting of the International Organisation of Securities Commissions (IOSCO). It would become effective once the requisite approval was obtained and procedures completed:

- On 21 March 1997, the SFC published in the Hong Kong Government Gazette the name of the OSC for the purposes of sections 59(2A) and 59A of the Securities and Futures Commission Ordinance and sections 63(3) and 63A of the Leveraged Foreign Exchange Trading Ordinance.
- On 23 April 1997, the MOU received approval from the Minister of Finance of Ontario and thus became effective on that date.
Mr Anthony Neoh, QC., Chairman of the SFC, said the Memorandum strengthened regulatory links between the Hong Kong and the Ontario securities and futures markets which in turn would increase the effectiveness of international securities regulation.

Mr John A. Geller, QC., Acting Chairman of the OSC, said that the OSC looked forward to continuing its close working relationship with the SFC both at the IOSCO and on a bilateral basis.

The SFC has entered into co-operative agreements with 24 securities and futures regulators around the world.
