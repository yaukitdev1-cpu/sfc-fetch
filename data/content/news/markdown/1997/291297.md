The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) is pleased to announce that the Financial Secretary of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region has appointed Mr Andrew Thomas Procter as Executive Director of the SFC's Intermediaries and Investment Products Division, with effect from 1 January 1998. His appointment is for a period of two years to 31 December 1999.

Mr Procter, who is currently the Senior Policy Advisor to the Commission, will succeed Mr Michael Wu in his capacity as Executive Director, Intermediaries Division. Mr Wu will be leaving the Commission on 31 December 1997.

Announcing Mr Procter's appointment, Mr Neoh said, "I welcome the Financial Secretary's appointment of Andrew Procter as Executive Director, Intermediaries and Investment Products Division. He has made a valuable contribution as the SFC's Senior Policy Advisor and his regulatory and legal experience, in both Australia and Hong Kong, will serve the Commission well."

Mr Neoh said, "Mr Procter's appointment comes together with a partial restructuring of the SFC. The Investment Products Division will join the Intermediaries Supervision and Licensing Departments to form an expanded Intermediaries and Investment Products Division. The new Intermediaries Supervision, Licensing, and Investment Products Departments will be headed by Mr Richard Yin, Mr Leo Lee and Ms Joan Wong respectively. The synergies created by the bringing together of the three operating divisions will result in a higher level of service to the market."

Prior to joining the SFC, Mr Procter was the National Enforcement Co-ordinator for, and an Acting Commission Member of, the Australian Securities Commission, where he served from 1992 through 1996. Previously, Mr Procter was a solicitor in private practice in South Australia. He holds an Honours Degree of Bachelor of Laws, and a Bachelor of Arts Degree, from the University of Adelaide.

The SFC presently consists of five Executive Directors, including the Chairman and Deputy Chairman, and five Non-Executive Directors.
