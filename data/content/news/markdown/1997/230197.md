The Securities and Futures Commission ("SFC") announced today that Mr X ("X") was convicted by Mr I S C Candy at Western Magistracy on 17 January 1997 for providing false or misleading representations in his application to become licensed with the SFC. He was fined $10,000 and ordered to pay cost of $3,500 to the SFC.


The offence came to light following an investigation initiated by the SFC and concluded by the Criminal Investigation Department of Waterfront Police Station.


In his application to become a licensed representative under the Leveraged Foreign Exchange Trading Ordinance accredited to Emperor International Exchange (Hong Kong) Limited, X claimed that he had no criminal conviction when such was not the case.


Commenting on the prosecution, Mr Paul Bailey, Senior Director of Enforcement, said "The SFC views such misrepresentation very seriously as it puts in jeopardy the integrity of the entire registration and licensing process which in turn has the potential to harm the interests of the investing public."
