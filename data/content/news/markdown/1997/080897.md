The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) and the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong (the Exchange) today announce that the consultation period on the review of the policy on offering mechanisms has been extended by two weeks to 22 August. Submissions after 22 August will not be considered.

A small number of copies of the Consultation Paper on Offering Mechanisms, published in June 1997, are available at the offices of the SFC and the Exchange.
