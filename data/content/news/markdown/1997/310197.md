The Securities and Futures Commission ("SFC") notes with regret Mr Herbert Hui's decision to resign his post as Deputy Chief Executive of the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong. 

Mr Anthony Neoh, Chairman of the Commission, said: "I would like to pay tribute to Mr Hui for his contribution to the Stock Exchange and to the development of the securities market in Hong Kong during his tenure with the Exchange. I am confident his dedication to the work of the Exchange, particularly the Listing Division, has built a solid foundation for his successors."

SFC Executive Director Mrs Laura Cha added: "Under the strong leadership of Mr Hui, the Listing Rules and policies have been modified over the years to accommodate various market developments and needs of the issuers, practitioners, and investors. This has helped place the Stock Exchange in an eminent position among international exchanges."

Mr Neoh and Mrs Cha said that the SFC would continue to give its full support to the Stock Exchange in maintaining a fair, efficient, competitive and informed market.

The members of the Commission and all its staff wish Mr Hui the very best in his future endeavours.
