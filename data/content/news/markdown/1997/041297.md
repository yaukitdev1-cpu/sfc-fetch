The Securities and Futures Commission today launches two additional investor education brochures as it enhances its existing publication programme for retail investors.

The titles of the brochures, available in English and Chinese versions, are:

- What Every Stock Investor Should Know
- What Every Fund Investor Should Know

The two new publications, complementing the more general and highly popular existing series of independent investor guides, focus on specific areas of interest.

*What Every Stock Investor Should Know* aims to help the investing public understand some basic issues in stock investment and their rights as shareholders. Its topics include:

- participation in initial public offerings and rights issues;
- right of access to company information;
- takeovers and privatisations; and
- circumstances leading to suspension of trading

*What Every Fund Investor Should Know* explains how the SFC authorizes and monitors funds in order to protect investors, and examines the rights of fund investors. Its topics include:

- studying offering documents and financial reports;
- how to monitor fund investment;
- suspension of dealing; and
- fund termination and merger

SFC Chairman, Mr Anthony Neoh, said: "It is important for all investors to make an effort to understand the nature of their investments, their rights as investors, and the risks involved. These new publications address some of the more specific issues investors in stocks and funds may be faced with."

The new publications are available free of charge at the SFC and all 16 Consumer Council centres.

While the new brochures may give useful guidance on certain specific issues, investors should seek professional advice where appropriate before making their investment decisions.
