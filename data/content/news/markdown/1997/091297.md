The Securities and Futures Commission is pleased to announce that the Chief Executive of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region has appointed Mrs Laura Cha as Deputy Chairman of the SFC with effect from 1 January 1998. Her appointment falls within the same term as her current appointment as an Executive Director of the SFC, which is to 28 February 1999.

Mrs Cha, currently Executive Director (Corporate Finance), will succeed Mr Michael Wu, who will be leaving the Commission on 31 December 1997.

Welcoming the appointment, Mr Anthony Neoh, Chairman of the SFC, said: "I am particularly pleased that Mr C. H. Tung has decided to appoint Mrs Cha as the Commission's Deputy Chairman. Her legal background and experience in the Commission will be of great value to us."

Mr Neoh also paid tribute to Mr Wu, who joined the SFC at its inception in 1989 and became Deputy Chairman on 1 May 1995. Mr Wu is leaving the SFC upon the expiry of the tenure of his appointment.

Mr Neoh said: "Mr Wu has been involved in the reform of the securities industry since 1988. The Commission and the industry owe him a great debt of gratitude for the sterling contributions he has made." Mr Wu was Secretary to the Securities Review Committee which recommended in 1988 extensive reforms of the securities markets.

Joining the SFC as Assistant Director of Corporate Finance in 1991, Mrs Cha was promoted to Senior Director in 1993 and appointed Executive Director in 1994. Previously she was an attorney in Hong Kong and San Francisco, USA.

The SFC presently consists of five Executive Directors including the Chairman and Deputy Chairman, and five Non-Executive Directors. An appointment to the Executive Director position to be left vacant by Mr Wu is being processed and an announcement will be made in the near future.
