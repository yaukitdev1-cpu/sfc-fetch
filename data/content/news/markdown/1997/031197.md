The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) congratulates Mr Lee Hon Chiu on his being elected as the Chairman of the Council of the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong.

"Mr Lee is a person of impeccable integrity with great leadership ability. We've worked together closely before and I look forward to working with him again in his new capacity as the Chairman of the Exchange," said Mr Anthony Neoh, Chairman of the SFC. Mr Lee was a Non-Executive Director of the SFC between August 1990 and July 1994.

The SFC also wishes to pay tribute to Dr Edgar Cheng for his contribution to the Hong Kong securities market during his tenure as the Chairman of the Exchange in the past three years.

"The stability and efficiency of the market systems in the last few weeks bears testimony to the achievements of the Exchange under the guidance of Dr Cheng," Mr Neoh said.

"I am also very grateful to Dr Cheng for taking on the chairmanship of the Steering Committee for the formation of the Securities Institute as this will be an important landmark for the further development of our markets," he added.
