The Securities and Futures Commission ("SFC") today launches an Announcement in the Public Interest ("API") which will appear on local television channels beginning 24 April 1997.

The API is part of the SFC's overall efforts to educate investors on their vital role of protecting themselves - that they should "do their homework" and ask the right questions before investing.

In August last year, the SFC published four free investor education brochures, available in Chinese and English, which were designed for both prospective and existing retail investors as a step by step guide to investment. The SFC has also sponsored two Chinese-language television series, produced by the RTHK, to help spread educational messages.

The new API, which has a Chinese and an English version, features an animated bull and seeks to inform investors that they need to know more before investing.

It aims to bring out these two key messages:

- that investors must do their homework and ask the right questions before investing; and
- that investor education information and brochures produced by the SFC are available at the SFC and the Consumer Council.
Mr Anthony Neoh, Chairman of the SFC, said: "We hope that this new API can further the Commission's objective of helping people invest wisely."

"I am grateful for the assistance of the Government Information Services Department in the production and transmission of the API," Mr Neoh added.

The 30-second API will be broadcast on both the Chinese and English channels of ATV and TVB, and on seven Cable TV channels.
