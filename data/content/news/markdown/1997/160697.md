The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) notes that certain additional risk management measures in relation to the handover period from 26 June to 3 July, 1997 will be taken by the clearing houses for the futures and options markets.

The measures relate to margins and mark-to-market procedures and are summarised below:

Hong Kong Futures Exchange (HKFE)

The HKFE Clearing Corporation Ltd plans to increase margin levels beginning on 26 June until 3 July. For example, minimum client margins for Hang Seng Index futures contracts will increase to $60,000 from $50,000 per contract. The $60,000 margin level is equivalent to 1,200 index points. Similar procedures will apply to all other HKFE products.

The clearing house will also make an intra-day margin call at 2.30pm on Friday, 27 June. This will mark positions to the market to ensure that they are approximately fully margined as of the close of trading on Friday.

The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong (SEHK)

For stock options, the SEHK Options Clearing House Ltd will calculate margins based on three-day volatilities and will issue an intra-day margin call in the afternoon of Friday, 27 June.

In relation to stock trading, the Hong Kong Securities Clearing Company Ltd (HKSCC) will enforce its normal procedures and mark open positions to market at 11am on Friday, 27 June. Brokers with unfavourable mark-to-market position in excess of $5 million will need to pay marks to HKSCC by 2pm on Friday afternoon.

Margin and mark-to-market procedures will return to normal after the handover holiday.

Investors are advised to consult their brokers if they have any questions.
