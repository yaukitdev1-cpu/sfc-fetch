The Securities and Futures Commission ("SFC") and the Ontario Securities Commission ("OSC") signed a Memorandum of Understanding ("MOU") in Mexico City yesterday during a meeting of the International Organization of Securities Commissions ("IOSCO"). 

The MOU, signed by Mr Anthony Neoh, QC, Chairman of the SFC, and Mr John A Geller, QC, Chairman of the OSC, establishes a framework for mutual assistance and facilitates the exchange of information between the two authorities.

"This Memorandum strengthens regulatory links between the Hong Kong and Ontario securities and futures markets, enhancing the two authorities' market protection and law enforcement abilities," said Mr Neoh. "This agreement is another important step in the SFC's continuing commitment to international regulatory cooperation."

Mr Geller said: " We are delighted to be able to continue and enhance our working relationship with the Hong Kong Securities and Futures Commission, and with its Chairman, Mr Neoh, with whom our Commission has worked closely in IOSCO."

The Memorandum will become effective once it is approved by the Ontario Minister of Finance in accordance with the Ontario Securities Act and once the name of the Ontario Securities Commission is published in the Hong Kong Government Gazette for the purposes of sections 59(2A) and 59A of the Securities and Futures Commission Ordinance and sections 63(3) and 63A of the Leveraged Foreign Exchange Trading Ordinance.

Including this MOU with the OSC, the SFC has signed a total of fifteen Memoranda of Understanding with the securities and futures regulators in Australia, British Columbia, China, France, Indonesia, Malaysia, New Zealand, Singapore, Spain, Taipei, Thailand, the United Kingdom and the United States.
