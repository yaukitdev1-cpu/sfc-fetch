Following an inquiry into the misconduct of staff of Seapower Securities Limited (Seapower) during the period May 1995 to July 1996 (the relevant period), the SFC announces today that it has publicly reprimanded Seapower for failing during the relevant period : 

(a) to implement internal controls and procedures to detect possible short selling and to act as a deterrent to persons wishing to short sell;

(b) to implement specific and effective controls and procedures for monitoring in house cross trades;

(c) to ensure that an adequate audit trail was in place in order to detect and deter client and staff trading malpractice;

(d) to have in place a written policy on client and staff dealing; and

(e) to ensure at all times that it had adequate resources to supervise diligently and did supervise diligently its employees and persons appointed by it to conduct its business.

In issuing this reprimand the SFC acknowledges that Seapower has introduced a number of initiatives since mid-1996 designed to both improve its compliance system and the commitment of its staff to a compliance culture.
