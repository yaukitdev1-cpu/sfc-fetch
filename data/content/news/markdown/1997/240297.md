A new series of investor education television programme sponsored by the Securities and Futures Commission ("SFC") will be broadcast on six consecutive Tuesdays starting tomorrow. 

Officiating at a ceremony today at RTHK to launch the series, SFC Executive Director Laura Cha said the six half-hour magazine programmes, *Invest Wisely*, would enable the public to know more about the financial markets and the issues they need to pay attention to while investing.

"Investors must know the market and safeguard their own interests. Through our investor education programme, which includes the production of documentaries, the SFC hopes to spread our educational messages to the public to help them invest wisely," Mrs Cha said.

The latest series of programme follows the success last year of eight episodes of investor education five-minuters, which were designed to deliver the key messages concisely.

The six new episodes feature interviews with regulators, market operators and practitioners, and combine documentary segments with situation dramas based on real issues that investors may encounter. Their topics are:

- Episode One - Market Structure
- Episode Two - The Regulatory Framework
- Episode Three - Do Your Homework
- Episodes Four to Six - Know Your Financial Professionals
RTHK, which produced the programme, has also commissioned the University of Hong Kong to conduct telephone polls to gauge public opinion on issues relating to each of the topics, the results of which will be presented in the programme.

The series will be broadcast in Cantonese on TVB Jade Channel at 7pm every Tuesday from tomorrow to 1 April 1997.
