The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong (SEHK), the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC), and the Hong Kong Trade Development Council (TDC) will jointly organise a seminar on 8 September, 1997 in Chengdu, Sichuan.

The Seminar on the Securities Market of Hong Kong aims to introduce to mainland enterprises the capital raising opportunities in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and to explain to them the listing procedures.

The seminar is fully supported by the Sichuan Provincial People’s Government and is expected to be attended by 250 representatives from enterprises in the region, mainland market professionals, and government officials.

The Financial Secretary of the Hong Kong SAR, the Honourable Mr Donald Tsang, and Sichuan Governor, Mr Song Baorui, have agreed to give keynote addresses at the event. The Chairmen of the SEHK and the SFC, the Executive Director of the TDC, and their senior executives will also make presentations.

Market practitioners and professionals from Hong Kong have been invited to speak on topics ranging from accounting and legal issues to fund managers’ view on investing in Chinese enterprises.

The seminar will be conducted in Putonghua at the Holiday Inn Crowne Plaza Hotel from 8.30am to 5pm.

For further information please contact Henry Law of the SEHK, at 2840 3862, Chan Chi-keung of the SFC, at 2842 7717 or Adrienne Leung of the TDC, at 2584 4262.
