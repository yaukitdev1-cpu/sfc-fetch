The SFC announced today that it had reprimanded {X}, a dealer's representative of Wong Chi Wing trading as Victory Enterprises Company, now known as Victory Enterprises (Investment) Limited (Victory), for misconduct under Section 56 of the Securities Ordinance.

The SFC's action stemmed from an investigation into events during a period from February 1996 to July 1996, in which it was found that Mr Wong Hing Wah (Wong), Ms Law Woon Chun (Law) and Ms Lam May Chi (Lam) had short sold securities listed on the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited through {X} at Victory. {X} was reprimanded for her failure to ensure that at the time each order was placed by Wong, Law and Lam, they had the stocks on hand to cover their sales.

As a result of the SFC's investigation, Wong, Law and Lam pleaded guilty at Western Magistracy on 12 August 1997 to a total of 31 summonses for short selling securities, in contravention of Section 80 of the Securities Ordinance, and were fined $34,000, $18,000and $10,000 respectively. They were also ordered to pay cost totalling $9,420 to the SFC.
