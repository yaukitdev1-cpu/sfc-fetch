The Securities and Futures Commission welcomes the judgment today by Mr Justice Keith in the Court of First Instance, which underlines the importance of the Executive's ability to ensure financial advisers meet the highest standards of professionalism and integrity required by the Takeovers Code.

In dismissing an application for judicial review by Goldwyn Capital Limited against the Commission, Mr Justice Keith said that the Commission's role as a financial market watchdog "would be emasculated if it could not give effect to any doubts it may have about the integrity, competence or expertise of an investment adviser when it acts as financial adviser to an offeror involved in a takeover without having first instituted disciplinary proceedings under section 56 [of the Securities Ordinance]".

In his judgment, he said: "The Commission's need to act quickly to prevent the possibility of the investing public being misinformed would be completely frustrated if it was first obliged to invoke a procedure which could take many months to complete."

The judicial review was filed by Goldwyn Capital Limited (Goldwyn) after the Commission had refused to process a draft announcement relating to an offer which named Goldwyn as the financial adviser to the offeror.

The judgment supported the Commission's view that it must have the power to refuse to process an announcement when the Executive has sufficient grounds for concern that a financial adviser may not be able to meet the highest standards of professionalism and integrity required by the Takeovers Code in order to safeguard the interests of the investing public.

Mr Justice Keith noted in his judgment: "The potential consequence of a failure on the part of a financial adviser to perform its duties properly are serious. Misleading information may be given, up-to-date and relevant information may not be given, and an uninformed and false market may occur to the detriment of the existing shareholders and the investing public."

Mr Justice Keith awarded costs to the Commission.
