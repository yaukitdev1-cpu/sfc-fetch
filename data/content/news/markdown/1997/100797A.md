The SFC today announced that it had reprimanded {X1} and {X2} for their misconduct whilst employed by Tai Shing Stock Investment Company as dealer's representatives.

{X1} was reprimanded for concealing his personal trading from Tai Shing by trading through a nominee account which he also used to short sell securities between February and March 1995. In relation to his short selling activities,{X1} had, on 25 February 1997, pleaded guilty at Western Magistracy to two summonses for contravening Section 80 of the Securities Ordinance; he was fined $5,000.

{X2} was reprimanded in relation to her failure to ensure, prior to executing sell orders received from two clients on a total of eight occasions during the period from 17 to 28 February 1995, that the clients had stock on hand to settle the sales at the time each order was placed.
