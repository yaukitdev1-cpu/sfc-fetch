On 15 April 1997 the Hong Kong High Court made an order dismissing proceedings brought by the Honourable Mr Chim Pui Chung against Mr Mark Dickens, Executive Director of Enforcement of the Securities and Futures Commission ("SFC"), and his predecessor, Mr Gerard McMahon, relating to Mandarin Resources Corporation Limited ("Mandarin Resources").

Mr Chim's action had claimed that Mr Dickens and Mr McMahon had been guilty of misfeasance in public office in connection with the question of relisting of Mandarin Resources, the presentation of the SFC's winding up petition against the company in June 1996, and the appointment of provisional liquidators over the company in July 1996.

Mr Dickens and Mr McMahon, with the support of the SFC, had applied to strike out the action and that application was due to be heard on 16 April 1997. The order dismissing the action was made a day before and was made with the consent of Mr Chim, who has agreed to pay the SFC's costs of the action.
