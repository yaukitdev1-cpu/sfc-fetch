The Securities and Futures Commission ("SFC") and the Monetary Authority of Singapore ("MAS") signed a Memorandum of Understanding in Singapore today. This Memorandum promotes mutual assistance within the laws of both jurisdictions by the exchange of information in relation to securities and futures matters. 

The Memorandum was signed by Mr Anthony Neoh, QC, Chairman of the SFC, and Mr Lee Ek Tieng, Managing Director of the MAS.

Mr Neoh said: "The SFC's objective in international co-operation is to promote the exchange of information and other mutual assistance with overseas regulators in order to strengthen the enforcement of laws and regulations for the protection of investors and the prevention of securities and futures fraud and market malpractice. This Memorandum establishes a framework whereby our two organisations can work closer together towards achieving this objective."

Within the Asia-Pacific region, the SFC has Memoranda of Understanding with the securities and futures regulators in China, Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia, Taipei, Australia and New Zealand. The SFC also has Memoranda of Understanding with 10 other securities and futures regulators in other parts of the world.
