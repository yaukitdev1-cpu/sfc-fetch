The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) yesterday (30 May) signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with the Commissione Nazionale per le Societ?e la Borsa (CONSOB) of Italy in Cape Town, South Africa.

This MOU establishes a framework for mutual assistance and facilitates the exchange of information.

The MOU was signed by Mr Anthony Neoh, QC, Chairman of the SFC, and Mr Tommaso Padoa-Schioppa, Chairman of CONSOB. They were in Cape Town to attend meetings of the Executive and Technical Committees of the International Organisation of Securities Commissions.

Mr Neoh said: "We are delighted to be able to formalise and enhance our working relationship with our counterpart in Italy. This Memorandum establishes co-operative arrangements with the Consob which enhance our respective abilities in market protection and law enforcement."

Mr Padoa-Schioppa said: "It enhances the protection of Hong Kong and Italian investors and promotes the integrity of the securities and futures and options markets. It is also an important contribution to the fight against international fraud, making it even more difficult for financial fradusters to hide."

To date, the SFC has entered into co-operative agreements with 26 securities and futures regulators around the world.
