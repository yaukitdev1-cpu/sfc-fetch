Referring to the Governor's extension of his appointment for a further year, from 30 June 1997 to 30 June 1998, the Chairman of the Securities and Futures Commission, Mr Anthony Neoh, expressed his thanks to the Governor and to the Financial Secretary for the confidence shown in him. 

"I am very happy that I shall be able to continue to serve with my colleagues, all of whom have committed themselves to serving the markets and the community to the highest professional standards. I am proud of their achievements and I look forward to working with them in the new era of the Special Administrative Region", Mr Neoh said.
