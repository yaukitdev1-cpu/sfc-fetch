The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced that it had successfully prosecuted {X} on 15 July 1997 for acting as a dealer's representative of Yardley Securities Limited (Yardley) without being registered as such under the Securities Ordinance. The SFC also successfully prosecuted Mr Chan Kin Sun (Chan), managing director and dealing director of Yardley, for aiding and abetting {X}'s unregistered dealing.


An investigation by the SFC found that, from 6 July 1994 to 31 December 1995, {X} performed the functions of a dealer's representative of Yardley by introducing clients to Yardley and receiving orders from such clients to buy or sell securities, making arrangements for execution of such orders, and confirming execution of such orders with the clients. Chan was responsible for recruiting {X} to perform such dealing functions during the period, knowing that she could not be granted a registration on account of her being an undischarged bankrupt.


{X} and Chan pleaded guilty before Ms Polly Lo at Western Magistracy, {X} to one summons in relation to a breach of Section 50 of the Securities Ordinance, which prohibits any person acting as a dealer's representative in Hong Kong unless he is registered under Part VI of this Ordinance, and Chan to aiding and abetting {X}. {X} and Chan were fined $5,000 and $35,000 respectively. They were also ordered to pay costs totalling $17,316 to the SFC.
