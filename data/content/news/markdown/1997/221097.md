The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today announced the launch of its newly revised website. The new website features a "Registry" of over 18,000 SFC-licensed securities, futures and leveraged foreign exchange intermediaries as well as over 1,400 SFC-authorised investment products, allowing the public to check whether an intermediary is licensed by, or if an investment product has been authorised with the SFC. The Registry will be updated on the third working day of each month.

The SFC website also features an Investor Education section, including the SFC's independent guides for investors on such topics as: issues to consider when investing, how to choose your broker or adviser, the importance of monitoring your investments, and when to pursue a complaint. The Investor Education section also includes a series of investor stories illustrating common problems faced by investors. In addition, website users may submit enquiries or complaints to the SFC via e-mail.

A wealth of other information on the site includes SFC consultation papers, guidelines, licensing information, press releases, speeches, and links to other websites.

Commenting on the launch of the new site, SFC Chairman Anthony Neoh said, "The new SFC website promises to be a useful resource to both investors and market practitioners, providing an up-to-date, centralised library of SFC information as well as an on-line channel for public enquiries."

The SFC plans further developments to the site in the months ahead, including a Chinese-language version and more investor education materials.

The address of the SFC website is [http://www.hksfc.org.hk](http://www.sfc.hk/)
