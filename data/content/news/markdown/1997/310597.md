The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) yesterday (30 May) signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with the Comissão de Valores Mobiliários (CVM) of Brazil in Cape Town, South Africa.

This MOU establishes a framework for mutual assistance and facilitates the exchange of information.

The MOU was signed by Mr Anthony Neoh, QC, Chairman of the SFC and Mr Francisco Costa e Silva, Chairman of CVM. They were in Cape Town to attend meetings of the Executive and Technical Committees of the International Organisation of Securities Commissions.

Mr Neoh said: "We are delighted to be able to formalise and enhance our working relationship with our counterpart in Brazil. This Memorandum establishes co-operative arrangements with the CVM which enhance our respective abilities in market protection and law enforcement."

Mr Francisco Costa e Silva said: "In achieving our objectives of efficiency, fairness, integrity, and confidence, this signing represents a big step towards a high level of cooperation between our two agencies to keep pace with the ever growing complexity and increasingly international nature of the capital markets in both jurisdictions."

To date, the SFC has entered into co-operative agreements with 26 securities and futures regulators around the world.
