The SFC announced today that it had successfully prosecuted {X1} and {X2} for short selling securities whilst they were employed as securities dealer's representatives of Megabase Securities Limited. {X1} and {X2} were also disciplined in relation to their short selling resulting in both being issued with a reprimand.

{X1} and {X2} were fined a total of $30,000 and $20,000 respectively by Ms Polly Lo at Western Magistracy on 11 March 1997 for short selling securities in contravention of Section 80 of the Securities Ordinance. They were also ordered to pay costs totalling $13,500 to the SFC.
