The SFC announced today that it had successfully prosecuted Mr Mak Wing Tak (Mak), {X} and Mr Chau Yi Ming (Chau) for short selling, and Chau for unregistered securities dealing. Mak and {X} are registered dealer's representatives of CAF Securities Company Limited (CAF) whilst Chau is its Managing Director.


Mak, {X} and Chau pleaded guilty before Ms Polly Lo at Western Magistracy on 4 November 1997 to summonses in relation to their short selling activities. They were fined $12,000, $8,000 and $21,000 respectively. In addition, Chau was fined $40,000 for unregistered securities dealing in his capacity as director of CAF. Costs totalling $14,280 were awarded to the SFC.


The SFC investigation covered the period December 1995 to February 1996 during which it was found Mak, {X} and Chau had short sold on the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited. In addition, Chau, whilst unregistered, was found to have handled orders of CAF's clients.
