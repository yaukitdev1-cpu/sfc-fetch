The Court of Appeal has dismissed the Securities and Futures Commission&rsquo;s (SFC) application for leave to appeal to the Court of Final Appeal against the Market Misconduct Tribunal&rsquo;s (MMT) findings that two former executives of Asia Telemedia Limited (now known as Yunfeng Financial Group Limited), Mr Yiu Hoi Ying and Ms Marian Wong Nam, had not engaged in insider dealing (Notes 1 to 3).


The SFC is reviewing the decision.


End
