The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the re-appointment by the Financial Secretary of Ms Mary Ma Xuezheng and Mr Lester Garson Huang as Non-Executive Directors for a term of two years with effect from 15 November 2017 (Note 1).


"Ms Ma and Mr Huang have made valuable contributions to the SFC and I look forward to continuing to work with them," said Mr Carlson Tong, the SFC&rsquo;s chairman.
