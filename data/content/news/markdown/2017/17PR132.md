The Court of Appeal today dismissed Moody&rsquo;s Investors Service Hong Kong Limited&rsquo;s (Moody&rsquo;s) application for leave to appeal to the Court of Final Appeal in the matter of the Securities and Futures Commission&rsquo;s (SFC) disciplinary action in relation to a special comment report published by Moody&rsquo;s in 2011 (the Report) (Notes 1 & 2).


Moody&rsquo;s application was made after the Court of Appeal dismissed its appeal against the determination of the Securities and Futures Appeals Tribunal (SFAT) on 31 March 2016 which affirmed the SFC&rsquo;s decision to reprimand and fine Moody&rsquo;s for breaching the Code of Conduct in its preparation and publication of the Report (Note 3).  In dismissing Moody&rsquo;s appeal, the Court of Appeal said in its 8 June 2017 ruling that it agreed that misconduct on the part of Moody&rsquo;s can be established on the basis that the preparation and publication of the Report formed part and parcel of the carrying on of the business of credit ratings by Moody&rsquo;s (Notes 4 & 5).
