The Court of Appeal has dismissed an appeal by the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) against the Market Misconduct Tribunal&rsquo;s (MMT) findings that two former executives of Asia Telemedia Limited (ATML) (now known as Reorient Group Limited), Mr Yiu Hoi Ying and Ms Marian Wong Nam, had not engaged in insider dealing (Notes 1 & 2).


The SFC is reviewing the decision.


The SFC has also instituted parallel proceedings in the Court of First Instance under section 213 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance against ATML&rsquo;s former chairman, Mr Lu Ruifeng, for his alleged insider dealing in ATML shares (Note 3).
