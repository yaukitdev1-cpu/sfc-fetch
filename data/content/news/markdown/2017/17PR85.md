MSCI announced today that beginning in June 2018, it will include A shares in the MSCI Emerging Markets Index.


"The inclusion of A shares in the MSCI Emerging Markets Index signifies the growing importance of the A-share market to international investors, and will further strengthen Hong Kong&rsquo;s role as the gateway to access the Mainland stock market under the existing Stock Connect programme." said Mr Ashley Alder, the SFC&rsquo;s Chief Executive Officer. 


The SFC will work closely with the China Securities Regulatory Commission to ensure smooth implementation.
