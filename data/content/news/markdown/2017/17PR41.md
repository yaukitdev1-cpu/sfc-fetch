The Market Misconduct Tribunal (MMT) has handed down its decision that Mr Cheng Chak Ngok , former executive director, chief financial officer and company secretary of ENN Energy Holdings Limited, had not engaged in insider dealing in the shares of China Gas Holdings Limited in 2011 (Notes 1 & 2).


The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) is studying the report.


End


Notes:



- The MMT was chaired by Mr Garry Tallentire with two lay members, Mr Anthony Kam Chi Chiu and Mr Dickson Wong Kai Tat.
