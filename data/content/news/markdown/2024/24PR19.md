The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the appointment by the Financial Secretary of Dr Eric Yip as Executive Director of Intermediaries for a term of three years effective from 2 May 2024 (Note 1).


The SFC&rsquo;s Chief Executive Officer, Ms Julia Leung, said: &ldquo;Dr Yip brings to the SFC a wealth of market experience in equities, banking and private funds, having held senior roles at leading financial intermediaries in Hong Kong.&rdquo;


&ldquo;I am confident Dr Yip&rsquo;s skill sets and knowledge of the financial markets in both Hong Kong and the Mainland will go a long way towards advancing the SFC&rsquo;s regulatory work and agenda,&rdquo; Ms Leung added.
