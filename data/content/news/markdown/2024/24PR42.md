The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the appointment of its Chief Executive Officer, Ms Julia Leung, as the Chair of the Asia-Pacific Regional Committee (APRC) of the International Organization of Securities Commissions (IOSCO) (Notes 1 and 2).


Her two-year term will start on the date of the IOSCO Annual Meeting in May 2024.


Ms Leung said: &ldquo;The IOSCO APRC plays an important role in building strong working relationships and enhancing collaboration between securities regulators in Asia-Pacific, as well as in ensuring that the regional voice is heard globally.&rdquo;


&ldquo;It is a privilege for me and the SFC to lead the APRC to help address common issues which securities regulators face and strengthen the resilience of capital markets in the region,&rdquo; she added.

Notes:



- [IOSCO](https://www.iosco.org/) is the international body that brings together the world's securities regulators and is recognised as the global standard setter for the securities sector. Its membership regulates more than 95% of the world&rsquo;s securities markets in more than 130 jurisdictions.

- The APRC is one of four regional committees constituted by IOSCO to focus on regional issues relating to securities regulation. It consists of 21 jurisdictions in the Asia-Pacific region as well as nine non-voting members. Information about IOSCO APRC membership is available at [its website](https://www.iosco.org/about/?subsection=display_committee&cmtid=6).
