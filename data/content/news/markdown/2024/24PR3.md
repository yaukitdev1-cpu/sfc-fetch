The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has teamed up with Radio Television Hong Kong (RTHK) to produce a new series of &ldquo;SFC in Action&rdquo;, a law-enforcement TV drama premiering on RTHK TV 31 on 11 January (Note 1). 


The true-to-life TV drama is the fourth series since 2010 (Note 2) to illustrate to the public prevalent investment scams and other market misconduct, to help them to avoid falling victim to these scams. Consisting of four half-hour episodes in Cantonese, the series recounts the SFC&rsquo;s major enforcement cases in recent years, focusing on social media investment scams, ramp and dump schemes, insider dealing and market manipulation. 


&ldquo;Our market evolves with the times and technological advances, but the fundamental nature of investment scams and misconduct has not changed,&rdquo; said Ms Julia Leung, the SFC&rsquo;s Chief Executive Officer. &ldquo;We appreciate the opportunity to work with RTHK once again to produce this drama series to alert the public to act with caution when making investment decisions by revealing the modus operandi of wrongdoers in the financial markets today.&rdquo;


&ldquo;Through action-packed dramatisation of enforcement cases, the series delivers a clear message to the market and the public by showcasing the SFC&rsquo;s relentless pursuit of wrongdoers and their misconduct,&rdquo; said Mr Christopher Wilson, the SFC&rsquo;s Executive Director of Enforcement. &ldquo;By bringing these cases to life, we aim to bridge the gap between complex fraudulent schemes and the experiences of the public, in addition to sending a strong signal that fraudulent activities will not escape scrutiny.&rdquo;


After the premiere on RTHK TV 31, all four episodes can also be viewed on the SFC&rsquo;s [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLBZhfeSdO-00pA6cWsRwmVQqvhGMPGGxQ).
