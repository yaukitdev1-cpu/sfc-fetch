The Market Misconduct Tribunal (MMT) has handed down its decision that Mr Lo Hang Fong, a former Company Secretary of Warderly International Holdings Limited (Warderly), and Mr Luu Hung Viet Derrick, a lender and potential investor of Warderly, had not engaged in insider dealing in the shares of Warderly in 2007 (Notes 1 & 2).


The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) is studying the report (Note 3).
