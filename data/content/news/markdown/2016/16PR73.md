The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the appointment by the Financial Secretary of Ms Christina Choi as Executive Director of Investment Products for a three-year term effective from 1 August 2016 (Note 1).


"Ms Choi has acquired a wealth of experience and expertise in the regulation of investment products during her 11 years with the SFC and I look forward to continue working closely with her in her new role," the SFC&rsquo;s Chief Executive Officer, Mr Ashley Alder said.

- Ms Choi is currently Senior Director of Investment Products and was most recently appointed acting head of the Investment Products Division after Ms Julia Leung was appointed as Executive Director of the Intermediaries Division in June 2016.
