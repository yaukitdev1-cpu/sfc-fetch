The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the re-appointment by the Financial Secretary of Dr Kelvin Wong Tin-yau as a Non-Executive Director for a term of two years with effect from 20 October 2016 (Note 1).


"Dr Wong has provided the Board with valuable advice over the past few years and I look forward to continuing to work closely with him in his new term," said Mr Carlson Tong, the SFC&rsquo;s Chairman.
