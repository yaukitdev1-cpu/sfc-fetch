The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has banned Mr Ng Hongs, a former account executive of China Merchants Securities (HK) Co., Limited (CMSHK), from re-entering the industry for 10 months from 12 March 2016 to 11 January 2017 for breach of the SFC&rsquo;s Code of Conduct (Notes 1 & 2).


The SFC found that Ng obtained a written authorization from his client which authorized him to conduct trades on a discretionary basis in the client&rsquo;s account. He did not, however, obtain CMSHK&rsquo;s management approval before entering into such arrangement with the client and effected transactions for the client on a discretionary basis between August 2010 and September 2011 without CMSHK&rsquo;s knowledge.


The SFC considers that the client&rsquo;s interests were prejudiced as Ng&rsquo;s failure deprived the client from the firm&rsquo;s protection on discretionary account. A written authorization which was signed by the client without the firm&rsquo;s knowledge and approval did not offer the client any protection as the client&rsquo;s securities account was not designated as a discretionary account by the firm and the operation of the client&rsquo;s securities account could not be properly monitored and supervised by the firm.

Notes:



- Ng was licensed under the Securities and Futures Ordinance to carry on Type 1 (dealing in securities) and Type 2 (dealing in futures contracts) regulated activities and was accredited to China Merchants Securities (HK) Co., Limited and China Merchants Futures (HK) Co., Limited from 5 August 2010 to 25 February 2013. Ng is currently not licensed by the SFC.

- Paragraphs 7.1(c) and (d) of the Code of Conduct for Persons Licensed by or Registered with the Securities and Futures Commission.
