The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today relaunched its [*Enforcement Reporter* ](http://www.sfc.hk/web/EN/published-resources/industry-related-publications/enforcement-reporter.html)as a biannual publication which aims to provide the market with updates on the SFC&rsquo;s key enforcement priorities (Note 1).


The new issue published today focuses on corporate fraud and misfeasance as well as anti-money laundering internal controls.


 &ldquo;Listed companies-related fraud and misfeasance have wiped out billions in market capitalisation and caused significant damage to the integrity and reputation of our markets,&rdquo; said Mr Thomas Atkinson, the SFC&rsquo;s Executive Director of Enforcement. &ldquo;The SFC has formed a specialised team to focus on these cases.&rdquo;


In addition, the SFC will adopt a holistic approach to identify systemic weaknesses at the corporate group level and focus its enforcement actions on companies and corporate groups with multiple failings.


 &ldquo;The SFC values timely, open and transparent disclosure of misconduct. To encourage more cooperation, the SFC may notify parties under investigation of its intention to take enforcement action,&rdquo; Mr Atkinson added. &ldquo;In response, the parties and their legal advisers would have an opportunity to make submissions which may be on the record.&rdquo;


Other SFC enforcement priorities discussed in the newsletter include market misconduct in the Growth Enterprise Market and surveillance of untoward market behaviour in real time and retrospectively.


The [newsletter](http://www.sfc.hk/web/EN/published-resources/industry-related-publications/enforcement-reporter.html) is available on the [SFC website](http://www.sfc.hk/web/EN/index.html). The public may subscribe by filling out the form available under the  &ldquo;Subscribe&rdquo; link.
