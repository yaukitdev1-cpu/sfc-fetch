The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the strategic direction and key initiatives set out by the Chief Executive in Hong Kong’s first Five-Year Plan and the 2026 Policy Address.


The SFC fully supports the Government’s strategy to strengthen Hong Kong’s world-leading position as an international financial centre. In particular, the SFC supports measures to strengthen Hong Kong’s roles as a global offshore RMB business hub, an international asset and wealth-management centre, and an international risk-management centre; enhance the depth, breadth and international connectivity of its capital markets; and promote finance in support of the real economy and high-quality development.


The Five-Year-Plan and the Policy Address provide a coherent framework for Hong Kong to leverage its unique advantages under “One Country, Two Systems”, deepen connectivity with the Mainland China and the world, and strengthen its competitiveness in an evolving global financial landscape.


Recognising the significance of the Five-Year Plan and the 2026 Policy Address for Hong Kong’s future development, Dr Kelvin Wong, the SFC’s chairman, said: “The SFC Board will consider how the Commission can further support the implementation of the key initiatives set out in the Chief Executive’s blueprint. These include strengthening Hong Kong’s role as a global offshore RMB business hub and international asset and wealth management centre, as well as enhancing the depth, breath and competitiveness of Hong Kong’s capital markets.”
