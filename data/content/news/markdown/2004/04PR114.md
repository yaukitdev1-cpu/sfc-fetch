The SFC today publishes the June issue of *SFC Alert*, a bi-monthly newsletter for both investors and intermediaries.

On the front page, *Regulating for better markets - 15 Years of SFC* reports the financial position of the SFC in the financial year ended 31 March 2004.

*All about Stock Investing* reminds readers not to miss a new Cantonese television drama series jointly produced by the SFC and Radio Television Hong Kong, which helps investors learn more about investing in stocks.

Inside, in the regular *Senior management interview* column, Senior Director of Corporate Finance, Mr Brian Ho, explains the work of the team on takeovers, mergers and share repurchases, and reveals some ideas for a review of the takeovers code.

The SFC found that some institutional investors might have had traded on non-public information relating to share placements. *Investigations into share placements* urges market practitioners to abide by the law when conducting pre-placement consultation.

In May, the SFC signed a Letter of Intent with Indonesia’s Ministry of Finance Capital Markets Supervisory Agency. *Regulatory co-operation with Indonesia* has more information.

Another regular column, *Investor Focus*, advises investors how to choose a trustworthy financial adviser.

*Dual filing puts sponsors under the spotlight* highlights our concern about the quality of work of some IPO sponsors who might not have sufficient resources or expertise to fulfil the professional and regulatory obligations required of them.

The June issue of *SFC Alert* is the 24th issue of the newsletter, which was first published in October 2001. Copies of the current issue of *SFC Alert* are available at the SFC office. All issues are available on the SFC website.
