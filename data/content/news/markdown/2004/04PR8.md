The SFC today publishes the January issue of the *SFC Enforcement Reporter*, summarising the SFC’s enforcement action in the month of December 2003 and discussing the compliance issues highlighted by recent cases.

In December, the SFC successfully prosecuted five persons for unlicensed dealing, aiding or abetting such activities, and making unsolicited calls inducing other to enter into agreements for trading in commodity contracts.

The SFC disciplined seven intermediaries during the month for not ensuring the accuracy of FRR returns, internal control failings, distortion of price mechanism and breaching the "Know You Clients" rule.

Since 1 April 2003, the SFC has successfully prosecuted 47 entities and disciplined 65 licensees for various regulatory breaches.

Copies of the January issue of the *SFC* *Enforcement Reporter* are available at the SFC office. All issues are also available on the SFC website under "Press Releases, Publications & Speeches" – "Publications".
