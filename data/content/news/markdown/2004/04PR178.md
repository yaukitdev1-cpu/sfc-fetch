The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X1} and {X2} for cold calling.

An SFC investigation found that on various dates between April 2003 and January 2004, {X1} and {X2} had made unsolicited calls inducing others to enter into agreements for trading in futures contracts on various foreign currencies and commodities.

{X1} and {X2}, who were licensed representatives of Tanrich Futures Limited at the material time, pleaded guilty today before Mr Johnny Chan, a Magistrate at Eastern Magistracy, to three and two summonses respectively. {X1} was fined $7,500 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $8,158 to the SFC. {X2} was fined $5,000 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $8,363.
