The SFC today publishes the Consultation Conclusions on the Securities and Futures (Contracts Limits and Reportable Positions) (Amendment) Rules 2003 and proposed amendments to the Guidance Note on Position Limits and Large Open Position Reporting Requirements.

On 17 September 2003, the SFC issued a consultation document on the draft Amendment Rules and the proposed amendments to the Guidance Note for a one-month public consultation.

The Rules set limits on the number of futures or options that may be held and require a person with a reportable position to notify HKEx. Under the existing Rules, Exchange Participants may apply the limits separately to their own accounts and to each client’s position. But non-Exchange Participants must add their own positions and all their clients’ positions in applying the limits. Industry participants commented that this creates an unlevel playing field and practical difficulties for non-Exchange Participants.

To address market concerns, the Amendment Rules allow any person who holds or controls futures or stock options to apply the reporting requirements and the prescribed limits separately to his own account and to each of the other persons except where the person has discretion in respect of the futures or stock options contracts which he holds for the other persons.

The Guidance Note is also revised to reflect the Amendment Rules.

The SFC received seven responses to the consultation. Most respondents supported the Amendment Rules. A number of comments on the Guidance Note were made. The Consultation Conclusions paper provides an analysis of the submissions and explains the SFC’s position.

The Amendment Rules will be gazetted on 5 March 2004 and will commence on 30 April 2004, subject to negative vetting by the Legislative Council. The Guidance Note, which is made under section 399 of the SFO, will be gazetted at a date closer to the commencement of the Amendment Rules.

Copies of the Consultation Conclusions are available at the SFC office and on the SFC website, under "Bills, Legislation & Codes" – "Consultation Papers & Conclusions".
