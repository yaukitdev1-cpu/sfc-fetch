The SFC is inviting teachers to enrol in a new round of investor education workshops in the fifth year that the SFC has organised these successful courses.

Teachers workshops are one of the SFC’s key investor education initiatives to assist secondary school teachers of Economics, Accounting, Commerce and related subjects to understand more about different investment products and the latest market developments so as to facilitate their teaching work.

Following positive feedback from participating teachers and strong demand from those who missed the workshops in the past, the SFC will offer another round of workshops with expanded contents from 19 April to 14 May 2004.

There are a total of eight workshops covering different themes. A new intermediate module on *Stocks* focuses on the stock trading and settlement processes as well as consumerism in securities services. Participating teachers will have the opportunity to visit a brokerage to see how clients’ orders are received and executed.

Previous modules on *Options & Futures* and *Alternative Investments* have been expanded to comprise three sessions to allow more discussion and interactive exercises. The elementary and advanced modules on *Stocks* and *Funds* will continue to be available for teachers who have not attended the workshops before.

All participants will receive a teaching kit and SFC investor education materials to facilitate their discussion with students in class.

Since 2000, the SFC has organised 54 teachers workshops reaching more than 2,200 participants. A "Teachers Corner" has been set up on the SFC website to provide easy access to the PowerPoint presentation materials, case studies and exercises used in the workshops.

Invitation letters for enrolment have been sent to all secondary schools. Interested teachers can also visit the "Teachers Corner" to enrol or contact the Investor Education and Communications Department at 2842 7711.
