The SFC issues the following update on migration of licensees to the new regime under the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

As at 30 June 2004, around 12,000 licensees representing 70% of all those migrating had either migrated or lodged their migration applications.  More than 10,400 have already received their new licences.

The SFC wishes to remind those licensees who were registered under the former regime but have yet to submit migration applications, that if they fail to lodge their applications on or before 31 March 2005 (i.e. end of the two-year transitional period), their licences to conduct regulated activities will cease to have effect.  Therefore, to avoid business interruption and last minute rush, the licensees concerned should take immediate action preparing for a smooth migration. 

In that regard, the SFC would like to appeal to the 42 sole proprietors who were registered under the former regime but have not submitted their migration applications.  In particular, each of them is required to incorporate his business and look for at least one extra competent individual to become a responsible officer supervising the business (in addition to the sole proprietor himself) (Note 1).

For further information, please refer to the Guidelines on Transitional Arrangements and the sample of completed migration application forms available on the SFC website (Note 2).  Licensees are also welcome to contact the Licensing Department of the SFC if they have any enquiries concerning migration.

Under the new regime, only corporations may be licensed to carry on businesses in regulated activities as principal.  Furthermore, it is required that at least two individuals have to be approved by the SFC as responsible officers of a corporation in respect of each type of regulated activity the corporation is licensed for.
