The SFC today publishes the November issue of Dr Wise’s Column, [*A Day in Macau*](http://www.eirc.hk/eirc/html/EN/features/doctor/features_87_226_312.htm).

In this issue, Dr Wise discusses the recent interest in concept stocks and reminds investors to understand what they are buying, the risks involved and know their limits.

Experience of the H shares and tech bubbles shows that whatever the concept is, it is important for a company to turn new ideas and business plans into revenues and profits.  Dr Wise urges an investor to consider the company’s actual business plan and strategies, current status of the project, its competitive edge and the management’s proven records in those areas, as well as the assumptions for revenue and profit estimates.
