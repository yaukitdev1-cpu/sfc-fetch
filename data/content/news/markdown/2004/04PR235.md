The SFC announces that it plans to conduct a theme inspection on sponsors after the first quarter of 2005.**   ** 

Sponsors are a type of corporate finance advisers subject to the SFC Corporate Finance Adviser Code of Conduct (SFC Code).  The SFC code imposes upon a corporate finance adviser, among other things, a duty to exercise due care and skill and to keep books and records in relation to the advisory work it has undertaken.  More specifically, corporate finance advisers that engage in corporate finance advisory work under the Listing Rules are required by the SFC Code to observe specific requirements under the Listing Rules.

In a joint press release of the SFC and SEHK issued on 19 October 2004, SEHK announced amendments to the Listing Rules relating to the regulation of sponsors and independent financial advisers.   The rule amendments make clear, among other things, the role and responsibilities of sponsors, including the due diligence SEHK expects the sponsors to typically perform on new applicants for listing.  Sponsors are urged to put in place effective systems and procedures to ensure compliance with the rule amendments which will come into effect on 1 January 2005.

The SFC, as the statutory regulator of sponsors licensed with the SFC, will perform a theme inspection on sponsors to review their compliance with the SFC Code, with particular reference to the due diligence practice note recently issued by the SEHK.

Mrs Alexa Lam, SFC’s Executive Director of Intermediaries and Investment Products, said: “In an IPO process, sponsors play the role of a gate-keeper.  They have the overriding duty to conduct proper due diligence.  The SFC will take disciplinary actions against the sponsors who fail in their duties.   Penalties of an SFC disciplinary action include imposition of fines, suspension and revocation of licence.”

A sponsor is a financial intermediary that assists an issuer (being the company to be listed) with its listing application.  According to the rules of SEHK, each new listing applicant must appoint a sponsor to assist it with the application process. More than 680 firms are licensed or deemed to be licensed by the SFC to conduct Type 6 (advising on corporate finance) regulated activity.  However, not all of them act as sponsors. According to Stock Exchange published information on its website, 57 firms (including licensed corporations and registered institutions) have been involved as sponsors in the listing on SEHK since January 2003.
