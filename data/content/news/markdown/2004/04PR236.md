The SFC announces that the prospectus-related provisions in Schedule 1 and Part 1 of Schedule 4 of the Companies (Amendment) Ordinance 2004 commence today.  

The principal purposes of Schedule 1 are to:

- specify the types of offers which can be made outside the prospectus regime;
- widen the SFC’s powers to grant exemptions under sections 38A/342A of the Companies Ordinance and require the SFC to publish particulars of exemptions granted;
- establish a regime for advertisements relating to prospectuses;
- specify how a prospectus consisting of one document may be amended and how a prospectus may consist of more than one document;
- specify that, for the purposes of prospectus civil and criminal liability, an untrue statement in relation to a prospectus includes a material omission;
- specify that the “snapshot” of a company provided by a prospectus should take into account the nature of the shares or debentures being offered and the nature of the company, and the nature of persons likely to consider acquiring them.
