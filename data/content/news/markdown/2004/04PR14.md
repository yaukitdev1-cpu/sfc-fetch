The SFC has successfully prosecuted Mr Tang Kin Lok for failing to comply with notices issued by SFC&rsquo;s investigators requiring attendance at interviews.

Two notices under Section 33(4) of the Securities and Futures Commission Ordinance and section 183(c) of Securities and Futures Ordinance were issued to Tang requiring him to attend interviews with investigators in connection with two investigations into market manipulation. However, Tang without reasonable excuse did not show up.

Today, Tang was convicted after trial before Magistrate Mr Shum Siu Man at Kowloon City Magistracy. Tang was remanded in custody until 10 February 2004 for sentencing, pending background report.
