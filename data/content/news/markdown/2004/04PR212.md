The SFC announces today the publication of the consultation conclusions on the proposed repeal of two prospectus class exemptions made under section 38A(2)/342A(2) of the Companies Ordinance and the gazettal of the Companies Ordinance (Exemption of Companies and Prospectuses from Compliance with Provisions) (Amendment) Notice 2004 (**Amendment Notice**) and the Securities and Futures (Price Stabilizing) (Amendment) Rules 2004 (**PS** **Amendment Rules**).

On 17 August 2004, the SFC issued a consultation paper on the proposed repeal of sections 3 and 7 of the Companies Ordinance (Exemption of Companies and Prospectuses from Compliance with Provisions) Notice (**Exemption Notice**) (Note 1). Four submissions were received before the close of the consultation period on 17 September 2004, all of which expressed support for the proposal. The consultation conclusions confirm that it is appropriate to proceed with the repeal, which is being implemented by publication of the Amendment Notice.

The PS Amendment Rules will amend section 3(d)(ii)(A)(II) of the Securities and Futures (Price Stabilizing) Rules (**PS Rules**) by substituting the reference to section 3 of the Exemption Notice with a reference to section 1 of Part 1 of the new Seventeenth Schedule to the Companies Ordinance. This technical amendment arises from, and is consequential to, the proposed repeal of section 3 of the Exemption Notice and involves no change of substance or principle to the PS Rules (Note 2). Accordingly, public consultation is not considered necessary.

The Amendment Notice and the PS Amendment Rules were gazetted today and are available in Legal Supplement No. 2 to the Government Gazette as L.N. 181 and 180 of 2004, which can be viewed on the Government’s website at [www.info.gov.hk/pd/egazette/](http://www.gld.gov.hk/cgi-bin/gld/egazette/ListDetailb.cgi?lang=e&year=2004&month=11&day=12&vol=8&no=46&header=0&acurrentpage=10&agree=1&gaz_type=ls2&pid=). Both the Amendment Notice and the PS Amendment Rules will, subject to negative vetting by the Legislative Council, come into operation on 7 January 2005 (Note 3).

Copies of the consultation conclusions are available at the SFC office and on the [SFC website at www.sfc.hk](/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/consultation/conclusion?refNo=04CP2).

Notes to Editor:

1. Section 3 of the Exemption Notice exempts Hong Kong-incorporated companies and their prospectuses from compliance with the content, dual Chinese/English language and maximum offer duration requirements of sections 38(1) and (3) and 44A(2) of the Companies Ordinance when making an offer of shares or debentures to *"persons whose ordinary business is to buy or sell shares or debentures whether as principal or agent*".

Section 7 of the Exemption Notice exempts Hong Kong and overseas-incorporated companies which are also collective investment schemes authorised by the SFC under section 104(1) of the Securities and Futures Ordinance from compliance with the content and dual language requirements (in the case of Hong Kong–incorporated companies) in section 38(1) and (3) and (in the case of overseas-incorporated companies) in section 342(1) and (3) of the Companies Ordinance.

Schedule 1 of the Companies (Amendment) Ordinance 2004 (**Amendment Ordinance**) provides for 12 categories of offers that shall be exempt from the prospectus regime. Two such exempt offers, namely offers to "professional investors" and offers by corporate entities which are also collective investment schemes authorized by the SFC, are already the subject of class exemptions contained in sections 3 and 7 of the Exemption Notice. The relevant class exemptions will be superseded and therefore become redundant upon the commencement of Schedule 1 of the Amendment Ordinance.

2. The effect of the technical amendment to the PS Rules will be that such rules will apply to secondary market offerings to professional investors of the same type as are referred to in the Securities and Futures Ordinance and the Companies (Amendment) Ordinance 2004.

The commencement notice in respect of Schedule 1 and Schedule 4 Part 1 of the Amendment Ordinance was gazetted on 8 October 2004 and will, subject to negative vetting by the Legislative Council, come into operation on 3 December 2004. Although there will be a time lag between the commencement of Schedule 1 of the Amendment Ordinance and the commencement of the Amendment Notice and the PS Amendment Rules, no adverse impact is expected as the market will in the interim be able to continue to rely on the existing class exemption in section 3 of the Exemption Notice.
