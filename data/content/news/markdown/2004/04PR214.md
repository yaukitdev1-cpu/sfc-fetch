The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X} of TradingGuru.com Securities Limited, for aiding and abetting Mr Cheung Tak Shun Dickson to conduct unregistered dealing (Note 1).

An SFC investigation revealed that between 5 March 2002 and 7 January 2003, {X}, who at that time was a responsible officer of TradingGuru.com and the supervisor of Cheung, aided and abetted Cheung to introduce clients to TradingGuru.com, take clients’ orders and confirm the executed trades with clients, knowing that Cheung did not have any registration with the SFC.

{X} today pleaded guilty to one summons before Mr Anthony Yuen at Eastern Magistracy. {X} was fined $2,500 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $10,700 to the SFC.

1. Cheung was earlier prosecuted for unregistered dealing and pleaded guilty. Please see [SFC press release dated 28 October 200](http://www.sfc.hk/sfcPressRelease/EN/sfcOpenDocServlet?docno=04PR200)4 for details.
