The SFC today releases its *Quarterly Report* for the financial period 1 July to 30 September 2004 (Note 1).

Surplus recorded

In the second quarter of 2004-2005, the SFC recorded a surplus of $33.1 million. The accumulated surplus for first six months of the financial year was $77.3 million, compared with a budgeted surplus of $1.3 million.

Total revenue for the six months was $280.2 million, 27% higher than that in the same period last year due to increased market turnover and activities. Total expenditure (including depreciation) was $202.9 million, almost the same as last year’s.

At the end of September, the reserves stood at $768.3 million, equivalent to 1.7 times the approved annual operating expenditure (including depreciation) of $443.4 million.

A surplus is expected in the quarter ending 31 December 2004, based on current information.

Work progress

Since the last Quarterly Report, the SFC has:


- continued to work on a consultation paper on the overall review of the disclosure of interests regime under Part XV of the Securities and Futures Ordinance, which would be released shortly;
- published a consultation paper on proposed measures to strengthen the regulatory framework for brokers involved in securities margin financing;
- released the joint consultation conclusions with HKEx on the regulation of sponsors and independent financial advisers;
- published new guidelines to address analyst conflicts of interest;
- published the findings of a Review of the Financial Performance of Category C Stock Exchange Participants since 2001;
- worked with Mainland authorities on the details of allowing SFC licensees who satisfy the CSRC requirements to set up joint venture futures brokerages in the Mainland under CEPA II;
- worked on the drafting of guidelines to regulate overseas property investments by SFC-authorised REITs;
- consulted on the proposed repeal of certain class exemptions made under the Companies Ordinance;
- approved HKEx rule amendments to enable the introduction of new features to its Investor Participant account service;
- prosecuted 23 people and companies, and disciplined 21 licensees for regulatory breaches;
- settled disciplinary proceedings against an investment adviser by severely reprimanding the firm, which offered ex-gratia payments to investors;
- published *Disciplinary Proceedings at a Glance* to explain the SFC’s disciplinary process and the procedural rights of persons who are subject to disciplinary proceedings;
- partnered with RTHK and The Salvation Army to tailor-make an investor education radio programme for senior citizens;
- worked with Lingnan University to offer students a course on foundations of investing.
