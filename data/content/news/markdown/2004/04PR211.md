The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X1} for advising on securities whilst unlicensed, and {X2} (Note 1) for aiding and abetting {X1} in the issuance of advertisement without authorisation from the SFC.
                                         

An SFC investigation revealed that on 29 April 2003, {X1}, who was not licensed by the SFC, gave investment advice to a client of TG Holborn, in contravention of section 114(3) of the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO). On the same occasion, {X2} aided and abetted {X1} in issuing marketing material of an unauthorised instrument to the client, in contravention of section 103(1) of the SFO.

{X1} and {X2} today pleaded guilty to one summons each before Mr Anthony Yuen at Eastern Magistracy. {X1} and {X2} were fined $3,000 and $5,000 respectively and ordered to pay investigation costs totalling $25,201 to the SFC.
