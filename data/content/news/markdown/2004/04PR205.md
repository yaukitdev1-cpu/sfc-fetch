The SFC has produced a new television documentary-drama series on wealth management in light of increasing investors’ demand for personal financial planning and the proliferation of complex investment products.

*Foundations in Wealth Management* comprises 10 six-minute episodes discussing investment advisory services and key features of various niche products, such as bonds, real estate investment trusts, hedge funds, structured products and investment-linked assurance schemes (Note 1).

Launching the television programme today, Mr Peter Au-Yang, SFC’s Executive Director and Chief Operating Officer, said: "There is growing demand for investment advisory services. Investors have to take charge of their own interests based on their own circumstances. It is important for investors to choose an appropriate investment adviser, understand the recommended products, and consider carefully whether an investment is suitable for them by asking the right questions."

"This television programme marks the debut of our awareness campaign on the engagement of investment advisory services. We will make use of different channels to get our messages across." Mr Au-Yang added.

The Cantonese version of the series will be available in VCD for free distribution to the public in December. At the same time, both the Cantonese version and a version with English dubbing will be web cast on the SFC-operated Electronic Investor Resources Centre at www.eirc.hk.

The public can give their views of the programme to the SFC by calling the Investor Hotline at 2840 9333 or sending an email to investor.info@sfc.hk.
