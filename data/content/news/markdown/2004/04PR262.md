The SFC is pleased to announce the appointment of Mr Eddy Fong to the Takeovers and Mergers Panel and the Takeovers Appeal Committee for the period from 1 January 2005 to 31 March 2005, the end of the current term*.* 

Mr Fong succeeds Mr Thomas Brian Stevenson, who will step down from the Panel and Appeal Committee with effect from 1 January 2005 following expiry of his appointment as an independent Non-Executive Director of the SFC.

The SFC would like to thank Mr Stevenson for his valuable contribution to the Panel and the Appeal Committee during his term of appointment.

The Panel and Appeal Committee are voluntary bodies which owe their success to the dedication and expertise of their members. The SFC is confident that the significant experience of members in takeovers and mergers and in the securities and financial markets will ensure the continued success of the Panel and the Appeal Committee.
