The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X} for manipulating the shares of Tradeeasy Holdings Limited (Note 1).

An SFC investigation found that shortly before market close on 18 October 2002, {X} had placed through his wife’s trading account a series of purchase orders to buy Tradeeasy shares at prices higher than the then prevailing market price. {X}’s orders caused the closing price of Tradeeasy shares to rise by 30%, thereby misleading the market to believe that the stock price was going up.

{X} pleaded guilty today to one summons before Mr Anthony Yuen, a Magistrate at Eastern Magistracy, for intentionally creating, causing to be created or carrying out an act or acts with the intention of creating a false market, contrary to sections 135(1)(b), 135(2)(b) or (c) and 139 of the Securities Ordinance. {X} was fined $5,000 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $44,390 to the SFC.

Notes:

1. {X} was a dealer’s representative at the material time. He has left the industry.
