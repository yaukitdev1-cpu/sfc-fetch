The Chairmen’s Task Force of the Technical Committee of the International Organization of Securities Commissions (IOSCO) has published a Consultation Report on *Code of Conduct Fundamentals for Credit Rating Agencies.*

In September 2003, the Technical Committee issued a Statement of Principles Regarding the Activities of Credit Rating Agencies (CRAs). These Principles laid out high-level objectives that CRAs, regulators, issuers and other market participants should strive toward in order to protect the integrity and analytical independence of the credit rating process (Note 1). The Consultation Report follows on the Principles by offering more specific and detailed guidance to CRAs on how the objectives of the CRA Principles can be achieved in practice.

In seeking public comment, the Chairmen’s Task Force is particularly interested in views of how the provisions contained within the *CRA Code of Conduct Fundamentals* advance the goals of investor protection, fairness, efficiency and transparency in securities markets, and the reduction of systemic risk.

The Consultation Report is available on the IOSCO website ([www.iosco.org](http://www.iosco.org/pubdocs/pdf/IOSCOPD173.pdf)). The public are invited to submit comments to the IOSCO by 8 November 2004. Instructions regarding the submission of comments are set out in the IOSCO press release, which is [attached](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/general/general/press_release/04/04pr183_attach.pdf).

The SFC actively participates in the Chairmen’s Task Force. The SFC welcomes the consultation and encourages the public, including issuers and investors, to submit their views.
