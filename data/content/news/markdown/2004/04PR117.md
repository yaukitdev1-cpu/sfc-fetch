The SFC today has issued a circular to licensed corporations to advise them how to protect investors and themselves from fake websites.

The SFC has from time to time received reports from licensed corporations about websites that imitate their websites or make unauthorised references to them (including the provision of links to their websites) in an apparent attempt to defraud investors.

The circular suggests that licensed corporations, when they discover such fake websites, adopt procedures to help protect investors from falling a prey to these scams and safeguard integrity of their business and that of the market. These procedures include reporting the incident to the SFC and to the Police as soon as possible, and alerting the investing public of such websites by effective means such as a press release, a newspaper announcement, or an announcement on the corporation’s website.

The circular is available on the SFC website under the section of *Bills, Legislation and Codes - Codes, Guidelines and Circulars*.

The SFC also maintains an Alert List of unlicensed overseas companies and suspected scam websites, including fake websites. As at today, there are eight fake websites on our list. We have also issued educational material for retail investors explaining the tactics used by fake websites and how to guard against them. The List and the educational material are available on the SFC website under the section of *Advice to Investors* – *Investor Alert*.
