The SFC announces the following in relation to the Link REIT to all investors in respect of an application to the High Court for judicial review against the Housing Authority’s divestment plan:

1. The Joint Global Co-ordinators (JGCs) are required to report to the SFC on a regular basis, twice every day from the commencement of the public offering until the completion of all procedures relating to the IPO of the Link REIT including the refund of cheques and dispatch of unit certificates.

2. In addition, the JGCs are required to report immediately on any exigent matters.

3. The JGCs have been required to submit an impact assessment in respect of the judicial review application and further announcements will be made as and when appropriate.

4. In the event the listing does not proceed in accordance with the manner disclosed in the Offering Circular for any reasons, the Link REIT is required to conduct an orderly refund of application monies to subscribers. This is specified in the Offering Circular.
