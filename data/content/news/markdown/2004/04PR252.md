The SFC today publishes the December issue of the [*SFC Enforcement Reporter*](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/enforcement/dec_04.pdf), summarising the SFC’s enforcement action in the month of November 2004 and discussing the compliance issues highlighted by recent cases. 

In November, the SFC successfully prosecuted two companies and seven people for market manipulation, unlicensed investment advising, aiding and abetting the issuance of unauthorised advertisements and the conduct of unlicensed dealing, and breaches of the disclosure of interests law.

The SFC disciplined eight licensees during the month.  One of them was banned for life from the securities industry due to poor financial integrity and dishonesty. In other cases, the licensees were disciplined for breaching the Financial Resources Rules and late reporting of the breach, publishing false and misleading advertisements, aiding and abetting unlicensed dealing, violating the know-your-clients requirement and internal control failings.

In November, the SFC also settled disciplinary action against a licensee who allowed a former dealer’s representative of another brokerage to conduct trading at the licensee’s firm without employer’s prior written consent. In settlement of a disciplinary action, the licensee agreed to pay $40,000 into the Government revenue.

Since 1 April 2004, the SFC has successfully prosecuted 60 entities. We have withdrawn summonses issued against eight entities resulting in their acquittals and a further five entities were acquitted after trial. The SFC has also disciplined 44 licensees for various regulatory breaches and entered into settlements with voluntary payments with three licensees. Disciplinary proceedings have been taken against 12 licensees, which were concluded with no formal sanction imposed, although 10 of them received private warnings. Such proceedings were also commenced and discontinued against four deemed licensees who left their firms before the conclusion of the action.
