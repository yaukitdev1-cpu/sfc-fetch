The SFC today publishes the April issue of the *SFC Enforcement Reporter*, summarising the SFC's enforcement action in the month of March 2004 and discussing the compliance issues highlighted by recent cases. 

In March, the SFC successfully prosecuted two companies and five people for unlicensed dealing, cold calling clients, and failure to disclose Financial Resources Rules deficiencies and securities interests on time.  

The SFC disciplined one licensee during the month for mishandling client accounts.

We have continued to publish figures on acquittals and disciplinary proceedings that have been concluded without sanction. Since 1 April 2003, the SFC has successfully prosecuted 60 entities. There have been no acquittals. The SFC has also disciplined 78 licensees for regulatory breaches. Disciplinary proceedings have been taken against 18 licensees, which were eventually discontinued with no formal sanction imposed. Such proceedings were also commenced and discontinued against another eight deemed licensees who left their firms before the conclusion of the actions.

Copies of the April issue of the [*SFC Enforcement Reporter*](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/enforcement/apr_04.pdf) are available at the SFC office. All issues are also available on the SFC website under "Press Releases, Publications & Speeches" – "Publications".
