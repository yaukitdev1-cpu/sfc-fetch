The SFC today publishes the Spring 2004 issue (No. 55) of the *SFC Quarterly Bulletin*.

The Bulletin contains an article entitled *Two-year Comparison of Intermediaries’ Business Activities (2001 – 2002)*.  It provides an update of the business profile of the intermediaries concerned regarding their business performance in 2002.  The article is a continuation of the three papers published last year, which gave a six-year comparison (1996 – 2001) of the business activities of securities dealers, commodity dealers and advisers before the commencement of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

Another article, *Profile of the Derivative Warrants Market in Hong Kong,* outlines the present major features of the derivative warrants market.  This is the first paper of a special research paper series which aims at providing information and analysis of exchange-traded securities and derivatives markets in Hong Kong.  The special research paper series is available on the SFC website at http: //www.hksfc.org.hk/eng/statistics/html/researches.htm.

In *The Implications of CEPA for the Hong Kong Securities Industry*, Mr Matthew Harrison of HKEx discusses the implications of CEPA for the securities industry and the long-term strategic outlook for the industry as the Mainland economy opens further.

*Trading Activities of Stocks on HKEx and Overseas Exchanges* analyses the trading activities of stocks currently traded on HKEx and some overseas markets during 2000-2003.

Another article, *FinNet – The Financial Network for Hong Kong,* introduces FinNet and the services provided by it and its users.  The article also discusses the benefits of using FinNet over the Internet.

The market update, *Narrowing of Premiums of A-shares over H-shares*, analyses the narrowing price differences between the A-shares and their H-share counterparts during 2000-2003.  It also reviews the performance of the Hong Kong and regional stock markets in the first quarter of 2004.

The Bulletin can be subscribed at $400 per year or downloaded from the SFC website under “Press Releases, Publications & Speeches” – “Publications”.  The public are welcome to sign up the SFC Website Update Email Alert service for the notification of new releases of the Bulletin.
