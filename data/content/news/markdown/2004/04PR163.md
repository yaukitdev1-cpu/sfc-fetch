The SFC today publishes the September issue of the *SFC Enforcement Reporter*, summarising the SFC’s enforcement action in the month of August 2004 and discussing the compliance issues highlighted by recent cases. 

In August, the SFC successfully prosecuted two people for illegal short selling and breaching disclosure of interests laws.

The SFC disciplined two licensees during the month. One of them breached the  Financial Resources Rules. The SFC settled its disciplinary proceedings against the other licensee, an investment adviser, by severely reprimanding it for a number of alleged failings in relation to the selling of two unauthorised hedge funds. Without any admission of liability, the licensee agreed to make ex-gratia payments to clients. The SFC regards the settlement where it reduced the penalty for the licensee in return for significant ex-gratia payments to investors to be in the public interest.

Since 1 April 2004, the SFC has successfully prosecuted 22 entities. We have withdrawn summonses issued against five persons resulting in their acquittals and a further three persons were acquitted after trial. The SFC has also disciplined 27 licensees for various regulatory breaches and entered into settlements with voluntary payments with two licensees. Disciplinary proceedings have been taken against six licensees, which were concluded with no formal sanction imposed, although five of them received private warnings. Such proceedings were also commenced and discontinued against one deemed licensee who left his firm before the conclusion of the action.
