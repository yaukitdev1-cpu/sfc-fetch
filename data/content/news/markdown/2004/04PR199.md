The SFC has successfully prosecuted Miss Tsui Muk Lan for her failure to make proper disclosures of interests.

Tsui today pleaded guilty to nine summonses in relation to her failure to make proper disclosures to Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited and First Asia Capital Investment Limited of the decrease of her notifiable interests in First Asia on 26 November 2003, and increase of her notifiable interests in First Asia on 19, 24 and 30 December 2003 and 7 January 2004.

Mr Timothy Casewell, a Magistrate at Eastern Magistracy, fined Tsui $18,000 and ordered her to pay investigation costs of $16,033 to the SFC.
