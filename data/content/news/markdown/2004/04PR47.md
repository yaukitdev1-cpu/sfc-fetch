The SFC announces the gazettal today of the Securities and Futures (Contracts Limits and Reportable Positions) (Amendment) Rules 2004, which allow a person who holds or controls futures contracts or stock options contracts for other persons to apply the large open position reporting requirements and the prescribed limits separately to his own positions and to each of the positions of the other persons.

Subject to negative vetting by LegCo, the Rules will come into effect on 30 April 2004. 

The Gazette is available at the Government Information Centre offices, and accessible via the [website of the Government Printer](http://www.gld.gov.hk/cgi-bin/gld/egazette/gazettefiles.cgi?lang=e&year=2004&month=3&day=5&vol=08&no=10&gn=35&header=1&part=0&df=1&nt=s2&newfile=1&acurrentpage=12&agree=1&gaz_type=ls2) and the SFC website, under "Bills, Legislation and Codes" – "Securities and Futures Ordinance" – "Subsidiary Legislation under the Securities and Futures Ordinance".
