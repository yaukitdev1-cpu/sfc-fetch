The Court today sentenced Mr Chan Wing Chuen Freeman, director of CSF Consultancy Limited, to perform community services for breaching the Protection of Investors Ordinance (Note 1).

Magistrate Mr Sham Siu Man at Kowloon City Magistracy sentenced Chan to perform 80 hours of community services within one year for the issuance of an unauthorised invitation on the internet website of CSF, contrary to the Protection of Investors Ordinance.

Ends

Notes to the Editor:

1. On 7 May 2004, Chan was found guilty in relation to two summonses for breaches of the Securities Ordinance and one summons under the Protection of Investors Ordinance. He was fined $10,000 for breaches of the Securities Ordinance. Sentence on the outstanding summons was adjourned to today.
