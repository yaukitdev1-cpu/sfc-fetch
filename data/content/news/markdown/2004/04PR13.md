SFC Chairman and Executive Directors reviewed the work of the SFC in 2003 and discussed its major tasks this year at a press briefing today.

Last year saw the successful commencement of the Securities and Futures Ordinance. The new regulatory framework has enabled the SFC to better protect the interest of investors, as evidenced by among other things the tougher penalties imposed on intermediaries for misconduct. The Dual Filing regime has been welcomed by the market, while the Single Licensing Regime has operated smoothly.

The SFC also made significant progress in keeping up the high standards of brokers, financial advisers and analysts. Various measures were taken to facilitate development of the financial market infrastructure, fund management industry and offering of securities. More disciplinary cases were concluded, more persons were prosecuted, and more company inspections were initiated.

In 2004, the SFC will continue with the projects in the major areas of upkeeping market standards, facilitating market development, and enforcing the law - with the aim of transforming the Hong Kong market into a market of higher quality.

In addition, the SFC considers investor education as being vital to the healthy development of the market and will introduce an awareness campaign on pooling risk this year.
