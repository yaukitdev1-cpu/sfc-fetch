The SFC today issues the September issue of the Dr Wise’s Column, [*Why Not Consider Investor Participant Accounts?*](http://www.eirc.hk/eirc/html/EN/features/doctor/features_87_226_309.htm) 

In this issue, Dr Wise talks about the investor participant accounts service in the Central Clearing and Settlement System (CCASS).  Apart from keeping shares at brokerages or holding share certificates by themselves, investors have the option to put their shares in the investor participant accounts which are direct custody accounts that provide investors with the convenience of electronic book-entry settlement and full control of their shares.

Recently, the Hong Kong Securities Clearing Company Limited has implemented several enhanced features for the investor participant accounts service. For instance, investors can now access their accounts almost round-the-clock and with the option of a self-assigned user ID and password.  Stock transfers that do not involve monetary settlement have been simplified.  In addition, investors will receive SMS alert messages whenever there are activities in their accounts pending their actions.

Dr Wise is a fictitious character.  He represents the collective wisdom and knowledge of market specialists and experts at the SFC.  Dr Wise, through his column, talks to investors about the key issues of investing and regulatory thoughts.
