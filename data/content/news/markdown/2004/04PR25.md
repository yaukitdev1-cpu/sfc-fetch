The Court has for the first time sentenced a person to imprisonment for not attending SFC investigation interviews.

Mr Sham Siu Man, a Magistrate at Kowloon City Magistracy, today sentenced Mr Tang Kin Lok, Eddie to two weeks’ imprisonment for each of two summonses, to be run concurrently. Tang was ordered to pay $2,248 investigation costs. Tang was convicted after trial on 27 January 2004 and had been remanded in custody until today for sentencing.

Meanwhile, Mr Ian Candy, Principal Magistrate at Eastern Magistracy, sentenced {X} to 80 hours of community service work and to pay $500 investigation costs, also for failing to attend SFC interviews. {X} pleaded guilty on 20 January 2004. Sentencing was adjourned until today for probation and community services order reports.
