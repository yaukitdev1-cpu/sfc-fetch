The SFC today launches its new corporate website and a revamped Electronic Investor Resources Centre (eIRC), with enriched contents and enhanced features. Market practitioners and the public can obtain regulatory information and investor advice with ease.
The URLs of the SFC website and eIRC have also been changed to www.sfc.hk (previously www.hksfc.org.hk) and www.eirc.hk (previously www.hkeirc.org) respectively.

SFC Website (www.sfc.hk)

Contents are updated and presented in nine key sections on the top of the new website. The central area on the default homepage offers information that is most commonly sought by users. Dedicated "Forms" and "FAQs" sections provide one-stop-shops for market practitioners to download various application and regulatory forms and to get answers to frequently asked questions.

In addition, the simple design, with minimal use of graphics and flash images, further speeds up the downloading and response time. New functions and features are built in to offer convenience:


- ***Useful Information for Seven Specific User Groups*** – Information that is of particular interest to investors, intermediaries, compliance officers, issuers, media, academics and regulators is presented on tailor-made web pages for their easy reference. For example, brokers can select "Intermediaries" to directly access SFC’s codes and guidelines, and forms for filing financial returns. Journalists can select "Media" to view SFC press releases, publications and upcoming prosecutions. Links to useful sections and websites are also provided.
- ***My Briefcase*** – Users can store their most frequently visited SFC web pages in a virtual personal briefcase for quick retrieval the next time they visit the SFC website.
- ***Enhanced Search Function*** – Users can specify the sections/sub-sections in which the search is performed. They can refine the search by searching within the results returned by the search engine.
- ***Email a Friend*** – Users can recommend SFC web pages to and share information with a friend or colleague with this function.
- ***Printer Friendly Page*** – An SFC web page will be reformatted to fit on A-4 pages for trouble-free printing.
- ***Instant Language Switching*** – Users can switch between English, Traditional Chinese and Simplified Chinese with a click of the mouse.

Electronic Investor Resources Centre (www.eirc.hk)

All investor education materials have been relocated from the SFC website to the eIRC. The investor portal now features comprehensive investor education and alert materials and a virtual library.

There are new sections and functions to make the eIRC more attractive and informative:


- ***New sections*** – "Education News" provides a calendar of the SFC’s investor education initiatives and events. "Features" includes the Dr Wise’s column. "Investor Alert", "Publications" and "Teachers Corner", previously on the SFC website, now reside with the eIRC.
- ***New functions*** – Similar to the SFC website, they include Email a Friend, Printer Friendly Page, and the language switch.

The "Studio" section continues to showcase the SFC’s investor education video and sound clips while the "Game Zone" makes use of interactive games and quizzes to enhance investors’ knowledge about the markets and investment products. Our "eLibrary" now contains over 720 hyperlinks to around 470 websites relating to investor education and investment knowledge.

Advice to Users

Users of the websites should note the following:


- **Update Alert Subscription remains valid** – Current subscribers of our Website Update Email Alert service need not subscribe again on the new websites. The SFC will continue to email latest website updates to subscribers.
- **Deep links / bookmarks void** – Due to the overhaul of website information architecture, previously created deep links to / bookmarks of specific web pages (other than the homepages of SFC website and the eIRC) have become invalid.

Mr Peter Au-Yang, Executive Director and Chief Operating Officer, said: "The SFC website and the eIRC were revamped in 2001 and 2002 respectively. Since then, we have continuously updated information and added new features. Taking into account our users’ feedback in a recent online survey, we have re-organised and re-designed the two sites to make them more user friendly."

The SFC welcomes comments from the public, which will help us improve the new websites continuously. Comments can be sent to [enquiry@sfc.hk](mailto:enquiry@sfc.hk).
