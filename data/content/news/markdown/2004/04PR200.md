The SFC has successfully prosecuted{X} for acting as a dealer’s representative of TradingGuru.com Securities Limited whilst unregistered.

Between 5 March 2002 and 7 January 2003, without having any registration with the SFC, {X} introduced clients to TradingGuru.com, took clients’ orders and confirmed the executed trades with clients, in contravention of the Securities Ordinance.

{X} today pleaded guilty to one summons before Mr Ian Candy at Eastern Magistracy. He was fined $2,500 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $5,000 to the SFC.
