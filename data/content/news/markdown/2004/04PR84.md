The SFC today publishes its Annual Report for the year ended 31 March 2004. The Annual Report is today laid before the LegCo following submission to the Financial Secretary.

At the same time, the third edition of the SFC’s English-Chinese Glossary of Securities, Futures and Financial Terms is also published to commemorate the SFC’s 15th Anniversary.

Annual Report 2003-2004

The theme of the Annual Report 2003 – 2004 is *Regulating for Better Markets – 15 Years of SFC*. There is a Chinese saying that it takes 10 years to grow a tree and 100 years to grow a man. An institution is like a tree and 15 years is just a short time for an institution like the SFC, which was established on 1 May 1989. In the years to come, the SFC will continue to regulate so we have better markets in Hong Kong

The Annual Report gives an account of the SFC’s finances. In 2003-2004, total revenue of the SFC increased to $551 million, 63% higher than the previous year mainly owing to improved market turnover which lifted levy income. We continued the cost control measures implemented since the second half of 2001. Total operating expenditure after depreciation edged up by 7% to $426 million, mainly because of one-off office relocation expenditure and additional staff costs for new functions. Excluding these expenditures, the increase was 2% or $7.4 million.

As a result, the SFC achieved a $125 million surplus after two years of deficit result. Our reserves at the end of March 2004 stood at $691 million, equivalent to 1.6 times of estimated expenditures (including depreciation) of $443 million for 2004-2005.

Besides the financial information, the Annual Report details the SFC’s achievements during 2003-2004 and our work in progress. Corporate governance disclosure has also been enhanced to demonstrate our support for the market’s effort in improving corporate governance. It also reports on the successful implementation of the Securities and Futures Ordinance since April 2003.

Glossary

The third edition of the English-Chinese Glossary of Securities, Futures and Financial Terms commemorates the 15th Anniversary of the SFC. With Hong Kong, Mainland and Taiwan common usage, the Glossary has been welcomed by the market. The third edition contains over 14,200 terms, increasing from 13,800 in the previous edition.

For the first time, the SFC has also produced its Annual Report and the Glossary in CD-ROM. The CD-ROM version of the Glossary comes with a search function and other user-friendly features to facilitate the use of the Glossary.
