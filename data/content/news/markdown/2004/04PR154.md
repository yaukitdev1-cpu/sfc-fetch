The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X1} for holding himself out as carrying on a business of advising on securities whilst unlicensed, and {X2} for aiding and abetting {X1} in carrying on such a business.

Between April and October 2003, {X1}, who was not licensed by the SFC, held himself out as carrying on a business, under the names of Leadway Enterprises Limited and HK Market Informer Club, of providing daily stock market research summaries to facilitate his clients to make decisions on stock trading. {X1}’s friend {X2} assisted the business operation at the material time by collecting the monthly subscription fees through Leadway Enterprises Limited.

{X1} and {X2} today each pleaded guilty to one summons before Mr Ian Candy at Eastern Magistracy. They were fined $5,000 each and ordered to pay investigation costs totalling $10,000 to the SFC.
