The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X} for hawking of futures contracts.

 An SFC investigation found that on various dates between April 2001 and December 2002, {X} had made unsolicited calls inducing others to enter into agreements for trading in futures contracts on commodities exchanges in Japan.

{X}, a registered commodities dealer’s representative at the material time, pleaded guilty today before Mr Ian Candy, a Magistrate at Eastern Magistracy, to one summons. {X} was fined $5,000 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $10,000 to the SFC.
