The SFC warmly welcomes the re-appointment by the Financial Secretary of Mr Alan Linning as Executive Director (Enforcement) of the Commission. The Financial Secretary has also agreed that Mr Peter Au-Yang, Executive Director and Chief Operating Officer of the SFC, be redeployed to double-up the post of Executive Director (Corporate Finance) following the departure of Mr Ashley Alder on completion of his contract.

SFC Chairman, Mr Andrew Sheng, said: "I am very glad to have the continuing service of Mr Linning, who will continue to lead our enforcement action against corporate misgovernance, market misconduct and serious intermediaries misconduct as our priorities.

"Mr Alder has made significant contributions to the SFC and to improving the quality of the Hong Kong market. Under his able leadership, the Dual Filing regime of the Securities and Futures Ordinance was implemented successfully. Mr Alder was also closely involved in the Government's initiative to enhance regulation of listing by giving statutory backing to important listing requirements. His input to the legislative blueprint and law drafting was invaluable. "

"Personally I have enjoyed working with Mr Alder. We all wish him every success in his new endeavours. I would also like to thank Mr Au-Yang for taking up Mr Alder's position in the meantime. It is a great challenge given the joint responsibilities as Chief Operating Officer and head of Corporate Finance. I am confident that Mr Au-Yang will discharge the responsibilities well," Mr Sheng added.

Mr Linning has been the SFC’s Executive Director (Enforcement) since 1 November 2001. Mr Au-Yang has been the SFC’s Executive Director and Chief Operating Officer since 26 May 2003. Mr Alder was appointed the SFC’s Executive Director (Corporate Finance) on 1 October 2001. His term was to expire on 30 September 2004 originally, but was extended to 22 October 2004.
