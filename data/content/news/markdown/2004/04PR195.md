The central policy goal of the SFC in the regulation of listed companies is to emphasise deterrence of and enforcement against corporate misconduct, according to Mr Ashley Alder, SFC’s Executive Director of Corporate Finance.

Mr Alder said today at a press conference that the SFC had made significant progress in addressing market concerns about over-regulation and under-enforcement. He was reviewing the work of the Corporate Finance Division before his departure from the SFC at the end of his term tomorrow (22 October 2004).

"We are moving to a system where the regulatory burden is lifted from those who play by the rules, and where serious sanctions are imposed on those who breach the rules and harm the public," Mr Alder said.

"We seek to achieve this by shifting the focus of regulation from front-end vetting to enforcement and deterrence," he said.

Mr Alder also highlighted the major past and current tasks in the corporate finance and takeovers area. These include:


- Implementation of Dual Filing in April 2003;
- Establishment of the Public Shareholders Group;
- Ongoing legislative work on giving statutory backing for important Listing Rules;
- Forthcoming consultation on a review of the Takeovers Code;
- Forthcoming Corporate Governance Code for listed companies;
- Enhanced regulation of listing Sponsors and Independent Financial Advisers; and
- Reform of the legal regime for securities offerings [guidelines and exemptions in 2003; Companies (Amendment) Ordinance 2004], and forthcoming proposals for an overall review to align Hong Kong law with leading financial centre best practice.

Mr Alder joined the SFC on 1 October 2001. His term was to expire on 30 September 2004 originally, but was extended to 22 October 2004.
