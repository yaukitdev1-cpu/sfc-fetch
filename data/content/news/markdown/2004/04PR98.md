The SFC today publishes the May issue of Dr Wise’s Column, *Investing in Bonds*.  In this issue, Dr Wise discusses with investors the key variables and risks of investing in plain vanilla bonds and structured bonds.    He also discusses some of the key questions that investors should ask before making any bond investment.

Dr Wise is a fictitious character.  He represents the collective wisdom and knowledge of market specialists and experts at the SFC.  Dr Wise, through his column, talks to investors about the key issues of investing.  He explains in plain language various regulatory issues in the increasingly complex financial market.

Dr Wise’s Column is published on the last Tuesday of every month, starting from December 2003.  Today’s issue is the seventh issue including the special Chinese New Year supplement in January 2004.  The column is posted on the SFC website under the “Advice to Investors” section.  Both current and previous issues can be viewed under the mentioned section.   Investors are welcome to send their feedback and questions to Dr Wise by e-mail at **investor.info@hksfc.org.hk**.
