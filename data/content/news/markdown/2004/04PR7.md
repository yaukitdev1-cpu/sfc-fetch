The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X} for failing to assist in an SFC investigation.

The investigation concerned involved a suspected market manipulation case in which {X}, a dealer’s representative of JS Cresvale Securities International Ltd at that time, failed to give all the assistance which he was reasonably able to give to the investigator in connection with the trades conducted by his clients.

{X} today pleaded guilty to one summons before Mr Ian Candy, a Magistrate at Eastern Magistracy.  Mr Candy fined {X} $10,000 and ordered him to pay investigation costs of $17,699 to the SFC.  In passing the sentence, Mr Candy commented that this was not a minor offence as the offence carried a maximum penalty of $100,000 and 6 months’ imprisonment.  

Mr Alan Linning, SFC’s Executive Director of Enforcement, said: “We welcome the court’s decision and comments on the seriousness of the offence.  Persons who are reasonably believed or suspected by an SFC investigator to have relevant information should give all reasonable assistance to the investigator.  The SFC will not tolerate deliberate attempts to thwart its investigations and will not hesitate to prosecute offenders in this regard.”
