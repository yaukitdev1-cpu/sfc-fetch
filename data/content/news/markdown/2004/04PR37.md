The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X} for engaging in leveraged foreign exchange trading activities whilst not being licensed under the Leveraged Foreign Exchange Trading Ordinance. 

{X} today pleaded guilty to one summons before Mr Daniel Ho, Magistrate at Tuen Mun Magistracy. {X} was fined $10,000 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $10,000 to the SFC.

The SFC investigation revealed that between October 2002 and March 2003, {X}, whilst not being a licensed trader with the SFC, had solicited persons to open accounts with his own unlicensed companies to trade leveraged foreign exchange contracts.
