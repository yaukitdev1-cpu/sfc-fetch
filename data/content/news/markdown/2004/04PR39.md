The SFC explained the proposals of the Working Group on Review of the Financial Regulatory Framework for Licensed Corporations to Members of the Legislative Council Panel on Financial Affairs (FA Panel) at a Panel meeting this morning.

SFC’s Executive Director of Intermediaries and Investment Products, Alexa Lam, introduced the proposals and answered Members’ questions. The proposals aim to address the risks arising from pooling and re-pledging of client collateral by securities margin finance providers. (Note 1)

The SFC thanks the Members for their comments. The SFC will carefully consider their comments when preparing a public consultation paper on the proposals. The paper is expected to be ready in the second quarter this year.

A copy of a report by the Working Group on the proposals, which has been submitted to the FA Panel, is available on the [SFC website](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/general/general/press_release/04/04pr39_report.pdf).
