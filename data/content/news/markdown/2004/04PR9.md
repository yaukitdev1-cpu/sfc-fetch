The SFC issues the following update on the operation of the Dual Filing arrangements under the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

As reported previously (Note 1), the SFC received, via the Stock Exchange, a total of 28 applications for listing in the three months from July to September and commented on 13 cases.

Of the 13 applications that the SFC commented on, the SFC has provided one or two rounds of comments on each case, again through the Exchange.  In five cases the applicants improved their disclosure following our comments and accordingly we did not need to comment further.

Of the other eight cases, two applications have lapsed and three other applicants have yet to make any response.  One applicant has responded but there are still outstanding issues.  The Exchange rejected one application and the SFC, after consulting the Dual Filing Advisory Group, indicated to the Exchange its intention to object to another because the disclosure was materially deficient.

Of the 15 cases on which the SFC had no comments, eight have become listed or been approved for listing.

For the three months from October to December, the SFC received 21 applications and commented on nine cases. Our average response time was seven working days.

The SFC has noticed that two sponsors were between them responsible for five of those nine cases.  These two sponsors, both with small staff, have each put in six applications in the last eight months.  Many of the 12 applications had disclosure deficiencies and the SFC had to make comments.  In one case, the draft prospectus submitted was essentially a copy of the prospectus of another company and failed to reflect the applicant’s business.

The SFC is concerned whether the two sponsors have sufficient resources to fulfil the professional and regulatory obligations required of them.  The SFC is monitoring both firms closely and will take appropriate action if they are found to have breached the relevant regulatory requirements.

An SFC spokesman said: “SFC licensees acting as listing sponsors must act professionally and diligently.  The relevant regulation will be amended in the light of the consultation on regulation of sponsors.  But in any event, sponsors should have no illusions that substandard work would be accepted by the SFC.”

The SFC issues this update as part of its effort to ensure transparency of the Dual Filing regime.

Since the implementation of regime on 1 April 2003, the SFC has received 91 listing applications and commented on 38 of them. The average response time is seven working days.
