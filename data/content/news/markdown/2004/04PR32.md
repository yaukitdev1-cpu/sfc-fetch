The SFC today publishes the Winter 2003 issue (No. 54) of the *SFC Quarterly Bulletin*.

The Bulletin contains an article entitled *CEPA Opens up Northern Conduit for Market Professionals*.  It outlines the competence requirements for an individual to act as a securities or futures professional in the Mainland and Hong Kong.  It then describes the framework formulated by the China Securities Regulatory Commission and the SFC in implementing the Mainland/Hong Kong Closer Economic Partnership Arrangement pertaining to mutual recognition of each other’s professional qualifications in the securities and futures industry.

This issue also includes an article entitled *Disclosure of Interests*.  It highlights some of the major changes under the new disclosure regime with the enactment of the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO) on 1 April 2003. It also provides a summary of the number of cases in this area handled by the Enforcement Division of the SFC in the first three months after the implementation of the SFO.  

Another article, *Impact of the Issuance of A-shares on the Turnover of H-shares*, provides an analysis of the turnover of A-shares and H-shares and illustrates that the issuance of A-shares either has no impact on or appears to raise the turnover of H-shares.  This article is primarily drawn from an SFC research paper of the same title, which is available on the SFC website at 


http: //www.hksfc.org.hk/eng/statistics/html/researches.htm.

The Bulletin also contains an article entitled *Investor Compensation Company Limited*.  It describes the mission, functions and operational structure of the Investor Compensation Company Limited, which was established under the SFO.

The market update, *Trading of H-shares on HKEx and NYSE*, analyses turnover of the H-shares on the two exchanges and also reviews the performance of the Hong Kong and regional stock markets in 2003.

The Bulletin can be subscribed at $400 per year or downloaded free of charge from the SFC website under “Press Releases, Publications & Speeches” – “Publications”.  The public are welcome to sign up the SFC Website Update Email Alert service for the notification of new releases of the Bulletin.
