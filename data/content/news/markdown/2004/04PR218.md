The SFC today publishes the November issue of the [*SFC Enforcement Reporter*](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/enforcement/nov_04.pdf), summarising the SFC’s enforcement action in the month of October 2004 and discussing the compliance issues highlighted by recent cases. 

In October, the SFC successfully prosecuted five companies and five people for issuing unauthorised investment advertisements, holding out as providing securities trading and advisory services while unlicensed, acting as a dealer’s representative whilst unlicensed, and breaching the disclosure of interests law.

The SFC disciplined one licensee during the month for lying to the SFC and Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited about his short selling of shares.

Since 1 April 2004, the SFC has successfully prosecuted 51 entities. We have withdrawn summonses issued against eight entities resulting in their acquittals and a further five entities were acquitted after trial. The SFC has also disciplined 36 licensees for various regulatory breaches and entered into settlements with voluntary payments with two licensees. Disciplinary proceedings have been taken against 10 licensees, which were concluded with no formal sanction imposed, although seven of them received private warnings. Such proceedings were also commenced and discontinued against four deemed licensees who left their firms before the conclusion of the action.

In October, the Takeovers Executive also imposed a cold shoulder order on the chairman and an executive director of a listed company, publicly censured the two, the company and five other directors for failing to issue an offeree document to the company’s shareholders on a voluntary conditional cash offer made by an offeror company.

The November issue of the *SFC Enforcement Reporter* is available at the SFC office. All issues are also available on the SFC website under ["Speeches & Publications” – “Publications”](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/speeches/public/enforcement/enforcement.html).
