The SFC today publishes the [Autumn 2004 issue (No.57) of the *SFC Quarterly Bulletin*](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/speeches/public/bulletin/sfc_bulletin/autumn_04.html).

The Bulletin contains an article entitled *The Listing of Mainland Companies on HKEx and the Implication for Hong Kong*.  It is found that the stock market of Hong Kong used to be dependent on the property sector, but now has a more diversified structure due to the increasing importance of Mainland companies (i.e. H-shares and red chips) which consist mostly of companies in the energy, telecom and industrial sectors.  Some Mainland stocks are also traded in overseas markets such as the US and the UK, but Hong Kong accounted for about 70% of the total trading.

*Profile of the Index Futures Market in Hong Kong* outlines the major features of the index futures market.  The study also analyses the trading patterns of HSI Futures and Mini HSI Futures and the behaviour of brokers toward trading in index futures.  This is the third paper in a special research paper series which aims at providing information and analysis of exchange-traded securities and derivatives in Hong Kong.  The series is available on the SFC website under ”Research Papers & Statistics” – “Research Papers”.

The article, *Overview of Fund Industry in China*, presents a landscape of the fund industry in China today and explores the relationship between the equity and fund management markets.

The market update, *Possible Impact of a Rise in US Interest Rates on the Hong Kong Stock Market*, reviews the last four interest rate cycles and finds that the relationship between interest rate movements and the performance of the HSI is inconclusive.  In addition, the current level of the Aggregate Balance is higher than the “normal” level. Even if there is a rise in the US interest rate and there is an outflow of capital, it may not necessarily lead to an immediate hike in Hong Kong by the same magnitude.  Therefore, the impact of an interest rate hike in the US on the Hong Kong stock market is expected to be relatively limited.  The article also reviews the performance of the Hong Kong and regional stock markets during the third quarter of 2004.

The securities industry update, *Quarterly Financial Review of the Securities Industry*, analyses the financial performance of the securities industry in the second quarter of 2004.  During the quarter, various uncertain market factors such as movements of the interest rate and oil prices affected the performance of the securities market.  Under these influences, market turnover and the profitability of the securities industry dropped as compared to the first quarter of 2004.
