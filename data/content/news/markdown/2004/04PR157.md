The SFC today publishes a new pamphlet entitled *Disciplinary Proceedings at a Glance* to explain its disciplinary process and describe the procedural rights of persons who are subject to disciplinary proceedings.

The pamphlet summarises:


- the reasons why disciplinary proceedings are necessary;
- the factors which the SFC may take into account in deciding whether to start disciplinary proceedings and the level of sanctions;
- the disciplinary measures available to the SFC;
- the SFC’s disciplinary process and rights of the subject of disciplinary proceedings in the process;
- the SFC’s policy on settlement of disciplinary proceedings; and
- the rights of appeal to the Securities and Futures Appeals Tribunal and the Court of Appeal.

Mr Alan Linning, SFC’s Executive Director of Enforcement, said: "The pamphlet is written in plain language and is intended to increase public understanding of the SFC’s disciplinary procedures and inform the subjects of disciplinary proceedings about their rights. It is part of the SFC’s continuing efforts to increase the transparency of its disciplinary policies and procedures. In future, we will explain more about the SFC’s enforcement work through various channels so that the public and the market can better understand how we enforce the law to safeguard investor interests and market integrity."

Copies of the pamphlet will be sent to persons who are subject to disciplinary proceedings together with the document initiating the proceedings.

Copies of the pamphlet are available at the SFC office and [on the SFC website](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/disciplinary_proceedings_eng.pdf).
