SFC Chairman, Mr Andrew Sheng, was invited by the US Senate Banking Committee to testify about the international impact of the Sarbanes-Oxley Act and developments concerning convergence of international securities laws. He gave his testimony before the Committee on 9 September 2004 in Washington, DC.
