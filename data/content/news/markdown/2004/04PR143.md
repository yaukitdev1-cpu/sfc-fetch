The SFC today publishes a Consultation Paper on the proposed repeal of certain class exemptions made under section 38A(2)/342A(2) of the Companies Ordinance (CO).  

Schedule 1 of the newly enacted Companies (Amendment) Ordinance 2004 (Amendment Ordinance) (Note 1) provides for 12 categories of offers that shall be exempt from the prospectus regime.  Two such categories, namely offers to “professional investors” and offers by corporate entities which are also collective investment schemes authorised by the SFC, are already the subjects of class exemptions contained in sections 3 and 7 of the Companies Ordinance (Exemption of Companies and Prospectuses from Compliance with Provisions) Notice (Exemption Notice) (Note 2).  The SFC considers that these two class exemptions will be superseded and therefore become redundant upon the commencement of the Amendment Ordinance.   To clarify the legislative framework upon the implementation of the Amendment Ordinance, the SFC proposes to repeal these two class exemptions (Note 3).

It is expected that the Amendment Ordinance will be brought into force towards the end of 2004 following publication of a Commencement Notice in the Gazette in October 2004.  The proposed repeal of sections 3 and 7 of the Exemption Notice will be synchronized with the commencement of the Amendment Ordinance.

Notes:

1. The Companies (Amendment) Ordinance 2004 was enacted on 23 July 2004 and will come into operation on a day to be appointed by notice published in the Gazette.

2. Section 3 of the Exemption Notice exempts Hong Kong-incorporated companies and their prospectuses from compliance with the content, dual Chinese/English language and maximum offer duration requirements of sections 38(1) and (3) and 44A(2) of the CO when making an offer of shares or debentures to “*persons whose ordinary business is to buy or sell shares or debentures whether as principal or agent*”.

Section 7 of the Exemption Notice exempts Hong Kong and overseas-incorporated companies which are also collective investment schemes authorised by the SFC under section 104(1) of the Securities and Futures Ordinance from compliance with the content and dual language requirements (in the case of Hong Kong–incorporated companies) in section 38(1) and (3) and (in the case of overseas-incorporated companies) in section 342(1) and (3) of the CO.

3. Please refer to paragraphs 4-8 of the Consultation Paper for detailed rationale behind the proposed repeal.
