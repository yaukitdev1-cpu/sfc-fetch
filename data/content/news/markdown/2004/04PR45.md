The SFC is pleased to announce that an industry working group (Note 1) has decided on the appointment of a new insurance broker to arrange and administer the fidelity insurance schemes for exchange participants for the next scheme year from 1 April 2004 to 31 March 2005.

Under the new arrangement, there will be two insurance schemes covering respectively the participants of the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited and the participants of the Hong Kong Futures Exchange Limited against their risks of losses of client assets due to robbery, theft, forgery and fraudulent acts by employees, etc.  As provided under the Securities and Futures (Insurance) Rules, each participant will be insured for an indemnity limit of $15 million per scheme period with a deductible amount of $3 million per claim/loss (Note 2).

SFC’s Executive Director of Intermediaries and Investment Products, Mrs Alexa Lam, said: “The SFC is grateful for the efforts of the industry working group in selecting the insurance broker and underwriter for the forthcoming scheme year.  Through a rigorous tender process, competitive bids from the insurance market have been obtained particularly in terms of insurance premium.” 

Overall, stock exchange participants will be paying about 35% less insurance premium in total than the payment for current scheme year.  For futures exchange participants, this will be the first time they are required to take out fidelity insurance.

“Fidelity insurance for exchange participants will further enhance protection to investors and foster market stability,” Mrs Lam said.

The SFC will issue a circular to the licensed corporations concerned detailing the arrangement of the new insurance schemes.
