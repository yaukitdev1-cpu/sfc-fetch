The SFC today issues the October issue of the Dr Wise’s Column, ["You Bet* His* Life"](http://www.eirc.hk/eirc/html/EN/features/doctor/features_87_226_310.htm).

In this issue, Dr Wise discusses viatical settlements, a specific type of products new to many local investors as the products are not commonly sold in Hong Kong yet. Viatical settlements allow a life insurance policy owner to sell the policy to a third party at a discount to the face value of the policy. Typically, the insured has a shortened life expectancy (for example because he or she is diagnosed with a terminal illness) and is in need of money to pay medical or other expenses.

Viatical settlements are often arranged through a middleman, the viatical provider, who locates and purchases life policies and offer them to investors. An investor acquires an interest in the policy and when the insured dies, the investor gets a share in the payout under the policy.

The risks associated with viatical settlements are very different from other investments such as stocks or bonds. The purchase price of viatical settlements and the returns are highly dependent on the accuracy of the estimate of the insured’s life expectancy. Also, the credibility of the viatical provider is very important.

To date, no viatical settlement product has been authorised by the SFC for public offer in Hong Kong. The SFC is looking into the offer of these products in Hong Kong and will take appropriate action if there is a breach of the law.
