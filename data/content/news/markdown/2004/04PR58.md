The SFC welcomes the re-appointment of Mr Thaddeus Beczak, Mr Stephen Clark, Ms Patricia Shih, Mr Carlson Tong and Mr V-Nee Yeh as market representatives of the SFC (HKEC Listing) Committee for a term of one year from 1 April 2004 to 31 March 2005.

The Committee was established pursuant to the Securities and Futures Commission Ordinance, re-enacted as the Securities and Futures Ordinance, in connection with the listing of Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited (HKEx) in June 2000. It exercises powers and functions equivalent to those of the Main Board and GEM Listing Committees of The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited (Stock Exchange) when real or potential conflicts of interests between the interests of HKEx and those of the proper performance of the Stock Exchange’s listing functions make it desirable for the relevant Stock Exchange functions to be exercised by the SFC.

The Committee consists of five market representatives and five SFC representatives. A full list of the members of the Committee is provided in Note 1.

An SFC spokesman said: "The Committee is a voluntary body which owes its success to the dedication and expertise of its members. The SFC is confident that the significant experience and expert knowledge of the Committee members in the securities and financial markets will ensure the continued success of the Committee."
