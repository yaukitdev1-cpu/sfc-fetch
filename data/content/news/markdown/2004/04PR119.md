The SFC welcomes the appointment by the Financial Secretary of Mr Richard Gnodde and Mr Blair Pickerell as members of the SFC Advisory Committee from 1 July 2004 to 31 May 2005.

"Mr Gnodde and Mr Pickerell are both experienced market practitioners who will give valuable advice to the SFC," Mr Peter Au-Yang, Acting Chairman of the SFC, said.

Mr Au-Yang also thanked Mr Mario Francescotti, who has retired from the Advisory Committee, for his contribution.

The Advisory Committee is set up pursuant to section 7 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance to advise the SFC on matters of policy regarding any of its regulatory objectives and functions.
