The SFC welcomes the Government's publication today of Consultation Conclusions on Proposals to Enhance the Regulation of Listing.

The SFC supports the legislative blueprint set out in the conclusions paper, which will introduce credible sanctions for issuers and their directors when they breach statutorily backed listing requirements.

SFC Chairman, Mr Andrew Sheng, said: "The result for Hong Kong will be a better quality market with improved corporate governance, which in turn will make a major contribution to Hong Kong's future as an international financial centre and capital raising hub of China. The SFC will work closely with the Administration and Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited to ensure that the roadmap for reform can be achieved as quickly as practicable."

The SFC will focus its work in the short term on the new legislative rules to be made by it under Section 36 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance, which will contain the important listing requirements to be given statutory backing. It also looks forward to developing with HKEx measures outlined in the conclusions paper to enhance further the transparency of listing regulation.
