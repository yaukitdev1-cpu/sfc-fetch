The SFC notes a newspaper report this morning about co-operation between the SFC and its Mainland counterpart in investigating Hong Kong listed Mainland companies and businesses.

The SFC wishes to emphasise that the China Securities Regulatory Commission (CSRC) and the SFC have long-standing and excellent co-operation including a Memorandum of Regulatory Co-operation (MORC) and regular staff exchanges.

Under the MORC, there are regular discussions between the SFC and CSRC on matters of mutual interest, including those concerning enforcement and corporate finance. The MORC enables the two regulators to share public and non-public information with and render regulatory assistance to each other.

Regular staff exchanges also focus on enforcement and corporate finance. Currently, a director of the SFC’s Corporate Finance Division responsible for listing matters is on secondment to the CSRC.

While there are differences between the legal frameworks in Hong Kong and the Mainland, both regulators have fully co-operated with each other within the respective frameworks.

“The SFC is grateful for the assistance that has been given by the CSRC in many work aspects including investigations. We look forward to continuing and strengthening this excellent relationship,” an SFC spokesman said.

“This co-operation is vital to Hong Kong’s continuing development as the premier capital formation centre of China,” the spokesman added.

The SFC has in the past received useful assistance from the CSRC which in turn facilitated the SFC’s enforcement actions.

Owing to secrecy provisions of its legislation, the SFC cannot publicly reveal details of regulatory co-operation or discuss specific cases, past or current.
