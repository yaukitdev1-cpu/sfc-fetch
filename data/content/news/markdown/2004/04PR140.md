The SFC today releases its *Quarterly Report* for the financial period 1 April to 30 June 2004 (Note 1).

Surplus recorded

In the first quarter of 2004-2005, the SFC recorded a surplus of $44.2 million, compared with a projected deficit of $1.4 million in the approved budget.

Total revenue was $145.1 million, 55% higher than the same period last year because of increased market turnover and higher fees and charges income due to increased market activity. Total expenditure (including depreciation) amounted to $101 million, same as that recorded last year.

At the end of June, the reserves stood at $735.2 million, equivalent to 1.7 times the approved annual operating expenditure (including depreciation) of $443 million.

A surplus is expected in the current quarter based on current information.

Work progress

During the first quarter, the SFC:

- continued to review the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO) to consider if certain provisions or the subsidiary legislation made under it need to be amended;
- worked on amendments to the SFO required for the proposed new statutory framework to implement Government's proposals to enhance the regulation of listing;
- worked with Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited (HKEx) to finalise consultation conclusions on the regulation of sponsors and independent financial advisers;
- continued to finalise a review of the Codes on Takeovers and Mergers and Share Repurchases;
- analysed the response to the consultation on the regulatory framework for addressing analyst conflicts of interest;
- commenced theme inspections on the business conduct of investment advisers and reviewed their compliance with law, rules and code;
- continued to review the geographical restriction on Real Estate Investment Trusts;
- released results of a survey on fund trading practices, which show that neither market timing nor late trading was widespread in Hong Kong;
- assisted in drafting the necessary legislation for the operational model for a scripless securities market proposed by HKEx;
- monitored closely the orderly return of clients assets by Sharegain Development Limited, a small licensed firm under receivership;
- issued a restriction notice on Charles Schmitt & Associates Limited (CSAL) following suspected misappropriation of assets of a CSAL fund, and obtained judgment from the High Court for appointing an administrator of its chief executive officer’s property;
- had 18 successful prosecutions and disciplined 15 licensees for various regulatory breaches;
- jointly produced with Radio Television Hong Kong a TV educational drama series, *All about Stock Investing*; and
- signed a Letter of Intent with Indonesia’s Ministry of Finance Capital Market Supervisory Agency on the establishment of regulatory co-operation relating to investment funds.
