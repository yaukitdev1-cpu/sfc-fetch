The SFC has for the first time partnered with the Radio Television Hong Kong (RTHK) and The Salvation Army to tailor-make an investor education radio programme for senior citizens.
 
Entitled “Invest Wisely for Seniors”, the programme comprises 12 15-minute episodes. Programme hosts will discuss with guest speakers on a wide range of issues that our senior citizens should pay attention to when investing, including portfolio planning, subscribing to IPO shares, margin trading, and fund and bond investments, etc. Guest speakers include experienced market practitioners, professionals and an academic (Note 1).
 
Broadcast will start on 21 September 2004 (next Tuesday). To listen to “Invest Wisely for Seniors", investors can tune in to RTHK 5 at AM783 every Tuesday at 11:30am. The series is part of the “Programme for the Elderly”. 
 
After each episode, the audience can call RTHK 5 and participate in a quiz. The first two callers who answer the hosts’ questions correctly will each receive a souvenir of The Salvation Army. The SFC will also arrange a seminar for all the winners for more in-depth discussion on investment issues that senior citizens should be aware of. 
 
Launching the radio programme at a ceremony today, Mr Peter Au-Yang, SFC's Executive Director and Chief Operating Officer, said: “In general, money invested by senior citizens is usually their life savings and retirement funds. They should invest carefully… They should choose the investment products which suit their investment needs and risk appetite.”
 
“We hope that senior citizens, after listening to the programme, can put into practice what they have learnt in investing,” Mr Au-Yang added.

The public can provide their views and feedback on the programme to the SFC by calling the Investor Hotline at 2840 9333 or sending an email to investor.info@hksfc.org.hk.

Since 1998, the SFC has jointly produced investor education radio programmes with various radio stations. Some of these radio series are now available on the “Studio” section of the SFC-operated Electronic Investor Resources Centre (eIRC) at www.hkeirc.org.
