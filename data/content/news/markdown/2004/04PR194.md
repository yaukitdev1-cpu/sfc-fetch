An exclusive group of leading corporate executives and securities regulators will gather in New York next week to discuss emerging international issues concerning the regulation of global securities markets.

The forum is the first annual conference hosted by the Technical Committee of the International Organization of Securities Commissions (IOSCO). It is taking place as the international corporate community, having overcome recent difficulties, enters a more confident phase when investors increasingly regain confidence in the operations of global financial markets.

SFC Chairman, Mr Andrew Sheng, who is Chairman of the Technical Committee, said:"It is important at this time that we look for ways to improve global capital markets and at the same time overcome problems facing corporations, financial firms, investors and regulators."

"We also view this as an opportunity to brainstorm at the very highest levels for solutions to issues that we do not yet face, but nonetheless see clearly looming on the horizon," he said.

The conference will comprise a series of roundtable discussions on topics ranging from the evolving role of stock exchanges and the rights of shareholders to the impact of new technology on securities business and regulation. Panelists and participants have been drawn from the academic, regulatory, industry, legal, and auditing communities and investor groups (Note 1).

The conference will take place between 27 and 29 October 2004.
