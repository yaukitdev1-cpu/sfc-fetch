The SFC today publishes the [August issue of *SFC Alert*](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/newsletter/jul_aug04_eng.pdf), a bi-monthly newsletter for both investors and intermediaries.

The front page story, *Adviser makes payments to fund investors following SFC negotiations*, reports on the SFC’s settlement of disciplinary proceedings against an investment adviser, which has agreed to pay up to about $256 million to investors of two unauthorised hedge funds.

*IPO refund cheques* introduces the latest measure to strengthen the security of refund cheques to IPO applicants.

The SFC recorded a $44.2 million surplus in the quarter to June. *First quarterly surplus* highlights our financial performance during the first quarter of the financial year 2004-2005.

Mr Keith Lui, Senior Director of Supervision of Markets, appears in the regular *Senior management interview* column. He talks about how the SFC monitors market operations and supervises the introduction of new services and products by HKEx.

Two separate surveys find that Hong Kong is a magnet for overseas funds and trading malpractices like market timing and late trading are not common in the local fund industry. *Survey confirms Hong Kong’s fund management centre status* and *"Market timing" not prevalent in Hong Kong* provide more details.

*Dual filing promotes quality of disclosure* reiterates the SFC’s emphasis on quality, not quantity, of disclosure. It discusses two serious disclosure deficiencies common to several recent listing applications.

*Providing you with security* gives a general overview of the investor compensation arrangement now in force.

To help investors better understand exotic derivative warrants, another regular column, *Investor Focus*, compares lock-in return and average return warrants and advises investors what they should be aware of when putting money into these sophisticated products.

At the back*, Enhanced investor participant account service* highlights the latest improvements to the service by HKEx.

The August issue of *SFC Alert* is the 25th issue of the newsletter, which was first published in October 2001. Copies of the current issue of *SFC Alert* are available at the SFC office. All issues are available on the SFC website.
