The SFC today publishes the March issue of the *SFC Enforcement Reporter*, summarising the SFC's enforcement action in the month of February 2004 and discussing the compliance issues highlighted by recent cases. 

In February, the SFC successfully prosecuted three persons and disciplined four licensees.

The sole proprietor of a brokerage who, without authorisation, transferred clients’ securities to obtain a bank overdraft facility was not only fined by the Court for the offence, but also faces SFC disciplinary action.

Meanwhile, a licensed representative of a futures firm had his licence suspended for three months for misconduct involving failure to execute a client’s option order as instructed and for creating a false and misleading appearance of option prices.

We have continued to publish figures on acquittals and disciplinary proceedings that have been concluded without sanction. Since 1 April 2003, the SFC has successfully prosecuted 53 entities. There have been no acquittals. The SFC has also disciplined 77 licensees for regulatory breaches. Disciplinary proceedings have been taken against 13 licensees, which were eventually discontinued with no formal sanction imposed. Such proceedings were also commenced and discontinued against another eight deemed licensees who left their firms before the conclusion of the actions.
