The SFC today publishes the revised Guidance Note on Position Limits and Large Open Position Reporting Requirements.  

The Guidance Note was first published in March 2003 to clarify the Commission’s policies and positions in the application of the Securities and Futures (Contracts Limits and Reportable Positions) Rules to help the industry comply with the law.

The revised Guidance Note, gazetted today, reflects amendments to the Rules made under the Securities and Futures (Contracts Limits and Reportable Positions) (Amendment) Rules 2004 which also come into effect today.

The Amendment Rules allow any person who holds or controls futures or stock options to apply the reporting requirements and the prescribed limits separately to his own account and to each of the other persons except where the person has discretion in respect of the futures or stock options contracts which he holds for other persons. 

The publication of the revised Guidance Note and the Amendment Rules reflects the SFC’s continuous effort to work with industry participants to address their concerns. 

The revised Guidance Note is available at the SFC office and on the SFC website under “Bills, Legislation and Codes” – “Regulatory Handbook”.
