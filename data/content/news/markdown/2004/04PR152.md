The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X} for aiding and abetting Mr Lam Hon Wing in carrying on a business of leveraged foreign exchange trading whilst not licensed under the Leveraged Foreign Exchange Trading Ordinance. (Note 1)

An SFC investigation revealed that between October 2002 and March 2003, {X}, knowing that Mr Lam Hon Wing was not a licensed representative with the SFC, aided and abetted Mr Lam Hon Wing trading as Trust Investment Company to solicit clients to open accounts to trade leveraged foreign exchange trading contracts.

{X} today pleaded guilty to one summons before Magistrate Peter Hui at Tuen Mun Magistracy. He was fined $8,000 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $10,000 to the SFC.
