The SFC has successfully prosecuted Value Partners Limited under the Securities (Disclosure of Interests) Ordinance. 

Value Partners Limited pleaded guilty to four summonses in relation to its failure to make both initial disclosure of its interest in Bright International Group Limited when the funds under its management acquired an interest in Bright totalling 10.78% of the company’s total issued share capital on 16 April 2002 and subsequent disclosure of a decrease of interest in Bright to below 10% on 24 April 2002.

Mr Johnny Chan, a Magistrate at Eastern Magistracy, fined Value Partners Limited a total of $16,000 and ordered it to pay investigation costs of $29,225 to the SFC.

The SFC noted that the contravention stemmed from a calculation error on the part of Value Partners Limited.  The SFC regards this as the end of the matter and has warned Value Partners Limited about its obligations as a licensee to have adequate systems to comply with the law and regulations.
