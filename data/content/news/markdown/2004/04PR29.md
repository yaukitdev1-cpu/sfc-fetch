SFC Chairman, Mr Andrew Sheng, gave a keynote address today at a conference in Hong Kong on “The Practical Implications of SEC Regulation Outside the United States”.

Mr Sheng notes that the US Securities and Exchange Commission and the SFC have long standing regulatory co-operation, which is crucial in the increasingly globalised financial markets.

“Indeed, the globalisation of markets and need for closer cross-border co-operation is subtly nudging all jurisdictions in the direction of an emerging international common practice.  In this, the US SEC, as the statutory regulator with the longest history, provides a lot of inspiration to its fellow regulators.  Many in Hong Kong have called for more steps towards the US model of disclosure regulation, and there is wide consensus for us to give our disclosure requirements statutory force,” he said.

Mr Sheng’s speech, *SFC’s Relationship with the SEC in International Market Regulation*, is attached for your reference. It is available on the SFC website.
