The SFC welcomes the Financial Secretary's commitment to reinforcing Hong Kong’s status as an international financial centre and further enhancing its role as the premier capital formation centre for China.

The SFC will fully co-operate with and assist the Government in order to achieve the objectives of the proposals outlined by the Financial Secretary, Mr Henry Tang.

SFC Chairman, Mr Andrew Sheng, said the SFC would work closely with the Administration, Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited (HKEx), the industry and other relevant parties to implement measures to enhance corporate governance standards in Hong Kong.

The SFC would also work with the Government and HKEx on the details and implementation timetable in relation to proposals to enhance the regulation of listing, Mr Sheng said.

The SFC will continue to build and improve on the measures it has already introduced to facilitate efficient and cost effective bond issuance.
