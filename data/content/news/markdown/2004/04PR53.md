The SFC today issues a circular to analysts advising them on how to handle non-public price sensitive information about listed company securities (Note 1).

Since the SFC disciplinary action last year against an analyst who selectively disclosed non-public price sensitive information (Note 2), the SFC has received queries from analysts and other market practitioners about how the SFC views the law, regulation and the SFC Code of Conduct regarding the handling of such information. Today’s circular is issued to answer these queries and give guidance on the relevant law, regulation and other SFC codes and guidelines. The SFC hopes analysts, the firms they work for and their legal and compliance advisers will find the circular useful.

The SFC will keep the situation under review and may revise the circular to deal with further queries analysts may have in this important area of regulation.

The circular is intended to complement HKEx’s "Guide on Disclosure of Price Sensitive Information" which guides listed companies on how to handle disclosure of price-sensitive information (Note 3).

The SFC's Executive Director of Enforcement, Mr Alan Linning said: "Analysts play a vital role in helping investors understand listed companies and make informed investment decisions. Owing to the nature of the work analysts perform, they may get access to information others do not have. As a crucial interface between investors and listed companies, analysts have a positive role to play, but they must remember to play that role fairly and in accordance with the law, especially in relation to the fair dissemination of accurate information. We hope this circular will guide analysts in this complex area. Separately, SFC will be consulting the public on the appropriate framework for addressing analysts’ conflicts of interest."
