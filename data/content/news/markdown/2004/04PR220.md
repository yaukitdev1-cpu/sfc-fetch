The Chairperson of The Philippine Stock Exchange, Inc., Mrs Alicia Rita M. Arroyo, today visited the SFC. SFC Chairman, Mr Andrew Sheng, discussed with Mrs Arroyo development of the markets in the two places and other matters of mutual interest.
