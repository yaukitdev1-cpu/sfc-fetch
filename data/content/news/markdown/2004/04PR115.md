The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X} of Television Broadcasts Limited (TVB), under Part XV of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

{X} pleaded guilty to two summonses in relation to his failure to notify both the Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited (HKEx) and TVB regarding his disposal of 100,000 TVB shares on 24 June 2003, which caused him to cease to have a notifiable interest in TVB. {X} did not notify HKEx or TVB until 31 October 2003.

Mr Ian Candy, a Magistrate at Eastern Magistracy, fined {X} $5,000 and ordered him to pay total investigation costs of $6,542 to the SFC.
