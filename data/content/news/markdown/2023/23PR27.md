The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) is pleased to announce new appointments and reappointments to the following committees with effect from 1 April 2023:



- Academic and Accreditation Advisory Committee

- Committee on Real Estate Investment Trusts

- Disciplinary Chair Committee

- Public Shareholders Group

- SFC (HKEC Listing) Committee

- Share Registrars&rsquo; Disciplinary Committee

- Takeovers and Mergers Panel

- Takeovers Appeal Committee


The SFC welcomes all the new committee members and expresses its gratitude to outgoing members for their service.

The membership changes are listed below. Details including [the committees&rsquo; responsibilities and full lists of members](https://www.sfc.hk/en/About-the-SFC/Organisational-chart/External-committees) with their titles and affiliations are available on the SFC website.
