The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has banned Mr Chan Wai Chun, a former customer relationship manager of Dah Sing Bank Limited (DSB), from re-entering the industry for life following his conviction for fraud (Notes 1 & 2).


The District Court found that in March 2021, Chan persuaded an elderly customer of DSB to redeem her investment in a fund and reinvest the redeemed amount in another fund to earn higher interest yield.


The customer agreed with Chan&rsquo;s suggestion and signed some documents under Chan&rsquo;s instructions for the redemption.  However, Chan never invested in any new fund for the customer and later transferred $1,195,000 from the customer&rsquo;s account to his personal account and used the money to repay his own debts.


The SFC considers that Chan is not a fit and proper person to be licensed or registered to carry on regulated activities as a result of his criminal conviction (Note 3).


The case was referred to the SFC by the HKMA.


End


Notes:



- Chan was registered with the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) to act as a relevant individual engaged by DSB in respect of Type 1 (dealing in securities) and Type 4 (advising on securities) regulated activities under the Securities and Futures Ordinance between 29 January 2021 and 1 April 2021.  Chan is currently not registered with the HKMA or licensed by the SFC.

- Chan was convicted of one count of fraud, contrary to section 16A of the Theft Ordinance at the District Court and was sentenced to two years and eight months&rsquo; imprisonment on 25 July 2022.
