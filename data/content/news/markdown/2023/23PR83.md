The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today began accepting applications for carrying on Type 13 regulated activity (RA 13) under a new regime which will bring depositaries of SFC-authorised collective investment schemes under the SFC&rsquo;s direct supervision.


The new regime will take effect on 2 October 2024 (Note 1). Depositaries operating in Hong Kong need to submit RA 13 applications through [WINGS](https://wings.sfc.hk/main/#/en), the SFC&rsquo;s online submission platform, on or before 30 November 2023 (Note 2).


Today the SFC issued a [circular](https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/circular/intermediaries/licensing/doc?refNo=23EC32) to the industry providing additional guidance on the scope of RA 13 and transitional arrangements.


End


Notes:



- On 24 March 2023, the SFC released [consultation conclusions](https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/consultation/conclusion?refNo=22CP1) on proposed amendments to subsidiary legislation and SFC codes and guidelines to implement the regulatory regime for depositaries of SFC-authorised collective investment schemes. The legislative process for introducing RA 13 into the Securities and Futures Ordinance was completed on 17 May 2023.

- The electronic licensing forms for RA 13 are now available on WINGS and the [SFC&rsquo;s website](https://www.sfc.hk/en/Forms/Intermediaries/Licensing-forms).
