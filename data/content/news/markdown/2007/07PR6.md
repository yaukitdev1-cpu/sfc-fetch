On-going investor education will continue to be an SFC priority this year, Mr Martin Wheatley, SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, says in the first issue of the[ SFC Alert](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/newsletter/06/nov_dec06.pdf) in 2007.

There are articles in this issue, published today, on the SFC’s initiatives in educating and protecting investors: January has been designated the Investor Education Month; the successful restructuring of Whole Win Securities so assets can be returned to clients; a warning to investors on a new type of online fraud; and action plan in addressing the risk of product mis-selling. There is also an article on a survey which shows that investors lack understanding of structured products.

This issue also carries an article on a visit made by SFC Chairman, Mr Eddy Fong, to the China Securities Regulatory Commission in Beijing last November.

The regular licensing matter bulletin board draws new applicants’ attention to some common shortcomings noted in applications. There is also an update on the Dual Filing arrangement, which reminds sponsors the importance of disclosure quality.

The SFC Alert is a bi-monthly newsletter for both investors and intermediaries. Copies of the current issue of SFC Alert are available at the SFC office. [All issues are available on the SFC website](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/speeches/public/newsletter/newsletter.html).
