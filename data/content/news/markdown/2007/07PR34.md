The SFC has issued FAQs to help licensed corporations or licence applicants better understand their choice of business premises.

The SFC has recently received enquiries from firms regarding the choice of premises for their business operations. The SFC would like to clarify that the Securities and Futures Ordinance does not prescribe any specific types of premises for a licensed corporation to conduct its business of regulated activities.

However, a firm should satisfy it that the premises chosen are suitable to conduct its business. In considering whether the premises are suitable, the firm’s management should take into account, amongst others, the following factors:


- The security of the premises and whether there is a proper segregated office area;
- Essential office equipment and telecommunication systems are situated in an area accessible only by the firm’s personnel;
- The firm has taken sufficient actions/measures to avoid confusion to its clients due to the co-existence of other firms in the same premises;
- Confidential or non-public information and client privacy will be sufficiently safeguarded against unauthorised access or leakage; and
- The premises are always accessible for visit by regulators.

For further information, please refer to the [FAQs](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/faqs/licensing/licensing.html) posted on the SFC website.
