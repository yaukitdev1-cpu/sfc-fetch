The SFC is pleased to announce that Mr Sun Jie will join the SFC as Senior Vice President – China Affairs, effective 17 May 2007.

Mr Sun has been with the China Securities Regulatory Commission since 1999 most recently as Director-General, Department of Investment Funds Supervision. He will join the SFC under a secondment arrangement for an initial term of one year.

The SFC's China Affairs team is responsible for advising on and implementing the SFC’s Mainland-related policy and work. Mr Sun will help the SFC with its response to the development of the Mainland financial markets and will play a key role in the SFC's liaison with the Central Government agencies, including the China Securities Regulatory Commission, China Banking Regulatory Commission, People’s Bank of China and State Administration of Foreign Exchange.

Mr Martin Wheatley, SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, said: “Mr Sun has extensive experience in the regulation of China's securities market and is well known to Mainland regulators. I am confident that Mr Sun will make a significant contribution through advising on and coordinating our increasing regulatory and market interaction with the Mainland.”

Before he joined the CSRC, Mr Sun had also worked in the Ministry of Foreign Trade and Economic Cooperation, the Chinese Mission to the United Nations (The Economic and Social Development Team) and the China International Center for Economic and Technical Exchanges. He is a senior economist with a master degree in Public Administration from Harvard University and a doctorate degree in Economics from Peking University.
