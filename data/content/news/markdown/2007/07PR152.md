The High Court today granted a final mareva injunction against Mr Ip Shu Kun, a licensed representative of Man Lung Hong Securities Limited, and his wife, Ms Chau Sau King, restraining them from removing any of their assets from Hong Kong, or dealing with or diminishing the value of any of their assets within Hong Kong. The injunction will remain in force until it is varied or discharged by a further order of the Court (Note 1).
