The latest Dr Wise’s Column reminds investors attracted to the potential higher return of emerging markets to consider the higher volatility too.

The SFC today publishes the January issue of Dr Wise’s Column, [*Investing in Emerging Markets*](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/features/doctor/features_87_226_339.htm), on its InvestEd website at [www.InvestEd.hk](http://www.InvestEd.hk).

Dr Wise notes that many emerging markets are still undergoing significant evolution in many areas, including their market regulation and infrastructure, and reporting standards. In addition, emerging markets investments are often less liquid as there may be government restrictions on free flow of capital.

As more companies listed in Hong Kong are from an emerging economy, the Hong Kong market also possesses some of the characteristics and risks of an emerging market including the exposure of Hong Kong listed companies to policy changes in Mainland China’s economy.

Dr Wise is a fictitious character. Through his column, he talks to investors about the key issues of investing and regulatory thoughts.
