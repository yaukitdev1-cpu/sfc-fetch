The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X} under Part XV of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

On 3 May 2006, {X}, a substantial shareholder of Grandy Corporation (now re-named as Hua Xia Healthcare Holdings Limited), sold 3.06 million shares in Grandy. He failed his duty to notify the Stock Exchange within the specified period regarding his disposal of Grandy shares.

{X} today pleaded guilty to one summons before Mr Joseph To, a Magistrate at Eastern Magistracy. {X} was fined $1,000 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $8,628 to the SFC.
