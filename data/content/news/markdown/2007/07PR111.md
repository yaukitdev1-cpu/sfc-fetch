The SFC is pleased to announce the Public Shareholders Group membership for 2007-2009.

To standardise the term of appointment of different committees established under section 8 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance, the term of appointment of the Group has been changed from one year to two years and the expiry date of each term from 30 June to 31 March.

The SFC has appointed 13 members to the Group for the next term, which commences on 1 July 2007 and ends on 31 March 2009.

Six new members have been appointed. They are Ms Melissa Brown, Mr Ashish Goyal, Mr Larry Kwok JP, Mr Anthony Muh, Mr Franco Ngan and Ms Elisabeth Scott. Five existing members retired from the Group. They are Mr Jamie Allen, Mr Rex Auyeung, Professor K C Chan, Mr Chan Wing Luk and Mr David Webb.

The Public Shareholders Group is a standing committee established under section 8 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance. The Group’s members include representatives of retail and institutional investors, professionals, academics and the current Vice Chairman of the Consumer Council. Last year, the Group held four meetings to discuss a wide range of topics. Views gathered from the Group have assisted the Corporate Finance Division of the SFC in considering issues relating to shareholders’ interests and formulation of regulatory proposals.

Mr Brian Ho, SFC’s Executive Director of Corporate Finance and Chairman of the Group, said: “The Public Shareholders Group has played an important role in providing insight and feedback to the Commission over the last year. The Commission very much appreciates members’ generous support and valuable advice. We wish to thank especially Mr Jamie Allen, Mr Rex Auyeung, Professor K C Chan, Mr Chan Wing Luk and Mr David Webb who are retiring from the Group. We also welcome the new members and look forward to working closely with them.”
