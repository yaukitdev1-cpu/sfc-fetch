The latest Dr Wise’s Column reminds investors that marking the closing price of a stock is a criminal offence of market manipulation.

The SFC today publishes the May issue of Dr Wise’s Column, [*“Marking the Close”*](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/features/doctor/features_87_226_343.htm), on its InvestEd website at www.InvestEd.hk.

Market manipulation includes fixing artificial prices for shares that is not the result of genuine forces of supply and demand. Therefore, if an investor places orders for a stock near the market close with the intention of fixing its closing price i.e. marking the close, that constitutes market manipulation.

Some of those who engaged in this kind of closing price manipulation have been convicted of criminal offences and received suspended jail sentences. Manipulators of this kind can also be fined up to $1 million.

Dr Wise is a fictitious character. Through his column, he talks to investors about the key issues of investing and regulatory thoughts.

Dr Wise’s Column is published on the last working Tuesday of every month. From time to time, additional articles in the form of special issues may be published on topical matters. The column is posted under the [“Features & Dr Wise”](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/features/index.htm) section of the InvestEd website.
