Stock exchange participant brokers reported total profits of $2.3 billion during the third quarter of 2006, a review in latest issue of the *SFC Quarterly Bulletin* shows.

The reported net profits of Category A, B and C brokers for July to September 2006 were $0.8 billion, $1 billion and $0.5 billion respectively. The total profit of $2.3 billion was 36% lower than the $3.6 billion in the preceding three months. This was primarily due to the shrinkage in market turnover.

The [*Quarterly Financial Review of the Securities Industry*](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/bulletin/sfc_bulletin/autumn06_feature1.pdf) is one of the feature articles in the [Autumn 2006 issue (No.65) of the *SFC Quarterly Bulletin*](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/bulletin/sfc_bulletin/autumn06_whole.pdf), which is published today. The issue also contains an article, [*Review of the Securities Market*](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/bulletin/sfc_bulletin/autumn06_feature2.pdf); and two research papers published in August and September respectively: [*Hong Kong as a Leading Financial Centre in Asia*](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/bulletin/sfc_bulletin/autumn06_feature3.pdf) and [*The Retail Structured Notes Market in Hong Kong amid a Rate Hike Cycle*](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/bulletin/sfc_bulletin/autumn06_feature4.pdf)*.*

The issue also includes a speech delivered by SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, Mr Martin Wheatley, at the Asia Pacific Real Estate Securitisation Summit Euromoney Seminars (Note 1).
