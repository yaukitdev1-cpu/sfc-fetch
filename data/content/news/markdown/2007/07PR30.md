Hong Kong offers international investors a single platform for access to good quality Mainland companies, SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, Mr Martin Wheatley told a group of senior executives from the Australian financial community today.

At a luncheon hosted by the Financial Services Institute of Australasia in Sydney, he said that Hong Kong as the premier fund raising centre for Mainland enterprises, with its significant capital market acting as conduit for funds investing both in and out of China, had indicated that it should be the prime choice for both investors and investment managers.

Mr Wheatley said Hong Kong had many qualities which make it the partner of choice for both Mainland enterprises looking to raise funds outside of the Mainland and international investors looking to tap into the growing Mainland economy.

During the talk, Mr Wheatley also highlighted the strengths of the Hong Kong capital market and the rapid growth of Hong Kong’s hedge funds market.

Mr Wheatley’s speech, “[Opportunities in Hong Kong and China](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/speeches/07/mw_20070306_fsia.pdf)”, is attached.
