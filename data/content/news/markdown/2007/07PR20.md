The latest Dr Wise’s Column reminds investors not to focus only on expected returns in selecting investments.

The SFC today publishes the February issue of Dr Wise’s Column, [*Balancing Risk Against Return*](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/features/doctor/features_87_226_340_h.htm), on its InvestEd website at [www.InvestEd.hk](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/index.htm).

Investors must take into account both risk and return to make optimal investment decisions. They should be aware of the concept of risk-adjusted returns for comparing different investments with different risks and returns.

One simple way to calculate the risk-adjusted return of an investment is to divide its return by its standard deviation of returns. The standard deviation is a measure of how much the returns on an investment vary from its average return. The more spread from its average return, the higher the standard deviation and thus the higher the volatility (risk) of the investment. Investors are reminded that higher risks do not always lead to proportionately higher returns.
