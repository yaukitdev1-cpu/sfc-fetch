The number of exchange-traded funds (ETFs) authorised by the SFC has been substantially increased with the recent approval of six products, including the first ETF that tracks an index on commodities futures.

In its ongoing effort to broaden the investment choice for investors, the SFC has in recent years facilitated the introduction of ETFs which cater to the needs of the market. These include the first ETF in Hong Kong tracking the performance of the China A-share market in 2004, Asia’s first bond index tracking ETFs in 2005 and the first ETF in Hong Kong tracking the India stock market last year. The six new ETFs, two of which were listed today on the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong, have brought the total number of SFC-authorised ETFs in Hong Kong to 15. (Note 1)

The ETF that tracks an index on commodities futures will provide a convenient and efficient way for investors to gain exposure to the commodities market, an asset class that was previously difficult to access. In view of the growing investors’ interest in commodities related investments and the special features of commodities futures ETFs, the SFC will work closely with the industry to enhance investors’ understanding of these new products. Frequently asked questions (FAQs) will be posted under the ["Features & Dr Wise\Funds\FAQs"](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/features/funds/features_82_36_298.htm) section of the InvestEd website at [www.InvestEd.hk](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/index.htm) today.

Mrs Alexa Lam, SFC’s Executive Director of Intermediaries and Investment Products, said: “Investors can buy from a wide range of funds from all over the world through Hong Kong. We will continue to work together with the industry to make this choice broader, deeper and richer.”
