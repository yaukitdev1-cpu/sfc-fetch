The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X} for carrying on a business of advising on securities whilst unlicensed, contrary to section 114 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

{X} was the controlling shareholder of Gladstone Morgan Portfolio Services Limited, a licensed intermediary to carry on business of advising in securities. The SFC’s investigation revealed that {X}, in December 2004 and June 2006, whilst unlicensed, introduced and recommended clients to invest in funds through Gladstone Morgan and assisted them to complete the subscription documentations. {X} received commissions from Gladstone Morgan as introduction fees. The unlicensed activities ceased in June 2006 as a result of the SFC’s investigation.

{X} pleaded guilty to two summonses today before Mr Garry Tallentire, Principal Magistrate at Eastern Magistracy. {X} was fined $2,000 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $17,106 to the SFC.
