Investors are invited to participate in a cross-media financial knowledge quiz, the “Investment Triathlon”.

As a new SFC investor education initiative for the general public, this competition aims to help investors understand financial investments and their risks.

The Investment Triathlon consists of three stages which will take place through three separate media channels. In the first stage, which runs for three consecutive Tuesdays starting tomorrow, the public will be invited to answer questions published in the Hong Kong Economic Times and the SFC’s InvestEd website at www.InvestEd.hk. Twenty of the contestants who correctly answer the questions will be drawn to enter the second stage which will be broadcast on Radio Television Hong Kong. They will compete in pairs, in the “Investment Era” programme on RTHK, each Saturday between 13 October and 15 December 2007 (Note 1). The 10 winners of this stage will proceed to the final which will be broadcast by Cable TV in January 2008.

SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, Mr Martin Wheatley, said: “The SFC aims to be innovative in its investor education programmes. Through this cross-media activity, we hope to reach a wide spectrum of investors and enhance their knowledge of investments and the risks associated with different financial products and services. We are grateful to the HKET, RTHK and Cable TV for their support.”

Winners will receive attractive awards, including cash prizes of $30,000, $10,000 and $5,000 for the champion, first runner-up and second runner-up, and a dinner for the 10 participants of the final stage. The SFC is very grateful to Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited, the Hong Kong Institute of Directors, the Hong Kong Stockbrokers Association Limited and the Institute of Securities Dealers Ltd for their sponsorship of the prizes.

The [rules](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/general/general/press_release/07/07pr158_rule.pdf) of the Investment Triathlon are available at www.InvestEd.hk. Investors can call the SFC’s Investor Hotline at 2840 9333 or email to [InvestEd@sfc.hk](mailto:%20InvestEd@sfc.hk) for enquiries.
