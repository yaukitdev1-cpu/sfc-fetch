The SFC today publishes the Takeovers Panel’s decision that the comparable offer price for the “H” shares of Sanmenxia Tianyuan Aluminum Company Limited should be at the same price paid for its Domestic Shares, being the Hong Kong dollar equivalent of RMB0.1577 per share calculated by reference to the exchange rate prevailing on the date the mandatory offer was triggered.

The background to this matter is that a mandatory general offer for the “H” shares of Sanmenxia was triggered as a result of an acquisition by Tianrui Group Company Limited of unlisted Domestic Shares of Sanmenxia, representing 67.02% of its total issued share capital at RMB0.1577 per Domestic Share. This raised the question of whether the price paid for the unlisted Domestic Shares would be a comparable offer price for the ‘H” shares of Sanmenxia under the Takeovers Code. The Executive referred the matter to the Panel as the matter related to particularly novel, important or difficult point at issue. The Panel met on 1 August 2007 to consider this matter.

A copy of the [Panel’s decision](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/cfd/mergers/panel/panel_decision_20070803.pdf) can be found in the “Prospectuses, Takeovers & Mergers” – “Takeovers and Mergers” – “Panel and Executive Decisions / Statements” section of the SFC Website at www.sfc.hk.
