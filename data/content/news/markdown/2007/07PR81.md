The SFC welcomes the re-appointment by the Financial Secretary of Dr York Liao as a Non-Executive Director for two years.

Mr Eddy Fong, SFC Chairman, said: “I am pleased to be able to continue working with Dr Liao, whose valuable insights the SFC has benefited from in recent years. I am confident that his expertise and experience will continue to benefit the SFC and the Hong Kong financial market.”

The SFC also welcomes the appointment and re-appointment by the Financial Secretary of members to the SFC Advisory Committee, for two years from 1 June 2007 to 31 May 2009. The new appointed members are Mr Chew Fook-aun, Mr Douglas Eu, Dr Frederick Hu, Mr Christophe Lee, Mr Wong Kwai-lam and Mr Thomas Wu; and the re-appointed members are Mr Henry Chan, Mr Jack Maisano, Mr Blair Pickerell, Mr Peter Sullivan, Ms Ada Tse and Mr Yip Lai-shing.

Mr Fong said: “I warmly welcome all new members of the Advisory Committee. The SFC has a lot to gain from their knowledge, experience and support. I also look forward to the continued support and advice of the re-appointed members.”

Mr Fong also thanked Mr Thaddeus Beczak, Mr Ambrose Cheung, Mr Paul Chow, Mr Paul Fan, Ms Christine Loh and Mr Samuel Poon, who will retire from the Advisory Committee as from 1 June 2007, for their invaluable contribution to the SFC.

The Advisory Committee is set up pursuant to section 7 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance to advise the SFC on matters of policy regarding any of its regulatory objectives and functions.
