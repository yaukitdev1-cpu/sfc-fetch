The SFC has commenced a review of the approval process for marketing materials in relation to SFC authorised funds. This is part of the ongoing initiative to facilitate development of the fund industry and reduce its compliance cost.

A working group has been formed with representatives of the industry and the Hong Kong Investment Funds Association to review the advertising guidelines in the Code on Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds, Mrs Alexa Lam, SFC’s Executive Director of Intermediaries and Investment Products, said at a conference today.

The working group will consider the possibility of streamlining the authorisation process of marketing materials of SFC authorised funds.

“We recognise that the current practice of detailed vetting of all marketing materials for SFC authorised funds imposes a regulatory and cost burden on the market. With the help of the industry we want to examine how this burden might be alleviated without impacting the overall level of investor protection that we seek,” said Mrs Lam.

Mrs Lam added that the SFC had also recently engaged an external consultant to study the retail funds authorisation regime and processes in overseas jurisdictions. Once this report is available it is expected that it will allow the SFC to both benchmark Hong Kong's regime and also determine areas for further examination with a view to ensuring the regulatory environment is appropriate in the bigger picture of Hong Kong's role as an asset management hub in Asia.
