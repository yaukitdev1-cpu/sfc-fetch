The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X} for abetting and counseling an ex-colleague to mislead the SFC (Note 1).

An SFC inquiry found that {X} had abetted and counseled an ex-colleague to provide misleading information to an SFC investigator during an interview, in contravention of section 184 of the SFO.

{X} today pleaded guilty to one summons before Mr Garry Tallentire, a Principal Magistrate at Eastern Magistracy. {X} was fined $10,000 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $16,324 to the SFC.
