The SFC plans to change the position limits regime in December in a move which will further promote market growth.

The SFC today publishes the consultation conclusions on proposed amendments to the Securities and Futures (Contracts Limits and Reportable Positions) Rules. The amendments aim to introduce flexibility to the existing regime by enabling the SFC to authorise Exchange Participants, or their affiliates, to exceed the statutory position limits for Hang Seng Index and H-share Index futures and options contracts, by up to 50%, where this is required to serve their clients’ needs. (Note 1)

The SFC received seven submissions from 13 respondents (including one joint submission). Respondents were largely supportive of the proposals although many suggested that the regime be relaxed further.

Having considered all views, the SFC does not consider it advisable to extend the relaxations further at this time. An analysis of the positions held in the market indicates that the further relaxations sought by respondents are not crucial at this time.

Mr Martin Wheatley, SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, said: “We believe we have at this point achieved a proper balance between market development and market stability. The need for further adjustment to the position limits can be reassessed in light of experience gained after implementation of the current proposals.”

The SFC has however proposed several further changes in light of comments received. These include measures that will enable any necessary future adjustments of the 50% upper limit to be made within a shorter period of time, greater flexibility for positions held by group companies and clarifications that will facilitate applications to exceed the limits.

Changes to the Rules will be tabled before the Legislative Council in October for negative vetting and if the LegCo does not object, are expected to come into effect in December 2007. Corresponding changes will be made to the SFC’s Guidance Note on Position Limits and Large Open Position Reporting Requirements, which will also be gazetted to take effect at the same time.

The [consultation conclusions paper](/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/consultation/conclusion?refNo=07CP1) and submissions received from respondents are available on the SFC website at www.sfc.hk.

The Rules prescribe limits (position limits) on the number of futures contracts or options contracts that may be held or controlled by a person and require a person holding or controlling a reportable position to notify the recognised exchange company. Position limits are tools for the surveillance and regulation of the derivatives markets. They help prevent the build up of excessive and over-concentrated positions which may pose a threat to the orderly functioning and financial stability of the markets.
