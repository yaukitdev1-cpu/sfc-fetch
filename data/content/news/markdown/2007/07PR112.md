Investors are reminded to find out about the company and make informed investment decisions based on the fundamentals before participating in an initial public offering (IPO).

As part of its investor education leaflet series, the SFC has published a new leaflet, *IPO Investing*, following the publication of *How to Make a Complaint* and *Know Your Broker* in April and June respectively. Leaflets on other investment topics will be published later this year.

The new leaflet discusses what investors should note when subscribing for IPO shares and the risks involved. Investors should:


- find out from the prospectus essential information on the company and the timetable of the IPO;
- read the instructions on the application form and fill in the form correctly;
- avoid submitting multiple applications;
- contact the share registrar if they have not received their refund cheques or share certificates on time; and
- take into account interest cost when subscribing on margin.

Last but not least, investors must understand that IPO shares may not necessarily rise above the issue price on commencement of trading.

The English and Chinese versions of the leaflet are now available at the SFC office, and can be viewed in the [“Publications”](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/publications/publications_2.htm) section of the SFC-operated investor portal, the InvestEd website at [www.InvestEd.hk](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/index.htm). Intermediaries are invited to display and distribute the leaflet to their clients.
