The SFC would continue to enhance the efficiency of the market, SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, Mr Martin Wheatley, said today.

This was critical to the development of the fund management industry in Hong Kong as managers tended to be drawn to efficient markets, he said at a [luncheon of the Hong Kong Investment Funds Association](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/speeches/07/mw_20070201_hkifa.pdf).

Mr Wheatley said the SFC had over the past years implemented many measures to reduce transaction costs, create a user-friendly trading environment and facilitate product development. The SFC would continue its work in the current competitive environment, he added.

For instance, the SFC has proposed amendment rules to adjust the position limit for H-shares index futures and options to cope with market growth. The SFC is also considering adjustments for the Hang Seng Index futures and options contracts.

Furthermore, the SFC is working on a mechanism that would allow for the suspension of the uptick rule on short selling. The rule may inhibit timely execution of transactions and affect the price-formation process in the stock market.

As one of the three key messages of his address, Mr Wheatley said: “The SFC is committed to maintaining a robust regulatory platform and an efficient marketplace and will continue to work closely with the industry to reinforce Hong Kong’s position as the premier asset management hub in Asia.”

He also said that market players should be ready to adapt rapidly to changes and seize the opportunities emerging from the Mainland market.

In addition, he said: “Hong Kong’s asset management industry was growing from strength to strength and all parties should all work together to ensure that it stayed healthy and competitive.”
