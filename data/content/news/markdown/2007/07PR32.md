Stock exchange participant brokers reported total profits of $14.1 billion during 2006, 151% higher than the year before, a review in latest issue of the [*SFC Quarterly Bulletin*](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/speeches/public/bulletin/sfc_bulletin/winter_06.htm) shows.

The reported net profits of Category A, B and C brokers were $5.9 billion (+119% from 2005), $5.7 billion (+160%) and $2.5 billion (+239%) respectively. The strong growth was underpinned by the surge in market turnover during the year.

The *Yearly Financial Review of the Securities Industry* is one of the feature articles in the Winter 2006 issue (No.66) of the *SFC Quarterly Bulletin*, which is published today. The issue also contains an article, *Review of the Securities Market*; and summary findings of the *Report of the Survey on Hedge Funds Managed by SFC Licensed Managers* published in October 2006 and the* SFC Stakeholder Survey* 2006 published in January 2007*.*

The issue also includes a speech by SFC’s Chairman, Mr Eddy Fong, on *The Role of the SFC Chairman – Towards a More Effective and Efficient Securities Market* (Note 1).

The Bulletin can be subscribed for at $400 per year or downloaded free of charge from the SFC website under [“Speeches & Publications” – “Publications”](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/speeches/public/bulletin/bulletin.html).
