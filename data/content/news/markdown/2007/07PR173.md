The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X1} for unlicensed leveraged forex dealing and {X2} for aiding and abetting {X1}’s unlicensed activities.

An SFC investigation revealed that between July and August 2006, {X1}, whilst unlicensed, solicited a person to open an account with Hong Kong Forex Investment Limited to trade leveraged foreign exchange contracts, in breach of section 114 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance. The investigation also revealed that {X2}, a licensed representative of Hong Kong Forex, aided and abetted {X1} to perform the unlicensed activity.

{X1} and {X2} today pleaded guilty to three summonses before Mr Garry Tallentire, Principal Magistrate at Eastern Magistracy. They were fined $1,000 and $2,000 respectively and ordered to pay total investigation costs of $30,283 to the SFC.
