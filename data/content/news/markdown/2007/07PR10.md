The January issue of the SFC Enforcement Reporter, published today, reminds licensees to have adequate internal controls to address the risks of misuse of confidential information arising from ‘sounding out’.

In December, the SFC suspended a licensee who misused confidential information that he was given for the purpose of ‘sounding out’.

During the month, the SFC disciplined three other licensees for market manipulation, cold calling, and false witness. The SFC also prosecuted four companies and six people for unlicensed activities, issuance of unauthorised advertisements, and disclosure failures.

Furthermore, the SFC obtained a bankruptcy order against a person to recover costs awarded by the Court and varied a Restriction Notice against Whole Win Securities Limited, enabling the prompt return of assets to clients.

Copies of the [January issue of the SFC Enforcement Reporter](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/enforcement/07/jan_07.pdf), which covers enforcement outcomes in the previous month, are available at the SFC office. All issues are also available on the SFC website under [“Speeches, Publications & Consultations” – “Publications”](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/speeches/public/enforcement/enforcement.html).

The public are able to subscribe to the newsletter by registering for the Update Alert service on the SFC website. They will then be able to receive the newsletter via email.

The public are also invited to provide feedback to improve the SFC Enforcement Reporter. Comments can be emailed to enfreporter@sfc.hk.
