The SFC is pleased to announce the appointment of members to the Committee on Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs) for a term from 1 September 2007 to 31 March 2009 inclusive.

The SFC welcomes Mr Glenn Fok as new member of the Committee.

The SFC also welcomes the re-appointment of Mr F K Au, Mrs Susan Chow, Mr Heung Shu Fai, Mr Stephen Hui, Mr C Y Leung, Mr Vernon Moore, Mr Alastair Murray, Mr David Richardson, Mr Brian Stevenson, Mr Kevin Westley and Mr K L Wong.
