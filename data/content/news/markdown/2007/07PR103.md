Investors are advised to exercise due diligence in finding and dealing with their broker.

The SFC has published a new investor leaflet, [*Know Your Broker*](http://www.invested.hk/invested/doc/EN/publications/Know%20Your%20Broker%20Leaflet_Eng_2007.pdf), to explain and remind investors of the key factors to consider when choosing a broker and the steps that they should take to protect their interest. Investors should:


- check whether a broker is licensed by the SFC;
- shop around and choose a suitable broker in terms of services provided as well as fees and charges;
- read the client agreement before signing it;
- beware of the pooling risk of margin accounts;
- closely monitor their account and deal with their broker properly; and
- consider opening an Investor Account with the Central Clearing And Settlement System to safe keep their shares.

Both the English and Chinese versions of the leaflet are now available for distribution at the SFC office. They are also posted on the “Publications” section of the SFC-operated investor portal, the InvestEd website at www.InvestEd.hk. Brokers are invited to display and distribute the leaflet to their clients.
