Proposed amendments to rules on the position limits of H-shares index futures and options have been [gazetted](http://www.gld.gov.hk/cgi-bin/gld/egazette/gazettefiles.cgi?lang=e&year=2007&month=2&day=2&vol=11&no=05&gn=18&header=1&part=0&df=1&nt=s2&newfile=1&acurrentpage=12&agree=1&gaz_type=ls2) today.

The amendments to the Securities and Futures (Contracts Limits and Reportable Positions) Rules aim to adjust the prescribed limits for Hang Seng China Enterprises Index (H-shares index) futures and options contracts from 6,000 contracts per futures contract month or options contracts series to an aggregate delta limit of 12,000 applicable to all futures contract months and options series (Note 1).

Subject to negative vetting by the Legislative Council, the new limit will come into operation on 30 March 2007.

The SFC has been working closely with HKEx to review the limits. The proposed adjustment will better meet the market’s needs and promote the growth of the futures and options markets.

SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, Mr Martin Wheatley, said: “The adjustment will help maintain Hong Kong’s leading position in the face of competition from the other exchanges which are keen to develop derivative products on Mainland related equities.”
The adjustment is also in line with the proposed measures outlined in the Report of the Focus Group on Financial Services under the Economic Summit on “China’s 11th Five-Year Plan and the Development of Hong Kong”.

The SFC is reviewing the positions limit of HSI futures and options contracts, and will consult the market within the next few months.

The SFC is also studying the other recommendations in the Economic Summit report relating to contracts limits and reportable positions.

Notes:

1. Prescribed limits are tools for the surveillance and regulation of the derivatives markets. They help prevent the build up of excessive and over-concentrated positions which may pose a threat to the orderly functioning and financial stability of the markets.
