The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X}, formerly known as {X}, for making unsolicited calls, contrary to section 174 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

An SFC investigation found that between April and May 2006, {X}, whilst employed as a licensed representative of Emperor Futures Limited, made unsolicited calls to a person whom she persuaded to open an account with the firm to trade in futures contracts. As a result, that person traded in corn futures through {X} and sustained loss.

{X} today pleaded guilty to one summons before Mr Symon Wong Yu Wing, a Magistrate at Eastern Magistracy. {X} was fined $5,000 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $8,790 to the SFC.
