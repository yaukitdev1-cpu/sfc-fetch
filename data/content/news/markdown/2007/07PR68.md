The latest Dr Wise’s Column reminds investors a callable bull / bear contract (CBBC) will be terminated once the call price is reached.

The SFC today publishes the April issue of Dr Wise’s Column, [*“Dating with Callable Bull / Bear Contracts”*](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/features/doctor/features_87_226_342.htm), on its InvestEd website at www.InvestEd.hk.

A CBBC tracks the performance of an underlying asset (such as a share or an index) on an almost one to one basis. A significant feature of a CBBC is that the issuer must terminate it and its trading before expiry once the price of the underlying asset moves against the anticipated trend, and reaches or goes beyond the call price. Such a call feature will limit an investor’s loss up to the initial investment. However, investors should be prepared for the worst-case scenario.

Before buying a CBBC, investors should understand all its key features and risks. Besides the call feature, investors should also note that the price movement of a CBBC may not exactly match that of the underlying asset when the underlying price is very close to the call price.

It is also important for investors to read the details of the listing documents of CBBC, not just the marketing materials which often do not contain full information.
