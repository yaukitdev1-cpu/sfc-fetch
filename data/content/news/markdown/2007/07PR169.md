In a move to further facilitate market development, the SFC has introduced greater flexibility for managers of authorised funds to delegate their investment management functions overseas.

The SFC has implemented this initiative in response to the globalisation of fund management firms and the growing trend for fund houses to set up local affiliates close to the markets in which they invest.

For SFC-authorised funds, the investment management operations of the fund management company or those of the investment delegates should be based in Hong Kong or an Acceptable Inspection Regime (AIR), where there is appropriate regulatory supervision (Note 1). Applications for delegation of investment management functions to non-AIR jurisdictions have been considered on a case-by-case and exceptional basis.

In view of growing industry interest and business need, the SFC has developed a more flexible approach in allowing non-AIR delegation by managers of authorised funds when required for business purposes. To enhance transparency, the SFC has today issued guidelines to explain the key criteria under which managers who are SFC-licensees or whose investment management functions are based in an AIR, may delegate investment management functions to affiliates in non-AIR jurisdictions. Fund houses are also encouraged to approach the SFC directly to discuss any planned delegation to non-AIR jurisdictions.

“The guidelines highlight our continuing commitment to facilitating market development without compromising investor protection. International fund houses will benefit from the greater flexibility in the delegation of investment management functions. This will ultimately benefit investors as it brings in more funds from around the world, managed by those closest to the markets where the funds invest,” said Mrs Alexa Lam, SFC’s Executive Director of Intermediaries and Investment Products.

Details of the guidelines are given in a circular issued today by the SFC to fund management companies of SFC-authorised funds. The [circular](http://www.sfc.hk/sfcRegulatoryHandbook/EN/displayFileServlet?docno=H475) and some [FAQs](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/faqs/products/FAQ-UTMF%20Authorization%20_2007.10.18_%20Eng.pdf) are available on the SFC website under “Intermediaries, Licensing and Investment Products” – “Investment Products Related Matters” – “Circulars” and “FAQs” respectively.
