The [December Issue of the Takeovers Bulletin](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/cfd/mergers/takeovers_bulletin/07/dec_07.pdf), published today, highlights the recent ruling of the Takeovers Panel that a mandatory general offer should be made for China Oriental Group Company Limited and also the publication of the Consultation Paper on new procedures for hearings under the Codes.

The Bulletin also contains three Practice Notes:
1. that appropriate offers should be made for convertibles or warrants even if they are not exercisable within the offer period;
2. the treatment of certain assets for valuation purposes under the Codes on Takeovers and Mergers and Share Repurchases; and
3. a reminder to market practitioners to consult the Executive at an early stage to clarify the basis on which they can properly proceed in a transaction.

The Takeovers Bulletin is a series of periodic newsletters which contain short informative articles and information relating to takeovers in Hong Kong as well as Practice Notes on relevant issues under the Codes. Copies of all issues of the Takeovers Bulletin are available on the SFC website under [“Speeches, Publications & Consultations” – “Publications”](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/speeches/public/public.html).

The public are able to subscribe to the Takeovers Bulletin by registering for the Update Alert service and select Takeovers and Mergers on the SFC website. They will then be able to receive the Takeovers Bulletin via email.

The public are also invited to provide feedback to improve the Takeovers Bulletin. Comments can be emailed to [takeoversbulletin@sfc.hk](mailto:takeoversbulletin@sfc.hk).
