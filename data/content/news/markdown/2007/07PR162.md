The [Takeovers Bulletin](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/cfd/mergers/takeovers_bulletin/07/sep_07.pdf) published today contains three Practice Notes on the impact of the implementation of the Hong Kong Stock Exchange’s Electronic Disclosure Project on takeovers results announcements, clarification of the circumstances in which an offeror can raise his offer price after a “no increase statement” has been made and a reminder to parties issuing Code related announcements and documents that they are ultimately responsible for the information disclosed as well as for compliance with the requirements of the Codes.

The September Issue also highlights two recently published decisions of the Takeovers Panel as well as the publication of the Consultation Paper on proposed amendments to the Codes on Takeovers and Mergers and Share Repurchases.

The Takeovers Bulletin is a series of periodic newsletters which contain short informative articles and information relating to takeovers in Hong Kong as well as Practice Notes on relevant issues under the Codes. Copies of all issues of the Takeovers Bulletin are available on the SFC website under [“Speeches, Publications & Consultations” – “Publications”](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/cfd/mergers/takeovers_bulletin/bulletin.html).

The public are able to subscribe to the Takeovers Bulletin by registering for the [Update Alert service](https://www.sfc.hk/sfcUpdateAlert/jsp/EN/update_alert.jsp) and select Takeovers and Mergers on the SFC website. They will then be able to receive the Takeovers Bulletin via email.

The public are also invited to provide feedback to improve the Takeovers Bulletin. Comments can be emailed to [takeoversbulletin@sfc.hk](mailto:%20takeoversbulletin@sfc.hk).
