The SFC has recently established two industry working groups to identify growth opportunities for the Hong Kong REIT market, according to a speech made today by Mrs Alexa Lam, the SFC’s Acting Chief Executive Officer. These working groups will focus on growth of existing REITs by way of acquisitions and the launch of new REITs IPOs.

The SFC issued a circular last month to REIT managers clarifying that no additional licensing or authorisation requirements were needed for acquisitions, provided that they had sufficient and appropriate skills and processes in place to manage the new properties.

As regards IPOs, the SFC is working on proposals to streamline the processes. “It is an appropriate time to simplify or streamline our processes further to facilitate development of the REIT market,” Mrs Lam said. However the SFC will also continue to be mindful of investors’ interest and strike a proper balance between protection and facilitation. In this respect, the SFC expects REIT managers and sponsors to conduct their due diligence to a high standard and to properly discharge their responsibilities.

 “We will eventually see the securitisation of Mainland properties into REITs. In the meantime, we will continue to develop and strengthen our regulatory platform in preparation for these REITs,” Mrs Lam said.
