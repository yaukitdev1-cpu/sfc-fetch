Dr Wise today reminds investors to understand the advantages and disadvantages of investment-linked assurance schemes (ILAS) before they invest in these products.

The SFC today publishes the September issue of Dr Wise’s Column, [*“Are Investment-linked Assurance Schemes Suitable for You?”*](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/features/doctor/features_87_226_347.htm), on its InvestEd website at www.InvestEd.hk.

There are several types of ILAS and those requiring regular premium payments within a pre-determined contribution term are the most popular. Some ILAS may offer investors a higher upfront bonus if they choose a longer term. However, it is likely that investors will also be charged a higher surrender fee if they withdraw their money early.

Before investing in an ILAS with a pre-determined contribution term, investors should consider how much they can set aside for making regular premium payments and for how long they want to invest. Investors should also assess how much risk they can take as the return of an ILAS, like other investment products with underlying assets, usually depends on the performance of the underlying investments that they choose.
