Over the past several months the SFC has been involved with various initiatives to facilitate the development of the market, particularly the fund industry.

In particular, the SFC signed an MOU with the China Banking Regulatory Commission, enabling Hong Kong to play a more active role in the development of Mainland banks’ QDII business. Licensing processes for overseas hedge fund managers were also streamlined and simplified.

In the SFC’s First Quarterly Report for the year ended 31 March 2008, published today, SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, Mr Martin Wheatley, said that the initiative taken with respect to overseas hedge fund managers was the first step of a commitment to streamlining licensing processes more generally for all types of licensees. He also noted that it was encouraging to see that the number of licensees had exceeded 30,000 for the first time.

On the regulatory front, the SFC prosecuted 26 entities, took disciplinary action against 12 entities and referred one case to the Financial Secretary for consideration of proceedings before the Market Misconduct Tribunal.

The SFC also launched a new full-length radio programme to enhance the public’s awareness of risks involved in IPOs and structured products.

The SFC’s revenue increased 17% over the previous quarter to $435 million as levy income rose on increased market activity. As a result the SFC recorded a surplus of $304 million for the quarter, and its reserves stood at $2.3 billion at end-June.

The [Quarterly Report](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/quartely/apr_jun_07.pdf) for 1 April to 30 June 2007 is available on the SFC website.
