Finding the right balance between investor protection and facilitation of market development is possibly the biggest challenge for the SFC over the next few years, according to Mr Eddy Fong, SFC’s Chairman.

At an Estate Agents Authority seminar, Mr Fong said the SFC aimed to achieve an appropriate balance between facilitating the efficient functioning and continuing development of the securities and futures markets and at the same time providing adequate, but not absolute, protection to investors.

In enforcement, the aim was not to identify and prosecute all minor breaches, said Mr Fong. Instead the SFC encouraged compliance and good business ethics through the selective sanctioning of malpractices and enforcement action, he said.

“Our experience shows that most of the market practitioners are compliant with the laws and regulations. For this group, the role of the SFC is to help and guide them on compliance,” he said.

In his speech, Mr Fong also examined the roles of the SFC and the Estate Agents Authority in the regulation of intermediaries. He said that both the financial and real estate sectors were major economic activities in Hong Kong and the two organisations were responsible for maintaining the standards in their respective markets so as to enhance the reputation of Hong Kong as an IFC.
