A series of investor education seminars on more advanced investment topics is to be launched for the public.

The new series of seminars will principally focus on stock analysis but will also include a session on retirement planning. The seminars are jointly organised by the SFC, The Hong Kong Society of Financial Analysts (HKSFA) and the Li Ka Shing Institute of Professional and Continuing Education (LiPACE) of The Open University of Hong Kong (OUHK).

Mr Martin Wheatley, SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, said: “Stock picking is only for experienced investors. These seminars will help provide experienced investors with useful information about stock analysis and risk management. We are pleased to partner with the HKSFA and the OUHK to host these seminars.”

The public can now enrol online at the OUHK website ([www.ouhk.edu.hk/lipace_seminars](http://www.ouhk.edu.hk/WCM/?FUELAP_OP=FUELOP_NewScreen&lang=eng&FUELAP_SITEDBID=SITE_-66&MYOUHK=false&ITEMID=CCLIPACECONTENT_965629&FUELAP_TEMPLATENAME=tcSingPage)) or contact LiPACE on 3120 9988 or [lipace@ouhk.edu.hk](mailto:lipace@ouhk.edu.hk) for details. Enrolment is on a first-come-first-served basis.
