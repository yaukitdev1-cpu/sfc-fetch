The SFC supports the initiatives of the Financial Secretary, Mr Henry Tang, to enhance Hong Kong’s competitiveness, so that it will flourish as a world-class international financial centre for China.

SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, Mr Martin Wheatley, said: “The SFC will continue to work with the Government, HKEx and market practitioners to implement the securities market related initiatives in the Budget Speech.”

The SFC has been working with the Government to give statutory backing to major listing requirements. Besides, the SFC and HKEx are considering ways to broaden the source of listing enterprises.

The SFC attaches great importance to investor protection and education and is studying ways to utilise part of the levy income to enhance the breadth and depth of investor education.

The SFC will also actively participate in other measures that are aimed to strengthen Hong Kong’s international financial centre status, including those identified in the Economic Summit on “China’s 11th Five-Year Plan and the Development of Hong Kong”.
