The SFC welcomes the findings contained in the latest Annual Report of the Process Review Panel for the SFC, which is published by the Government today.

In 2006, the Panel reviewed 40 completed cases and SFC procedures covering various areas of its work. The SFC is pleased to note the Panel’s conclusion that, in general, the SFC had been adhering to established internal procedures in its decisions and actions.

The SFC welcomes the recommendations made by the Panel and has adopted certain specific recommendations. The SFC notes that some of the Panel's recommendations had already been fully or partially addressed as the SFC constantly upgrades its internal procedures to enhance the effectiveness and efficiency of regulation.

The SFC also thanks the industry practitioners for their comments.

The SFC looks forward to continuing its co-operation with the Panel this year.
