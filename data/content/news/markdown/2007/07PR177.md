Dr Wise today reminds investors to take into account their ability to withstand losses as well as the desire to make gains before investing, especially in a volatile market.

The SFC today publishes the October issue of Dr Wise’s Column, [*“Remember the Basics and Beware of the Herd”*](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/features/doctor/features_87_226_348.htm), on its InvestEd website at [www.InvestEd.hk](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/index.htm).

Before making any investment decisions, new and the more experienced investors are reminded to consider the investment basics such as knowing what they are buying, understanding the risk, knowing their ability and limits, and setting clear investment objectives. Investors should be extra cautious in a volatile market when quick profits seem to be gained easily but where quick losses are also possible. They should take into account their ability to withstand losses as well as the desire to make gains.

Investors are reminded that Hong Kong is influenced by economic issues in other major markets, not just the Mainland, and it is important that investors take this into account.

Dr Wise is a fictitious character. Through his column, he talks to investors about the key issues of investing and regulatory thoughts.
