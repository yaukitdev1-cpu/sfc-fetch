The SFC proposes to introduce procedural rules for hearings under the Codes on Takeovers and Mergers and Share Repurchases to facilitate fair, efficient and timely decision-making.

These proposals, formulated in close consultation with the Takeovers Panel, are set out in a consultation paper issued by the SFC today.

In the interests of fairness and transparency it is proposed that disciplinary proceedings before the Panel should be held in public and chaired by an experienced litigation counsel/solicitor or a retired judge. This should facilitate the smooth management of the hearings process. Clear and concise procedures are also proposed for non-disciplinary hearings under the Codes.

The public are invited to submit comments in writing before close of business on 14 December 2007. A consultation conclusions paper will be published after the end of the consultation period.
