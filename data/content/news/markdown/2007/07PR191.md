The SFC proposes to require that all licensed corporations submit their financial returns electronically. This will facilitate efficient handling and minimise manual errors in the current data collection process. The proposed amendments to the Securities and Futures (Financial Resources) Rules, or FRRs, are set out in a consultation paper issued today.

At present, licensed corporations may submit their financial returns electronically on a voluntary basis. They can do so through the FinNet, a closed financial industry network, or by sending a computer diskette to the SFC. Under the proposal, e-submission will be mandatory and will have to be made either via FinNet or the Internet. To ensure security of e-submissions the SFC will issue private e-certificates to licensed corporations to validate their electronic financial returns.

The SFC considers that e-submission will be beneficial to both the industry and the SFC as the built in arithmetic checking function will help reduce clerical and input errors and receipt of data electronically will allow straight through processing and prompt analysis. The e-submission options also provide more flexibility to licensed corporations as they can submit the returns at any time before the submission deadline and can use any computer to do so rather than the present situation where it must be a computer specified for use with FinNet. This move to e-submission is in line with the practice adopted by other local and overseas financial sectors.

There should be no additional cost to the industry as the firms will only need a computer that meets basic technical requirements and the SFC will bear the cost of issuing e-certificates and provide necessary IT training and assistance, including on-site support. A trial run of e-submissions will also be conducted before the FRR amendments become effective.
