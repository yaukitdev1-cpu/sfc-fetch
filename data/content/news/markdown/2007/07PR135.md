The High Court today upheld an appeal against sentence by Mr {X}, who was convicted in April of dealing in securities without an SFC licence.

Earlier this year, ICICI Bank Limited was fined $40,000 and ordered to pay SFC&rsquo;s investigation costs of $54,860 by a Magistrate at Eastern Magistracy following an SFC investigation into unlicensed activities by the bank and seven staff, including {X}. {X} was fined $4,000 while two senior banking officers were fined $25,000 each. The other four staff received conditional discharges (Note 1).

The Hon Mr Justice Tong upheld {X}&rsquo;s appeal against sentence, set aside the fine and ordered that {X} be conditionally discharged on his own recognisance for the sum of $3,000 for 12 months.

In doing so, the Court recognised the disparity between {X}&rsquo;s fine and the conditional discharges given to four of his colleagues.
