The SFC has named January the Investor Education Month of 2008 to kick off another year of investor education initiatives.

Mr Martin Wheatley, the SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, said: “The New Year is a time for resolutions and we encourage investors to undertake a review of their investment skills and in particular their understanding of the risks that are always involved. Smart investors always do their homework and understand the risks before making investments and this is particularly important at present when markets are more volatile.”

For the second year running, “Know Your Risk” has been chosen as the theme of the SFC’s investor education month. The following are some of the activities which will take place:


- The highlight will be the final of the “Investment Triathlon”, a cross-media financial knowledge quiz. Ten contestants who have made their way through two qualifying rounds will compete for the championship and the top cash prize of $30,000 on 26 January. Cable TV will broadcast the event in February.
- For two consecutive weeks in January, the SFC will sponsor 10 time-check messages every day on Commercial Radio 1. Investors will be listening to important but easy-to-remember educational messages one at a time.
- From late January to early February, five-minute segments on hot topics will be broadcast during two prime-time programmes on the Radio Television Hong Kong’s Putonghua Channel (Note 1).
- Commuters will be able to watch an informative video on the common fallacies about warrants. On five days in January, the video will be screened on RoadShow services on board 1,600 buses every day (Note 2).
- For in-depth coverage on topical issues and in addition to the regular Dr Wise’s Column, feature articles will be published in the four January issues of Sing Tao Daily’s Investment Weekly magazine and in other newspapers and magazines.
- A series of four seminars is co-organised with the Li Ka Shing Institute of Professional and Continuing Education (LiPACE) of the Open University of Hong Kong. Experienced practitioners will speak on topics such as investment strategies, market risks and portfolio management (Note 3).
- Workshops on personal financial management will be run in collaboration with the Institute of Financial Planners of Hong Kong, targeting secondary school teachers.

The SFC is grateful for the co-operation of the media, where many investors look to for information and knowledge, and for the support of organisations and individuals participating in this meaningful campaign.
