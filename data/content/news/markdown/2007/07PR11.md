It is essential for the SFC to find the right balance between investor protection and facilitation of market development, SFC Chairman, Mr Eddy Fong, said today.

Mr Fong said that the SFC had a key role to play in supporting Hong Kong’s continued development as a leading international financial centre. In fulfilling this role, the SFC would join hands with its stakeholders to leverage on the existing strengths of Hong Kong as a financial centre, he said.

Speaking at a luncheon of the Hong Kong Institute of Directors, Mr Fong said striking the appropriate balance would remain the biggest challenge for the SFC.

"The SFC has a core mission to protect the interest of investors. We must therefore set high regulatory standards for the market and encourage the use of best practices. We must continue to enforce against misconduct and illegal practices in the market firmly and fairly. This cannot be compromised,” he said.

He said Hong Kong’s rules and regulations must be effective without creating unnecessary or undue compliance burdens, and processes must be efficient and user friendly.

In his speech, Mr Fong also outlined the respective roles and responsibilities of the Chairman and the Chief Executive Officer of the SFC.

“I believe that the most vital role of the Chairman is to call upon all the members of the Board to fully participate in the affairs of the Commission, which include not only spending time considering and endorsing proposals put forward by the executives, but also setting strategies and directions for the future,” he said. “Together as a Board, we should bring ‘an independent and informed judgment’ to bear on every matter that comes to the Board for decision.”
