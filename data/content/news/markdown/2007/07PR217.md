Hedge fund managers have benefited from the SFC's streamlined licensing process and the total number of licences granted in the first nine months of 2007 has exceeded that for the whole of last year, according to the SFC’s second [Quarterly Report](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/quartely/jul_sep_07.pdf) for the year ending 31 March 2008, which is published today.

The SFC's Chief Executive Officer, Mr Martin Wheatley, said the continued growth of the market had also lifted the total number of all licensed intermediaries at the end of September to another record of 32,208.

The Quarterly Report also outlines the measures that the SFC has introduced to facilitate development of the market in the past months. These included a proposal to increase the positions that Exchange Participants and their clients can take in the derivatives market, and initiatives to support the CEPA IV arrangements and the QDII scheme.

During the quarter, the SFC took regulatory action against a broker to protect client interests following a report of misappropriation of client assets by a licensed representative.

The SFC launched its first financial knowledge quiz in September with three different media channels to enhance investors’ knowledge of investment and the risks associated with different financial products and services.

The SFC’s revenue increased 49% over the previous quarter to $650 million as levy income rose on increased market activity. The SFC recorded a surplus of $516 million for the quarter, and its reserves stood at $2.8 billion at end-September.

The [Quarterly Report for 1 July to 30 September 2007](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/quartely/jul_sep_07.pdf) is available on the SFC website.
