The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X} for holding himself out as carrying on a business in a regulated activity, namely asset management, in contravention of section 114 (1)(b) of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

An SFC investigation revealed that between May 2005 and May 2006, whilst not licensed by the SFC in any capacity, {X} proposed to a number of clients that he could manage their online trading accounts and trade in securities on their behalf for a commission on any profits made from the transactions.

{X} today pleaded guilty to one summons before Mr Symon Wong Yu Wing, a Magistrate at Eastern Magistracy. {X} was fined $5,000 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $14,326 to the SFC.
