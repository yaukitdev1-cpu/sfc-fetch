Investors are advised to ask the right questions before investing in a fund so as to make informed decisions.

Following the recent publications of *How to Make a Complaint,* *Know Your Broker* and *IPO Investing*, the SFC has published another leaflet, [*Fund Investing*](http://www.invested.hk/invested/doc/EN/publications/Fund_Investing_Leaflet_2007_EN.pdf)*,* as part of its investor education leaflet series*.*

The new leaflet provides investors with a checklist of questions to ask before investing in funds, covering the authorisation status, key features, dealing procedures, fees and charges. For example, investors should find out:


- Is the fund SFC-authorised?
- What are the fund’s investment objective, strategies and asset mix?
- What are the major risks?
- What are the buying and selling procedures?
- What are the main fees and expenses?
- How can price information be obtained?

Investors can find out the answers to such questions from the fund’s product literature, such as the offering document, fact sheet and financial reports.

The English and Chinese versions of the leaflet are now available at the SFC office, and can be viewed in the “Publications” section of the SFC-operated investor portal, the InvestEd website at www.InvestEd.hk. Intermediaries are invited to display and distribute the leaflet to their clients.
