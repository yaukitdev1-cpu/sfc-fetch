Changes to the position limits regime, arising from amendments to the Securities and Futures (Contracts Limits and Reportable Positions) Rules (Rules) and the SFC’s Guidance Note on Position Limits and Large Open Position Reporting Requirements (Guidance Note), came into effect today.

The changes introduce flexibility to the existing regime by enabling the SFC to authorise Exchange Participants, or their affiliates, to exceed the statutory position limits for Hang Seng Index futures and options contracts and H-share Index futures and options contracts, by up to 50% where this is required to serve clients’ needs.

The changes to the Rules were subject to negative vetting by the Legislative Council. That process ended on 19 December 2007 and the changes came into effect today. A copy of the [final changes to the Rules](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/general/general/press_release/07/07pr228_annex.pdf) is attached for ready reference. The final version of the [Guidance Note](http://www.sfc.hk/sfcRegulatoryHandbook/EN/displayFileServlet?docno=H483) is accessible via the SFC’s website.
