The SFC is pleased to announce the appointment of three new members to the Dual Filing Advisory Group.

The SFC welcomes the appointment of Mr Alex Fong, Mr Christopher Howe and Ms Eirene Yeung, and the re-appointment of Mr Ashley Alder, Mr David Cheung, Mr Raymond Lee, Mr Gavin Nesbitt, Mr Terry Ng, Mr James Siu, Mr Frank J Slevin, Mr Carlson Tong, JP and Mr V-Nee Yeh. The appointment and re-appointment are for a term of one year from 1 April 2007 to 31 March 2008.

The SFC would like to thank Mr Dennis Cassidy, Mr W Gage McAfee and Mr Mike Scales whose tenures end tomorrow, for their valuable contributions to the Advisory Group over the years.

The Advisory Group is established under section 8 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance. Members include investors, directors of listed companies, fund managers, and market practitioners, including investment bankers, legal advisers and accountants. Since its establishment in May 2003, the Advisory Group has provided valuable advice to the SFC on treatment of cases under the Dual Filing regime and the relevant regulatory provisions, as well as on related policy issues.

The SFC very much appreciates the efforts of the members in performing the functions of the Advisory Group effectively and transparently. The SFC is confident that with their expertise and significant experience in the financial and investment sector, the members will continue to make valuable contributions to the Advisory Group.
