The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X1} for unlicensed securities dealing and {X2} for aiding and abetting unlicensed securities dealing.

An SFC investigation found that in March 2006, {X1}, whilst unlicensed, received trading orders of shares and warrants from clients of Kee Cheong Securities Company Limited. {X2}, his brother and a responsible officer of Kee Cheong, aided and abetted him to perform the unlicensed activities.

{X1} and {X2} each pleaded guilty to three summonses today before Mr Garry Tallentire, Principal Magistrate at Eastern Magistracy. They were fined $3,000 and $4,500 respectively, and ordered to pay total investigation costs of $9,492 to the SFC.
