Investors are reminded that although hedge funds are often marketed as “all weather” funds for different market conditions, their strategies do not always work for all market conditions.

The SFC today publishes an updated [leaflet on hedge funds](http://www.invested.hk/invested/doc/EN/publications/Hedge_Fund_Leaflet_2007_EN.pdf) as part of its investor education leaflet series (Note 1)*.*

The leaflet explains the basic concepts and risks of hedge funds. It stresses that hedge funds are only suitable for those who can understand and bear the risks involved. When considering a hedge fund, investors should:


- read the offering documents to understand the investment strategies and risks;
- ask questions about anything they do not understand; and
- avoid signing anything that they don’t understand.

In addition, investors are reminded that although unauthorised hedge funds cannot be offered to the public, they may be offered to private clients. If you are offered an unauthorised hedge fund, you should note that the fund’s structure and operations are not subject to SFC regulatory requirements.

The English and Chinese versions of the leaflet are now available at the SFC office, and can be viewed in the [“Publications”](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/publications/publications_2.htm) section of the SFC-operated investor portal, the InvestEd website at www.InvestEd.hk. Intermediaries are invited to display and distribute the leaflet to their clients.
