The SFC welcomes the new appointment by the Financial Secretary of the Hon Chan Kam-lam and the re-appointment of Mr Christopher Cheng as Non-Executive Directors from 15 November 2007 to 14 November 2009.

Mr Eddy Fong, SFC Chairman, said: “Mr Chan has considerable expertise and experience in public services. The SFC has received constructive comments from Mr Chan through his involvement in the Legislative Council Panel on Financial Affairs, both in his present capacity as the Chairman and in his previous capacity as a member. I am also very pleased to be able to continue working with Mr Cheng, whose insights have benefited the SFC a great deal.”

Mr Chan will succeed the Hon Jasper Tsang Yok-sing, whose term expires on 14 November 2007.

Mr Fong said: “I am grateful to Mr Tsang for his valuable independent advice on the work of the SFC in the past six years.”

Mr Tsang has been an SFC Non-Executive Director since 15 November 2001. He sits on the Remuneration Committee and SFC (HKEC Listing) Appeals Committee.
