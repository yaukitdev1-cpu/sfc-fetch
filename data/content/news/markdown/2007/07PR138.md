The SFC is publishing a new bulletin to focus on providing intermediaries and other market practitioners with useful information about regulatory developments and practice.

The SFC Bulletin, which replaces the SFC Alert, is published today. This issue reports on the growth in Hong Kong’s fund management business, as reflected in the SFC’s Fund Management Activities Survey. The article also highlights the SFC’s initiatives to streamline and simplify the licensing process of overseas hedge fund managers.

There has been an increase in newly approved hedge fund managers this year. We have published in this issue a profile of some local start-ups. We also draw market practitioners’ attention to the importance of compliance with anti-money laundering regulations and providing meaningful prospectus disclosure relating to the use of IPO proceeds.

The [SFC Bulletin](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/newsletter/07/aug_07.pdf) is available on the [SFC website](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/). Interested parties can subscribe to the newsletter by registering for the [Update Alert](https://www.sfc.hk/sfcUpdateAlert/jsp/EN/update_alert.jsp) service on the SFC website. They will then be able to receive the newsletter via email.

The public are also invited to provide feedback to improve the SFC Bulletin. Comments can be emailed to [sfcbulletin@sfc.hk](mailto:sfcbulletin@sfc.hk).
