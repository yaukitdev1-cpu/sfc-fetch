The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X1} and {X2} for holding out as performing regulated functions for an unlicensed company in relation to leveraged foreign exchange trading.

An SFC investigation found that between March and August 2005, {X1} and {X2}, a business director and an account executive of Sincere Bullion Limited respectively, induced a subordinate to open an account with an unlicensed company incorporated in Macau, Tse&rsquo;s Investimento Internacional (Macau) Limitada, to trade leveraged foreign exchange contracts (Note 1).

In addition, {X1}, who was also a licensed representative of Hong Kong Forex Investment Limited, was also successfully prosecuted for aiding and abetting an unlicensed subordinate in June 2005 to receive and place orders for a client to trade leveraged foreign exchange contracts through Hong Kong Forex, a licensed corporation.

{X1} and {X2} today pleaded guilty to two summonses and one summons respectively before Madam Adriana Tse, a Magistrate at Eastern Magistracy. {X1} and {X2} were fined $3,000 and $1,500 respectively, and were ordered to pay total investigation costs of $31,221 to the SFC.
