Dr Wise today explains to investors the features of an investment strategy called the 130/30 strategy.

The SFC today publishes the November issue of Dr Wise’s Column, [*“130/30 Investment Funds”*](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/features/doctor/features_87_226_349.htm), on its InvestEd website at www.InvestEd.hk.

A fund which adopts the 130/30 strategy may have a combination of long and short positions of up to 130% and 30% of its asset value respectively. With the SFC’s recent authorization of the first such fund in Hong Kong, investors are reminded to understand the risks associated with 130/30 funds’ possible use of financial derivative instruments (FDIs) to achieve the intended short exposure.

The use of FDIs may increase the volatility of the fund and magnify investment losses. Investors need to know that the long and short positions taken by a 130/30 fund may not be correlated, and both positions could be exposed to untoward market movements at the same time, subjecting the fund to higher risks compared to a traditional long-only equity fund.
