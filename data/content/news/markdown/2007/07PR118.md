Investors are reminded to be wary of investment plans that sound too-good-to-be-true.

Recently, the SFC has received reports of an investment company holding seminars in Hong Kong to invite the public to invest in a mutual fund plan. The plan claims to offer a lucrative return of 25% per month (i.e. 300% per year). However, the plan only explains vaguely how it invests the money pooled. It has also been reported that participants are told that they will get bonuses by referring other people to join the plan, similar to a pyramid selling scheme.

Investors should note that the plan is not authorised by the SFC and the company and its representatives marketing the plan are not SFC licensees. The SFC continues to monitor the matter but does not consider it appropriate to name the parties involved at this time. However, several websites that are believed to be related to the company have been identified in the SFC’s Alert List (note 1).

The SFC would like to remind investors to be vigilant if they are approached to join investment plans:


- Check whether the investment plans are authorised by the SFC or other legitimate authorities.
- Check the background of the persons or companies selling the investment plans, including whether they are licensed. Take great care if they are on an Alert List or similar warnings have been issued by other regulators.
- Don’t invest in any investment products which are too-good-to-be-true.
