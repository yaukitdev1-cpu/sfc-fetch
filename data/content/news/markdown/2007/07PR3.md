The SFC is highly regarded by stakeholders, who believe its activities have a positive impact on the Hong Kong market, according to a survey.

The survey of stakeholders was commissioned by the SFC in 2006 and conducted by Chant Link & Associates Pty Limited, an independent market research firm (Note 1).

The survey sought to identify key changes in the perceptions of stakeholders, as compared with the previous survey conducted in 2001, also by Chant Link (Note 2). Professionals and market participants were asked for their views about the SFC’s programmes, its effectiveness as a regulator, and the areas where it should apply more focus. [A summary of the report is attached.](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/surveys/07/stakeholder_survey_20070111.pdf)

The survey found that stakeholders continued to regard the SFC highly and believed that its programmes had a positive impact on the securities and futures markets in Hong Kong. Overall, the SFC was viewed to have conducted its regulatory activities impartially and to have effectively enforced the Hong Kong market. As in the 2001 survey, the SFC was perceived as focussing on relevant regulatory issues. Overall, stakeholders regarded the SFC as approachable, easy to deal with and, overall, the best securities regulator in Asia.

The survey also found that stakeholders generally wanted the SFC to:


- Fill senior positions.
- Ensure the quality of listings, particularly from the Mainland.
- Support Hong Kong’s competitiveness in a global market: SFC should be more proactive in developing Hong Kong as a major financial centre and be less conservative in its approach to the introduction of new products.
- Exercise its powers to take on large enforcement cases and in the administration of dual filing.
- Address the high turnover of middle and junior level staff and the need to have more experienced/market familiar staff at these levels.
- Improve clarity of rules, speed of response and consistency of decisions.
- Reduce the need for unnecessary documentation.

SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, Mr Martin Wheatley, said: “The SFC is also a stakeholder in the Hong Kong capital market, and we value open and frank feedback, both good and bad, to help us make our programmes and activities more effective. It is a challenge for regulators to strike the right balance between regulating in the public interest and ensuring that regulation does not impose an unnecessary burden or stifle healthy competition and market development. The SFC is committed to working with stakeholders to achieve the right balance.”

“The SFC remains committed to providing the necessary training for its staff, and will focus more resources on attracting and retaining talent. The SFC strives for efficiency and effectiveness, and we will be considering how we can improve in this regard,” he added.

Notes:

1.
