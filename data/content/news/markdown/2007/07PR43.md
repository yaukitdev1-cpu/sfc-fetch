The SFC is pleased to announce the appointment of six new members and the re-appointment of five members to the Investor Education Advisory Committee (IEAC) for a term of one year from 1 April 2007 to 31 March 2008.

New members



Re-appointed members



Mr Paul Kennedy, SFC’s Chief Operating Officer and IEAC Chairman, said: “The varied membership of the IEAC provides the SFC with access to a broad spectrum of market knowledge and professional expertise which is invaluable in planning our investor education efforts. I welcome the new members and would also like to offer thanks to our outgoing members Mr Mark Fong, Ms Connie Lau, Mr Henry Law, Mr Siu Sai Wo, Professor Raymond So and Mr Howard Sou, for the valuable contributions they have made.”

Established under section 8 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance, the IEAC plays a crucial role in identifying areas that the SFC should study in order to further the interests of investors, and aspects of the securities and futures industry where investor education is desirable.
