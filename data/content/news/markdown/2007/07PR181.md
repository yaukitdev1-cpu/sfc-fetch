The [SFC Bulletin](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/newsletter/07/nov_07.pdf) published today outlines some recent steps that we have taken to facilitate market development.

We note that there are two significant trends in the financial markets – the competition between financial centres for the status as a leading international financial centre, and the vulnerability of the world’s financial markets to the impact of financial innovation. We believe these trends have long term implications for Hong Kong. As the market regulator, we must balance the desire for growth in products and services with the need to maintain quality and, ultimately, protect investors.

This issue of the SFC Bulletin reports on the initiatives that we have introduced to facilitate the growth of the market, bearing in the mind the need for protection and stability. These include the increased position limits that Exchange Participants and their clients can take in the derivatives market, greater flexibility for managers of authorised funds to delegate their investment management functions overseas, and other initiatives to tap the opportunities for the fund management industry that CEPA IV represents.

The SFC Bulletin is a bi-monthly newsletter for intermediaries and market practitioners. The Bulletin is available on the SFC website.
