Hong Kong provides a one-stop shop for Mainland investors who seek to diversify their investment opportunity outside of the Mainland, SFC’s Executive Director of Intermediaries and Investment Products, Mrs Alexa Lam said at a conference today.

She said Hong Kong offered a broad range of authorised investment products that could cater to all levels of investors with different risk appetites. She added that Hong Kong was ideally positioned to act as a bridge between the Mainland and the rest of the world for its close relations and proximity to the Mainland, and familiarity with the languages, cultures, practices and systems of the Mainland.

“Hong Kong possesses a rich pool of talent and can offer a comprehensive set of skills, experience and advisory services for Mainland institutions seeking to invest overseas,” Mrs Lam said at the Investment Company Institute’s 2007 Conference on Mutual Funds and Investment Management in Asia.

Mrs Lam also suggested that Hong Kong based fund managers take the opportunity to explore the possibility of how they could capture the promising opportunities that QDII opened up, as the actual investment in QDII products had so far been limited.

At the conference, Mrs Lam pointed out some global issues facing regulators around the world, including: (a) preferential treatments to certain investors through side letters and a lack of clear valuation policies for hedge funds; (b) issues concerning mis-selling of financial products; and (c) use of soft commission.

To address these issues, she said the SFC would work closely with the industry, other regulators and IOSCO.
