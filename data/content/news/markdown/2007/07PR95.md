The SFC has made significant achievements in strengthening market regulation, facilitating market development and educating investors last year.

In the [SFC’s 2006-07 Annual Report](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/speeches/public/annual/rep06-07/index.html), published today, we detail our major achievements in enhancing the standards of intermediaries and quality of the market, widening investment product choices and facilitating development of market opportunities, as well as strengthening investor education. These achievements are consistent with our philosophy of considering the interest of investors first when discharging our regulatory responsibilities.

Mr Eddy Fong in the Chairman’s Statement said: “Our core mission is to provide a regulatory environment that is conducive to both market development and market regulation.”

Mr Martin Wheatley in the Chief Executive Officer’s Statement said: “We need to constantly improve the efficiency of the market, maintain and improve its integrity and above all take measures to ensure that we promote and maintain investors’ confidence.”

Apart from a detailed operational review for the year ended 31 March 2007, the report also carries a discussion of our commitments on corporate social responsibility and gives an account of our finances.

The report was laid before the LegCo today following submission to the Financial Secretary.
