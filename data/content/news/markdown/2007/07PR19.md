The SFC undertook several measures to facilitate market development during the quarter ended 31 December 2006.

The SFC approved stock exchange rule amendments to make Stock Segregated Account services more convenient to use, while investors pay less in transaction cost with the reduced levy coming into effect on 1 December. The SFC also reviewed the prescribed position limits of H-shares index futures and options, which will come into operation on 30 March subject to negative vetting by the LegCo.

In the SFC’s Quarterly Report for October – December 2006, which is published today, SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, Mr Martin Wheatley said the SFC was reviewing the position limits of HSI futures and options contracts, and would consult the market within the next few months.

“We are also studying the other recommendations under the Economic Summit on “China’s 11th Five-Year Plan and the Development of Hong Kong” including those relating to contracts limits, reportable positions, and short selling, to enhance market efficiency,” he said.

On the regulatory front, the SFC introduced a new sponsor regime setting out eligibility requirements for those who wished to continue their sponsor/compliance adviser work. The SFC prosecuted 12 entities and took disciplinary actions against 22 entities for various breaches, including life-bans from the industry of several persons who misappropriated client assets.

The SFC continued to remind investors through education of the risks in investments, including market volatility, increasing complexity of products, scams and malpractices.

“Investors should seriously consider the suitability and the downside risk of an investment product before committing to it. They should manage their risks and money properly and should seek to improve their financial knowledge to make better investment decisions, ” Mr Wheatley said.

The SFC has designated January as the Investor Education Month with the theme “Know Your Risk” to promote the importance of understanding risks in investments.

On SFC’s finances, total revenue for October – December increased 40% to $372 million from $266 million in the previous quarter as levy income rose. The SFC recorded a surplus of $239 million for the quarter, and its reserves stood at $1.8 billion at end-December.

The [Quarterly Report](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/quartely/oct_dec_06.pdf) for 1 October to 31 December 2006 is accessible on the SFC website. Printed copies are available at the SFC’s office.
