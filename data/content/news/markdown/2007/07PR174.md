Investors are reminded that an index tracking exchange traded fund (ETF) may not necessarily follow the performance of its underlying index and the trading price may deviate from its net asset value.

The SFC today publishes an [updated leaflet on ETFs](http://www.invested.hk/invested/doc/EN/publications/ETF_Leaflet_Eng_2007.pdf) as part of its investor education leaflet series (Note 1)*.*

The leaflet explains the key features, benefits and risks of authorised ETFs, as well as the major differences between ETFs and unlisted index funds. For example, ETFs may not exactly match their underlying indices because of tracking error, which is explained in the leaflet.

Before investing in an ETF, investors should:


- find out from the offering document the information about its underlying index and management company, its investment objective and strategy, and the fees and charges; and
- assess whether the ETF is suitable for them in light of their investment objectives and risk appetite, and the outlay required to make the investment.

The English and Chinese versions of the leaflet are now available at the SFC office, and can be viewed in the [“Publications”](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/publications/publications_5.htm) section of the SFC-operated investor portal, the InvestEd website at [www.InvestEd.hk](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/index.htm). Intermediaries are invited to display and distribute the leaflet to their clients.
