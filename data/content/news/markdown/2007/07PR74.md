For the first time the SFC will run a full-length investor education radio programme. This has been conceived as part of the “Know Your Risk” investor education campaign for 2007. (Note 1)

Entitled “Investing 101”, the weekly, 30-minute radio programme on Commercial Radio One aims to enhance the public’s risk awareness. The series will run for 15 weeks starting on 3 May and will cover some of the hottest questions raised by investors, such as IPOs and structured products. Market professionals and SFC representatives will give investor education advice, and at the end of the show the first caller who correctly answers a question related to the episode will receive an SFC souvenir. (Note 2)

SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, Mr Martin Wheatley, said: “The mass media has always been a supportive partner in delivering investor education messages and we are grateful to Commercial Radio One for their help with this latest project. We hope that this new half-hour show will be effective in communicating some basic yet crucial investment knowledge to investors.”

The SFC has also arranged for highlights of its Cantonese TV drama series, “Wising up with Experience”, to be screened on the 1,200 buses of KMB and Citybus from 9 May to 8 June. An English-dubbed version of the series can now also be viewed in the [“Studio”](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/studio/studio_audio15.htm) section of the SFC’s InvestEd website at [www.InvestEd.hk](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/index.htm). In addition, new animation videos on various topics have been added under the [“Features & Dr Wise”](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/features/index.htm) section. The new topics include IPO subscription, company announcements and annual reports, bonds, real estate investment trusts and options, etc.

Investors are welcome to provide their views and feedback on the various investor education initiatives to the SFC by calling the Investor Hotline at 2840 9333 or sending an email to [InvestEd@sfc.hk](mailto:InvestEd@sfc.hk).
