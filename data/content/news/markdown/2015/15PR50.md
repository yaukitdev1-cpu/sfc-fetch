The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the appointments and re-appointments by the Financial Secretary of members to the SFC Advisory Committee for two years from 1 June 2015 to 31 May 2017 (Note 1).


"I warmly welcome the three new members who I trust will give valuable insights to the SFC, drawing from their professional expertise and experience. I also look forward to the continued support and advice of the nine re-appointed members," said Mr Carlson Tong, the SFC&rsquo;s Chairman.


"I would like to express my appreciation to the three outgoing members for their contribution," Mr Tong added.
