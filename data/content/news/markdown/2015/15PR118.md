The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today published its [Quarterly Report](http://www.sfc.hk/web/EN/files/ER/Reports/QR/201507-09/Eng/00_full_pdf.pdf) summarising key developments from July to September 2015.


Among the highlights featured in the report was the launch of the Mainland-Hong Kong Mutual Recognition of Funds scheme on 1 July. A symposium was organised to provide further details to the industry and discuss opportunities presented by the scheme.


The SFC&rsquo;s Intermediaries Division was reorganised during the quarter to allow for greater specialisation and a more proactive supervisory focus around key market segments.


In July, the SFC launched a consultation on proposed changes to financial resources rules for licensed corporations. In September it began a joint consultation with the Hong Kong Monetary Authority on mandatory clearing and reporting for over-the-counter derivatives transactions.


The SFC&rsquo;s annual *Fund Management Activities Survey* for 2014, released in July, showed that Hong Kong&rsquo;s combined fund management business experienced 10.5% growth year-on-year.


The SFC received 2,416 licence applications this quarter, up 20% from the same period last year. Sixty-one listing applications were received under the dual filing regime, an annual increase of 35%.


On the enforcement front, three licensed corporations were disciplined, resulting in total fines of $19.9 million. The SFC also started the first-ever proceedings in the Market Misconduct Tribunal over a listed company&rsquo;s breach of its obligation to announce inside information.


The report is available on the [SFC website](http://www.sfc.hk/web/EN/) under "[Published resources> Corporate publications> Quarterly reports](http://www.sfc.hk/web/EN/published-resources/corporate-publications/quarterly-reports.html)".
