The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) is pleased to note the publication of the annual report by the Process Review Panel (PRP) for the SFC.


The PRP, which published its report today, conducted a comprehensive review of 58 cases in 2014-2015 covering various areas of the SFC&rsquo;s work.


The SFC takes note of the observations and recommendations of the PRP and looks forward to working with the PRP in the coming year.
