The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the appointment by the Financial Secretary of Mr Lester Garson Huang and the re-appointment of Ms Mary Ma Xuezheng as Non-Executive Directors for a term of two years with effect from 15 November 2015 (Note 1).


"I would like to extend a warm welcome to Mr Huang who brings with him a wealth of expertise to the Board. I also look forward to continuing to work with Ms Ma who has provided the SFC with valuable insights in the past two years," said Mr Carlson Tong, the SFC&rsquo;s chairman.


"I would also like to express our sincere gratitude to outgoing Non-Executive Director Mr Lawrence Lee Kam-hung for his invaluable contribution during his time with the SFC," Mr Tong added (Note 2).


End


Notes:



- Ms Ma was first appointed as a Non-Executive Director of the SFC on 15 November 2013.

- Mr Lee has been a member of the Board of the SFC since 2009. His current term expires on 14 November 2015.
