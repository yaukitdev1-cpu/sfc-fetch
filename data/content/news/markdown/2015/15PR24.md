The Honourable Mr Justice Hartmann, chairman of the Market Misconduct Tribunal (MMT), has fixed a hearing date for the proceedings commenced by the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) against Mr Andrew Left of Citron Research in relation to alleged market misconduct involving the publication of a research report on Evergrande Real Estate Group Limited in June 2012 (Note 1).


The MMT has reserved 22 February to 8 March 2016 for the hearing.
