The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the measures outlined in the Chief Executive&rsquo;s Policy Address 2025 to support Hong Kong&rsquo;s continued growth as a leading international financial centre.


The proposed initiatives to strengthen the stock market and enhance the listing regime will reinforce Hong Kong&rsquo;s status as a preferred listing destination globally. Plans to bring renminbi counters and real estate investment trusts into Stock Connect are also set to deepen the city&rsquo;s connectivity with Mainland markets. On the innovation front, the proposed expansion in digital asset (DA) product and service offerings to professional investors promises to boost growth for Hong Kong&rsquo;s DA ecosystem (Note 1). These measures are well aligned with the SFC&rsquo;s strategic priorities.


As a key focus of the Policy Address, the fixed income and currency (FIC) markets will be further developed through initiatives including fostering bond issuance, promoting the establishment of a commercial repo market, launching Treasury futures, and upgrading financial infrastructure. These efforts will significantly diversify Hong Kong&rsquo;s markets and raise its appeal to global investors. The SFC and the Hong Kong Monetary Authority are working closely to set out a comprehensive FIC Roadmap, and will also co-host the Hong Kong FIC Forum on 25 September to explore new opportunities (Note 2).


&ldquo;The SFC is fully dedicated to sowing the seeds for Hong Kong&rsquo;s continued success by building on its established strengths in fund-raising and connectivity whilst actively driving innovation and diversification,&rdquo; said Dr Kelvin Wong, Chairman of the SFC. &ldquo;We will unwaveringly support the HKSAR&rsquo;s key initiatives to ensure Hong Kong continues to flourish as a leading global financial centre and plays a vital role in the high-quality opening-up of Mainland markets.&rdquo;


The SFC remains steadfast in its collaboration with its local, Mainland and international stakeholders to further the long-term sustainable development of Hong Kong&rsquo;s capital markets.
