The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) is pleased to announce new appointments and reappointments to the following committees with effect from 1 April 2025:



- Academic and Accreditation Advisory Committee

- Committee on Real Estate Investment Trusts

- Disciplinary Chair Committee

- Nominations Committee

- Public Shareholders Group

- SFC (HKEC Listing) Committee

- Share Registrars&rsquo; Disciplinary Committee

- Takeovers and Mergers Panel

- Takeovers Appeal Committee


The SFC welcomes all the new committee members and expresses its gratitude to outgoing members for their dedicated service and contribution.

The membership changes are listed below. Details are available on the SFC website, including [the committees&rsquo; responsibilities and full lists of members](https://www.sfc.hk/en/About-the-SFC/Organisational-chart/External-committees) with their titles and affiliations.
