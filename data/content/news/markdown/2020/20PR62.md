The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today commenced criminal proceedings at the Eastern Magistrates&rsquo; Court against Mr Ke Wen Hua for alleged false trading in the shares of Carry Wealth Holdings Limited on 4 September 2012 (Notes 1 & 2).


The Court adjourned the case to 27 August 2020 for plea-taking after Ke asked for more time to obtain legal advice at today&rsquo;s hearing.
