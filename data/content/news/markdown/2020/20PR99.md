The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today introduced upgrades to its [website](https://www.sfc.hk/en) following a comprehensive redesign.


The changes make it easier for users to find information and to view the website on mobile devices. A "New releases" section on the new homepage provides easy access to the SFC&rsquo;s most recent regulatory updates, including publications, press releases, announcements and executive speeches. Links to the SFC on [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/sfchkofficial) and [LinkedIn](https://hk.linkedin.com/company/securities-and-futures-commission) appear prominently at the top of every page.


Since 1996, the SFC has maintained a public website to provide the most up-to-date regulatory information. The rules, codes and guidelines administered by the SFC are available in readily searchable formats. Industry practitioners can find information relating to enforcement actions, short positions, authorisation of investment products and takeovers activities involving public companies. The website also highlights the SFC&rsquo;s global regulatory engagement and green and sustainable finance initiatives.


The SFC website provides easy access to the SFC&rsquo;s online portal, WINGS (Note 1), and to the SFC Fintech Contact Point (Note 2). In addition, it contains resources for the public to check the [SFC licence status](https://www.sfc.hk/en/Regulatory-functions/Intermediaries/Licensing/Register-of-licensed-persons-and-registered-institutions) of companies and individuals and to identify [SFC-authorised investment products](https://apps.sfc.hk/productlistWeb/searchProduct/main.do?lang=EN). An [alert list](https://www.sfc.hk/en/alert-list) warns of unlicensed companies which target local investors or falsely claim to have an association with Hong Kong.


Members of the public can use the "[Subscriptions](https://apps.sfc.hk/CampaignHelper/campaignForm.jsp?lang=EN)" service on the website to sign up to receive email alerts when new information is available.
