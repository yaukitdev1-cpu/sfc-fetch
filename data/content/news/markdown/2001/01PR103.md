The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it has reprimanded {X}, a dealer's representative of Concord Capital Brokerage Limited (Concord) under the Securities Ordinance. The reprimand stems from an inquiry which revealed that {X} had

- signed on a client's account opening documents as witness acknowledging that he was present when the client signed the documents when in fact he had never met this client;
- signed on the risk disclosure statements regarding trading in securities listed in the Growth Enterprises Market in this client's account opening form indicating that he had explained the content of these statements to the client when in fact he had not done so; and
knowingly allowed a third person to operate this client's account without proper authority and without the knowledge of Concord.
Commenting on the reprimand, an SFC spokesman said "it is essential for a registered person to know with whom he is dealing. Therefore when an account is opened proper account opening procedures must be followed. In this case {X} failed to follow such procedures by failing to meet the client even though he purported to do so. He also exposed both his client and employer to risk by allowing a third party to trade through the account without written evidence of the authority to do so."
