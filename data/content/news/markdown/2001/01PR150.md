The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today released for public consultation the draft Securities and Futures (Recognized Counterparty) Rules (draft Rules). The rules are to be made under the Securities and Futures Bill (Bill) when the Bill is enacted.

The Bill is currently being considered by a Legislative Council Bills Committee. Under the existing legislative framework, a recognized counterparty is defined to mean an authorized institution, another licensed leveraged foreign exchange trader or an institution designated in writing by the Commission as a recognized counterparty. The Bill has amended the last limb of the definition and provides the Commission with the necessary rule-making power to prescribe the qualifying criteria of a recognized counterparty.

This approach is consistent with modern securities legislation in order that regulators can respond efficiently to changing market conditions and practices.

In essence, the draft Rules captures, in addition to authorised institutions and licensed leveraged foreign exchange traders, four other types of institutions under the definition of recognized counterparty. The draft Rules also cater for institutions which the SFC believes should qualify as recognized counterparties but which do not fit into any of the prescribed categories by listing out such institutions in Schedule 2 to the Rules.

While there are controls already built into the legislative system whereby any rules made by the SFC must be subject to negative vetting by the Legislative Council, the SFC now releases the draft Rules for public consultation.

In the formulation of these draft Rules, the SFC has already consulted selected market participants.

Copies of the consultation document have been sent to all licensed leveraged foreign exchange traders by mail. In addition, copies of the consultation document are available for pick up at the Commission's office and can also be located at the Commission's Internet website at [http://www.hksfc.org.hk](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/consult/e_con_counterparty.pdf).
