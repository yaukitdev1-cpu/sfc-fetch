The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) warmly welcomes the appointments of Mr Donald Tsang as Chief Secretary for Administration and Mr Antony Leung as Financial Secretary. 

The SFC believes that both Mr Tsang and Mr Leung, with their wholehearted devotion to Hong Kong, excellent leadership skills and solid experience in the public service and financial sector, will take Hong Kong and the local securities and futures markets to a new stage of development.

To help Hong Kong remain one of the world's premier markets, and the principal fund raising centre for the Mainland of China, the SFC is working closely with the Government on the enactment and implementation of the new Securities and Futures Bill and the promotion of the Hong Kong markets to the international community.

With the leadership and guidance of Mr Tsang and Mr Leung, the SFC is confident of meeting the many challenges arising from market globalization. The SFC will continue its cooperation with the Government and strive to further strengthen Hong Kong's position as a major international financial centre.
