The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has today commenced its free monthly eNewsletter service (available in English and Chinese) for investors. 

The eNewsletter service is one of the SFC's initiatives in its ongoing investor education programme which aims to promote informed investors who are better able to protect themselves and who understand the risks of investing, the markets and the need to ascertain the key facts before investing.

The monthly eNewsletter will cover:

- Topical issues in the market
- "Investor Alert" update
- Latest news from the Electronic Investor Resources Centre (eIRC) and SFC
- Disciplinary actions taken by the SFC
From this month onwards, the eNewsletter, which is free, will be sent monthly to subscribers' email accounts. To sign up to have the eNewsletter delivered or to view the eNewsletter, investors can now visit the eIRC which is located at [http://www.hkeirc.org.](http://www.eirc.hk/) 

The inaugural issue of the eNewsletter, which was released today (1 June 2001), contains articles explaining how investors can take steps to protect themselves against unauthorised transactions and discussing certain key issues investors should consider when buying guaranteed funds. The "Investor Alert" section in the eNewsletter warns about unsolicited calls from unregistered brokerages that claim to have an office in Hong Kong.

The eNewsletter also contains news about the publication of the new investor education booklet "The A-Z of Stock Investing" and the airing of the radio series "Investors' Mastermind" on Metro Finance. On the corporate front, it informs investors that the SFC has published its Annual Report which highlights its major achievements in the financial year of 2000-2001. A comparative summary of enforcement actions taken in the last two financial years is also included in the eNewsletter.

Notes for Editors: The Electronic Investor Resources Centre (eIRC) is run by the SFC and is located at [http://www.hkeirc.org](http://www.eirc.hk/). The eIRC is a one-stop site which provides investors with the information that they need to learn about investing and helps them to carry out research on potential investments. The eIRC is currently hyperlinked to over 450 websites of regulators, market institutions and other Internet sites of potential investor interest. It features a "Special Topics" section containing multi-media materials on areas of special focus or topical interest. Since its launch in June 2000, the eIRC has received over 5 million hits.
