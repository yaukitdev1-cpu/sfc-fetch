The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) is pleased to announce the appointments of members of the Takeovers and Mergers Panel and the Takeovers Appeal Committee for the year 1 April 2001 to 31 March 2002 (please see attached list). 

The SFC welcomes Mr David Graham, Mr Gavin Nesbitt, Ms Patricia Shih, Mr Frank Slevin, Ms May Tan, Mr Carlson Tong and Mr David Webb as new members of the Panel. The SFC also welcomes the re-appointment of Mr Kevin Westley and Mr Clifton Chiu as Chairman and Deputy Chairman of the Panel and the appointment of Mr Henry Fan as additional Deputy Chairman of the Panel.

The SFC would like to thank Mr David Blackett, Mr David Halperin, Mr Anthony Lo and Mr Patrick Sun, who are stepping down from the Panel, for their valuable contribution over the years.

The Panel and Appeal Committee are voluntary bodies which owe their success to the dedication and expertise of their members. The SFC is confident that the significant experience of members in takeovers and mergers and the securities and financial markets will ensure the continued success of the Panel and Appeal Committee.
