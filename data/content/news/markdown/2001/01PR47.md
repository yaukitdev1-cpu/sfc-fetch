The Securities and Futures Commission today published a CIS Internet Guidance Note (Guidance Note for Persons Advertising or Offering Collective Investment Schemes on the Internet). 

The Note provides regulatory guidance to fund management companies and other financial intermediaries who advertise or offer collective investment schemes (CIS) through the Internet to the public in Hong Kong.

A draft Note was published last year for public consultation. Eleven written submissions were received and the SFC also met with some of the respondents. The industry has generally welcomed the new guidelines. The SFC has carefully considered the industry's comments and introduced some changes as a result.

Comments by the respondents and the SFC's conclusions are set out in a Consultation Conclusions paper, which is published with the revised Note.

There are no concessions to the fundamental principles of regulation from which the Note has been derived. A requirement to make an electronic prospectus available in an online offering of a CIS is generally considered necessary to ensure investors have the opportunity to access information that they may need for making an informed investment decision. Nevertheless, the SFC is prepared to consider alternative arrangements that may satisfy the same objective.

The Note is effective immediately. New applications for CIS advertising or offering on websites will be reviewed accordingly. There is a grace period of three months for existing websites to make any necessary amendments. Staff will implement the Note in a pragmatic manner.

The SFC would like to thank all the respondents for their useful comments.

Both the Note and the Consultation Conclusions are available on the SFC website. A limited number of printed copies are available at the SFC's office. The draft Note that was published for consultation is also available on the SFC website for reference.
