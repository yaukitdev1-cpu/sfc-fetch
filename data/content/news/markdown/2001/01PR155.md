The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the re-appointment by the Financial Secretary of Mr Mark Dickens as Executive Director (Supervision of Markets) for a period of three years from 1 April 2002 to 31 March 2005.

Mr Andrew Sheng, SFC Chairman, is pleased to have Mr Dickens' continued service. "The re-appointment is a recognition of Mr Dickens' outstanding performance and commitment to the Hong Kong markets," Mr Sheng said.

"Mr Dickens has contributed significantly to the Commission's work in the areas of corporate finance, enforcement and supervision of markets. With his wide-ranging experience and strong legal background, he will continue to play a pivotal role in supervising the operations of exchanges and clearing houses, enhancing the local financial infrastructure and strengthening the leading position of Hong Kong as an international financial centre," Mr Sheng added.

Mr Dickens joined the Commission in March 1991. He was Senior Director of Corporate Finance before his appointment as Executive Director of Enforcement in January 1997. In April 1999, he was re-designated to head the Supervision of Markets Division.

Prior to joining the SFC, Mr Dickens served in a number of positions in the Australian National Companies and Securities Commission from 1980, including General Counsel and Head of the Operations Division. He is experienced in all aspects of securities and futures industry regulation in two Asia-Pacific markets and has participated in the work of the International Organization of Securities Commissions (IOSCO) through various working parties.
