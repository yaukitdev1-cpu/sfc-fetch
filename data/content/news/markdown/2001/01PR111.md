The Chief Executive announced today the extension of Mr Andrew Sheng's appointment as the Chairman of the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) for a period of two years, from 1 October 2001 to 30 September 2003. In accepting the re-appointment, Mr Sheng expressed his sincere thanks to the Chief Executive for the confidence shown in him. 

Mr Sheng said: "I am honored and delighted to be able to continue working with my colleagues in the SFC and to serve the markets and the community. I am particularly grateful for the hard work, dedication and support of my colleagues over the years.

"Together with the newly appointed Executive Directors and all staff members of the Commission, we aim to further uplift the standards of the SFC as a world-class market regulator, which is fair, transparent and accountable to the public.

"Following the merger and demutualisation of the exchanges and clearing houses, work to enhance the local financial infrastructure and to modernise Hong Kong's securities legislation are now in full swing. I am committed to seeing through the implementation of these major market reform initiatives and to help Hong Kong excel as a world-class financial market," Mr Sheng said.
