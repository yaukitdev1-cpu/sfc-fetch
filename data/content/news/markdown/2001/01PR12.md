The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today announced the launch of its revamped website with a new design and additional functions. Market practitioners and members of the public can visit the website at www.hksfc.org.hk to find out the most up-to-date information about the SFC and the regulatory framework in Hong Kong. 

**A Wealth of Practical and Timely Information** 
In addition to the latest facts and figures about the SFC, the Securities and Futures Bill and the Takeovers and Mergers Panel's decisions, as well as the press releases and publications issued by the SFC, market practitioners will find the "Licensing Related Matters" under the "Intermediaries, Licensing & Investment Products" section particularly useful. Licensing requirements under the Securities Ordinance, Commodities Trading Ordinance and Leveraged Foreign Exchange Trading Ordinance are explained in detail. Comprehensive information on application procedure, fees and tips are also provided.

The investing public will also be interested in the "Registry" under the "Intermediaries, Licensing & Investment Products" section, which allows them to check whether an intermediary is registered with, or if an investment product has been authorized by the SFC. The "Investor Education" section also provides up-to-date information on investors' rights, obligations and risk exposure for their better protection.

**New Functions to Make Navigation Easy** 
The bilingual website has built in an instant language switching function to allow visitors viewing a specific page to go to the corresponding page in another language in seconds, just with a click of the mouse. It also combines a powerful search engine with a simple structure to enable visitors to quickly locate, read or download the information required.

The website will be an effective tool for the SFC to reach out to the market and the public, who can also get in touch with the SFC via the website. "The new website is designed to enhance the SFC's transparency, facilitate its communications with market practitioners and the general public, as well as promote investor awareness and protection," Mr Andrew Sheng, Chairman of the SFC, said.

The SFC welcomes suggestions from the public on how to improve the website. It will continue to introduce new features to the website as the market develops.
