The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today published a summary survey report on the business activities of securities and futures intermediaries and exempt persons during 2000. It is an annual exercise conducted since 1996. 

The summary survey report includes findings on primary business activity, gross operating income, client profile, custody of client assets, human resources profile, financial performance and business development.

Some of the major findings of the 2000 survey include:

- 79% of the securities dealers and 85% of the commodity dealers respondents cited stockbroking and futures/options contracts broking respectively as their primary business;
- In 2000, securities and futures intermediaries recorded a 21% growth in total gross operating income which was mainly attributable to the increase in stock broking income. The rise in brokerage income was in line with the market sentiment during the early part of 2000;
- In 2000, the proportion of exempt persons' share of gross operating income derived from the securities market decreased from 26% to 18%;
- The number of active client accounts in the securities and futures markets increased by 39% in 2000. Most of these new client accounts were retail clients dealing in stocks, unit trusts and mutual funds;
- Registered securities and futures intermediaries employed about 32,000 persons in 2000. Many investment adviser firms employed more sales and marketing staff in order to position themselves in the Mandatory Provident Fund market; and
- There were 90 firms who reported that they were providing online trading in year 2000. These firms were mainly securities dealers. 13% of the total number of clients traded through electronic means and generated about 11% of the gross operating income.
Information collected in the survey enables the SFC to maintain an up to date profile of market intermediaries which is valuable to the SFC when it formulates policies for the industry. 

The SFC wishes to thank those intermediaries and exempt persons who had responded to the survey.
