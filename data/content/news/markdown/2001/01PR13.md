The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the appointment by the Financial Secretary of Mrs Alexa Lam as a new Executive Director. 

SFC Chairman Mr Andrew Sheng said: "I am delighted to welcome Mrs Lam as our new Executive Director. Mrs Lam has played a key role in streamlining 10 existing ordinances into a unified Securities and Futures Bill. I am sure that given her expertise and substantial experience in corporate and securities law as well as her understanding of Hong Kong markets, Mrs Lam will be able to make very useful contribution to the Commission."

"Mrs Lam will, in addition to the role of Chief Counsel, be responsible for co-ordinating relations with the China Securities Regulatory Commission, corporate communications, investor education and assisting the other Executive Directors in internal administration and policy formulation. She will continue to play a key role in coordinating efforts within the SFC in pursuing the Securities and Futures Bill now under consideration by the Legislative Council," Mr Sheng said.

Mrs Lam graduated with First Class Honours in Law from the University of Hong Kong. She was also a Commonwealth Scholar and received her Master of Laws, with distinction from the London School of Economics.

Mrs Lam practiced law with various firms in Hong Kong, Chicago and New York, specializing in corporate commercial law. In 1997-98, she taught law at the University of Hong Kong, and joined the SFC in October 1998. She was appointed the Chief Counsel in August 1999.

Mrs Lam is married with two sons.
