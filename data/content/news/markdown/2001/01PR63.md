The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it had publicly reprimanded {X}, a dealer's representative under the Securities Ordinance (SO).

The reprimand stemmed from an inquiry into the conduct of {X} during the period January 2000 to August 2000 when he was a dealer of ASG Futures Limited (ASG Futures) under the Commodities Trading Ordinance. The inquiry found that {X} did not make proper inquiry to establish (i) the client's investment experience and objectives, and (ii) the identity of persons authorised to give instructions on behalf of the client prior to the establishment of the account. {X}'s conduct exposed his client and his employer to unnecessary risks.

{X} had since left ASG Futures on his own accord.

As a result of the above findings, the SFC concluded his conduct above impugned his fitness and properness as a dealer's representative under the SO and had therefore decided to publicly reprimand him.
