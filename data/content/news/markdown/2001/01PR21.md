The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced that it had successfully prosecuted Wong Kong Yui, {X1}, {X2} and {X3} for acting as a dealer's representative of SEG International Securities (HK) Limited (SEG) whilst not registered with the SFC, and Wong Kong Yui, Lau Cheung Man and Tam Cham Kai for aiding and abetting, counselling and procuring employees of SEG to act as its unregistered representatives. All pleaded guilty before Ms Polly Lo at Western Magistracy, the four unregistered representatives were fined a total of $14,500 and ordered to pay total costs of $21,580 and the three aiders and abetters were fined a total of $37,500 and ordered to pay total costs of $23,167.
