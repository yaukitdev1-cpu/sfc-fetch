The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has today published a *Consultation Paper on the Draft Code of Conduct for Share Registrars.* 

The Commission has been working with the Federation of Share Registrars (the Federation) to produce a non-statutory code of standards.

The resulting Consultation Paper, released today, sets out the proposed regulatory structure and represents the consensus of opinion of the Share Registrar industry and the Commission regarding the standards of commercial conduct and behaviour considered acceptable for Share Registrars. The proposals include:

- general principles and standards of conduct a share registrar should adhere to;
- requirements of a share registrar to ensure sufficient system capacity and data integrity;
- disciplinary proceedings for a share registrar;
- formation of a Disciplinary Committee and a Disciplinary Appeals Committee; and
- the Federation's role in implementing penalties
The SFC welcomes comments from industry practitioners and members of the investing public on the proposals in the Consultation Paper.
