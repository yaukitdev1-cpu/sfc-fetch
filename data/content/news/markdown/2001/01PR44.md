The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it had suspended the registration of Mr Kwong Kin Fai, Anthony (Kwong), a dealer's representative under the Securities Ordinance for a period of 3 weeks. 

The suspension followed an inquiry in which the SFC found that Kwong, a dealer's representative of South China Securities Limited had, by furnishing to an unregistered employee details of another employee, enabled the unregistered employee to dishonestly gain access to the trading hall of the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited and to execute orders in contravention of the registration requirements of section 50 of the Securities Ordinance.

The SFC concluded that Kwong's actions impugned his fitness and properness and decided to suspend his registration. In considering the period of suspension, the SFC had taken into account that at the time of the event Kwong acted thoughtlessly at a time of heavy trading activity and staff shortages due to illness.
