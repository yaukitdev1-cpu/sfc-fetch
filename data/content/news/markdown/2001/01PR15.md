The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the appointment by the Chief Executive of Mr Dannis Lee Jor-hung, Mr Michael Sze Tsai-ping and Mr Peter Wong Shiu-hoi as new members of the Securities and Futures Appeals Panel. The SFC also welcomes the re-appointment of Mr Iain Ferguson Bruce, Mr Marvin Cheung Kin-tung, Mr Dudley Leslie Harding, Mr Peter Clarke, Mr Tim Freshwater and Mr Rajat Jindal as members of the Panel. 

The SFC is committed to uphold the accountability and credibility of its operations for the benefits of the market and the investing public. The SFC is confident that members of the Securities and Futures Appeals Panel, who bring with them valuable expertise and significant experience in the legal, accounting and financial services profession, will assess appeals made against the Commission in an independent and impartial perspective.

The SFC will continue to co-operate with the Securities and Futures Appeals Panel and perform its regulatory duty in an open and consistent manner.
