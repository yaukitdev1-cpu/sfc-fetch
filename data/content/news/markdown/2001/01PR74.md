The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the appointment by the Financial Secretary of Mr Ashley Alder as Executive Director (Corporate Finance) of the Commission. A Partner of Herbert Smith with responsibility for Asian Equity Capital Markets and Mergers & Acquisitions (Hong Kong, Beijing, Singapore, Bangkok & Tokyo), Mr Alder has substantial experience in corporate finance and M&A transactions involving listed companies in Hong Kong. In 1999, he acted for the Hong Kong Futures Exchange in connection with its merger with the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited and the Hong Kong Securities Clearing Company Limited. He was also a committee member of the working party of the Government's Company Law Review project in 1997.

Mr Alder received his Master of Law with First Class Honours from the University of Cambridge, UK in 1983 and was admitted a Solicitor in England and Wales in 1986.

SFC Chairman Mr Andrew Sheng said: "I am pleased to welcome Mr Alder as our new Executive Director. I am confident that with his professional expertise and wide-ranging experience in the corporate finance field, Mr Alder will contribute significantly to the work of the Commission, especially in its effort to uplift the Corporate Governance standard in Hong Kong."

Mr Alder will succeed Mr David Stannard as head of the Corporate Finance Division effective 1 October 2001. Mr Sheng is thankful for the valuable service of Mr Stannard. " Mr Stannard has done an outstanding job during his tenure. He saw through the merger of the exchanges and clearing companies and the review on the Codes on Takeovers and Mergers and Share Repurchases. In effect, he has helped enhance Hong Kong's financial infrastructure and regulatory framework. I thank him for his dedication and contribution to the Commission and the Hong Kong market," Mr Sheng said.
