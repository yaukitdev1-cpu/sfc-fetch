The SFC announced today that it had reprimanded {X} under section 56 of the Securities Ordinance. 

The SFC's actions stemmed from an investigation into suspected unregistered dealing activities at Core Pacific-Yamaichi International (HK) Limited (CPYI) and Core Pacific-Yamaichi Securities (HK) Limited (CPYS) during the period of March 1998 and January 1999. The SFC found that {X} as a dealer's representative of CPYI and CPYS had:

allowed his name and account executive code to be used by unregistered staff to book their trades; and signed as a witness and as the responsible account executive of 1 client, and signed as the responsible account executive of 2 clients on the account opening documents when he had not been present when the account opening procedures had been carried out. Staff members of CPYI/CPYS were convicted for acting as dealer's representatives without being registered and were sentenced accordingly. The SFC concluded that the fitness and properness of {X} as a securities dealer's representative under the Securities Ordinance has been impugned.

The SFC notes {X}'s co-operation during its investigation, his prompt voluntary resignation from his then position with CPYI/CPYS and his perception that he was acting under management pressure and has taken all this into account when deciding to publicly reprimand him.
