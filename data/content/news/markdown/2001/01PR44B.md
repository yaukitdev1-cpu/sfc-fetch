The SFC announced today that it had reprimanded {X1} and {X2} under the Securities Ordinance. {X1} and {X2} are both securities dealer's representatives of Phillip Securities (HK) Limited. 

The SFC's actions stemmed from an investigation into suspected unregistered securities dealing activities. The SFC found that during the period from May 1999 to November 1999, {X1} and {X2} had:

- assisted an unregistered person in conducting his unregistered activities; and
- breached the provisions of Rule 534(2) of the Rules of the Stock Exchange Hong Kong Limited which provides that no Exchange Participant or Dealing Partnership shall give any rebate of any kind to any person for the purpose of circumventing or evading any minimum brokerage requirement. Therefore, the SFC concluded that the fitness and properness of {X1} and {X2} was impugned.
