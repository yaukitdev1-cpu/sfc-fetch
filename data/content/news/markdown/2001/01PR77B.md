The SFC announced today that it had publicly reprimanded {X} a securities dealer's representative under section 56 of the Securities Ordinance (SO).

The reprimand arose from an investigation into the activities of Phillip Securities (HK) Limited's (Phillip) Shatin Branch during January and February 2000. The SFC found that a staff member of Phillip who was unregistered was permitted to take and process clients' phone orders contrary to the registration requirements of the SO.

{X} as the then Branch Manager at Shatin Branch with direct responsibility for supervising the activities of the employee had failed to ensure that the registration requirements of the SO had been complied with.

In November 2000, the employee pleaded guilty to acting as a dealer's representative during the relevant period whilst unregistered in contrary of sections 50(1) and (2) of the SO.
