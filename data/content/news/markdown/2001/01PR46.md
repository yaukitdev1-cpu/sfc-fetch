The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it has reprimanded {X}, a dealer's representative registered under the Securities Ordinance (SO). 

The action stemmed from an inquiry conducted under section 56 of the SO which revealed that {X}, whilst employed by Koffman Securities Limited, had:

- failed to ensure that a written agreement was entered into with a client before any brokerage service was provided to that client;
- failed to obtain written authorisation from a client for the purpose of managing the client's account on a discretionary basis; and
purchased securities for a client on a discretionary basis without obtaining the client's prior consent. The SFC concluded that {X} was guilty of misconduct and his conduct had impugned his fitness and properness to remain as a registered person.
