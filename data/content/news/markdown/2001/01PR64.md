The Fit and Proper Criteria published in December 2000 reflect the new competence and continuous professional training (CPT) requirements applicable to applicants and licensees effective from 1 April 2001. 

The Commission welcomes inquiries about the new requirements. To promote a better understanding of how the Commission will approach these requirements, the Commission has now collated a list of Frequently Asked Questions.

The new requirements also provide for the recognition of industry based qualifications and for recognition of an institution for providing continuous professional training. The "Application Form for Approval as Recognized Industry Qualifications" and "Application Form for Professional Bodies and Tertiary Institutions for Approval as Recognized Institutions" are available for those who wish to obtain that recognition from the Commission's Academic and Accreditation Advisory Committee (AAAC).

The public can find the Frequently Asked Questions and the application forms under "Licensing Related Matters: What's New" in the Intermediaries, Licensing & Investment Products section of the SFC website at [http ://www.hksfc.org.hk](http://www.sfc.hk/) or collect a copy from the Licensing Counter of the Commission.
