The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today it had successfully prosecuted Trinity 2000 Ltd (Trinity) and {X} for issuing unauthorized advertisements on the internet.


{X} in his personal capacity and also on behalf of Trinity pleaded guilty before Ms Polly Lo at Western Magistracy. {X} was fined $10,000, Trinity fined $20,000 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $6,000 to the SFC.


The SFC's investigation into Trinity's advertisements on an internet auction website commenced following its daily surveillance of the internet. During the period 8 March to 7 April 2000 the information on Trinity's advertisements could have lead to the investing public purchasing an interest in an employment listing website, the advertising of which had not been authorised.


Advertisers of financial products should be aware that a requirement for authorisation of promotional material and advertising apply to all modes of advertising including the internet. In order to ensure compliance the SFC has a program of internet surveillance.
