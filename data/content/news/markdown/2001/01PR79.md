The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today publishes for public consultation proposed new provisions on index funds for incorporation into the Code on Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds (the Code). 

There has been an increased interest of the market in offering index funds to the public in Hong Kong. The SFC considers it necessary to provide clearer guidance to the industry on the authorisation of such funds in order to facilitate market development and ensure protection of investors.

A [consultation paper](/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/consultation/openFile?refNo=01CP8) is available on the SFC website at http: //www.hksfc.org.hk. Copies may also be obtained from the SFC offices.

Interested parties are invited to send in their comments by 20 August 2001.
