The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today released a Paper on the Offering of Hedge Funds (the Consultation Paper) for public consultation. 

"There has been an increased interest of the market in offering hedge funds to the public in Hong Kong. Investors are also looking for alternative investment choices. The SFC has reviewed the regulatory issues and we believe it is possible to satisfy market demand without compromising investor protection." said Mr Andrew Procter, SFC Executive Director of Intermediaries and Investment Products Division.

"The SFC is committed to facilitating product innovation and investor access to a wide range of investment choices. In issuing the Consultation Paper, we are asking for views from different sectors of the market to help us formulate our policy on hedge funds." said Mr Procter.

The SFC would like to invite comments on the two alternative approaches discussed in the Consultation Paper, namely:

(a) a market segmentation approach, i.e. to allow hedge funds to be offered to a specific segment of the public; and

(b) a full public offering approach, i.e. to allow hedge funds to be offered to the general public without any restrictions.

In both cases there would be strong requirements for disclosure to potential investors of the risks of investment and an obligation for financial advisers to assess whether the investment was suitable for the investor.

In drafting the Consultation Paper, the SFC has taken into consideration the experience and regulatory approaches in overseas jurisdictions and conducted informal discussions with various bodies and interested parties. The Consultation Paper is part of an overall review of the Code on Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds.

The consultation will last for a period of six weeks and will end on 7 December 2001. Following the consultation, the SFC will prepare a consultation conclusion document to summarize the views expressed by the respondents and set out the final guidelines. The new hedge fund guidelines will be incorporated into the Code on Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds, and is expected to take effect in early 2002.

To increase the transparency of the consultation process, the SFC intends to publish all the names of the commentators and their comments on the SFC website. Commentators who wish their names and comments be withheld may ask for them to be kept confidential when they make their submissions.

Copies of the Consultation Paper are available free of charge at the SFC's office and may also be found on the SFC's Internet website at [http://www.hksfc.org.hk](/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/consultation/doc?refNo=01CP12).
