On Thursday, 13 September 2001, the Takeovers and Mergers Panel ruled that UDL shares issued to the Scheme Administrator under a Scheme of Arrangement in May 2000 amounted to voting rights within the definition of the Hong Kong Code on Takeovers and Mergers. In light of the Panel's ruling, technically two general offer obligations arose under Rule 26 of the Takeovers Code: the first, when shares were issued to the Scheme Administrator in May 2000 (as these shares amounted to 50% of UDL's issued share capital) and the second, as a result of a subscription of UDL shares by Harbour Front Limited (UDL's controlling shareholder) in May 2001 (thus increasing its shareholding from below to above the 35% threshold). The Panel decided to exercise its powers under section 2.1 of the Introduction to the Takeovers Code and waived both the Scheme Administrator's and Harbour Front's general offer obligations on the basis that there were exceptional circumstances in this case. 

Accordingly, there will be no general offer.

A full written decision of the Panel will be issued in due course. At that time, a further press release will also be issued specifying the exceptional circumstances justifying the waiver.
