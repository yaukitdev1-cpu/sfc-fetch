The SFC has today posted an article on the Licensing Related Publications section and two other articles on the Investors Alert section on its website, drawing the attention of registered persons and investors to the risks of unauthorised trades and over-trading, and to the useful[ measures](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/intermediaries/supervision/measures_eng.pdf) that would help them to reduce these risks.

These measures include:

To Registered Persons

¡P never allow a third party to operate a client's account without authorisation;
 ¡P all authorisations granted by clients must be in writing;
¡P discretionary power granted to the account executive by the client must be in writing and the broker firm's record and the client agreement should designate the account as "discretionary account";
¡P ensure placement of orders by clients can be recorded by the intermediaries' telephone recording system;
¡P segregate the front-office and back-office functions, particularly order placing and settlement;
¡P payment to clients should be made by crossed cheques to the clients and delivered to them directly;
¡P payment by clients should be made directly by the account holders;
¡P discourage hold mail arrangements and where hold mail arrangements are in place, ensure clear and strong compensating controls;
¡P ensure clients receive timely contract notes and statements of account directly from the broker firm; and
¡P closely monitor clients' accounts where there is an overdue balance and consider liquidating the position if clients fail to settle within a reasonable period.

To Investors

¡P any authorisation granted to the account executive or any third party must be in writing;
¡P avoid placing orders through the account executive's mobile phone to ensure that there is an audit trail of all orders;
¡P settle transactions directly with the broker firm and not through the account executive;
¡P check the contract notes and the statements of account received; verify the account balances regularly with the settlement department of the broker firm to ensure their correctness;
¡P report any discrepancies or alteration in the contract notes and statements to the management of the firm immediately; and
¡P trade within your financial means and understand the risk of over-trading.

The SFC believes that failure to adopt the measures suggested without any other compensating control may cast doubts on the effectiveness and adequacy of a firm's internal control procedures and may reflect adversely on its fitness and properness. Investors, on the other hand, should be mindful of these measures to prevent disputes and to safeguard their own interest.

Interested parties can visit the SFC's website http: //www.hksfc.org.hk for a copy of these articles.
