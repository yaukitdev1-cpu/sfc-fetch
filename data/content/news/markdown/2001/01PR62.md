During a number of current investigations, the Commission has noted unacceptable behaviour relating to the initial placement of and early trading in derivative warrants listed on The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited (Stock Exchange). Concern has arisen that issuers of derivative warrants may be arranging for brokers, placing agents and investors to take up derivative warrants on the understanding that the issuer will repurchase the derivative warrants. In most cases, the prearranged repurchases are completed the day after the placement and before the derivative warrants are listed on the Stock Exchange. These repurchases are then recorded on the Stock Exchange as special trades on the first trading day of the derivative warrants. 

The Commission considers that placing derivative warrants and carrying out prearranged repurchases may deceive the market by giving a false and misleading appearance of the demand, the price and the trading activity in the derivative warrants. The Commission may take disciplinary action against any parties engaged in such activities.

The Commission advises issuers, brokers, placement agents and investors involved in the initial placement of derivative warrants, to monitor carefully all arrangements relating to the placement and any subsequent trading, particularly prior to the listing of the derivative warrant, to ensure that they are aware of any related arrangements made by their staff and to alert the Commission to any inappropriate activity.

Mr Paul Bailey, Executive Director of Enforcement commented: "The Commission takes very seriously any activity that may affect the integrity of the market and will consider disciplinary action against any registered person involved".
