The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the appointment by the Financial Secretary of the 12 members to the Advisory Committee of the SFC for a period of two years from 1 June 2001 to 31 May 2003 (Please see Appendix 1).

Mr Andrew Sheng, Chairman of the SFC, paid tribute to the seven members who are retiring from the Advisory Committee - Mr David Carse, Mr Linus Cheung, Mr Peter Clarke, Mr Leong Ka Chai, Mr Dannis Lee Jor Hung, Mr Sydney Leong and Mr Andrew Lo, for their valuable contribution over the past two years.

The Advisory Committee is set up pursuant to section 10 of the Securities and Futures Commission Ordinance to advise the SFC on any matter of policy regarding the performance of any of its functions.

The SFC has also today formed a new Shareholders Group and is glad that the individuals (please see Appendix 2) have agreed to participate. The Group would provide advice and feedback to the SFC on issues relating to shareholders' rights and interests.
