The SFC announced today that it had publicly reprimanded {X}, a dealer's representative under the Securities Ordinance and a commodity dealer's representative under the Commodities Trading Ordinance. 

The reprimand stems from an inquiry in which the SFC found that during the period from 1995 to July 2000, {X}, while employed by Seapower Futures Limited, Seapower Securities Limited, KGI Seapower Futures Limited, KGI Seapower Securities Limited, Shenyin Wanguo Securities (HK) Limited, and Shenyin Wanguo Futures (HK) Limited in the capacity of a branch manager, had failed to properly carry out his supervisory responsibilities by knowingly allowing-

(a) two of the staff under his supervision to operate two of their clients' accounts without first obtaining written authorization from those clients in contravention of the internal rules of his employers; and

(b) the two staff to conduct their own trading through these accounts, in contravention of the staff dealing policies of his employers.

As a result of the above findings, the SFC concluded that {X}'s fitness and properness had been impugned and decided to publicly reprimand him.
