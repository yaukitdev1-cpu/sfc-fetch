The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announces that payments are being made today from the Unified Exchange Compensation Fund to 25 claimants of Win Successful Securities Ltd. (Win Successful) who suffered pecuniary losses as a result of the default of Win Successful in January 2000 and whose claims, with a total of $4,832,893.73, were allowed or partially allowed by the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited (Exchange). The Exchange has decided, in consultation with the SFC, that the per claimant limit of $150,000.00 will apply. Under this arrangement, this tranche of payment out of the Compensation Fund will be $1,930,401.38 to 25 claimants out of a total of 286 claimants.
