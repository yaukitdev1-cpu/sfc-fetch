The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today released for public consultation the draft Securities and Futures (Contract Notes, Statements of Account and Receipts) Rules ("draft Rules"). These are to be made under the Securities and Futures Bill ("Bill") when enacted. The Bill is currently being considered by a Legislative Council Bills Committee. 

Unlike the existing legislative framework, the Bill does not contain any detailed requirements in relation to the issuance of contract notes, statements of account or receipts; it merely provides the SFC with the necessary rule-making power to prescribe detailed and technical requirements in subsidiary legislation. This approach is consistent with modern securities legislation in order that regulators can respond efficiently to changing market conditions and practices.

While there are controls already built into the legislative system whereby any rules made by the Commission must be subject to negative vetting by the Legislative Council, the Commission now releases the draft Rules for public consultation.

In the formulation of these draft Rules, the SFC has already consulted selected market participants.

In essence, the draft Rules prescribe how an intermediary or its associated entity (essentially a nominee company it operates or one within its group of companies) should prepare and provide contract notes, statements of account and receipts to its clients.

The SFC has used the FinNet communication network to send copies of the consultation document to registered dealers that have lodged their Financial Resources Rules returns electronically with the SFC via FinNet. In addition, copies of the consultation document are available free of charge at the SFC's office and can also be found on the SFC's Internet website at http://www.hksfc.org.hk.
