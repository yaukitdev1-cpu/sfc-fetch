The SFC announced today that it had publicly reprimanded {X}, a dealer representative under the Securities Ordinance. 

The reprimand stems from an inquiry in which it was found that on 12 January 2000 {X} gained entry to the trading hall of the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited (SEHK) using the details of an authorised colleague and proceeded to execute orders for his employer when his accreditation to act for them as a dealer's representative had not been approved by the SFC. His actions were in contravention of Rule 548 of the SEHK's rules and in breach of the registration requirements of section 50 (1C) of the Securities Ordinance.

As a result of the above findings, SFC concluded that {X}'s fitness and properness had been impugned and decided to publicly reprimand him. In reaching this decision, the SFC had taken into account that at the time of the incident of 12 January 2000 he was acting under instructions of a senior colleague of his employer and that he was fully cooperative in the SFC's inquiry.
