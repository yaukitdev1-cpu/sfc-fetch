The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today published the [*Consultation Conclusions on the Draft Code of Conduct for Share Registrars*](/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/consultation/conclusion?refNo=01CP9) and the [*Code of Conduct for Share Registrars*](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/legislation/regulatory/codes_guide/share-reg-coc(eng).pdf). 

The SFC has been working with the Federation of Share Registrars (the Federation) to produce this non-statutory code of standards.

The Consultation Conclusions summarise the comments received by the SFC during the one-month consultation period between August and September 2001. Most of the comments received have been incorporated into the Code.

The Code sets out:

- general principles and standards of conduct a share registrar should adhere to;
- requirements of a share registrar to ensure sufficient system capacity and data integrity;
- disciplinary proceedings for a share registrar;
- formation of a Disciplinary Committee and a Disciplinary Appeals Committee; and
- the Federation's role in implementing penalties.
The Code will take effect on 1 November 2001. 

Hard copies of the Consultation Conclusions and the Code, in Chinese and English, are available at the SFC office. Copies may also be downloaded from the SFC website at www.hksfc.org.hk.
