Further to its press release on 17 September 2001, the Takeovers Panel announces that it has released its written ruling that UDL shares issued to the Scheme Administrator under a Scheme of Arrangement in May 2000 amounted to voting rights within the definition of the Hong Kong Code on Takeovers and Mergers. In light of the Panel's ruling, technically two general offer obligations arose under Rule 26 of the Takeovers Code: the first, when shares were issued to the Scheme Administrator in May 2000 (as these shares amounted to 50% of UDL's issued share capital) and the second, as a result of a subscription of UDL shares by Harbour Front Limited (UDL's controlling shareholder) in May 2001 (which increased its shareholding from below to above the 35% threshold). The Panel decided to exercise its powers under section 2.1 of the Introduction to the Takeovers Code and waive both the Scheme Administrator's and Harbour Front's general offer obligations on the basis that there were exceptional circumstances in this case (please see paragraphs 23 and 25 of the attached decision dated 28 September 2001). 

Accordingly, there will be no general offer.

The Takeovers Panel has also released its decision on a preliminary issue that arose in this matter. The background to this is that Charterbase Management Limited, a minority shareholder of UDL, applied to the Takeovers Panel for a review of a ruling by the Executive to the effect that the UDL shares issued to the Scheme Administrator do not carry voting rights within the meaning of the Takeovers Code. After Charterbase had lodged its application, a number of allegations were made concerning the identity of the person behind Charterbase and the motive for bringing this application. As a result, on Friday, 17 August 2001, the Panel met to consider whether Charterbase's application should proceed to a full substantive hearing. The Panel decided that it should. The Panel's reasons are set out in a separate written decision dated 4 September 2001.

A copy of the these decisions and the relevant Takeovers Code provisions can be found in the Takeovers and Mergers Panel Decisions section of the SFC Website at http://www.hksfc.org.hk.
