The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it had publicly reprimanded {X}, formerly a registered dealer under the Securities Ordinance (SO).

The public reprimand stems from an inquiry which revealed that {X} had failed to discharge his supervisory responsibilities over the operations of C.A. Pacific Securities Ltd (CAPSL) in his former capacity as a director and registered dealer of CAPSL. Instead, {X} had knowingly allowed persons who were not registered with the Commission to assume direct supervision and management of the operations and business of CAPSL. The SFC also found that {X} had failed to exercise due care and diligence in performing his function as an authorised signatory of C.A Pacific Finance Ltd (CAPFL), a fellow group company of CAPSL. {X} had signed a cheque involving a substantial amount drawn on the bank account of CAPFL without making proper enquiry as to the purpose for which it was drawn. Given the very close relationship between CAPSL and CAPFL, this failure resulted in the business of CAPFL, and CAPSL being conducted in such a way so as to have prejudiced the interest of the members of the investing public.

In light of these findings, the SFC has concluded that the fitness and properness of {X} as a director and person concerned in the management of CAPSL had been impugned.
