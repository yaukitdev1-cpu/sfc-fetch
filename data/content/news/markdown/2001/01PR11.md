A new series of investor education radio segments entitled: "Investment Encyclopaedia", which is sponsored by the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC), will begin airing on 5 February 2001 on launch of Metro Finance.

This radio series is the latest in a variety of investor education initiatives by the SFC. There are 60 one-minute episodes, which through lively dialogues, aim to give the audience all the key facts they need to know in stock, fund and derivative investments, such as major product features, dealing procedures, risks, market activities, shareholders' rights, scams and market malpractice.

Beginning 5 February 2001, the series will run for three months on Metro Finance at frequency FM102.4 - 106.3 and on the Internet at its website http://www.104metrofinance.com. Each episode is aired three times a day at around 10:38 a.m., 12:38 p.m. and 3:38 p.m., Monday to Friday.

Since 1998, the SFC has sponsored educational radio programmes on RTHK's Putonghua and Cantonese channels and on Commercial Radio, which received positive response. Some of these radio series are now available on the SFC's corporate website, located at [http://www.hksfc.org.hk](http://www.sfc.hk/).

"As the statutory regulator of the securities and futures markets, the SFC considers that investor education is of paramount importance to enhance the quality of our markets. We are committed to running a proactive investor education programme in order to assist the retail investors by providing, or helping them to find, information, which might help them to make informed investment decisions," said an SFC spokesman.
