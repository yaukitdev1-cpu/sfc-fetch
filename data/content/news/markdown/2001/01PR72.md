The Securities and Futures Commission announces amendments to the Guidance Note on Competence (GNC) and the Guidance Note on Continuous Professional Training (GNCPT).

In the December 2000 version of the GNC, the Commission may license a director applicant who has yet to pass the regulatory paper on condition that the applicant pass the local regulatory paper within three months of obtaining a licence. It is considered appropriate that the same approach be adopted for representative applicants. In addition, it was decided that a more flexible time frame should be allowed. In this connection, paragraph 28 under "Points to Note" has been amended.

Also, paragraphs 14, 15, 20 and 25 of the GNCPT have been amended to reflect, among other things, the Commission and the Academic and Accreditation Advisory Committee's (AAAC) decision that internally organised training provided by corporate licensees will not be pre-endorsed by the AAAC. However, this does not necessarily mean that these internally organised training will not meet the continuous professional training requirement. Corporate licensees are expected to organise appropriate training for their staff and to notify the Commission in advance of the date and details of the training. The Commission may attend training so organised for quality assurance.

The amended version of the Guidance Notes is posted on the SFC web-site at http://www.hksfc.org.hk and also available for collection from the Commission.

Note to Editor: The Academic and Accreditation Advisory Committee is empowered, by and on the Commission's behalf, to approve industry based courses and examinations for the purpose of meeting the competence requirement. It will also endorse professional bodies and tertiary institutions organising training programmes for the purpose of meeting the continuous professional training requirement. The committee comprises 4 industry representatives, 4 academic representatives and 2 Commission representatives including the Chairman.
