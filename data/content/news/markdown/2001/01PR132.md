The Securities and Futures Commission (the "SFC") today issued the [Corporate Finance Adviser Code of Conduct](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/legislation/regulatory/codes_guide/cfa-coc(eng).pdf) (the "Code"). The SFC also released a [conclusion report](/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/consultation/conclusion?refNo=00CP4) following earlier public consultation on the proposed Code (the "Conclusion Report"). 

In May 2000, the SFC sought public comments on the proposed guidelines on the responsibilities and conduct expected of licensees engaging in the activity of advising on corporate finance in Hong Kong.

The consultation period, which was originally scheduled to end on 31 May 2000, was extended to 30 June 2000 in response to market requests. The SFC received 23 responses from various market participants and professional institutions. The SFC also conducted a second round of consultation with respondents to the public consultation in February 2001 to seek their comments on a draft Conclusion Report and a revised draft Code. Eight responses were received in this round.

The Code will become effective on 1 December 2001. Mr Ashley Alder, SFC's Executive Director of Corporate Finance said: "The Code will help uphold and enhance the level of ethics and standards amongst corporate finance practitioners in Hong Kong, which will in turn benefit investors."

Copies of the Conclusion Report and the Code are available at the SFC's office and on the SFC's Website [http://www.hksfc.org.hk](http://www.sfc.hk/sfcRegulatoryHandbook/EN/sfcRegulatoryHandbookTBServlet?openAgent%2520%2520).
