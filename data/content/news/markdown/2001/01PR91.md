The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it had publicly reprimanded {X1}, {X2}, both registered dealing directors of SEG International Securities (H.K.) Limited (SEG), and {X3}, a director of SEG, under the Securities Ordinance (SO). 

The public reprimands stemmed from inquiries covering the period 1 April 1999 to May 2000 when it was found that 6 employees at SEG and {X3} solicited clients and handled clients' orders for SEG whilst not registered with the SFC contrary to the registration requirements of the SO.

{X1} was the sole dealing director at the time and knew of the unregistered status of these 6 staff and {X3} but still approved their activities. {X2}, who was at the time a dealer's representative of SEG with the responsibility of supervising all dealing staff, gave approval for staff who he knew to be unregistered to carry out dealing activities for remuneration whilst unregistered.

{X3} knowingly solicited clients for SEG and handled client orders whilst not registered and recruited one of the 6 employees concerned to act as a dealer's representative whilst not registered.

The SFC concluded that the above matters impugned the fitness and properness of {X1} and {X2}. The SFC also concluded that {X3} was not a fit and proper person to be a director of SEG. Accordingly the SFC decided to publicly reprimand them.
