The SFC announced today that it has reprimanded {X}, a securities dealer's representative registered under the Securities Ordinance (SO).

The SFC's action stemmed from an investigation into suspected unregistered dealing activities during the period of December 1998 and May 1999 at {X}'s former employer. These matters occurred before {X} was employed by his current employer.

The SFC found that {X}:

· had as supervisor of a number of staff, who were not registered with the SFC in any capacity, took inadequate steps to ensure such staff did not open accounts and handle client orders; and

· had signed account opening documents for clients acknowledging that he had been present when the prospective clients signed the documents, even though he had not.

The SFC concluded that the fitness and properness of {X} as a dealer's representative under the SO has been impugned. In deciding on this action, the SFC took into account that {X} had fully co-operated with the SFC's investigation.
