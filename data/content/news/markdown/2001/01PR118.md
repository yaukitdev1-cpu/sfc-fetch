The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) and the Australian Securities & Investments Commission (ASIC) announced today the signing of a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU). The signing ceremony took place in Hong Kong. 

In September 1993, the SFC signed an MOU with the Australian Securities Commission, the predecessor of ASIC, for the exchange of information and investigatory assistance between the two Commissions. The new MOU replaces the earlier one and updates the provisions to reflect changes that have taken place in both jurisdictions since that time. The cooperation between the SFC and the ASIC remains the cornerstone of the Memorandum signed today.

In the signing ceremony, Mr Andrew Sheng, Chairman of the SFC said: "The ASIC has always been a close friend of the SFC. We have been working closely with our colleagues at the ASIC on a wide range of matters. This MOU further strengthens our regulatory link and helps to protect investors in our mutual markets."

Mr David Knott, Chairman of ASIC, said: "This new MOU recognises two important aspects of the relationship between the SFC and ASIC: that we have a history of interaction and cooperation on many matters and that our relationship continues to grow to take account of new developments, such as the greater regional linkages between the financial markets. Arrangements like this are essential to the proper regulation of world markets."
