The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it had publicly reprimanded {X}, a registered securities dealer's representative of Ever-Long Securities Company Limited (Ever-Long), under the Securities Ordinance. 

The SFC found that {X} used her mother's account at Ever-Long to trade shares without her employer's written authorization.

{X} had also failed to verify the identities of a number of her clients and allowed unauthorized persons to trade for and on behalf of these clients. She had also signed account opening forms witnessing clients' signatures when she had not met these clients. In view of the above findings the SFC has publicly reprimanded {X}.
