The Commission issued today the [Revised Code of Conduct for Persons Registered with the Securities and Futures Commission](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/legislation/regulatory/codes_guide/Code%20of%20Conduct%20(12%20Feb%202001)%20-%20FINAL%20(14%20font).pdf) ("Revised Code"). The Commission also released the [Consultation Conclusion Report relating to the consultation for the Proposed Revised Code of Conduct for Persons Registered with the Securities and Futures Commission](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/consult/coc%20conclusions.pdf) ("Consultation Conclusion Report"). 

The Commission received 27 submissions during the one-month consultation period, which ended on 27 October 2000. The Consultation Conclusion Report highlights the main comments raised and explains the rationale for the Commission's conclusions in relation to the Code amendments. Some amendments to the original proposed revisions have been made, where appropriate, to reflect some of the comments.

At the same time, the Commission has released its [Consultation Conclusions on Code of Conduct for Regulated Persons Serving the Professional or Sophisticated Market](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/consult/coc%20conclusions.pdf). As a result of this consultation, the Code of Conduct will include a new section providing for the relaxation of certain requirements in relation to a registered person serving professional investors.

The Revised Code will take effect on 1 April 2001, with the exception of a few paragraphs relating to telephone recording and clients agreements, which will take effect on 1 October 2001 to give more time for the registered persons to comply with these new requirements.

The Consultation Conclusion Reports and the Revised Code have been posted on the SFC Web-site at (http: //www.hksfc.org.hk.). The Revised Code can also be purchased at the SFC's office at 12th floor, Edinburgh Tower, The Landmark, 15 Queen's Road Central, Hong Kong.
