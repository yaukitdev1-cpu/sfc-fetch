The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it had revoked the registration of Ms Chan Choi Ha Wendy (Chan), a dealer's representative under the Securities Ordinance.

Following an investigation, the SFC found that Chan, during the period June 1998 to December 2000, whilst employed by Sharegain Development Limited (Sharegain), had misappropriated clients' assets, including both cash and securities, by conducting unauthorized trading in clients' accounts and misled clients as to the state of their accounts by falsifying their statements of account.

Chan's conduct was serious as it resulted in substantial losses to both Sharegain and its clients. The SFC concluded that Chan was guilty of misconduct and her fitness and properness has been seriously impugned. The SFC therefore decided to revoke her registration.
