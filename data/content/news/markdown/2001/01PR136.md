The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it had revoked the registration of Mr Fung Tat Shing Frank (Fung), a dealer's representative under the Securities Ordinance.

Following an investigation, the SFC found that Fung, during the period May to August 2000, whilst employed by Megabase Securities Limited (Megabase) as a dealer's representative, conducted unauthorized trading through one client's account resulting in loss to that client; and in two other accounts, Fung had traded in them without written authorization, contrary to Megabase's internal policy.

Fung's conduct was serious as it resulted in loss to a client of Megabase and exposed Megabase to unnecessary financial risks. The SFC concluded that Fung was guilty of misconduct and his fitness and properness has been seriously impugned. The SFC therefore decided to revoke his registration.
