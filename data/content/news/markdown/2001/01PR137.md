The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) warmly welcomed the new appointment by the Financial Secretary of Mr. Tsang Yok-sing, Jasper as a Non-executive Director to succeed Mr. Bernard Chan. The Commission also welcomed the re-appointment of Mr. Henry Fan as a Non-Executive Director. 

"We are grateful to our non-executive directors for their continuous support. In modernizing Hong Kong's legislative framework and strengthening our financial infrastructure, we have benefited tremendously from their invaluable insight and market expertise," said SFC Chairman Mr. Andrew Sheng.

"I am thankful to Mr. Bernard Chan for the contribution he has made to the Commission. Taking this opportunity, I would like to welcome Mr. Tsang on behalf of the Board. All of us at the Commission look forward to working closely with him in the coming months," continued Mr. Sheng.
