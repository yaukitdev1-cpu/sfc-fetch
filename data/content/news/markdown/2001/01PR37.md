The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it had publicly reprimanded {X1} and {X2}, formerly registered dealers under the Securities Ordinance (SO).

The public reprimands stem from inquiries conducted under section 56 of the SO which revealed that {X1} and {X2} had failed to discharge their supervisory responsibilities over the operations of C.A. Pacific Securities Ltd. (CAPSL) in their former capacities as directors and registered dealers of CAPSL. Instead, {X1} and {X2} had knowingly allowed a person who was not registered with the Commission to assume direct supervision and management of the operations and business of CAPSL. The SFC also found that they had failed to exercise due care and diligence in performing their functions as authorised signatories of a fellow group company of CAPSL. As a result of their conduct, the business of CAPSL was conducted in such a way so as to have prejudiced the interest of the members of the investing public.

In light of these findings, the SFC has concluded that the fitness and properness of {X1} and {X2} as directors and persons concerned in the management of CAPSL had been impugned.
