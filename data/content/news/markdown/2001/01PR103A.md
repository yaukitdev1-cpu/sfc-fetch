The SFC announced today that it had publicly reprimanded {X}, a registered securities dealer's representative of Grand Securities Company Limited (Grand) under the Securities Ordinance and a commodity dealer's representative of Grand Futures Limited under the Commodities Trading Ordinance. 

The reprimand stems from an SFC's investigation which revealed that in June 1999 {X} had:-

(a) executed orders for two prospective clients even before those clients had opened accounts with Grand;

(b) in opening an account, failed to make proper inquiry into the true identities of the clients and the beneficial owners of the account;

(c) allowed a third party to operate a client's account without written authorization thereby exposing the client and Grand to financial risk which culminated in substantial loss to Grand; and

(d) signed as witness to a client's signature on the account opening documentation even though she was not present when the client signed the documentation.

On this basis the SFC concluded that {X}'s fitness and properness as a registered person has been called into question and decided to publicly reprimand her. An SFC spokesman said: "It is incumbent on registered persons to ensure that accounts opening procedures are followed and information concerning identities of clients and those authorized to give instructions are obtained and properly recorded."
