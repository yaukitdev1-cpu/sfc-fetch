The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it had reprimanded {X}, a dealer's representative registered under the Securities Ordinance (SO). 

The action stemmed from an inquiry conducted under section 56 of the SO which revealed that {X} had executed a substantial number of transactions in a client's account on the basis of instructions from a third party without the written authorisation of the account holder. {X}'s conduct had exposed his employer to potential liabilities.

The SFC concluded that {X}'s conduct had impugned his fitness and properness to remain as a registered person.
