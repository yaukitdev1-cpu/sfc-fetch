The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) published today the 42nd issue of the Quarterly Bulletin. From this issue onwards, we have a new section called the Bulletin Board to inform readers about our latest consultation exercise, rules and guidelines. 

The lead article of this issue, "Watch Out for Boiler-Rooms" warns investors of the dangers of boiler-room scams. Latest information suggests that boiler-room activities have become active again. This article describes the tactics boiler-room operators frequently use, explains how the SFC deals with boiler-room scams and suggests ways for the investing public to avoid such scams.

The second article is on the year-end remarks by the SFC Chairman Mr Andrew Sheng. Mr Sheng noted that the three-pronged financial market reforms were largely implemented, and many significant and memorable events took place in 2000. The third article highlights a number of recommendations in the Consultation Paper on On-line Trading of Securities and Futures.

FAQ on Compliance is on the differences between two types of market misconduct - market manipulation and insider dealing.
