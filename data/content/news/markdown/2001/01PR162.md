The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today invited professional consultants to submit proposals on the design, implementation and management of a new Investor Compensation Company (ICC).

The successful candidate will be appointed for one to three years to provide some or all of the following services, initially to the SFC and subsequently direct to the ICC once it is incorporated:

a. Advice on corporate structure, including the governance of ICC and implementation of necessary corporate arrangements;
b. Assistance in development of Business Plan for ICC;
 c. Advice on funding requirements of ICC and means of provision;
 d. Provision of consulting and administrative services to the management of the ICC;
 e. Claims management services;
 f. Information systems development and implementation;
 g. Potential design and placement of contingent capital support to the ICC;
 h. Development and integration of a risk analysis and projection model to allow interactive assessment of exposure, funding requirement and compensation limit adjustments;
i. Investor education and public relations strategy, both day to day and in times of stress.

The new ICC will be established under the Securities and Futures Bill to administer the new compensation arrangements. This will be a new single facility that will replace the existing Unified Exchange Compensation Fund, the Commodity Exchange Compensation Fund and the Dealers' Deposit Schemes for non-exchange participant dealers.

The arrangements will cover defaults by licensed persons authorised to deal in securities, licensed persons authorised to deal in futures contracts, licensed persons authorised to provide securities margin financing, and certain authorised financial institutions all in relation to products traded through the Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Ltd.
