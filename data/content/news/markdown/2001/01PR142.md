The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today released for public consultation the draft Securities and Futures (Licensed Persons And Registered Institutions) Rules (draft Rules). These are to be made under the Securities and Futures Bill (Bill) when enacted. The Bill is currently being considered by a Legislative Council Bills Committee. 

The Bill gives the Commission the necessary rule-making power to prescribe requirements in the subsidiary legislation. This approach is consistent with modern securities legislation in order that regulators can respond efficiently to changing market conditions and practices.

There are controls already built into the legislative system whereby any rules made by the Commission must be subject to negative vetting by the Legislative Council. In addition, the Commission now releases the draft Rules for public consultation.

In essence, the draft Rules are derived from, and will replace, the current Securities (Dealers, Investment Advisers, Partnerships and Representatives) Rules, Securities (Miscellaneous) Rules, and the Commodities (Dealers, Commodity Trading Advisers and Representatives) Rules with certain significant changes. These changes include additional information to be entered in the public register, obligations to return certificates of registration and requirements to notify the Commission on particular changed details.

The SFC has used the FinNet communication network to send copies of the consultation document to registered dealers that have lodged their Financial Resources Rules returns electronically with the SFC via FinNet. In addition, copies of the consultation document are available free of charge at the SFC's office and can also be found on the SFC's Internet website at http://www.hksfc.org.hk.
