The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today releases the findings of its Fund Management Activities Survey 2000. 

The survey is an annual poll of the fund management activities of SFC registered and exempt persons whose primary business is management of funds or portfolios. It provides a better understanding of the state of the industry and helps in the Commission's efforts to facilitate market development.

Some key findings of FMAS 2000 are (figures as of 31 December 2000):

- Total assets under management (AUM) by the respondents amounted to $1,485 billion.
- Total assets managed in Hong Kong were $626 billion.
- A larger proportion of total AUM was managed in Hong Kong - doubling to 42%.
- About 27% of total AUM were attributable to Hong Kong investors.
- The number of fund management companies increased by 11% from 183 to 203.
Mr Andrew Procter, Executive Director of Intermediaries and Investment Products, SFC, said: "The year 2000 had been a challenging year for market practitioners. The SFC has strengthened efforts to understand the needs of the industry in order to help maintain Hong Kong's competitiveness and its status as a leading fund management centre in the region." 

The SFC will continue to consult and work closely with the industry on important policy proposals to facilitate market development and encourage new products.

Regulatory guidelines on the authorisation of index funds and alternative investment funds are being considered and will be issued for consultation in due course. Public consultation on short form disclosure documents or "key fact sheets" of retail funds is expected in the second half of this year.

In addition, a general review is underway to bring the Code on Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds in line with changing market and regulatory developments locally and internationally.

These initiatives are against a background of significant growth in Hong Kong's retail fund sector. The number of unit trusts, mutual funds, and other collective investment schemes authorised by the SFC for public offering reached a record 2,267 as of 31 March 2001, an increase of 26% over a year ago. The fund sector is also expected to benefit from the introduction of Mandatory Provident Fund.

Meanwhile, to ensure local and international fund management companies may find the talents they need, the Commission is updating the requirements for the competence and professionalism of intermediaries.
