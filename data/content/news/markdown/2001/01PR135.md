The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has today published the latest issue of its journal, the SFC Quarterly Bulletin, which has been revamped. 

Since 1993, the journal's mission to inform and educate the investing public and industry participants remains unchanged. However, the revamped journal has moved up to the next level in terms of approach and depth of analysis.

The SFC Quarterly Bulletin now features a greater variety of content and has more emphasis on research, analysis and debate. From this issue onwards, the SFC Quarterly Bulletin will have sections for market updates, statistics on market performance and industry structure and feature articles on key issues relevant to industry participants and investors.

This issue's feature article examines some key factors behind the change in market turnover in Hong Kong. This issue also includes an overview of the role of circuit breakers in various jurisdictions, a market update discussing the impact of the September 11 attacks, statistics providing updates on market performance and industry structure and an editorial explaining the reasons for the SFC Quarterly Bulletin's new approach.

Interested parties may subscribe to the Bulletin at $500 per annum or download it from our website (http://www.hksfc.org.hk).
