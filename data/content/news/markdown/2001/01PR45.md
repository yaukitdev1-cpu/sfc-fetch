The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) published today the 43rd issue of the Quarterly Bulletin, which carries two interesting articles and one speech.

The lead article of this issue, Hong Kong Strengths Meet China Opportunities, analyses factors which have enabled Hong Kong to become a leading market in Asia. The article then points out that there is much more Hong Kong can and needs to do in marrying Hong Kong's strengths with China opportunities in the areas of derivatives, risk management, and fund management.

In An Analysis of Misappropriation of Assets Cases, the SFC shares with readers its analysis of certain practices that have led to misappropriation of company or client assets by brokers' employees. The SFC in the article describes the common tricks dishonest brokers and account executives use in transferring or using their clients' assets. It then gives suggestions on enhancing brokers' internal control system to prevent assets misappropriation.

This issue of the Quarterly Bulletin includes a speech on Corporate Governance and the Quality of Markets by SFC Chairman Mr Andrew Sheng.

Interested parties may obtain a copy of the Quarterly Bulletin from the SFC office or download it from http://www.hksfc.org.hk.
