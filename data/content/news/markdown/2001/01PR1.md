The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomed the new appointment by the Financial Secretary of Hon Bernard Chan as a Non-Executive Director to succeed Mr Ronald Arculli. The SFC also welcomed the re-appointment of Mr Daniel Fung, Mr Thomas Brian Stevenson and Ms Anna Wu as Non-Executive Directors.

“Our Non-Executive Directors have brought with them unique and valuable market experience to our board. I want to thank all of them for their contribution,” said SFC Chairman Mr Andrew Sheng.

“I am particularly grateful to Mr Arculli who has given tremendous advice and valuable contribution to the reform of the financial market in the past three years. On behalf of the board, I welcome Mr Chan who will bring with him both financial industry expertise and experience in the Legislative Council,” Mr Sheng said.
