On April 20, a joint IMF-World Bank mission gave a presentation on the IMF and World Bank work on international standards and codes, in conjunction with the Hong Kong Securities and Futures Commission. Work on standards and codes has received increased attention following the international financial crises of the 1990s. In response to the crises, IMF and World Bank also launched a joint initiative on "Reports on the Observance of Standards and Codes" (ROSCs) as part of the international community's efforts to strengthen the international financial architecture.

In the seminar, IMF and World Bank staff explained the objectives, approach, and status of implementation of this important initiative. Standards and codes are intended to increase transparency in financial markets and to help guide investment decisions. The IMF and World Bank are currently assessing 11 key standards covering market transparency, regulation and infrastructure. As of April 2001, over 110 such assessments have been completed and some further 100 are expected to be added over the coming six to 12 months.

This is being the second time that an outreach mission on standards and codes by IMF and World Bank has visited Hongkong. Hongkong is an important focus for outreach efforts because of its large financial community and its key position in the Asian financial markets. The IMF and World Bank team sought feedback from an audience of market participants on further development of the ROSC initiative. The audience was highly supportive of this work, which is increasingly being used in investment allocation and risk management decisions.
