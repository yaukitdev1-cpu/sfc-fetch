The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announces that the independent Shareholders Group held its first meeting on Wednesday 27 June 2001. The Group discussed a number of issues relating to its role and work items to be addressed in the time ahead.

"We are glad that these experts with their deep and diverse experience have agreed to join in forming this Shareholders Group. Their advice - given from the perspective of shareholders - will be valuable in our work in furthering the interests of public investors," David Stannard, Executive Director of the Corporate Finance Division, said.

The Group discussed and commented on its Term of Reference in its first meeting. It will be advising the SFC on a wide variety of areas of shareholder interest. It will not, however, be involved in any specific case or dispute.

The Terms of Reference and a previous [press release dated 28 May 2001 on the formation of the Group](/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/news-and-announcements/news/doc?refNo=01PR57) are attached.

Securities and Futures Commission
 Shareholders Group
 Terms of Reference


1. Maintenance of a level playing field among all shareholders is fundamental to investor confidence and market integrity. Proper functioning of a securities market also depends on directors and management of listed companies being held accountable to shareholders. This ultimately benefits both the listed companies as well as their shareholders.

2. With a view towards furthering the above principles in practice, the Group shall advise the Commission on (principally non-statutory) aspects of the regulatory framework, including issues relating to -

a. quality of information available to public shareholders;

b. protection of minority shareholders;

c. role of institutional investors and their development as a force of checks and balance;

d. promoting a high degree of shareholder involvement;

e. enhancing good corporate governance in achieving best practice and world-class standards.

3. The Group aims to meet bi-monthly, subject to needs and workload.
