The Securities and Futures Commission is pleased to announce the publication of the Companies Ordinance (Exemption of Companies and Prospectuses From Compliance with Provisions Notice) on 30 March 2001 in the Government Gazette. The Notice has been tabled before the Legislative Council and, subject to negative vetting, the Notice will become effective on 11 May 2001. 

The Notice was issued by the SFC pursuant to its exemption powers under sections 38A and 342A of the Ordinance to exempt classes of companies and prospectuses from compliance with those requirements in the Ordinance which are unduly burdensome or irrelevant to them.

The Notice contains the following four class exemptions:

- Prospectuses relating to shares or debentures of companies incorporated in Hong Kong pursuant to the Ordinance will be exempted from the dual language and content requirements and other prospectus related requirements on the condition that such prospectuses are issued only to "professional investors";
- Companies seeking listing on the main board of the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited and the Growth Enterprises Market may distribute listing prospectuses in English and Chinese separately subject to compliance with certain conditions;
- The accountant's reporting period required for listing prospectuses of GEM companies is shortened from 3 to 2 years;
- No valuation report for operating leases that have been adjudicated at zero value is required for inclusion in listing prospectuses for companies seeking listing on the main board of the SEHK or GEM.
 

The Companies Ordinance requires a prospectus to be issued in Hong Kong, whether by or on behalf of a Hong Kong incorporated company or an overseas company, to comply with content and language requirements set down in the Ordinance. The Ordinance also prescribes time limit for allotment of shares or debentures and other conditions relating to distribution of application forms in connection with a prospectus.

The SFC has powers under the Companies Ordinance to issue exemptions from compliance with any or all of the foregoing requirements on the basis that compliance with them will be unduly burdensome or irrelevant. The SFC's powers to exempt may be exercised either on a case by case basis pursuant to Section 38A(1) and 342A(1) respectively or by way of blanket exemptions pursuant to sections 38A(2) and 342A(2) of the Ordinance.

In recent years, the SFC has granted individual exemptions, which are of nature similar to the ones contained in the Notice, on a relatively regular basis. The SFC noted the increasing need for these exemptions due to the undue burden on companies for complying with the corresponding prospectus requirements in the Ordinance. The SFC therefore decided to exercise its exemption powers under sections 38A and 342A to issue the four class exemptions as contained in the Notice. Once the Notice becomes effective, prospectuses and companies that meet the conditions under a specific class exemption need not apply to the SFC for the related exemption on an individual basis. The SFC believes that this will help facilitate the market in conducting securities offer without, at the same time, compromising investors' protection.

The Notice was published on 30 March 2001 in Legal Supplement No. 2 to the Government Gazette as L.N 76 of 2001 and can be viewed on the Government's website at www.info.gov.hk/pd/egazette/. The Notice has been tabled before the Legislative Council and, subject to negative vetting, will take effect on 11 May 2001. The SFC will keep the market informed of any further development.

"Exemptions for offerings to professional investors only"

This exempts companies which are incorporated in Hong Kong and their prospectuses from compliance with the dual language requirement and the content requirements under sections 38(1) of the Ordinance on the basis that these prospectuses are issued only to persons whose ordinary course of business is the sale and purchase of shares or debentures, whether as principal or agent (commonly referred to as "professional investors"). It also exempts from compliance with the 30-day time limit for allotment of shares and debentures after issuance of a prospectus by such company and the requirement to distribute prospectuses together with the application forms to such investors.

The SFC notes that in Part XII of the Ordinance, which governs prospectus requirements for overseas companies, section 343(2) does not require offering documents issued only to professional investors to be registered as a prospectus. Accordingly, there is no need for such prospectuses to comply with the content requirements and other conditions applicable to distribution of prospectus and application forms. On the other hand, similar relaxation is not available in the Ordinance to companies incorporated in Hong Kong.

The SFC considers that professional investors would have sufficient knowledge and experience to make a thorough assessment of any investments before they invest. To require a prospectus that is offered only to professional investors to contain information that is required for retail investors is unduly burdensome on the companies incorporated in Hong Kong and the SFC believes that this exemption will not compromise investors' protection as a whole.

The SFC believes that this class exemption will help create a more level playing field for Hong Kong incorporated companies and overseas companies when they conduct securities offering to professional investors only.

It should be noted that prospectuses issued only to professional investors by companies incorporated in Hong Kong, though entitled to rely on this class exemption, are still subject to the registration requirements under section 38D of the Ordinance.

"Bilingual prospectuses requirements"

The SFC and the SEHK jointly announced a policy statement in September 2000 to allow split language prospectuses to be issued and distributed subject to the condition that both the Chinese and English versions of the prospectus are made available to the public at each place where, and for so long as, the distribution of the documents takes place. Such policy has been incorporated in the SEHK listing rules and the GEM listing rules since then.

It is in line with the joint policy statement that this class exemption is issued. This class exemption contains the same conditions as described in the joint policy for the distribution of split Chinese and English prospectuses of a company seeking listing on the main board of the SEHK or GEM. This exemption is also in support of the Government's overall environmental protection policy where the volume of printed materials may be reduced by allowing split language prospectuses to be distributed. This exemption is applicable to prospectuses issued by Hong Kong incorporated or overseas companies seeking listing on the main board of the SEHK or GEM.

"Exemption relating to accounting period for GEM companies"

This class exemption serves to shorten the period to be covered in the accountant's report from three preceding years to two preceding years, for prospectuses issued by companies to be listed on GEM. This would bring the requirements under the Ordinance in line with the two-year requirement under the GEM Listing Rules. In view of the nature of these growth companies and the requirement under the GEM Listing Rules, the SFC considers that it would be unduly burdensome for them to include a three-year accountant's report in their prospectuses.

This exemption is applicable to prospectuses issued by Hong Kong incorporated companies and overseas companies seeking listing on GEM.

"Exemption for valuation of operating leases"

Pursuant to paragraph 34 of the Third Schedule to the Companies Ordinance, a company has to provide in its prospectus a valuation report for all its property interests (including interests held as a lessee under an operating lease) if such property interests are shown in the company's last accounts to have exceeded 10% of the company's asset value or to have a value of not less than HK$3,000,000.

At present, the listing rules of the main board of the SEHK and the GEM listing rules provide that valuation report on properties under operating leases may be excluded from any listing document or circulars to shareholders on the basis that such leases are adjudicated to be of zero commercial value by a qualified valuer and that certain other conditions are met.

This class exemption serves to harmonise the treatment of valuation reports of operating leases that have a zero value under the above listing rules and the Ordinance insofar as a listing prospectus is concerned. By excluding the production of a full valuation report on a company's operating leases, this exemption also helps in reducing the bulkiness of a prospectus.

This exemption is applicable to Hong Kong incorporated companies and overseas companies seeking listing on the main board of the SEHK or GEM.
