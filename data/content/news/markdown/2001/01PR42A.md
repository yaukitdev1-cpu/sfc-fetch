The SFC announced today that it has publicly reprimanded {X}, a dealing director of Grand Securities Company Limited. 

The reprimand stems from an inquiry which revealed that in 1998 {X} had

- received and executed orders from a third party to operate four accounts without proper authorisation from the clients;
- signed as a witness on the account opening forms of these clients without witnessing their signatures;
failed to establish the true and full identity of these clients and their financial situation, investment experience and investment objectives. In deciding to publicly reprimand {X}, the SFC took into account that

- {X} acknowledges and accepts that his conduct had not met the standard expected by the SFC of a registered dealing director;
{X} has co-operated with the SFC throughout the inquiries.
