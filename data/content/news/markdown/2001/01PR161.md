The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today released for public consultation the draft Securities and Futures (Accounts and Audit) Rules (draft Rules). The rules are to be made under the Securities and Futures Bill (Bill) when the Bill is enacted. The Bill is currently being considered by a Legislative Council Bills Committee.

As is the position under the Securities Ordinance, the Commodities Trading Ordinance and the Leveraged Foreign Exchange Trading Ordinance, the Bill does not contain any detailed requirements in relation to the annual accounts to be submitted by licensed corporations or associated entities. It merely provides the SFC with the necessary rule-making power to prescribe requirements in the subsidiary legislation. This approach is consistent with modern securities legislation in order that regulators can respond efficiently to changing market conditions and practices.

While there are controls already built into the legislative system whereby any rules made by the SFC must be subject to negative vetting by the Legislative Council, the SFC now releases the draft Rules for public consultation.

In essence, the draft Rules prescribe the contents of the annual accounts to be submitted by licensed corporations and their associated entities to the SFC.

In the formulation of these draft Rules, the SFC has already consulted selected representatives from the brokerage industry and the Hong Kong Society of Accountants.

The SFC has used the new FinNet communication network to send copies of the consultation document to registered dealers that have lodged their Financial Resources Rules returns electronically with the SFC via FinNet. In addition, copies of the consultation document are available free of charge at the SFC's office and can also be downloaded from the SFC's Internet website at [http://www.hksfc.org.hk](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/consult/e-con-accountsrules.pdf).
