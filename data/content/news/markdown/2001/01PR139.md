On 9 November 2001, the HKSAR Government published in the Gazette an additional list of persons designated by a United Nations Security Council Committee to be individuals and entities of the Taliban and Taliban undertakings, or connected with Usama bin Laden. The two Gazette notices ([G.N.6934](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/intermediaries/supervision/e_gazette6934.pdf) and [G.N.6935](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/intermediaries/supervision/e_gazette6935.pdf)) are published pursuant to section 3(3)(b) of the United Nations Sanctions (Afghanistan) Regulation and section 19 of the United Nations Sanctions (Afghanistan) (Arms Embargoes) Regulation respectively. The sanctions imposed under these Regulations include, among other requirements, the freezing of funds and financial resources of individuals and entities designated by the relevant United Nations Security Council Committee, as well as prohibiting any action which makes funds or financial resources available to them. The funds and financial resources which are required to be frozen also include any gold, securities or investments.

The SFC reminds all registered intermediaries to check the names in the above two Gazette notices against their records, and take all necessary steps to ensure compliance with the Regulations. These names have been designated under the Regulations in addition to the entities and individuals which were previously designated and mentioned in the SFC's circular to registered intermediaries dated 22 October 2001. Registered intermediaries should also report to the [Joint Financial Intelligence Unit](http://www.oecd.org/) if any of the names are identified in their records.

For reference, a copy of the Gazette notices ([G.N.6934](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/intermediaries/supervision/e_gazette6934.pdf) and [G.N.6935](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/intermediaries/supervision/e_gazette6935.pdf)) are available in SFC's website www.hksfc.org.hk together with this press release under the General News section.
