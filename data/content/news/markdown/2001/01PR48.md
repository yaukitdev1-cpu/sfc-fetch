The Financial Secretary of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, Mr Antony Leung hosted a seminar entitled "Asia and Global Finance: New Opportunities" today. Government officials as well as senior executives from the banking and securities industries in the Mainland, the Asia-Pacific region, and the US shared their views on market reforms and financing in Asia with over 200 participants. The seminar was organised by the Hong Kong Securities Institute, with the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) and Hong Kong Monetary Authority acting as advisers.

Following the Financial Secretary's welcoming remarks, Mr Dai Xianglong, Governor of People's Bank of China, talked about financial development and co-operation in Asia. Mr Dai said that China would continue to pursue proactive fiscal policy and sound monetary policy and expand domestic market. He also anticipated that China's imminent accession to the WTO would lead to greater openness of the Chinese economy and financial market and closer economic co-operation in the region.

Two panel discussion sessions followed. Panel 1 discussed East Asia reforms from the perspectives of China, Japan, Singapore and the US. Panel 2 focused on the implications of financing in Asia for strategic, portfolio and direct investors and how Fortune 500 multinationals could tap Asian markets to manage their risks and achieve growth.

During the luncheon, Mr Mickey Kantor, former Secretary of Commerce and United States Trade Representative, talked about the implications of globalisation of trade and finance for Asia. The Secretary for Financial Services, Mr Stephen Ip then concluded the seminar with his closing remarks.
