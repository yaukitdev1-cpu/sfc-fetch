The Revised Code of Conduct for Persons Registered with the Securities and Futures Commission issued in February 2001 reflects the new conduct requirements applicable to registered persons effective from 1 April 2001, with the exception of the requirements related to telephone recording and client agreements which will take effect on 1 October 2001. 

The Commission has already conducted 6 seminars, briefing about 1,200 market participants on the revised requirements. To promote a better understanding of how the Commission will approach these requirements, the Commission has now collated a list of [Frequently Asked Questions.](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/intermediaries/supervision/coc-faq.pdf)

The public can find the Frequently Asked Questions under "Update for Intermediaries: Others" in the Intermediaries, Licensing & Investment Products section of the SFC website at http: //www.hksfc.org.hk.
