The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) announced today that it had successfully prosecuted both Brightline Futures Company Limited (Brightline) and its director {X} under the Securities (Disclosure of Interests) Ordinance.


The case against Brightline related to its acquisitions and disposals of a total of 80 million and 44.472 million shares of Star Bio-Tech (Holdings) Limited (Star Bio-Tech) (previously known as Chung Hwa Development Holdings Limited) on four occasions during the period 18 January 2000 to 31 March 2000. Brightline, a substantial shareholder of Star Bio-Tech, failed to report to the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong its changes of interests in the shares of Star Bio-Tech within 5 days of its becoming aware of such changes. At the material time, {X} was the director of Brightline responsible for reporting the changes.


Ms Polly Lo, a Magistrate at Western Magistracy, fined Brightline and {X} of $26,000 and $5,000 respectively and ordered them to pay investigation costs of a total of $29,707 to the SFC.
