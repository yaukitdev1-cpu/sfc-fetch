The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today published a monthly newsletter named "[SFC Alert](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/newsletter/october.pdf)" to enhance its communication with investors and the industry. 

SFC Chairman Mr Andrew Sheng said: "By publishing the newsletter on a regular basis, we hope that the public will know more about what we do. We will, in each issue, inform the public of our latest policy initiatives and major enforcement actions, as well as explaining what we hope to achieve."

"The newsletter is written in plain language to help everyone understand our work. It provides links to our web site for those who want to get more information."

The lead story in the launch issue, "Open for business", reports the performance of Hong Kong markets after the events of September 11 and notes that Hong Kong has come a long way since the closure of October 1987. Another main story in the newsletter is "The decline of the passive investor" which highlights key findings of our latest investor survey. The survey finds that nearly 80% of retail investors want to know more about their rights. The feature piece, "Bailey busts bungling broker", gives a lively account of how SFC Executive Director of Enforcement Mr Paul Bailey was offered an investment opportunity too good to be true.

The newsletter will have a regular column on enforcement actions and the column this time talks about recent cases of the Securities (Disclosure of Interests) Ordinance. The legal updates column reminds intermediaries of the provisions in the new Code of Conduct for Persons Registered with the Securities and Futures Commission that came into effect on 1 October 2001.

The newsletter is available on the SFC web site at http://www.hksfc.org.hk, under "Publications" of "Press Releases, Publications & Speeches" section. Copies are also available at the SFC office.
