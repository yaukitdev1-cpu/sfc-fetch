Mrs Alexa Lam, SFC's Executive Director of Intermediaries and Investment Products, gave some opening remarks this morning at the Asia Pacific Convention organised by the Global Association of Risk Professionals.

Mrs Lam said the well-being of investors was key to the healthy development of financial markets and the SFC sought to ensure that our regulatory framework would provide sufficient investor protection without unnecessarily impeding market innovation.

Mrs Lam called on financial intermediaries to put in place proper internal controls and risk management measures to comply with all statutory and regulatory requirements, including the general high-level principles in all SFC Codes and Guidelines. In particular, she expected financial intermediaries offering investment advice to act in the clients’ best interests and give reasonably suitable recommendations. These intermediaries were reminded to:
 

- know their clients;
- conduct adequate product due diligence;
- make suitable recommendations given the specific circumstances of each of their clients; and
- help clients make informed investment decisions by explaining the basis of their recommendations and the risks involved.

Mrs Lam said the SFC would be working closely with the industry to further enhance investor protection and facilitate market development.
