The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X} for short selling securities on 25 February 2004.

{X} pleaded guilty today before Ms Bina Chainrai, a Magistrate at Eastern Magistracy, to two summonses in relation to his short selling of securities in contravention of Section 170 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance. Section 170 (1) prohibits the sale of securities when the person does not have a presently exercisable and unconditional right to sell them.

{X} was fined a total of $10,000 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $9,152 to the SFC.
