The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X} for advising on futures contracts whilst unlicensed.

An SFC investigation revealed that between August and November 2004, {X}, who was not licensed by the SFC, set up a website and invited the public to join as members. Members had to pay a monthly subscription in order to have access to the advice given by {X} on the website regarding trading in Hang Seng Index futures contracts. The provision of such advice by an unlicensed person contravened section 114 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

{X} today pleaded guilty to one summons before Magistrate Mr Anthony Yuen at Eastern Magistracy. {X} was fined $3,000 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $18,138 to the SFC.
