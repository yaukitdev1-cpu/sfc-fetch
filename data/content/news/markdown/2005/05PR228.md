The SFC is pleased to introduce a wallet-sized card to remind investors to ask the right questions when choosing a financial product. [*Protecting Your Interests Starts with Asking the Right Questions*](http://www.eirc.hk/eirc/doc/EN/publications/cue_card.pdf) is a handy tool that investors can carry around. It is particularly useful when assessing a recommendation of financial products made by intermediaries.

As market regulator, the SFC has a responsibility to educate the public and enable investors to protect their own interests. Further to the investor leaflet [*Questions to Ask When Seeking Investment Advice*](http://www.eirc.hk/eirc/doc/EN/publications/ia_leaflet_eng.pdf) (Note 1), the wallet-sized card lists out the key questions to ask when investors are recommended to invest in a fund, investment-linked assurance scheme, etc.

The key questions are:


- Why is the recommended product suitable to you, based on your personal circumstances (including your investment objectives, investment horizon and attitude to risk), and the product's features and risks?
- Is the recommendation confined to products offered by specific providers? If so, why only those providers?
- How much commission will the intermediary receive from the product provider?
- If the recommendation is to switch from one product to another, what are the costs you might incur and how much commission will the intermediary receive if you switch?
- What are the alternatives available and why are they not being recommended?
- Have you been given all the relevant information about the recommendation? (If you are not sure, ask for a detailed written record of such information.)

As with all other SFC investor publications, the card is available free at the SFC office and the Consumer Advice Centres of the Consumer Council. The electronic version is available under the section of "[Publications" – "Dealing with Intermediaries](http://www.eirc.hk/eirc/html/EN/publications/publications_4.htm)" on the SFC-operated Electronic Investor Resources Centre website at www.eirc.hk.
