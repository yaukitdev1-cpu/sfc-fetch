The SFC welcomes the Financial Secretary's commitment to encouraging the further development of Hong Kong as an international financial centre.

The SFC will fully co-operate with and assist the Government in achieving the objectives of the proposals outlined by the Financial Secretary, Mr Henry Tang.

SFC Chairman, Mr Andrew Sheng, said: "The SFC will continue to work closely with the Administration, Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited and the industry to enhance corporate governance and strengthen investor protection."

"The SFC will also continue to facilitate the development and innovation of the fund industry, in line with the Government’s objective to reinforce Hong Kong’s status as an asset management centre in Asia," Mr Sheng said.
