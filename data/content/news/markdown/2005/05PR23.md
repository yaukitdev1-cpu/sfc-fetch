In response to recent enquiries by market participants and the media, the SFC issues the following update in relation to licensing of hedge fund managers.

Having regard to the rapid growth in the hedge fund industry, the SFC has designated a focus team to handle licence applications of hedge fund managers and answer related enquiries since October 2004. To date, it has received nine such licence applications, of which three have been approved. On average, it took approximately 10 weeks to process a fund management company application including assessing its corporate governance and control systems.

Mrs Alexa Lam, SFC’s Executive Director of Intermediaries and Investment Products, said: "We would like to stress that the robust licensing framework in Hong Kong serves to protect investors and maintain market integrity and quality. While performing a critical gate-keeping function to guard against unsuitable persons entering the industry, the SFC, as a proactive regulator, continuously seeks to facilitate the entry of qualified market participants. However, under no circumstances will this be done at the expense of lowering regulatory standards or investor protection."

Where an overseas fund manager wishes to gain an earlier foothold in Hong Kong, it may consider also applying for a temporary licence for Type 4 regulated activity (i.e. advising on securities). Such a temporary licence will allow the fund manager to provide related advisory services to clients before its normal asset management licence is granted (Note 1).

Enquiries of hedge fund managers on related licensing matters can be sent to [hedgefund@sfc.hk](mailto:hedgefund@sfc.hk).
