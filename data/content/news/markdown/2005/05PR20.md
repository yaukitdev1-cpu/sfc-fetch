The SFC is working closely with the fund industry with a view to facilitating the processing of UCITS III funds. The SFC has informed market practitioners that the SFC will process UCITS III fund applications. In fact, the SFC has already started doing so.

Due to the expansion of the investment scope and powers of UCITS III funds, there must be appropriate disclosures of the changes in the investment scope and associated risks of the relevant UCITS III fund. In this regard, the SFC has already laid down the guiding principles for the industry to work on the details to reflect current market development. On the operational side, the SFC expects fund managers to have in place adequate risk management and controls systems to ensure that their systems are commensurate with the relevant UCITS III funds.

The SFC will review the Code on Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds (the Code) consult the market as to how the relevant features of UCITS III could be accommodated in the Code on a longer-term basis. An investor education programme will also be conducted to inform investors of the new investment flexibility offered under UCITS III funds.

Mrs Alexa Lam, Executive Director of Intermediaries and Investment Products, said: "In light of the above measures, the SFC believes that the Hong Kong public will continue to have access to a wide range of investment products as a result of the transition to the UCITS III regime. The SFC is committed to working with the industry on the processing of UCITS III fund applications to ensure that investors are properly informed of the new features and associated risks of UCITS III funds. The SFC therefore expects fund documents to clearly disclose the changes under UCITS III, the resultant risks and the risk management measures put in place. Such information has to be presented in plain language for investors’ easy understanding."
