The SFC has today [gazetted the revised Codes on Takeovers and Mergers and Share Repurchases](http://www.gld.gov.hk/cgi-bin/gld/egazette/gazettefiles.cgi?lang=e&extra=&year=2005&month=9&day=16&vol=9&no=37&header=1&acurrentpage=10&agree=1&df=0&gaz_type=ls4&level=2&pid=18359&gn=18359&newfile=0). The effective date of operation of the revised Codes is 1 October 2005 except for the amended provisions under Notes 1 and 2 to Rule 8 of the Takeovers Code which are effective on 1 January 2006.
