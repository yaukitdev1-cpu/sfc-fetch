The SFC welcomes the appointment by the HKSAR Chief Executive of [Mr Martin Wheatley](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/image/EN/aboutsfc/structure/commission/martin_wheatley_050930.jpg) as SFC Chairman from 1 October 2005.

The Commission looks forward to working with Mr Wheatley, who has extensive experience in the securities industry in a leading international financial centre.

Before joining the SFC last June, Mr Wheatley had been with the London Stock Exchange for 18 years, his last position being Deputy Chief Executive. He had served on the Board of the London Stock Exchange for six years. Mr Wheatley is currently SFC’s Executive Director of Supervision of Markets.

Mr Wheatley said: “I am honoured to be appointed by the Chief Executive as Chairman of the SFC and feel privileged to succeed Mr Andrew Sheng, who has ably led the Commission in the last seven years.”

“Andrew is highly regarded among the international securities and financial regulators, and has made tremendous contribution to raising the international profile of the SFC and Hong Kong,” he said. “He has built a more professional team at the SFC and made the organisation more transparent and accountable. It is an efficient and highly motivated group of people and I am delighted to be able to take over at this critical time for the market.”

“The challenge is for Hong Kong to develop its very strong financial services base to build its unique capital centre position for the whole of China. I look forward to playing a full part in developing and implementing that vision,” Mr Wheatley said.

The Commission wishes to express its most sincere gratitude to Mr Sheng. Mr Sheng joined the SFC as its fourth Chairman on 1 October 1998. His tenure ends today.

As the longest serving Chairman of the SFC, Mr Sheng has led the SFC in many reforms of the Hong Kong securities and futures market over the last seven years. One of the most notable achievements is the making and implementation of the Securities and Futures Ordinance. The SFO, which became effective in 2003, modernised the regulatory framework in Hong Kong to international standards.

The Board of Directors passed a vote of thanks to Mr Sheng for his leadership over the past seven years, when he attended the last Commission meeting on 20 September.

Mrs Alexa Lam, Acting Chairman of the SFC, said: “Mr Sheng will be remembered as a respected and kindly Chairman whose leadership, enthusiasm and charisma set examples to all those who have worked with him. On behalf of all the colleagues of the Commission, I wish Mr Sheng all the best in his future pursuits.”
