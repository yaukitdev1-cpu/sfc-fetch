The SFC today publishes a new investor education leaflet, [*Real Estate Investment Trusts*](http://www.eirc.hk/eirc/doc/EN/publications/REIT_2005_E.pdf) (REITs), which aims to update investors on the basics of SFC-authorised REITs under the revised SFC Code on REITs (Note 1).

Following the SFC’s revision of its Code on REITs in June 2005, SFC-authorised REITs are allowed to invest in properties in both Hong Kong and overseas. While REITs will be subject to the same set of regulations regardless of the location of their properties, we would like to refresh investors’ understanding of REITs and highlight to them the salient issues to consider prior to making any investment decisions.

Investors are advised to note the followings:


- Always read the offering circular before making an investment decision to subscribe units in a REIT. Aside from financial information on the pricing and the yield, investors should also consider other important factors, such as the experience and track record of the REIT manager, the investment objective and strategy of the REIT, its underlying investments and the attendant risks, the dividend distribution policy, and the fees structure.
- The listing of SFC-authorised REITs does not necessarily mean that there will be a liquid market for the REITs or that investors are able to trade them at better prices.
- A REIT may trade at a premium or discount to its net asset value.

Mrs Alexa Lam, SFC’s Executive Director of Intermediaries and Investment Products, said: “The SFC is committed to facilitating market development and product innovation while ensuring investors’ interests are properly protected. REITs provide investors an alternative means to invest in real estate with a recurrent stream of dividend income. However, investors should note that the dividends are not guaranteed and are subject to the profitability of REITs. It is of paramount importance that investors understand the characteristics and risk profile of a REIT before making an investment decision.”

As with all other SFC investor publications, the new investor leaflet is available free at the SFC office and the Consumer Advice Centres of the Consumer Council. The electronic version is available under the section of [“Publications – Funds”](http://www.eirc.hk/eirc/html/EN/publications/publications_5.htm) on the SFC’s investor education portal, Electronic Investor Resources Centre at [www.eirc.hk](http://www.eirc.hk).

Notes to Editor:

1. A REIT is a collective investment scheme that invests in a portfolio of income-generating real estate, such as shopping malls, offices or service apartments in Hong Kong and/or overseas.
