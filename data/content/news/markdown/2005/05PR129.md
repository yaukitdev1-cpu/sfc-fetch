The SFC welcomes the appointments by the Financial Secretary of Mr Henry Chan, Mr Jack Maisano, Mr Peter Sullivan, Ms Ada Tse, and Mr Yip Lai-shing to the SFC Advisory Committee.

The SFC also welcomes the re-appointments by the Financial Secretary of Mr Thaddeus Beczak, Mr Ambrose Cheung, Mr Paul Chow, Mr Paul Fan, Ms Christine Loh, Mr Samuel Poon and Mr Blair Pickerell.

SFC Chairman, Mr Andrew Sheng, said: "I warmly welcome all new members of the Advisory Committee. The SFC will benefit greatly from their knowledge and experience. I also look forward to the continuous support and advice of the re-appointed members."

Mr Sheng also thanked Mr Stephen Hui, Mr Roger Luk and Dr Eden Woon, who have retired from the Advisory Committee, for their valuable contribution.

The Advisory Committee is set up pursuant to section 7 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance to advise the SFC on matters of policy regarding any of its regulatory objectives and functions.
