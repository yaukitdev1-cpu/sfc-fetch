The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X1} and {X2} for making unsolicited calls.

An SFC investigation found that between early and mid-2004, {X1} and {X2}, who at that time were representatives of Hantec International Limited, had made unsolicited calls on various occasions inducing others to enter into agreements for trading in leveraged foreign exchange contracts, in contravention of Section.174 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

{X1} and {X2} pleaded guilty to the summonses today before Mr Ian Candy, a Magistrate at Eastern Magistracy.  {X1} was fined $5,000 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $5,000 to the SFC.  {X2} was fined $10,000 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $6,000.
