The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X} for engaging in leveraged foreign exchange trading activities whilst unlicensed.

An SFC investigation revealed that between May and July 2004, {X}, who was not licensed by the SFC, solicited a person to open an account with Hong Kong Forex Investment Limited to trade leveraged foreign exchange contracts in breach of section 114 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

{X} today pleaded guilty to one summons before Magistrate Mr Ian Candy at Eastern Magistracy. {X} was fined $5,000 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $5,000 to the SFC.
