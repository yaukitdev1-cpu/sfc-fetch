The SFC today publishes the [December issue of SFC Alert](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/newsletter/05/nov_dec05_eng.pdf), a bi-monthly newsletter for both investors and intermediaries.

On page one, *A Healthy Market for Informed Investors* outlines the SFC’s Six-Point Plan to enhance the regulation of derivative warrants.

In the regular Senior Management Interview, Mr Eugène Goyne, Senior Director, Enforcement, explains the SFC’s disciplinary work and its settlement policy.

Inside, *Notification of interests in REITs* covers the SFC policy to require that provisions substantially equivalent to those in Part XV (Disclosure of Interests) of the Securities and Futures Ordinance be adopted in trust deeds of REITs. Meanwhile, another regular column, Investor focus, provides useful investor education information about REITs.

Further to the special on FAQs in the July/August issue, this issue covers more FAQs regarding the licensing of fund managers, including those concerning responsible officers.

Copies of the current issue of SFC Alert are available at the SFC office. [All issues are available on the SFC website](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/speeches/public/newsletter/newsletter.html).
