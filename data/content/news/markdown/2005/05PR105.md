The SFC today publishes the [April issue of the *SFC Enforcement Reporter*](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/enforcement/05/apr_05.pdf), summarising the SFC’s enforcement action in the month of March 2005 and discussing the compliance issues highlighted by recent cases. 

In March, the SFC successfully prosecuted five companies and four people for mishandling of clients’ securities, disclosure of interests breaches, unlicensed advising on corporate finance, and short selling.

We disciplined 15 licensees for conflict of interests and unsound advice, facilitating suspicious orders, price stabilizing rules breaches, Financial Resources Rules breaches, failings related to client accounts, unlicensed activities, and poor conduct standards.

In the year from 1 April 2004 to 31 March 2005, the SFC has successfully prosecuted 76 entities (60 in 2003-2004 and 37 in 2002-2003). We have withdrawn summonses issued against eight entities, and a further six entities were acquitted after trial. We offered no evidence against three entities.

In disciplinary proceedings, we took action against a total of 88 licensees in 2004-2005. Among them we disciplined 78 licensees for various regulatory breaches and entered into settlements without formal sanctions with 10 licensees, of which four were settled with voluntary payments. In addition, the SFC took disciplinary actions against 28 licensees, which were concluded with no formal sanction imposed, although 15 of them received private warnings*.* Disciplinary proceedings were also commenced and discontinued against four deemed licensees who left their firms before conclusion of the action.

The April issue of the *SFC Enforcement Reporter* is available at the SFC office. All issues are also available on the SFC website under ["Speeches & Publications” – “Publications”](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/speeches/public/enforcement/enforcement.html).
