SFC Chairman, Mr Martin Wheatley, has visited the China Securities Regulatory Commission in Beijing.

During his two-day visit that ended yesterday (27 October), Mr Wheatley had met CSRC Chairman, Mr Shang Fulin, and other senior CSRC officials. This was Mr Wheatley’s first visit to Beijing since he was appointed as SFC Chairman on 1 October.

The visit was a courtesy call, and the two Chairmen took the opportunity to discuss matters of mutual interest. They discussed topics including Mainland’s state-owned shares reform and Hong Kong’s role in the development of the PRC capital market.

During the discussion, Mr Shang and Mr Wheatley agreed that the CSRC and the SFC should further develop the excellent working relationship that had been built between the two Commissions over the past years.

While he was in Beijing, Mr Wheatley also met Mr Zhou Xiaochuan, Governor of The People’s Bank of China, and Mr Liu Mingkang, Chairman of the China Banking Regulatory Commission.

Mr Wheatley said: “I was very impressed with the hospitality of Chairman Shang and our other Mainland hosts. It was my great pleasure to meet them. We had very useful discussions and I am pleased to say that we would continue this useful dialogue in the future.”

Mr Wheatley was accompanied by Mr Peter Au-Yang, SFC’s Chief Operating Officer.
