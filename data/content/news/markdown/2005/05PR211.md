The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X} for advising on mutual funds whilst unlicensed.

An SFC investigation revealed that {X}, whilst unlicensed, had advised a client to subscribe for shares in mutual fund corporations in July 2003, in contravention of section 114 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

{X} today pleaded guilty before Mr Eddie Yip, a Magistrate at Eastern Magistracy, and was fined $5,000, which will be paid to the SFC as investigation costs.
