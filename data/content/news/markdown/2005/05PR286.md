The SFC wishes to remind the market that Notes 1 and 2 to Rule 8 of the Takeovers Code (as contained in the current October 2005 version of the Codes on Takeovers and Mergers and Share Repurchases) in relation to documents being made available for inspection on websites will become effective from 1 January 2006.

The Notes will apply, where applicable, to any transaction subject to the Codes which is announced on or after 1 January 2006.

Pursuant to Note 2, a copy of each document required to be displayed under Note 1 must also be provided in electronic form acceptable to the Executive for display on the SFC website. The Documents on Display (DoD) Submission Form and a “How to use” guide will be made available for downloading in the “Forms – Takeovers & Mergers Related” section of the SFC website from 1 January 2006.
