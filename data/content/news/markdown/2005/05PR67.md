The SFC has today gazetted an amendment to section 11.3 of the Introduction to the Hong Kong Codes on Takeovers and Mergers and Share Repurchases to increase the membership of the Takeovers Panel from 26 to 30. The amendment is intended to facilitate the expeditious and efficient dealing of matters by the Takeovers Panel. The effective date of operation of the revised provision is 1 April 2005.
