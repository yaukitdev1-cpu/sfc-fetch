The SFC today publishes the [July issue of the SFC Enforcement Reporter](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/enforcement/05/july05_eng.pdf), summarising the SFC's enforcement action in the month of June 2005 and discussing the compliance issues highlighted by recent cases.

In June, the SFC successfully prosecuted two companies and seven people for market manipulation, Financial Resources Rules breaches, unlicensed activities, disclosure of interests breaches and short selling of derivatives.

We disciplined four licensees for Financial Resources Rules breaches and providing misleading information to the SFC.

From 1 April 2005 to end of June, the SFC successfully prosecuted 18 entities and offered no evidence against one entity. In the same period, the SFC took action against 14 licensees for various regulatory breaches, of which one case was settled with voluntary payment and with no formal sanction imposed.

Copies of the July issue of the SFC Enforcement Reporter are available at the SFC office. All issues are also available on the SFC website under ["Speeches, Publications & Consultations" – "Publications"](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/speeches/public/enforcement/enforcement.html).

The public are welcome to subscribe to the newsletter by registering for the [Update Alert](https://www.sfc.hk/sfcUpdateAlert/jsp/EN/update_alert.jsp) service on the SFC website. They will then be able to receive the newsletter via email.

The public are also invited to provide feedback so we can improve the SFC Enforcement Reporter. Comments can be emailed to [enfreporter@sfc.hk](mailto:enfreporter@sfc.hk).
