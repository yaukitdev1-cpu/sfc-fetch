The SFC is pleased to announce the appointment of a new Chairman and a new Deputy Chairman to the Takeovers Appeal Committee for the period from 22 February 2005 to 31 March 2005, the end of the current term.

The SFC welcomes Ms Angelina Lee as new Chairman of the Takeovers Appeal Committee in replacement of Mr Kenneth Kwok SC who stepped down from Chairmanship of the Takeovers Appeal Committee with effect from 1 January 2005 following his appointment as an independent Non-Executive Director of the SFC. Prior to this appointment, Ms Lee was the Deputy Chairman of the Takeovers Appeal Committee.

The SFC also welcomes Ms Teresa Ko as new Deputy Chairman of the Takeovers Appeal Committee to succeed Ms Angelina Lee following her appointment as Chairman. Prior to this appointment, Ms Ko was a member of the Takeovers Appeal Committee.
