The SFC warmly welcomes the re-appointment by the Financial Secretary of Dr York Liao as a Non-Executive Director of the SFC.
 
“Our Non-Executive Directors have played a crucial role in the SFC’s formulation of its policies. Their professional expertise and valuable insight has helped enhance the quality of our regulations and strengthen the Commission’s governance structure,” said SFC Chairman, Mr Andrew Sheng. “We look forward to the continuous support and advice of Dr Liao.”
 
Dr Liao has been an SFC Non-Executive Director since May 2003. He is also a member of the Remuneration Committee, Committee on Investment-Linked Assurance and Pooled Retirement Funds, Committee on Unit Trusts and SFC (HKEC Listing) Appeals Committee.
