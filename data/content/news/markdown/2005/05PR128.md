The SFC today publishes the [Spring 2005 issue (No.59) of the *SFC Quarterly Bulletin*](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/speeches/public/bulletin/sfc_bulletin/spring_05.htm).

The first feature article *The Challenge of the Global Securities Markets* discusses the major challenges to the global clearing and settlement system amid the tremendous growth of cross-border trading.

The second feature article *A Comparison Between Shenzhen SME Companies and Hong Kong GEM Companies* compares the basic characteristics of the two boards.  The contrasting characteristics and performance are mainly due to certain fundamental differences such as the listing requirements of the boards.

The speech* Attracting Global Investors to Emerging Markets* was presented by SFC Chairman at the IOSCO in Taipei.  It discusses the development of the financial markets and challenges in the emerging markets.  The speech remarks that emerging markets should transform from a relationship-based economy to a regulation-based economy and encourage the participation of foreign players in order to improve the transparency, efficiency and fairness of the markets. These are the qualities of a market that will attract global investors.

This issue also includes the *Quarterly Review of the Securities Market* and the *Quarterly Financial Review of the Securities Industry.*  The former reviews the performance of the local and regional stock markets during the first quarter of 2005, whilst the latter analyses the financial performance of SEHK participants over the same period.  The Reports found that:

- Hong Kong’s securities market consolidated after the advance in the fourth quarter of 2004; and
- overall profitability of the securities industry had improved by 34%, when compared with the fourth quarter of 2004 primarily owing to a significant reduction in overhead expenses.
