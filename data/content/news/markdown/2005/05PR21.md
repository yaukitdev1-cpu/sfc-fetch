The latest SFC-RTHK drama series, “All about Stock Investing”, is now available in VCD to the public free of charge.
 
The Cantonese drama series features nine five-minute episodes of investment stories surrounding several commuters on a public mini-bus. They discussed hot investor topics such as subscribing for shares in initial public offerings, finding the right intermediary, pooling risk of margin accounts, stock custody, dealing with market news and rumours, shareholder activism, and corporate governance. The series was previously aired on the television and public buses.
 
“All about Stock Investing” is one of SFC’s initiatives to educate investors to invest more wisely. Artiste Ms BoBo Chan, market professionals Mr Chan Wing Luk and Mr Ben Kwong, SFC Public Shareholders Group Member Mr David Lui and the Hong Kong Institute of Directors Chairman Mr Herbert Hui are among those guests appearing in the programme.
 
The public may obtain free copies of the VCD at the SFC office. The series can also be watched on the [“Studio” section of the SFC-operated Electronic Investor Resources Centre (www.eirc.hk)](http://www.eirc.hk/eirc/html/EN/studio/studio_audio11.htm).
