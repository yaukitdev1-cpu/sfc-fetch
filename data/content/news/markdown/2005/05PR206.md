The SFC today makes the following statement regarding {X}.  {X} was registered as a dealer's representative under the Securities Ordinance (repealed) in March 1999.  He left the securities industry in March 2002 before the SFC concluded a disciplinary action on his misconduct as follows:


{X} admitted to the SFC that while he was a dealer's representative from December 2000 to February 2002, he had failed to:-



- inquire into questionable trades placed in his clients' accounts; and

- keep proper recording of his clients' orders.



The SFC takes the view that {X}'s conduct has impugned his fitness and properness to be a licensed person.  {X} expressed regret for his misconduct and consented to the publication of this statement by the SFC.


In deciding on this course of action, the SFC has taken into consideration that {X} has been out of the securities industry for more than three years, his remorse about his misconduct and co-operation with the SFC in frankly admitting to the incidents of concern (Note 1).


Licensed persons are reminded to act in the best interest of their clients and the integrity of the market.  These are essential for the protection of investors and to ensure that investors are dealing with honest and effective licensed persons.


Ends


Note:


1. {X} is currently re-applying for a licence.
