The SFC is pleased that its Annual Report 2004-2005 has been given the Platinum Award in the Public Sector/Not-for-profit Organisations Category in the Hong Kong Institute of Certified Public Accountants’ Best Corporate Governance Disclosure Awards 2005.

SFC Chairman, Mr Martin Wheatley, received the award today at the HKICPA ceremony to announce the results.

Mr Wheatley said that in the same way that listed companies provided confidence to investors by demonstrating adherence to high standards of corporate governance, regulators also needed to show that they had adopted high standards of corporate governance in order to gain the confidence of market practitioners and investors.

“High standards of corporate governance are essential attributes for all types of entities and organisations, be they profit making or non-profit making,” he said.

“We need to account for our performance and describe our systems, procedures and processes in sufficient detail to give our stakeholders confidence in our performance,” Mr Wheatley said.
