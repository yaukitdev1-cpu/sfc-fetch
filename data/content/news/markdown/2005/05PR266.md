The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X} for making unsolicited calls.

An SFC investigation found that in August and September 2003, {X}, who was licensed as a representative of Tanrich Futures Limited at the time, made telephone calls to and visited some persons who were not clients of Tanrich with a view to inducing them to enter into agreements for trading in futures contracts, in contravention of section 174 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

{X} today pleaded guilty to two summonses before Mr Ian Candy, a Magistrate at Eastern Magistracy. {X}was fined $10,000 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $15,000 to the SFC.
