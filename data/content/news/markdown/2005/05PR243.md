The SFC today publishes the [October issue of SFC Alert](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/newsletter/05/sep_oct05.pdf), a bi-monthly newsletter for both investors and intermediaries.

The HKSAR Chief Executive’s appointment of Mr Martin Wheatley as the SFC’s fifth Chairman is the lead story. The SFC welcomes the appointment and pays tribute to former Chairman Mr Andrew Sheng.

Also on the front page, *Public Perception Survey* outlines the findings of the SFC’s first such survey, which seeks to assess the public’s recognition of the SFC’s image, role and responsibilities.

In the regular *Senior Management Interview*, Ms Wang Yuan, Adviser, China Policy, Chairman’s Office, introduces the work of her department and discusses several topical issues concerning the Mainland and Hong Kong.

Inside, *LOI with Jersey* reports the SFC’s second LOI with a non-Asian partner. *IOSCO update* reports that the SFC is inviting interested parties to register for the 31st Annual Conference of the International Organization of Securities Commissions (IOSCO), which will be held in Hong Kong on 5-8 June 2006.

There are articles on the recent changes to the Hedge Fund Guidelines and Codes on Takeovers and Mergers and Share Repurchases, as well as the proposals to modernise the prospectus regime governing the public offering of shares and debentures.

Another regular column, *Investor focus*, tells investors that derivative warrants are far more complicated than a geared substitute of their underlying stocks.

Copies of the current issue of *SFC Alert* are available at the SFC office. [All issues are available on the SFC website](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/speeches/public/newsletter/newsletter.html).
