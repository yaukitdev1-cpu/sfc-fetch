The SFC today publishes the February issue of *SFC Alert*, a bi-monthly newsletter for both investors and intermediaries.

*2004 – A year of progress* gives an overview of the SFC’s accomplishments in 2004 and reports on our latest quarterly financial results.

In the regular *Senior management interview*, Mr Ringo Chiu, Chief Information Officer, talks about how information technology helps the SFC enhance operational efficiency, ensure information security and contribute to the development of Hong Kong’s financial infrastructure.

*Licensing of hedge fund managers* stresses that the SFC will not compromise on regulatory standards while facilitating the entry of qualified market participants. Another article advises investors to check if their intermediaries have applied for migration and, if not, make alternative arrangements with their assets or the services provided by the intermediaries.

*IOSCO strengthens cross-border co-operation* introduces several recent initiatives of the International Organization of Securities Commissions to enhance enforcement co-operation worldwide, assist financial intermediaries to determine the steps to take when outsourcing business activities, and protect the analytical independence of credit rating agencies.

*Transition of UCITS III funds* reports that the SFC is working closely with the fund industry to ensure a smooth transition to the UCITS III regime.

The regular *Investor Focus* column urges investors to safeguard all personal data to avoid losses arising from identity theft.

There are also articles outlining the key proposals made by the SFC in two consultation papers to simplify the current disclosure of interests regime and suspend the investor compensation levies respectively.

The February issue of *SFC Alert* is the 28th issue of the newsletter, which was first published in October 2001.
