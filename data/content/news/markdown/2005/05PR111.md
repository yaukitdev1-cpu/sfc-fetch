The SFC today publishes the [April issue of *SFC Alert*](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/newsletter/05/mar_apr05.pdf), a bi-monthly newsletter for both investors and intermediaries. 

*HK is ready for cross-border REITs* introduces SFC’s proposal to allow SFC authorised REITs to invest in properties worldwide and other facilitative measures.

There are two stories covering the International Organization of Securities Commissions (IOSCO).  The front page article reports the key achievements of the Conference in Colombo and the signing of a Letter of Intent on regulatory co-operation between the SFC and its counterpart in Sri Lanka.  The second story is about an IOSCO report released earlier on how to strengthen capital markets against financial fraud.

In the regular *Senior management interview*, Ms Doris Pak, Commission Secretary, talks about her main responsibilities.  In *Selling practices of licensed IAs*, the SFC says there will be another theme inspection of licensed investment advisers next year.  *Successful SFO migration* notes a high percentage of migration to the new licensing regime under the Securities and Futures Ordinance at the end of a two-year transitional period.

Elsewhere, investors will find useful advice and information in *Look beyond analysts’ recommendations*, which is the topic of this issue’s Investor focus, and *FAQs on UCITS III*.

Copies of the current issue of *SFC Alert* are available at the SFC office. All issues are available on the SFC website.
