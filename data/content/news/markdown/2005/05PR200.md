The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X} for acting as an investment representative of Hong Kong Wealth Management Limited whilst unregistered.

An SFC investigation revealed that between October and November 2002, {X}, who was not registered with the SFC, had advised clients to invest in certain mutual funds authorised by the SFC, in contravention of section 50(1)(b) of the Securities Ordinance.

{X} today pleaded guilty before Mr Anthony Yuen, a Magistrate at Eastern Magistracy. {X} was fined $2,000 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $9,053 to the SFC.
