The SFC is pleased to announce the re-appointment of Mr Thaddeus Beczak, Mr Stephen Clark, Ms Patricia Shih, Mr Carlson Tong and Mr V-Nee Yeh as market representatives for a term of one year from 1 April 2005 to 31 March 2006, and the appointment of Ms Christina Choi as an SFC representative on the SFC (HKEC Listing) Committee.

The Committee was established pursuant to section 8 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance, in connection with the listing of Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited (HKEx) in June 2000. It exercises powers and functions equivalent to those of the Main Board and GEM Listing Committees of The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited when real or potential conflicts of interests between the interests of HKEx and those of the proper performance of the Stock Exchange’s listing functions make it desirable for the relevant Stock Exchange functions to be exercised by the SFC.

The Committee consists of five market representatives and five SFC representatives. A full list of the members of the Committee is provided in Note 1.

The SFC very much appreciates the efforts of the members of the Committee in performing the functions of the body effectively and transparently. The SFC is confident that with their expertise and significant experience in the financial and investment sector, the members will continue to make valuable contributions to the Committee.
