The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X1} for acting as a dealer’s representative of Bright Smart Securities International (HK) Limited whilst unlicensed and {X2} for aiding and abetting {X1}.

An SFC investigation revealed that between 4 May 2004 and 6 August 2004, {X1}, who was not licensed by the SFC, had received orders from clients, input them into Bright Smart’s computerised trading system and confirmed execution of trades with clients, in contravention of section 114(3) of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.  At the material time, {X2}, a responsible officer of Bright Smart and supervisor of {X1}, aided and abetted {X1} to perform those functions.

{X1} and {X2} today pleaded guilty to one summons each before Ms Bina Chainrai at Eastern Magistracy. {X1} and {X2} were fined $3,000 each and ordered to pay investigation costs totalling $19,342 to the SFC.
