The SFC has successfully prosecuted Ms Kong Kwok Yee for misleading the SFC.

Kong was identified as a suspected trader in a market manipulation investigation. The SFC inquiry found that Kong had provided misleading information to the SFC in relation to the operation of her account at a brokerage house in Hong Kong, in contravention of section 184 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

Kong today pleaded guilty to a summons before Magistrate Mr Ian Candy at Eastern Magistracy. Kong was fined $20,000 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $11,849 to the SFC.
