The SFC today publishes the February issue of the *SFC Enforcement Reporter*, summarising the SFC’s enforcement action in the month of January 2005 and discussing the compliance issues highlighted by recent cases. 

In January, the SFC successfully prosecuted an unlicensed person for issuing unauthorised investment-related advertisements and giving investment advice.

The SFC disciplined six licensees during the month for impersonating a client to open an account, facilitating unlicensed dealing, improper trading, breaching staff trading policy,  violation of investment restrictions and failing to detect such violations.

We also settled disciplinary action against one licensed corporation which agreed to pay $30 million. The SFC alleged that the corporation had not exercised due skill, care and diligence while performing its duties as a sponsor for the listing of a company. The SFC considers that the settlement to be in the public interest. The settlement amount will be paid to the government revenue.

Since 1 April 2004, the SFC has successfully prosecuted 64 entities. We have withdrawn summonses issued against eight entities and a further five entities were acquitted after trial. We offered no evidence in four cases. The SFC has disciplined 66 licensees for various regulatory breaches and entered into settlements with voluntary payments with four licensees. Disciplinary proceedings have been taken against 26 licensees, which were concluded with no formal sanction imposed, although 13 of them received private warnings*.* Such proceedings were also commenced and discontinued against four deemed licensees who left their firms before conclusion of the action.
