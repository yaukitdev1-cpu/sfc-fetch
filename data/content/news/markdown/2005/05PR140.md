The SFC today publishes the [June issue of *SFC Alert*](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/newsletter/05/may_jun05_eng.pdf), a bi-monthly newsletter for both investors and intermediaries.

The SFC last month published its Annual Report for the year ended 31 March 2005. *Annual Report 2004-2005: Investors First* provides an overview of our financial results and explains our pledge to put investor interests as our priority. *Revised REITs Code effective* reports that SFC-authorised REITs may invest in overseas properties. Also on the front page is an article setting out SFC’s efforts in enhancing regulatory co-operation with four regulators in the past two months.

In the regular *Senior Management Interview*, Mr Stephen Birkett, Senior Director, Corporate Finance, tells us why he quit private law practice and joined the SFC last January. He also briefly outlined the progress of several projects including the review of the prospectus regime.

*Review of Hedge Funds Guidelines* reports on our consultation to strengthen the regulation of SFC-authorised hedge funds. *Fine-tuning of disclosure regime* reports that the market supports our recommendations to rationalise the disclosure of interests regime.

Another column, *Investor focus*, reminds investors that they should cast their votes at shareholders’ meetings to make their voice heard by the management of the companies.

The SFC has created a section on the SFC website on the development of XBRL in Hong Kong. There is an article in this issue to introduce this evolving global standard for handling business and financial data.

Copies of the current issue of *SFC Alert* are available at the SFC office. [All issues](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/speeches/public/newsletter/newsletter.html) are available on the SFC website.
