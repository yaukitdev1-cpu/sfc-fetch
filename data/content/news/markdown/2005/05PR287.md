The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X} for making unsolicited calls.

An SFC investigation found that in about August 2003, {X}, who was licensed as a representative of Tanrich Futures Limited at the material time, had made telephone calls to and visited a person who was not a client of Tanrich, with a view to inducing that person to enter into agreements to trade in futures contracts, in contravention of section 174 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

{X} today pleaded guilty to one summons before Mr Li Wai Chi, a Magistrate at Eastern Magistracy. {X} was fined $2,000 and was ordered to pay investigation costs of $14,533 to the SFC.
