The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X1} for acting as a dealer’s representative of Sun Hung Kai Investment Services Limited whilst unlicensed and {X2} for aiding and abetting {X1}.

An SFC investigation revealed that between 24 April 2003 and 2 October 2003, {X1}, who was not licensed by the SFC, had introduced clients to open trading accounts at Sun Hung Kai, received orders from clients and confirmed execution of trades with clients, in contravention of section 114(3) of the Securities and Futures Ordinance. At the material time, {X2}, the branch manageress of Sun Hung Kai and supervisor of {X1}, aided and abetted {X1} to perform those functions.

{X1} and {X2} were convicted today after trial before Mr Li Wai Chi, a Magistrate at Eastern Magistracy. {X1} and {X2} were fined $1,500 each and ordered to pay investigation costs totalling $26,634 to the SFC.
