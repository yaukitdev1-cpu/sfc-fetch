The SFC invites secondary school and vocational education teachers to enrol in a new round of investor education workshops.

Teachers workshops are part of the SFC’s initiatives to assist secondary school teachers of Economics, Accounting, Commerce and related subjects to better understand financial investment activities and investor protection measures in Hong Kong.  The ultimate aim of these workshops is to help students improve their financial literacy, develop positive values and attitudes, and learn the basic skills of personal financial management.

A total of 16 workshops covering a wide range of investment products and services will be held from 13 June to 14 July.  There will be a new Personal Financial Planning module, which will focus on the financial planning process, factors to consider in constructing a personal investment portfolio and consumerism in engaging investment advisory services.

Some of the workshops also include visits to the Viewing Gallery of the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong, a stock brokerage firm and a fund management company.

Since 2000, the SFC has organised 80 workshops attracting over 4,400 enrolments.  A “Teachers Corner” has been set up on the Electronic Investor Resources Centre (eIRC) located at [www.eirc.hk](http://www.eirc.hk/) to provide numerous references to teachers to facilitate their discussions with students in class.

The new workshops are now open for enrolment. Invitation letters have been sent to all secondary schools and vocational education institutes. For the first time, interested teachers can enrol online at the “Teachers Corner” on the eIRC. Alternatively, teachers can fax their details to the Investor Education and Communications Department at 2524 3718 to enrol. 

The Education and Manpower Bureau (EMB) is also supportive of SFC’s teachers workshops and has included their details on its website ([www.emb.gov.hk](http://www.emb.gov.hk/)) under the “EMB Training Calendar” subsection (course code: CDI020050402).
