The SFC welcomes the findings contained in the [Process Review Panel's latest Annual Report](http://www.fstb.gov.hk/fsb/topical/preport04.htm), which is published by the Government today.

The Panel reviewed 47 completed cases and SFC procedures covering various areas of SFC work. The SFC is pleased to note the Panel’s conclusion that, in general, the SFC had been adhering to its established internal procedures in its decisions and actions.

The SFC welcomes the valuable suggestions made by the Panel for improving its procedures. The SFC also thanks the industry practitioners for their comments.

The SFC has adopted a number of specific recommendations of the Panel. In the few instances where there are difficulties in adopting or accepting in full the recommendations of the Panel, the SFC has carefully explained to the Panel the reasons why it has not adopted the recommendations.

The SFC looks forward to continuing its co-operation with the Panel this year.
