The SFC has introduced new educational games and feature articles on the Electronic Investor Resources Centre (eIRC), located at [www.eirc.hk](http://www.eirc.hk), in the Phase II enhancement of the investor portal. An online survey has also been launched to gauge the public’s views on the eIRC (Note 1).

New interactive games on warrants, IPOs and hedge funds

Investors are invited to try out the following new games in the "[Game Zone](http://www.eirc.hk/eirc/html/EN/game/index.htm)" to learn more about different kinds of investment activities :


- [*Warrant Morse*](http://www.eirc.hk/eirc/html/EN/game/warrant.html) *–* Find out if you can crack the code of the complex world of warrants investment and understand the basic concepts, trading know-how and analytic information of warrants.
 

- [*IPO Explorer*](http://www.eirc.hk/eirc/html/EN/game/ipo.html)* –* Find out how to make informed investment decisions through this simulated investment adventure comprising scenarios of an IPO and a takeover bid.
- [*Hedge Fund Watch*](http://www.eirc.hk/eirc/html/EN/game/hedge.html)* –* See if you can find and understand performance data and other information in a hedge fund’s quarterly report. 
- [*Test Your FQ (Financial Quotient)*](http://www.eirc.hk/eirc/html/EN/game/quiz.html)* /* [*Mix and Match*](http://www.eirc.hk/eirc/html/EN/game/mix.html)* -* New questions on derivatives, structured products, bonds and specialised funds have been added to these two games.
- [*Get the Whole Pic*](http://www.eirc.hk/eirc/html/EN/game/puzzle.html)* -* There are new puzzles on different kinds of charges in a stock transaction and parallel trading procedures in a listed company’s capital re-organisation.
New articles on ILAS

Investment-linked assurance schemes (ILAS) are generally long-term hybrid investment products combining life insurance with fund investment. The SFC has added new articles to the existing feature article series on ILAS. Certain existing articles have also been updated.

The expanded series discusses ILAS’ key characteristics, charge structures and some of the jargons used in their documentation. Investors are reminded of the possible application of market-value adjustments to with-profits funds available under ILAS, which will reduce the value of their investments. Unlike traditional insurance policies, investing in ILAS involves capital risk. Using a geared strategy of taking out a loan to increase investment in ILAS could also be highly risky. The series further provides a list of questions that investors should ask before investing in ILAS.

The [feature article series on ILAS](http://www.eirc.hk/eirc/html/EN/features/funds/features_82_37_261.htm) is located under "Features & Dr Wise" - "Funds" section.

Mr Ian Johnston, Adviser to the Commission, said: "The latest initiatives reinforce our commitment to providing up-to-date, relevant information to investors, in a way that is easy to use. We urge investors to make use of the user-friendly resources at the eIRC."

User Survey

The SFC has also launched an [online User Survey](http://www.sfc.hk/sfcConsultation/EN/sfcConsultMainServlet?name=eircsurvey) to solicit the public’s views on the functions and navigation of the eIRC and the usefulness of its contents. The public can access the User Survey via an icon on the eIRC homepage. Feedback from the public will help the SFC further improve the eIRC.

Enhancement in the near future

In view of the increasing complexity of retail investment products and other market developments, the SFC constantly enhances its educational resources on the eIRC. To complement the text information, the SFC is now making short flash videos to provide an alternative to investors to learn the steps that they should take to protect their interests. The new flash videos are targeted for launch in early 2006.
