The SFC has introduced new functions and contents on its corporate website (www.sfc.hk) in Phase II of its website revamp project (Note 1).

With the following new functions and contents, the SFC website has become more accessible and informative to the public and market practitioners:


- a text-only version of the website, which includes information of the *"About the SFC"* section in the graphical version. Visually impaired visitors will be able to navigate and browse the text-only version for key information about the SFC (Note 2);
- an *"Upcoming Events and Calendar"* section that lists out all upcoming SFC public activities, publications and functions that will be attended by SFC executives. The section will be of particular interest to the media and market practitioners;
 

- a *"Contingency Information"* section that details the current market contingency arrangements and provides contact information of the relevant market bodies;
 

- an expanded *"Prospectuses, Takeovers & Mergers – Prospectuses & Exemptions under Companies Ordinance"* section where full prospectuses of unlisted shares/debentures authorised, exemptions granted and marketing materials authorised by the SFC are available. Previously, only extracts relating to exemptions were posted;
- an *"XBRL"* section on the development in Hong Kong of eXtensible Business Reporting Language, an evolving global standard for the electronic communication of business and financial data; and
- a *"Complaints Against Staff"* sub-section under *"About the SFC – Complaints Against the SFC"*, which explains how the public can lodge a complaint against SFC staff and the SFC’s handling procedures. It enhances our transparency and accountability.

In addition, certain existing information has been re-organised and presented in a more user-friendly manner. For example, *"Consultation Papers & Conclusions"*, now a sub-section created under *"Speeches, Publications & Conclusions"*, provides a one-stop-shop of our policy proposals and the market’s responses. Within this sub-section, consultation papers issued since 2003 and their corresponding conclusions papers are posted side by side, hence facilitating research and cross-referencing.

An SFC spokesman said: "These improvements have been introduced continuously since the Phase I revamp. This demonstrates our commitment to further strengthen our on-line services to the public and market practitioners."

The SFC welcomes comments from the public, which will help us to continue to improve the website. Comments can be sent to [enquiry@sfc.hk](mailto:enquiry@sfc.hk).

Notes to Editor:

1. Phase I of the SFC website revamp, involving major structural changes and enhancements of functions and contents, was launched in November 2004.

2. Visually impaired visitors usually browse the Internet with the aid of screen access programmes called screen readers. One way to facilitate their access to websites is to provide an additional text-only version. It is considered that a text-only version can cater for the cognitively-impaired who have difficulties in comprehending complicated patterns, the visually impaired with less advanced screen readers, or users with less powerful computer equipment.
