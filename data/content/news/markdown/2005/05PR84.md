The SFC wishes to remind licensed persons and registered institutions that the guidelines to address analyst conflicts of interest will come into effect on 1 April 2005.

Following a public consultation, the guidelines were published with the consultation conclusions five months earlier on 3 November 2004 to give the industry sufficient time to establish the necessary compliance systems.  The guidelines aim to remove, reduce and manage conflicts faced by analysts and their firms, and have taken into consideration the International Organization of Securities Commissions’ (IOSCO) principles, developments in other jurisdictions and local characteristics.

In tandem with the publication of the consultation conclusions and the guidelines, a set of “Frequently Asked Questions and Answers” was also issued.  All these documents are available on the SFC website.

The SFC has planned an investor education campaign to increase the public’s awareness of the potential conflicts that an analyst and his firm may face and how these guidelines may serve to help.  The campaign will commence in April 2005.
