The SFC today publishes the [September issue of the SFC Enforcement Reporter](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/enforcement/05/sep05_eng.pdf), summarising the SFC’s enforcement action in the month of August 2005 and discussing the compliance issues highlighted by recent cases.

In August, the SFC successfully prosecuted three persons and one company for disclosure of interests breaches and unlicensed investment advising.

We disciplined five licensees for breaches of SFC Codes and company compliance manual, internal control failings and short selling.

We also settled disciplinary actions against four licensees who agreed to make voluntary payment, with three of them receiving statutory sanctions. They had internal control failings or did not handle clients’ accounts properly.

From 1 April 2005 to end of August, the SFC successfully prosecuted 26 entities and offered no evidence against three entities. In the same period, the SFC took action against 30 licensees for various regulatory breaches, of which three cases were settled with voluntary payment and with no statutory sanction imposed. Another three licensees settled with voluntary payment and received statutory sanctions.

Copies of the September issue of the SFC Enforcement Reporter are available at the SFC office. All issues are also available on the SFC website under ["Speeches, Publications & Consultations" – "Publications"](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/speeches/public/enforcement/enforcement.html).

The public are welcome to subscribe to the newsletter by registering for the [Update Alert](https://www.sfc.hk/sfcUpdateAlert/jsp/EN/update_alert.jsp) service on the SFC website. They will then be able to receive the newsletter via email.

The public are also invited to provide feedback so we can improve the SFC Enforcement Reporter. Comments can be emailed to [enfreporter@sfc.hk](mailto:enfreporter@sfc.hk).
