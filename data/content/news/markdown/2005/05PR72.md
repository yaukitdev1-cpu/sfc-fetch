Mr Peter Au-Yang, SFC Executive Director and Chief Operating Officer, who chairs the IEAC, said: “I am confident the new members will bring valuable insights to the Committee. I also thank the re-appointed members for continuing to share with us their expert views and experience.”
 
Mr Au-Yang also paid tribute to outgoing members for their valuable contribution. They are The Honourable Fred Li Wah Ming, Professor Chan Ka Lok, Mr Robert Chow, Mr Allen Cham, Mr Victor Ng and Ms Sandra Lee. 

The IEAC was established under section 8 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance to advise the SFC on its investor education work. It plays a crucial role in identifying areas that the SFC should study in order to further the interests of investors, and aspects of the securities and futures industry where investor education is desirable.

During the past year, the IEAC discussed empowering investors against mis-selling of financial products by investment advisers, enhancing investor education on the use of stock tips provided by analysts in the print and electronic media, as well as how the SFC can effectively make use of television and radio commercials to disseminate education messages. 

In the year ahead, the SFC will continue to rely on the IEAC as the think-tank in formulating its investor education initiatives.  The SFC will consult the IEAC on enhancing the public’s understanding of the SFC’s role and functions, on a new retail investors’ investment behaviour survey and on the formation of an investor educators group to facilitate the SFC’s investor education work.
