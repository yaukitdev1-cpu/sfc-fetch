The Joint Forum has released a consultation paper entitled “High-level principles for business continuity” (Note 1). The objective of the paper is to support international standard setting organisations and national authorities in developing effective business continuity management and improving the resilience of their financial systems to major operational disruptions. The seven high-level principles in the paper are directed at both financial industry participants and financial authorities globally (Note 2).

Mr Ian Johnston, Chairman of the Joint Forum and Adviser to Chairman’s Office of the SFC, said the paper took business continuity a step further by establishing a common global framework for approaching key business continuity issues and highlighting new concepts. He said organisations would find the paper particularly useful as the case studies of recent instances of major operational disruption were set out and the lessons learned were explicitly linked to the relevant principle. He encouraged financial industry participants and financial authorities alike to provide comments during the consultation period, which ends on 10 March 2006.

Mr Johnston will step down as Chairman of the Joint Forum at the end of the year. The Joint Forum expressed its gratitude to Mr Johnston for his leadership and keen enthusiasm for the work of the Joint Forum (Note 3).
