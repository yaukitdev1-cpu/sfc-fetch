The SFC today publishes the August issue of Dr Wise’s Column, [*Never Lend Your Account*](http://www.eirc.hk/eirc/html/EN/features/doctor/features_87_226_321.htm)*.*

In this issue, Dr Wise reminds investors not to let other people use their securities trading account.  Regardless of who initiates the trades, the account owner is responsible for all trading activities in the account.  The brokerage will go after the account owner and may even take legal action against the account owner for any unsettled amounts.

Apart from financial losses, the account owner may be in trouble if illegal activities such as market manipulation and insider dealing are conducted by the other people in the account.   The account owner may be regarded as having assisted in the crime by lending the account and be liable for criminal charges.  Besides, the account owner may also unknowingly become involved in money laundering or stealing if monies or shares are illegally deposited by the user into the account.
