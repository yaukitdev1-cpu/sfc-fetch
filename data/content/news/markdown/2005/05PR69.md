SFC authorised funds which are domiciled in the EU member states, including Luxembourg, Ireland and the UK, will generally be affected by the new UCITS III regulations issued by the EU Commission. Existing EU funds set up under the UCITS I framework are required by the laws of their home countries to convert into UCITS III funds by February 2007 or earlier. 

Being a premier financial centre of the region, funds authorised in Hong Kong continue to exhibit strong international flavours. In order to provide investors with a basic understanding of the new UCITS III regulations and to increase investors’ awareness of the implications for their existing or potential investments, the SFC is pleased to announce the launch of a series of frequently asked questions (FAQs). The FAQs aim to address some basic queries regarding UCITS III and to provide investors with some general information about what they need to do and where they can obtain more information concerning the UCITS III conversion. These [FAQs on UCITS III](http://www.eirc.hk/eirc/html/EN/features/funds/features_82_36_297.htm) can be found on the SFC-operated investor portal, the Electronic Investor Resources Centre (eIRC) at www.eirc.hk.

The SFC encourages the fund industry to dedicate their efforts to educating investors and explaining the implications of the new regulations in the context of their clients’ investments. They are most welcome to make use of the SFC’s FAQs as part of their investor education programme, where applicable.
