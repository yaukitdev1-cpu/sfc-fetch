The SFC would not compromise its regulatory standards to attract business to Hong Kong, SFC’s Executive Director of Intermediaries and Investment Products, Mrs Alexa Lam, said at a hedge funds conference today.

Mrs Lam said the SFC’s licensing system aimed to ensure that all entrants to the Hong Kong market meet high standards.

“I would like to make clear that any fund manager who uses the Hong Kong platform, whether he manages for institutional or retail investors, must get a licence,” she said at the Hong Kong: Hedge Fund Hub of Asia – Conference 2005.

“The reason is simple: even if the clients are not Hong Kong investors, the Hong Kong market and its reputation can be put at risk. Therefore, it is very important that the person that comes demonstrates that he has passed certain thresholds in competence, corporate governance, internal control and risk management, etc,” Mrs Lam said.

“We are not going for numbers and we will not lower the standards for the sake of numbers. That is not the Hong Kong philosophy,” she said.

Mrs Lam also reminded intermediaries of their responsibility to give clients proper advice and help them make informed investment decisions.

“You have to understand your clients, know who they are, their risk tolerance, their short, medium and long term objectives, how much money they could set aside for investing, their employment status and family situation, etc. The fund, or product that you recommend must match the needs and financial situation of your client,” she said.

Concluding her remarks, Mrs Lam said: “Finally, I would like to say that we do welcome hedge funds to come to HK but they have to satisfy our standards. On this, there is no compromise.”
