The SFC today publishes the January issue of Dr Wise’s Column, [*Behind the Scenes of an IPO*](http://www.eirc.hk/eirc/html/EN/features/doctor/features_87_226_314.htm).

In this issue, Dr Wise discusses the roles of sponsors in an Initial Public Offering (IPO) as well as how their work relates to investors.

Every company applying to be listed on the Stock Exchange must have a sponsor to act as the company’s corporate finance adviser. A sponsor’s function covers a wide range of activities, such as advising the company on listing requirements, carrying out due diligence work on the company’s business, and assessing the veracity of the information disclosed in the prospectus. A sponsor is closely involved in preparing the prospectus which provides investors with vital information about the company and helps them make informed investment decisions. The sponsor also manages the IPO process and is responsible for ensuring that it proceeds orderly.
