The SFC has successfully prosecuted Mr Chu Yat Lam under Part XV of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.  Chu was a substantial shareholder of China Northern Enterprises Investment Fund Limited at the material time.   

Chu today pleaded guilty to 13 summonses related to his failure to notify the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong regarding 13 acquisitions and disposals of China Northern shares during the period between 16 April 2004 and 1 March 2005, which caused him to have or cease to have a notifiable interest in China Northern.

Mr Leung Wing Chung Winston, a Magistrate at Eastern Magistracy, fined Chu $13,000, and ordered him to pay investigation costs of $2,265 to the SFC.
