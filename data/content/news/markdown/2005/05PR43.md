The SFC welcomes the re-appointment by the Financial Secretary of Mrs Alexa Lam as Executive Director (Intermediaries and Investment Products) for three years from 1 March 2005 to 29 February 2008.

Mr Andrew Sheng, SFC Chairman, said: "With her dedication and professionalism, Mrs Lam has been working very closely with the industry over the years to implement many of the reforms that enhance intermediaries’ standards and facilitate market innovation. These in turn help raise market quality and strengthen investor confidence. The SFC will continue to work in partnership with the industry to further strengthen Hong Kong’s status as an international financial centre."

Mrs Lam joined the SFC in October 1998. She was appointed Chief Counsel in 1999, and Chief Counsel and Executive Director in March 2001. In December 2001, she was re-designated to head the Intermediaries and Investment Products Division.
