The SFC today publishes the [Winter 2004 issue (No.58) of the *SFC Quarterly Bulletin*](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/speeches/public/bulletin/sfc_bulletin/winter_04.html).

The first feature article *Convergence of A-share and H-share Prices* discusses the phenomenon that while A-shares trade at premiums over H-shares, the premiums have been narrowing.  However, the narrowing process slowed down in 2004.  This appeared to be the result of macro-economic control in the Mainland and the increasing supply of H-shares.

The second feature article *International Organization of Securities Commissions’ (IOSCO) Code of Conduct Fundamentals for Credit Rating Agencies (CRAs)* introduces the CRA Code.  The main objective of the Code is to safeguard the interests of investors who rely on credit rating opinions in assessing the credit risks they are exposed to when making certain investments.

*Interest Rate and Capital Movements* discusses the further widening of the interest rate gap between Hong Kong and the US.  This was largely attributable to an inflow of capital, which may be temporary.  When the bullish sentiment over the RMB settles, capital may be drawn out from the HKD and there may be upward pressure on local interest rates.

This issue also includes the *Half-Yearly Financial Stability Report – the Securities Market* and the *Half-Yearly Financial Stability Report – the Securities Industry.*  The former reviews the performance of the local and regional stock markets during the second half of 2004, whilst the latter analyses the financial performance of broker firms over the same period.  The Reports found  that:

- the Hong Kong securities market had maintained its momentum in the second half of 2004; and
- the profits of SEHK participants had soared 77% in 2004, compared to 2003, primarily owing to a 60% increase in total value of transactions.
