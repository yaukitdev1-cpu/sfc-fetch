The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X} for holding out as carrying on a business in a regulated activity whilst unlicensed.

An SFC investigation revealed that between February and July 2004, MCC Capital Consulting Limited, which was not licensed by the SFC, held itself out on its website as carrying on a business of advising on corporate finance, in contravention of section 114 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance. MCC pleaded guilty to one summons earlier on 10 March 2005 (Note 1). {X}, being a director of MCC, consented to MCC’s offence and was guilty of the same offence.

{X} was convicted after trial today on one summons before Mr Josiah Lam at Eastern Magistracy. {X} was fined $6,000 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $13,131 to the SFC.
