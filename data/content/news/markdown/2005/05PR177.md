The SFC today publishes the[ August issue of *SFC Alert*](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/newsletter/05/jul_aug2005_eng.pdf), a bi-monthly newsletter for both investors and intermediaries.

The lead story reports some of the activities the SFC has undertaken in its busy first quarter in the financial year to 31 March 2006. *Robust fund industry development* tells readers that our latest Fund Management Activities Survey has re-confirmed Hong Kong’s status as the regional fund management hub. *LOI with SC, Malaysia* outlines the SFC’s further regulatory co-operation with Malaysia.

In the regular *Senior Management Interview*, Mr Ian Johnston, Adviser, Chairman’s Office, shares with us why he decided to leave Australia for Hong Kong, and his role at the SFC.

Inside, *Better sponsor regulation* reminds the market to comment on our proposed specific entry criteria and ongoing compliance obligations on sponsors. Meanwhile, the SFC has added new functions and contents to the website. *SFC website enhancement* illustrates the changes. Listed company shareholders should read *Investor focus* to understand the relationship between a share consolidation and an investor’s interest in a listed company.

This issue carries a double-page special on FAQs regarding the licensing of fund managers. The article covers extensively issues including the arrangements for licensing exams and the business information required in the first application submission.

Copies of the current issue of *SFC Alert* are available at the SFC office. [All issues](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/speeches/public/newsletter/newsletter.html) are available on the SFC website.
