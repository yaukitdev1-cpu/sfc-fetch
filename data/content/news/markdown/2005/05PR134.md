The SFC is pleased to announce that it has signed a [Letter of Intent](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/aboutsfc/cooperation/guernsey_loi_050603.pdf) with Guernsey’s Financial Services Commission (GFSC) on the enhancement of regulatory co-operation between the GFSC and the SFC (Note 1).

The two regulators aim to work towards the mutual recognition of investment funds authorised or traded in their respective jurisdictions for cross-border distribution.

This Letter of Intent, the fifth to be signed by the SFC, marks a progression in the SFC’s established initiative to dismantle the barriers to the cross-border trading of investment products by extending its intention to forge a more cohesive Asian market for investment products, including mutual funds, to promote the cross-border distribution of Hong Kong investment products in Europe and *vice versa* (Note 2).

Under this Letter of Intent, both authorities agree to strengthen regulatory co-operation, particularly in matters relating to the cross-border trading and the supervision of investment products. It is hoped that with enhanced communication and regulatory equivalence of their respective regulatory regimes, both Hong Kong and Guernsey will be able to facilitate the development of deeper and broader investment markets through the cross-border trading of investment products that are acceptable to both authorities.

Both the GFSC and the SFC are members of the International Organization of Securities Commissions (IOSCO) and this Letter of Intent is signed in the spirit of mutual assistance and co-operation among member securities regulators fostered by IOSCO.

The Letter of Intent was signed in Hong Kong today by Mr Peter Neville, Director General of the GFSC, and Mrs Alexa Lam, Executive Director of the Intermediaries and Investment Products Division, and currently acting Chairman, of the SFC.

Both authorities have also established a bilateral working group which has begun working towards the achievement of the objectives set out in the Letter of Intent.

Mrs Lam said: "The SFC is committed to facilitating the development of deeper and broader investment markets globally. We are delighted that the Financial Services Commission of Guernsey is our first non-Asian partner in this endeavour. Given Guernsey’s strategic location and important role in the European investment products market, it is ideally placed to explore with the SFC the means of achieving the cross-border trading of investment products between our respective markets to our mutual benefit."

Mr Neville said: "I am delighted to have the opportunity to sign this Letter of Intent with the Hong Kong SFC. The Guernsey Commission and the SFC have developed a very close working relationship over the years. For example, Guernsey funds have been authorised by the SFC for marketing to the public in Hong Kong. The Letter of Intent is an important further step in deepening that relationship. We look forward to exchanging information and working together, in order not only to protect the international financial system but also to provide greater commercial opportunities for financial services firms in both jurisdictions."

Notes to Editor:

1. The Financial Services Commission of Guernsey (GFSC) was established under the Financial Services Commission (Bailiwick of Guernsey) Law, 1987. The GFSC is the regulatory body for financial services businesses in the Bailiwick of Guernsey. The Financial Services Commission Law provides the Commission with general and statutory functions. The general functions of the GFSC include responsibility for the supervision of the finance sector and the countering of financial crime and terrorist financing. The statutory functions include the administration of the laws which provide the Commission with the ability to regulate financial services businesses, including the Protection of Investors (Bailiwick of Guernsey) Law, 1987. In all, 721 investment funds, with a value of ￡75,570 million, are managed or administered in Guernsey.

2.
