The SFC is pleased to announce the Public Shareholders Group membership for 2005-2006. 

The SFC has appointed 13 members to the Group for a one-year term, effective 1 July 2005. 

Five new members have been appointed. They are Mr Vincent Chan, Prof. Stephen Cheung, Mr William Kerr, Mr Stuart Leckie, JP and Mr Karl Lung.  Five existing members retired from the Group.  They are Mr Man Wing Chung, Mr David Lui, Mr Richard Mak, Mr Douglas Naismith and Mr David Sun.

The Public Shareholders Group is a standing committee established under section 8 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance. The Group’s members include representatives of retail and institutional investors, professionals, academics, prominent advocates of investors’ rights, and the current Chairman of the Consumer Council.  Last year, the Group held eight meetings to discuss a wide range of topics.  Views gathered from the Group have assisted the Corporate Finance and other divisions of the SFC in considering issues relating to shareholders’ interests and formulation of regulatory proposals.

Mr Peter Au-Yang, SFC’s Executive Director of Corporate Finance and Chairman of the Group, said: “The Public Shareholders Group has played an important role in providing insight and feedback to the Commission over the last year. The Commission very much appreciates members’ generous support and valuable advice.  We wish to thank especially  Mr Man Wing Chung, Mr David Lui, Mr Richard Mak, Mr Douglas Naismith and Mr David Sun who are retiring from the Group.  We also welcome all the new and reappointed members and look forward to working closely with them”
