The SFC is pleased to announce the appointment of new members to the Takeovers and Mergers Panel and the Takeovers Appeal Committee for the period from 1 October 2005 to 31 March 2006. 

The SFC welcomes Ms Julia Charlton, Mr Stephen Cheung, Mr Herbert Hui, Mr Bill Kwok, Mr Michael Palin, Mr Gregory Terry, Mr Kenneth Willman, Ms Christine Wong and Mr Harold Wong as new members of both the Panel and the Appeal Committee.

The Panel and Appeal Committee are voluntary bodies which owe their success to the dedication and expertise of their members. The SFC is confident that the significant experience of members in takeovers and mergers, and in the securities and financial markets will ensure the continued success of the Panel and the Appeal Committee.
