The SFC warmly welcomes the re-appointment by the Financial Secretary of the Hon Jasper Tsang and Mr Christopher Cheng as Non-Executive Directors.

SFC Chairman, Mr Martin Wheatley, said: “We are pleased to be able to continue working with Mr Tsang and Mr Cheng, whose advice has benefited the SFC a great deal. I am confident that their immense expertise and experience in public services will continue to contribute greatly to the SFC and the Hong Kong market.”

Mr Tsang and Mr Cheng have been SFC NEDs since 15 November 2001 and 15 November 2003 respectively. They both sit on the Commission’s Remuneration Committee and SFC (HKEC Listing) Appeals Committee; Mr Cheng also sits on the Budget Committee.
