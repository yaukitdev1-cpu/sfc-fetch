The SFC is pleased to announce the appointment of members to the Committee on Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs) for the year 1 September 2005 to 31 August 2006.

The SFC welcomes Mrs Susan Chow, Mr Stephen Hui and Mr Kevin Westley as new members of the Committee.

The SFC also welcomes the re-appointment of Mr F K Au, Mr Henry Cheong, Mr Martin Cubbon, Mr Heung Shu Fai, Mr C Y Leung, Mr Alastair Murray, Mr David Richardson and Mr Brian Stevenson.

The SFC would like to thank Mr Roger Hepper, who has made valuable contribution to the Committee during the past two years.
