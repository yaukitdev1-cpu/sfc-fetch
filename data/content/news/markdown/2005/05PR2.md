The SFC issues the following update on the operation of the Dual Filing arrangements under the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

For the three months from October to December 2004, the SFC received, via the Stock Exchange, 18 listing applications. This is the lowest number of applications in any quarter since the Dual Filing regime came into effect on 1 April 2003. It follows the highest number of applications (42) in any quarter during the three months from July to September 2004.

Of the 18 applications, we commented on 8 cases. Our average response time was eight working days.

Disclosure on Relationships with Parent Companies and Fellow Subsidiaries

It is common in the Hong Kong market for many listing applicants to be established as a result of restructuring exercises of their parent groups. It is also common for these applicants to rely heavily on substantial continuing connected transactions with their parent companies and/or fellow subsidiaries.

In one case reviewed during the quarter covered by this update, the listing applicant sources nearly all its key raw materials from its parent group and sells all its products to its parent group. Moreover, it had agreements with its parent to purchase certain upstream and downstream operations of some fellow subsidiaries, but cancelled those agreements shortly before applying for listing.

The SFC understands the listing of part of a restructured corporate group is a feature of the Hong Kong market. But this presents special disclosure concerns, such as in relation to:


- The pricing power of the listing applicant and pricing policy for the continuing connected transactions;
- Potential competition with and allocation of business opportunities within the parent group;
- Plans for reintegration, in particular the possibility of the parent injecting other assets into the listing applicant.

In some instances the only way to present a meaningful picture to potential investors is to include quantitative information about the parent group, and sponsors would have to understand the operations of the whole business group in order to advise their clients on how to disclose all material information.

Mr Peter Au-Yang, SFC’s Executive Director of Corporate Finance, said: "The SFC understands that the Hong Kong market has special features. This increases the need for a listing applicant to disclose a full story. The sponsor must also perform its due diligence to understand the context in which its client operates and present a fair and accurate picture of the whole story in its listing documents."

The SFC issues this quarterly update as part of its efforts to ensure transparency of the Dual Filing regime.
