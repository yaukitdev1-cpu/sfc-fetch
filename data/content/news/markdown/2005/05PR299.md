The SFC today publishes the [December issue of the SFC Enforcement Reporter](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/enforcement/05/dec05_eng.pdf), summarising the SFC's enforcement action in the month of November 2005 and discussing the compliance issues highlighted by recent cases.

In November, the SFC successfully prosecuted four companies and eight people for reckless and fraudulent misrepresentations, unlicensed activities, unsolicited calls and disclosure of interests failures.

We disciplined seven licensees for conflicts of interest, providing misleading information to the SFC, deceiving employer, breaching the Financial Resources Rules, failure to monitor manipulative trades and conducting discretionary trades without authority.

In November, the Takeovers Executive also criticised the board of directors of a company for breaching Rule 21.3 of the Takeovers Code, which restricts share dealings and transactions by an offeror and parties acting in concert with it during securities exchange offers.

On 7 December 2005, three people, including former general manager of Shanghai Land Holdings Limited Ms Mo Yuk Ping, were convicted for manipulating the shares of Shanghai Land and perverting the course of justice by giving false information to the SFC. The conviction followed an ICAC investigation which was fully assisted by the SFC. The expert evidence given by an SFC officer on the manipulative trades was fully accepted by the Court.

From 1 April 2005 to the end of November, the SFC successfully prosecuted 51 entities and offered no evidence against three entities. In the same period, the SFC took action against 66 licensees for various regulatory breaches, of which three cases were settled with voluntary payments and with no statutory sanction imposed. Another four licensees settled with voluntary payment and received statutory sanctions.

Copies of the December issue of the SFC Enforcement Reporter are available at the SFC office. All issues are also available on the SFC website under [“Speeches, Publications & Consultations” – “Publications”](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/speeches/public/enforcement/enforcement.html).
