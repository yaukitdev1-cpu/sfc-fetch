The SFC and the Radio Television Hong Kong (RTHK) have jointly produced a radio drama series to increase the public’s awareness of the issue of analysts’ conflicts.  The series aims to educate investors what potential conflicts stock analysts and their firms may face, and to remind investors to look beyond an investment recommendation.
 
Entitled “The Star Analysts’ Fans Family”, the series comprises 10 five-minute episodes. Each episode features a short drama, followed by interviews with financial analysts or stock commentators, and explanations of how the SFC guidelines may serve to help remove, reduce and manage analyst conflicts of interest issues.
 
The drama surrounds a family of four. Star Daddy and Star Mommy are keen investors and worship celebrity stock analysts. Star Son and Star Daughter, like many children, run into all kinds of trouble at school. The interaction of the family with their neighbours, workmates, classmates and teachers set the scene for stories which highlight various issues investors are faced with regarding analysts' advice.
 
Broadcast will start on 22 April 2005 (Friday). The public can tune in to RTHK 1 at FM92.6-94.4 every Friday at 11:30am. The series is part of RTHK 1’s “Happy Daily” programme. After each episode, two audiences who call RTHK 1 at 187 2311 and are selected can compete in a quiz. The winner will get a $300 gift coupon.  The consolation prize is a $100 coupon. The programme will be repeated on the following Saturday at 10:15am in the “Investment Era” programme.
 
In tandem with the show, educational articles will be published in two local Chinese newspapers, the Apple Daily and the Hong Kong Economic Times.

The public can provide their views and feedback on the radio segments and the newspaper articles to the SFC by calling the Investor Hotline at 2840 9333 or sending an email to investor.info@sfc.hk.

Since 1998, the SFC has jointly produced investor education radio programmes with various radio stations. Some of these radio series are now available on the “Studio” section of the SFC-operated Electronic Investor Resources Centre (eIRC) at www.eirc.hk.
