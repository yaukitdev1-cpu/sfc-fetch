The SFC today records its thanks to Mr Mark Dickens, Executive Director of Supervision of Markets, for his contribution to the SFC and the Hong Kong securities market over the last 14 years.  Mr Dickens will leave the SFC upon expiry of his contract today.

SFC Chairman, Mr Andrew Sheng, expresses his gratitude on behalf of the Commission to Mr Dickens for his long and dedicated service to the Commission.

“Mr Dickens joined the Commission in its early years and witnessed its development to become a leading securities regulator. During his tenure, Mr Dickens has served in a wide range of activities of the Commission,” Mr Sheng said.

Mr Dickens joined the SFC in February 1991 as an Assistant Director of Corporate Finance. In July 1992, he was promoted to a position equivalent to the present rank of Senior Director. In January 1997, he was appointed Executive Director of Enforcement. He was appointed to his present position in April 1999.

Under his stewardship, the divisions put into effect a number of the reform activities of the Commission. Recent ones included:

- Formulation of Securities and Futures Ordinance provisions on exchanges, investor compensation fund and market misconduct;
- Review of the disclosure of interests regime under Part XV of the SFO;
- Review of the level and funding of the new Investor Compensation Fund; and
- Overseeing the SFC’s efforts in market contingency planning

Mr Sheng said: “Mr Dickens’ wide knowledge of securities law and experience contributed to the formulation of many of the policies and measures of the Commission. His work will be remembered.”

Before joining the SFC, Mr Dickens worked for the Australian National Companies and Securities Commission in various positions including General Counsel and Senior Director, Market Supervision.
