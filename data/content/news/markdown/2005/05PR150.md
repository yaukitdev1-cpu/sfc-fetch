The SFC has successfully prosecuted Mr Ha But Yee for short selling a warrant with stock name SG-CMOB@EC0404.

An SFC investigation found that between 17 October and 11 November 2003, Ha sold SG-CMOB@EC0404 through four accounts under his control while knowing that he and the account holders did not have any unit of warrant on hand, in contravention of Section 170 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance. Section 170(1) prohibits the sale of securities when the person does not have a presently exercisable and unconditional right to sell them.

Ha pleaded guilty today before Mr Anthony Yuen, a Magistrate at Eastern Magistracy, to sixteen summonses in relation to his short selling of securities. He was fined a total of $64,000 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $20,952 to the SFC.
