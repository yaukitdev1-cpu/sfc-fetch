The SFC has successfully prosecuted Mr Chan Kim Wing for failing to comply with notices issued by SFC’s investigators requiring attendance at interviews.

Two notices under Section 33(4) of the Securities and Futures Commission Ordinance and section 183(c) of Securities and Futures Ordinance were issued to Chan requiring him to attend interviews with investigators in connection with two investigations into market manipulation.  However, Chan without reasonable excuse did not show up.

Today, Chan pleaded guilty to two summonses before Magistrate Ms Amanda Jane Woodcock at Kwun Tong Magistracy.  Chan was fined $30,000 and ordered to pay $2,248 investigation costs.
