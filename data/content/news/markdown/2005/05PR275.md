The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X} for making unsolicited calls.

An SFC investigation found that between August and September 2003, {X}, who was a licensed representative of Tanrich Futures Limited at the time, had made telephone calls to and visited a person who was not a client of Tanrich with a view to inducing him to enter into an agreement for trading in futures contracts, in contravention of section 174 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

{X} was convicted after trial on one summons today before Mr Leung Wing Chung Winston, a Magistrate at Eastern Magistracy. {X} was fined $5,000 and ordered to pay the SFC investigation costs of $9,975 and legal costs incurred by the SFC.
