SFC Chairman, Mr Andrew Sheng, gave a speech today at the Opening Ceremony of the 30th Annual Conference of the International Organization of Securities Commissions. Mr Sheng is Chairman of the Technical Committee of IOSCO (Note 1). 

The 30th Annual Conference is being held in Colombo, Sri Lanka from 4 - 7 April. There are 500 participants from around the world. Hong Kong will host the 31st Annual Conference in 2006. The SFC has set up an exhibition booth in the Colombo conference venue to publicise the 2006 event and attract visitors to Hong Kong (Note 2).

[Mr Sheng's speech](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/speeches/05/as_050406_iosco.pdf) is attached.

Notes:

1. IOSCO is the preeminent forum for international co-operation among securities regulators and is recognised as the international standard setter for the securities sector. The Technical Committee is made up of 15 agencies that regulate some of the world's larger, more developed and internationalised markets. Its objective is to review major regulatory issues related to international securities and futures transactions and to coordinate practical responses to these concerns. [The report by the Technical Committee Chairman in the IOSCO 2004 Annual Report](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/general/general/press_release/05/05pr95_ar_2004_tc.pdf) is attached to the electronic version of this press release. The full Annual Report is available on the IOSCO website at [www.iosco.org](http://www.iosco.org/).
