The SFC today publishes the March issue of the [*SFC Enforcement Reporter*](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/enforcement/05/mar_05.pdf), summarising the SFC’s enforcement action in the month of February 2005 and discussing the compliance issues highlighted by recent cases. 

In February, the SFC successfully prosecuted two companies and three people for misleading the SFC, issuing share subscription forms, disclosure of interests breaches, and aiding and abetting market manipulation.

We disciplined two licensees for facilitating a colleague’s theft of client money, and conducting personal trades via client accounts.

During the month, we settled disciplinary action against two licensees. One of them surrendered her licence and undertook not to re-apply for a licence for five years due to  serious conflicts of interest. An investment manager who allowed late trading by two clients also settled with the SFC and agreed to make ex-gratia payments to the affected parties.  The SFC considers the settlements to be in the public interest.

Since 1 April 2004, the SFC has successfully prosecuted 68 entities. We have withdrawn summonses issued against eight entities and a further six entities were acquitted after trial. We offered no evidence in four cases. The SFC has disciplined 68 licensees for various regulatory breaches and entered into settlements with voluntary payments with four licensees. Disciplinary proceedings have been taken against 27 licensees, which were concluded with no formal sanction imposed, although 14 of them received private warnings*.* Such proceedings were also commenced and discontinued against four deemed licensees who left their firms before conclusion of the action.
