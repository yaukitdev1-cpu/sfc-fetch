The SFC welcomes the appointment by the Financial Secretary of Mr Martin Wheatley as the Executive Director (Supervision of Markets).

SFC Chairman, Mr Andrew Sheng, said: "Mr Wheatley will bring with him his valuable regulatory and market experience in one of the most important markets in the world. We look forward to working with him."

Mr Wheatley, formerly Deputy Chief Executive of the London Stock Exchange, takes over from Mr Mark Dickens, who left the SFC at the end of March 2005.
