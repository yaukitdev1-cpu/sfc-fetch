The SFC is pleased to announce the appointment of members to the Takeovers and Mergers Panel and the Takeovers Appeal Committee for the year 1 April 2005 to 31 March 2006.

The SFC welcomes Mrs Grace Fung Oei, Ms Benita Yu, Mr Roy Chen, Mr Michael Liu, Mr Nicholas Norris and Mr William Ryback as new members of the Panel and the Appeal Committee. The SFC also welcomes the re-appointment of Mr Kevin Westley as Chairman, and Mr Henry Fan, Mr Clifton Chiu, Mr Stephen Clark, Mr David Graham and Ms Teresa Ko as Deputy Chairmen of the Panel.

The SFC would like to thank Mr Edward W S Cheng and Mr David Lui, who have stepped down from the Panel, for their valuable contribution over the years.

The SFC also welcomes the re-appointment of Mrs Angelina Lee and Ms Teresa Ko as Chairman and Deputy Chairman of the Appeal Committee.

The Panel and Appeal Committee are voluntary bodies which owe their success to the dedication and expertise of their members. The SFC is confident that the significant experience of members in takeovers and mergers, and in the securities and financial markets will ensure the continued success of the Panel and the Appeal Committee.
