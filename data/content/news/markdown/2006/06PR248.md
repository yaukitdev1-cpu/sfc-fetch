The latest Dr Wise’s Column reminds prospective investors to understand the features and risks of any retail structured note before investing in it.

The SFC today publishes the November issue of Dr Wise’s Column, [*Retail Structured Notes – Buyer Beware*](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/features/doctor/features_87_226_337.htm), on its InvestEd website at www.InvestEd.hk.

The notes market is more sophisticated nowadays and there are a variety of structures to suit different investment objectives and risk appetites. Before placing an order for any retail structured note, investors should read all offering documents and should not simply rely on marketing materials that do not have full information.

Investors should also understand that a note linked to a basket of shares is riskier than one linked to a single share since the note is exposed to the price fluctuations of a greater number of shares. They must be aware of and prepared for the worst-case scenario.

Dr Wise is a fictitious character. Through his column, he talks to investors about the key issues of investing and regulatory thoughts.

Dr Wise’s Column is published on the last working Tuesday of every month. From time to time, additional articles in the form of special issues may be published on topical matters.
