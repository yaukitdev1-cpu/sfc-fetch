The SFC announces that the following Amendment Rules and amendments to the Code of Conduct are gazetted today:

- [Securities and Futures (Financial Resources) (Amendment) Rules 2006](http://www.gld.gov.hk/cgi-bin/gld/egazette/gazettefiles.cgi?lang=e&year=2006&month=5&day=19&vol=10&no=20&gn=117&header=1&part=0&df=1&nt=s2&newfile=1&acurrentpage=12&agree=1&gaz_type=ls2);
- [Securities and Futures (Client Securities) (Amendment) Rules 2006](http://www.gld.gov.hk/cgi-bin/gld/egazette/gazettefiles.cgi?lang=e&year=2006&month=5&day=19&vol=10&no=20&gn=118&header=1&part=0&df=1&nt=s2&newfile=1&acurrentpage=12&agree=1&gaz_type=ls2);
- [Securities and Futures (Contract Notes, Statements of Account and Receipts) (Amendment) Rules 2006](http://www.gld.gov.hk/cgi-bin/gld/egazette/gazettefiles.cgi?lang=e&year=2006&month=5&day=19&vol=10&no=20&gn=119&header=1&part=0&df=1&nt=s2&newfile=1&acurrentpage=12&agree=1&gaz_type=ls2); and
- [Amendments to the Code of Conduct for Persons Licensed by or Registered with the Securities and Futures Commission (Code of Conduct)](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/general/general/press_release/06/06pr104_code.pdf).

These amendments are to implement the proposed measures set out in the Consultation Conclusions on Proposed Measures to Address Risks Arising from Securities Margin Financing published on 7 April 2006. The Securities and Futures (Financial Resources) (Amendment) Rules 2006 also imposes paid-up share capital requirements for sponsors, as proposed in the Consultation Conclusions on the Consultation Paper on the Regulation of Sponsors and Compliance Advisers published on 10 April 2006 (Note 1).

The Amendment Rules will be tabled at the Legislative Council (LegCo) for negative vetting on 24 May 2006.

Subject to the expiry of LegCo’s negative vetting period on 12 July 2006, the Amendment Rules and changes to the Code of Conduct relating to the Securities Margin Financing consultation conclusions will come into effect on 1 October 2006. The paid-up share capital requirements for sponsors will come into effect on 1 August 2007.

The Gazette is available at the Government Publications Centre, and accessible on the website of the Government Logistics Department. The three sets of Amendment Rules are also accessible via the SFC website under [“Legislation and Regulatory Handbook” – “Legislation” – “Legislative Amendments”](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/legislation/securities/amendment/amendment.html). The amendments to the Code of Conduct is accessible on the SFC website under [“Legislation and Regulatory Handbook” – “Regulatory Handbook” – “Code, Guidelines and Circulars”](http://www.sfc.hk/sfcRegulatoryHandbook/EN/sfcRegulatoryHandbookTBServlet?openAgent).
