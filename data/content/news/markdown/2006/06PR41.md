The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X} for dealing in a mutual fund whilst unregistered.

An SFC investigation revealed that around February 2003, {X}, whilst unregistered, had arranged an investor to enter into an agreement to subscribe for certain shares in a guaranteed mutual fund, in contravention of section 50 of the Securities Ordinance.

{X} today pleaded guilty to one summons before Magistrate Mr Josiah Lam at Eastern Magistracy. She was fined $3,000 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $10,686 to the SFC.
