SFC Chairman, Mr Martin Wheatley, gave a speech at the Opening Ceremony of the 31st Annual Conference of IOSCO.

Mr Wheatley said the securities market of Hong Kong had come a long way since the establishment of the SFC in 1989, noting the encouraging growth in the number and market capitalisation of Mainland companies, the variety of derivative products and the sizable growth of the fund sector.

Hong Kong also witnessed thriving growth of hedge funds over the past few years, Mr Wheatley said, and more achievements were to be made in the development of REIT market.

Mr Wheatley said Hong Kong had great potential to expand its asset management business on the back of its proximity to Mainland China, high domestic savings, promising regional economic outlook, vast pension scheme assets held by banks, fund managers and insurance companies in the region, and the Mainland’s policy direction towards relaxing and expanding outward investment.

He said the SFC would continue its efforts to foster the healthy growth of the Hong Kong market and maintain its status as an international financial centre.

Towards the end of his speech, Mr Wheatley thanked all IOSCO members, the panellists of the public panels, the IOSCO Secretary-General and all participants for their support. He also expressed gratitude towards the Honourable Rafael SY Hui, Acting Chief Executive of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, for officiating the Opening Ceremony and distinguished guests who attended the occasion.
