The SFC today publishes the July issue of Dr Wise’s Column, [*Privatisations – Your Vote Can Make a Difference*](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/features/doctor/features_87_226_333.htm), on its InvestEd website at www.InvestEd.hk.

In this issue, Dr Wise focuses on the voting requirements under the Hong Kong Code on Takeovers and Mergers relating to privatisation of companies by way of a scheme of arrangement.

Dr Wise reminds shareholders facing a privatisation proposal which is conducted by way of a scheme of arrangement to carefully consider the terms of the scheme and to exercise their voting right accordingly at the shareholders’ meeting held to vote on the scheme. Shareholders must understand that whether a scheme can become effective depends not only on the number of votes approving it but also on the number of votes disapproving it.
