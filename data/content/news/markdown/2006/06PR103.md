The SFC is pleased to announce that its Investor Education Workshops for Teachers have been recognised as Continuing Professional Development (CPD) courses by the Education and Manpower Bureau (EMB). The Government encourages teachers to participate in CPD activities to enrich their professional knowledge for the benefit of students.

The recognition is given on the basis that personal financial management is one of the proposed learning elements for the New Senior Secondary Curriculum and that the SFC’s workshops can provide teachers with the knowledge and skills to design and develop their school-based curriculum.

SFC Chairman, Mr Martin Wheatley, said: “We are pleased to have our Investor Education Workshops recognised as CPD courses for teachers. It is a great endorsement of our investor education work. Above all, we are delighted to join with the EMB to provide support to teachers in acquiring professional knowledge and skills on personal financial management. We hope that this train-the-trainer initiative will help to raise the financial literacy of our next generation.”

Investor Education Workshops for Teachers are part of the SFC’s initiatives to facilitate secondary school and vocational education teachers’ understanding of a wide range of investment activities. The ultimate aim of these workshops is to help students develop positive values and investment attitudes and learn the basic skills of personal financial management.

In its seventh year of running, a new series of 15 workshops covering a wide range of investment products and services will be held from 29 May to 14 July 2006 (Note 1). Two modules are new, namely Scams and Malpractices and A Sharing With An Analyst. In addition, the Personal Financial Planning module has been expanded to cover youth financial planning. Experienced market professionals will be conducting some workshops. There will also be visits to the HKEx’s Exchange Exhibition Hall, a broker firm or a bank, and a fund management company.

Participating teachers will receive a certificate for each workshop attendance issued by the SFC. They will also be awarded the CPD hours, provided that they have attended a post-training workshop on “Learning and Teaching of Investor Education” organised by the EMB (Note 2).

Since 2000, the SFC has organised 96 workshops attracting over 6,300 enrolments. A “[Teachers Corner](http://www.eirc.hk/eirc/html/EN/teachers/index.htm)” has been set up on the SFC’s InvestEd website at [www.InvestEd.hk](http://www.InvestEd.hk) to provide numerous references to teachers to facilitate their discussions with students.

The new workshops are now open for enrolment. Invitation letters have been sent to secondary schools and vocational education institutes. Enrolment can be made online on the EMB website at [www.emb.gov.hk](http://www.emb.gov.hk/) under the section of “Training Calendar”. Alternatively, teachers can visit the “[Teachers Corner](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/teachers/index.htm)” on the SFC’s InvestEd website, where a direct link is established to the EMB website for enrolment.
