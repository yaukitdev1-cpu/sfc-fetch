The SFC is making final preparation for the 31st IOSCO Annual Conference, which has so far attracted some 600 registrations from regulators, practitioners and market leaders from nearly 100 jurisdictions.

The Annual Conference will be hosted by the SFC on 5 – 8 June in Hong Kong for the first time. The SFC is pleased to announce that Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited, Shanghai Stock Exchange, Shenzhen Stock Exchange and Shanghai Futures Exchange will support the event as major sponsors and exhibitors.

HKEx will sponsor the Come Horseracing Night Dinner on 7 June, while the three Mainland exchanges will sponsor the Farewell Gala Dinner that concludes the four-day Annual Conference on 8 June. Delegates from the three Mainland exchanges will also participate in the international event.

Dell and Microsoft will provide information, communications and technology equipment at an Internet Lounge for delegates throughout the four days. Other sponsors of the event include professional associations, media organisations etc (Note 2).

During the first two days of the Annual Conference, IOSCO members will assemble in closed meetings to discuss important regulatory issues related to the world's securities and futures markets. On the last two days, all participants may join the public panel discussions. This year, eminent panelists will discuss regulatory issues concerning International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS), hedge funds, bond markets and the emerging markets.

The SFC has arranged a variety of spectacular performances for the delegates in the evening social programmes. A cross-over musical performance of Er-hu and piano, and performance by the Hong Kong Juvenile & Youth Chinese Classical Orchestra will be presented at the Welcome Dinner on 5 June. The Guangzhou Acrobatic Troupe will perform at the Farewell Gala Dinner, along with a master of “mask changing”- one of the nation’s most closely guarded secrets, and jazz and blues vocalist Marjorie Mullings.

SFC Chairman, Mr Martin Wheatley, said: “We look forward to welcoming our fellow regulators and industry practitioners from around the world to take part in this significant event. The Annual Conference is set to be a valuable forum for us to exchange views on various issues in order to promote and establish high standards of regulation.”

For details of registration, conference programmes and a list of confirmed panelists, please visit the conference website: [www.iosco2006.org](http://www.iosco2006.org).
