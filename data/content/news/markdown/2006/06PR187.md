The SFC is pleased to announce the appointment of members to the Committee on Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs) for the year 1 September 2006 to 31 August 2007.

The SFC welcomes Mr Vernon Moore and Mr K L Wong as new members of the Committee.

The SFC also welcomes the re-appointment of Mr F K Au, Mrs Susan Chow, Mr Heung Shu Fai, Mr Stephen Hui, Mr C Y Leung, Mr Alastair Murray, Mr David Richardson, Mr Brian Stevenson and Mr Kevin Westley.

The SFC would like to thank Mr Henry Cheong and Mr Martin Cubbon, who have made valuable contribution to the Committee during the past three years.
