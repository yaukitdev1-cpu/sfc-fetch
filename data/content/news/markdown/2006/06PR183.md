The SFC welcomes the appointment by the Financial Secretary of three Executive Directors.

They are Mr Brian Ho, Executive Director of Corporate Finance; Mr Keith Lui, Executive Director of Supervision of Markets; and Mr Mark Steward, Executive Director of Enforcement.

SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, Mr Martin Wheatley, said: “I welcome the Financial Secretary’s appointments. We are particularly heartened by the fact that existing staff are recognised and promoted to the Executive Director positions. Both Mr Ho and Mr Lui have been with the SFC for more than 10 years and are familiar with its operation.”

“We also look forward to working with Mr Steward, who has rich experience in regulatory investigation and enforcement in one of the leading securities regulators in the world,” he said.
