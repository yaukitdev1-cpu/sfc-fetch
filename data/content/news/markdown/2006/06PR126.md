The 31st Annual Conference of IOSCO was officially opened this afternoon by the Honourable Rafael SY Hui, GBS, JP, Acting Chief Executive of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, in the presence of more than 700 distinguished guests, regulators and market practitioners from over 135 jurisdictions around the world (Note 1).

Today’s Opening Ceremony was followed by a public panel discussion on International Financial Reporting Standards. Three more public panels will be held tomorrow. The SFC cordially invites you and your representatives to attend.

You are also invited to attend tomorrow’s Closing Press Conference that will conclude the four-day Annual Conference. The Closing Press Conference will be attended by Mr Martin Wheatley, Chairman of the SFC and also Chairman of the Presidents’ Committee for this year’s Conference, Ms Jane Diplock, Chairperson of the IOSCO Executive Committee, Mr Michel Prada, Chairman of the IOSCO Technical Committee, Dr Doğan Cansizlar, Chairman of the IOSCO Emerging Markets Committee, and Mr Philippe Richard, Secretary General of IOSCO.
