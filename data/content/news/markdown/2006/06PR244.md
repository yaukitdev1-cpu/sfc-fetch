The SFC will continue its focus on ensuring that financial products are not mis-sold in Hong Kong and that investors are provided with appropriate advice from their advisers, SFC’s Executive Director of Intermediaries and Investment Products, Mrs Alexa Lam, today said.

Giving the keynote address at the Asia Risk 2006 conference, Mrs Lam said that more retail investors relied on investment advisers (IAs) for guidance on increasingly complex financial products. “Risks arise when IAs, due to various reasons, are unable to provide professional and objective investment advice to their clients. Once investors lose confidence in their IAs, the danger is that they lose confidence in the market and this would be detrimental to all market participants,” she said.

Mrs Lam discussed the SFC’s action plan to address mis-selling:

(a) the SFC is currently conducting a second round of inspection on IA activities;

(b) the SFC will continue to enforce against breaches of the rules and regulations;

(c) the SFC will provide more detailed guidance on existing regulatory requirements concerning the duties and obligations of IAs to their clients; and

(d) the SFC will continue to help investors know their rights, responsibilities and risks when seeking advice from IAs.

Mrs Lam reminded IAs that they had a duty to act in the clients’ best interests by knowing their clients, ensuring adequate due diligence, making reasonable recommendation, helping clients make informed decisions, and employing competent staff with appropriate training. The SFC expects senior management to take responsibility for ensuring that proper standards of conduct and compliance are adhered to.

She added: “However, regulation alone will not help those investors who rush into investments without first understanding the product, the risks, the fees and obligations. We will be seeking to improve investors’ awareness in this regard.”
