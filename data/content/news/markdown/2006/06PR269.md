The December issue of the SFC Enforcement Reporter, published today, is a reminder that the SFC will impose life bans on those who steal client assets.

In November, the SFC banned five licensees from the industry for life for stealing client assets and market manipulation.

The SFC further warns that it does not subsidise unsuccessful litigants and will seek to recover its costs when costs are awarded. The SFC obtained for the first time last month a bankruptcy order against a former licensed person, who failed to pay costs awarded to the SFC despite repeated demands.

During the month, the SFC prosecuted two people for misleading the SFC, cold calling and aiding and abetting unlicensed activities. The SFC also disciplined another eight licensees for cold calling, condoning and facilitating unlicensed activities, failure to keep proper records, supervisory failings, and breach of staff dealing policy.

In late November the Insider Dealing Tribunal identified 11 persons as insider dealers in relation to the shares of Vanda Systems and Communications Holdings Limited (renamed as Hutchison Global Communications Holdings Limited) between 14 and 17 February 2000.

Copies of the [December issue of the SFC Enforcement Reporter](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/enforcement/06/dec_06.pdf), which covers enforcement outcomes in the previous month, are available at the SFC office. All issues are also available on the SFC website under [“Speeches, Publications & Consultations” – “Publications”](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/speeches/public/public.html).

The public are able to subscribe to the newsletter by registering for the Update Alert service on the SFC website. They will then be able to receive the newsletter via email.
