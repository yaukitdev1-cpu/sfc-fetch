The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X} for holding {X} out as performing a regulated function in relation to leveraged foreign exchange trading whilst unlicensed.

An SFC investigation revealed that between February and May 2005, {X}, as a senior manager of Sincere Bullion Limited, had introduced a client to open an account with a company incorporated in Macau, Tse’s Investimento Internacional (Macau) Limitada (also known as Tse’s International Investment (Macau) Limited), to trade leveraged foreign exchange contracts. {X} handled the account opening procedures and placed orders for the client whilst unlicensed. These acts contravened section 114 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

{X} today pleaded guilty to one summons before Mr Allan Wyeth, a Magistrate at Eastern Magistracy. {X} was fined $2,000 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $20,000 to the SFC.
