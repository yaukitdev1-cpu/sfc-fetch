In May, the SFC successfully prosecuted three companies and four people and disciplined 12 licensees, including a settlement with an investment adviser over mis-selling of certain funds and concerns about the sale of geared products. As a result of the settlement, the licensee agreed to provide investors with a compensation arrangement. The SFC also imposed a life ban on a licensee from re-entering into the industry for stealing client’s securities, giving false information to the SFC and conspiring to pervert the course of justice.

The Securities and Futures Appeals Tribunal in May upheld the SFC’s decision to suspend a responsible officer of a broker firm in relation to his breaches of the Code of Conduct, and the Money Laundering Guidance Notes.

Apart from the prosecutions and disciplinary actions, the SFC issued a restriction notice prohibiting a broker from dealing in securities, disposing of or dealing with any assets held by it or held on behalf of its clients without the SFC’s prior written consent. Upon an SFC application, an administrator was appointed by the Court to administer the property of the broker firm and that of its clients.

The above and other actions are summarised in the June issue of the SFC Enforcement Reporter published today. The monthly publication also discusses the compliance issues highlighted by these cases.
