The SFC will proceed with a proposal to set a securities collateral re-pledging limit immediately in light of the general support by members of the Legislative Council Panel on Financial Affairs.

The SFC is encouraged by the comments made by Panel members this morning that the matter must be addressed as one of great importance and urgency.

Mrs Alexa Lam, SFC’s Executive Director of Intermediaries and Investment Products, said the SFC would lose no time in submitting to the Government the conclusions to the exercise and the draft legislation. The SFC will also inform industry representatives of the LegCo members’ views.

“The proposal aims to benefit the over 1.3 million Hong Kong investors, and to protect the reputation of the Hong Kong securities market. Everyone in Hong Kong stands to gain. I am confident the industry will join hands with us to reduce pooling risks for the larger good of Hong Kong as a whole,” Mrs Lam said.

Earlier, at the Panel meeting, Mrs Lam said only a limited number of broker firms would be affected by the proposal.

The SFC intends to set the re-pledging limit at a range of between 130% and 150% in a two-phase approach:

(a) implementing the re-pledging limit of 180% soonest after completing the legislative process (negative vetting of rules);

(b) after a further period of 12 months, the re-pledging limit will be fixed at a level of between 130% and 150%.
