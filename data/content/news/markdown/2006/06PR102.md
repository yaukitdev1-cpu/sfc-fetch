The SFC successfully prosecuted five entities and disciplined 19 licensees in April 2006, including the first joint disciplinary action by the SFC and the Hong Kong Monetary Authority since the Securities and Futures Ordinance became effective.

The SFC today publishes the May issue of the SFC Enforcement Reporter, summarising the SFC’s enforcement action in the month of April and discussing the compliance issues highlighted by recent cases.

In April, the SFC prosecuted four people and one company for market manipulation, cold calling and unlicensed dealing. We also disciplined 19 licensees for unlicensed dealing and aiding and abetting unlicensed dealing, failures to answer SFC questions candidly, turning a blind eye to possibly illegal activities, internal control and supervisory failures, cold calling, and breaching the Code of Conduct and the employer’s internal policy.

The unlicensed dealing case resulted in both a criminal prosecution and a disciplinary action. The SFC and the HKMA for the first time co-operated to take disciplinary action against parties who were, at the material time, licensed persons under the SFO and relevant individuals under the Banking Ordinance since the scheme for regulating securities and futures activities under the SFO and the Banking (Amendment) Ordinance came into force on 1 April 2003.

Also, the Securities and Futures Appeals Tribunal upheld the SFC’s decision against a broker firm and its responsible officer for their failures in relation to telephone recordings of an investor’s complaints and client transactions, and the handling of client complaints.
