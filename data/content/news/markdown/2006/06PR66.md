The SFC is pleased to announce that it has signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with the Israel Securities Authority (ISA) (Note 1).

The MoU establishes a framework to facilitate co-operation in the enforcement of securities law as well as the exchange of information between the SFC and the ISA. Mutual assistance will be rendered by the respective securities commissions under the terms of the MoU to combat cross-border financial crimes and misconduct, hence better protecting the integrity of the markets and investor interest (Note 2).

The MoU was separately signed in March by Mr Martin Wheatley, Chairman of the SFC, in Hong Kong and Mr Moshe Tery, Chairman of the ISA, in Jerusalem.

Mr Wheatley said: “Borders are no barriers to financial markets but serious obstacles for regulators. With this MoU, our respective abilities to protect our markets and enforce law against cross-border fraud will be enhanced. I am delighted with the formal establishment of co-operation between the SFC and ISA under this MoU”.

Mr Tery believes that the MoU will not only enhance cooperation on the regulatory front but will help consolidate ties between the two markets. "Both Hong Kong and Israel constitute growth markets which attract avid interest from the international financial community. I look forward to the opportunity to work closely with the SFC and to deepen the exchange of information and ideas between our two commissions," Mr Tery said.
