Hong Kong plays a unique role as the main bridge between the Mainland and the rest of the world in the regional economic transformation process, SFC Chairman, Mr Martin Wheatley, said today.

Mr Wheatley was making a welcome address entitled “The Role of Hong Kong in Developing Asia’s Capital Markets” at the 3rd Annual Asia Pacific Bond Congress (Note 1). He outlined the strengths of Hong Kong and other reasons for Hong Kong to perform the role as both a prime fund raising centre for Mainland enterprises and a platform for Mainland investors looking to invest abroad.

“As the main platform for bringing international capital into the Mainland and channelling the Mainland’s huge savings to the international market, including the rest of Asia, Hong Kong is able to facilitate not only the continuing growth of the Mainland economy but ultimately also the continuing growth of other Asian economies, thus bringing win-win benefits to all parties,” he said.
