SFC Chairman, Mr Martin Wheatley, has visited the Shanghai stock and futures exchanges.

During his one-day visit yesterday (23 February), Mr Wheatley had met Mr Geng Liang, Chairman of the Shanghai Stock Exchange, and Mr Jiang Yang, Chief Executive Officer of the Shanghai Futures Exchange.

The visit was a courtesy call. Mr Wheatley and the Shanghai hosts took the opportunity to exchange views on matters of mutual interest. They discussed topics including the development of Shanghai’s financial market and Hong Kong’s role in the continuing development of the Mainland capital market.

Mr Wheatley said: “Shanghai is a very dynamic city and its financial market is well developed. I believe the Shanghai and Hong Kong markets would continue to serve the various needs of the Mainland enterprises and market users and contribute to the development of the Mainland capital market.”

Mr Wheatley also invited the exchanges and market practitioners in Shanghai to come to Hong Kong in June to join the Annual Conference of the International Organization of Securities Commissions. (Note 2)

This was Mr Wheatley’s first visit to Shanghai as SFC Chairman. He visited the China Securities Regulatory Commission in Beijing last October.
