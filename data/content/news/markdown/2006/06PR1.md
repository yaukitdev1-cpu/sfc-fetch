Acting SFC Chairman, Mr Peter Au-Yang, gave an opening statement this morning at a meeting of the LegCo Panel on Financial Affairs.

Mr Au-Yang briefed the FAP about the SFC review report on Hong Kong’s derivative warrants market, which was issued on 25 November 2005.

“In devising the proposals, we have focused on achieving the right balance between securing an appropriate degree of protection for members of the investing public and not over-regulating the market so as to stifle continuous innovation and development,” Mr Au-Yang said.
