The SFC today publishes the March issue of Dr Wise’s Column, [*Keeping an Eye on Your Investments*](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/features/doctor/features_87_226_329.htm), on its InvestEd website at www.InvestEd.hk.

In this issue, Dr Wise tells of the unfortunate experience of three investors who ignored his advice and eventually became victims of theft of their monies and shares by broker firm staff.

Dr Wise emphasises that the most effective investor protection starts with the investor himself, and enumerates several easy-to-remember tips to avoid misappropriation. For instance, investors should


- ensure that they receive their account statements at least once a month;
- verify the accuracy of statement information and independently check with the back-office department of the broker firm if there are discrepancies; and
- understand that audit confirmation or circularisation for verification of client’s cash and stock balances should be conducted by independent professional auditors, and any irregularities noted should accordingly be reported directly to the independent auditor firm concerned (Note 1).

Dr Wise is a fictitious character. Through his column, he talks to investors about the key issues of investing and regulatory thoughts.

Dr Wise’s Column is published on the last working Tuesday of every month. From time to time, additional articles in the form of special issues may be published on topical matters. The column is posted under the “Features & Dr Wise” section of the InvestEd website.
