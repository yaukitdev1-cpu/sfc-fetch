The SFC is pleased to release its third [*Quarterly Report* for the financial year to 31 March 2006](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/quartely/oct_dec05_eng.pdf).

During October to December, the SFC took several initiatives to raise market standards and facilitate market development. We authorised the first REITs in Hong Kong and announced a policy on disclosure of interests in REITs for greater transparency. We also consulted the public on a six-point plan to improve the derivative warrants market. On enforcement, we successfully prosecuted 23 persons and firms, and disciplined 37 entities. Both figures were higher than those in the previous quarter and the same quarter in 2004.

On the investors’ front, we launched new TV and radio commercials, an investor education icon reinforcing the importance of “Learn before you invest” and a re-branded information portal now called InvestEd ([www.InvestEd.hk](http://www.InvestEd.hk)). Investors are also exempt from paying investor compensation levies from 19 December, following changes to the levy collection mechanism.

We also note that the number of licensed persons had risen to 25,292 at the end of December. The brokerage industry also saw an increase in total shareholders’ fund as trading had remained buoyant.

The SFC’s total revenue for the third quarter was $202 million, while total expenditure was $122 million. Total revenue for the first nine months of the fiscal year was $575 million, with total expenditure at $359 million.

SFC Chairman, Mr Martin Wheatley, said that based on the current information and by managing the budget prudently, a surplus was expected in the fourth quarter.

The [*Quarterly Report* is accessible on the SFC website](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/speeches/public/annual/annual.html). Printed copies are available at the SFC’s office.
