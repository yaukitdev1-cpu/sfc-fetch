The SFC welcomes the new appointment by the Financial Secretary of Mr Shengman Zhang and the re-appointment of Mr Kenneth Kwok and Professor Liu Pak-wai as Non-Executive Directors.

Mr Eddy Fong, SFC Chairman, said: “Mr Zhang has rich experience in the banking and financial field in the Mainland and internationally. I look forward to his invaluable contributions to the SFC. I am also pleased to be able to continue working with Mr Kenneth Kwok and Professor Liu Pak-wai.”

Both Mr Kwok and Professor Liu have been SFC Non-Executive Directors since 1 January 2005. From 1 January 2007, the SFC’s board will be composed of the Chairman, six Executive Directors, and seven Non-Executive Directors.
