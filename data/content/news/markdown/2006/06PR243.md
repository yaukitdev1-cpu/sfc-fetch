The SFC will not tolerate misappropriation and fraudulent practice by licensees and will ban licensees who engage in such serious dishonesty from the industry for life. The warning is contained in the November issue of the SFC Enforcement Reporter, which is published today.

In October, the SFC banned a former licensee from re-entering the industry for life for misappropriating client assets and being convicted of false accounting. The SFC also disciplined four licensees for facilitating unlicensed activities or manipulating the calculated opening price of HSI futures contracts during the pre-market opening period.

The SFC will also continue to take tough action against market manipulators and those supervisors who aid and abet unlicensed activities by excluding them from the industry for lengthy periods.

Copies of the [November issue](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/enforcement/06/nov_06.pdf) of the SFC Enforcement Reporter are available at the SFC office. All issues are also available on the SFC website under [“Speeches, Publications & Consultations” – “Publications”](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/speeches/public/enforcement/enforcement.html).

The public are able to subscribe to the newsletter by registering for the Update Alert service on the SFC website. They will then be able to receive the newsletter via email.

The public are also invited to provide feedback to improve the SFC Enforcement Reporter. Comments can be emailed to [enfreporter@sfc.hk](mailto:enfreporter@sfc.hk).
