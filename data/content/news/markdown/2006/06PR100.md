The SFC has published its Annual Report for the year ended 31 March 2006. The Annual Report was laid before the LegCo today following submission to the Financial Secretary.

“Investors First” is the theme of this year’s Annual Report, demonstrating the value we attach to this objective. Investors, like consumers, will only be attracted to a market that is well regulated and where firm and fair enforcement action will be taken against malpractice. The regulatory environment should allow innovation, and enable educated investors to make informed choices from a wide range of products.

The Annual Report reviews our work progress and gives an account of our finances. Mr Martin Wheatley said in the Chairman’s Statement: “By using our resources appropriately, and with the support of the Government, market bodies, market practitioners and the public, we can confidently meet the regulatory challenges in the next few years.”

The more significant achievements last year included:

To regulate, we


- mapped out measures to strengthen the derivative warrants market;
- reviewed the disclosure of interests requirements;
- secured public support for securities margin financing reform;
- proposed tighter regulation of sponsors; and
- continued work on statutory backing for important listing requirements.

To enforce, we


- concluded 84 disciplinary inquiries and took action against 98 companies and people;
- disciplined a sponsor for due diligence failure;
- banned a securities analyst from the industry for five years for conflicts of interest;
- prosecuted 72 companies and people;
- identified 18 cases of suspect warrants market activities for investigation;
- investigated 27 cases involving listed companies;
- assisted the Court in a successful ICAC prosecution of a high profile market manipulation case; and
- referred 24 cases to the Police.

To facilitate, we


- authorised the first REIT for listing in Hong Kong;
- authorised the first bond index-tracking ETFs in Asia;
- consulted the market on improving the securities offering prospectus regime;
- signed regulatory agreements with eight jurisdictions;
- reviewed the investor compensation fund collection mechanism, resulting in levy exemption since last December; and
- supported Government’s proposal to reduce the transaction levies.

To educate, we


- introduced the theme of “Before you invest, ask the right questions”, completed with a new TV commercial;
- re-named the investor website InvestEd for a clearer identity and with stronger content;
- surveyed retail investors, in particular warrant investors, to gauge levels of knowledge and understanding; and
- attracted 8,000 people to 79 education seminars.

In this year’s Annual Report, we have expanded discussions on our staff and our corporate social responsibility policies.

In 2005-2006, total revenue of the SFC amounted to $852 million, with total expenditure of $497 million. We recorded a surplus of $355 million for the year and reserves of $1.2 billion at the year-end.

New edition of the glossary of financial terms published

The SFC has also published the Fourth Edition of An English-Chinese Glossary of Securities, Futures And Financial Terms, with a collection of nearly 15,000 common financial terms. Special efforts have been made to expand the terms commonly used in Mainland China, given the increasing cross-border interactions.

Mr Wheatley said: “The Glossary will serve as a practical tool for investors, market practitioners, professionals, academia and students, translators, and people engaging in China business.”
