The SFC is pleased to announce the appointment of Mr Anthony Espina, Mr Andreas Mondovits and Ms Judy Vas, and the re-appointment of Professor Raymond So, to its Academic and Accreditation Advisory Committee** (**AAAC) for a term of two years, from 19 February 2006 to 18 February 2008 (Note 1).

“I welcome Mr Anthony Espina, Mr Andreas Mondovits and Ms Judy Vas and the re-appointment of Professor Raymond So.  They will bring their professional expertise and valuable experience to the AAAC and help further raise the standards of our intermediaries,” said Mrs Alexa Lam, Chairman of the AAAC and SFC’s Executive Director of Intermediaries and Investment Products.

Mrs Lam also paid tribute to Mr Stephen Chiu Chung Hui, Ms Sandra Yuen Man Lee and Mr Vincent Marshall Kwan Ho Lee, who have retired from the AAAC, for their valuable contribution.

The AAAC was established under section 8 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance to advise the SFC on any policy matters regarding competence and continuous professional training requirements for the industry.  It plays a crucial role in raising and promoting industry professionalism, thereby enhancing investor confidence in our market practitioners.

Last year, the AAAC approved the Hong Kong Securities Institute’s Professional Diploma in Financial Markets programme as a recognised industry qualification and local regulatory framework paper towards meeting the competence requirements (Note 2). The AAAC also approved the Department of Economics and Finance of the City University of Hong Kong for providing continuous professional training.

In pursuit of its mission, the AAAC will continue to identify training and re-training needs of the industry and assist intermediaries in gaining relevant skills and product knowledge to diversify their businesses and remain competitive.
