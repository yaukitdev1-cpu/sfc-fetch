The SFC welcomes the appointment by the Financial Secretary of Mrs Angelina Lee as Non-Executive Director of the SFC from 1 August 2006 to 31 July 2008.

Mr Martin Wheatley, SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, said: “On behalf of all staff, I warmly welcome Mrs Lee. With her rich experience in the legal profession and active public service, I am confident that she will make important contributions to the SFC.”

Mrs Lee will succeed Mr Raymond Kwok, whose term expires on 31 July 2006.

“I am grateful to Mr Kwok, who has been one of our longest-serving Non-Executive Directors, for his tremendous contribution in the past eight years to the SFC and the Hong Kong financial market,” Mr Wheatley said.

Mr Kwok has been an SFC Non-Executive Director since 1 August 1998. He chairs the Commission’s Budget Committee, and is a member of the Audit Committee, Remuneration Committee and SFC (HKEC Listing) Appeals Committee.
