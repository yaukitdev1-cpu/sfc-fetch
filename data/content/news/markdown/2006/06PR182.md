An investor from France has avoided becoming a boiler room scam victim because of the SFC Alert List – the August issue of the SFC Alert, which is published today, has the story.

In this issue, the lead story outlines the SFC measures to increase investor protection against misappropriation. There are also other investor education articles inside, giving you tips on choosing a broker and explaining the purpose of circularisation.

There are articles on the Fund Management Activities Survey, which shows another year of record growth of fund management business in Hong Kong last year, and on research papers on HSI constituent changes and derivatives trading.

The SFC Alert is a bi-monthly newsletter for both investors and intermediaries. Copies of the [current issue of SFC Alert](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/newsletter/06/jul_aug06.pdf) are available at the SFC office. [All issues are available on the SFC website](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/speeches/public/newsletter/newsletter.html).
