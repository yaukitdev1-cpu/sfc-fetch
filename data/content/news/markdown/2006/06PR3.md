The SFC today launches its investor education programmes for 2006 with various activities in January, which has been designated the Investor Education Month.

Throughout 2006, the SFC will adopt the theme “Before you invest, ask the right questions”. The public are encouraged to ask the right questions about investment products and services and recommendations offered by advisers in order to make informed choices.

Officiating the launch ceremony at the SFC today, the Secretary for Financial Services and the Treasury, Mr Frederick Ma, said: “Both the Government and the SFC attach great importance to investor education. Apart from being the market police, the SFC also carries an investor education mission. Investor education does not mean telling people what and when to invest, but aims to promote public understanding of investment products and associated risks. Armed with this knowledge, investors can make better decisions.”

SFC Chairman, Mr Martin Wheatley, said: “It is important for all parties – regulators, market operators, investment advisers, brokers and educators – to play a role in increasing the knowledge of our retail investors. To do our part, we have named January the Investor Education Month and set a focused annual theme for our investor education activities. Our investor education website, which has been renamed InvestEd, is a round-the-clock educational service for retail investors. We hope that more and more investors would take advantage of this website.” (Note 1)

InvestEd and Investor Education (IE) icon

The SFC has unveiled a new icon to highlight its IE role. The public will be able to instantly recognise the SFC’s IE activities with the icon. The word “InvestEd” is a short form for “Investor Education”.






The icon signifies that, like a seedling which needs nutrients and sunlight to grow healthily, investors must also learn in order to be able to invest wisely. The slogan “Learn Before You Invest” echoes this meaning.

The SFC has also re-named its IE portal, previously known as the eIRC or the Electronic Investor Resources Centre, as the InvestEd website, making it easier to remember. The URL of the InvestEd website is [www.InvestEd.hk](http://www.InvestEd.hk).

Public interest TV and radio commercials

The SFC has produced new TV and radio commercials featuring a “Mr Sheep”. However, unlike his predecessor in SFC’s TV commercials, Mr Sheep does not just follow the crowd and knows the importance of protecting his own interests when investing. For instance, Mr Sheep and his friends ask the right questions when buying warrants, opening a margin account or seeking investment advice (Note 2).

The TV commercial will start screening from tomorrow (11 January 2006) on both the English and Cantonese channels of TVB and ATV, and on Cable TV. The radio commercial will be broadcast in English, Cantonese and Putonghua on local radio stations.

To explain the issues in the commercials, the SFC will place a full-page advertisement that includes an open quiz in the Headline Daily tomorrow. Those who correctly answer a question in the advert may have the opportunity to receive an IE souvenir (Note 3).

There will also be articles in various newspapers and magazines elaborating on the questions that investors should ask under different investment scenarios.

Flash videos

The InvestEd website will continue to make use of innovative and interactive means to educate investors online. From the end of January, text educational information under the “Features & Dr Wise” section will be complemented by lively flash animation videos.

Investor forums

In light of the overwhelming response to the public seminars offered jointly with the Li Ka Shing Institute of Professional and Continuing Education (LiPACE) of The Open University of Hong Kong in November and December 2005, two new investor forums will be run on 14 and 15 January 2006. Market professionals will explain how to evaluate stock and fund performance and how to build a personal investment portfolio.

There will also be ongoing workplace and school talks covering a wide range of topics.

Future plan

In March, the SFC will adapt the 10 best stories from "My Investment Story" competition (Note 4) into radio dramas for broadcast on Radio 5 of RTHK and publication in newspapers.

IE highlights in the second quarter of 2006 will include: a new TV series on warrants and alternative investments; train-the-trainer workshops for secondary school and vocational education teachers; and an InvestEd ambassador campaign. The SFC will also release the findings of its Retail Investor Survey. Key initiatives for the second half will be announced later.
