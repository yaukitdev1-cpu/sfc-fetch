The Closer Economic Partnership Arrangement (CEPA) has brought about a lot of opportunities for the securities and futures sector in Hong Kong and the Mainland, according to Mrs Alexa Lam, SFC’s Executive Director of Intermediaries and Investment Products.

Mrs Lam today gave a speech at the CEPA Forum, Beijing 2006 - New Opportunities and Challenges Ahead for Mainland and Hong Kong Financial Practitioners.

Since the implementation of CEPA I, the Hong Kong Securities Institute and The Securities Association of China have arranged local examinations for Hong Kong and Mainland securities practitioners seeking to be qualified in the Mainland and Hong Kong respectively.

Under CEPA II, Hong Kong futures intermediaries are permitted to set up joint venture companies in the Mainland. “We know that some Hong Kong futures intermediaries have already started talks with Mainland futures brokerage companies on joint venture co-operation. Some of them have expressed interest in filing or have already filed applications with the China Securities Regulatory Commission,” Mrs Lam said.

CEPA III allows Mainland securities and futures companies to set up subsidiaries in Hong Kong. Mrs Lam said that some Mainland futures companies had contacted the SFC, which had explained to them Hong Kong’s regulation and licensing and operating requirements.

“CEPA is significant for the financial market development in Hong Kong and the Mainland. The SFC, in close co-operation with the CSRC, will provide assistance in policy and operational matters for the implementation of CEPA,” Mrs Lam said.
