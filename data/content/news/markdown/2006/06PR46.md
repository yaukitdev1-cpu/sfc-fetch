The SFC today publishes the [March issue of the SFC Enforcement Reporter](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/enforcement/06/mar06_eng.pdf), summarising the SFC’s enforcement action in the month of February 2006 and discussing the compliance issues highlighted by recent cases.

In February, the SFC successfully prosecuted four companies and two people for aiding and abetting unlicensed activities, failing to assist SFC investigation and disclosure of interests failures.

We disciplined four licensees for improper conduct in handling internal documents, failures relating to unsolicited calls, facilitating unlicensed dealing, lying to the SFC, allowing clandestine and unauthorised third party orders and operating discretionary accounts without written authorisations. We also issued a public statement about a former securities dealer’s representative who facilitated unlicensed dealing.

From 1 April 2005 to end of February 2006, the SFC successfully prosecuted 67 entities and offered no evidence against three entities. In the same period, the SFC took action against 84 licensees for various regulatory breaches, of which three cases were settled with voluntary payments and with no statutory sanction imposed. Another six licensees settled with voluntary payments and received statutory sanctions.

Copies of the March issue of the SFC Enforcement Reporter are available at the SFC office. All issues are also available on the SFC website under [“Speeches, Publications & Consultations” – “Publications”](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/speeches/public/enforcement/enforcement.html).
