The latest Dr Wise’s Column explains the function of the Investor Compensation Fund as a safety net, but also reminds investors to stay alert to activities relating to their trading accounts.

The SFC today publishes the August issue of Dr Wise’s Column, [*Investor Compensation Fund – Your Safety Net*](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/features/doctor/features_87_226_334.htm), on its InvestEd website at www.InvestEd.hk.

Investors trading products on the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong and Hong Kong Futures Exchange could seek compensation from the Investor Compensation Fund if they suffer losses as a result of defaults by SFC-licensed or registered intermediaries, securities margin financiers and banks. An investor may be compensated for up to HK$150,000 for trading securities on the stock exchange and also for trading futures contracts on the futures exchange. Both cash clients and margin clients are entitled to compensation if their intermediaries default. The claims are processed and determined by the Investor Compensation Company.

Despite the existence of such a safety net, Dr Wise advises investors to take preventive measures to better safeguard their interests.

In particular, investors are reminded to instruct their intermediaries to send statements or documents to them direct. Investors should check the documents in a timely manner, and at once follow up any discrepancies. Investors can also consider opening an Investor Participant account in the Central Clearing and Settlement System.
