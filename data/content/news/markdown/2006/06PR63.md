The SFC today releases a Consultation Conclusions paper on the Proposed Measures to Address Risks Arising from Securities Margin Financing.

After carefully considering all public comments, the measures set out in the Consultation Conclusions paper constitute a balanced and fair package that would offer better protection to investors, while having minimum impact on the industry. The measures seek to:


- introduce limits on repledging in two stages - set at 180% initially (target date being 1 October 2006) and after a period of 12 months reduced to 140% as recommended by the Working Group (Note 1);
- make some moderate changes to selected FRR haircut percentages;
- improve transparency by providing margin clients with better disclosure about the risks of pooling and repledging; and
- provide relaxation on certain requirements currently imposed on firms, as pooling risk will be reduced by the proposed measures.

Mrs Alexa Lam, SFC’s Executive Director of Intermediaries and Investment Products, said: “The market has been doing well in last two years. Brokers have generally benefited from the significant increases in market capitalisation and daily trading volume. On the other hand, factors such as increased market volatility and recent reported cases of misappropriation may give rise to additional risks to investors and the industry. Therefore, the SFC considers that this is the right time to proceed with the proposed measures to give investors better protection.”

“We believe that this balanced package of measures would help protect the investing public and provide a stronger foundation for the long-term development of the industry. Furthermore, it would strengthen Hong Kong’s reputation as an international financial centre,” she said.

Subject to negative vetting by the Legislative Council, the new measures are expected to come into effect on 1 October 2006.

The Consultation Conclusions paper is available at the SFC office and on the SFC website at [www.sfc.hk](/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/consultation/conclusion?refNo=04CP3).
