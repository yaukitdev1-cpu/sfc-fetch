In response to media enquiries, the SFC wishes to clarify that its authorisation requirements for REITs and the licensing requirements of REIT managers have remained unchanged.

Since their introduction, the SFC has been reviewing REIT applications consistently in accordance with the applicable licensing requirements and the Code on REITs. No changes have been brought to these since the last Code amendment in June 2005 to allow overseas investment by SFC-authorised REITs and increase their gearing limit to 45% of gross asset value.

The SFC expects all applications to be complete and in compliance with the required standards and requirements. Otherwise, they are liable to be refused or returned.

The SFC continues to welcome discussions with potential issuers and their advisers, especially if there are new product features.
