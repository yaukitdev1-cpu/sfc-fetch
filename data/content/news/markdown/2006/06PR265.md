The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X} for performing a regulated function in relation to leveraged foreign exchange trading whilst unlicensed.

An SFC investigation revealed that between March and August 2004, {X}, as an account manager of Sincere Bullion Limited, had introduced two clients to open an account with Hong Kong Forex Investment Limited to trade leveraged foreign exchange contracts whilst unlicensed, in contravention of section 114 of the SFO.

{X} was convicted after trial today of two summonses before Mr Winston Leung Wing Chung, a Magistrate at Eastern Magistracy. {X} was fined a total of $4,000 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $13,429 to the SFC.
