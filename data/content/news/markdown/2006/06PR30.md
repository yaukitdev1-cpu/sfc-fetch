The SFC today publishes the February issue of Dr Wise’s Column, [*Trading Stocks in a “Wise” Way*](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/features/doctor/features_87_226_327.htm), on its InvestEd website at [www.InvestEd.hk](http://www.InvestEd.hk).

In this issue, Dr Wise advises a young investor on the main issues to look out for at different stages of stock trading. Dr Wise suggests that an investor should:


- consider carefully the risks associated with margin financing and stock re-pledging before opening a margin account with a broker or bank (the firm);
- understand the fee structure of the firm instead of solely relying on the eye-catching headings on its marketing materials;
- try not to place orders via the mobile phone of the firm’s account executives;
- never allow anyone else to use his/her account for trading or handle the contract notes and statements provided by the firm; and
- check the contract notes and statements and clarify any errors or irregularities noted with the firm directly.

Dr Wise is a fictitious character. He represents the collective wisdom and knowledge of market specialists and experts at the SFC. Dr Wise, through his column, talks to investors about the key issues of investing and regulatory thoughts.

Dr Wise’s Column is published on the last working Tuesday of every month. From time to time, additional articles may be published on topical issues. The column is posted under the “[Features & Dr Wise](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/features/index.htm)” section of the InvestEd website.
