Delegates from 135 jurisdictions around the world are arriving this weekend for the 31st Annual Conference of the International Organization of Securities Commissions (IOSCO). More than 650 regulators and market practitioners have registered for the four-day event starting Monday (5 June).

The delegates include 380 IOSCO members. The others include local market practitioners and other interested parties. Hosted by the Securities and Futures Commission of Hong Kong, the Annual Conference is held in Hong Kong for the first time. More than 30 delegates from the China Securities Regulatory Commission, Shanghai Stock Exchange, Shenzhen Stock Exchange and Shanghai Futures Exchange will participate.

IOSCO members will discuss regulatory issues important to the world’s securities and futures markets on the first two days. All participants of the Annual Conference may join the four public panel discussions, which will be moderated and joined by eminent regulators and market leaders during the last two days of the Annual Conference.

The Annual Conference will be officially opened on Wednesday, 7 June by the Honourable Rafael SY Hui, Acting Chief Executive of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region. Mr Hui will be joined by Mr Martin Wheatley, Chairman of the SFC and also Chairman of the Presidents’ Committee for this year’s Conference, Ms Jane Diplock, Chairperson of the IOSCO Executive Committee, Mr Michel Prada, Chairman of the IOSCO Technical Committee, Dr Doğan Cansizlar, Chairman of the IOSCO Emerging Markets Committee, and Mr Philippe Richard, Secretary General of IOSCO.
