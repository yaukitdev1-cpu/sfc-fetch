The SFC today issues a set of Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) on the InvestEd website to provide investors more guidance on distributions paid by real estate investment trusts (REITs) and the “step-up” interest rate swap arrangement that they may enter into.

Since a key consideration in choosing which REIT to invest in is the distributions provided by a REIT, investors should pay attention to information about the components of the distributions from a particular REIT as disclosed in the offering circular, results announcements and financial reports. It should be noted that distributions paid to unitholders by REITs may be derived from rental income and a return of capital, and may be affected by various factors such as revaluation of properties and “step-up” interest rate swap arrangements.

The FAQs explain in details:


- the components of distributions of REITs
- the mechanism and implications of “step up” interest rate swap arrangement
- the mechanism of distribution entitlement waiver and its implications to investors
- the impact of downward revaluation of properties in a REIT to investors

The FAQs can be found on the InvestEd website under [“Features & Dr Wise” – “Funds” – “FAQs: REITs and Others”](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/features/funds/features_82_36_297.htm).
