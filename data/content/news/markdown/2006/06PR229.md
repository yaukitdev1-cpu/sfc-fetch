Mr Eddy Fong has been appointed by the HKSAR Chief Executive as the Chairman of the SFC for a term of three years, effective from October 20, 2006. Find out about Mr Fong’s priorities in the latest issue of the [SFC Alert](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/newsletter/06/sep_oct06.pdf), which is published today.

In addition, readers will find the first group photograph of all the six Executive Directors of the SFC. Following several recent appointments, the SFC now has a full team of Executive Directors.

This issue also highlights the findings of our first survey of hedge funds. The hedge fund management market in Hong Kong has recorded remarkable growth in the past two years. The findings confirm Hong Kong’s position as a leading hedge fund management centre in Asia.

There are articles on our efforts to improve the regulatory regime and investors protection, including reforms to the prospectus regime, new guidelines on the marketing of listed structured products, and measures to minimise broker frauds.

In light of the recent increases in warrants turnover, we have issued a reminder to investors to explain the price characteristics of warrants. Our licensing matter *bulletin board* reminds prospective substantial shareholders of licensed corporations to make sure they seek the necessary SFC approval to become substantial shareholders.

The SFC Alert is a bi-monthly newsletter for both investors and intermediaries. Copies of the current issue of SFC Alert are available at the SFC office. [All issues are available on the SFC website](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/speeches/public/newsletter/newsletter.html).
