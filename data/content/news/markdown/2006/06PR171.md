The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X} for engaging in securities dealing whilst unlicensed.

An SFC investigation revealed that between 9 November 2004 and 9 March 2005, {X}, while working as a bank executive of DBS Bank (Hong Kong) Limited, accepted clients’ telephone orders to trade in securities and relayed those orders to the bank’s dealing room for execution. {X} was never licensed with the SFC or registered with the Hong Kong Monetary Authority as a relevant individual. {X}’s act was in breach of section 114 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

{X} today pleaded guilty to one summons before Mr Allan Wyeth, a Magistrate at Eastern Magistracy, and was fined $1,000.
