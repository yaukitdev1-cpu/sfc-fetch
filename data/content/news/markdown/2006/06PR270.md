The SFC will fully implement a five-day working week starting 1 January 2007.

The majority of the SFC’s operations moved to a five-day week from 1 July 2006, in line with a similar move by the Government (Note 1). However, a few services were retained on Saturday mornings e.g. manning the reception and the Investor Hotline, to gauge whether or not there was a demand for these services on Saturday mornings with a commitment to review this situation after six months.

The SFC has now reviewed these arrangements and found that the demand for services has been minimal over the past few months. The SFC believes the decision to fully implement a five-day week will have no material impact on the market and the public.

From 1 January 2007, the SFC offices on 8/F Chater House, Central, will be closed on Saturdays. Documents delivered to the SFC on Saturday mornings between 9:00 am and 1:00 pm may be dropped into a mailbox, with a time stamp facility, at 8/F Chater House, 8 Connaught Road Central.

The Investor Hotline (2840 9333) will not be operated on Saturday mornings but callers may still leave a message, which will be responded to on the following working day. Investors may also send their enquiries by post, by fax to 2524 3718, or by completing the on-line enquiry form on the SFC InvestEd website (www.InvestEd.hk).

Market practitioners have already been advised of contact numbers for SFC staff in emergency situations and measures are in place to ensure that there will be adequate staff to deal with these circumstances.
