The SFC today publishes the April issue of the SFC Enforcement Reporter, summarising the SFC’s enforcement action in the month of March 2006 and discussing the compliance issues highlighted by recent cases.

In March, the SFC successfully prosecuted six people for market manipulation, cold calling and unregistered dealing.

We disciplined 10 licensees for dishonestly signing internal documents, cold calling, failing to give reasonable advice, breaching the Code of Conduct and internal control failings. We settled with another three licensees who undertook voluntarily not to act as sponsor and principal supervisors for specified periods of time for due diligence failures.

In the year from 1 April 2005 to 31 March 2006, the SFC successfully prosecuted 72 entities (76 in 2004-2005). We offered no evidence against five entities and one was acquitted after trial.

In disciplinary proceedings, we took action against 98 companies and people in 2005-2006 for various regulatory breaches, up from 88 in the previous year. Among them we entered into settlements with no formal sanction imposed with six licensees, of which three were settled with voluntary payments; another seven licensees settled with a voluntary payment and a statutory sanction. The SFC also commenced disciplinary actions against five licensees, which were eventually concluded with no formal sanction imposed, and issued 54 private warnings.
