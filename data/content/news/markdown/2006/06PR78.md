The SFC today publishes the [April issue of SFC Alert](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/newsletter/06/mar_apr06_eng.pdf), a bi-monthly newsletter for both investors and intermediaries.

This issue reports on several major SFC initiatives in the past two months, including the publication of a strategic document setting out our responses to the regulatory challenges in the next three years, the conclusions of our consultations to enhance the derivative warrants market and to strengthen sponsors’ regulation, and new measures to reduce margin financing risks.

There are also summary findings of two investor surveys, the Retail Investor Survey and the Warrant Investor Survey.

The 31st International Organization of Securities Commissions Annual Conference will take place in Hong Kong in six weeks. Prominent speakers have been invited to attend and speak on topical issues. *Register early for IOSCO meeting* has more details.

Regular column *FAQs* tells investors not to judge an emerging market fund simply by its short-term performance. Another regular column *Investor focus* explains the difference between margin and cash accounts for securities trading and asks investors not to open a margin account if they do not plan to use the facility.

Copies of the current issue of SFC Alert are available at the SFC office. [All issues are available on the SFC website.](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/speeches/public/newsletter/newsletter.html)
