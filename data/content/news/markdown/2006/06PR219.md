The SFC welcomes the appointment by the Chief Executive of Mr Eddy Fong as the SFC Chairman.

“We warmly welcome Mr Fong as the Commission’s Chairman. Mr Fong has been a Non-Executive Director since 1 January 2005 and has participated actively in a number of our key committees including as Chairman of our Audit Committee. I have thoroughly enjoyed working with him and am delighted to be able to work with him in his new capacity,” Mr Martin Wheatley, SFC’s Chief Executive Officer, said.

“The SFC has an important role in the continuing development of Hong Kong as an international financial centre. With his extensive knowledge and experience in Hong Kong’s financial and securities market, Mr Fong will be in a position to ensure that the SFC continues to fulfil this responsibility,” Mr Wheatley said.

Mr Fong was a member of the SFC Advisory Committee between June 2003 and December 2004. As the Chairman of the Audit Committee, he is responsible for reviewing the audit findings and monitoring the effectiveness of SFC's accounting and internal controls. Mr Fong has also made valuable contributions as a member of the Remuneration Committee, Investor Compensation Fund Committee, Takeovers and Mergers Panel and other committees.

Mr Wheatley said: “Mr Fong is familiar with various aspects of the SFC’s operations both as a Non-Executive Director and through his direct involvement in the committees. We look forward to Mr Fong’s leadership of the Board in the next few years in setting the overall directions, policies, strategies, agendas and priorities of the Commission.”
