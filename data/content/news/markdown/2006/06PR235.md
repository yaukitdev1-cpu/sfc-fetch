The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X} for cold calling and aiding and abetting unlicensed leveraged foreign exchange trading (Note 1).

An SFC investigation revealed that during a meeting in May 2005, {X}, a licensed representative of Hantec International Limited, had offered to make agreements with an investor with a view to inducing him to sell or purchase leveraged foreign exchange contracts at Hantec. Prior to the meeting, the investor had never met {X} nor had he agreed or arranged to meet {X}. The meeting resulted from an unsolicited personal visit made by {X}.

In addition, during the meeting, {X} had aided and abetted his unlicensed subordinate Mr Lai Chung Wong to induce the investor to trade in leveraged foreign exchange contracts through Hantec.

On 8 June 2006, Lai was convicted of performing unlicensed leveraged foreign exchange trading (Note 2).

{X} today pleaded guilty to two summonses before Mr Winston Leung Wing Chung, a Magistrate at Eastern Magistracy. He was fined a total of $4,500 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $7,339 to the SFC.
