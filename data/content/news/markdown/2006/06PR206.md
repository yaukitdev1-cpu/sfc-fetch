The latest Dr Wise’s Column explains the major differences between the Investor Participant (IP) Account and the Stock Segregated Account (SSA) with Statement Service provided by Hong Kong Securities and Clearing Company (HKSCC).

The SFC today publishes the September issue of Dr Wise’s Column, [*Investor Participant Account and Stock Segregated Account*](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/features/doctor/features_87_226_335.htm), on its InvestEd website at www.InvestEd.hk.

Investors have full control of stocks kept in their IP accounts as only they themselves can authorise the transfer of stocks out of their accounts. For SSA, investors’ stocks remain under the full control of their brokers, but they will receive an account balance statement from HKSCC when there is any change in the stock balance. Investors may consider opening both an IP account and an SSA, and allocate stocks between the two accounts according to their investment objectives.
