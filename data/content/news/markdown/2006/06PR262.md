The latest Dr Wise’s Column reminds investors to manage their risks and money properly.

The SFC today publishes the December issue of Dr Wise’s Column, [*Mah-jong and Investment*](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/features/doctor/features_87_226_338.htm), on its InvestEd website at www.InvestEd.hk.

Similar to applying skills to playing mah-jong, investors should always think carefully when deciding what and when to buy. They then need to manage their risks and decide when to sell.

Investors should not get carried away by the initial public offering euphoria and over-commit to stocks that they would not otherwise want to own for the long term. For “out-of-the-money” warrants which are a wasting asset with a finite time limit, investors need to consider getting out and minimising their losses. If they wait, hoping for a miracle turnaround, they risk their warrants expiring worthless.

Dr Wise is a fictitious character. Through his column, he talks to investors about the key issues of investing and regulatory thoughts.

Dr Wise’s Column is published on the last working Tuesday of every month. From time to time, additional articles in the form of special issues may be published on topical matters. The column is posted under the [“Features & Dr Wise”](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/features/index.htm) section of the InvestEd website. Investors are welcome to send their feedback and questions to Dr Wise by email to [InvestEd@sfc.hk](mailto:%20InvestEd@sfc.hk).
