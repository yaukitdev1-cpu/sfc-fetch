SFC Chairman, Mr Martin Wheatley, today called for international efforts to promote best standards and regulatory co-operation in the increasingly globalised securities market.

The standards for securities regulation advocated by IOSCO and the Financial Sector Assessment Programme (FSAP) jointly sponsored by the World Bank and the International Monetary Fund are two major initiatives to deal with challenges faced by the securities markets, he said.

Speaking at a seminar organised by the World Bank and the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (Note 1), Mr Wheatley said regulators needed to set, and encourage compliance with, global standards in regulation, accounting, corporate governance, transparency, and other areas that are critical to the sustained and healthy growth of securities markets.

He noted international securities regulators recognised the importance of the IOSCO Objectives and Principles of Securities Regulation and regulatory co-operation. An important resolution in enforcement co-operation was adopted at the IOSCO Annual Conference in Hong Kong this month: the resolution on asset freezing encourages IOSCO members to examine the legal framework under which they operate and to enable the freezing of assets derived from cross-border securities and derivatives violations.

“In this way, those who break the laws of the securities sector will be unable to benefit from any gains made as a result of their illegal actions,” Mr Wheatley said.

Meanwhile, FSAP gives participating countries a comprehensive assessment of their financial systems in a bid to reduce vulnerabilities and improve the effective functioning of the system.

Mr Wheatley said that regulators around the world had begun to adopt regulatory measures to address the potential adverse market impact which hedge funds could bring about with their use of leverage and aggressive trading style.

The SFC would maintain regular dialogues with the industry, including the major prime brokers, to better understand hedge fund activities, he said.

“As the global network becomes more integrated and extends its coverage, implementing global best standards and practices and promoting regulatory cooperation becomes key to ensuring market integrity and investor confidence. We have already embarked on this course through the work of various international organisations. I believe that over time, we can have a global market that is fair, efficient and transparent to all,” he said.
