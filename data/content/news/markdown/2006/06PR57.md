The SFC is pleased to announce the appointment of three new members to the SFC (HKEC Listing) Committee.

The SFC welcomes the appointment of Ms Teresa Ko, a partner at Freshfields Bruckhaus Deringer, and Mr Michael Lee, Managing Director of Hysan Development Company Limited, and the re-appointment of Mr Stephen Clark, Mr Carlson Tong and Mr V-Nee Yeh as market representatives. The appointments and re-appointments of the market representatives are for a term of one year from 1 April 2006 to 31 March 2007. Mr Keith Lui, SFC’s Senior Director of the Supervision of Markets Division, has been appointed as a new SFC representative.

The SFC wishes to thank Mr Thaddeus Beczak and Ms Patricia Shih for their valuable contributions to the Committee. They have been market representatives on the Committee since June 2000 and their tenures end today.

The Committee was established pursuant to section 8 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance, in connection with the listing of Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited (HKEx) in June 2000. It exercises powers and functions equivalent to those of the Main Board and GEM Listing Committees of The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited when real or potential conflicts of interests between the interests of HKEx and those of the proper performance of the Stock Exchange’s listing functions make it desirable for the relevant Stock Exchange functions to be exercised by the SFC.

A full list of the members of the Committee is provided in Note 1.

The SFC very much appreciates the efforts of the members in performing the functions of the Committee effectively and transparently. The SFC is confident that with their expertise and significant experience in the financial and investment sector, the members will continue to make valuable contributions to the Committee.
