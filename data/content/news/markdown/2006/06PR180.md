The SFC successfully prosecuted two people in July. Both cases concerned leveraged foreign exchange trading.

In the first prosecution, a person was convicted of cold calling. Cold calling seems a widespread phenomenon in some sections of the futures and leveraged foreign exchange trading industry, according to the August issue of the SFC Enforcement Reporter published today. Cold calling is a criminal offence, and licensed companies that tolerate it can expect tough disciplinary action. The second prosecution concerned unlicensed leveraged foreign exchange trading.

Further, in July, the SFC disciplined six licensees for various breaches, including banning an ex-licensee from re-entering the industry for six months for facilitating cold calling.

Apart from the prosecutions and disciplinary actions, the SFC issued a restriction notice against a broker, Tiffit Securities (Hong Kong) Limited.
