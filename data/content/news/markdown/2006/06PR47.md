The SFC today publishes a special Dr Wise issue, [*Danger in Jumping on the Bandwagon*](http://www.invested.hk/invested/html/EN/features/doctor/features_87_226_328.htm), on its InvestEd website at www.InvestEd.hk.

In this issue, Dr Wise notes with concern many recent media reports relating to listed Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs) and potential REITs. Quoting sources, some reports provided details on the particulars of the assets, their valuations and timing for the authorisation of the REITs, while others talked about future business plans, and acquisition and disposal potential of currently listed REITs. These comments have engendered market speculation.

Dr Wise reminds investors that many of the reports contained unconfirmed information, while others might concern purely preliminary plans which are subject to uncertainties. He also points out that REIT initial public offerings and many other transactions relating to REITs are subject to prior regulatory approvals and unitholders’ approvals. The REIT manager has to obtain a licence from the SFC. The SFC only grants licences to managers and authorises REITs that satisfy the SFC’s requirements. Therefore, investors should always exercise caution in dealing in the shares of listed companies or units of listed REITs. They should check official announcements and carefully assess the possible implications of any reported or announced plans for the listed companies or REITs concerned. Dr Wise has set out a few key issues that are useful in evaluating such reports or plans.
