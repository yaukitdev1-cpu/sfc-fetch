The SFC is pleased that its Annual Report 2005-06 for the year ended 31 March 2006 has been given the Platinum Award in the Public Sector/Not-for-profit Organisations Category in the Hong Kong Institute of Certified Public Accountants’ Best Corporate Governance Disclosure Awards 2006.

SFC’s Chief Operating Officer, Mr Paul Kennedy, received the award today. Mr Kennedy said that the SFC had a key role to play in ensuring that companies and their directors fully embraced the principles of good corporate governance, which it believed were a fundamental investor expectation from a leading international financial centre.

He said whilst corporate governance was an indispensable pillar of corporate social responsibility, it was also important that all organisations, profit making or not, build a socially responsible culture in their business behaviour.
