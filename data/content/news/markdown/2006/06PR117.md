Profits of stock exchange participant brokers in the first quarter of 2006 had jumped three times from the previous quarter, a review in latest issue of the [*SFC Quarterly Bulletin*](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/speeches/public/bulletin/sfc_bulletin/spring_06.htm) shows.

Total net profits of these stockbrokers in January to March 2006 amounted to $3.8 billion, as compared to $1.2 billion for October to December 2005. This was on the back of a 49% increase in their total turnover. The total operating income has increased by 40% to $9.7 billion while the total expenses of stockbrokers remained at a similar level of $5.9 billionlast quarter.

All categories of stockbrokers recorded a strong growth in turnover, with increases ranging from 37% to 62% in the first quarter of 2006. In addition, net profits for the period reported by brokers significantly increased by 2 times for Category A brokers, 1 time for Category B brokers and 4 times for Category C brokersrespectively.

The *Financial Review of the Securities Industry* is one of the feature articles in the Spring 2006 issue (No.63) of the *SFC Quarterly Bulletin*, which is published today.

Another article, *Review of the Securities Market,* reviews the performance of major overseas markets during the first quarter of 2006. In Hong Kong, the HSI rose 6.2% on improved earnings. H-shares and red chips outperformed, underpinned by the expectations about implementation of the QDII scheme. The HSCEI and HSCCI advanced 25.8% and 12.9% respectively during the quarter.
