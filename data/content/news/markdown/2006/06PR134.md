The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X} for making unsolicited calls.

An SFC investigation found that in August 2004, {X}, a licensed representative of Hantec International Limited during or as a consequence of unsolicited calls, had offered to make an agreement with a person with a view to inducing the person to sell or purchase leveraged foreign exchange contracts at Hantec, contrary to section 174 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

{X} today pleaded guilty to one summons before Mr Leung Wing Chung Winston, a Magistrate at Eastern Magistracy. {X} was fined $3,000 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $3,545 to the SFC.
