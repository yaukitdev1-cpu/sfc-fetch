The SFC and Radio Television Hong Kong (RTHK) have jointly produced a new Cantonese television drama series entitled “Investment Challenge”. This is the SFC’s latest investor education initiative to meet its regulatory challenge of tackling risks arising from complex and structured products, and mis-selling to retail investors (Note 1).

Investment Challenge comprises 10 five-minute episodes. They talk about the features and the risks of a wide range of investment products including equity-linked instruments, managed funds, bonds, warrants, futures, investment-linked assurance schemes, real estate investment trusts and leveraged foreign exchange contracts (Note 2).

All the 10 episodes are related to the interesting investment experience of the driver and the passengers of a public mini-bus. The cast includes popular actors and actresses, such as Sam Lee, who acts as a punter; Monie Tung, a conservative investor; Viann Leung, a rational investor; Berg Ng, the driver; and Marx Cheung, a herd-instinct investor. Interviews with market professionals are intercut into the drama to explain the topics.

Today, Mr Peter Au-Yang, SFC’s Executive Director and Chief Operating Officer, and Mr Forever Sze, Controller of RTHK’s Educational Television Division, kicked off the ceremony by starting the engine of the mini-bus used in the dramas. Artistes Ms Ella Koon, Ms Jade Kwan and Ms Lily Hong shared their personal investment experience. Mr Shek Kang Chuen, Associate Publisher of Hong Kong Economic Times, and Mr Andrew Fung, market practitioner, gave their valuable advice on investment strategies. The ceremony was open to the public. Through quiz and games, the public could better understand the various kinds of investment products and some had obtained an SFC souvenir.

At the launch ceremony, Mr Au-Yang said: “Alternative investment products are different from stocks. Each product has its unique features, thus, before investing, it is important for investors to do their homework and evaluate whether the product meets their financial needs.”

“The SFC expects licensed investment advisers to assess the risk profiles of their clients and give them suitable product recommendations. At the same time, investors also have a duty to consider whether the recommended products are within their risk tolerance and fit their investment goals, ” Mr Au-Yang added.

Investment Challenge will be aired on 10 consecutive Sundays starting 14 May 2006, at 11 am on ATV Home and 3:25 pm on TVB Jade. There will be a simultaneous webcast of the programme on RTHK’s website at [http://tv.rthk.org.hk](http://tv.rthk.org.hk) and on the designated webpage in RTHK’s Education Television website at [www.eTVonline.tv](http://www.eTVonline.tv). The series will also be re-screened at 8:55 pm on Financial Channel of Cable TV starting 2 July 2006 for 10 consecutive Sundays.

On-line game

To enhance public interest in the programme, an on-line game will be launched at [www.eTVonline.tv](http://www.eTVonline.tv). For 10 consecutive weeks, after the broadcast of each episode, a question referencing to the content of the episode will be posted on the website. The first 10 participants who score the highest marks out of the 10 questions will each receive a USB memory stick from the RTHK. The deadline for participation in the game is 22 July 2006.
