The SFC today publishes the [June issue of SFC Alert](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/newsletter/06/may_jun06_eng.pdf), a bi-monthly newsletter for both investors and intermediaries.

This issue reports on several major SFC initiatives in the past two months.

The 31st IOSCO Annual Conference was successfully held in Hong Kong with significant accomplishments. Over 650 regulators and market practitioners from 135 jurisdictions around the world attended the Conference.

The column *Bulletin board – licensing matters* reminds responsible officers matters they should note for dual accreditation applications. We also remind intermediaries to take account of their anniversary dates in deciding the cessation of their regulated activities. Regular column *FAQs* explains whether boiler room scam is the same as cold calling.

Starting 1 July, the majority of SFC’s operations will move to a five-day week, while essential services such as the Investor Hotline and receipt of documents will remain available on Saturdays.

Copies of the [current issue of SFC Alert](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/doc/EN/speeches/public/newsletter/06/may_jun06_eng.pdf) are available at the SFC office. [All issues are available on the SFC website](http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/speeches/public/newsletter/newsletter.html).
