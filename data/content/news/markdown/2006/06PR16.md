The SFC has successfully prosecuted {X} for aiding and abetting a person to engage in unlicensed leveraged foreign exchange trading and also for failing to provide SFC investigators with assistance in connection with an investigation.

An SFC investigation revealed that between March and July 2004,{X}, a sales manager of Hong Kong Forex Investment Limited (Note 1), had aided and abetted a sales trainee, who was not licensed with the SFC, to solicit people with a view to opening accounts with Hong Kong Forex to conduct leveraged foreign exchange trading. At all material times, {X} was the supervisor of the sales trainee. {X} breached section 114 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance. The sales trainee was convicted earlier for engaging in unlicensed leveraged foreign exchange trading (Note 2).

It was also found that {X}, without reasonable excuse, had failed to give SFC investigators assistance in connection with the investigation, in breach of section 184 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance.

{X} today pleaded guilty to two summonses before Magistrate Mr Winston Leung at Eastern Magistracy. {X} was fined $10,000 and ordered to pay investigation costs of $5,000 to the SFC.
