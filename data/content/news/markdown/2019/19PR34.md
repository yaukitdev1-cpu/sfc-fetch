The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the appointments and re-appointments by the Financial Secretary of members to the SFC Advisory Committee for two years from 1 June 2019 to 31 May 2021.


"I warmly welcome the five new members whom I trust will bring to the SFC valuable insights on matters related to the SFC&rsquo;s policy and market regulation.  I also look forward to the continued support and advice of the seven re-appointed members," said Mr Tim Lui, the SFC&rsquo;s Chairman.


"I would also like to express my sincere gratitude to the five outgoing members for their contribution to the SFC during their term," Mr Lui added.
