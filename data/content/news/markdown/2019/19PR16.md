The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the appointment by the Financial Secretary of Mr Nicky Lo Kar-chun and Mr Clement Chan Kam-wing as Non-Executive Directors for a term of two years (Note 1). 


"I would like to extend a warm welcome to both Mr Lo and Mr Chan who bring to the SFC a wealth of experience and expertise and I look forward to working with them in the Board," said Mr Tim Lui, the SFC&rsquo;s chairman.


"I also would like to express our gratitude to outgoing Non-Executive Directors Ms Mary Ma Xuezheng and Mr Albert Au Siu-cheung for their invaluable contribution to the SFC&rsquo;s development during their tenures," Mr Lui added (Note 2).


End


Notes:



- The terms of Mr Lo and Mr Chan will begin on 24 April 2019 and 26 May 2019, respectively.

- The terms of Ms Ma and Mr Au will end on 23 April 2019 and 25 May 2019, respectively.
