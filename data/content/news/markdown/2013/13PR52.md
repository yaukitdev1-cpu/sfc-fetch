The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) welcomes the re-appointment by the Financial Secretary of Mr Wong Kai-man as a Non-Executive Director for a term of two years effective from 26 May 2013.


"Mr Wong has contributed significantly to the work of the SFC in the past four years. I look forward to working closely with him in his next term to advance the regulatory agenda of the SFC," said Mr Carlson Tong, the SFC&rsquo;s Chairman.


Mr Wong was first appointed as a Non-Executive Director of the SFC in May 2009.
