The second series of law-enforcement themed TV drama SFC in Action will start airing next month, highlighting the importance of vigorous enforcement actions by the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) in tackling market misconduct for the protection of investors.


Produced jointly with Radio Television Hong Kong (RTHK), SFC in Action II will be broadcast on TVB Jade and simultaneously on [RTHK&rsquo;s website](http://rthk.hk/index_eng.htm#3) on 2 April.  



The TV series is a fictional representation of the SFC&rsquo;s work spanning six episodes, with each featuring a standalone story dealing with a key strategic enforcement priority including, insider dealing, market manipulation, mis-selling by intermediaries, corporate governance involving IPO listings and sponsors, and unlicensed dealing (Note 1).


"SFC in Action II showcases some of the issues and challenges our teams face day-to-day in enforcing our rules and protecting investors. We hope the series will help educate the public about issues they can watch out for. The exciting stories show the pressures our staff are dealing with every day. At the end of the day, our investigators, who nail the wrongdoers, are the real heroes of the series and this is a valuable message on its own,&rdquo; said Mr Mark Steward, the SFC&rsquo;s Executive Director of Enforcement.
