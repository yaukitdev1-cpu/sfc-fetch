The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) is pleased to announce new appointments and re-appointments to the following committees with effect from 1 April 2013:



- Academic and Accreditation Advisory Committee

- Committee on Real Estate Investment Trusts

- Nominations Committee 

- Public Shareholders Group                              

- SFC (HKEC Listing) Committee

- Share Registrars&rsquo; Disciplinary Committee

- Takeovers and Mergers Panel

- Takeovers Appeal Committee


The SFC welcomes new committee members whose significant expertise and experience will contribute to the effective functioning of the respective committees. It is also grateful to outgoing members for their services to the committees over the years.
