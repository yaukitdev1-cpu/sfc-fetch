Circular to Licensed Corporations
which are Participants of The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited
Compliance with the Securities and Futures (Insurance) Rules
Following our circular of 31 March 2003 regarding the Securities and Futures (Insurance)
Rules, you should have by now received the debit note from Jardine Lloyd Thompson
Limited (“JLT”) for payment of your insurance premium covering the period from 1 April
2003 to 31 March 2004 inclusive (unless you have formally notified us of your cessation of
securities dealing business effective before 1 April 2003). Please promptly respond to the
debit note by paying your insurance premium to JLT without delay if you have not
already done so.
As set out in our circular of 31 March 2003, you are obliged to take out and maintain
insurance under the approved master policy of insurance. This obligation is a statutory
licensing condition which you must comply with as a licensed corporation under the
Securities and Futures Ordinance (“SFO”). Failure to do so will result in your breaching the
licensing condition. This may render you subject to possible disciplinary actions for
misconduct.
Your obligation to be insured became effective on 1 April 2003 upon the implementation of
the SFO, and you have been covered under the master insurance policy as from that date.
Therefore, you are required to pay the insurance premium even though you may have notified
us of your cessation of securities dealing business after 1 April 2003. Similar to the
arrangements under the previous Brokers’ Fidelity Insurance Scheme of the Stock Exchange
of Hong Kong Limited, there will be no refund of insurance premium for participants exiting
the industry during the insurance period.
