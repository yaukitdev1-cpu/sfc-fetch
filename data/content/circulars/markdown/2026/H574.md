Circular to Licensed Corporations
Effect of the cessation of compilation of the Hang Seng Hong Kong LargeCap Index
(“HSHKLCI”) and Hang Seng Hong Kong MidCap Index (“HSHKMCI”)
The Hang Seng Indexes Company Limited has recently ceased to compile the HSHKLCI and
HSHKMCI. Following the cessation of compilation of the HSHKLCI and HSHKMCI, the
following provisions of the Securities and Futures (Financial Resources) Rules ("FRR") have
ceased to be effective:
-

Items 1(a) & (b) of Table 1 of Schedule 2 to the FRR and items 1(a) & (b) of
Table 1A of Schedule 2 to the FRR, which apply 15% and 20% haircut
percentages respectively to constituent stocks of the HSHKLCI and HSHKMCI
for the purposes of calculating haircut amounts for such stocks under the
FRR;

-

Sections 22(4)(d)(ii) &(iii) of the FRR, which exclude constituent stocks of the
HSHKLCI and HSHKMCI from the definition of the meaning of illiquid
collateral,

and the previous constituent stocks of the now abolished HSHKLCI and HSHKMCI will be
subject to such other applicable haircut percentage or adjustment as set out in other
provisions of the FRR.
If as a result of the abolition of the HSHKLCI and HSHKMCI you have any question about
compliance with the FRR, you should immediately contact your case officer in the
Intermediaries Supervision Department of the Commission.
