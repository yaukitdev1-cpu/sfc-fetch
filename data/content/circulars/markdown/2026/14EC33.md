Circular to All Licensed Corporations
Financial Stability Board’s Advisory Notice to Financial Institutions concerning
Venezuela
The Financial Stability Board (FSB) issued an advisory notice on 19 June 2014 regarding the
determination of Venezuela as a non-cooperative jurisdiction with respect to regulatory and
supervisory standards on international cooperation and information exchange.
The advisory notice can be found on the FSB’s website:
http://www.financialstabilityboard.org/press/pr_140616.htm
Licensed corporations should take the notice into account in conducting business.
Should you have any questions regarding the contents of this circular, please contact Ms
Madonna Yip at 2231 1618.
