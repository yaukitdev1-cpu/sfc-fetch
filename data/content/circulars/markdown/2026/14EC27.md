Circular

2 May 2014

Circular to Licensed Corporations in relation to the reporting of OTC
derivatives transactions to the HKMA trade repository – Launch of the HKMA
trade repository website

The Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) announced that the new website for the overthe-counter derivatives trade repository of the HKMA (HKTR) is launched today.
The new HKTR website provides operational information necessary for registered HKTR
members to conduct reporting activities as well as registration information for those who wish
to join as HKTR member.
Attached with this Circular is the letter from HKMA regarding the above announcement.
Should you have any queries regarding the above, please contact the HKMA at
hktr@hkma.gov.hk.
