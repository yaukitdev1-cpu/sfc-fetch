Circular to all Licensed and Registered Persons relating to Licensing Forms
This circular is issued to inform all licensed and registered persons concerning the
introduction of revised licensing forms.

Licensing Forms
The enactment of the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) (“CO”), which will come into force on
3 March 2014, has given rise to a need for the revision of six of the Commission’s licensing
forms1. The changes made to the six licensing forms principally arise out of changes
introduced into Hong Kong law under the CO concerning the share capital of Hong Kong
companies.
On 21 February 2014, the Commission, by notices published in the Government of the Hong
Kong Special Administrative Region Gazette under section 402 of the Securities and Futures
Ordinance (Cap.571) (G.N. 973 and G.N. 974), specified the six revised licensing forms, both
in paper form and as separately published by electronic means on the SFC Online
Portal. The revised paper forms are now available on the Commission’s website and the
revised electronic forms will be available on the SFC Online Portal from 3 March 2014. With
effect from 3 March 2014, the revised licensing forms (both paper and electronic) will
supersede the previous versions of the same forms and will be required to be used for the
purpose of submitting licensing applications and notifications to the Commission. You may
download blank forms or print out completed forms from the Commission’s website at
http://www.sfc.hk/web/EN/forms/intermediaries/licensing-application-and-notification-forms/.
The Commission prefers and encourages the use of the SFC Online Portal
(http://www.sfc.hk/web/EN/regulatory-functions/intermediaries/licensing/online-portal.html) for
the submission of licensing applications and notifications.

Reminder
Pursuant to the CO, any amount standing to the credit of a company’s share premium
account and capital redemption reserve becomes part of the company’s share capital. An
overview of the CO can be found on the website of the Companies Registry:
http://www.cr.gov.hk/en/companies_ordinance/overview.htm.
You are reminded that if there is any change in your share capital as a result of the
implementation of the CO, you must notify the Commission within 7 days after the change
takes place by completing and submitting a Form 5 or by making such notification
electronically via the SFC Online Portal.
