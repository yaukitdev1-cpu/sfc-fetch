Circular to Licensed Corporations
Anti-bribery Guidance
Under paragraph 2.4 of the Code of Conduct1, intermediaries are expected to be “familiar
with the Prevention of Bribery Ordinance (Cap. 201) and follow related guidance issued by
the Independent Commission Against Corruption (“ICAC”)”.
Pursuant to a request by the ICAC, the Commission brings to the attention of all licensed
corporations a “Sample Code of Conduct” issued by the ICAC which a licensed corporation
may adopt or make reference to when implementing its internal anti-bribery policy.
Should any licensed corporation like to have a copy of the “Sample Code of Conduct”
relevant to licensed corporations or have any enquiries, please contact the advisory services
of the ICAC direct on 2526 6363 or by sending an email to asg@cpd.icac.org.hk.
Should you have any queries regarding the contents of this circular, please contact Ms
Kammy Kwok at 2231 1455.
