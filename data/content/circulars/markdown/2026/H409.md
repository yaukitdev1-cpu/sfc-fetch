Frequently Asked Questions on Financial Return
Form 12 – Aggregate net value of assets under management
The SFC publishes on its website today a set of Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) on the
reporting requirement under Form 12 – Part B of the financial return.
Under section 56 of the Securities and Futures (Financial Resources) Rules, licensed
corporations which are licensed for Type 9 regulated activity are required to submit an
“analysis of the assets under its management” (the “ AUM Analysis”) quarterly (or semiannually for those who are subjected to the specified licensing condition) by completing
Form 12 – Part B of the financial return.
In the past, the Commission has received enquiries from licensed corporations on how to
complete the AUM Analysis. It has also come to our attention that some licensed
corporations have not completed, or have failed to correctly fill in, the AUM Analysis for
funds/portfolios under their management.
In this connection, we have prepared a set of FAQs that are designed to assist in
understanding the reporting requirements in respect of the AUM Analysis. We would also
like to take this opportunity to remind licensed corporations of their obligation to ensure that
financial returns are fully completed and correctly compiled before submission.
For your ease of reference, a copy of the FAQs is attached as Appendix I. The e-version of
the FAQs is available for download on the SFC website at http://www.sfc.hk under the
section “Intermediaries, Licensing & Investment Products - Intermediaries Supervision
Related Matters - FAQs - Financial Return”. The FAQs will be updated regularly and you are
encouraged to check the website for new information.

Appendix I
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FINANCIAL RETURN FORM 12 – PART B)
1. What are “authorised unit trusts and mutual funds”? Do they include Real Estate
Investment Trusts authorised by the SFC?
“Authorised unit trusts and mutual funds” are those unit trusts and mutual funds which
have been authorised by the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) under Part IV of
the Securities and Futures Ordinance. These include Real Estate Investment Trusts
authorised by the SFC.
2. Should the value of funds/portfolios be reported under Part B of Form 12 if a licensed
corporation has delegated its asset management function of the funds/portfolios to
other parties?
Yes. This is irrespective of whether the asset management function is delegated to group
companies or third parties.
3. If a licensed corporation has been delegated an asset management function, does it
have to report the value of assets in respect of which the asset management function
has been delegated to it under Part B of Form 12?
Yes.
