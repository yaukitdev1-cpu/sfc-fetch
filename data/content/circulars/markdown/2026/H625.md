Circular to Licensed Corporations
2011 edition of ELI booklet is now available for distribution
The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has added the latest edition of
“Equity-linked Investments” booklet to its list of investor publications.
The new booklet discusses the major features and risks of unlisted equity-linked
investments (ELIs) and uses numerical examples to illustrate the calculation of return
of different types of ELIs, including ELIs with special features.
We cordially invite the industry to help distribute this booklet to the investing public, as
well as other publications, of which a full list is given at the Annex. The electronic
versions of these publications are available on the “Publications” section of the SFC
investor portal, the InvestEd website, at www.InvestEd.hk.
Please contact Crystal Lau at 2840 9549 for the number of copies required.

Annex

SFC investor publications
1. Equity-linked Investments (2011 edition) - discusses the major features and
risks of unlisted equity-linked investments (ELIs) and uses numerical examples to
illustrate the calculation of return of different types of ELIs, including ELIs with
special features
2. Index Tracking ETFs - Know the Risks (2010 edition) - highlights the features
and associated risks, including counterparty risk, of synthetic exchange-traded
funds (ETFs)
3. How to Read Product Key Facts Statements - explains how to use the user
friendly summary to understand key features and risks of an investment product
4. Unlisted Structured Investment Products - Post-sale cooling-off
arrangement - discusses the features and mechanism of the post-sale cooling-off
arrangement for applicable unlisted structured investment products
5. Guaranteed Funds - Know the limitations - explains the key features and
associated risks of guaranteed funds
6. InvestEd website - Highlights the hot topics and features of the SFC’s InvestEd
website for investors
7. IPO Investing - explains what investors should note when subscribing for shares
in initial public offerings(IPOs) and the risks involved
8. Know Your Broker - remind investors the key factors to consider in choosing a
broker and the steps that they should take to protect their interests
9. How to Make a Complaint - explains to investors where they can channel
complaints when they encounter suspected malpractice in the securities and
futures markets, and the SFC’s procedures and powers in handling public
complaints
10. The SFC Guide - explains how the securities and the futures markets are
regulated and the SFC's roles in the overall regulatory structure
