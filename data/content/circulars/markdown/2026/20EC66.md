Circular to intermediaries – Workshops to introduce changes to the SFC online portal
The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) will organise workshops to introduce WINGS
2.0, the revamped version of its online portal. WINGS 2.0 is designed to help improve the
efficiency and transparency of the SFC’s licensing processes, which will eventually become
paperless.
During an initial phase beginning in December 2020, licence applicants, licensees and
registrants, as well as their professional advisors, will be able to activate or create an
account on WINGS 2.0. Users will then have the opportunity to familiarise themselves with
new features such as web-based licensing forms and a means of signing licensing
applications electronically.
Interested parties are invited to attend one of the introductory workshops via Zoom.
