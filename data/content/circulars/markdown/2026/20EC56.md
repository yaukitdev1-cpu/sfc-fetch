Circular

11 September 2020

Circular on implementation of changes to the open-ended fund companies
regime
1.

This circular sets out further details on the implementation of changes to the openended fund companies (OFC) regime, which came into effect today.
Implementation of changes to the OFC regime

2. The SFC released the consultation conclusions on enhancements to the OFC regime
on 2 September 2020.
3. The amendments to the Code on Open-ended Fund Companies (OFC Code) to
implement the changes were gazetted today and became immediately effective. The
revised OFC Code is available on the SFC website.
Transition period for existing private OFC custodians
4. To enable existing private OFC custodians to ensure compliance with the requirements
prescribed in the new Appendix A to the OFC Code, these custodians will be given a
six-month transition period from today to 10 March 2021 to comply.
Updated Information Checklists, Template of Instrument of Incorporation for
Umbrella Private OFC and FAQs
5. To implement and provide further guidance in relation to the enhancements to the OFC
regime, the following documents have been updated and published on the SFC website:

an updated Information Checklist for Application for Authorization of Unit
Trusts and Mutual Funds under the Revamped Process;

(b)

an updated Information Checklist for (i) a Change of Director of a Public
Open-ended Fund Company; or (ii) a Change of Name of a Public Openended Fund Company or a Publicly Offered Sub-fund of an Open-ended
Fund Company;

(c)

an updated Information Checklist for Application for Registration of a
Private Open-ended Fund Company or Establishment of a Privately offered
Sub-fund of an Open-ended Fund Company;

(d)

an updated Information Checklist for Application for Approval of
Appointment of Director, Custodian or Investment Manager of a Private
Open-ended Fund Company;

(e)

an updated Template of Instrument of Incorporation for Umbrella Private
