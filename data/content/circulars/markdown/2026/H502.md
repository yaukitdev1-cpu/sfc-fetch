Circular to management companies / product issuers of
SFC-authorized schemes
Consultation conclusions on the proposed streamlining of
the pre-vetting of notices and advertisements of relevant authorized
collective investment schemes
The Commission has released the Consultation Conclusions1 today setting out the
streamlining measures adopted regarding the pre-vetting of notices of collective investment
schemes authorized by the Commission under the Product Codes2 and their advertisements
that are exempted under section 103 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO).
The revisions to the Product Codes and the revised Advertising Guidelines3 will be published
in the Government Gazette and their effective date will be no later than 1 August 2008 (the
“Effective Date”) and the Commission will then concurrently cease pre-vetting eligible notices
and advertisements. Advertisement issuers are permitted to continue to follow the current
Advertising Guidelines included in the respective Product Codes until 31 December 2008.
Partial adoption of provisions in the current and revised Advertising Guidelines will not be
permitted. All advertisement issuers shall adopt and comply with the revised Advertising
Guidelines starting from 1 January 2009.
Pursuant to section 104(4) of the SFO, the Commission will issue a notice to the approved
persons of relevant collective investment schemes approved under section 104(3) of the
SFO informing them of the amendments to the authorization conditions for such schemes as
a result of the adoption of the streamlined approach.
The Commission will post, prior to the Effective Date, a set of Frequently Asked Questions
on Advertising Materials of Authorized Schemes on the website to give examples on the
practical applications of the revised Advertising Guidelines.
Management companies / product issuers are advised to inform the relevant distributors
regarding the above revisions to the codes and guidelines.
The Consultation Conclusions are available on our website at www.sfc.hk.
