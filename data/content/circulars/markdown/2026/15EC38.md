Circular

10 July 2015

Circular to Licensed Corporations in relation to the reporting and record
keeping rules for the new OTC derivatives regime
This is to remind all Licensed Corporations that the Securities and Futures (OTC Derivative
Transactions – Reporting and Record Keeping Obligations) Rules (Reporting Rules) has
come into effect today.
To help market participants better understand how the Reporting Rules operate, the Hong
Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) and the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) have
prepared a set of frequently asked questions (FAQs) that provide additional information
about the mandatory reporting and record keeping obligations under the new regime. A draft
of the FAQs was previously circulated (see circular dated 23 June 2015). The finalized FAQs
can be accessed at: http://www.sfc.hk/web/EN/faqs/otc-derivatives/.
Additionally, the HKMA has today issued the attached circular concerning the directions and
instructions for use of the HKMA’s Electronic Reporting System and the submission of
transaction information under the Reporting Rules. These directions and instructions can be
accessed at: http://hktr.hkma.gov.hk/.
Licensed Corporations whose activities may render them subject to the mandatory reporting
obligation are advised to refer to the FAQs, and to the aforesaid directions and instructions,
to ensure compliance with their reporting and record keeping obligations.
