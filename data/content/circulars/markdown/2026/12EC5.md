Circular

11 September 2012

Circular to Licensed Corporations in relation to the reporting of OTC
derivatives transactions to the HKMA trade repository – Matching and
Confirmation Service for OTC derivatives transactions

As previously announced, the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) is developing a trade
repository (HKTR) for market participants to report their OTC derivatives transactions. At the
same time, Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited (HKEx) is establishing a central
counterparty (CCP) clearing facility for clearing OTC derivatives transactions. A link will be
established between the HKTR and the HKEx CCP for passing eligible OTC derivatives
transactions to the CCP for clearing.
The HKMA has today announced that it intends to start in December 2012 to offer the
“Matching and Confirmation Service” for market participants in respect of their OTC
derivatives transactions, and the transmission of these transactions to the HKEx CCP for
voluntary clearing.
The circular issued by HKMA regarding the above announcement, as well as the
implementation timeline, reference manual and technical specifications, are attached to this
circular. Licensed Corporations, who are interested to make use of the Matching and
Confirmation Service mentioned above, are advised to properly prepare for the
implementation. The HKMA will organise two briefing sessions on 18 September 2012 to
provide more information. Interested parties should bring along identity document and the
circular issued by HKMA to join either of the two briefing sessions on that day.
Should you have any queries regarding the above, please contact the HKMA at
hktr@hkma.gov.hk.
