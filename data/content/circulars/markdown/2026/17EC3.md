13 January 2017

Circular to Licensed Corporations in relation to the reporting of OTC
derivatives transactions to the HKMA trade repository – Revised fee charge
scheme of trade repository to be effective on 1 July 2017

Since the establishment of the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) trade repository
(HKTR) in July 2013, the HKTR has been supporting reporting entities to fulfil their
mandatory reporting obligations as required under different phases of the implementation of
the Securities and Future (OTC Derivatives Transactions - Reporting and Record Keeping
Obligations) Rules (the “Reporting Rules”).
The HKTR seeks to recover the recurrent operating costs for providing the day-to-day
reporting services from the HKTR participants, whereas the HKMA has absorbed the
substantial capital investments required for developing the HKTR systems and linkage. In a
recent review by the HKMA on the operating costs of the HKTR, it was noted that the
recurrent costs for ongoing maintenance and system enhancements for meeting the evolving
regulatory reporting requirements, international reporting standards and technology
advancements and increasing support to the industry have been increasing significantly
during the past years. In order to recover the increasing costs, the HKMA announced today
that while keeping unchanged the fee of HK$4.5 per outstanding transaction at each month
end, the fees cap of HK$1.5 million per reporting entity per annum will be removed for all
HKTR participants effective from 1 July 2017. The billing process and payment methods will
remain unchanged.
Attached to this Circular is the letter issued by the HKMA.
Should you have any queries regarding the above, please contact the HKMA at
hktr@hkma.gov.hk.
