Circular to all Intermediaries and Licensed Individuals
relating to Printed Licences
This circular is issued to inform all intermediaries and licensed individuals that the Securities
and Futures Commission (SFC) will no longer issue printed licences to individuals licensed
under the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO).
The Securities and Futures (Amendment) Ordinance 2015, which comes into force today,
has dispensed with the issuing of printed licences to licensed individuals. Any printed licence
issued to an individual by the SFC ceases to be effective for indicating that the individual is
licensed.
However, the SFC will continue to issue printed licences and certificates of registration
(respectively) for licensed corporations and registered institutions. Moreover, these
intermediaries are still required to exhibit their printed licences or certificates of registration in
a prominent place at their places of business.
Licensing information about licensed persons and registered institutions will continue to be
published on the SFC’s online Public Register of Licensed Persons and Registered
Institutions.
Reminder
Licensed individuals are encouraged to return their printed licences to the SFC as soon as
practicable.
