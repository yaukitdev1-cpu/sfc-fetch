Circular to intermediaries
Updates on HKEX market rehearsals for its backbone network upgrade for
securities market systems
Reference is made to the Securities and Futures Commission’s (SFC) Circular on 26
February 2026 regarding the Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited’s (HKEX) market
rehearsals (MRs) for its backbone network upgrade for securities market systems.
HKEX will arrange two mandatory MRs in the second quarter of 2026. Subsequent to these,
further rehearsals will be scheduled from the late second quarter to the early third quarter,
with details to be announced in due course. These rehearsals aim to validate connectivity by
simulating daily operations over the enhanced HKEX backbone network for securities market
systems.
All Relevant Regulated Intermediaries (RRIs) 1 which use the Secure File Transfer Protocol
(SFTP) channel to submit the BCAN-CID Mapping File and Reporting Forms to the Stock
Exchange of Hong Kong Limited’s data repository, as required under the Hong Kong Investor
Identification Regime, are invited to participate in these rehearsals.
The two rehearsals are mandatory for Exchange Participants (EPs) and China Connect
Exchange Participants. All other RRIs (ie, non-EPs) are strongly encouraged to participate in
these rehearsals to ensure operational readiness and facilitate a seamless transition.
For the avoidance of doubt, only RRIs which are EPs should submit the Notification Form via
HKEX’s Electronic Communication Platform by 27 March 2026 (Friday). No enrolment will
be required for non-EP RRIs.
RRIs are advised to refer to HKEX’s Circular dated 23 March 2026 for further information and
guidance, and remain alert to subsequent communications from both the SFC and HKEX
regarding this initiative.
Supervision of Markets Division
Securities and Futures Commission

1

RRIs refer to SFC-licensed corporations and SFC-registered institutions which are subject to the Hong Kong Investor
