Circular to all licensed corporations on consultation conclusions and
further consultation on proposed changes to
the Securities and Futures (Financial Resources) Rules
The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) today published consultation conclusions on
proposed changes to the Securities and Futures (Financial Resources) Rules (FRR).
The SFC concludes the July 2015 consultation on the proposed regulatory capital regime for
licensed corporations engaged in over-the-counter derivatives activities together with certain
changes relating to other areas of the FRR, which include the May 2011 proposal to update
the list of specified exchanges in the FRR.
In summary, the following proposals will be implemented subject to certain modifications:

the minimum capital requirements for licensed corporations engaging in different types of
over-the-counter derivatives activities, for example, a HK$1 billion tangible capital
requirement and a HK$156 million liquid capital requirement for over-the-counter

million liquid capital requirement for over-the-counter derivatives clearing activity (Note
1);
the proposed frameworks to address market risk and counterparty credit risk of licensed
corporations, which comprise two approaches of different levels of complexity for each
type of risk;
an internal models approach to calculate the capital requirements for market risk and
counterparty credit risk of licensed corporations;
the measures to address the operational risk of licensed corporations engaging in certain
types of regulated over-the-counter derivatives activities or opting into the more
advanced market risk or counterparty credit risk approaches;
the notification and reporting requirements relating to over-the-counter derivatives
activities; and
miscellaneous technical changes to other areas of the FRR, such as lowering the haircut
percentages for shares listed on certain Mainland stock exchanges and allowing certain
amounts receivable from a general clearing participant or a clearing client relating to
third-party clearing to be included as liquid assets.

The SFC also seeks to further consult on a number of modified and additional FRR
proposals, such as reducing the minimum capital requirements for fund managers’ central
dealing desks which meet certain conditions, adding four Mainland commodity exchanges to
the list of specified exchanges in the FRR, recognising credit ratings issued by Fitch Ratings
and extending the transitional period for full compliance with the new FRR requirements by
licensed corporations engaging in certain types of regulated over-the-counter derivatives
activities from six months to one year. This consultation also covers a set of draft FRR
amendments for implementing those proposals which are not specific to over-the-counter
derivatives activities.
