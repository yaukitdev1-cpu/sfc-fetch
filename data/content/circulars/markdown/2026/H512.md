Circular to Licensed Corporations
Application for SFC e-Certificate and
Training of Electronic Submission of Financial Returns
This is the second 1 circular the Commission has issued to licensed corporations on
electronic submission of financial returns which will become mandatory on 1 November
2008.
Application for SFC e-Certificate
The Commission now invites all relevant persons 2 who intend to sign and submit the e-FRR
return by using SFC e-Certificate to make their application in the application periods stated
as follows:

29 Sep 08 ~ 24 Oct 08

Two SFC e-Certificates will be issued to each licensed corporation but licensed corporations
may request for more if needed.
Application should be made by completing the attached application form (Annex 1), which
can also be downloaded from the Commission’s website 3 and returning the same to the
Commission by hand or by post.
A valid SFC e-Certificate will be needed for participation in the industry testing.
Training of Electronic Submission of Financial Returns
Training of the electronic submission of e-FRR returns using SFC e-Certificate will be held in
late August 2008 for persons required to submit returns on a monthly basis and October
2008 for persons required to submit returns on a half-yearly basis. The training schedule is
enclosed for your reference (Annex 2). Those of you who intend to attend the training
should complete the attached form (Annex 3) and return it to our e-FRR System Helpdesk
by e-mail e-frrhelp@sfc.hk or by fax (852) 2293 5824 to make the reservation.

Notes 備註
4.

The term “SFC e-Certificate” refers to an electronic signature key file secured by a password (or PIN) and stored
in a USB storage token. It is issued by a certification authority, on the instructions of the SFC, for the purpose of
electronic signing of an e-FRR return and authentication of the person who signs and submits an e-FRR return.
“證監會電子證書＂一詞，指根據證監會指示的核證機構所發出並以密碼（或私人密碼）保密和儲存在 USB 記憶體
內的電子簽署匙檔案。電子證書旨在利便以電子方式簽署電子申報表及鑑定簽署和遞交電子申報表人士的身分。

5.

每家持牌法團一般會獲發兩張證監會電子證書。

Section D 丁部 – Terms and Conditions 條款及條件
1.

The SFC e-Certificate shall only be used for the purpose of submitting e-FRR returns to the Securities
and Futures Commission and for no other purpose.
證監會電子證書只能用作向證券及期貨事務監察委員會遞交電子申報表之用，及不得用作其他用途。

2.

The SFC may, at its absolute discretion, revoke any SFC e-Certificate(s) without notice.
證監會可行使絕對酌情權撤銷證監會電子證書，而無須作出任何通知。

3.

The Applicant will be strictly responsible for all e-FRR return(s) submitted and signed by using his/her
SFC e-Certificate(s) unless he/she has notified SFC about any loss/compromise of the SFC
e-Certificate(s).
除非申請人已將遺失證監會電子證書／證監會電子證書資料外洩一事通知證監會，否則他將須對使用其
證監會電子證書簽署及遞交的所有電子申報表負上全部責任。

4.

The Applicant shall notify the SFC immediately upon the occurrence of the following:
loss of the SFC e-Certificate;
compromise or suspected compromise of the SFC e-Certificate; or
failure of the protection of the SFC e-Certificate, or suspected failure of the protection.
如發生下列事件，申請人須即時通知證監會：
遺失證監會電子證書；
證監會電子證書資料外洩／懷疑外洩；或
未有保護，或懷疑未有保護證監會電子證書。

5.

The Applicant shall follow the SFC’s directions and instructions in using the SFC e-Certificate.
申請人在使用證監會電子證書時，須遵照證監會的指令及指示。

6.

These Terms and Conditions shall also apply to any renewal or re-issuance of SFC e-Certificate(s).
本條款及條件同樣適用於證監會電子證書的續期或補領。

7.

These Terms and Conditions shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the
Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.
本條款及條件須受香港特別行政區的法例管限及按照該等法例詮釋。
