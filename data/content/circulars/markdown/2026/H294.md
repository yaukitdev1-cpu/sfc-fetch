Circular to Licensed Corporations and Associated Entities
Proper segregation and safeguarding of client money
This circular is issued for the purpose of reminding Licensed Corporations and Associated
Entities of their obligations in relation to client money received or held in Hong Kong. The
term "client money" is defined in Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the Securities and Futures
Ordinance (Cap. 571) ("SFO").
Licensed Corporations and, to a lesser extent, Associated Entities may receive client money
in a variety of circumstances, for example, when a client settles the purchase consideration of
securities or when securities are sold on behalf of a client or when dividends are received on
a client’s behalf.
Licensed Corporations and Associated Entities are required to hold and deal with client
money in the manner prescribed by the Securities and Futures (Client Money) Rules ("the
Rules"). Licensed Corporations and Associated Entities are reminded that section 4(4) of the
Rules requires them within 1 business day of receiving client money to pay it (a) into a
segregated account, (b) to the client, or (c) in accordance with the written direction or
standing authority of the client. There are no other options. Any client money held in a
segregated account must be retained there until paid out in accordance with section 5 of the
Rules (a) to the client, (b) in accordance with the written direction or standing authority of the
client or (c) to meet amounts owed by the client to the Licensed Corporation or the
Associated Entity. Failure to observe sections 4 and 5 of the Rules, without reasonable excuse
or with intent to defraud, is a criminal offence punishable on conviction with imprisonment
and a fine. Other provisions of the Rules also carry criminal penalties under section 12 of the
Rules.
In the interval between receiving client money and dealing with it in accordance with section
4(4) of the Rules, Licensed Corporations and Associated Entities are reminded that they hold
the client money as trustee and have a fiduciary duty to the client to safeguard the client
money and to hold or use it only in accordance with the client's instructions. Failure to
honour such fiduciary duty may expose a Licensed Corporation or Associated Entity to civil
suit and liability to pay damages to the client.

It follows that Licensed Corporations and Associated Entities should not use client money
during this interval for their own purposes, such as settling or reducing overdraft facilities,
settling purchases of house investments or lending to a related corporation. Any such use of
client money is likely to constitute a breach of fiduciary duty and is contrary to General
Principle 8 of the Code of Conduct for persons licensed by or registered with the Securities
and Futures Commission. General Principle 8 provides that a licensed person should ensure
that client assets are promptly and properly accounted for and adequately safeguarded. If a
Licensed Corporation uses client money for its own purposes, its fitness and properness will
be impugned, which may result in disciplinary proceedings under the SFO.
Licensed Corporations and Associated Entities are reminded to establish and maintain
adequate internal controls to ensure that all client money is adequately safeguarded and to
prevent unauthorized and illegal use of client money.
