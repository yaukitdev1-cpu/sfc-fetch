Financial Resources (Amendment) Rules 2002
The SFC issued a circular dated 13 June 2002 to all registered securities dealers and
securities margin financiers drawing their attention to the two new Financial
Resources Rules requirements, i.e. illiquid collateral haircut and gearing adjustment
which will come into effect on 1 October 2002.
The SFC wish to remind all registered securities margin financiers and securities
dealers providing securities margin financing again that they should establish by now
necessary information technology and access to market data for the purposes of the
calculations required under the above new requirements.
All registered securities dealer and securities margin financier are welcome to visit
our website at http://www.hksfc.org.hk to review a briefing summary on the Financial
Resources (Amendment) Rules 2002 and an illustration example.
Please note that the two new requirements are interim measures to address the
identified risks in the securities margin financing business. In the event that a
registered intermediary faces genuine difficulties in complying with the new
requirements when they come into effect, the SFC will consider granting modification
of the new requirements where appropriate provided that the interest of the investing
public is not compromised.
The monthly FRR return form has been revised to incorporate the amendments
required under section 4 of the Financial Resources (Amendment) Rules 2002.
Registered dealers and securities margin financiers should continue to use the current
return forms for accounting period up to 30 September 2002. The revised form shall
be used for accounting period starting from 1 October 2002.
The revised form is available for collection at SFC office and available for download
from the SFC website and FinNet.
If you have any further questions regarding the new requirements, please contact your
case officer in the Intermediaries Supervision Department.

Illiquid collateral haircut (section 13(4) of FRR)
the margin shortfall amount, calculated as the amount by

which the amount receivable exceeds the aggregate of –
(i) the market value of collateral, other than illiquid collateral,
provided by the client, less the applicable haircut amount and
multiplied by the applicable concentration discounting
factor*;
(ia) the market value of all illiquid collateral provided by the
client, multiplied by 20%;
(ii) the amount of cash deposited as security by the client; and
(iii) in the case of a dealer, the maximum amount that can be
drawn by him under a bank guarantee which is provided by
the client to him and which is issued by an authorized
institution or an approved bank formed or established outside
Hong Kong.
*

It will be acceptable to exclude illiquid collateral from related
stock for CDF calculation
2

Illiquid collateral
Any listed or traded share or warrant which is identified as the
top 3 collateral where the aggregate market value of shares or
warrants comprising the collateral is equal to or greater than –
(a) the average monthly turnover of that share or warrant; or
(b) in the case of a share, 5% of the total market capitalization of
that share as at the end of the month immediately preceding the
month prior to the month in which the calculation is made; or
(c) in the case of a warrant, 5% of the warrant issue as at the end of
the month immediately preceding the month prior to the month
in which the calculation is made,
3

Illiquid collateral (cont’d)
but does not include –
(i)

any listed or traded share or warrant which has been listed
or traded for less than 6 consecutive months (including
any period during which the share or warrant is
suspended from trading) immediately preceding the
month prior to the month in which the calculation is
made;

(ii) any listed share which is a constituent stock of any of the
following indices –
(A) Hang Seng Index;
(B) Hang Seng Hong Kong LargeCap Index;
(C) Hang Seng Hong Kong MidCap Index;
(D) FTSE-100 Index;
(E) Nikkei 225 Index; or
(F) Standard & Poor’s 500 Index.
4

Top 3 collateral
Any collateral (listed or traded shares or warrants)
which is identified as being the 3 highest in terms of
market value given by each of the 20 margin clients
with the largest outstanding margin loan balances
with the dealer or securities margin financier.

Gearing adjustment (section 21(2) of FRR)
Where a dealer or securities margin financier obtains financial
accommodation wholly or partly secured by collateral
provided by a margin client, he shall include in his ranking
liabilities the amount by which such borrowings exceed 65%
of the aggregate amount receivable from his margin clients
arising from the provision of securities margin financing.
