Circular to Management Companies of
SFC-authorised Real Estate Investment Trusts (“REITs”)
Clarification on the application of various
Listing Rules amendments to SFC-authorised REITs

Background
1.

2.

The SFC notes that the Exchange has recently announced various amendments to the
Listing Rules (the “Listing Rules Amendments”):
(a)

to implement the proposals outlined in the “Combined Consultation Paper on
Proposed Changes to the Listing Rules” in respect of the 15 issues, subject to
certain modifications in the “Consultation Conclusions on Proposals in the 2008
Combined Consultation Paper” and its news release dated 12 February 2009;
and

(b)

to shorten the current deadlines for the release of half-year and annual results
announcements by one month for all listed issuers.

The SFC hereby clarifies the application of these Listing Rules Amendments to SFCauthorised REITs.

Listing Rules Amendments
3.

The SFC in general seeks to regulate REITs, to the extent appropriate and practicable,
with reference to the requirements applicable to listed companies under the Listing
Rules (as amended from time to time), except otherwise provided in the Code on
REITs or the guidelines issued by the SFC from time to time.

4.

In view of the obligation to adhere to good corporate governance principles and best
industry standards under General Principle 6 of the Code on REITs for all activities and
transactions conducted in relation to the REIT and matters arising out of its listing or
trading on Exchange, managers of SFC-authorised REITs are expected to comply with
the enhanced corporate governance practices and disclosure requirements under the
revised Listing Rules, with necessary changes, as if they were applicable to REITs.

5.

In particular, REIT managers are expected to comply with:
•
•
•

the extended “black-out” period for directors’ dealings
the notice periods for general meetings
the accelerated reporting deadlines for annual results announcements covering
annual accounting periods ending on or after 31 December 2010

REIT managers are expected to comply with the extended black-out period for annual
results announcements covering accounting periods ending on or after 31 March 2009
provided that the earliest date on which such extended black-out period would start is 1
April 2009. REIT managers are also expected to comply with the other enhanced
corporate governance practices and disclosure requirements.

7.

REIT managers should also note that the Listing Rules Amendments in relation to
disclosure of changes in number of units will be applicable to SFC-authorised REITs as
a result of the amendments to Listing Agreement applicable to collective investment
schemes set out in Appendix 7G to the Listing Rules.

8.

REIT managers should review and update their compliance manuals and/or other
relevant documents accordingly as soon as practicable.

9.

REIT managers are welcome to contact the relevant case officers should they have
any questions on the above.
