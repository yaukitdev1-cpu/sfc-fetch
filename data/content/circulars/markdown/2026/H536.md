Circular

22 January 2009

Circular to issuers of advertisements relating to SFC-authorised
Collective Investment Schemes
Reminder of deadline for submission of extension applications
for compliance with Revised Advertising Guidelines
Reference is made to the circular to Issuers dated 30 December 2008 announcing the
phased application of the Revised Advertising Guidelines to existing marketing materials
(“December Circular”). Terms defined in the December Circular shall have the same
meanings when used herein unless the context otherwise requires.
The SFC reminds Issuers of existing marketing materials in reliance on the phased
application that the deadline for making an Application to seek an extension of time for
compliance with the Revised Advertising Guidelines is 6 February 2009. Issuers should
refer to the December Circular for further details.
Issuers are welcome to approach and discuss with the relevant case officer if they have any
doubt as to their obligations.
