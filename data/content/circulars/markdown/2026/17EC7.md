Circular

20 January 2017

SFC issues guideline to sponsors, underwriters and placing agents involved in
the listing and placing of GEM stocks
This circular is to inform intermediaries that the SFC has issued today a guideline to
sponsors, underwriters and placing agents1 involved in the listing and placing of GEM2 stocks
(Guideline).
The Guideline, which is published under section 399(1) of the Securities and Futures
Ordinance, is designed to provide guidance to underwriters and placing agents on the
standards of conduct that is expected of them when conducting GEM IPO3 placings. The
Guideline also extends to sponsors given their obligation to declare to the Exchange4 that
new applicants seeking to list on the GEM have complied with the relevant GEM Listing
Rules5.
A failure to comply with any of the requirements of the Guideline by a sponsor, underwriter or
placing agent (and their representatives) may reflect adversely on its fitness and properness
and may result in disciplinary action. The SFC will adopt a pragmatic approach taking into
account each firm’s particular circumstances when assessing a sponsor’s, underwriter’s or
placing agent’s compliance with this Guideline.
A copy of the Guideline is attached to this circular for your information. The e-version of the
guideline is available for download on the SFC website at www.sfc.hk under the section
“Rules & standards – Codes & Guidelines – Guidelines – Guideline to sponsors, underwriters
and placing agents involved in the listing and placing of GEM stocks”.
