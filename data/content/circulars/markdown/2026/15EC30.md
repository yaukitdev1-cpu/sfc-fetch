Circular

18 May 2015

Circular to Licensed Corporations in relation to the reporting of OTC
derivatives transactions to the HKMA trade repository – Training and testing
for commencement of the new OTC derivatives regime

We refer to the public consultation conclusions jointly published by the Hong Kong Monetary
Authority (HKMA) and Securities and Futures Commission on 15 May 2015 on the
mandatory reporting and record keeping obligations under the over-the-counter (OTC)
derivatives regime. The published document indicated that subject to the legislative process,
the obligations under the regime for regulated entities, including Licensed Corporations, are
targeted to commence in the second week of July 2015.
To prepare for the commencement of the said obligations, the HKMA announced that a
series of training and simulation tests will be organised for the regulated entities. Attached to
this Circular are the letter and enrolment form issued by the HKMA, which also include the
schedule for the training and simulation tests to be held in the third and fourth quarters of
2015.
Licensed Corporations which have activities that may be subject to mandatory reporting
obligations are advised to sign up for the reporting service of the HKTR and complete the
simulation test as early as possible. However, those who had already signed up and
completed a simulation test before are not required to do the same again.
Should you have any queries regarding the above, please contact the HKMA at
hktr@hkma.gov.hk.
