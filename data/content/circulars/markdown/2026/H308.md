(1)

Non-cooperative Countries and Territories (“NCCTs”)
The Financial Action Task Force on Money Laundering (“FATF”) regularly updates
its list of NCCTs. At its recent plenary meeting, it has decided to remove Ukraine and
Egypt from the list of NCCTs. The updated list of the remaining 7 NCCTs is as
follows:
Cook Islands
Myanmar
Philippines

Guatemala
Nauru

Indonesia
Nigeria

Licensed corporations and associated entities are reminded to give special attention to
businesses and transactions with persons, including companies and financial
institutions, in the above NCCTs, in accordance with Recommendation 21Note 1 of the
FATF Forty Recommendations.
The FATF will regularly review the NCCT list. Changes to the list, if any, will be
conveyed to licensed corporations and associated entities through circulars in due
course. Further information on the FATF work in respect of NCCTs is also available
on the website of FATF at www.fatf-gafi.org.

Note 1
Recommendation 21: Financial institutions should give special attention to business relationships and
transactions with persons, including companies and financial institutions, from countries which do not or
insufficiently apply the FATF Forty Recommendations.
Whenever these transactions have no apparent
economic or visible lawful purpose, their background and purpose should, as far as possible, be examined, the
findings established in writing, and be available to help competent authorities. Where such a country continues
not to apply or insufficiently applies the FATF Recommendations, countries should be able to apply appropriate
countermeasures.

The SFC reminds licensed corporations and associated entities to take all necessary
steps to ensure compliance with the Regulation. Besides reporting to the JFIU of any
transactions or relationships you have or have had with the named individuals and
entities, you should refrain from, amongst other things, providing or collecting, by
any means, directly or indirectly, funds to the named individuals and entities pursuant
to the Regulation. Previous circulars on this subject are available in SFC’s website
www.hksfc.org.hk.

in this circular regularly for the latest information as the designated names are updated from
