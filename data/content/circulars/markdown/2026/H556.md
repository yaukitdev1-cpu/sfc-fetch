(1)

FATF Statement on Iran, Uzbekistan, Turkmenistan, Pakistan and São Tomé and
Príncipe
Further to our circular issued on 31 March 2009, this is to inform you that the Financial
Action Task Force on Money Laundering (“FATF”) issued an updated statement on 26
June 2009 concerning the anti-money laundering/combating the financing of terrorism
(“AML/CFT”) regimes in Iran, Uzbekistan, Turkmenistan, Pakistan and São Tomé and
Príncipe which are subject to periodic review by the FATF. The statement can be
found on the website of FATF (http://www.fatfgafi.org/document/15/0,3343,en_32250379_32236836_43193871_1_1_1_1,00.html).
In the statement, the FATF reaffirms its call on members and urges all jurisdictions to
apply effective counter-measures to protect their financial sectors from the money
laundering and terrorist financing risks emanating from Iran. The FATF statement also
notes the progress made by Uzbekistan, Turkmenistan, Pakistan and São Tomé and
Príncipe in addressing the deficiencies in their AML/CFT systems. Nevertheless, as
the identified deficiencies have not been fully addressed, the FATF remains concerned
about the risks to the integrity of the international financial system posed by these
jurisdictions.
Licensed corporations (“LCs”) and associated entities (“AEs”) should continue to apply
the measures specified in our circular of 31 March 2009 when handling transactions
related to these jurisdictions. As the FATF will continue to assess the progress made
by these jurisdictions in addressing the deficiencies in their AML/CFT systems and
issue updated statement from time to time, LCs and AEs are reminded to browse the
website of the FATF for the latest information.
