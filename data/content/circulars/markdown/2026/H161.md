Circular to All Securities Dealers, Securities Margin Financiers
and Exempt Dealers
Obtaining Clients’ Standing Authority
under the Securities and Futures (Client Securities) Rules
The Securities and Futures Ordinance (the “SFO”) will come into effect on 1 April
2003 and the relevant existing legislation, such as the Securities Ordinance, will be
repealed on the same day.
Under the new regime, all legal requirements governing dealings with client securities
and securities collateral will be set out in the Securities and Futures (Client Securities)
Rules (the “Rules”).
If you wish to treat client securities or securities collateral in the manner envisaged in
sections 7 to 9 of the Rules, and the standing authorities that you may have obtained
from your clients under the existing law do not satisfy the requirements of section 4
of the Rules, you should obtain new standing authorities on or before 1 April 2003
with the scope of the authority limited to the treatment specified in section 7, 8 or 9 as
applicable.
As this process is likely to take time, you are urged to start preparing now.
We would like to draw your attention to the following relevant changes in the Rules
as compared to section 81, 81A and 121AB of the existing Securities Ordinance:
(a)

the standing authority must specify the validity period (limited to 12 months
unless the client in question is a professional investor) and the manner in
which it may be revoked (please refer to section 4(1) and (2));

(b)

“deemed” renewal of standing authority is now accepted (please refer to
section 4(3)); and

(c)

the scope of the authority has been slightly modified for securities dealers.
