Circular to all Licensed and Registered Persons
Practical Tips for Using the SFC Online Portal
Mandatory online submission of licensing applications, notifications and annual returns in
relation to individuals was implemented on 1 February 2017. Updated paper forms and
supplements for corporate applicants and intermediaries also took effect on the same day.
To assist the industry in making submissions online, the Commission has issued “Practical
Tips for Using the SFC Online Portal”:
http://www.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/circular/openAppendix?refNo=17EC16&app
endix=0
The Commission has also published a user guide for corporate applicants and intermediaries
on the updated paper licensing forms and supplements which are generally required for
some common corporate applications and notifications. Please refer to the “Quick User
Guide on Licensing Forms (paper versions)” for details:
http://www.sfc.hk/web/EN/files/LIC/form/Quick%20user%20guide_11012017final.pdf
