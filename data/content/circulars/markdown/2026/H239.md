Securities and Futures (Insurance) Rules
The insurance requirements
As you are aware, the Securities and Futures (Insurance) Rules (“the Rules”) were gazetted
on 17 January 2003. These Rules, which were made under section 116(5) of the Securities
and Futures Ordinance (“SFO”), set out the obligations of certain licensed corporations to
take out and maintain insurance in relation to the specified risks for not less than the
specified amount. Pursuant to section 118(1)(a)(i)(B) of the SFO, maintaining the required
insurance is one of the conditions of a licence granted under section 116(1) for carrying on a
regulated activity.
As far as the participants of The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited (“SEHK”) are
concerned, section 4(2) of the Rules requires that the SEHK Participants shall take out and
maintain insurance under a master policy of insurance approved by the SFC if the SFC has
approved such a master policy under section 5(2)(a) of the Rules. In that regard, please be
informed that the SFC will approve a master policy of insurance under section 5(2)(a) of the
Rules for the period from 1 April 2003 to 31 March 2004. This master policy will cover the
SEHK Participants in respect of their regulated activity of dealing in securities during that
insurance period.
Arrangement of the new insurance scheme
The proposed master policy was arranged by an Industry Working Group (“IWG”) which is
chaired by the Honorable Henry KC Wu and comprises representatives of various industry
associations and other market participants. In addition, an insurance broker company,

and administrator of the new insurance scheme.

Risk coverage and indemnity level
Basically, the risk coverage (except for risk relating to errors and omissions) and indemnity
level of the new insurance policy will follow the Brokers’ Fidelity Insurance (“BFI”) Scheme
currently arranged by the SEHK for its participants. In essence, it will indemnify the insured
licensees against fidelity risks (e.g. loss of client assets due to theft by employees or other
fraudulent acts) in relation to their securities dealing activities subject to an insurance limit
and a deductible of $15 million and $3 million respectively.
Effective date
As mentioned above, the master insurance policy will become effective on 1 April 2003, and
hence the related insurance requirements will be applicable to the SEHK Participants on the
same date. This commencement date will tie in with the expiry of the current BFI Scheme on
31 March 2003.
Premium allocation
Your share of premium under the new insurance scheme will be calculated based on an
equitable formula similar to that adopted under the BFI Scheme. For each insured licensee, it
is required to pay a basic minimum premium of $16,000 plus a variable premium according
to its turnover1 in the previous calendar year as a proportion of the total turnover of all
scheme participants. Furthermore, as decided by the IWG, an additional premium loading
will apply on those insured firms which have any paid claims in the past 5 years under the
BFI Scheme. This claims loading mechanism will continue to operate in the future with
reference to claims paid out under the new insurance scheme.
The claims loading will be computed as a percentage (listed below) of the premium amount
that the insured licensee would have been required to pay had there been no claim(s) paid to
it before. For each claim paid out in the past 5 years, the following diminishing loading
factors on the premium will apply:• 30% in the 1st year after the paid claim
• 24% in the 2nd year after the paid claim
• 18% in the 3rd year after the paid claim
• 12% in the 4th year after the paid claim
• 6% in the 5th year after the paid claim

Administration of the new insurance scheme
As the scheme administrator, JLT will be responsible for issuing formal documentation, debit
notes to the participants in early April 2003. They will also assist with other administration
issues including claims matters. However, please note that formal notifications of claims or
potential circumstances that could give rise to claims under the new insurance scheme should
be made to Federal Insurance Company. This procedure is the same as that under the current
BFI Scheme.
