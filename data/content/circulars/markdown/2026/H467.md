25 July 2007

Circular to Licensed Corporations
Investor Leaflet “Fund Investing”

We wish to invite you to display and distribute to your clients our new investor leaflet, “Fund
Investing”.
“Fund Investing” provides a checklist of questions for investors to ask before buying a fund.
Both the English and Chinese versions of the leaflet are available.
“Fund Investing” is the latest publication of the SFC’s new investor leaflet series for this
year, following three earlier issues entitled How to Make a Complaint, Know Your Broker
and IPO Investing.
To allow investors to check the availability of the above leaflets, the names and addresses of
the licensed corporations distributing these leaflets will be posted on the “Publications”
section of the SFC-operated investor portal, the InvestEd website at www.InvestEd.hk.
Please contact Ms Gloria Chan at 2842 7711 concerning requests for copies of the leaflets.
