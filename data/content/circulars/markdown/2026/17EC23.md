Circular to Licensed Corporations
Cybersecurity Workshops with the Hong Kong Police Force and the Hong
Kong Computer Emergency Response Team Coordination Centre

In our continued efforts to increase the cybersecurity awareness of the industry, the SFC will
co-host two separate cybersecurity workshops with the Hong Kong Police Force (“Police”)
and the Hong Kong Computer Emergency Response Team Coordination Centre of the Hong
Kong Productivity Council (“HKCERT”) on 25 and 26 May 2017 respectively. Both
workshops will be delivered in Cantonese when the presentation materials will be in English.
(1) Cybersecurity workshop co-hosted with Police on 25 May 2017 (“SFC/Police
workshop”)
This workshop will cover the following cybersecurity topics, among others:
 Key lessons learnt from unauthorized internet trading incidents
 Cybercrime prevention tips
 Cybersecurity baseline controls on protection of clients’ internet trading accounts,
infrastructure security management, cybersecurity management and supervision.
It will last for approximately three hours, ie, 9:00 a.m. – 12:00 p.m., and the venue is 8/F,
Auditorium, Police Headquarters, 1 Arsenal Street, Wanchai.
(2) Cybersecurity workshop co-hosted with HKCERT on 26 May 2017 (“SFC/HKCERT
workshop”)
This workshop will cover the following cybersecurity topics, among others:
 Towards “Cyber-Safe” Financial Services
 Cybersecurity baseline controls on protection of clients’ internet trading accounts,
infrastructure security management, cybersecurity management and supervision.
It will last for approximately two and a half hours, ie, 2:30 p.m. – 5:00 p.m., and the venue is
9/F, Auditorium, the Hong Kong Federation of Youth Groups Building, 21 Pak Fuk Road,
North Point.
Who should attend
If your firm presently offers clients the use of internet trading systems or is planning to do so
in the near future, you are invited to nominate relevant management / supervisory personnel,
compliance and/or IT officers to attend. Both workshops are free of charge.
Enrolment
Each firm is allowed to enroll for both workshops by submitting one enrolment form.
