Circular to Licensed Corporations and Associated Entities
Anti-Money Laundering Training Seminar
Money laundering1 has become a global concern and actions to combat against the threats
posed by money laundering are continuing to increase. Failure to observe the anti-money
laundering (“AML”) requirements and combat money laundering could pose serious
consequences to you. These could result in disciplinary actions or even criminal actions. The
reputation of your firm may also be severely tarnished and as a result adversely affect your
business.
To protect your firm against the threat of money laundering and to ensure compliance with
the relevant AML requirements, you should adopt a more pro-active approach against money
laundering. In the coming AML seminar, you could learn more about the money laundering
typologies and your AML obligations under the laws and regulatory requirements. Senior
management are strongly urged to attend the seminars.
The AML training seminar is a joint effort of the Narcotics Division, the Joint Financial
Intelligence Unit, the Department of Justice and the SFC. The English and Chinese sessions
of the seminar will be held on 12 and 20 June 2007 respectively. The invitation letter,
programme and enrolment form for the seminar are attached herewith.
The seminar is free of charge and 3 CPT hours will be offered to those who attend the whole
seminar. Enrolments will close on 30 May 2007 and are processed on a first-come-first-serve
basis. The SFC will confirm with the admitted participants by e-mail or fax. Please present
your confirmation slip at the reception counter for admission.
