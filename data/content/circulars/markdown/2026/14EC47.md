Circular to Licensed Corporations and Associated Entities
Anti-Money Laundering / Counter-Terrorist Financing
Seminar Materials
The PowerPoint slides of the presentation (in both English and Chinese versions) that SFC
staff made at the recent Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing (“AML/CFT”)
Seminars Note 1 have been posted on SFC’s website Note 2 under the heading “Training materials Presented by SFC staff”.
In addition, the presentation materials used by other speakers at the aforesaid AML/CFT
seminars have been posted on SFC’s website Note 2 under the heading “Training materials Presented by external parties”.
Licensed corporations and associated entities are encouraged to download the presentation
materials for reference and internal training as appropriate.
Should you have any queries regarding the contents of this circular, please contact Ms Kiki
Wong on 2231 1569.
