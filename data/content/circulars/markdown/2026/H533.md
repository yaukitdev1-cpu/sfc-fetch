Circular to Licensed Corporations
Mandatory E-Submission for Persons
who are required to sign and submit half-yearly return
This is the Sixth 1 Circular the SFC has issued to licensed corporations on electronic
submission of financial returns.
Mandatory e-submission
Persons who are required to sign and submit return on a half-yearly basis should submit the
returns for the period ending 31 December 2008 electronically on or before 21 January 2009.
You can access the e-FRR System on Internet through the hyperlink at SFC website
http://www.sfc.hk/ to conduct your e-submission of FRR returns via Internet using SFC eCertificate.
If you wish to use a SFC e-Certificate for the e-submission but your Responsible Officers
(“RO”) have not applied for any such certificate, you should arrange for your ROs to make
such application immediately. Please refer to the Second Circular for the details of SFC eCertificate application.
Good practices before submission
Your attention is drawn to the following before submitting your returns.
1.

Save a copy of the latest financial return template downloadable from the Internet
website at http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/forms/supervision/return/return.html and use
the saved return in your local computer for entering your data;

2.

After filling in all the data, use the built-in validation function by pressing the "Validate
This Sheet" buttons at the bottom of each form of the return to identify potential
mistakes for rectification before submission;

3.

Check the data carefully to ensure they are accurately and completely entered. For
instance, make sure that
(a) the licensing status stated on the "Form 1"; and
(b) the boxes ticked against "Forms to be submitted" in the "Declaration"
correctly reflect your current business activities and are consistent with the information
provided in the rest of the return;
