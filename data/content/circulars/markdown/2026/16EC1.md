12 January 2016

Circular to applicants of SFC-authorized mandatory provident fund products
related to Default Investment Strategy (“DIS”)

1.

This circular is supplemental to the SFC’s circular on “Application lapse policy”
issued on 9 October 2015 (“Oct 2015 Circular”). The purpose of this circular is to
provide guidance to applicants seeking the SFC’s authorization of new constituent
funds (“CFs”) of mandatory provident fund schemes (“MPF Schemes”) and pooled
investment funds (“PIFs”) related to DIS (“DIS-related applications”) that are subject
to the 6-month application lapse policy and the concurrent review by the MPFA and
SFC with reference to the Oct 2015 Circular. Capitalised terms shall have the same
meaning as defined in the Oct 2015 Circular unless otherwise provided.

Background
2.

Subject to the passage of the Mandatory Provident Fund Schemes (Amendment)
Bill 2015 at the Legislative Council, in view of the proposed requirement for the
implementation of DIS in MPF Schemes within 2016, new CFs and PIFs to be used
as building blocks for DIS subject to MPFA’s approval and SFC’s authorization are
required to be set up for such purpose.

3.

After consulting with the MPFA which is the primary regulator of DIS, arrangements
in relation to the SFC’s review and authorization processes of DIS-related
applications are set out below in order to facilitate the authorization process.

Prerequisite for making DIS-related applications
4.

As set out in the Oct 2015 Circular, the approval-in-principle letter issued by the
MPFA (“AIP”) is a prerequisite for making applications for new MPF products
(including MPF Schemes, CFs and PIFs) to the SFC and it must be submitted to the
SFC at the time of application.

5.

Given the tight time frame, DIS-related applications are to be reviewed by the MPFA
and the SFC concurrently. Hence, in respect of the DIS-related applications,
applicants are not required to obtain MPFA’s AIP for DIS-related application on the
condition that written confirmations from the MPFA (i) of its acceptance of the
application; and (ii) that the application is related to DIS, must be submitted by the
applicant to the SFC at the time of formal application.

6.

Notwithstanding the above, SFC reserves the right to refuse a new DIS-related
application which is deemed to be an incomplete application and/or the application
fails to meet the applicable regulatory requirements.

7.

Except for the arrangements referred to in paragraphs 5 and 6 above, SFC’s review
and authorization processes regarding DIS-related applications remain the same as

8.

Applicants are reminded that for DIS-related applications, the 6-month application
period will commence from the Take-up Date during which, the SFC will process
and review the application concurrently with the MPFA.

9.

Applicants are expected to deploy sufficient resources to manage their applications
to ensure SFC’s requisitions are addressed to the SFC’s satisfaction within the
applicable response time limit(s) under the 6-month application lapse policy. For
illustration on the application process, applicants may refer to the attached flow
chart entitled “Flow chart showing the application process for new default
investment strategy (“DIS”) related CFs and PIFs expected to be launched by the
end of 2016”.

10.

Applicants who wish to make DIS-related applications should use the same
Application Form and applicable compliance checklists pertaining to MPF Schemes
and PIFs respectively currently posted on the SFC’s website in support of their
applications while the requirement regarding MPFA’s AIP letter will be replaced by
the written confirmations from the MPFA indicated in paragraph 5 above.

11.

Applicants are encouraged to submit draft applications for new CFs related to DIS
concurrently to both the SFC and the MPFA prior to their formal submissions in
order to facilitate the SFC’s review.

12.

The specific interim arrangement in processing DIS-related applications as set out in
this circular will only apply to the DIS-related CFs and PIFs which are expected to
be launched by the end of 2016.

General
13.

Applicants should ensure the applications are well-prepared and in compliance with
all applicable requirements and consult the MPFA in respect of DIS-related issues in
order to facilitate the application process.

14.

For the avoidance of doubt, in respect of any applications for new unlisted index
funds and exchange traded funds seeking SFC’s authorization solely pursuant to
the Code on Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds, such applications will be processed
under the revamped process in accordance with the SFC’s circular on “Launch of
pilot revamped fund authorization process” issued on 9 October 2015
notwithstanding that such funds may be invested by DIS-related CFs or PIFs.

15.

Should you wish to clarify any aspects of this circular and/or in case of doubt, please
contact the relevant case officers in charge.
