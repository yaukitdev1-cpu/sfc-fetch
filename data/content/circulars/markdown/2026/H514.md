Circular to Licensed Corporations
Gazette Notices of New e-Submission Regime and
Test Run of New e-FRR System
This is the Third 1 Circular the Commission has issued to licensed corporations on electronic
submission of financial returns which will become mandatory on 1 November 2008.
Gazette Notices of New e-Submission Regime
The SFC gazetted the following notices on 22 August 2008:
1. The SFC has gazetted the directions and instructions of the use of the online
communications system so as to provide a high level e-submission framework for filing
financial returns to the SFC. The Gazette notice (Annex 1) makes clear that:
(a) There are 3 modes of online submission under the new regime;
(b) The responsible officer and other approved officer can apply for a SFC e-Certificate
from the SFC; and
(c) The SFC e-Certificate can only be used for the purpose of submitting financial returns
under the FRR.
2.

The SFC has made some minor textual amendments to the existing financial return form
mainly to do away with the requirement of signing on the hardcopy of the declaration
form. In this connection, the SFC has published the Gazette notice (Annex 2) to specify
the new electronic form will be published at
http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/forms/supervision/return/return.html

G.N. 5769

SECURITIES AND FUTURES (FINANCIAL RESOURCES)
RULES (Chapter 571N) (“the Rules”)
(Published under section 58(8))
DIRECTIONS AND INSTRUCTIONS FOR USE OF THE ONLINE
COMMUNICATIONS SYSTEM FOR SUBMITTING A RETURN UNDER S.56 OF
THE RULES

Pursuant to section 58(8) of the Rules, the Commission now publishes directions and
instructions for the use of the online communication system approved by the
Commission under section 58(7) of the Rules, with effect from 1 November 2008, as
follows:
(a)

Returns that are required to be submitted by licensed corporations under section
56 of the Rules shall be submitted to the Commission by one of the following
means:
(i)

via “FinNet” (the secure private closed network sponsored and operated by
the Commission) and signed by attaching a digital signature of a
responsible officer of the licensed corporation or another officer of the
licensed corporation approved by the Commission under section 58(5)(e)
of the Rules (”approved officer”);

(ii)

via “FinNet” and signed by attaching an electronic signature of the
responsible officer or approved officer; or

(iii) via Internet and signed by attaching an electronic signature of the
responsible officer or approved officer.
(b)

A responsible officer or an approved officer can obtain an electronic signature
by applying to the Commission for a private label digital certificate (“SFC eCertificate”) protected by a password (or PIN). The SFC e-certificate is
contained in a USB storage token, issued on behalf of the Commission by a
local certification authority that is recognised under section 21 of the Electronic
Transactions Ordinance (Cap. 553). To access the online communication
system, the responsible officer or other approved officer shall also login with a
login password assigned to him by the Commission.

(c)

The SFC e-certificate shall only be used for the purpose of submitting the
returns under section 56 of the Rules.
