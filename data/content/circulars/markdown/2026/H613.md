Circular to management companies / product issuers of
SFC-authorized schemes
Implementation arrangements to the Code on Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds
(“UT Code”)

1. The purposes of this circular are to provide guidance and clarification to the industry on
the implementation arrangements in respect of the production of a product key facts
statement (“KFS”) and revisions to the offering documents as required under the new
regulatory regime.
Background
2. In May 2010, the Commission released the consultation conclusions to the Consultation
Paper on Proposals to Enhance Protection for the Investing Public (“Consultation
Conclusions”).
3. The measures in the Consultation Conclusions included a consolidated product
handbook with revised product codes for unit trusts and mutual funds and for investmentlinked assurance schemes as well as a new product code for unlisted structured
investment products (“Handbook”). There was also the requirement for issuers of retail
investment products to prepare a KFS that summarises the key features and risks of
investment products. During the public consultation, the Commission received
overwhelming support for the proposal to introduce KFS.
4. The Handbook was gazetted and became effective on 25 June 2010. Different
transitional arrangements apply to different products under the applicable product codes.
5. We appreciate that some fund houses have already commenced the production of KFS
and revision of the offering documents to reflect the new regulatory regime. In the
process, we have identified some areas which we believe may need clarification from us.
In light of this and the impending approach of the deadline date of 25 June 2011 for the
full implementation of the new regulatory regime, we recently held two industry briefing
sessions to provide guidance and explain to the industry how we plan the transit. These
briefing sessions were well attended (total attendance of more than 180). The
Commission will continue to work closely with the industry and will hold further seminars
and workshops where necessary to ensure a smooth transition to the revised regime.
Meanwhile, those industry members who wish to discuss their individual concerns with us
are welcome to contact their case officers to arrange a time to meet.

Consultation Conclusions
7. In respect of the implementation arrangements for the production of KFS for funds, under
the Consultation Conclusions:
Existing Schemes1
(i) Existing Schemes that continue to be marketed to the public in Hong Kong are
required to produce a KFS and comply with other disclosure requirements set out in
the revised UT Code commencing 25 June 2011.
(ii) Existing Schemes using global offering documents2 are required to submit KFS to the
Commission for prior approval since their KFS may not form part of the global offering
documents.
(iii) For Existing Schemes, other than those using global offering documents, their KFS
are not required to be submitted to the Commission for prior approval.
(iv) Existing Schemes that are no longer marketed to the public in Hong Kong are not
required to produce KFS.
New Schemes3
(i) New Schemes are expected to include KFS in their applications for vetting by the
Commission, and comply with other disclosure requirements set out in the revised UT
Code.
Timetable for submission of KFS and offering document
KFS that do not require the Commission’s prior approval
8. All Existing Schemes will have a KFS commencing 25 June 2011 in order for them to
continue to be marketed to the public in Hong Kong thereafter.
9. As mentioned earlier, for Existing Schemes, other than those using global offering
documents, their KFS are not required to be submitted to the Commission for prior
approval.

KFS that require the Commission’s prior approval
10. In respect of Existing Schemes that use global offering documents, their KFS will require
prior approval from the Commission. It is important that they get the Commission’s
approval before 25 June 2011. Accordingly, we would urge management companies /
product issuers to submit their KFS to us as soon as possible and preferably by 1 March
2011 so as to give a reasonable time for the KFS to be reviewed and approved by the
Commission before 25 June 2011.
Revisions to the offering document that do not require the Commission’s prior approval
11. Revisions to the offering document of a scheme which solely reflect the new disclosure
requirements in the revised UT Code (“Regulatory Changes”) do not fall within 11.1 of the
UT Code. Accordingly, these amendments do not require the Commission’s prior
approval. The offering document must, nevertheless, be filed with the Commission
within one week from the date of issuance in accordance with 11.1B of the UT Code.
Revisions to the offering document that require the Commission’s prior approval
12. If management companies / product issuers for their own business reasons also wish to
include in the offering document other changes that:
•

fall under 11.1 of the UT Code and hence require the Commission’s prior approval
(e.g. changes in investment objectives, policies and restrictions), and

•

where such changes are intended to take effect commencing 25 June 2011,

we would strongly urge these management companies / product issuers to submit the
revised offering documents to the Commission as soon as possible and preferably by 1
March 2011 so as to give a reasonable time for the processing of the applications.
Early submission
13. In the vetting of any KFS or revised offering documents, we assess their quality and
disclosures against the requirements in the revised UT Code. We would urge
management companies / product issuers to use good care in preparing these
documents to ensure that their KFS and revised offering documents (where necessary)
meet the requirements and standards. This would assist in facilitating a smooth and
efficient process in time to meet the 25 June 2011 commencement date. Management
companies / product issuers are therefore strongly encouraged to submit their KFS
and/or the revised offering documents to the Commission as early as possible.

Indicative timetable
14. In order to ensure a smooth transition to the new regime and for the purpose of the
Commission’s planning for its resources, the Commission would like your co-operation to
provide the following information:
(i) the number of funds that use global offering documents and that would continue to be
marketed to the public in Hong Kong commencing 25 June 2011;
(ii) in respect of funds falling within (i) above, the date on which the management
companies / product issuers have submitted, or intend to submit, their KFS for the
Commission’s prior approval;
(iii) the number of funds that intend to revise their offering documents to include changes
that require the Commission’s prior approval and where such changes are intended
to take effect commencing 25 June 2011; and
(iv) in respect of funds falling within (iii) above, the date on which the management
companies/ product issuers have submitted, or intend to submit, the offering
documents of the schemes for the Commission’s prior approval.
15. For this purpose, we have attached to this circular a template requesting such
information. Please complete the template and return it to the relevant case officers of
the Commission on or before 10 January 2011. Again, for those who have questions we
would encourage them to call their case officers.
16. To further provide guidance and clarification to the industry on the transitional
arrangements for implementing the new regulatory regime, we have issued today two
Frequently Asked Questions, which are attached to this circular. The Frequently Asked
Questions relate to the implementation arrangements for the production of a KFS and the
responsibility statement set out in C22 of Appendix C to the UT Code, which were also
explained during our industry briefing sessions (see paragraph 5).
17. Thank you in advance for your cooperation. If you wish to clarify any aspects of this
circular, please contact the Investment Products Department.
