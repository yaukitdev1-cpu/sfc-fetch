Circular to all Licensed and Registered Persons
Mandatory online submission of licensing applications, notifications and
annual returns by individuals
Under the current licensing regime, licensing applications, notifications and annual returns
may be submitted either in paper form or via the SFC Online Portal.
From 1 February 2017, all applications, notifications and annual returns by individual
applicants and licensees will have to be submitted via the SFC Online Portal. This will
improve the efficiency of the licensing application process and enable the Commission to
handle a significant increase in the number of licensing applications.
Applications, notifications and annual returns submitted by corporations will not be affected
under this initiative. Corporate applicants and intermediaries may continue to submit their
applications, notifications and annual returns either in paper form or via the SFC Online
Portal. Where an individual’s application is a necessary component of a corporation’s
application (eg application by a proposed responsible officer of a corporate applicant for a
new licence) it may be submitted together with the corporate application in paper form.
The Commission will implement this initiative by making various changes to the licensing
forms. New online portal forms for use by individual applicants and licensees will be effective
on 2 December 2016. New paper forms for use by corporate applicants and intermediaries
will be effective on 1 February 2017.
For a transitional period, from 2 December 2016 to 31 January 2017, individual applicants
and licensees may submit their applications, notifications and annual returns either in paper
form (using the existing paper forms) or via the SFC Online Portal (using the new online
portal forms).
To facilitate online submission, the Commission has enhanced the flow, speed and capacity
of the SFC Online Portal. In addition, the Commission will hold four briefing sessions to help
the industry gain a better understanding of the enhanced SFC Online Portal. The briefing
sessions are open for registration until 7 December 2016 on a first-come, first-served basis,
with a maximum capacity of 100 attendants per session. Please refer to the details below:
