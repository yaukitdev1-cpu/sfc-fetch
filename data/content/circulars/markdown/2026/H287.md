Circular to Licensed Corporations
Who wish to act as Financial Intermediaries for
Entrants under the Capital Investment Entrant Scheme

Background
The Capital Investment Entrant Scheme (“the Scheme”) was launched on 27 October 2003.
The response was good with over 50 applications received from people of various countries
within the first three weeks. The SFC would like to alert licensed intermediaries of the
salient features of the Scheme and their role if they wish to act as financial intermediaries for
Applicants/Entrants of the Scheme.
The Scheme
Under and subject to the terms and rules of the Scheme in force from time to time, an
Applicant/Entrant is required to invest initially not less than HK$6.5 million in permissible
investment assets to which he is absolutely entitled and thereafter remain invested in
permissible investment assets throughout the period of the Scheme applicable to him.
Permissible investment assets comprise two classes, namely, real estate in Hong Kong and
specified financial assets. Subject to the “Rules for the Capital Investment Entrant Scheme”
(“the Scheme Rules”) published by the Immigration Department, the Applicant/Entrant may
invest in either or both of these two classes of permissible investment assets in such
proportions as he wishes. The Applicant/Entrant wishing to invest in specified financial
assets, whether or not in combination with investment(s) in permissible real estate, has to
hold the investment in the specified financial assets in a designated account in his own name
to be operated by a single financial intermediary.
Subject to, among others, the criteria and obligations described below, relevant licensed
corporations may act as financial intermediaries for the Applicants/Entrants of the Scheme in
respect of their investment in relevant specified financial assets. Applications are NOT
required.

Eligibility
The basic criteria to be a financial intermediary for the Scheme include the following:(a)

A corporation licensed by the Securities and Futures Commission to carry on Type 1
(dealing in securities), 4 (advising on securities) and/or 9 (asset management)
regulated activities under the Securities and Futures Ordinance; or

(b)

An authorized financial institution as defined in the Banking Ordinance.

Obligations
Under the Scheme Rules, financial intermediaries are required to enter into an agreement
with the Applicant/Entrant for the management and operation of the designated account
which includes the terms set out at Annex. The role of the financial intermediary is to
operate the designated account in accordance with the instruction of the Applicant/Entrant,
and to report to the Immigration Department the status of his holding(s) in a timely manner as
stipulated under the terms referred to in the Annex.
Specified Financial Assets
Specified financial assets permitted under the Scheme, which must be Hong Kong dollar
denominated, include, among others, equities listed on the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong
Limited as well as eligible collective investment schemes that are published and updated
from time to time by the Immigration Department. Detailed requirements of the specified
financial assets are available at the Immigration Department’s website at
http://www.immd.gov.hk.
Full details of the Scheme and the Scheme Rules are available at the offices of the
Immigration Department and on its website. For enquiries regarding the Scheme and the
Scheme Rules, please contact Other Visas and Permits Section of the Immigration
Department at 2829-3232.
Should you have other queries on the above, please refer to the “Frequently Asked Questions
on the Scheme relating to financial intermediaries” on the SFC’s website (www.hksfc.org.hk)
under “Licensing Related Matters” of the Intermediaries, Licensing and Investment Products
section or contact your responsible Intermediaries Relationship Manager of the Licensing
Department.
