Circular

21 March 2014
Circular to issuers of SFC-authorized investment products concerning
the US Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act

1. The purpose of this circular is to remind issuers of SFC-authorized funds, investmentlinked assurance schemes, unlisted structured investment products and real estate
investment trusts (collectively, the “SFC-authorized investment products”) to critically
consider and assess the potential implications of the US Foreign Account Tax
Compliance Act (“FATCA”) on their SFC-authorized investment products.
Background
2. FATCA was enacted in March 2010 with various requirements scheduled to become
effective in 2014 according to the US Department of the Treasury (the “US Treasury”)
and the US Internal Revenue Service (the “IRS”).
3. According to the IRS, FATCA requires, amongst others, foreign financial institutions
(“FFIs”) to comply with certain obligations (such as to finalize the registration with the IRS
to appear on the FFI list before 1 July 2014 (where applicable)) in order to avoid a range
of implications, including the imposition of a 30% withholding tax on certain payments
made to them.
4. Further information on FATCA can be found on the IRS website1. The Government of the
Hong Kong Special Administrative Region (the “HKSAR”) has been in discussions with
the US Treasury, with the objective of concluding an inter-governmental agreement
(“IGA”) designed to facilitate compliance with FATCA by FFIs in Hong Kong. The extent
to which issuers of SFC-authorized investment products (“Issuers”) might be affected and
exempted will depend on the final scope of the IGA and its terms. As such, please pay
close attention to any updates and announcements which may be made by the HKSAR
as regards the conclusion of the IGA with the US Government.
5. In view of the FATCA implementation timeline, Issuers are reminded of the following:(i) Issuers are expected to critically assess the potential implications of FATCA on
their business operations and the impact on their SFC-authorized investment
products, and consider the need to implement any necessary process and
controls to ensure compliance with FATCA. As such, Issuers should take
professional advice as soon as possible with their own tax advisors regarding the
possible implications of FATCA on their SFC-authorized investment products.
(ii) Issuers of SFC-authorized investment products should act in the best interests of
investors.
(iii) Issuers should keep investors informed of information concerning their SFCauthorized investment products which is necessary to enable investors to

In this connection, it is the Issuers’ duty to ensure that the offering documents
(including the product key facts statement (the “KFS”)) of the SFC-authorized
investment products are up-to-date and contain the information necessary for
investors to make an informed judgement of their investments in the relevant
products (including, amongst others, the relevant description/warning of the
FATCA regime, the FATCA status and the withholding tax implications of the
SFC-authorized investment products, and the relevant FATCA risk disclosures).
(iv) In particular, Issuers are urged to monitor and conduct assessment on the
likelihood of imposition of withholding tax to their SFC-authorized investment
products. In the event an SFC-authorized investment product is likely to become
subject to the withholding tax requirements under FATCA, Issuers are expected
to provide at least one month’s prior written notice to investors setting out the
potential impact on the SFC-authorized investment products. Issuers should also
include prominent risks disclosure in the offering documents (including the KFS)
in a timely manner in accordance with the applicable product codes. Disclosure in
the offering documents (including the KFS) should be accurate, fair and not
misleading.
General
6. If in doubt concerning the obligations under FATCA, Issuers should seek appropriate
professional advice as soon as possible. Nothing in this circular is intended to be, or
should be construed to constitute legal or tax advice.
7. If you wish to clarify any aspects of this circular, please contact the relevant case officer
in charge.
