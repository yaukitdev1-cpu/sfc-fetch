Circular to Licensed Corporations and Associated Entities
Anti-Money Laundering / Counter-Terrorist Financing
Suspicious Transaction Report
Further to our circular issued on 9 December 2015 Note 1 which reminds licensed corporations
(“LCs”) and associated entities (“AEs”) to ensure that any suspicious transaction report
submitted by them contains accurate, sufficient information of the transaction, person and entity
involved, as well as a reasonably detailed analysis of the suspicion triggering the report, this
circular draws the attention of LCs and AEs to a feedback report recently issued by the Joint
Financial Intelligence Unit (“JFIU”) on the quality of suspicious transaction report (“STR”).
The feedback report (Feedback No. 191), which is set out in Annex A to the STR Quarterly
Analysis - 3rd Issue 2017 available on the JFIU’s website Note 2, is reproduced in Appendix 1 to
this circular. LCs and AEs should have regard to the criteria identified therein which may assist
them in filing better quality STRs.
LCs and AEs are also encouraged to make reference to the reporting methods and advice
available on the JFIU website at http://www.jfiu.gov.hk/index.html on fulfilling their statutory
obligations to file STRs, as well as to browse from time to time for relevant materials in the STR
Quarterly Analysis published in the secure area of the JFIU website.
LCs and AEs who have not already registered for access to the secure area of the JFIU
website are strongly advised to apply for a login name and password by completing the
registration form in Appendix 2 to this circular. The completed form should be submitted to the
JFIU by fax to 2529 4013 or by email to jfiu@police.gov.hk.
Should you have any queries regarding the contents of this circular, please contact Ms Kiki
Wong on 2231 1569.
