Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Financing of Terrorism Seminars
The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) will host three half-day anti-money laundering
and counter-financing of terrorism (AML/CFT) seminars on 22, 25 and 27 November 2019
respectively. The seminars will be held at the Lecture Theatre, G/F, Hong Kong Central
Library, Causeway Bay.
Objectives and rundown
The objectives of the seminars are –


To present the key findings and areas for improvement from the Mutual Evaluation
Report of Hong Kong published by the Financial Action Task Force as they relate to the
securities sector;



To provide an update on major AML/CFT regulatory developments;



To share inspection findings and other supervisory observations on AML/CFT; and



To present money laundering typologies and feedback on the suspicious transaction
reporting of the securities sector.

Please refer to Appendix for rundown and speakers of the seminars.
Who should attend
You are invited to nominate relevant management / supervisory personnel and compliance
officers to attend. The seminar is free of charge and three CPT hours will be offered to those
who attend the whole session.
Enrolment
Each firm should only submit one enrolment form via the following link on or before 8
November 2019:
https://www.sfc.hk/web/EN/anti-money-laundering-and-counter-financing-of-terrorismseminar-2019/
Enrolments are subject to a maximum of two places per firm. As seats are limited, we will
attempt to allocate seats to all nominees listed as the first priority in enrolment forms
submitted by the deadline. Any remaining seats will be allocated to the second nominee on a
first-come, first-served basis.
