Circular to licensed corporations which had distributed / sold
Lehman Programs Securities
This circular is issued to remind licensed corporations which had distributed / sold Lehman
Programs Securities to notify the respective clients about the deadline of filing claims against
Lehman Brothers Holdings Inc. (“LBHI”) on or before 2 November 2009.
“Lehman Programs Securities” are securities that are included on the “Lehman Programs
Securities” list dated 17 July 2009 (“the List”)1. According to the Notice of Deadlines for Filing
Proofs of Claim Based on Lehman Programs Securities dated 27 July 2009 (“the Notice”)1, if
your clients have claims against LBHI based on the securities identified on the List that arose
prior to 15 September 2008, the date on which LBHI commenced its bankruptcy case in the
US, they should file a Securities Programs Proof of Claim1 to the Lehman Brothers Holdings
Claims Processing Center in the US on or before 2 November 2009 in order to preserve
their claims against LBHI. For details of the filing procedures, please refer to the Notice.
We would like to remind you to take appropriate steps to bring the above information to the
attention of the clients who have bought Lehman Programs Securities in a timely manner,
including but not limited to alerting the clients to check the information on the relevant
websites.
