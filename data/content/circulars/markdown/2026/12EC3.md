Circular to all Licensed and Registered Persons relating to Licensing Forms
This circular is issued to inform all licensed and registered persons of the launch of new
licensing forms and the major changes made therein.
On 3 August 2012, the Commission specified 26 licensing forms1 under section 402 of the
Securities and Futures Ordinance (Cap. 571) and published the same in the Gazette (G.N.
5186) of the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region. With effect
from 3 August 2012, the new licensing forms superseded all previous versions of such
paper forms and shall be used for the purpose of submitting licensing applications and
notifications to the Commission.
In line with the objective of revamping the Commission website2, the format and content
of licensing forms are improved to facilitate users’ submission. Major changes include:


All licensing forms have adopted the same font style and layout setting for
consistency purpose.



The term “overseas company” in some forms is changed to “non-Hong Kong
company” with reference to the amendments made to the Companies Ordinance
(Cap. 32), and in the case of a corporation having one director only, single
signature is accepted.



To increase flexibility for users, person authorized by the board of directors is
allowed to sign certain forms on behalf of a body corporate.



Additional notes are inserted and outdated references are removed in some forms
to clarify the submission requirements.



Vetting Authorization Form – Corporation is incorporated into the main body of
some forms to simplify the submission process.

You may download blank forms or print out completed forms from our website at
http://www.sfc.hk/web/EN/forms/intermediaries/licensing-application-and-notificationforms/. The Commission prefers and encourages the use of Online Portal
(http://www.sfc.hk/web/EN/regulatory-functions/intermediaries/licensing/online-portal.html)
for submission of licensing applications and notifications.
