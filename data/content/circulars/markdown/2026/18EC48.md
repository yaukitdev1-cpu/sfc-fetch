1.

The purpose of this circular is to inform management companies of SFC-authorized real
estate investment trusts (“REITs”) of the launch of a streamlined approach for vetting
and approval of announcements and circulars under 10.2 of the Code on Real Estate
Investment Trusts (“REIT Code”) with effect from 3 July 2018 (“Effective Date”).

Background
2.

Under 10.2 of the REIT Code, all announcements, circulars and notices of an SFCauthorized REIT shall be submitted to the Commission for prior approval before they are
issued.

3.

To facilitate more efficient management of SFC-authorized REITs and dissemination of
information in a more timely manner, the SFC is prepared to adopt a streamlined
approach for vetting and approval of certain announcements and circulars under 10.2 of
the REIT Code as set out below.

4.

The streamlined approach is implemented with a view to promoting REIT managers’
self-compliance without compromising investor protection.

Streamlined approach
Relevant Announcements/Circulars
5.

Subject to the paragraphs below, with effect from the Effective Date, announcements
and/or circulars relating to the following matters (“Relevant Announcements/Circulars”)
will not be required to be submitted to the SFC for pre-vetting before their publication:

For the avoidance of doubt, where a Relevant Announcement/Circular covers any

Filing and post-vetting
9.

REIT managers are required to file the Relevant Announcements/Circulars with the SFC
immediately following their submission to the Hong Kong Stock Exchange for
publication.

10. The SFC will conduct post-vetting of the Relevant Announcements/Circulars filed with
the SFC to monitor compliance. REIT managers should ensure that the SFC-authorized
REITs under their management and all disclosures made are compliant with all relevant
requirements set out in the REIT Code and the trust deeds constituting the REITs.
11. Notwithstanding the above, the SFC retains its power to require REIT managers to
submit any announcement and circular for pre-vetting where the SFC considers it
appropriate in individual cases.
12. The SFC may make follow-up enquiries and/or take other regulatory actions (including
requiring a REIT manager to issue a clarification announcement and/or take other
remedial actions) as considered appropriate. REIT managers are expected to respond
to any enquiries made by the SFC promptly and in an open and co-operative manner.

General
13. The Frequently Asked Questions relating to Real Estate Investment Trusts (“FAQs”)
have been updated to reflect, amongst others, the launch of the streamlined approach
(please refer to updated Questions 16, 28, 28A and 29). The updated FAQs are
accessible on our website at: https://www.sfc.hk/web/EN/faqs/publicly-offeredinvestment-product/real-estate-investment-trusts.html.
