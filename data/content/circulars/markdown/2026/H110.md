Financial Resources (Amendment) Rules 2002
The Securities and Futures Commission has recently amended the Financial Resources Rules
(“the FRR”) to incorporate the following two new requirements:
-

To apply a 80% haircut to collateral that may be difficult to liquidate quickly because
of the size of holding relative to monthly turnover (taking an average over 6 months)
and market capitalization;

-

To include a financial adjustment in ranking liabilities where a firm’s total borrowings,
which are secured by repledging margin clients’ securities, exceed 65% of aggregate
margin loans provided to margin clients.

Details of these requirements are described in the Financial Resources (Amendment) Rules
2002 (“the Amendment Rules”) which were gazetted on 31 May 2002. A copy of the
Amendment Rules and the Consultation Conclusions can now be found on the SFC’s Internet
website at http://www.hksfc.org.hk.
The above new requirements will come into effect on 1st October 2002 and will be applicable
to all registered securities margin financiers and securities dealers providing securities margin
financing. Firms are strongly advised to carefully consider the financial and operational
implications of the new requirements and ensure that they have the necessary data, systems
and capital for complying with the new requirements for 1st October 2002.
If you have any questions about the new requirements, you are welcome to contact your case
officer in the Intermediaries Supervision Department (please refer to the attached list for
name and telephone number of case officers).
