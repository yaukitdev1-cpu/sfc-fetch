Circular to Licensed Corporations and Associated Entities

Anti-Money Laundering / Combating Terrorist Financing


(1) Non-cooperative Countries and Territories
(2) United Nations Sanctions (Democratic Republic of the Congo) Regulation 2005
(3) United Nations Sanctions (Cote d’Ivoire) Regulation 2006

(1) Non-cooperative Countries and Territories (“NCCTs”)

   Further to our circular issued on 20 October 2005, this is to inform you that the Financial Action Task Force on Money Laundering (“FATF”) decided at its recent plenary meeting to remove Nigeria from the list of NCCTs. At present, Myanmar is the only country remaining on the list.


   The related press release issued by the FATF can be found on its website (http://www.fatf-gafi.org).


   Licensed corporations (“LCs”) and associated entities (“AEs”) are reminded to give special attention to businesses and transactions with persons, including companies and financial institutions, in the above country remaining on the list, in accordance with FATF Recommendation 21[1].

(2) United Nations Sanctions (Democratic Republic of the Congo) Regulation 2005 (the “Congo Regulation”)

   This is to inform you that pursuant to section 36 of the Congo Regulation, an updated list of “relevant persons and entities” specified for the purposes of paragraph 15 of the United Nations Security Council Resolution 1596 was published in the Gazette (G.N. 3816) on 23 June 2006.


   Compared with the previous list referred to in our circular dated 16 February 2006, information of one individual (Dr Ignace MURWANASHY-AKA) has been updated.


   The new list can be found on the Government website http://www.gld.gov.hk/eng/welcome.htm under eGazette [2] in Services to Public.  A copy of the list in MS Word format is attached to facilitate the update of your electronic database (Attachment 1).

3) United Nations Sanctions (Cote d’Ivoire) Regulation 2006 (the “Cote d’Ivoire Regulation”)

   Also, pursuant to section 37 of the Cote d’Ivoire Regulation, an updated list of “relevant persons and entities” specified for the purposes of paragraph 11 of the United Nations Security Council Resolution 1572 was published in the Gazette (G.N. 3817) on 23 June 2006.


   Compared with the previous list referred to in our circular dated 10 May 2006, information of two individuals (Charles Goude Ble and Djue N’goran Eugene Kouadio) has been updated.


   The new list can be found on the Government website http://www.gld.gov.hk/eng/welcome.htm under eGazette4 in Services to Public.  A copy of the list in MS Word format is attached to facilitate the update of your electronic database (Attachment 2).


The SFC reminds all LCs and AEs to check the names in the above lists against their records, and report any transactions or relationships they have or have had with the named individuals or entities to the Joint Financial Intelligence Unit.  LCs and AEs are also reminded to browse the above website regularly for the latest information.

Should you have any queries regarding the contents of this circular, please contact Ms Maggie Cheung at 2840-9455.
