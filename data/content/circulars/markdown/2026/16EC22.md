Circular

22 April 2016
Formal adoption of the six-month application lapse policy for SFC-authorized
mandatory provident fund (MPF) products and pooled retirement funds (PRFs)

1. The purpose of this circular is to announce that, with effect from 9 May 2016
(“Effective Date”), the six-month application lapse policy for processing
applications seeking SFC’s authorization of (i) new MPF products (including MPF
schemes, constituent funds and pooled investment funds) pursuant to the SFC
Code on MPF Products and the Code on Unit Trust and Mutual Funds (where
applicable); and (ii) PRFs pursuant to the Code on Pooled Retirement Funds will
be formally adopted.
2. Capitalized terms shall have the same meaning as defined in the SFC’s circular
issued on 9 October 2015 entitled “Application Lapse Policy” (“Oct 2015
Circular”) and the circular issued on 12 January 2016 entitled “Circular to
applicants of SFC-authorized mandatory provident fund products related to
Default Investment Strategy (“DIS”)” (“Jan 2016 Circular”) unless otherwise
defined.
Background
3. Pursuant to the Oct 2015 Circular, the application lapse policy was implemented
on 9 November 2015 for a six-month pilot period ending 8 May 2016 (“Pilot
Period”).
4. The application lapse policy was introduced with a view to enhance the overall
efficiency in the authorization process of MPF and PRF products, in line with the
six-month application lapse period currently applied to other SFC-authorized
investment products, without compromising investor interest and protection. For
details regarding the application lapse policy for MPF and PRF products and DISrelated applications, please refer to the Oct 2015 Circular and the Jan 2016
Circular respectively.
Formal adoption of the six-month application lapse policy
5. Since its implementation, the SFC has been closely monitoring the operation of
the six-month application lapse policy and consulted with the MPFA regarding the
formal adoption of such policy on the Effective Date.
