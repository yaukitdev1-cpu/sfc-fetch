Circular to Licensed Corporations and Associated Entities
Amendments to Prevention of Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing
Guidance Note (“AML Guidance Note”)

The SFC today publishes amendments to paragraph 5.2(c) of its AML Guidance Note to
clarify the existing requirements on how to determine the risk profile of customers. The
amendments were made in light of the Race Discrimination Ordinance (“RDO”) (Chapter
602) which recently came into operation. These amendments take immediate effect.
Under the RDO, it is unlawful for any person concerned with the provision of goods, facilities
or services to discriminate against another person who seeks to obtain or use those goods,
facilities or services based on race. To avoid any potential misunderstanding that the AML
Guidance Note requires licensed corporations and associated entities to differentiate
between customers of different “national or ethnic origin” 1 which falls within the definition of
“race” in the RDO, the revised paragraph 5.2(c) now reads as follows:
“(c)

the nationality, citizenship and resident status of the customer (in the case of a
corporate customer, the place of incorporation), the place of establishment of the
customer’s business and location of the counterparties with which the customer does
business, such as NCCTs designated by the FATF or those known to the licensed
corporations and associated entities to lack proper standards in the prevention of
money laundering or customer due diligence process;” [Emphasis added]
