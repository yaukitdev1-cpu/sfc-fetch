19 June 2014

Circular to issuers of SFC-authorized investment products
Issuers (“Issuers”) of investment products authorized by the Commission pursuant to Part IV of the
Securities and Futures Ordinance (“SFC-authorized investment products”) are reminded that they
should have up-to-date and effective business continuity plan (“BCP”) in place to protect their SFCauthorized investment products and their investors from any instances of business or market
disruption, including any circumstances that might restrict access to their places of business.
In particular, Issuers should review their BCP to ensure the continuous provision of material
information and services to investors in SFC-authorized investment products.
The BCP is expected to include detailed measures covering, (i) inaccessibility to part or all of the
business premises, (ii) inaccessibility to email service, telephone system and other corporate
computing or communication application systems in the business premises; and (iii) disruption or
suspension of dealing arrangements. Issuers are also reminded that they should discuss their BCP
with key third party service providers, including trustees/custodians, administrators and distributors,
and the BCP should take account of these services.
Please note that the above is by no means exhaustive. It is incumbent upon the Issuers to properly
monitor and manage any business/market disruption, and to take prompt and effective measures to
mitigate any potential adverse impact on SFC-authorized investment products and investors in them.
Issuers should, as part of their duties under the SFC Handbook for Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds,
Investment-Linked Assurance Schemes and Unlisted Structured Investment Products and applicable
product codes, keep investors informed about any developments concerning any SFC-authorized
investment product as necessary to enable investors to understand the product.
Issuers are expected to inform the Commission as soon as possible if there is any development or
issue that may have a significant impact on SFC-authorized investment products and/or investors in
them.
In the event of any business or market disruption, Issuers should directly contact relevant
Director and Senior Manager of the Investment Products Division of the Commission in respect
of any urgent matters concerning any SFC-authorized investment product that may require our
immediate attention.
Issuers should also designate appropriate personnel to be emergency contact person(s) on
behalf of the Issuers, who can be contacted by the Commission at all times in respect of the
implementation of the BCP. Emergency contact person(s) is/are expected to be senior
management of the Issuers.
Issuers are welcome to contact the relevant case officer in case of any questions on the above.
