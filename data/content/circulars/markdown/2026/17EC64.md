Circular to Licensed Corporations
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) on Mandatory Reporting Regime
Today the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) and the Hong Kong Monetary Authority
(HKMA) issued a revised set of FAQs to provide clarification on reporting and record keeping
requirements under the Securities and Futures (OTC Derivative Transactions - Reporting
and Record Keeping Obligations) Rules. Major changes include new questions and updates
incidental to the implementation of phase 2 reporting which took effect on 1 July 2017.
The revised FAQs document is accessible at the SFC’s website:
http://www.sfc.hk/web/EN/faqs/otc-derivatives/
Licensed Corporations that may be subject to mandatory reporting obligations are advised to
refer to the revised FAQs above.
If you have any questions regarding the substance of the reporting requirements, please call
(852) 2231 1788. For questions about the service and operation of the electronic reporting
system, or about training and simulation tests, please contact the HKTR on (852) 8100 3115
or email hktr@hkma.gov.hk.
