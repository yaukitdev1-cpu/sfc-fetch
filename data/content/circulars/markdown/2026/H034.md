Letter to all dealers and securities margin financiers registered under the Securities Ordinance and dealers registered under the Commodities Trading Ordinance

Dear Sir or Madam,

## Effect of Cessation of the Hang Seng 100 Index

The HSI Services Limited has recently announced that it will cease to compile the Hang Seng 100 Index from 3 October 2001.

The Financial Resources Rules ("FRR") currently apply a 20% haircut to Hang Seng 100 Index constituent stocks ("HS100 stocks") which are held by dealers and securities margin financiers as house positions or as collateral. In addition, if the market value of a HS100 stock and its related securities held by a dealer or securities margin financier as margin loan collateral exceeds 15% (the "concentration threshold percentage") of the market value of all margin loan collateral held by the dealer or financier, that HS100 stock and its related securities must be subject to a concentration discount.

Following the cessation of compilation of the Hang Seng 100 Index, the above provisions of the FRR will cease to be effective. The current HS100 stocks will be subject to the haircut percentage applied to non-HS100 stocks, i.e. 30%. The concentration threshold percentage for margin loan collateral in current HS100 stocks and their related securities will also be lowered to the level applied to non-HS100 stocks, i.e. 10%.

It is conceivable that a number of dealers and securities margin financiers might require additional capital to comply with the FRR as a result of the change of FRR requirements following the cessation of compilation of the Hang Seng 100 Index.

As an interim measure to relieve dealers and securities margin financiers from these changes, the Commission hereby informs you that if as a result of the abolition of the Hang Seng 100 Index you have a difficulty in complying with the FRR, you should immediately inform the Commission and consider the need for an application for modification under section 29 of the Securities and Futures Commission Ordinance.

Please note that the above measure is a temporary position and the Commission will effect a change to the FRR to address the situation.

Yours faithfully

Andrew Procter Executive Director Intermediaries and Investment Products
