Circular to all Licensed and Registered Persons relating to
the publication of a revised edition of the Licensing Information Booklet
This circular is issued to inform all licensed and registered persons of the publication of a
revised edition of the Licensing Information Booklet (“the Revised Booklet”) and to highlight
the relevant changes.
To bring the information contained in the Licensing Information Booklet into line with the
current licensing regime and the practices that the Commission adopts in relation to the
licensing or registration of intermediaries under the Securities and Futures Ordinance, the
Revised Booklet has been published on the Commission’s website today. The changes
principally relate to the following areas:
•

inclusion of contents in relation to Type 10 regulated activity (i.e. providing credit
rating services);
revision of the section concerning licensed holders insurance;
inclusion of references to the SFC Online Portal (http://portal.sfc.hk) as an additional
channel for the submission to the Commission of licensing applications, annual
returns and notifications, and the payment of fees;
revision of Part 7 to clarify application procedures; and
removal of previous Appendices A (Reference guide to the SFC Infoline) and B
(Definitions of regulated activities).
