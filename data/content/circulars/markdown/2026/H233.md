Circular to Licensed Corporations and Registered Institutions
SFC to Move to Chater House
Please be advised that the SFC will move its office to Chater House by the end of June 2003. We
seek to ensure a smooth relocation and continuously provide quality services to market
intermediaries. Attached please find a press release containing details of the exercise.
The Licensing Department also takes this opportunity to vary its counter services to better utilize
resources. In view that all licensing application, notification and financial return forms have been
posted on to the SFC website, as well as made obtainable from the SFC’s “InfoLine fax-on-demand
service”, a drop-in box together with an electronic time stamp will be provided instead. The
following arrangements will be made to ensure intermediaries’ smooth operations.
Submitting Documents to the Licensing or Intermediaries Supervision Department
The special drop-in box will be placed on the other side of the lift lobby opposite the reception area
at the SFC’s office on 8/F Chater House, 8 Connaught Road Central, Hong Kong. Intermediaries
could time stamp their documents before depositing them into the drop-in box. The opening hours
are from 9.00 am to 5.30 pm on Mondays through Fridays and from 9.00 am to 12.30 pm on
Saturdays.
Obtaining Licensing and Financial Return Forms
In addition to obtaining the licensing application, notification and financial return forms from the
SFC website (www.hksfc.org.hk/eng/licensing/html/index/index3.html), intermediaries may also
request a copy of the relevant forms (which may be photo-copied for subsequent use) by
completing and returning the enclosed reply slip. Alternatively, intermediaries could also obtain a
facsimile copy of the forms by calling the SFC’s “InfoLine fax-on-demand service” at:
