Subject:

Guarantors of Guaranteed Funds – Amendment to the Code on Unit
Trusts and Mutual Funds

Background
1.

Chapter 8.5(b) of the Code on Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds (“Code”) sets out the
qualification requirement of the guarantor of a guaranteed fund as follow:
“The guarantor must be a substantial financial institution approved by the
Commission to act as guarantor of the scheme.”

2.

A “substantial financial institution” is defined in Chapter 3.12 of the Code as a
licensed banking institution authorized under the Banking Ordinance or financial
institution with a minimum paid-up capital of HK$150m or its equivalent in foreign
currency.

3.

The current requirement was adopted at a time when there were only a limited number
of guaranteed funds in the market. In view of the proliferation of guaranteed funds in
the retail market and the increasing diversity of guarantors, the SFC considers it
necessary to review the qualification requirement of guarantors.

4.

The ability of the guarantor to honour the guarantee at maturity date is of paramount
importance to investors. It is therefore necessary to set some qualification
requirements on the guarantor of guaranteed funds. In particular, the reputation,
financial resources and regulatory status of the guarantor need to be critically
examined.

5.

A review of the guarantors which have been approved by the SFC shows that a
majority of them are either licensed banks or restricted licensed banks which fall
within the definition of “authorized institutions” as defined in the Banking
Ordinance. The others are either approved insurers as defined by the Insurance
Companies Ordinance or financial institutions with paid-up capital equivalent to at
least HK$150m.

6.

It is considered that the current requirement of the guarantor being a substantial
financial institution should be further strengthened to ensure that the guarantor has
sufficient financial resources to meet its obligations. Moreover, it is necessary to

Revised Qualification Requirement of a Guarantor
7.

With immediate effect, Chapter 8.5(b) is to be revised as follows:
“ The guarantor must be:
(a)
(b)

a licensed banking institution authorized under the Banking Ordinance; or
an authorized insurer authorized under the Insurance Companies Ordinance.

Note: The Commission may consider other substantial financial institutions to act as
guarantor on a case-by-case basis. The Commission must be satisfied that the
institution is, on an ongoing basis, subject to regulatory supervision and of
acceptable financial standing.”
8.

As a general entry requirement, guarantors must be either licensed banks (including
restricted licensed banks) or authorized insurers, though other financial institutions
may apply for approval as such. To be approved, the applicant must satisfy the SFC
that it is a reputable institution of good financial standing and integrity, and that it is
subject to prudential regulation and on-going monitoring. The SFC will also consider
other investor protection issues such as conflicts of interest and past compliance
history.

9.

Further to the above-mentioned amendment, the SFC is currently revising other
provisions in Chapter 8.5 of the Code in relation to guaranteed funds. Such revisions
primarily codify the existing practice of strengthening the disclosure in the offering
documents and marketing materials of guaranteed funds, thereby facilitating
investors’ better understanding of the nature of the funds.
