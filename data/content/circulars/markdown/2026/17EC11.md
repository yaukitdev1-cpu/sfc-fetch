Circular to Licensed Corporations on
Reporting of Over-the-counter (OTC) Derivatives Transactions

On 27 January 2017, the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) announced that the trade
repository operated by it (HKTR) had published on its website (https://hktr.hkma.gov.hk/) a
note setting out updates and clarifications to the Supplementary Reporting Instructions for
OTC Derivative Transactions and the Supplementary Reporting Instructions for OTC
Derivatives Transactions – Part 2.
Licensed Corporations that may be subject to mandatory reporting obligations are advised to
refer to the note issued by HKTR above.
If you have any questions regarding the substance of the reporting requirements, please call
(852) 2231 1788. For questions about the service and operation of the electronic reporting
system, or about training and simulation tests, please contact the HKTR on (852) 8100 3115
or email hktr@hkma.gov.hk.
