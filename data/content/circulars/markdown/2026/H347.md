Migration to the New Regime under the Securities and Futures Ordinance (“SFO”)
As you are aware, the two-year transitional period for licensees registered under the former
regime to migrate to the new regime will end on 31 March 2005. We note that you have yet
to submit your migration application.
Please be reminded that if you fail to lodge your application by the end of the
transitional period, your existing licence to conduct regulated activities will
automatically cease to have effect. We therefore urge you to submit your migration
application (including those of your licensed representatives and regardless of whether their
licences have been suspended) as soon as practicable before the expiry of the transitional
period. To assist with our planning purpose, please complete and return the attached Reply
Slip by 5 November 2004.
If you intend migrating to the new regime, please note that it is a requirement under the SFO
that at least two individuals have to be approved by us as your responsible officers in respect
of each type of regulated activity you are licensed.1 In this regard, you may refer to the
circular we issued on 28 May 2004 entitled “Responsible Officers”2 for more information.
Should you have any questions in complying with this requirement, please do not hesitate to
discuss with your Intermediaries Relationship Manager.
If you do not intend migrating to the new regime, please provide reasons (such as group
consolidation) for such decision in the Reply Slip. Under the circumstances, you would also
need to advise us your plan and time frame to wind down or transfer your business as
appropriate.
