Circular to all Licensed and Registered Persons relating to Licensing Forms
This circular is issued to inform all licensed and registered persons of the launch of new
licensing forms and the major changes made therein.
On 30 August 2013, the Commission specified 26 licensing forms1 under section 402 of the
Securities and Futures Ordinance (Cap. 571) and published the same in the Gazette of the
Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region (G.N. 5045). With effect from
1 October 2013, the new licensing forms will supersede all previous versions of such paper
forms and shall be used for the purpose of submitting licensing applications and notifications
to the Commission.
The format and content of the licensing forms have been revised and improved to facilitate
submission. Major changes include:
•

The submission forms for Sponsors and Sponsor Principals (i.e. Form 1(s) and
Supplement 10(s)) have been revised to align them with the new additional eligibility
criteria for Sponsor Principals under the revised Additional Fit and Proper Guidelines
for Corporations and Authorized Financial Institutions applying or continuing to act as
Sponsors and Compliance Advisers and the requirements under the new paragraph
17 of the Code of Conduct for Persons Licensed by or Registered with the Securities
and Futures Commission.

•

Additional clauses and references to relevant statutory requirements are inserted in
some forms to clarify the submission requirements.

•

The wording adopted in the declaration sections and other sections in all licensing
forms have been aligned for consistency.

•

The date on which the forms are published has been inserted in all licensing forms for
the purpose of identification.

The Commission has also taken this opportunity to make minor formatting and other changes
to the non-statutory licensing forms. In addition, it has introduced a new non-statutory
consent form relating to the imposition and revocation of a standard licensing condition
concerning the period of validity of temporary licences granted to individuals, to simplify and
streamline the process by which temporary licences are granted to individuals. All of these
new non-statutory licensing forms will supersede all previous versions with effect from 1
October 2013.
You may download blank forms or print out completed forms from our website at
http://www.sfc.hk/web/EN/forms/intermediaries/licensing-application-and-notification-forms/.
