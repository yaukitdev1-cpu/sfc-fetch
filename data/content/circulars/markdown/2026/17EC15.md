28 February 2017

Circular to Licensed Corporations on
Reporting of Over-the-counter (OTC) Derivatives Transactions

Today the trade repository (HKTR) operated by the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA)
published on its website another note for updating the Supplementary Reporting Instructions
for OTC Derivative Transactions (SRI 1) and the Supplementary Reporting Instructions for
OTC Derivatives Transactions – Part 2 (SRI 2):
https://hktr.hkma.gov.hk/ContentDetail.aspx?pageName=HKTR-RPT-Administration-andInterface-Development-Guide
This note postpones the implementation of the reporting instructions for certain dates in
relation to novation transactions until further notice and provides reporting instructions for
backloading and reporting of valuation transaction information for forward rate agreements.
This note, together with another note issued on 27 January 2017, the SRI 1 and SRI 2
published on 25 November 2016 should be read together as the complete set of reporting
instructions. Licensed Corporations that may be subject to mandatory reporting obligations
are advised to refer to these documents to ensure compliance with applicable reporting
requirements.
If you have any questions regarding the above documents and reporting requirements,
please call (852) 2231 1788. For questions about the service and operation of the electronic
reporting system, or about training and simulation tests, please contact the HKTR on (852)
8100 3115 or email hktr@hkma.gov.hk.
