Circular

7 June 2016

Circular to Licensed Corporations in relation to the reporting of OTC
derivatives transactions to the HKMA trade repository – Technical
specifications for new phase system implementation

We refer to the Circular issued on 30 December 2015 in relation to the new phase of system
implementation for over-the-counter (OTC) derivatives trade repository (HKTR) of the Hong
Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA), which aims to support the expansion of the scope of
products under the mandatory reporting obligations and the reporting of valuation information.
The HKMA announced yesterday that the HKTR system implementation for the new phase
was launched on 6 June 2016. The HKMA’s announcement also includes an updated version
of the Administration and Interface Development Guide (AIDG) that contains information on
the new products to be reported, new data fields for reporting valuation information of
transactions and new technical specifications. The HKMA will provide a testing environment
from 6 June 2016 for HKTR members and reporting agents to test their systems in respect of
the above new changes. Training sessions on these new changes will also be provided by
the HKMA.
Please also note that an updated “Operating Procedures for Hong Kong Trade Repository –
User Manual for Participants” has been published in the HKTR website for the refined
procedures and enhanced functionalities of the HKTR system.
Attached to this Circular are the letter from the HKMA regarding the above announcement,
and the updated AIDG.
Licensed Corporations with activities that may be subject to mandatory reporting obligations
are advised to take the attached into account when preparing for reporting to the HKTR.
Should you have any queries regarding the above, please contact the HKMA at
hktr@hkma.gov.hk.
