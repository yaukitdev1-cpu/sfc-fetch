Circular

19 December 2016

Circular to Licensed Corporations in relation to the reporting of OTC
derivatives transactions to the HKMA trade repository – Revised Technical
Specifications for Expanded Scope Mandatory Reporting

We refer to our circular on 31 October 2016, which provided the revised Administrative and
Interface Development Guide (“AIDG”) version 1.5.2 in respect of the technical specifications
for the expanded scope of the reporting requirements under the next phase of the mandatory
reporting regime (Phase 2 Reporting).
The Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) announced today that the corresponding macro
program of the AIDG version 1.5.2 has been published on its website, which will start to
apply from 19 December 2016 and can be downloaded at
https://hktr.hkma.gov.hk/ContentDetail.aspx?pageName=Revised-Technical-Specificationsfor-Expanded-Scope-Mandatory-Reporting-(2016-12-19).
Attached to this Circular is the letter from the HKMA regarding the above announcement.
Should you have any queries regarding the above, please contact the HKMA at
hktr@hkma.gov.hk.
