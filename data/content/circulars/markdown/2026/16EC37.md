Circular to Licensed Corporations in relation to the clearing and record
keeping rules for the OTC derivatives regime
This is to remind all Licensed Corporations that the Securities and Futures (OTC Derivative
Transactions – Clearing and Record Keeping Obligations and Designation of Central Counterparties)
Rules (Clearing Rules) has come into effect today.
To help market participants better understand how the Clearing Rules operate, the Hong Kong
Monetary Authority and the Securities and Futures Commission have prepared a set of frequently
asked questions (FAQs) that provides clarification about the mandatory clearing and record keeping
obligations. The FAQs can be accessed at: http://www.sfc.hk/web/EN/faqs/otc-derivatives/
Licensed Corporations whose activities may render them subject to the mandatory clearing obligation
are advised to refer to the FAQs, to ensure compliance with their clearing and record keeping
obligations.
