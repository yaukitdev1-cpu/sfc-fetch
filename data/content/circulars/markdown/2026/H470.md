Circular to Licensed Corporations and Registered Institutions
Investor Leaflet “Hedge Funds”

We wish to invite you to display and distribute to your clients our updated investor leaflet,
“Hedge Funds”.
The leaflet explains the basic concepts and risks of hedge funds, and issues to note when
investors consider buying these funds. Both the English and Chinese versions of the leaflet
are available.
“Hedge Funds” is the latest publication of the SFC’s new investor leaflet series, following
four earlier issues entitled How to Make a Complaint, Know Your Broker, IPO Investing and
Fund Investing.
To allow investors to check the availability of the above leaflets, the names and addresses of
the licensed corporations distributing these leaflets will be posted on the “Publications”
section of the SFC-operated investor portal, the InvestEd website at www.InvestEd.hk.
Please contact Ms Gloria Chan at 2842 7711 concerning requests for copies of the leaflets.
