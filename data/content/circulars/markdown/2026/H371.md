Circular to SFC Licensed Fund Managers and Investment Advisers
FAQs for Investors on UCITS III

We wish to invite you to disseminate to your business partners and clients our set of
frequently asked questions (FAQs) for investors on Undertakings for Collective Investment
in Transferable Securities III (UCITS III).
SFC authorised funds which are domiciled in the EU member states will generally be
affected by the new UCITS III regulations issued by the EU Commission. Existing EU funds
set up under the UCITS I framework, are required by the laws of their home countries to
convert into UCITS III funds by February 2007 or earlier. To provide investors with some
general information and to address some basic queries regarding UCITS III, the SFC has
prepared a set of FAQs set out in the Annex. The e-version of the FAQs is available under
“Funds” in the “Features and Dr Wise” section of the SFC-operated investor portal, the
Electronic Investor Resources Centre (eIRC) at www.eirc.hk.
You are welcome to distribute the FAQs to your business partners and clients, or carry them
in your client communications with acknowledgement of the source of the information. If you
have any queries on quoting the FAQs in your company’s publications, please contact our
Investor Education and Communications Department at 2840 9219.
