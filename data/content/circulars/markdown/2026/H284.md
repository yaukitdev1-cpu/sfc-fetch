Circular to Licensed Corporations
Observe the Know Your Clients Rules to Avoid Losses
This circular is to alert licensed corporations to be on the look out for fraudulent activities
perpetrated by walk-in clients. The Commission urges licensed corporations to protect
themselves from fraud by observing the “Know Your Clients” Rules and putting in place
effective control measures. Failure to observe the Rules may result in substantial financial
losses to your firm due to fraud.
The Commission has recently received a number of reports regarding fraud by walk-in clients.
Two circulars and a press release have already been issued in the past two years to alert
intermediaries of such fraudulent activities.
The Common Practice
The Commission strongly urges licensed corporations to pay extra attention to walk-in clients.
The common practices used by fraudsters in the guise of walk-in clients are as follows:
-

They use false/forged identity cards and proof of address when opening trading
accounts with brokerages; they then place orders to buy inactively traded stocks and
in unusually large quantities;
They produce pay-in slips that show that funds have been deposited into the broker’s
bank account but these cheques will eventually bounce after the purchases have been
concluded; or they produce forged cashier’s orders which get rejected. By the time
this happens, the fraudster(s) are nowhere to be found leaving the brokers to bear the
responsibility to settle the purchases.

General guidelines to prevent losses
The Commission would like to remind licensed corporations to observe the “Know Your
Clients” Rules and ensure effective control procedures are put in place and enhanced if
necessary to protect your operations from fraud and other dishonest acts.
It is in your interests to ensure that you have:
-

carefully verified your clients’ identity and their contact details and asked for the
original identity and proof of address documents
