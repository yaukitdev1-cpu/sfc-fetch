Invitation to Seminars on the New Requirements to Address Risks Arising from
Securities Margin Financing
The Commission will conduct two seminars in August 2006 on the new measures to
address risks arising from securities margin financing in order to help licensed corporations,
which are dealing in securities or providing securities margin financing, to understand the
new requirements under the following Amendment Rules:
-

Securities and Futures (Financial Resources) (Amendment) Rules 2006;
Securities and Futures (Client Securities) (Amendment) Rules 2006; and
Securities and Futures (Contract Notes, Statements of Account and Receipts)
(Amendment) Rules 2006;

and amendments to Code of Conduct for Persons Licensed by or Registered with the
Securities and Futures Commission which will come into effect on 1 October 2006.
