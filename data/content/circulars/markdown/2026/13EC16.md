Circular

2 May 2013

Circular to Licensed Corporations in relation to the reporting of OTC
derivatives transactions to the HKMA trade repository – launch of the trade
repository service and the Service Agreement

Reference is made to the circular in December 2012 in which the Hong Kong Monetary
Authority (HKMA) provided an implementation schedule for the reporting service for over-thecounter (OTC) derivatives (Reporting Service) to be offered by the trade repository (TR) of
the HKMA.
The HKMA announced on 30 April 2013 that it intends to launch the Reporting Service by
July 2013 according to the implementation schedule and users of the Reporting Service have
to enter into a contractual agreement with the HKMA.
The letter issued by the HKMA to all Authorized Institutions regarding the above
announcement, as well as the revised reference manual for the scope and enrolment
procedures of the Reporting Service are attached to this circular.
It is also mentioned in HKMA’s letter that an Interim Reporting Arrangement (Arrangement)
will be implemented to require licensed banks to report OTC derivatives transactions to the
TR of the HKMA before the Securities and Futures Ordinance can be amended to mandate
TR reporting. Although such Arrangement does not apply to Licensed Corporations,
Licensed Corporations who are interested to participate in the Arrangement on a voluntary
basis are reminded to complete the Reporting Service Agreement, the relevant enrolment
procedures and the relevant technical and logistical arrangements in time.
Should you have any queries regarding the above, please contact the TR of the HKMA at its
hotline at 8100 3115 or through email to hktr@hkma.gov.hk.
