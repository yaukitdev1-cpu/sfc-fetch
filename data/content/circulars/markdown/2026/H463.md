Circular to Licensed Corporations and Associated Entities


Apply for a New Password to Access


Joint Financial Intelligence Unit’s New Website


The Joint Financial Intelligence Unit (“JFIU”) of the HKSAR Government has launched a new website in a more user-friendly layout.  Licensed corporations (“LCs”) and associated entities (“AEs”) can continue to find the Quarterly Suspicious Transaction Report Analysis (“Quarterly Analysis Reports”) issued by the JFIU on this  new website (http://www.jfiu.gov.hk) under the section of “Typologies and Feedback” – “Feedback”. The latest release includes reports of the 3rd and 4th quarter of year 2006 and 1st quarter of year 2007.

To preserve the confidentiality of such information, only authorized persons with the necessary passwords are allowed access to such information.  As this secured area is now operating under a new platform, the passwords currently used by LCs and AEs to access such information will be invalid after 31 July 2007.  Compliance officers have to re-apply for a new password.  Please complete the attached registration form and send it to JFIU as follows:


           By fax to: 	(852) 2529-4013

           By e-mail to: 	jfiu@police.gov.hk

If you have any questions in relation to the application procedures, please contact Mr. Ricky Wong of the JFIU directly on 2860-3559.

The Quarterly Analysis Reports issued by the JFIU are one of the useful sources where LCs and AEs can find information such as sanitized examples of actual money laundering cases that are highly relevant to the securities and futures industry.   The latest release of the reports provides examples of suspicious transactions and fund transfer patterns, illustrates what JFIU considers to be good due diligence procedures, and highlights areas which require improvement in order to enhance the quality of suspicious transaction reporting. LCs and AEs should draw on these examples and guidelines to improve their customer due diligence procedures and to provide training to their staff.

LCs and AEs are reminded that each individual has the legal and regulatory obligations to report suspicious transactions to the JFIU.  In order to meet such obligations, they should have systems to enable their staff to detect unusual or suspicious activities and report them to their compliance officers.  LCs and AEs should also educate and train their staff regularly so as to create and maintain their awareness and to remind them of their obligations to report suspicious transactions.

Should you have any queries regarding the contents of this circular, please contact Ms. Kammy Kwok on 2840-9455.
