Circular to All Licensed Corporations
Step Up Preparedness for Human Swine Influenza in Hong Kong
The World Health Organisation has now escalated its pandemic alert level from Phase 5 to 6.
Further to our circular dated 30 April 2009, the Commission would like to urge licensed
corporations to put in place necessary measures to limit the potential impact of human swine
influenza to their operations and workforce. In so doing, licensed corporations should refer to
relevant circulars issued by the SFC 1 and guidance from the Centre for Health Protection 2
and, in particular, consider the following measures (which should not be taken to be
exhaustive or comprehensive):
Oversight and monitoring
• establish a clear command and communication structure for crisis management and
effective monitoring of the latest developments of human swine influenza pandemic;
Staff arrangement
• Prepare for split team arrangement, alterative site office or staff working from home,
whilst ensuring sufficient equipment and devices to support such arrangements;
• ensure sufficient back-up personnel for a staff member who is either suspected or has
been diagnosed as having a human swine influenza;
Service providers or other third parties
• plan ahead for contingency closing out of positions, clearing and settlement matters with
counterparts or exchanges if needed;
• for operations outsourced to third parties, ensure that the service providers have
appropriate contingency plans in place;
Customers
• encourage customers to use telephone or online facilities in place of physical branch
presence;
• plan for any customer communication and assurance if needed; and
Infrastructure capacity
• ensure that there is sufficient infrastructure capacity to support contingency measures for
a prolonged period and increased demand for online and self-service options.
Licensed corporations should inform the Commission immediately if any of their staff is either
suspected of or has been diagnosed as having human swine influenza.
