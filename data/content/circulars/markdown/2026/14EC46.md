Circular

13 November 2014
Circular to all Licensed Corporations and Registered Institutions concerning the U.S.
Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act
This Circular is issued further to the Commission’s Circular of 15 May 2014 for the purpose
of informing licensed corporations and registered institutions that the governments of the
Hong Kong SAR and the United States of America have concluded an inter-governmental
agreement (IGA), which is intended to facilitate compliance with the Foreign Account Tax
Compliance Act (FATCA) by financial institutions in Hong Kong.
The Hong Kong SAR Government has issued a press release advising that the IGA was
signed on 13 November 2014. The Hong Kong SAR Government has also published the IGA
and an updated set of frequently asked questions (FAQs) providing background information.
The press release, the IGA and the updated FAQs are available through the following links:
Press release: http://www.fstb.gov.hk/fsb/ppr/press/doc/pr131114_e.pdf
IGA: http://www.fstb.gov.hk/fsb/topical/doc/HK-USIGA.pdf
FAQs: http://www.fstb.gov.hk/fsb/topical/doc/fatca-faq2_e.pdf
Licensed corporations and registered institutions are strongly encouraged to consider
whether they are affected by the obligations imposed on foreign financial institutions under
FATCA and to take appropriate action. If licensed corporations and registered institutions are
in doubt concerning their obligations under FATCA, they are encouraged to seek appropriate
legal or other advice.
