Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Financing of Terrorism Webinar
The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) will host two sessions of anti-money
laundering and counter-financing of terrorism (AML/CFT) webinar on 30 November and 1
December 2022 respectively. The webinar will be conducted via Zoom.
Objectives and rundown
The objectives of the webinar are –


To present the key findings of the latest Hong Kong’s Money Laundering and Terrorist
Financing Risk Assessment Report including the money laundering threats and
vulnerabilities for the securities and virtual assets sectors, the terrorist financing and
proliferation financing risks of Hong Kong;



To provide an update on major AML/CFT regulatory developments;



To share inspection findings and other supervisory observations on AML/CFT; and



To present money laundering typologies and feedback on the suspicious transaction
reporting of the securities sector.

Please refer to Appendix for rundown and speakers of the webinar.
Who should attend
You are invited to nominate relevant management / supervisory personnel and compliance
officers to attend. The webinar is free of charge and 3 CPT hours will be offered to those who
attend the whole session.
Enrolment
Each firm should only submit one enrolment form via the following link on or before 11
November 2022:
https://www.sfc.hk/en/News-and-announcements/Events/Anti-Money-Laundering-andCounter-Financing-of-Terrorism-Webinar-2022
Enrolments are subject to a maximum of two representatives per firm. We will allocate places
to the first nominees listed in enrolment forms submitted to the SFC by the deadline on a first
come first served basis. Any remaining places will be allocated to the second nominees on
the same basis.
