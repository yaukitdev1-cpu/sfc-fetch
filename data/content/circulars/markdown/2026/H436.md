Circular to intermediaries1 licensed or registered for Type 6 regulated activity
Sponsor regime
In view of the forthcoming implementation of the Guidelines for Sponsors and Compliance
Advisers (“Sponsor Guidelines”) on 1 January 2007, we would like to draw your attention to the
following matters concerning your licence or registration under the Securities and Futures
Ordinance (“SFO”).
Under the new regime, only intermediaries that meet the eligibility criteria set out in the Sponsor
Guidelines will be eligible to act as sponsors and/or compliance advisers. The onus is on the
intermediary and its management to demonstrate that the firm should satisfy all necessary
requirements of the Sponsor Guidelines before it can engage in such activities. The Sponsor
Guidelines were inserted as part of the Fit and Proper Guidelines and were gazetted on 1
September 2006.
Existing intermediaries that DO NOT INTEND to act as sponsors after 1 January 2007
If you do not intend to act as sponsor on or after 1 January 2007, please complete and return to us
the attached Reply Slip. You will be imposed with a licensing condition to the effect that you
will not be allowed to act as sponsors or undertake sponsor work (in relation to Type 6 regulated
activity) on 1 January 2007. To assist with our planning, please complete and return the attached
Reply Slip by 6 October 2006.
Where a corporation is licensed for Type 6 regulated activity and the licence is subject to the “no
sponsor work” licensing condition imposed pursuant to Section 116(6)2/Section 119(5)3 of the
SFO, the corporation will qualify for a lower financial resources requirement in terms of the level
of paid-up share capital. In this regard, we refer you to our circular entitled “Securities and
Futures (Financial Resources) (Amendment) Rules 2006 for Sponsors” 4 dated 5 September 2006.
The Securities and Futures (Financial Resources) (Amendment) Rules 2006 (the “FRR
Amendment”) regarding the minimum paid-up share capital requirements for licensed
corporations acting as sponsors will come into effect on 1 August 2007.

Existing intermediaries that INTEND to act as sponsors after 1 January 2007
If you intend to act or continue to act as sponsor, you must make a written submission to the SFC
as soon as practicable to demonstrate that you will meet the new eligibility criteria to act as a
sponsor pursuant to the Sponsor Guidelines. The SFC will assess, based on the information in the
submission, as to whether you are eligible to act as a sponsor and to determine whether you shall
be permitted under your licence or certificate of registration5 to act or continue to act as sponsor.
The sponsor submission forms6 for licensed corporations and registered institutions for Type 6
regulated activity can be downloaded from the SFC website (http://www.sfc.hk). If there are any
subsequent changes in the information provided in the forms, you must notify the SFC
immediately.
For registered institutions, a copy of the submission forms should be sent to Hong Kong
Monetary Authority at the time of submission to the SFC.
As the Sponsor Guidelines will come into effect on 1 January 2007, we suggest that:
•

if you intend to act as sponsor after 1 January 2007, you should ensure that you will meet the
eligibility criteria under the Sponsor Guidelines, including the minimum paid-up capital
requirement of HK$10 million, and make the relevant submission to the SFC as soon as
practicable;

•

if you do not intend to act as sponsor, you should submit the Reply Slip to the SFC by 6
October 2006 in order that you may qualify for the lower financial resources requirements
when the FRR Amendment provisions relating to the minimum paid-up share capital
requirement for sponsors come into effect on 1 August 2007.

Enquiry
For more information on the Sponsor Guidelines and the sponsor regime, please refer to the
Frequently Asked Questions7 on the SFC website. Should you have any queries, please contact
your responsible Intermediaries Relationship Manager.
