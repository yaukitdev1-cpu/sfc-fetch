Circular to All Licensed Corporations
Reminder of Prudent Risk Management Measures
In light of recent market volatility and the forthcoming Lunar New Year holiday when overseas
markets will remain open, the SFC reminds all licensed corporations to vigilantly review their
risk exposures and where appropriate, take precautionary measures.
In particular, licensed corporations are reminded to


promptly collect outstanding margin calls from clients, especially those offshore clients who
may not have access to banking services during their local public holidays;



vigilantly monitor changes in market conditions and rigorously assess their impact on the
firm’s risk exposures and liquid capital, such as by conducting stress testing; and



ensure the firm has adequate capital and liquidity for operating its business and meeting its
obligations to clients and other counterparties.

The abovementioned measures are by no means exhaustive. Firms should take into account
their own circumstances and aim to ensure that the risks associated with their business and
operations are identified and managed in an appropriate and timely manner1.
