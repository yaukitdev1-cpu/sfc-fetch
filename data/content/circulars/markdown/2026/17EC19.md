Circular to All Intermediaries, Responsible Officers and Representatives
Regarding the Waiver of Annual Licensing Fees
This circular is issued to remind all intermediaries, responsible officers and representatives of
the annual licensing fee waiver announced on 24 March 20161.
The objective of the Annual Fee Waiver is to relieve licensed corporations, registered
institutions, responsible officers and representatives of the obligation to pay the annual
licensing fees that would otherwise be payable by them during the period from 1 April 2016
to 31 March 2018. Accordingly, the Commission will not issue a demand for payment for any
annual licensing fee that would otherwise become payable up to and including 31 March
2018.
The fees payable in connection with new licence applications and transfers will not be
waived.
