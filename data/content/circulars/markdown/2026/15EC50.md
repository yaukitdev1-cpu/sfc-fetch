Circular

9 October 2015

Circular to applicants of SFC-authorized Mandatory Provident Fund (MPF)
products and pooled retirement funds (PRFs)
Application lapse policy
1.

The purpose of this circular is to inform applicants seeking the SFC’s authorization
of (i) new MPF products (including MPF schemes, constituent funds and pooled
investment funds) pursuant to the SFC Code on MPF Products and Code on Unit
Trusts and Mutual Funds (where applicable) and (ii) PRFs pursuant to the Code on
Pooled Retirement Funds of the application lapse policy to be adopted under a pilot
arrangement with effect from 9 November 2015 (“Effective Date”).

2.

The application lapse policy will apply for a 6-month pilot period ending 8 May 2016
(“Pilot Period”). We may extend the Pilot Period and/or make changes to the
application lapse policy where appropriate at the end of the Pilot Period.

Background
3.

We regularly review and seek to improve our authorization process wherever
practicable without compromising investor interests and protection.

4.

In order to enhance overall efficiency in the authorization of MPF and PRF products
and taking into account recommendations of the Process Review Panel for the SFC,
we have decided, after consulting key stakeholders, to apply a 6-month application
lapse policy (“6-month application lapse policy”) to all MPF and PRF products
seeking the SFC’s authorization in line with the 6-month application lapse period
currently applied to other SFC-authorized investment products.

6-month application lapse policy
5.

All applications for authorization of new MPF and PRF products received by the
SFC on or after the Effective Date will be processed as follows:
(a)

If, for any reason, 6 months have elapsed from the date of take-up of an
authorization application by the SFC (“Take-up Date”) and no authorization has
been granted by the SFC, the application will lapse subject to the SFC’s right
to grant an extension at its sole discretion in limited circumstances. The
application fee in respect of the application will not be refunded to the
applicant.

(b)

Once an application has lapsed, if the applicant wishes to seek the SFC’s
authorization of the same product again, it shall make a new application,
whereupon it should pay the application fee for the new application and repeat
the SFC’s application procedures.

6.

As new MPF products also require approval of the Mandatory Provident Fund
Schemes Authority (“MPFA”) (the primary regulator of MPF products), applications
for any new MPF products must first be submitted to the MPFA for its review. The
approval-in-principle letter issued by the MPFA will be a prerequisite for making the
application to the SFC and must be submitted to the SFC at the time of application.
The 6-month application period will commence from the Take-up Date during which,
the SFC will process and review the application.

7.

It is the responsibility of all applicants to ensure that the relevant laws, regulations
and guidelines applicable to MPF or PRF products are complied with.

Revised performance pledges
8.

To facilitate the implementation of the 6-month application lapse policy, the SFC will
pledge to (i) take up or refuse to take up a new application for authorization of the
relevant MPF and PRF product(s) within 5 business days upon receipt of the
application; and (ii) issue the first set of responses / requisitions within 14 business
days from the Take-up Date (“Revised Performance Pledges”).

9.

The Revised Performance Pledges will enable the SFC to conduct a more thorough
initial screening and assessment of the new product applications.

10.

The Revised Performance Pledges will apply to all new applications for
authorization of MPF and PRF products that are taken up by the SFC under the 6month application lapse policy.

Revised application form and compliance checklists and new Frequently Asked Questions
(“FAQs”) on applications
11.

In light of the introduction of the 6-month application lapse policy, the form for the
“Application for Authorization of Mandatory Provident Fund Products or Pooled
Retirement Funds under Part IV of the Securities and Futures Ordinance”1 (the
“Application Form”) has been revised and posted on the SFC’s website replacing
the existing version with effect from the Effective Date. Standardised templates for
confirmations of compliance required to be submitted by the applicants at the time of
application and before the SFC’s authorization (if granted) becoming effective are
included in the respective compliance checklists1 posted on the SFC’s website with
effect from the Effective Date, and for MPF products, shall replace the existing
versions.

12.

A set of FAQs in respect of the 6-month application lapse policy for both the MPF
and PRF products have been prepared and posted on the SFC’s website2.

Applications received before the Effective Date
13.

Existing applications for authorization of MPF and PRF products received by the
SFC before the Effective Date in respect of which no authorization has been granted
will not be subject to the 6-month application lapse policy.

14.

Applicants who wish to make applications for authorization of new MPF and PRF
products during the period from the date of this circular but before the Effective Date
should use the existing application form3 and compliance checklists3 in support of
their applications.

Applications received on or after the Effective Date
15.

Applications for authorization of new MPF and PRF products received on or after
the Effective Date will be processed under the 6-month application lapse policy.
Applicants must, among other requirements, complete the revised application form
and the respective latest version of compliance checklist available on the SFC’s
website in support of their applications.

Review of the 6-month application lapse policy
16.

The SFC will review the operation of the 6-month application lapse policy during the
Pilot Period.

17.

Further changes to the 6-month application lapse policy may be considered and/or
introduced by the SFC with a view to further enhancing the 6-month application
lapse policy where appropriate.

General
18.

We believe that this 6-month application lapse policy initiative should encourage
applicants to respond to requisitions in a timely fashion, so that more expedited
process can be achieved. Whilst this policy is facilitative, quality and timely
submissions are essential to enhancing the overall application process.

19.

Should you wish to clarify any aspects of the 6-month application lapse policy, this
circular, the revised Application Form, compliance checklists and the new FAQs
and/or in case of doubt, please contact the relevant case officers in charge.
