Circular to All Licensed Corporations
Reminder of Importance of Prudent Risk Management and Effective Controls

In view of recent market volatility, the SFC reminds all licensed corporations the importance of
implementing appropriate risk management and effective internal controls.
Senior management of licensed corporations are responsible for properly managing the risks
and operations of their firms, including evaluation of the firm’s risk exposures and
maintenance of appropriate risk management policies and control procedures and adequate
resources.
Under changing market conditions, any failure to prudently manage risks, or mistakes or
omissions in operations may result in significant adverse financial and/or operational
consequences. In light of current market condition, senior management of licensed
corporations should vigilantly manage the firm’s financial risks and ensure that the firm has
adequate capital and liquidity for operating its business and meeting its obligations to clients
and other counterparties. In particular, senior management are reminded to:


regularly review the effectiveness of their risk management policies and procedures on
liquidity, credit and market risks and ensure that the policies and procedures are
up-to-date and strictly adhered to;



prudently review the credit limits, trading limits and position limits granted to individual
client accounts to ensure that the limits are commensurate with the client’s financial status
and the firm’s financial resources and appropriate under the current market condition;



promptly collect outstanding margin calls from clients;



closely monitor concentration risks in client credits, client collateral and proprietary
positions and avoid undue concentration risk; and



rigorously assess and monitor the impacts on the firm’s financial position of extreme
market conditions, such as by conducting stress testing of the firm’s liquid capital and
liquidity regularly and in times of volatile market movements.

Senior management are also reminded to diligently supervise their business operations in
order to mitigate operational risks and safeguard their business operations and clients’
interest. In particular, they should put in place appropriate and effective operational and IT
controls1 for preventing and detecting errors, omissions and improper activities and ensuring
compliance with applicable law and regulatory requirements and regularly review the
effectiveness of such controls.
