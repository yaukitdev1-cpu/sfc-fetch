Circular to Management Companies of SFC-authorised Funds
Streamlined Measures to Enhance the Processing of Application for Scheme
Changes and Revision of Offering Documents of SFC-authorised Funds
1.

The purpose of this circular is to introduce the streamlined measures with an aim to
enhance the processing of applications for the approval of scheme changes and/or
authorisation of revised offering documents of SFC-authorised funds (collectively,
referred to as “Applications”).

Background
2.

Currently, all scheme changes that fall within 11.1 of the Code on Unit Trusts and Mutual
Funds (the “UT Code”) must be submitted to the Commission for prior approval and
prior notification is normally required to be provided to holders before the changes are
to take effect. The revised Hong Kong offering documents reflecting such changes must
also be submitted to the Commission for authorisation.

3.

The Commission regularly reviews and seeks to improve its process wherever
practicable without compromising investor interests and protection. In order to facilitate
management companies for more efficient management of their SFC-authorised funds,
the Commission has introduced the following streamlined measures pursuant to which
prior approval would not be required from the Commission in respect of certain
immaterial changes proposed to be made to the relevant SFC-authorised funds and
consequential amendments to their offering documents (collectively, referred to as
“Immaterial Changes and Related Revised Documents”).

Scheme changes and revised offering documents
4.

With respect to applications for scheme changes, the Commission is prepared to
streamline the prior approval and/or notice requirements in 11.1 and 11.1A of the UT
Code and adopt a post-vetting approach in respect of certain immaterial changes to
SFC-authorised funds.

5.

Prior approval is not required from the Commission in respect of changes that might fall
within 11.1(c) of the UT Code (“Immaterial Changes”) provided that the following
overriding principles and requirements (the “Overriding Requirements”) are satisfied:

6.

(i)

the changes do not amount to a material change to the fund;

(ii)

there will be no material change or increase in the overall risk profile of the fund
following the changes; and

(iii)

the changes do not materially prejudice the rights or interests of investors of the
fund.

No further authorisation of the revised offering document of an existing SFC-authorised
fund is required to be obtained from the Commission to the extent it solely reflects the
Immaterial Changes.

However, the management companies are required to file the Immaterial Changes and

8.

Notwithstanding the streamlined measures mentioned above, the Commission will
continue to monitor compliance with UT Code through post-vetting of the Immaterial
Changes and Related Revised Documents filed with the Commission in a vigilant
manner.

9.

In order to provide further guidance to the industry, illustrative examples of Immaterial
Changes and Related Revised Documents can be found in the updated Frequently
Asked Questions on Post Authorisation Compliance Issues of SFC-Authorised Unit
Trusts and Mutual Funds (“Updated FAQs”) published on our website today.

10. The illustrative examples mentioned in the Updated FAQs are not intended to be
exhaustive and are subject to amendments and updates from time to time, as and
where appropriate. Management companies are encouraged to contact the Investment
Products Division in case of doubt concerning specific circumstances.
Obligations of the management companies
11. The streamlined measures mentioned above are expected to enhance the flexibility and
efficiency of management companies when making changes to SFC-authorised funds
and their offering documents. Management companies are reminded of their duty to
use due care and professional judgement to decide whether a scheme change or
revised offering documents would meet the Overriding Requirements and such other
applicable requirements as set out in the related Updated FAQs.
12. Management companies are further reminded of their obligations to ensure that the
offering documents (including the product key facts statements) of the SFC-authorised
funds under their management are up-to-date and contain the information necessary for
investors to make an informed judgement of the investment proposed to them.
Management companies should also keep investors informed of information concerning
their funds which is necessary to enable investors to appraise the position of the funds
from time to time.
13. Management companies should ensure that SFC-authorised funds under their
management and the offering documents are compliant with the UT Code and other
applicable principles and requirements contained in the SFC’s Handbook for Unit Trusts
and Mutual Funds, Investment-linked Assurance Schemes and Unlisted Structured
Investment Products, and other guidelines that are published by the Commission from
time to time.
General
14. The streamlined measures mentioned above shall take effect on 24 June, 2013 (the
“Effective Date”) following the publication of this circular and will apply to all Applications

submitted on or after the Effective Date. For all existing Applications, which include
Immaterial Changes and Related Revised Documents, submitted to the Commission
before the Effective Date but authorisation in respect of which have not been granted as
at the Effective Date (“Existing Applications”), the management companies are
encouraged to adopt the streamlined measures to the extent applicable. The
management companies may, however, choose to continue with the Existing
Applications and not make use of the streamlined measures.
15. The Commission will continue to conduct post-vetting of Immaterial Changes and
Related Revised Documents filed with us pursuant to the streamlined measures to
monitor compliance. The Commission may make follow-up enquiries and/or take other
regulatory actions as considered appropriate. Management companies are expected to
respond to any enquiries made by the Commission promptly and in an open and cooperative manner.
16. The Commission will keep in view of the streamlined measures and may impose
additional requirements/changes from time to time where appropriate. The Commission
retains its power to require management companies to submit any proposed scheme
changes and/or revised offering documents for review where the Commission considers
it appropriate with respect to particular cases.
17. If you wish to clarify any aspects of this circular and/or in case of doubt on any issues
arising from the application of the streamlined measures mentioned above, please
contact the team supervisor or case officer of the Investment Products Division who is
responsible for overseeing the SFC-authorised funds of your fund group or client.
