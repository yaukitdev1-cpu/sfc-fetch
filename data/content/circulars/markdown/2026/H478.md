26 October 2007

Circular to Licensed Corporations
Investor Leaflet “Index Tracking Exchange Traded Funds”

We wish to invite you to display and distribute to your clients our updated investor leaflet,
“Index Tracking Exchange Traded Funds” (ETFs), the latest publication of the SFC’s new
investor leaflet series.
The leaflet explains the key features, benefits and risks of SFC-authorised ETFs, as well as
the major differences between ETFs and unlisted index funds. Both the English and Chinese
versions of the leaflet are available.
We have also attached a full list of our new investor leaflets at the Annex. The electronic
versions of these investor leaflets are available on the “Publications” section of the SFCoperated investor portal, the InvestEd website at www.InvestEd.hk.
To allow investors to check the availability of these leaflets, the names and addresses of the
licensed corporations distributing them will also be posted on the “Publications” section.
Please contact Ms Gloria Chan at 2842 7711 concerning requests for copies of the leaflets.

Annex
SFC New Investor Leaflets (Available for Distribution)

1. Index Tracking Exchange Traded Funds – discusses the key features, benefits and
risks of SFC-authorised exchange traded funds (ETFs), as well as the major differences
between ETFs and unlisted index funds.
2. Know Your Broker – explains to investors the key factors to consider when choosing a
broker as well as the account opening, dealing and settlement process.
3. IPO Investing – explains what investors should note when subscribing for IPO shares
and the risks involved.
4. Fund Investing – provides a checklist of questions for investors to ask before buying a
fund e.g. the features, dealings, fees and charges of a fund.
5. Hedge Funds - discusses the basic concepts and risks of hedge funds, and issues to note
when investors consider buying these funds.
6. How to Make a Complaint – explains to investors where they can channel complaints if
misconduct occurs in the securities and futures markets, and the SFC’s procedures and
powers in handling investor complaints.
