Circular to SFC’s Registered Investment Adviser Firms
Submission of Audited Accounts
under the Securities and Futures Ordinance
We write to remind you of your obligation to submit audited accounts under the Securities and
Futures Ordinance (“SFO”), which is targeted to commence on 1 April 2003.
Under the existing regime, an investment adviser firm is required to submit its audited accounts to the
SFC together with its annual return on or before its registration anniversary date each year, and that
the audited accounts should be made up to the last day of the financial year which expired on a date
not more than 16 months before the date of the annual return. These submission requirements are
going to change.
Upon the commencement of the SFO, a corporation, a sole proprietor or a partnership currently
registered as an investment adviser under the Securities Ordinance will be deemed to be a corporation
licensed for Type 4 (advising on securities), Type 6 (advising on corporate finance) and Type 9 (asset
management) regulated activities for a period of two years, subject to any licensing conditions where
applicable.
As required under section 156(1)(b) of the SFO, a licensed corporation, or an associated entity1 of an
intermediary2, shall submit its financial statements and other relevant documents, together with an
auditor’s report, to the SFC not later than 4 months after the end of the financial year to which
these documents relate.
Your attention is drawn to the changes of the requirements for submission of audited accounts. These
changes will take effect immediately after the SFO commences, and no transitional or grace period
will be provided for compliance with the new requirements.
Please be reminded that failure to comply with the submission requirements would impugn your
fitness and properness as a licensed person and may render you subject to prosecution pursuant to
sections 156(5) and (6) of the SFO. You should therefore be aware of the changes and make
necessary arrangements to comply with the new requirements under the SFO. Should you have any
queries on the above, please contact your responsible Intermediaries Relationship Manager of the
Licensing Department.

Licensing Department
Securities and Futures Commission
1

“Associated entity” is a company which is in a controlling entity relationship with you, and receives or holds
in Hong Kong your client assets. “Controlling entity relationship” is defined in Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the
SFO. Section 156 of the SFO does not apply to an associated entity that is an authorized financial institution.

2

As defined under Schedule 1 to the SFO, an “intermediary” means a licensed corporation or a registered
institution.
