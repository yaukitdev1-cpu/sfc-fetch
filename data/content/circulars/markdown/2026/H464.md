Circular to Licensed Corporations
Investor Leaflet “Know Your Broker”

We wish to invite you to display and distribute to your clients our new investor education
leaflet, “Know Your Broker”.
“Know Your Broker” explains to investors the key factors to consider when choosing a
broker as well as the account opening, dealing and settlement process. Both the English and
Chinese versions of the leaflet are available.
To allow investors to check the availability of the leaflet, the names and addresses of the
licensed corporations distributing this leaflet will be posted on the “Publications” section of
the SFC-operated investor portal, the InvestEd website at www.InvestEd.hk.
Please contact Ms Gloria Chan at 2842 7711 concerning requests for copies of the leaflet.
