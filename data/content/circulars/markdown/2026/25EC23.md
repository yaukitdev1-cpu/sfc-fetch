Mutual Recognition of Funds (MRF) between Ireland and Hong Kong
1.

The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) and the Central Bank of Ireland (CBI)
entered into a memorandum of understanding dated 5 November 1997 pursuant to
which the SFC and CBI declared their intention to provide to each other the fullest
possible cooperation in relation to the supervision of cross-border investment
management activity. The SFC and the CBI entered a further memorandum of
understanding on 14 May 2025 concerning mutual recognition of covered funds and
covered management companies and related cooperation (Memorandum). The
purpose of the Memorandum is to enhance cooperation in relation to (i) collective
investment schemes domiciled in either Hong Kong or Ireland and offered to the public
in Ireland and/or Hong Kong on a cross-border basis, and (ii) management companies
of collective investment schemes, based in either Hong Kong or Ireland.

2.

In this circular, “Irish Covered Fund” refers to an Irish fund that is eligible for or have
received SFC authorisation under MRF, while “Irish Covered Management
Company” refers to an Irish management company that is eligible to manage Irish
Covered Funds.

General principles
3.

MRF operates on the principles that, in respect of a CBI-authorised Irish Covered
Fund that is seeking or has received authorisation for offering to the public in Hong
Kong:
a. the Irish Covered Fund shall meet the eligibility requirements in accordance
with this circular and comply with all of the applicable requirements set out in
this circular (see below);
b. the Irish Covered Fund shall remain authorised by the CBI and be allowed to
be offered, marketed and distributed to retail investors in Ireland;
c. the Irish Covered Fund shall operate and be managed in accordance with
the relevant laws and regulations in Ireland and its constitutive documents;
d. the sale and distribution of the Irish Covered Fund in Hong Kong shall
comply with the applicable laws and regulations in Hong Kong;
e. where relevant, the Irish Covered Fund and the Irish Covered Management
Company shall comply with the additional rules released by the SFC
governing the authorisation, post-authorisation and ongoing compliance in
the context of the offering, marketing and distribution of the Irish Covered
Fund to the public in Hong Kong;
f.

the Irish Covered Management Company of the Irish Covered Fund shall
ensure that investors in both Ireland and Hong Kong receive fair treatment,

4.

In general, funds that are seeking or have received SFC authorisation for offering to
the public in Hong Kong pursuant to Section 104 of the Securities and Futures
Ordinance (Chapter 571 of the Laws of Hong Kong) (SFO) must comply with the
SFC Handbook for Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds, Investment- Linked Assurance
Schemes and Unlisted Structured Investment Products (SFC Handbook) and the
circulars, guidelines and other requirements as may be issued by the SFC from time
to time, together with the SFO, relevant Hong Kong laws and regulations. Based on
the principles set out above, if an Irish Covered Fund complies with the relevant Irish
laws and regulations and the conditions as set out in this circular, it is generally
deemed to have complied in substance with the relevant Hong Kong laws and
regulations and will enjoy a streamlined process of authorisation for offering to the
public in Hong Kong.

5.

In view of the differences between the regulatory regimes in Hong Kong and Ireland,
to ensure proper investor protection and consistency with the existing Hong Kong
regime for authorised retail funds, this circular sets out the additional requirements
with which an Irish Covered Fund must comply when applying for SFC authorisation
pursuant to Section 104 of the SFO for offering to the public in Hong Kong under the
Memorandum, as well as other requirements which an Irish Covered Management
Company and an Irish Covered Fund must observe after obtaining SFC
authorisation. The CBI will issue a separate circular regarding the authorisation of
eligible Hong Kong funds for public offering in Ireland.

6.

The existing Acceptable Inspection Regimes on managers and Recognised
Jurisdiction Schemes on funds, and the streamlined measures currently adopted by
the SFC in processing the authorisation of undertakings for collective investment in
transferable securities (UCITS)1 funds as set out in the “Application of the Code on
Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds on UCITS funds” updated on 6 February 2020 (as
may be amended from time to time) will continue to apply to Irish UCITS that are not
eligible under MRF.

Changes to Irish Covered Funds
14.

Changes to an Irish Covered Fund must be made in accordance with the applicable
Irish and Hong Kong laws and regulations and the provisions of its constitutive
documents.

15.

Any proposed changes to an Irish Covered Fund that fall within 11.1 of the UT
Code4 and revisions made to its offering documents relating to such changes must
be submitted by its Irish Covered Management Company for approval by the SFC in
accordance with the relevant requirements set out in Annex A. These changes may
take effect only upon approval by the SFC and compliance with any relevant notice
requirement.

16.

Any proposed changes to an Irish Covered Fund that do not require the SFC’s prior
approval pursuant to 11.1 of the UT Code and revisions made to its offering
documents relating to such changes must be filed with the SFC and comply with the
relevant requirements set out in Annex A.

17.

Investors in Ireland and Hong Kong must be notified at the same time (so far as is
reasonably practicable given the different time zones and public holidays of the
jurisdictions) about any changes to the Irish Covered Fund by the Irish Covered
Management Company. Equal treatment should be given to investors in both
jurisdictions in terms of the form which that notice takes5.

18.

For avoidance of doubt, changes that render the Irish Covered Fund ineligible under
this MRF (for example, the fund changes strategy and no longer meets the eligible
fund type definition) and revisions made to its offering documents relating to such
changes require the SFC’s prior approval. The Irish Covered Management
Company must notify such changes to the CBI and the SFC as soon as possible.
Breach

19.

In the event of a breach of Irish domestic laws or the requirements set out or
referred to in this circular, which is notifiable to the CBI and could affect Hong Kong
investors in an Irish Covered Fund, the Irish Covered Management Company must
endeavour to notify the CBI and file or report to the SFC at the same time and rectify
the breach promptly6. The Irish Covered Management Company will notify the SFC
once the breach has been rectified.

Following SFC authorisation of an Irish Covered Fund under Section 104 of the

The Irish Covered Fund must not continue its offering to the public in Hong Kong or

Withdrawal of authorisation

21.

Following SFC authorisation of an Irish Covered Fund, if its Irish Covered
Management Company no longer wishes to maintain the authorisation of the fund, it
should apply for withdrawal of authorisation of the fund from the SFC and provide
notice to Hong Kong investors of its intention not to maintain such authorisation in
accordance with the relevant Hong Kong laws and regulations.

22.

The SFC may at any time review its authorisation of an Irish Covered Fund and may
modify, add to or withdraw any of the conditions of such authorisation, or withdraw
the authorisation, as it considers appropriate.

Sale/distribution, offering documents, ongoing disclosure and advertisements
Sale/distribution
23.

The sale and distribution of an Irish Covered Fund in Hong Kong must be conducted
by intermediaries properly licensed by or registered with the SFC and must comply
with the relevant Hong Kong laws and regulations relating to the sale and
distribution of funds.
Offering documents

24.

The disclosure of information relating to an Irish Covered Fund must be complete,
accurate, fair, clear, and effective. It must be easily understood by investors.

25.

The offering documents of an Irish Covered Fund must be up-to-date and contain
information necessary for investors to make informed judgement of the investment
proposed to them.

Application process
35.

Applications of Irish Covered Funds seeking authorisation will be processed under
the “Fund Authorisation Simple Track” (“FASTrack” in short) or a two-stream
approach.

36.

To start an application, the following documents (Application Documents) shall be
submitted to the SFC:
a. a duly signed and completed Application Form;
b. a duly signed and completed Information Checklist9;
c. an advanced draft of the Irish Covered Fund’s offering document in English
(including the product key facts statement(s)) and where applicable, marked
up against the latest version filed with the SFC;
d. the constitutive documents of the Irish Covered Fund(s) in either English or
Chinese;
e. the documents (including any confirmations and/or undertakings) required to
be submitted pursuant to the Information Checklist; and
f.

a cheque made payable to “Securities and Futures Commission” in the
amount of the applicable application fee (see Q.2 of the FAQs on Application
Procedures for Authorization of Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds10).

37.

Upon receipt of the Application Documents, the SFC will assess in five business
days if all Application Documents have been duly and properly submitted.

38.

Applicants shall request the CBI to provide directly to the SFC a certificate
confirming that the Eligibility Requirements listed in Annex B to this circular are met.
The SFC will not take up the application if no such certificate is received from the
CBI.

39.

Eligibility Requirements
Irish Covered Fund
1. The Irish Covered Fund must be established, domiciled and managed in accordance
with Irish laws and regulations and its constitutive documents. It must be a UCITS
which is formally authorised by the CBI in accordance with the UCITS Regulations.
2. The Irish Covered Fund must fall within one of the following eligible fund types14
a. general equity funds, bond funds, mixed funds and funds that invest in other
schemes;
b. feeder funds, where the underlying funds fall within one of the fund types in
paragraphs 2(a), (c), (d) or (e) of this Annex and complies with the
requirements in this circular; or
c. unlisted index funds;
d. passively managed index tracking ETFs;
e. listed open-ended funds (also known as active ETFs15).
3. The Irish Covered Fund must not use leverage (arising from derivatives) exceeding
100% of the fund’s net asset value as calculated under the commitment approach
provided by Schedule 9, paragraph 11(a) of the UCITS Regulations.
4. The Irish Covered Fund must not invest in (i) physical commodities including
precious metals or commodity based investments or real estate, (ii) crypto-assets or
crypto-currencies, or (iii) certificates representing the assets referred to under (i) or
(ii).
5. The Irish Covered Fund must not have share classes with hedging arrangements
other than currency hedging.
6. The Irish Covered Fund must have at least one dealing day for redemption every
two weeks.
