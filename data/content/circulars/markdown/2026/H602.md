Circular to Licensed Corporations and Associated Entities
Anti-Money Laundering / Combating Terrorist Financing
(1) Suspicious Transaction Report – Quarterly Analysis Reports
(2) Suspicious Transaction Report – e-reporting system
(3) Anti-Money Laundering Training Seminar Materials
1)

Suspicious Transaction Report – Quarterly Analysis Report
As part of the Joint Financial Intelligence Unit (“JFIU”)’s effort to heighten AML/CFT
awareness and to provide appropriate feedback on suspicious transaction reporting
(“STR”), the JFIU has been posting their STR Quarterly Analysis Reports in the
secured section of their website (http://www.jfiu.gov.hk). Licensed corporations
(“LCs”) and Associated Entities (“AEs”) are strongly advised to read the reports as
they are useful sources of information where LCs and AEs can find sanitized
examples of actual money laundering cases and other feedback considered of use in
implementing customer due diligence measures, particularly ongoing due diligence
and scrutiny, in a risk sensitive manner.
LCs and AEs who have not already applied for a login name and password to access
the secured section of the JFIU website can do so by completing the registration form
(Attachment 1) and submit it to JFIU by fax to 2529-4013 or by email to
jfiu@police.gov.hk .

2)

Suspicious Transaction Report – e-reporting system
LCs and AEs are also encouraged to submit any STR to the JFIU through the ereporting system provided by JFIU. LCs and AEs who have not already applied for a
login name and password to access the e-reporting system of the JFIU can do so by
completing the registration form (Attachment 2) and submit it to JFIU by fax to 25294013 or by email to jfiu@police.gov.hk .
If you have any questions regarding the application procedures for the above, please
directly contact Mr. Ricky Wong of the JFIU on 2860-3559 or Miss Fung of JFIU on
2860-3357.
