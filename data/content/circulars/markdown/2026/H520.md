Circular to Licensed Corporations
Application for SFC e-Certificate –
Persons who are required to sign and submit half-yearly return

This is the Fourth 1 Circular the SFC has issued to licensed corporations on electronic
submission of financial returns which will become mandatory on 1 November 2008.
Application for SFC e-Certificate
For licensed corporations that are required to sign and submit FRR return on a half-yearly
basis, all relevant persons 2 who are required to sign and submit such return and intend to
use SFC e-Certificate should make their application between now and 24 October 2008.
Two SFC e-Certificates will generally be issued to each licensed corporation. If additional
SFC e-Certificate is applied for, written explanation should be provided to SFC justifying why
such is considered to be necessary.
Application should be made by completing the attached application form (Annex 1), which
can also be downloaded from the SFC’s website 3 and returning the signed copy of
completed application form to the SFC by hand or by post.
Please refer to the Second and Third Circulars for the details of enrolling for our training and
test run of the new e-FRR System respectively. A valid SFC e-Certificate will be needed for
participation in the industry testing.
If you need any other assistance or have any other questions, please contact our e-FRR
System Helpdesk hotline on (852) 2283 6883.

The Applicant shall notify the SFC immediately upon the occurrence of the following:

申請人在使用證監會電子證書時，須遵照證監會的指令及指示。

These Terms and Conditions shall also apply to any renewal or re-issuance of SFC e-Certificate(s).
