Circular

Circular to management companies of
SFC-authorized unit trusts and mutual funds
Formal adoption of revamped fund authorization process
1.

The purpose of this circular is to announce that, with effect from 9 May 2016 (“Adoption
Date”):
a) the enhanced process ("Revamped Process") for applications seeking SFC's
authorization of unit trusts and mutual funds will be formally adopted upon expiry of
the 6-month pilot period ("Pilot Period"); and
b) the Revamped Process will be extended to applications of Mainland funds seeking
authorization under the mutual recognition of funds (“MRF”) arrangement between
the Mainland and Hong Kong (“MRF Applications”).

2.

Unless otherwise defined, capitalized terms in this circular are as defined in the SFC's
circular dated 9 October 2015 entitled "Circular to management companies of SFCauthorized unit trusts and mutual funds - Launch of pilot revamped fund authorization
process" ("Oct 2015 Circular").

Background
3.

Pursuant to the Oct 2015 Circular, the Revamped Process was implemented on 9
November 2015 for the Pilot Period for processing applications seeking SFC’s
authorization of unit trusts and mutual funds other than MRF Applications.

4.

The Revamped Process was introduced with a view to promoting fund providers'
compliance and reducing the overall processing time for new fund applications without
compromising investor protection. For details of the Revamped Process, please refer
to the Oct 2015 Circular.

Smooth implementation of the Revamped Process during the Pilot Period
5.

Since its implementation, the SFC has been closely monitoring the operation of the
Revamped Process. In addition to maintaining an ongoing and open dialogue with the
applicants, the SFC has, during the Pilot Period, continued to engage various industry
participants as well as a technical working group1 in order to obtain a better
understanding of the industry’s feedback on the Revamped Process.

6.

The Revamped Process has received broad industry support and the overall
implementation and experience has been smooth and positive. New fund applications
received under the Revamped Process (including both Standard Applications and Non-

Formal adoption of the Revamped Process with effect from the Adoption Date
Full Adoption of the Revamped Process
7.

As the Revamped Process has facilitated an overall enhancement of the authorization
process, the SFC has decided to extend the use of the Revamped Process beyond the
Pilot Period by formally adopting it as policy with effect from the Adoption Date.

Updated Guide, Updated New Information Checklist and Updated Revamped Process FAQ
8.

The following documents have been revised and published to provide further guidance
and clarification in order to facilitate applicants' preparation for their new fund
applications3, taking into account the operational experience of the SFC and comments
from the industry as well as the technical working group:
a) an updated New Information Checklist (“Updated New Information Checklist”);
b) an updated Guide; and
c) an updated set of Revamped Process FAQ.

Applicability of the Revamped Process for MRF Applications
9.

The Revamped Process will be extended to MRF Applications received by the SFC on
or after the Adoption Date so as to align their processing with other new fund
applications. MRF Applications will be classified into “Standard Applications” stream
and “Non-standard Applications” stream in accordance with the criteria set out in the
Frequently Asked Questions on Mainland-Hong Kong Mutual Recognition of Funds as
amended from time to time.

10. In connection with the extension of the Revamped Process to MRF Applications, the
following documents have been published to provide further guidance in order to
facilitate applicants’ preparation for their new MRF Applications:
a) a new Information Checklist for Application for Authorization of Mainland Funds
under the Mutual Recognition of Funds Arrangement and the Revamped Process

11. For all new MRF Applications received by the SFC on or after the Adoption Date, the
Revamped Process will apply and applicants must, among other requirements,
complete the New MRF Information Checklist in support of their applications. For the
avoidance of doubt, applicants should continue to use the existing Application Form for
Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds, Investment-Linked Assurance Schemes and Unlisted
Structured Investment Products for their new MRF Applications.
New fund applications received before the Adoption Date
12. The Revamped Process will continue to apply to all new fund applications (other than
MRF Applications) received by the SFC until the end of the Pilot Period. For all new
fund applications3 received by the SFC on or before 6 May 2016, applicants may
choose to continue using the existing New Information Checklist. For all new fund
applications3 received by the SFC on or after the Adoption Date, the Revamped
Process will continue to apply and applicants must, among other requirements,
complete the Updated New Information Checklist in support of their applications.
13. Existing MRF Applications that are received by the SFC before the Adoption Date in
respect of which no authorization has been granted will be processed by the SFC in
accordance with the existing authorization process subject to the existing application
lapse policy of 6 months. Applicants who wish to make an MRF Application during the
period from the date of this circular but before the Adoption Date should use the
existing Information Checklist for Application for Authorization of Mainland Funds
seeking SFC’s Authorization under the Mutual Recognition of Funds Arrangement in
support of their applications.
General
14. We will continue to regularly review and seek to improve our authorization process
wherever appropriate and practicable. All SFC-authorized funds processed under the
Revamped Process will be subject to post-vetting and the SFC will, where appropriate,
take necessary regulatory action in cases of non-compliance to ensure investor
protection is not compromised.
