Circular to Licensed Corporations and Associated Entities
Anti-Money Laundering Training Seminar
The Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau, the Joint Financial Intelligence Unit and the
SFC will jointly organize two half-day anti-money laundering seminars on 27 September
2010. The two seminars, conducted in English and Cantonese respectively, will be held at
the Lecture Theatre, G/F, Hong Kong Central Library, Causeway Bay.
Objectives & Rundown
The objectives of the seminars are

To provide the securities sector with an overview of the latest international and domestic
anti-money laundering developments;



To enhance their understanding of the statutory obligations and practices of suspicious
transaction reporting; and



To provide a Q&A session for participants to discuss matters of common concern with
the speakers on their presented topics.

Please refer to Appendix 1 for rundown and speakers of the seminars.
Who should attend
You are invited to nominate management staff and compliance officers engaging in antimoney laundering work to attend. The seminar is free of charge and 3 CPT hours will be
offered to those who attend the whole seminar.
Enrolment
Please complete and return the attached enrolment form (Appendix 2) before 10 September
2010 by fax at 2293-5721 or email at aml_seminar@sfc.hk. As seats are limited, enrolments
will be processed on a first-come-first-served basis subject to a maximum of 3 places per
firm. Please indicate your order of preference in the enrolment form and we will allocate you
to the session of your 1st choice if seats are still available when your enrolment form reaches
us. The SFC will send the confirmation slip directly to all individuals who are allocated a
place at least one week before the seminar. Please present your confirmation slip for
admission at the venue.
If you have any queries, please contact Ms Seine Luk at 2842-7696.
