Circular to Licensed Corporations
Submission of information on Managers-In-Charge of
Core Functions (MIC) and organisational charts
As set out in the circular published by the Commission on 16 December 20161, the MIC
measures will be implemented on 18 April 2017.
To facilitate submission of the required MIC information and organisational charts by
corporations applying for a licence under section 116(1) of the Securities and Futures
Ordinance, the Commission has (a) revised the existing form, Supplement 8 - Business Plan
and Proposed Business Activities, and (b) made a new form, Supplement 8A - Manager-lnCharge of Core Function(s). They can now be found on the Commission’s website for
advance viewing. Corporate licence applicants should use the revised/new licensing forms
for submission of applications on or after 18 April 2017.
Revised Supplement 8 and New Supplement 8A:
http://www.sfc.hk/web/EN/forms/intermediaries/licensing-application-and-notificationforms/supplements.html
For existing licensed corporations, the MIC information and organisational charts should be
submitted via the SFC Online Portal. Submissions via the SFC Online Portal will commence
on 18 April 2017, and should be completed by 17 July 2017.
An online demonstration clip showing the steps to submit MIC information and organisational
charts via the SFC Online Portal is available on the Commission’s website.
Online Demonstration Clip:
https://portal.sfc.hk/sfcportal/onlinedemo/en/Clip13.html
Since February 2017, the Commission has conducted 12 industry workshops to further
explain the MIC measures and the new features of the SFC Online Portal for submitting the
MIC information and organisational charts. The presentation materials from the workshop
(English version only) are available for reference at Appendix.
