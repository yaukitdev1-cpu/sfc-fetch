Circular to Licensed Corporations on
Reporting of Over-the-counter (OTC) Derivatives Transactions

The trade repository (HKTR) operated by the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA)
published on its website yesterday a revised document on Supplementary Reporting
Instructions for OTC Derivatives Transactions (SRI):
https://hktr.hkma.gov.hk/ContentDetail.aspx?pageName=HKTR-RPT-Administration-andInterface-Development-Guide
Please note that this document has consolidated and superseded the previously issued SRI
– Part 1, SRI – Part 2 and the Updates to the SRI issued in January, February and June
2017. Licensed Corporations that may be subject to mandatory reporting obligations are
advised to refer to this document.
If you have any questions regarding the substance of the reporting requirements, please call
(852) 2231 1788. For questions about the service and operation of the electronic reporting
system, or about training and simulation tests, please contact the HKTR on (852) 8100 3115
or email hktr@hkma.gov.hk.
