Circular to All Licensed Corporations
Importance of Prudent Risk Management
In view of recent market volatility, the SFC reminds1 all licensed corporations again to
establish and implement effective risk management polices. Prudent risk management is a
fundamental principle of the Code of Conduct2 and the foundation of the Internal Control
Guidelines3. Licensed corporations should have effective policies and procedures to enable
the identification, quantification and proper management of risks in order to ensure that
appropriate action can be taken on a timely basis to contain and manage the risks. In
particular, the SFC would like to draw attention to the following areas:
I.

Credit risk management
Licensed corporations are reminded to adequately monitor and control risk exposures
arising from clients’ trading activities, in particular when those firms:
▪ extend credit to their clients that far exceeds levels that befit the clients’ financial
situations;
▪ tolerate their clients for not meeting their margin requirements on a timely basis;
▪ allow their clients to engage in significant day trade activities without close and
effective monitoring; or
▪ permit their clients to directly access their trading systems to execute orders.
To properly monitor and manage the risks, licensed corporations are required to:
▪ take all reasonable steps to establish each client’s financial background to ensure
the risk arising from the client’s transactions is commensurate with the client’s
financial status;

II.

Financial management
It is contrary to prudent risk management principle for a licensed corporation to
provide, directly or indirectly, a financial guarantee of an unlimited amount.
Therefore, the SFC takes a view that licensed corporations should not give such a
financial guarantee for any other party because this is likely to adversely affect the
financial soundness of the licensed corporation and hence impugn its fitness and
properness to remain licensed with the SFC.
In the event that you have provided an unlimited guarantee that is presently
outstanding, you should notify the Commission immediately and discuss appropriate
remedial actions.

III. Role of senior management
The SFC would like to remind senior management of the licensed corporations of
their responsibilities under the Code of Conduct2 to properly manage the risks
associated with the business of their firms. This should include performing periodic
evaluation of the risk management processes and fine-tuning the processes if
necessary. Senior management assumes full responsibilities for the firm’s operations
and bears primary responsibility for ensuring the maintenance of appropriate
standards of conduct and adherence to proper procedures by the firms. They should
ensure that key functions are assigned to appropriate staff members and closely
supervise their work to ensure the delegated functions are properly carried out.
