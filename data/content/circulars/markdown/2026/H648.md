Circular to Licensed Corporations
New investor publications are now available for distribution
The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has added two new booklets, namely
Know your right in the sales process and How to read product key facts statements
(2011 edition), to its list of investor publications.
Know your right in the sales process
This booklet introduces a series of new investor protection measures that the SFC
has launched to strengthen the sales process of investment products since June 2010.
In plain language, the finer points of the measures in each stage of a product sale are
illustrated with photos in a series of six stories.
How to read product key facts statements (2011 edition)
This new edition explains how investors may make use of product key facts
statements (KFS) of different products, namely funds, investment-linked assurance
schemes and unlisted structured investment products. It also includes a section that
sets out the additional product information in the offering documents under the
enhanced disclosure requirements.
We cordially invite the industry to help distribute to the investing public this booklet
and other investor publications as listed in the Annex. Besides, the electronic versions
of these publications are available on the “Publications” section of the SFC’s InvestEd
website: www.InvestEd.hk.

Annex

SFC investor publications
1. Equity-linked Investments (2011 edition) discusses the major features and risks
of unlisted equity-linked investments (ELIs) and uses numerical examples to
illustrate the calculation of return of different types of ELIs, including ELIs with
special features.
2. Index Tracking ETFs - Know the Risks (2010 edition) highlights the features
and associated risks, including counterparty risk, of synthetic exchange-traded
funds (ETFs).
3. Unlisted Structured Investment Products - Post-sale cooling-off
arrangement discusses the features and mechanism of the post-sale cooling-off
arrangement for applicable unlisted structured investment products.
4. Guaranteed Funds - Know the limitations explains the key features and
associated risks of guaranteed funds.
5. InvestEd website highlights the hot topics and features of the SFC’s InvestEd
website for investors.
6. IPO Investing explains what investors should note when subscribing for shares in
initial public offerings(IPOs) and the risks involved.
7. Know Your Broker reminds investors the key factors to consider in choosing a
broker and the steps that they should take to protect their interests.
8. How to Make a Complaint explains to investors where they can channel
complaints when they encounter suspected malpractice in the securities and
futures markets, and the SFC’s procedures and powers in handling public
complaints.
9. The SFC Guide explains how the securities and the futures markets are regulated
and the SFC's roles in the overall regulatory regime in Hong Kong.
