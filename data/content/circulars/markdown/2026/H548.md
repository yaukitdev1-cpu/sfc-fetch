Circular to Licensed Corporations
Preparedness for Human Swine Influenza in Hong Kong
In view of the recent cases of the human swine influenza in other parts of the world, the
Commission would like to remind licensed corporations to review their business contingency
plan (BCP) against the potential outbreak of the human swine influenza in Hong Kong and
consider invoking their contingency measures where necessary.
In particular, licensed corporations should take the following precautions (which should not
be taken to be exhaustive or comprehensive) to ensure that their operation is not affected by
the outbreak of the human swine influenza in Hong Kong and minimize potential damage to
their clients:
Assigning a senior staff member to take care of the reviewing and implementation of the
BCP, including any drills or walk-through exercises to verify the BCP and related
procedures;
Establishing a clear command and communication structure for crisis management and
effective monitoring of the latest developments of the human swine influenza outbreak;
Identifying critical systems and functions;
Assessing the impact of high absentee and loss of key staff and setting up back-up
arrangement for all key staff;
Mapping out appropriate contingency measures with a view to ensure that at least bare
minimum services can continue to be provided to clients in a worst case scenario, such
as:
o Making split team arrangement
o Establishing an alternative site office
o Enabling staff to work from home
Reviewing the readiness of computing equipment to support contingency measures for a
prolonged period;
Reviewing the capacity of electronic channels in case of increased demand for online
and self-service options;
Safeguarding and backing up vital business and transaction data, physical files and tapes;
and
Planning ahead for communicating any special service arrangements clearly to clients if
needed.
Lastly, licensed corporations are also reminded to refer to the Government website of the
Centre for Health Protection (Note 1) when appropriate for updated information of disease
prevention and control.
