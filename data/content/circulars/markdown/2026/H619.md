Key Messages of the SFC / HKEx Joint Briefing Session on Preparation for
Listed Renminbi (“RMB”) Securities Business
The SFC and HKEx have jointly conducted the Joint Briefing Session on Preparation for
Listed RMB Securities Business on 28 February 2011 and 1 March 2011 (Note 1). The SFC
and HKEx have posted the presentation materials of the Joint Briefing Session on their
respective websites for Participants’ reference:

Participants are suggested to refer to those presentation materials and other relevant
guidance when making their preparation and self-examination of readiness.
Key Messages of the Joint Briefing Session
Participants wanting to conduct listed RMB securities business were reminded that they
should
o

apply to the HKSCC for setting up a RMB Designated Bank Account at CCASS by 7
March 2011;

o

participate in the HKEx’s RMB Readiness Tests and successfully complete the HKEx’s
RMB Readiness Tests; AND

o

ensure all of their relevant systems and procedures are ready and submit their
confirmation of readiness to the HKEx before 6pm, 21 March 2011.

Those who fail to accomplish any of the above steps should refrain from dealing in
RMB securities to be listed on the SEHK or clearing transactions in such securities
(please also see related warnings contained in the HKEx’s circulars referred to in Note
2 below).

Consequence of failing to apply for setting up RMB Designated Bank Account at
CCASS by 7 March 2011
Currently, around 300 CCASS Participants (around 60% of the total number of CCASS
Participants) have either set up RMB Designated Bank Accounts at CCASS or submitted
applications to HKSCC. Those CCASS participants who have not submitted application
for setting up RMB Designated Bank Account at CCASS should note that if they fail to
apply by 7 March 2011 to the HKSCC for setting up a RMB Designated Bank Account at
CCASS, they may risk not being able to participate in the Readiness Tests, and
consequently may run the risk of not being accepted by IPO sponsors to take part in
RMB-denominated IPOs when they are launched in Hong Kong.
Reminders to Brokers who are not yet ready
Brokers who are not ready for conducting listed RMB securities business should put in place
measures to prevent inadvertent trading of, or acceptance of client trading instructions on,
listed RMB securities. Please refer to the SFC presentation materials of the Joint Briefing
Session for further guidance.
