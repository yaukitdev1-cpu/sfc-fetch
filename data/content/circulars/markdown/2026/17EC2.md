Circular to all Licensed and Registered Persons
Updated licensing forms for corporate applicants and intermediaries
As outlined in the circular published by the Commission on 29 November 20161, applications,
notifications and annual returns by individual applicants and licensees should from 1
February 2017 be submitted via the SFC Online Portal.
Applications, notifications and annual returns by corporate applicants or intermediaries may
be submitted via the SFC Online Portal, or using the paper forms published on the
Commission’s website. For an individual substantial shareholder or responsible officer whose
application is a necessary component of a corporate application, the application may be
submitted together with the corporate application in paper form.
The Commission is implementing this initiative by making various changes to the licensing
forms. The updated paper forms can now be found on the Commission’s website for advance
review: http://www.sfc.hk/web/EN/forms/intermediaries/licensing-application-and-notificationforms. Corporate applicants and intermediaries are reminded that the updated paper forms
should only be used for applications, notifications and annual returns submitted to the
Commission on or after 1 February 2017. Until then, the old versions of the paper forms will
also continue to be available on the Commission’s website.
