Circular

10 December 2012

Circular to Licensed Corporations in relation to the reporting of OTC
derivatives transactions to the HKMA trade repository
– revised Administration and Interface Development Guide (AIDG)

In December 2011, the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) issued an Administration and
Interface Development Guide (AIDG) on the technical and logistical arrangements for
reporting over-the-counter (OTC) derivatives transactions to the trade repository (TR) of the
HKMA. Following a change in the proposed legislative timetable, the HKMA is preparing to
launch the TR by July 2013.
In the meantime, the HKMA has revised the AIDG to make the TR system more user-friendly.
To provide Licensed Corporations with more information about the revised AIDG, the HKMA
will arrange three briefing sessions on 17 and 18 December 2012. Please find attached to
this circular the letter from HKMA together with the revised AIDG, reference manual,
implementation schedule and details of the briefing sessions. Licensed Corporations with
activities that will be subject to mandatory reporting obligations are advised to review the
above-mentioned documents and properly prepare for the launch of the TR.
Should you have any queries regarding the above, please contact the HKMA at
hktr@hkma.gov.hk.
