Circular to SFC’s Registered Intermediaries
Guidelines for Exemptions from the Recognised Industry Qualification
and Local Regulatory Framework Paper Requirements
In April 2001, the Commission introduced the competence requirements with the objective of
ensuring that licensed persons are adequately equipped to carry out their functions and are
aware of their legal responsibilities. Applicants seeking to be licensed by the Commission
may demonstrate competence by obtaining certain recognised industry qualification and by
passing a local regulatory framework examination.
With a view to facilitating entry to the securities and futures industry but without
compromising investor protection, the Commission may consider granting an exemption
from the recognised industry qualification if the applicant has attained certain level of
academic achievement or possesses sufficient relevant working experience.
For applicant seeking exemption from the applicable local regulatory framework paper(s), the
Commission may take into consideration the following factors:
(a)
experience of the applicant;
(b)
scope of activities the applicant will engage in;
(c)
regulatory support from other personnel; and
(d)
internal control system of the corporation to which the applicant will accredit.
Representatives seeking licence for another regulated activity under a different Ordinance
may also apply for exemption from taking the regulatory framework paper.
However, all individual applicants seeking exemption from the regulatory framework paper
have to fulfill, among other things, additional five Continuous Professional Training hours in
the specific area.
While it is at the sole discretion of the Commission in granting any exemption, extra
regulatory comfort such as conditions on the licensed corporation and/or the individual
applicant and undertakings by the licensed corporation, may be required.
For details, please refer to the attached Guidelines for Exemptions from the Recognised
Industry Qualification and Local Regulatory Framework Paper Requirements.
