Circular to all Licensed Corporations in relation to the consultation paper on proposed
changes to the Securities and Futures (Financial Resources) Rules
The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has begun today a three-month consultation
on certain changes to the Securities and Futures (Financial Resources) Rules (FRR). The
proposals mainly relate to capital and other prudential requirements for activities involving
over-the-counter derivatives. The consultation also proposes to update the requirements in
certain areas of the FRR which are unrelated to over-the-counter derivatives activities with a
view to promoting prudent risk management.
Please refer to the SFC’s press release and the consultation paper for details of the
consultation.
