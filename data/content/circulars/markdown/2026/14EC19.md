Circular to Licensed Corporations in relation to the reporting of OTC
derivatives transactions to the HKMA trade repository – New Phase of System
Implementation and Refinements to Reporting Procedures

Further to our Circular dated 30 July 2013 on the launch of the reporting service of the Hong
Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) trade repository (TR), the HKMA announced on 31 March
2014 that a new phase of the HKMA TR is scheduled to be launched in September 2014 to
cover a batch of 15 new products. It is anticipated that another batch of products will be
added by the end of 2015. The announcement also describes certain refinements to
procedures and technical specifications for reporting, and the enhancement and version
update of file and information transmission standards.
Attached with this Circular are the letter from HKMA regarding the above announcement, and
the updated Reference Manual for Reporting Service and Administration and Interface
Development Guide (AIDG).
Licensed Corporations with activities that may be subject to mandatory reporting obligations
are advised to take into account the above changes when preparing for reporting to the
HKMA TR.
Should you have any queries regarding the above, please contact the HKMA at
hktr@hkma.gov.hk.
