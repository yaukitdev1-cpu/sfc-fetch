Circular to Licensed Corporations
Compliance with the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance
Further to our circulars of 16 August and 13 September 2010, we would like to draw licensed
corporations’ attention to the guidance note, titled “Guidance on the Collection and Use of
Personal Data in Direct Marketing” (the Guidance Note), issued by the Office of the Privacy
Commissioner for Personal Data, Hong Kong (PCPD) on 18 October 2010. A copy of the
Guidance Note is attached, and can also be downloaded from PCPD’s website
(http://www.pcpd.org.hk/english/files/publications/DM_e.pdf).
Should you have any queries regarding this circular, please contact Ms Lorraine Chan on
2842 7751.
