Circular

19 September 2014

Circular to Licensed Corporations in relation to the reporting of OTC
derivatives transactions to the HKMA trade repository – Updated Requirements
and Technical Specifications for Reporting

We refer to our Circulars dated 2 April 2014 and 8 July 2014 on the launch of the new phase
of system implementation for the Hong Kong Monetary Authority trade repository (HKTR)
scheduled for 29 September 2014 to cover a batch of 15 new products. Please note that the
HKMA announced on 18 September 2014 that as the reporting entities need more time to
adopt the refined technical specifications and to get ready for the provision of the unique
Trade ID (TID), the HKTR considers it appropriate to defer the deadlines for :


reporting entities to adopt the refined technical specifications (version 1.4.1) to the
end of December 2014, although the HKTR will apply such specifications from 29
September 2014; and



reporting of unique TID to the end of March 2015.

Nevertheless the launch of the new phase of system implementation scheduled for 29
September 2014 remains unchanged.
Attached to this Circular are the letter and supplementary note from the HKMA regarding the
above announcement, and the updated Administration and Interface Development Guide
(AIDG) version 1.4.1. Licensed Corporations which have activities that may be subject to
mandatory reporting obligations are advised to take into account the above changes when
preparing for reporting to the HKTR.
Should you have any queries regarding the above, please contact the HKMA at
hktr@hkma.gov.hk.
