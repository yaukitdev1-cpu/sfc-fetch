Circular to All Licensed Corporations
Requirements under certain amendment rules and amended Code of Conduct
will come into effect on 1 October 2006
The Commission reminds licensed corporations that the requirements introduced by the
following Amendment Rules and amended Code of Conduct will come into effect on 1
October 20061:
-

Securities and Futures (Financial Resources) (Amendment) Rules 2006
Securities and Futures (Client Securities) (Amendment) Rules 2006;
Securities and Futures (Contract Notes, Statements of Account and Receipts)
(Amendment) Rules 2006; and
the amendments to the Code of Conduct for Persons Licensed by or Registered
with the Securities and Futures Commission gazetted on 19 May 2006.

Those requirements were intended, principally, to address risks arising from share
margin financing business.
A copy of these Amendment Rules and amendments to the Code of Conduct can be
found on the Commission’s website 2 . The Commission has also posted a set of
frequently asked questions (“FAQs”) on its website to assist licensed corporations in
understanding the new requirements. The FAQs for the Securities and Futures
(Financial Resources) Rules have also been updated in order to provide further
guidance on the application of certain existing requirements under the rules. The FAQs
can be downloaded from the Commission’s website under the section “Intermediaries,
Licensing & Investment Products - Intermediaries Supervision Related Matters FAQs”.
Should you have any queries regarding the contents of this circular, please contact Ms
