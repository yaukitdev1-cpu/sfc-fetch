(1) New Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing (AML/CFT) Guidelines
The SFC published in the Gazette on 27 January 2012 (G.N. 410 of 2012) Note 1 a new set of
guidelines consisting of:


Guideline on Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing (the Guideline); and



Prevention of Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing Guideline Issued by the SFC for
Associated Entities (AE Guideline)

to implement proposals Note 2 to provide guidance to the industry relating to, among others, the
operation of the provisions of Schedule 2 to the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist
Financing (Financial Institutions) Ordinance (AMLO). These guidelines will assist licensed
corporations (LCs) and associated entities (AEs) in designing and implementing appropriate and
effective policies, procedures and controls so as to comply with the AMLO requirements and/or
other applicable AML/CFT legislation and regulatory requirements.
The Guideline and AE Guideline will come into effect on 1 April 2012 and supersede the existing
Prevention of Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing Guidance Note.
It is important that LCs and AEs apprise themselves now of these new guidelines, and make
preparation forthwith to ensure compliance with all applicable requirements on 1 April 2012.
(2) Briefing Seminars
To assist LCs and AEs to prepare for the implementation of the new guidelines, the SFC will
conduct a briefing seminar at 9:30 a.m. of 20 February 2012. Reruns of this briefing seminar will
be offered at 11:15 a.m. of the same day as well as on 22 February 2012 (see Appendix 1 for
details).
