Circular to SFC’s Registered Intermediaries
and Exempt Persons which are not Authorized Institutions
Compliance under the Securities and Futures Ordinance (Cap. 571)
As you are aware, the Securities and Futures Ordinance (“SFO”) is targeted to commence on 1
April 2003. Upon commencement of the SFO, you will be deemed to be a corporation licensed for
certain type(s) of regulated activity for a transitional period of two years. The type(s) of regulated
activity that you will be so deemed correspond to those activities that you are carrying out under
your current registration or exempt status.
As provided under the SFO, you may continue to carry on your existing business of regulated
activities during the transitional period without taking any action on your part. However, you will
need to apply for migration to the new regime if you intend to continue such business beyond the
transitional period. We recommend that you prepare early and submit your application around, if
not before, your first anniversary date during the transitional period. A detailed guideline relating
to transitional arrangements will be issued early next year.
You are reminded that except for those provisions of the SFO noted below, all other provisions
including the subsidiary legislation, codes and guidelines applicable to licensed persons will
be effective upon the commencement of the SFO and will apply with full force and without
exception to you and your conduct of regulated activities. It is important therefore that you
apprise yourself now of the changes under the new regime, and make preparation forthwith
to ensure compliance with all applicable requirements on 1 April 2003.
•

Schedule 10 to the SFO provides that during the transitional period, existing registrants
which are deemed to be licensed corporations under the SFO are also deemed to have
complied with the requirements on the appointment of responsible officers under sections
125(1)(a) and (b).

•

That Schedule further provides that such requirements relating to responsible officers and
the requirements for approval of substantial shareholders under section 131(1) are not
applicable to existing exempt persons (which are not authorized institutions) deemed to be
licensed corporations during the transitional period.

The Securities and Futures (Financial Resources) Rules contain transitional provisions that
investment advisers and exempt persons (which are not authorized institutions) are not
required to comply with the new financial resources requirements in the first 6 months
after the SFO commences. However, during that 6-month period, investment advisers are
still required to comply with the requirements on net tangible assets as provided under the
current Financial Resources Rules.

The SFC website (www.hksfc.org.hk) contains useful information on the new regime and links to
the SFO and its subsidiary legislation gazetted. We are pleased to advise also that in January 2003,
we will be organizing two workshops, one in English and the other in Chinese, to assist the
industry in understanding the new regime and the provisions thereunder. Announcements will be
made regarding these workshops in the coming week through FinNet.
