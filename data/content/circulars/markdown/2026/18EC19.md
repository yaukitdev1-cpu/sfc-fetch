Circular to All Intermediaries, Responsible Officers and Representatives
Regarding the Waiver of Annual Licensing Fees

This circular advises all licensed corporations, registered institutions, responsible officers and
licensed representatives that the Commission will waive payment of licensing fees from 1
April 2018 to 31 March 2019 (“Waiver Period”). The Commission will review the situation
again and may reinstate the annual licensing fees next year.
The waiver applies only to annual licensing fees that become payable during the Waiver
Period. It does not apply to any other fees, such as licence application fees and transfer fees.
Where annual licensing fees would ordinarily become payable during the Waiver Period, the
Commission will not issue the usual demand for payment.
