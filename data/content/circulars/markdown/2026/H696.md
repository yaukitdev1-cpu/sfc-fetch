Circular to Licensed Corporations and Associated Entities
Anti-Money Laundering / Combating Terrorist Financing
Amendments to Guideline on Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist
Financing

SFC published in the Gazette today (G.N. 4725 of 2012) amendments to Guideline on AntiMoney Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing (“AML Guideline”) (see Attachment 1).
The amendments take immediate effect.
These amendments were made to the AML Guideline in order to reflect the recently enacted
United Nations (Anti-Terrorism Measures) (Amendment) Ordinance 2012 (“UNATMO 2012”)
(L.N. 20 of 2012) (Note 1) which:
(1) amended the United Nations (Anti-Terrorism Measures) Ordinance (“UNATMO”) in,
among others, the following areas:
(a)

repealing the definition of “funds” and replacing the term “funds” by “property”; and

(b)

expanding the prohibition on assistance for terrorists and terrorist associates to also
cover the collection of property for terrorists and terrorist associates.

(2) made consequential amendments to the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist
Financing (Financial Institutions) Ordinance (“AMLO”) that have ensured that the
definition of “terrorist financing” under the AMLO continues to tally with the UNATMO.
As the AML Guideline makes reference to and explains various provisions of the UNATMO
and AMLO, corresponding amendments were made to the AML Guideline. The amended
AML Guideline is available on the SFC’s website
(http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/intermediaries/supervision/prevention/prevention.html).
Licensed corporations and associated entities are advised to familiarise themselves with the
amended UNATMO, AMLO and AML Guideline and take all necessary steps to ensure
compliance with the applicable requirements.
