Circular to Licensed Corporations
Questionnaire on Sale of Non-exchange traded
Securities and Futures Products
The Commission is collecting information on the types and values of non-exchange traded
securities and futures products sold or distributed by licensed corporations over the 12
months ended 30 June 2010. The information will be collected by means of a questionnaire.
The objective of this exercise is to facilitate the Commission’s understanding of licensed
corporations’ activities in this area.
Please complete the questionnaire and return it to the Commission on or before 27
September 2010. A copy of the questionnaire can be downloaded from the Commission’s
designated webpage through the internet as follows:
[https://www.sfc.hk/sfc_ProductSurvey/jsp/EN/submit.jsp]
(Please enable Macros when opening the Excel file.)
Licensed corporations are asked whenever possible to submit the questionnaire via the
above designated webpage to facilitate processing of data. Instructions for completing the
questionnaire are set out in the cover page.
Please be assured that all information supplied will be treated as strictly confidential. Thank
you in advance for your cooperation. Should you have any questions regarding this circular,
please contact your case officers in the Intermediaries Supervision Department or Ms Rachel
Chung at 2283 6153.

Intermediaries Supervision Department
Securities and Futures Commission
