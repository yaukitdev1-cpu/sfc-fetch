Circular Regarding Waiver of Annual Licensing Fees
The Commission will waive annual licensing fees from 1 April 2016 to 31 March 2018
(“Waiver Period”).
The fee waiver applies to annual licensing fees for all licensed corporations, registered
institutions, responsible officers and licensed representatives. It does not apply to any other
fees, such as licence application fees and transfer fees.
Where annual licensing fees would ordinarily become payable during the Waiver Period, the
Commission will not issue the usual demand for payment.

Licensing Department
Intermediaries Division
Securities and Futures Commission

Note:
For the avoidance of doubt, applicants who become licensed or registered or who are
approved for additional types of regulated activity during the Waiver Period will be eligible for
waiver of any annual licensing fees that would ordinarily become payable during the Waiver
Period. However, fee waivers cannot be carried forward to future years. In other words, this
initiative does not cover annual licensing fees payable after 31 March 2018.
