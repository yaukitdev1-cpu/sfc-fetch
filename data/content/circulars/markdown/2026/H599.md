Circular to SEHK Exchange Participants
Preparation for trading and settlement of listed renminbi (RMB) denominated products

Further to our circular issued on 31 August 2010, this is to inform you that the Hong Kong
Monetary Authority (HKMA) issued a circular to authorized institutions on 3 September 2010
in relation to the above matter. A copy of that circular can be found on the website of the
HKMA.
http://www.info.gov.hk/hkma/eng/guide/circu_date/20100903e1.pdf
Brokers should take note of the restrictions on RMB lending by banks as set out in the HKMA’s
circular. Brokers are reminded again to arrange with your clearing banks as soon as
possible to set up RMB credit facilities, if needed, so as to facilitate the trading and settlement
of listed RMB denominated products in the near future.
Should you have any queries regarding the contents of this circular, please contact Ms Denise
Chan on 2283-6188.
