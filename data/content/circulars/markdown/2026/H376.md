FAQs for Investors on UCITS III
I would like to invite you and your staff who are involved in the investment fund advisory
and distribution business to make use of the Commission’s recently released set of
Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) for investors on Undertakings for Collective
Investment in Transferable Securities III (UCITS III).
As you may know, the European Commission has adopted two new directives, generally
referred to as UCITS III, relating to the authorisation of investment schemes. SFC
authorised funds which are domiciled in the EU member states will generally have to be in
compliance with the new UCITS III regulations by February 2007 or earlier. To provide
investors with some general information and address some basic queries regarding UCITS
III, the Commission has prepared a set of FAQs set out in the Annex. The e-version of the
FAQs is available under “Funds” in the “Features and Dr Wise” section of the SFCoperated investor portal, the Electronic Investor Resources Centre (eIRC) at www.eirc.hk.
The Commission encourages fund managers and their distributors, such as yourselves, to
dedicate their efforts in explaining the implications of these new regulations in the context
of their clients’ investments. You and your staff are very much welcome to distribute the
FAQs to your relevant fund clients, or carry them in your client communications with
acknowledgement of the source of the information. If you have any queries on quoting the
FAQs in your company’s publications, please contact our Investor Education and
Communications Department at 2840 9219.
