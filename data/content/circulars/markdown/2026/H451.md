Circular to Licensed Corporations and Associated Entities

Anti-Money Laundering / Combating Terrorist Financing


   1) United Nations Sanctions (Democratic Republic of the Congo) Regulation 2006
   2) US President’s Executive Order 13224
   3) Non-cooperative Countries and Territories
   4) Anti-Money Laundering Training Seminar Materials



1) 	United Nations Sanctions (Democratic Republic of the Congo) Regulation 2006 (the “Regulation”)

      The Regulation was made under the United Nations Sanctions Ordinance (Cap. 537) and was published in the Gazette on 17 November 2006 (L.N. 257).  Pursuant to section 36 of the Regulation, a list of “relevant persons and entities” has been specified.


      The above list was published in the Gazette on 8 December 2006 (G.N. 7724).  The Regulation and the list can be found on the Government website http://www.gld.gov.hk/eng/welcome.htm under eGazette Note [1] in Services to Public.  A copy of the list in MS Word format is attached to facilitate the update of your electronic database (Attachment).

      Licensed corporations (“LCs”) and associated entities (“AEs”) should check the names in the list against their records, and report any transactions or relationships they have or have had with the named individuals or entities to the Joint Financial Intelligence Unit (“JFIU”).  LCs and AEs should not make available any funds or other financial assets or economic resources to or for the benefit of a relevant person or a relevant entity except under the authority of a licence granted under the Regulation.


   2) US President’s Executive Order 13224 (“Executive Order”)

      Further to our circular issued on 17 October 2006, this is to inform you that the US Government has added individuals and entities under the Executive Order.  The name of the additional individuals and entities can be found on the website of the Office of Foreign Assets Control of the US Treasury (“OFAC”) http://www.ustreas.gov/offices/enforcement/ofac/programs/terror/terror.pdf under “Name of Individuals and Entities Designated on 6 December 06”, “Name of Individuals designated on 7 December 06” and  “Name of Individuals designated on 19 December 06”.


      LCs and AEs should check whether they have had transactions or dealings with the individuals and the entities named in the list.  In order to fulfil the obligations under section 12 of the United Nations (Anti-Terrorism Measures) Ordinance, you should promptly notify the JFIU if such reviews reveal any suspicious accounts, transactions or dealings.


      As the designated individuals and entities under the Executive Order may be updated by the US Government from time to time, LCs and AEs are reminded to browse the website of the OFAC regularly for the latest information.

   3) Non-cooperative Countries and Territories (“NCCTs”)

      Further to our circular issued on 18 July 2006, this is to inform you that the Financial Action Task Force on Money Laundering (“FATF”) decided at its October 2006 plenary meeting to remove Myanmar from the list of NCCTs. Currently, no country or jurisdiction is on the list. The related press release issued by the FATF on 13 October 2006 can be found on its website (http://www.fatf-gafi.org).


      As the list of NCCTs may be updated by the FATF from time to time, LCs and AEs are reminded to browse the website of the FATF regularly for the latest information.

   4) Anti-Money Laundering Training Seminar (“AML training seminar”) Materials


      The JFIU has posted the training materials for the AML training seminar jointly organized by the Narcotics Division, the JFIU and the SFC on 6 September 2006 in the “References” section of the JFIU’s website (http://www.info.gov.hk/police/jfiu/). This section is accessible by passwords provided by the JFIU[2].
