Circular to Management Companies of SFC-authorised Real Estate Investment Trusts
(“REITs”)
Acquisitions by SFC-authorised REITs
1.

Since the authorisation and listing of the first REIT in November 2005, Hong Kong has
seven listed REITs invested in different types of real estate assets both in Hong Kong
and in the Mainland. The SFC believes that the Hong Kong REIT market is now ready
for the next phase of growth. Experience in other markets tells us that growth
predominantly comes from acquisitions by existing REITs. The SFC will give its full
support to such expansion efforts, whether locally or overseas.

2.

In response to some recent feedback from industry participants, the SFC hereby clarifies
that in acquiring overseas and/or new types of properties, REIT managers do not have to
seek re-approval of their licences or re-authorisation of the relevant REIT by the SFC
before proceeding to make such acquisitions. In other words, there are no additional
licensing or authorisation requirements for REITs planning to expand their operations
into other markets or jurisdictions or different property types.

3.

In making acquisition decisions, it is the REIT managers’ obligation to satisfy
themselves that they have sufficient and appropriate skills and processes in place to
manage the new properties, either as part of their in-house skills or by way of
outsourcing to a competent external party under their oversight. This would be a
business decision for REIT managers having regard to their fiduciary duties to
unitholders as a whole.

4.

The SFC believes investors expect their REIT managers to manage and grow their REIT
businesses. While a REIT may not have set out any strategic/business plan for growth
and acquisition in its offering document, it may and is entitled to develop new
acquisition and growth plans after listing, just like any listed company. For those new
REIT applicants who already have a strategic/business plan for acquisition and growth at
the time of listing, they should disclose such plans in their IPO offering documents as
investors would like to know about such plans.

5.

REIT managers are welcomed to seek further guidance from the SFC on any regulatory
aspects concerning their proposed acquisition plans.

6.

To enhance regulatory transparency and also as part of our wider initiatives to facilitate
the development and growth of the REIT market in Hong Kong, we will issue a separate
Circular to provide practical guidance on the disclosure and unitholders’ approval
requirements concerning acquisitions by SFC-authorised REITs shortly.
