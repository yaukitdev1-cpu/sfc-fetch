Circular to SEHK Exchange Participants and CCASS Participants
(collectively “Participants”)
Practice Sessions organized by the SEHK and HKSCC on 2 and 3 June 2012
on trading and clearing operations under dual counter model
(the “Practice Sessions”)
Participants are encouraged to participate in the Practice Sessions to prepare themselves on
handling securities transactions under the dual counter model.
The Practice Sessions provide an opportunity for Participants to familiarize their staff with the
operation of the dual counter model and to review the readiness of their systems and
operations to handle securities transactions under such model. Details of the Practice
Sessions are included in the circulars issued by the SEHK and the HKSCC on 16 May 2012
(Note). The circulars can be found on the HKEx website’s “Listing, Trading and Clearing of
RMB Products” section:
http://www.hkex.com.hk/eng/market/sec_tradinfra/rmbequity/rmbequity.htm
Participants are also advised to refer to the other information and guidance issued by the
HKEx in the above webpage in relation to the dual counter model when preparing for
handling securities transactions under such model.
Should you have any questions regarding the contents of this circular, please contact Ms
Denise Chan at 2283 6188.
