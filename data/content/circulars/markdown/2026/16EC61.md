Circular to Licensed Corporations on
Briefing Sessions for Phase 2 Mandatory Reporting Requirements
under the Over-the-counter (OTC) Derivatives Regime

The phase 2 mandatory reporting requirements under the OTC derivatives regime will come
into effect on 1 July 2017 and will cover OTC derivative transactions in all 5 key asset
classes*, as well as the daily reporting of valuation information.
Three briefing sessions on the subject matter will be conducted on 13 December 2016 by the
Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA), its Trade Repository (HKTR) and the Securities and
Futures Commission (SFC). Access to the reporting service offered by the HKTR will be
covered during the briefing sessions as well. Details of the briefing sessions and enrolment
procedures can be found in the attached letter issued by HKMA on 25 November 2016.
Licensed Corporations whose activities may be subject to mandatory reporting may want to
attend the briefing session to ensure they fully understand their obligations and the
preparations to be completed ahead of 1 July 2017.
If you have any enquiries, please contact the HKTR at (852) 8100 3115 or e-mail to
hktr@hkma.gov.hk.

Supervision of Markets Division
Securities and Futures Commission

Note: * the 5 key asset classes are interest rate derivatives, foreign exchange derivatives,
equity derivatives, credit derivatives and commodity derivatives.
