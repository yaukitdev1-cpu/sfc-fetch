Circular to All Licensed Corporations
Prepare for Implementation of the New Requirements to Address Risks Arising from
Securities Margin Financing
The legislative process for the following Amendment Rules have completed recently:
-

Securities and Futures (Financial Resources) (Amendment) Rules 2006;
Securities and Futures (Client Securities) (Amendment) Rules 2006; and
Securities and Futures (Contract Notes, Statements of Account and Receipts)
(Amendment) Rules 2006.

These Amendment Rules, together with the amendments to the Code of Conduct for
Persons Licensed by or Registered with the Securities and Futures Commission (“Code of
Conduct amendments”) gazetted on 19 May 2006, will take effect as from 1 October 20061.
Most of the new requirements introduced in the Amendments Rules and amendments to
the Code of Conduct are only applicable to securities dealers and securities margin
financiers that repledge client securities collateral. The new requirements, inter alia,
include:
-

Introducing limits on repledging of client securities collateral in two stages –
set at 180% initially and 140% after 30 September 2007;
Making some changes to selected haircut percentages as specified in the
Securities and Futures (Financial Resources) Rules (“FRR”);
Improving transparency by providing margin clients with better disclosure
about the risks of pooling and repledging; and
Providing relaxation on certain existing FRR requirements, given that pooling
risk will be reduced by the new measures.

Nevertheless, there is one particular amendment that affects all licensed corporations
and that is the increase in the FRR haircut percentage applicable to listed warrants
from 40% to 100%.

Licensed corporations that will be affected by these new requirements are strongly advised
to thoroughly review the financial and operational implications of the new measures
introduced under these Amendment Rules and amendments to the Code of Conduct, and
ensure that they will have the necessary systems and capital for complying with the new
requirements.
A copy of the respective Amendment Rules and amendments to the Code of Conduct can
be found on the Commission’s website at http://www.sfc.hk.
