Circular to Licensed Corporations and Associated Entities
Anti-Money Laundering Seminar for Compliance Officers
The Narcotics Division and the Joint Financial Intelligence Unit of the HKSAR Government
will hold a seminar for the securities industry on 15 September 2005 to brief compliance
officers on suspicious transaction reporting issues and related developments. Attached are
the programme, invitation letter and reply slip for the seminar. If you have any questions,
please contact Bruce C H Lee on 2866 3366 of the Joint Financial Intelligence Unit.
Kindly note that the seminar is free of charge. Enrolments will close on 22 August 2005, and
are processed on a first-come-first-serve basis.
