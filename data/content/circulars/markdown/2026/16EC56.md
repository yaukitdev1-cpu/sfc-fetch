Circular to Licensed Corporations on Providing Securities Dealing Services
under Shanghai-Hong Kong and Shenzhen-Hong Kong Stock Connects
Shanghai-Hong Kong Stock Connect has been operating for about two years since its launch
on 17 November 2014. Licensed corporations who are exchange participants of The Stock
Exchange of Hong Kong Limited may provide Northbound trading services to their clients
and such activities are subject to the relevant requirements of the SFC, the HKEX1, the SSE2
and the ChinaClear3.
In the light of the upcoming Shenzhen-Hong Kong Stock Connect and the SFC’s
observations noted in the recent inspections of licensed corporations on their business
activities under the Shanghai-Hong Kong Stock Connect, the SFC wishes to remind licensed
corporations to ensure compliance with the relevant requirements when conducting such
business activities, in particular in relation to the following aspects:
1.

Information to clients
Licensed corporations should ensure that their clients are aware of the differences in
rules and regulations, trading and settlement arrangements and risks associated when
trading SSE Securities4 under Northbound trading by:
a)

making available of updated client agreement and other account opening
documents and ensure that they contain terms and conditions5 covering the
Northbound trading under Shanghai-Hong Kong Stock Connect and adequate
disclosure of risks associated with investing in SSE Securities through such
arrangement before services are provided to the clients;

b)

providing the clients with information regarding the trading and settlement
arrangements, including but not limited to eligible stocks for Northbound trading,
daily quota, trading restrictions6, different securities and money settlement day7,
shareholding disclosure obligations, foreign investor shareholding restrictions and
forced-sale arrangements, under Shanghai-Hong Kong Stock Connect; and

c)

stating clearly the scopes of services available to the clients such as provision of
margin financing, stock borrowing and lending or short-selling services and the
corresponding terms and conditions.

Operation and system controls
a)

During our inspections, we noted that while there were firms that use automated
system to monitor the clients’ compliance with the shareholding restrictions and
disclosure obligations for SSE Securities and the changes to the list of SSE
Securities eligible for margin trading, there were firms relying merely on eyeballing/
manual checking to perform such monitoring which might be susceptible to errors or
omissions. In the light of the perceived increase in trading volume relating to
Northbound trading activities after the launch of Shenzhen-Hong Kong Stock
Connect, licensed corporations are encouraged to enhance their controls and
systems in this respect.

b)

Licensed corporations are reminded to put in place adequate policies and
procedures to ensure compliance with all applicable regulatory requirements as well
as the firm’s own internal policies and procedures, including:
 customer due diligence and transaction monitoring to detect any unusual or
suspicious transactions which may indicate money laundering or other unlawful
acts requiring disclosure to the Joint Financial Intelligence Unit and/or other
follow up actions;
 pre-trade checking and controls to prevent day trading of SSE Securities and
ensure compliance with the short-selling requirements;
 arrangements to effectively monitor and alert the clients to comply with the
applicable shareholding disclosure obligations and shareholding restrictions for
SSE Securities under the Rules of the Exchange;
 measures to ensure margin trading is confined to those SSE Securities that are
included in the List of Eligible SSE Securities for Margin Trading published by the
HKEX from time to time;
 measures to ensure prompt segregation of SSE Securities of the clients into
designated accounts;
 controls to prevent any trading or providing services to facilitate the trading of
SSE Securities held within CCASS8 through any venue other than through the
SSE Market System9;
 measures to ensure compliance with the record keeping requirements relating to
Shanghai-Hong Kong Stock Connect business; and
 adequate training to both front and back office and ensure that they are familiar
with the relevant regulatory requirements, policies and procedures.

Shenzhen-Hong Kong Stock Connect
Licensed corporations are reminded that the above requirements are also applicable to
the provision of Northbound trading services under Shenzhen-Hong Kong Stock
Connect. In addition, licensed corporations, including those that provide Northbound
trading services to their clients through Exchange Participants, should implement
appropriate and effective measures and take reasonable steps to ensure that trading of
stocks that are listed on the ChiNext Board of SZSE10 will be limited to institutional
professional investors11.
Licensed corporations should observe any changes to the relevant requirements relating
to Shanghai-Hong Kong Stock Connect and Shenzhen-Hong Kong Stock Connect from
time to time and review their policies and procedures to ensure ongoing compliance with
all relevant regulatory requirements.
