Circular to Licensed Corporations in relation to the reporting of OTC derivative
transactions to the HKMA trade repository – Training and testing for the first
half of 2016
We refer to the Circular issued on 10 July 2015, which announced that the Securities and
Futures (OTC Derivative Transactions – Reporting and Record Keeping Obligations) Rules
(Reporting Rules) has come into effect.
According to the Reporting Rules, regulated entities are required to start reporting and to
complete backloading of trades within 6 months and 9 months respectively from the effective
date of the new regime. Regulated entities are required to sign up for the reporting service of
the HKMA Trade Repository (HKTR) and complete a simulation test before they will be
accepted for reporting.
To assist regulated entities to prepare for the commencement of the reporting obligations,
the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) has organised a series of training and simulation
tests in the second half of 2015, which was announced in our Circular on 18 May 2015.
On 27 October 2015, the HKMA announced that another round of training and simulation
tests will be arranged in the first half of 2016. Attached to this Circular are the letter and
enrolment form issued by the HKMA, which also include the schedule for the training and
simulation tests to be conducted in the first half of 2016.
Licensed Corporations which have activities that may be subject to mandatory reporting
obligations are advised to sign up for the reporting service of the HKTR and complete the
simulation test as early as possible. However, those who had already signed up and
completed a simulation test before are not required to do the same again.
Should you have any queries regarding the above, please contact the HKMA at
hktr@hkma.gov.hk.
