Circular to Licensed Corporations
Compliance with the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance
The Commission would like to remind licensed corporations to be particularly vigilant in
ensuring compliance with the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (“PDPO”), breaches of which
may give rise to concerns as to the fitness and properness of a licensed corporation to remain
licensed.
In relation to the protection of customer data, licensed corporations should have controls in
place to ensure that they observe the PDPO and any relevant codes of practice or guidance
issued by the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data (“PCPD”).
Further, licensed corporations should pay attention to an interim report on investigation
concerning personal data collected and disclosed under the Octopus Rewards Programme
(http://www.pcpd.org.hk/english/publications/files/OctopusReport_e.pdf) and a statement
recently published by the PCPD reminding data users of the requirements of the PDPO when
engaging in direct marketing (http://www.pcpd.org.hk/english/infocentre/press_20100812.html).
In this regard, licensed corporations should consider areas including, but not limited to, the
following when handling customer data:

the personal data is collected for a lawful purpose directly related to the function or
activity of the licensed corporation and that the data is adequate but not excessive in
relation to that purpose;
the personal data is only used for the purposes for which it is collected or a directly
related purpose and that the customers give their express consent before their personal
data is used for any other purpose;
the personal data held by licensed corporations is protected against unauthorised or
accidental access.
where the collection of particular items of personal data is optional, this should be made
clear to the customers;
any contractual provisions that may have an impact on personal data privacy should be
in a font size that is reasonably readable;
the classes of person to whom a customer’s personal data could be transferred should
be reasonably specific;
personal data transferred to a third party should not be excessive with regard to the
purpose for which it is to be used; and
ensure that a business partner to whom customers’ personal data would be transferred
has adequate data privacy protection measures in place.

Licensed corporations should regularly review their internal policies, controls and practices so
as to ensure compliance with all applicable legal and regulatory requirements.
