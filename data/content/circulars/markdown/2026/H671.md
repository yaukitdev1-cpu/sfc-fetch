Circular to Licensed Corporations in relation to the proposed reporting of OTC
derivatives transactions to the Hong Kong trade repository – briefing by the
HKMA on technical arrangements
According to the consultation paper jointly issued by the Securities and Futures Commission
and the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) on 17 October 2011 on the proposed
regulatory regime for the OTC derivatives market in Hong Kong, Authorised Institutions and
Licensed Corporations that have engaged in OTC derivatives transactions will be required to
report their OTC derivatives transactions, initially interest rate swaps and non-deliverable
foreign exchange forwards, to the HKMA’s trade repository (HKTR) after the proposed
regulatory regime is introduced.
On 30 December 2011, the HKMA issued an Administration and Interface Development
Guide (the Guide) which specified the technical standards and requirements for connecting
and submitting OTC derivatives transaction data to the HKTR. We forwarded the Guide to
Licensed Corporations via a circular dated 30 December 2011.
The HKMA will be organising a series of briefing sessions in March 2012 to facilitate a better
understanding by industry participants of the operating model of the HKTR and the technical
requirements set out in the Guide. HKMA’s letter addressed to Authorised Institutions about
the briefing sessions is attached to this circular.
We encourage all affected Licensed Corporations to send representatives to attend the
briefing. If your institution is interested in attending the briefing, kindly complete and return
the enrolment form in the attached letter to the HKMA on or before 9 March 2012.
Should you have any queries regarding the above, please contact the HKMA at
hktr@hkma.gov.hk.
