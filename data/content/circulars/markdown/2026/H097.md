## Circular to SFC's Registered Intermediaries

## Money Laundering (1) Non-cooperative Countries and Territories; and (2) US President's Executive Order

## Suspension Of Additional Counter-Measures Against The Philippines

In our circular dated 28 September 2001, we advised, among other issues, that the Financial Action  Task  Force  on  Money  Laundering  (FATF)  had  recommended  the  application  of certain  additional  counter-measures  to  the  Philippines  as  of  30  September  2001.    The jurisdiction-specific advisory in respect of the Philippines was attached as Appendix 1 to that circular.

The  FATF  subsequently  received  a  joint  letter  from  the  Secretary  of  the  Department  of Finance and Governor of Central Bank of the Philippines, confirming that the President of the Philippines has signed into law 'An Act Defining the Crime of Money Laundering, Providing Penalties Therefor and For Other Purposes' (otherwise  known  as  the  'Anti-Money Laundering  Act  of  2001').    FATF  has  now  decided  to  suspend  the  application  of  the proposed  additional  counter-measures  pending  an  assessment  on  whether  the  new  law  has addressed  all  the  identified  money  laundering  concerns.    A  decision  as  to  whether  the Philippines should be subject to additional counter-measures given the current development will  be  made  in  January  /  February  2002  at  the  latest,  if  not  earlier.    We  will  keep  you informed of any future developments on this subject.

Notwithstanding  the  suspension  of  the  application  of  the  proposed  additional  countermeasures, the Philippines is still included in the FATF's list of non-cooperative countries and territories  (NCCTs).    Registered  intermediaries  are  therefore  advised  to  continue  to  give special attention to business and transactions with person, including companies and financial institutions, in the Philippines, in accordance with Recommendation 21 of the FATF Forty Recommendations Note . ## US President's Executive Order

The US President issued an Executive Order on 24 September 2001. The Order applies to financial  assets  of,  and  transactions  with,  27  entities  believed  to  be  terrorist  organisations, terrorist leaders, or to have links with such persons.  The text of the Executive Order and the list  of  the  27  entities  are  available  on  the  website  of  the  Office  of  Foreign  Assets  Control under the US Treasury (www.ustreas.gov/ofac).

Registered  intermediaries  should  review  carefully  the  list  of  27  entities  attached  to  the Executive Order to check whether they have had transactions or dealings with such entities. In  order  to  fulfil  the  obligations  under  section  25A  of  the  Organised  and  Serious  Crimes Ordinance, registered intermediaries should promptly report to the JFIU in the usual way if such reviews reveal any suspicious accounts, transactions or dealings.
