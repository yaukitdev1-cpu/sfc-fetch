Circular

31 August 2016

Circular to Licensed Corporations in relation to the reporting of OTC
derivatives transactions to the HKMA trade repository – Technical
specifications for new phase system implementation

We refer to the Further Consultation Conclusions on Introducing Mandatory Clearing and
Expanding Mandatory Reporting published in July 2016, which summarised the revised
regulatory proposals on various technical aspects and mandatory reporting data field
requirements.
In response to the above revised requirements in the further consultation conclusions, the
Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) announced today that a set of updated requirements
and technical specifications for reporting, to be set out in version 1.5.2 of the Administration
and Interface Development Guide (“AIDG”), will be published in October 2016. The
announcement also reminded reporting entities to carefully read the revised AIDG to ensure
they are aware of the changes between version 1.5.2 and the earlier version 1.5.1 that was
published on 6 June 2016. Testing environment for the revised AIDG (version 1.5.2) will be
available in Q4 2016.
Attached to this Circular is the letter from the HKMA regarding the above announcement.
Should you have any queries regarding the above, please contact the HKMA at
hktr@hkma.gov.hk.
