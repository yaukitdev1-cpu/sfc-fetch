Circular to Licensed Corporations
Change of Accreditation by Deemed Licensed Representatives

We have recently received enquiries on the arrangement for deemed licensed representatives
changing employment. As noted in paragraph 1.5.8 of Chapter 1 of the Guidelines on
Transitional Arrangements, upon a deemed licensed representative ceasing to act for his
principal for all regulated activities, he will cease to be a deemed licensed representative
pursuant to section 53(6)(c) of Schedule 10 to the Securities and Futures Ordinance. If he
wishes to change employment and act for another licensed corporation after the cessation, he
would have to apply for a new licence using Form 3 and pay an application fee of $1,790 per
regulated activity.
However, a deemed licensed representative may at anytime during the transitional period
migrate to the new regime by completing a simplified Form 3(X). No migration fee is
required. Upon obtaining a new licence, the representative intending to change employment
may apply for transfer of accreditation of his licence by completing Form 4 and paying a fee
of $200 for each regulated activity. Simultaneous submission of Form 3(X) (signed by the
current principal) and Form 4 is acceptable for these purposes. Such a licensed representative
will also have 180 days to change employment before his licence lapses.
Please also note that a deemed licensed representative intending to carry on an additional
regulated activity, or seeking to act as a responsible officer (if he is not already a responsible
officer), should first migrate to the new regime before lodging such applications. This
applies irrespective of whether or not he changes employment. Simultaneous submission of
Form 3(X) and Form 4 for these purposes is also acceptable.
