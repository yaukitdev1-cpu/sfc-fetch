Circular

1 November 2021
Circular to Issuers of SFC-authorised investment-linked assurance schemes (ILAS)
Enhanced Authorisation Process for New ILAS Applications
1.

The purpose of this circular is to introduce an enhanced process for applications for
authorisation of new ILAS (Enhanced Process).

Background
2.

The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) regularly reviews and seeks to improve
our authorisation process wherever practicable without compromising investor interests
and protection. In January 2014, we introduced the 6-month application lapse policy
with a view to enhancing the overall efficiency in the authorisation process (Lapse
Policy).

3.

In performing our regulatory functions, we have regard to, among others, the need to
make efficient use of our resources. An authorisation process that is more efficient can
also meet the issuers’ wish to reduce the “time to market” of their ILAS products for
public offering. As such, the Enhanced Process is introduced to increase the
transparency of the authorisation process, promote applicants’ compliance and reduce
the overall processing time.

4.

To achieve this, applicants must provide proper and quality submissions at the time of
application and throughout the application process in a timely manner. Applicants must
also ensure that their applications are in good order and are accompanied by relevant
documents that meet all applicable regulatory requirements.

5.

The Enhanced Process only relates to the authorisation process of a new ILAS
application under section 104 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance by the SFC.
Applicants are still required to obtain a “no objection” confirmation from the Insurance
Authority’s (IA) before submission of the new ILAS applications to the SFC (generally
referred to as the “green light” process).

The Enhanced Process
6.

Under the Enhanced Process, a “two-stream” approach will be adopted by the SFC in
processing new ILAS applications.

7.

Under the two-stream approach, new ILAS applications will be classified into “Standard
Applications” stream and “Non-standard Applications” stream. Standard Applications
will be fast-tracked with an aim that SFC authorisation (if granted) will be given around
2 to 3 months from the take-up date of the applications. Non-standard Applications will
be also processed under an enhanced process with, among others, more discipline in
response time with an aim that SFC authorisation will be granted in a more efficient
manner under the Lapse Policy.

8.

In general, a new ILAS application will be processed as a Standard Application if the
ILAS under application is more simple and standard in nature. Please refer to the
Frequently Asked Questions relating to Investment-Linked Assurance Schemes (ILAS
FAQs) published on the SFC website for a detailed list of criteria for Standard
Applications (see paragraph 16 below).

9.

The SFC reserves its right not to take up a new ILAS application which is deemed to be
an incomplete application and/or an application that fails to meet the applicable
regulatory requirements 1.

10. Given that Standard Applications are intended to cover less complicated applications,
applicants are required to provide proper, complete, and substantive response(s) to the
SFC’s requisition(s) within 2 months from the date of the first requisition issued by the
SFC, failing which the applications may be refused subject to the SFC’s right to grant
an extension at its sole discretion.
11. To shorten the turnaround time for both Standard Applications and Non-standard
Applications, after an application has been taken up by the SFC, time limit(s) will be
imposed on the applicants to provide substantive response to properly address SFC’s
requisition(s) in a timely manner, failing which the applications may be refused subject
to the SFC’s right to grant an extension at its sole discretion. For further details on the
time limit(s) for applicants to respond to SFC’s requisitions and related matters, please
refer to the ILAS FAQs (see paragraph 16 below).
12. Authorisation of Standard Applications and Non-standard Applications by the SFC will
be granted subject to authorisation conditions which are required to be fulfilled before
the authorisation may become effective. Such conditions may include, among others,
the receipt of a “no further comment” confirmation on the application from the IA if not
yet obtained.
Revised Performance Pledges
13. To standardise the performance pledges for all new ILAS applications (i.e. Standard
Applications and Non-standard Applications) under the Enhanced Process, the SFC will
pledge to (i) take up or refuse to take up a new ILAS application within 5 business days
upon receipt of the application; and (ii) issue the first set of response/requisitions to the
new ILAS application within 14 business days from the take-up date (Revised
Performance Pledges).
14. The Revised Performance Pledges will enable the SFC to conduct a more thorough
initial screening and assessment of new ILAS applications.
15. All new ILAS applications that are taken up by the SFC under the Enhanced Process
will be subject to the Revised Performance Pledges and the Lapse Policy.

Updated Information Checklist and ILAS FAQs
16.

To implement and provide further guidance on the Enhanced Process, the following
documents have been updated and published on the SFC website:
(a) an updated Information Checklist for Investment-Linked Assurance Schemes
(Information Checklist); and
(b) an updated Frequently Asked Questions relating to Investment-Linked Assurance
Schemes (please refer to new Quesitons 4A – 4H, 16A and 16B in Section 1 and
updated Questions 2, 4 and 9 in Section 1).

Post-vetting after authorisation
17.

Applicants must duly discharge their compliance responsibility and ensure that their
SFC-authorised ILAS are in compliance with all applicable requirements including all
applicable overarching principles and the eligibility, structural and disclosure
requirements under the SFC Handbook for Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds, InvestmentLinked Assurance Schemes and Unlisted Structured Investment Products (including
the Code on Investment-Linked Assurance Schemes) at all times.

18.

Investor protection is a fundamental regulatory principle. To safeguard investor
interests, for SFC-authorised ILAS processed under the Enhanced Process, the SFC
will conduct post-vetting to ensure applicable authorisation conditions are complied
with. The SFC will, where appropriate, take necessary regulatory action in cases of
non-compliance to ensure that investor protection is not compromised.

General
19.

The Enhanced Process shall take effect immediately and apply to all new ILAS
applications submitted on or after today.

20.

Applicants are welcome to contact SFC to discuss any new ILAS product proposals
prior to the submission of the applications to the SFC.

21.

The SFC will review the Enhanced Process from time to time and may impose
additional requirements/changes where appropriate.

22.

Should you wish to clarify any aspects of the Enhanced Process, this circular, the
Information Checklist and the ILAS FAQs and/or in case of doubt, please contact the
relevant case officers in charge.
