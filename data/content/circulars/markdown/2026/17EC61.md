Circular to Intermediaries, Responsible Officers and Representatives on
the Launch of Advanced Learning Platform by
the Hong Kong Securities and Investment Institute
The SFC is supportive of enhancing the professional competence of industry practitioners and
licensed representatives in the financial industry. To advance this objective, the SFC has
funded an important initiative of the Hong Kong Securities and Investment Institute (HKSI),
the online Advanced Learning Platform (ALP), which is launched today.
The ALP is an online portal that enables financial industry practitioners to access technologybased learning solutions at a time and place of their own choice. It complements the structured
HKSI courses taught in traditional class-room style. The e-learning portal was created to cater
for a change in the learning mode and a need for quicker response to learning needs in a
highly competitive environment. Some e-courses are free of charge, one of which is
“Measures for the Accountability of Senior Management”.
Completion of designated e-courses will be rewarded with Continuous Professional Training
(CPT) hours. Individuals who enrol in the courses would be able to check progress on their
fulfilment of CPT obligations, and intermediaries may record and monitor their staff’s CPT
completion records on the ALP to ensure compliance with the relevant codes and guidelines.
The e-learning portal is open to members and non-members of HKSI. Industry practitioners
are encouraged to check out the HKSI e-learning website.
