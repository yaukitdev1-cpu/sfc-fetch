1 November 2012

Circular to all Licensed Corporations and Registered Institutions relating to change of
SFC e-circulars distribution channel
Please be informed that a new SFC e-circulars distribution channel will take effect from 1
December 2012.
At present, the SFC uses the FinNet[Note 1] communication network (“FinNet”) as a channel to
send circulars to licensed corporations (LCs) and registered institutions (RIs).
FinNet will be terminated in the first quarter of 2013. From 1 December 2012, circulars will no
longer be distributed through FinNet and all existing e-circulars, email addresses and
mailboxes (email addresses in the format of “Your Corporate CE Number”eCirculars@finnet.hk) on FinNet also will be discontinued.
The SFC e-circulars will be distributed through Internet email starting from 1
December 2012
The SFC will post circulars on its website http://www.sfc.hk/ and notify LCs and RIs. Those
recipients with email addresses registered under the SFC Online Portal also will be notified
by email.
For LCs, such recipients are responsible officers and their delegates registered through the
SFC Online Portal.
For RIs and associated entities, it is executive officers and their delegates registered through
the SFC Online Portal who will be notified.
We trust the new distribution channel will be more convenient and timely in keeping you
updated with the SFC circulars.
If you have any query, please feel free to contact our FinNet hotline at 2283 6883 during
office hours.
