Circular to Licensed Corporations
Common Anniversary Date Program
This circular serves to reiterate the Common Anniversary Date (“CAD”) Program
implemented by the SFC for licensed persons. While participation in this Program is not
mandatory, licensed corporations are encouraged to join the Program for their own benefits.
Annual submission requirements
Pursuant to section 138 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance, licensed corporations and
licensed representatives are required to pay annual fees and submit annual returns within one
month after each anniversary date of the grant of their licences, or by such other date as may
be approved by the SFC by notice in writing.
CAD Program
As in the former regime, the SFC implements an optional annual submission arrangement
through which a licensed corporation or a group of related licensed corporations may select
one common anniversary date to submit annual returns and pay annual fees for all the
corporation(s) and representatives concerned. A common anniversary date can be any date
proposed by the licensed corporation(s) and agreed by the SFC.
It is believed that participation in the CAD Program could save the administrative costs
incurred by licensed corporations in preparing annual submissions for their representatives as
they can enjoy the convenience of processing all related annual submissions in one single
batch.
After joining the CAD program, a licensed corporation will receive from the SFC an Annual
Fee and Annual Return Combined Listing (“Combined Listing”) around its common
anniversary date every year. The Combined Listing will show a breakdown of the licensed
corporations and representatives belonging to the same CAD group as at its common
anniversary date. It will also state the annual fee payable by each of the CAD group
members. A sample Combined Listing is attached at Annex 1.

On receipt of the Combined Listing, a licensed corporation should verify the information
contained therein. The Combined Listing as completed and duly certified true and accurate
by a responsible officer1 of the corporation should be returned to the SFC together with the
required annual fee and annual returns (or no change declarations, where appropriate) within
the submission deadline. If a corporation wishes to make any amendment to the Combined
Listing (for instance, one of its representatives included in the list had recently ceased to act
for the corporation due to resignation but such cessation had not yet notified the SFC), it can
mark the amendment on the Combined Listing and state the reasons for such amendment
under the “Remarks” column. It should be noted however that the corporation is still
required to formally notify the SFC by completing Form 5 of such cessation within 7
business days after the cessation.
New representatives
A representative with accreditation to a licensed corporation that participates in the CAD
Program will be included in the CAD group of his principal automatically. Therefore, upon
the grant of the representative’s licence or on the approval of the representative’s change of
accreditation application, the SFC will adjust the annual fee payable by that representative
with a view to aligning his original anniversary date with his principal’s common anniversary
date. If it happens that the representative’s original anniversary date falls on a date earlier
than his principal’s common anniversary date, a shortfall in his annual fee will occur. In that
case, the SFC will issue a debit note to both the representative and his principal requesting
for payment of the annual fee shortfall.
How to join the CAD program
Licensed corporations interested in joining the CAD Program may apply to the SFC in
writing with 2 common anniversary dates proposed in order of preference. No application
fee is required. You may refer to Annex 2 for a sample application letter.
Existing participants of the CAD Program need not re-apply and they will continue to enjoy
the convenience brought by the Program.
Should you have any queries on the above, please contact your responsible Intermediaries
Relationship Manager of the Licensing Department.
