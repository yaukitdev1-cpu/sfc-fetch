Circular to Licensed Corporations, SFC-licensed Virtual Asset Service
Providers and Associated Entities
Cybersecurity Webinar
The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) will host two sessions of cybersecurity
webinar on 17 and 19 February 2025 respectively. The webinar will be conducted via Zoom.
Objectives and rundown
The objectives of the webinar are –


To share our observations on the report of the 2023/24 thematic cybersecurity review of
licensed corporations; and



To raise awareness on emerging cybersecurity threats in Hong Kong.

Please refer to the Appendix for the rundown and speakers of the webinar.
Who should attend
You are invited to nominate relevant management or supervisory personnel to attend. The
webinar is free of charge and 2.5 Continuous Professional Training (CPT) hours will be
offered to those who attend the whole session.
Enrolment
Each firm should only submit one enrolment form via the following link on or before 7
February 2025:
https://sfc-cybersecurity-2025.open-exchange.net/
Enrolments is limited to a maximum of two representatives per firm. Places will first be
allocated to the first nominee in each of the enrolment forms submitted by the stated
deadline on a first come first served basis. Any remaining places will be allocated to the
second nominee on the same basis.
Successful applicants will be notified by email at least two days before the webinar date.
