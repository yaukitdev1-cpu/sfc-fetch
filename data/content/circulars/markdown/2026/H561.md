Circular to All Licensed Corporations, Responsible Officers, Licensed
Representatives, Registered Institutions and Associated Entities
Regarding the Launch of the SFC Online Portal
This circular is issued to notify all licensed corporations, responsible officers, licensed
representatives, registered institutions and associated entities that the SFC has today
launched an on-line portal, which will streamline its licensing processes and procedures and
enhance communication efficiency between users of the portal and the SFC.
Initially, the SFC online portal (https://portal.sfc.hk/) will offer more than 36,000 licensees,
registrants and associated entities the option of submitting their licensing notifications and
annual returns electronically. Using the portal, users may also view their profile information
and receive messages from the SFC.
When the portal is fully operational in 2011, users will be able to carry out virtually all
licensing processes and procedures, including the submission of applications and payments,
via the Internet. At that time, the use of paper forms will largely be eliminated.
A pilot run of the portal, involving approximately 1,600 users, was launched on 14 August
2009 and has generated helpful feedback from users. The portal has undergone some
modification and enhancement during that period and is operating very satisfactorily.
Between now and the end of October 2009, all remaining users will receive letters inviting
them to use the portal. These letters will provide user names, passwords and usage
instructions.
The SFC is committed to streamlining its licensing processes and procedures and to making
them more user-friendly. Accordingly, the SFC will welcome any feedback from users who
feel that the portal can be improved in any way. Such feedback can be provided by
telephone on the numbers set out in the above-mentioned letters, or by sending an e-mail to
PortalEnquiries@sfc.hk.
