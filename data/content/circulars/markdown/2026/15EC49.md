Circular

9 October 2015
Circular to management companies of
SFC-authorized unit trusts and mutual funds
Launch of pilot revamped fund authorization process
1.

The purpose of this circular is to describe the revamped process (the “Revamped
Process”) which will be adopted by the SFC under a pilot arrangement with effect from
9 November 2015 (“Effective Date”) in processing applications seeking SFC’s
authorization of unit trusts and mutual funds (“SFC-authorized Funds”).

2.

The Revamped Process will be adopted for a 6-month pilot period ending 8 May 2016
(“Pilot Period”). We may extend the Pilot Period and/or make changes to the
Revamped Process where appropriate for the Revamped Process to be adopted
formally at the end of the Pilot Period.

Background
3.

We regularly review and seek to improve our authorization process wherever
practicable without compromising investor interests and protection.

4.

In January 2014, we reduced the time period for the lapse of new fund applications to 6
months (“Lapse Policy”). Since then, the average processing time for new fund
applications decreased by about 38% to less than four and a half months on average
while the number of funds authorized by the SFC in 2014 increased by more than a
third.

5.

The SFC believes that there is room to streamline the authorization process and further
reduce the processing time. In performing our regulatory functions, we have regard to,
among others, the need to make efficient use of our resources. An authorization
process that is more efficient and focuses more on key risks can meet the fund
providers’ wish to reduce the “time to market” of their funds for public offering.

6.

To achieve this, we need applicants to provide proper and quality submissions at the
time of application and throughout the application process in a timely manner. It is the
SFC’s expectation that applicants must ensure that their applications are in good order
and are accompanied by documents that meet all applicable regulatory requirements.

7.

To ensure that the industry is adequately consulted on the process, a technical working
group comprising representatives from asset management companies, industry
associations and legal professionals, was established in June. The working group has
met four times before the SFC finalised the revamped procedures, the minimum
disclosure requirements for offering documents and the New Information Checklist (see
paragraphs 18 & 19 below) for application under the Revamped Process.

The Revamped Process
Overview
8.

Under the Revamped Process, a “two-stream” approach will be adopted by the SFC in
processing new fund applications with the support of the New Information Checklist
(see paragraph 19 below) and a comprehensive guide on practices and procedures for
applications prepared by the SFC to facilitate applicants’ preparation for their
applications and compliance with the applicable regulatory requirements (including the
requirements under the SFC Handbook for Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds, InvestmentLinked Assurance Schemes and Unlisted Structured Investment Products (“Handbook”)
and the Code on Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds (“UT Code”)) (see paragraph 18 below).

A two-stream approach
9.

Under the two-stream approach, new fund applications1 will be classified into “Standard
Applications” stream and “Non-standard Applications” stream. Standard Applications
will be fast-tracked with an aim that SFC authorization (if granted) will be given on
average between 1 to 2 months from the take-up date of the applications. Nonstandard Applications will be processed under an enhanced process with an aim that
SFC authorization (if granted) will be given on average within 2 to 3 months from the
take-up date of the applications.

10. In general, a new fund application1 will be processed as a Standard Application if the
following criteria are met:

11. The SFC reserves its right not to take up a new fund application which is deemed to be
an incomplete application and/or an application that fails to meet the applicable
regulatory requirements3.
12. Given that Standard Applications are intended to cover less complicated applications
and with the enhanced guidance published by the SFC including the minimum
disclosure requirements for preparation of the funds’ offering documents set out in the
Guide (see paragraph 18 below), applicants are required to provide proper, complete,
and substantive response(s) to the SFC requisition(s) within 1 month from the date of
the first requisition issued by the SFC, failing which the applications may be refused
subject to the SFC’s right to grant an extension at its sole discretion.
13. For Non-standard Applications, after an application has been taken up by the SFC,
time limit(s) will be imposed on the applicants to provide substantive response to
properly address SFC’s requisition(s) in a timely manner, failing which the applications
may be refused subject to the SFC’s right to grant an extension at its sole discretion.
This aims to shorten the turnaround time of the applicants. For further details on the
time limit(s) for applicants to respond to SFC’s requisitions and related matters, please
refer to the Revamped Process FAQ (see paragraph 20 below).
14. Authorization of Standard Applications and Non-standard Applications by the SFC will
be granted subject to authorization conditions which are required to be fulfilled before
the authorization may become effective4.
Revised Performance Pledges and the Lapse Policy
15. To standardize the performance pledges for all new fund applications1 (i.e. Standard
Applications and Non-standard Applications), under the Revamped Process, the SFC
will pledge to (i) take up or refuse to take up a new fund application1 within 5 business
days upon receipt of the application; and (ii) issue the first set of response/requisitions
to the new fund application1 within 14 business days from the take-up date (“Revised
Performance Pledges”).
16. The Revised Performance Pledges will enable the SFC to conduct a more thorough
initial screening and assessment of new fund applications.
17. All new fund applications1 that are taken up by the SFC under the Revamped Process
will be subject to the Revised Performance Pledges and the Lapse Policy.

Comprehensive guide on applications, New Information Checklist and the Revamped
Process FAQ on applications
18.

In order to facilitate applicants’ preparation for their new fund applications including
submission of quality advanced drafts of the funds’ offering documents with proper
disclosure, a technical working group has recommended minimum disclosure
requirements for certain types of SFC-authorized Funds, which have been included in
the new Guide on Practices and Procedures for Application for Authorization of Unit
Trusts and Mutual Funds (“Guide”) published by the SFC.

19.

In connection with the Revamped Process, a new Information Checklist for Application
for Authorization of Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds (“New Information Checklist”) has
been prepared and posted on the SFC’s website replacing the current version with
effect from the Effective Date. Standardized templates for confirmations on
compliance required to be submitted by the applicants at the time of application are
included in the New Information Checklist. Applicants should continue to use the
existing Application Form for Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds, Investment-Linked
Assurance Schemes and Unlisted Structured Investment Products (“Application
Form”).

20.

A set of Frequently Asked Questions on Application Procedures for Authorization of
Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds under the Revamped Process (“Revamped Process
FAQ”) has been prepared and posted on the SFC’s website.

Post-vetting after authorization
21.

Applicants must duly discharge their compliance responsibility and ensure that their
SFC-authorized Funds are in compliance with all applicable requirements including all
applicable overarching principles and the eligibility, structural and disclosure
requirements under the Handbook and the UT Code at all times.

22.

Investor protection is a fundamental regulatory principle. To safeguard investor
interests, for SFC-authorized Funds processed under the Revamped Process, the
SFC will conduct post-vetting to ensure applicable authorization conditions are
complied with. The SFC will, where appropriate, take necessary regulatory action in
cases of non-compliance to ensure that investor protection is not compromised.

Transitional arrangements
Applications received before the Effective Date
23.

Existing applications for authorization of SFC-authorized Funds that were received by
the SFC before the Effective Date in respect of which no authorization has been
granted will be processed by the SFC in accordance with the existing authorization
process subject to the Lapse Policy. For further details on the existing authorization
process, please refer to the existing Frequently Asked Questions on Application
Procedures for Authorization of Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds posted on the SFC’s
website.

24.

Applicants who wish to make new fund applications during the period from the date of
this circular but before the Effective Date should use the existing Information Checklist
for Application for Authorization of Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds in support of their
applications.

Applications received on or after the Effective Date
25.

New fund applications1 received on or after the Effective Date will be processed under
the Revamped Process. Applicants must, among other requirements, complete the
New Information Checklist in support of their applications.

MRF applications
26.

The mutual recognition of funds (MRF) arrangement between the Mainland and Hong
Kong effective on 1 July 2015 is a new regulatory initiative and eligible Mainland funds
seeking authorization under the MRF arrangement (“MRF Applications”) are already
processed on a streamlined basis in accordance with the circular dated 22 May 2015
issued by the SFC. As such, the Revamped Process will not apply to MRF
Applications for the time being. It is our current intention to assess the applicability of
the Revamped Process for MRF Applications after expiry of the Pilot Period.

27.

For the avoidance of doubt, until further notice, MRF Applications will continue to be
subject to the Lapse Policy and the SFC pledges to take up or refuse to take up a
new MRF Application within 2 business days upon receipt of the application; and
issue the first set of responses/requisitions within 14 business days from the take-up
date. MRF applicants should complete the existing Application Form and Information
Checklist for Application for Authorization of Mainland Funds seeking SFC’s
Authorization under the Mutual Recognition of Funds Arrangement.

Review of the Revamped Process
28.

The SFC will review the operation of the Revamped Process during the Pilot Period.

29.

Further changes to the new fund application process may be further considered and/or
introduced by the SFC with a view to further enhancing the Revamped Process where
appropriate.

30.

Among other matters, the SFC will keep in view the possible introduction of any
demerit / deterrent measures, such as a moratorium period for applicants during which
an applicant which is in material non-compliance with respect to the application and/or
management of any SFC-authorized Funds and/or related matters will not be allowed
to submit new fund applications for a period of time. When considering such
measures, the SFC will engage relevant industry participants and strive to maintain a
balance between effective deterrence against non-compliant applications and the
overall competitiveness of the asset management industry in Hong Kong.
