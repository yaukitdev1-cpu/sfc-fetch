Circular to Licensed Corporations
Mandatory Electronic Submission of Financial Returns
This is the Fifth 1 Circular the SFC has issued to licensed corporations on electronic
submission of financial returns.
Mandatory Electronic Submission
With effect from 1 November 2008, all licensed corporations are required to submit their FRR
returns electronically and the returns can be signed by using a SFC e-Certificate or an
HKPost e-Cert (Organisational) Certificate. Please refer to the First Circular for the different
modes of electronic submission supported.
You can access the e-FRR System on Internet through the hyperlink at SFC website
http://www.sfc.hk/ to conduct your e-submission of FRR returns via Internet using SFC
e-Certificate.
If you wish to use a SFC e-Certificate but your Responsible Officers (“RO”) have not applied
for any such certificate, you should arrange for your ROs to make such application
immediately. Please refer to the Second Circular for the details of SFC e-Certificate
application.
Test Run of New e-FRR System
Persons who are required to sign and submit return on a half-yearly basis can go to the
website below in November and December 2008 to access the e-FRR System (testing
version). http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/intermediaries/supervision/efrr_trial/efrr_trial.html
Kindly note that a valid SFC e-Certificate will be needed for the industry testing.
If you need any other assistance or have any other questions, please contact our e-FRR
System Helpdesk hotline on (852) 2283 6883.
