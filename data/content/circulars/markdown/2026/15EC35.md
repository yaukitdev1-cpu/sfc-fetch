Circular to Licensed Corporations in relation to the reporting of OTC
derivatives transactions to the HKMA trade repository – Launch of mandatory
reporting under the new OTC derivatives regime
We refer to the circular issued on 18 May 2015 regarding the commencement of the
mandatory reporting and record keeping obligations under the over-the-counter (OTC)
derivatives regime to be effective in the second week of July 2015 and the invitation to enrol
in the training and simulation tests arranged by the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA).
In addition to the training and simulation tests, the HKMA and the Securities and Futures
Commission will jointly conduct three briefing sessions on 3 July 2015 in order to let the
industry better understand and prepare for the mandatory reporting obligations and access to
the reporting services offered by the HKMA Trade Repository (HKTR). The schedule of the
briefing sessions are as follows:

HKMA office (55/F Two International Finance
Centre, 8 Finance Street, Central, Hong Kong)

Attached to this Circular are the letter and enrolment form issued by the HKMA regarding the
above briefing sessions. Licensed Corporations which have activities that may be subject to
mandatory reporting obligations and are interested to attend any of the above briefing
sessions may complete the enrolment form and return to the HKTR by fax at (852) 2878
1679 or by email to hktr@hkma.gov.hk on or before 30 June 2015.
To facilitate Licensed Corporations in understanding more about the background of the
briefing session, we also attach to this Circular for your reference the Securities and Futures
(OTC Derivative Transactions -- Reporting and Record Keeping Obligations) Rules, their
relevant draft Frequently Asked Questions, and the draft Supplementary Reporting
Instructions for the OTC Derivative Transactions.
Please also note that the previously scheduled user training session of the HKTR on 3 July
2015 will be deferred to 26 August 2015. Should you have any queries regarding the above,
please contact the HKMA at hktr@hkma.gov.hk.
