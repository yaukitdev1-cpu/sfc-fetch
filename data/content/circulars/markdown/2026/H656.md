Circular to Licensed Corporations and Associated Entities
Anti-Money Laundering Seminar
The SFC, together with the Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau and the Joint Financial
Intelligence Unit, will jointly host two half-day anti-money laundering seminars on 30 November
2011 and 15 December 2011. The seminars, conducted in English and Cantonese respectively, will
be held at the Lecture Theatre, G/F, Hong Kong Central Library, Causeway Bay.
(1) Objectives & Rundown
The objectives of the seminars are

To provide participants with an overview of the latest developments in relation to antimoney laundering and counter terrorist financing (AML/CFT);



To enhance their understanding of the statutory obligations and practices of suspicious
transaction reporting; and



To examine some key aspects of AML systems and controls, from the regulator’s
perspective and a private sector perspective.

Please refer to Appendices 1 & 2 for rundown and speakers of the seminars.
(2) Who should attend
You are invited to nominate management / supervisory staff and compliance officers
responsible for AML compliance work to attend. The seminar is free of charge and 3 CPT
hours will be offered to those who attend the whole seminar.
(3) Enrolment
Please complete and return the attached enrolment form (Appendix 3) on or before
21 November 2011 by fax at 2293-5721 or email at aml_seminar@sfc.hk.
Enrolments are subject to a maximum of 2 places per firm. As seats are very limited, we will
attempt to allocate the nominees listed as first priority in all enrolment forms received by the
deadline on a first come first served basis. If there are seats remaining, we will proceed to
allocate those listed as second priority.
The SFC will send the confirmation slip directly to all individuals who are allocated a place at
least two days before the seminar. Please present your confirmation slip for admission at the
venue.
