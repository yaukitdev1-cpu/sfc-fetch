Circular to Licensed Corporations in relation to
Commencement of Resolution Regime for Financial Institutions

Further to our circular issued on 15 October 20151, the resolution regime established under
the Financial Institutions (Resolution) Ordinance (Cap. 628) (the Ordinance) commenced
operation on 7 July 2017.
Under the Ordinance, the Securities and Futures Commission is the resolution authority of
within scope licensed corporations (LCs), which include any of the following2:
(a) LCs which are themselves designated as non-bank non-insurer global systemically
important financial institutions (NBNI G-SIFIs3);
(b) LCs which are subsidiaries or branches of, or are subsidiaries of a holding companies
of, groups which are identified as being (or containing) G-SIFIs4.
For those LCs that are subject to the regime, they will still have to meet certain conditions5
before resolution can be initiated in Hong Kong.
For details of the commencement of the Ordinance, please refer to the Government’s press
release.
