Circular to Intermediaries
Proposed Voluntary Delisting and Deauthorisation of Lyxor Exchange Traded
Funds
The Commission would like to draw intermediaries’ attention to the announcement issued by
Lyxor International Asset Management (Lyxor) today on HKExnews.hk
(http://www.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2011/1207/LTN20111207194.pdf) that it
has decided to voluntarily seek the deauthorisation by the Commission pursuant to section
106 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance of 12 Lyxor exchange traded funds (ETFs) and
the delisting of these ETFs from The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited (SEHK).
In this regard, intermediaries should inform and liaise with their clients holding units in the
Lyxor ETFs as soon as possible to take any appropriate action. Intermediaries should also
take note of the following dates:
Date

Key events

8 December 2011 to 7
March 2012
(3-month period)

Investors may sell units of Lyxor ETFs on the SEHK at the
prevailing market price. Alternatively, investors may, through
their intermediaries, sell the units to SG Securities (HK)
Limited (SGSHK) at net asset value (subject to applicable fees
and charges) under the special daily buy-back arrangement.

7 March 2012

Expected last trading day of the Lyxor ETFs on the SEHK
(Last Trading Day).

13 March 2012

Expected deauthorisation and delisting day of the Lyxor ETFs.

8 March 2012 to 7
September 2013
(18-month period)

Investors may, through their intermediaries holding their units
of the Lyxor ETFs or iFAST Financial (HK) Limited (iFAST),
the service agent appointed by Lyxor and SGSHK, sell the
units to SGSHK at net asset value (subject to applicable fees
and charges) under the special weekly buy-back arrangement.

For further details, intermediaries should refer to Lyxor’s announcement or contact Lyxor on
(852) 2166 4620 or the Hong Kong representative of the Lyxor ETFs, SGSHK, on (852) 2166
4266. Intermediaries should also refer to the updated notices issued by Lyxor from time to
time.
Intermediaries are hereby advised to put in place appropriate procedures to timely
communicate material information to their clients holding units in the Lyxor ETFs and handle
the clients’ instructions, including, but not limited to, the following:
