Circular to Licensed Corporations on
Reporting of Over-the-counter (OTC) Derivatives Transactions

Today the trade repository (HKTR) operated by the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA)
published on its website another note for updating the Supplementary Reporting Instructions
for OTC Derivative Transactions (SRI 1) and the Supplementary Reporting Instructions for
OTC Derivatives Transactions – Part 2 (SRI 2):
https://hktr.hkma.gov.hk/ContentDetail.aspx?pageName=HKTR-RPT-Administration-andInterface-Development-Guide
This is the third note of update to the SRIs. Previous notes were issued in January 2017 and
February 2017. All the notes should be read together with SRI 1 and SRI 2 as the complete
set of supplementary reporting instructions. Licensed Corporations that may be subject to
mandatory reporting obligations are advised to refer to these documents.
If you have any questions regarding the substance of the reporting requirements, please call
(852) 2231 1788. For questions about the service and operation of the electronic reporting
system, or about training and simulation tests, please contact the HKTR on (852) 8100 3115
or email hktr@hkma.gov.hk.
