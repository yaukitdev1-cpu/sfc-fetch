Circular to Licensed Corporations which are Participants of the
Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited and/or Hong Kong Futures Exchange Limited
Fidelity Insurance for Exchange Participants
This circular concerns corporations which are licensed under the Securities and Futures
Ordinance (“SFO”) and are under the following categories:
Category 1: Participants of the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited and licensed for
Type 1 regulated activity (i.e. dealing in securities); and
Category 2: Participants of the Hong Kong Futures Exchange Limited and licensed for
Type 2 regulated activity (i.e. dealing in futures contracts).
It informs you of the arrangements of the brokers’ fidelity insurance scheme applicable to
you for the year commencing 1 April 2005.
The insurance requirements
Under the SFO, corporations that are licensed to carry on certain regulated activities are
required, as a condition of their licences, to take out and maintain insurance in the manner
prescribed by the Securities and Futures (Insurance) Rules (“the Rules”).
The Rules provide that, among other things, the licensed corporations mentioned above must
take out and maintain insurance in relation to specified risks for not less than the specified
amount when the SFC has approved a master policy of insurance applicable to them.
Arrangement of the scheme
For the purposes of arranging the insurance scheme under the Rules, an Industry Working
Group (“IWG”) represented by various brokers’ associations and brokerage houses has been
formed. It operates with the secretariat support from the SFC.
On submission by the IWG, the SFC will approve a master policy of insurance applicable to
licensed corporations under Category 1 and a master policy of insurance applicable to those
under Category 2 for the year from 1 April 2005 to 31 March 2006.
Following a tender conducted by the IWG, Jardine Lloyd Thompson Limited (“JLT”) has
been selected to become the insurance broker and scheme administrator for the forthcoming
scheme year. As arranged by JLT and endorsed by the IWG, each master policy of insurance
will be equally underwritten by The Insurance Company of North America, (part of the Ace
Insurance Group) (50%) and Federal Insurance Company (50%). Both insurers satisfy the
requirement on credit rating as stipulated in the Rules.

Risk coverage and indemnity level
In essence, each master policy of insurance will indemnify an insured participant against its
financial losses due to fidelity risks relating to its activities of dealing in securities and/or
futures contracts (as the case may be), subject to an indemnity limit of $15 million per
regulated activity per insurance period and a deductible amount of $3 million per claim/loss.
For details of risk coverage, indemnity level, deductible amount and other terms and
conditions, please refer to the insurance summary (evidence of insurance) and master policy
to be distributed to you by JLT separately.
Premium allocation
To allocate the gross premium among the participants, the IWG has agreed to follow the
basic principles adopted in the existing insurance scheme. Each insured participant is
required to pay a basic minimum premium1 plus a variable premium calculated based on its
annual turnover2 during the previous calendar year as a proportion of the total turnovers of all
participants under the same Category.
Furthermore, an additional premium loading will be imposed on those participants which
have any paid claims in the past five years. In that connection, the following diminishing
loading factors on their premiums will apply:•
•

30% in the 1st year after the paid claim
24% in the 2nd year after the paid claim
18% in the 3rd year after the paid claim
12% in the 4th year after the paid claim
6% in the 5th year after the paid claim

The claims loading will be computed as a percentage (as listed above) of the premium
amount that the insured participants would have been required to pay had there been no
claim(s) paid to it before. The gross premium after deducting the premiums shared by those
participants with claims paid in the past five years (including their claims loadings) will then
be allocated among those without such claims.
The claims loading will only apply to those participants that had claims actually paid above
the deductible. Therefore, the participants will not be penalized for notifying incidents that
do not materialize into claims or when the claim does not exceed the deductible.
Scheme administration
As the appointed scheme administrator, JLT is responsible for implementing and providing
administrative services to the participants the year commencing 1 April 2005. It will send
you the debit note, evidence of insurance, claims manual and a copy of the master policy.
