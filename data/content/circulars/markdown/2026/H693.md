Circular to all licensed corporations
Investor education on RQFII A-share ETFs1
The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has recently published frequently asked
questions (FAQs) on its InvestEd website (www.InvestEd.hk)2 to help investors understand
more about the new Renminbi Qualified Foreign Institutional Investor (RQFII) A-share
exchange traded funds (ETFs), which will be listed on the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong
Limited.
In addition to explaining features of this new class of investment products, the FAQs also
highlight the following areas:


transaction costs involved in trading of RQFII A-share ETFs;



key risks associated with investment in RQFII A-share ETFs; and



differences between RQFII A-share ETFs and other A-share ETFs and renminbidenominated retail funds available in Hong Kong.

The SFC encourages the industry, in particular frontline staff who deal with retail investors, to
make use of the FAQs to facilitate their handling of clients’ enquiries. New education
materials and updates will be published from time to time as new developments come into
light.

External Relations Department
Securities and Futures Commission

1

Renminbi Qualified Foreign Institutional Investor (RQFII) A-share exchange traded funds (ETFs) are renminbi-denominated
physical ETFs giving investors an access to invest in the Mainland’s A-shares. Through the RQFII investment quota granted, an
RQFII ETF can track the performance of an A-share underlying index by adopting a full replication strategy, ie, investing directly
in a portfolio of the Mainland securities with the same weightings and composition of the underlying index.
