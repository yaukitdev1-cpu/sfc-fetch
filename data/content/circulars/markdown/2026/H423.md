Circular to Licensed Corporations and Associated Entities

Anti-Money Laundering / Combating Terrorist Financing


Frequently Asked Questions on the Guidance Note
on Prevention of Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing





The SFC publishes on its website today a set of Frequently Asked Questions (“FAQs”) on the revised Guidance Note on Prevention of Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing (“Guidance Note”) which recently came into effect on 30 April 2006.


The FAQs aim to help licensed corporations and associated entities understand the application of the requirements of the Guidance Note in some commonly encountered scenarios.  It is part of the SFC’s ongoing efforts to improve market practitioners’ understanding of its regulatory requirements and raise the compliance level of the industry.  A copy of the FAQs is enclosed in the Appendix for your information.  The e-version of the FAQs is available for download on the SFC website at http://www.sfc.hk under the section “Intermediaries, Licensing & Investment Products – Intermediaries Supervision Related Matters – FAQs – Guidance Note on Prevention of Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing”.  They will be updated from time to time and licensed corporations and associated entities are encouraged to check the website regularly for any new information.





Should you have any queries regarding the contents of this circular, please contact Ms Maggie Cheung at 2840-9455.
