Circular to Licensed Corporations which are Participants of the
Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited and/or Hong Kong Futures Exchange Limited
Fidelity Insurance for Exchange Participants
This circular concerns corporations which are licensed under the Securities and Futures
Ordinance (Cap. 571) (the “SFO”) and are under the following categories:
Category 1: Participants of the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited and licensed for
Type 1 regulated activity (i.e. dealing in securities); and
Category 2: Participants of the Hong Kong Futures Exchange Limited and licensed for
Type 2 regulated activity (i.e. dealing in futures contracts).
It informs you of the arrangements of the brokers’ fidelity insurance scheme applicable to
you for the year from 1 April 2006 to 31 March 2007 (the “Insurance Period”).
The insurance requirements
Under the SFO, corporations that are licensed to carry on certain regulated activities are
required, as a condition of their licences, to take out and maintain insurance in the manner
prescribed by the Securities and Futures (Insurance) Rules (Cap. 571AI) (the “Rules”).
The Rules provide that, among other things, the licensed corporations mentioned above must
take out and maintain insurance in relation to specified risks, for not less than an amount
specified in the Rules, when the SFC has approved a master policy of insurance applicable to
them.
Arrangement of the scheme and approval of master policies of insurance
In order to arrange the insurance scheme for the purposes of the Rules, an Industry Working
Group (the “IWG”) comprising representatives of various brokers’ associations and
brokerage houses has been formed.1 The IWG operates with the secretarial support from the
SFC.
Following a rigorous tender, the IWG has decided to select Aon Hong Kong Limited (“Aon”)
as the insurance broker and scheme administrator for the Insurance Period. Aon will arrange
placements of two master policies of insurance covering respectively the licensed
corporations in Category 1 and those in Category 2 for the Insurance Period. Each master

Meanwhile, the SFC will provide Aon with the information necessary for the arrangement of

on the methodology agreed by the IWG (see below).
Premium allocation for the Insurance Period
For each scheme year, the IWG seeks professional advice from the selected insurance broker
regarding premium allocation methodology with a view to catering for any unforeseen
circumstances and looking for possible refinements to the methodology where appropriate.
Basic premium and variable premium
To allocate the gross premium among the participants, the IWG has approved an arrangement
largely following the principles adopted in the past scheme years whereby each insured
participant is required to pay a minimum basic premium2 plus a variable premium calculated
based on its annual turnover3 during the previous calendar year as a proportion of the total
turnovers of all participants under the same Category, subject to adjustments for premium
loading and/or discount where applicable.
Premium loading
In view of the impact of several recent claims on the gross premium now payable, the IWG
has considered the necessity of imposing a premium loading based on claims notified in the
preceding scheme year (i.e. from 1 April 2005 to 31 March 2006).4 After obtaining Aon’s
advice, the IWG has decided that a fixed amount of $15,000 will apply to a participant with
respect to each claim notification lodged by that participant during the preceding scheme
year. This fixed amount represents the average cost that Aon estimates will be incurred in
reviewing each notification case. The IWG has asked Aon to conduct further study and
advise the IWG on the claims loading algorithm, taking into account the experience of this
scheme year.
