Circular to Licensed Corporations
Anti-Money Laundering / Combating Terrorist Financing
Gazettal of the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing
(Financial Institutions) Bill
The Government has gazetted the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing
(Financial Institutions) Bill (the “Bill”) today for introduction into the Legislative Council
(“LegCo”) on 10 November 2010. Subject to the passage of the Bill by LegCo, the
Government aims to implement the requirements on 1 April 2012.
The Bill seeks to improve Hong Kong’s anti-money laundering (“AML”) regime for the
financial sectors to better align with the prevailing international standards as set by the
Financial Action Task Force, without affecting legitimate business transactions or the
development of Hong Kong’s financial markets. The proposed statutory CDD and recordkeeping requirements largely reflect the existing requirements set out in the current AML
guidelines issued by the SFC and the other relevant financial regulators.
The Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau has previously conducted two rounds of
public consultation on this legislative initiative. Conclusions from the second round of
consultation can be found on the following website:
http://www.fstb.gov.hk/fsb/aml/eng/latest/amlbill_conclusion.pdf. The gazetted Bill can be
