22 September 2008

Invitation to Securities Dealers and Securities Margin Financiers
Seminar on Credit Controls of Securities Margin Financing Providers and
Related Key Regulatory Requirements
The Commission will conduct a seminar in October 2008 on key findings of a review of credit
controls of securities margin financing providers. The seminar will also discuss key
regulatory requirements applicable to securities margin financing activities.
Details of the seminar are as follows:
Date

: 15 October 2008 (Cantonese session)
16 October 2008 (English session)

Time

: 4:30 pm to 5:00 pm, Registration
5:00 pm to 6:00 pm, Seminar / Q&As

Venue

: 8/F, Chater House, 8 Connaught Road Central, Hong Kong

Cost

: Free of charge

You are cordially invited to attend the seminar. To enrol, please complete and return the
attached Application Form to the Commission by email (Email address:
dlmchan@sfc.hk) or by fax (No. 2523 4598) on or before 8 October 2008. One CPT hour
will be offered to those who attend the whole seminar. Due to limited space, applications will
be considered on a first-come-first-served basis and each licensed firm can nominate up to 2
persons to attend the seminar (one in each session or both in the same session). Successful
applicants will be issued a confirmation by email or fax directly. Please present your
confirmation slip at the reception counter for registration.
Should you have any enquiries regarding the above, please contact Ms Denise Chan at 2283
6188.
