14 November 2016
Circular to all Licensed Corporations and Registered Institutions concerning
automatic exchange of financial account information
This circular is issued for the purpose of drawing the attention of licensed corporations and
registered institutions to new statutory requirements regarding automatic exchange of
financial account information (AEOI).
The Inland Revenue (Amendment) (No.3) Ordinance 2016 is intended to enhance tax
transparency and combat cross-border tax evasion. It stipulates the due diligence
obligations on financial institutions (FIs) to identify financial accounts held by tax residents of
reportable jurisdictions1, collect the reportable information of these accounts and furnish the
information to the Inland Revenue Department (IRD). The IRD will exchange the information
with the tax authorities of the AEOI partner jurisdictions on an annual basis.
Hong Kong aims to conclude negotiations with AEOI partners by the end of 2016. It is
anticipated that FIs will need to collect information of the relevant financial accounts each
calendar year from 2017, and furnish the information to the IRD each following year.
Further information regarding AEOI is available on the IRD’s website
(http://www.ird.gov.hk/eng/tax/dta_aeoi.htm).
It includes guidance notes, sample selfcertification forms and pamphlets, and answers to Frequently Asked Questions.
Licensed corporations and registered institutions are reminded to comply with the new
requirements and to note any further announcements regarding AEOI on the IRD’s website.
If in doubt concerning their obligations in relation to AEOI, licensed corporations and
registered institutions should seek appropriate legal or other advice.
