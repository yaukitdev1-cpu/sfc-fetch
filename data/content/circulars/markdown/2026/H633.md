Circular to Licensed Corporations and Registered Institutions
in relation to Investor Characterization Requirement
Following the issuance of the Consultation Conclusions on Proposals to Enhance Protection
for the Investing Public on 28 May 2010, the consequential amendments to the Code of
Conduct1 were published in the Gazette on 4 June 2010 (G.N. 3217). Amongst the
amendments, a new paragraph 5.1A relating to investor characterization requirement was
added. This provision is due to become effective from 4 June 2011.
Following representation from the industry, the Commission has considered and agreed to
treat the effective date of the investor characterization requirement under paragraph 5.1A of
the Code of Conduct as extended to 4 September 2011.
For the avoidance of doubt, please note that the effective date of the consequential
amendments of the Code of Conduct relating to:


pre-sale disclosure of monetary and non-monetary benefits under paragraph 8.3;



disclosure of sales related information under paragraph 8.3A; and



relevant changes under paragraphs 15.3 to 15.3B for professional investors

remains unchanged and will become effective on 4 June 2011.
