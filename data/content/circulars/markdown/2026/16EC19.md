Circular

11 April 2016

Circular to Licensed Corporations in relation to the reporting of OTC
derivatives transactions to the HKMA trade repository – Training and testing
for the second half of 2016 and billing arrangements for reporting services

We refer to the Circular issued on 29 October 2015 in relation to the training and simulation
tests organised by the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) and conducted in the first half
of 2016 for regulated entities to prepare for the commencement of the mandatory reporting
obligation under the new OTC derivatives regulatory regime.
On 8 April 2016, the HKMA announced another round of training and simulation tests to be
conducted in the second half of 2016. Licensed Corporations which have activities that may
be subject to mandatory reporting obligation are advised to sign up for the reporting service
of the HKMA’s OTC derivatives trade repository (HKTR) and complete the simulation test as
early as possible. However, those who had already signed up and completed a simulation
test before are not required to do the same again.
The HKMA also announced the billing arrangements for the reporting services provided by
the HKTR, which will take effect on 1 May 2016. HKTR Members are advised to complete
the relevant preparations mentioned in the announcement before the above effective date.
Attached to this Circular are the letter, billing arrangements for the HKTR’s reporting services
and enrolment forms for the training and simulation tests issued by the HKMA.
Should you have any queries regarding the above, please contact the HKMA at
hktr@hkma.gov.hk.
