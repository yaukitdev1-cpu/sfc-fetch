20 October 2016

Circular to issuers of SFC-authorized collective investment schemes
concerning automatic exchange of financial account information
1.

The purpose of this circular is to remind issuers (“Issuers”) of SFC-authorised
collective investment schemes (“CIS”) (including SFC-authorized funds, ETFs, ILAS,
REITs and leveraged and inverse products) to critically consider and assess the
potential implications of the automatic exchange of financial account information
(“AEOI”) on their SFC-authorized CIS.

Background
2.

The Inland Revenue (Amendment) (No. 3) Ordinance 2016, which puts in place a
legislative framework for Hong Kong to implement AEOI, came into effect on 30
June 2016. Under the AEOI framework, reportable financial institutions (“FIs”) are
required to identify financial accounts held by tax residents of reportable jurisdictions,
collect such information and provide such information to the Hong Kong Inland
Revenue Department (“IRD”) on an annual basis. IRD will exchange such
information on a reciprocal basis with the tax authorities of the AEOI partner
jurisdictions.

3.

According to the IRD, it aims to conclude negotiations with Hong Kong’s AEOI
partners by the end of 2016, with reportable FIs starting to conduct the due diligence
procedures to identify and collect information of the relevant financial accounts in
2017 and to provide the information to the IRD in 2018 for transmission to the
relevant AEOI partners.

4.

Further information on the AEOI can be obtained from the Inland Revenue
Department’s website 1.

5.

In view of the AEOI commencement time-frame, Issuers are reminded of the
following:

1

(a)

Issuers are expected to critically assess the potential implications of AEOI
on their business operations and the impact on their SFC-authorized CIS
and investors, and consider the need to implement any necessary process
and controls to ensure compliance with AEOI (in particular, management
companies of SFC-authorized CIS are reminded to make appropriate
arrangements with trustees and/or intermediaries to properly discharge the
obligations applicable to the CIS under AEOI). Issuers should also take
professional advice as soon as possible with their own advisors regarding
the possible implications of AEOI.

(b)

Issuers should keep investors informed of information concerning their
SFC-authorized CIS which is necessary to enable investors to appraise the

General
6.

If in doubt concerning the obligations under the AEOI, Issuers should seek
professional advice as soon as possible. Nothing in this circular is intended to be, or
should be construed to constitute legal or tax advice.
