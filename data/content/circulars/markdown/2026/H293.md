Background
1.

Under and subject to the terms of the Capital Investment Entrant
Scheme, from time to time in force (“the Scheme”), Entrants are
required to invest initially not less than HK$6.5M in total in
permissible investments and thereafter remain invested in
permissible investments for the period of the Scheme applicable to
them. Permissible investments comprise 2 classes namely real estate
in Hong Kong and specified financial assets in Hong Kong. Subject
to the terms of the Scheme, Entrants may invest in either or both of
these two classes of permissible investments in such proportions as
they wish.

2.

For the purpose of the Scheme the specified financial asset class will
include one or more Collective Investment Schemes (“CISs”)
provided they are eligible for the Scheme. Not all CISs may
become or remain eligible for the Scheme. In order to be and remain
an eligible CIS for the purpose and period of the Scheme the
following requirements MUST be satisfied.

3.

Eligibility Requirements for CISs under the Scheme

3.1 The CISs must be and remain at all material timesNote 1:
(a) authorised by the Securities and Futures Commission of the
HKSAR (“SFC”) and satisfy the conditions for authorisation
imposed by the SFC pursuant to section 104 of the Securities
and Futures Ordinance (“SFO”) and in accordance with the
Code of Unit Trust and Mutual Funds and Code on Real Estate
Investment Trust;
(b) denominated in Hong Kong dollars;

Submission
4.

Fund managers are now invited to submit to the Director names and
relevant details of CISs which they would like to include as CISs
eligible under the Scheme. Fund managers are also required to give
an Undertaking to the Director to confirm that these CISs satisfy the
eligibility requirements described in the Undertaking. A copy of the
standard Undertaking required is at Annex A.

5.

The Government plans to launch the Scheme in or about September
2003, the exact date to be announced later. The list of eligible CISs
permitted under the Scheme will be posted on the website of the
Immigration Department (http://www.immd.gov.hk) commencing on
11 September 2003. If you wish your CISs to be included by then in
the first batch of eligible CISs under the Scheme, you are required to
submit your list of CISs together with the signed Undertaking no
later than 25 August 2003 to the following address:
Other Visas & Permits Section,
Immigration Department 7/F.,
Immigration Tower, 7 Gloucester Road,
Wanchai, Hong Kong.

6.

The list of eligible CISs under the Scheme on the website of the
Immigration Department will be updated on the first business day of
every week. Fund managers of CISs eligible under the Scheme
should NOT promote CIS for the purpose of the Scheme before they
have been included in the list of eligible CISs on the Immigration
Departments’s website. Fund managers are reminded that it is a
serious criminal offence under section 103 of the SFO to issue
advertisements, invitations or documents which to their knowledge

Deletion
7.

Fund managers can also choose to delete any CISs put forward by
them as eligible under the Scheme from the list of eligible CISs on
the Immigration Department’s website should they wish those CISs
to be excluded from the Scheme. Fund managers should give at least
two months’ prior written notice to the Director if they wish to i)
remove a CIS from the list of eligible CISs maintained by the
Immigration Department; ii) withdraw a CIS as an authorised CIS in
Hong Kong; or iii) change the investment policy of a CIS such that it
no longer fulfils the eligibility criteria for a CIS for the purpose of the
Scheme.

Failure to comply
8.

In the event that any CIS included in the list of eligible CISs on the
Immigration Department’s website is found to be unable to satisfy the
relevant eligibility requirements under the Scheme, or the fund
manager is found to have broken its Undertaking, the Director may
remove the CIS concerned from the eligible list with immediate
effect. In consequence an Entrant may not be qualified for, and may
be disqualified from, the Scheme. If the fund manager is found to
have broken its Undertaking to the Director in relation to the Scheme,
the Director may report the matter.
