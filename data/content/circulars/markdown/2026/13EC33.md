Circular

12 November 2013

Circular to issuers of
SFC-authorized Investment-Linked Assurance Schemes (“ILAS”)
Streamlined Measures to Enhance the Processing of Applications for Scheme
Changes and Revision of Offering Documents in respect of ILAS
1.

The purpose of this circular is to introduce certain streamlined measures for enhancing
the processing of applications for the approval of scheme changes and the authorization
of revised offering documents in respect of ILAS (collectively, the “Applications”).

Background
2.

Under the existing requirements, ILAS scheme changes that fall within 7.1 of the Code
on Investment-Linked Assurance Schemes (the “ILAS Code”) must be submitted to the
Commission for prior approval, and prior written notification is normally required to be
provided to policyholders before such changes become effective. The revised ILAS
offering documents reflecting such changes must also be submitted to the Commission
for authorization.

3.

Currently, these requirements are streamlined under limited circumstances pursuant to
Q13 of the Frequently Asked Questions relating to Investment-Linked Assurance
Schemes published on the Commission’s website (the “Relevant FAQ”).

Streamlined measures
4.

The Commission regularly reviews and seeks to improve its processes wherever
practicable without compromising investor interests or protection. In order to facilitate
the efficient compliance management of SFC-authorized ILAS by ILAS issuers, the
Commission now introduces the following streamlined measures pursuant to which prior
approval would not be required from the Commission in respect of certain scheme
changes to the relevant SFC-authorized ILAS and the consequential amendments to
their offering documents.

ILAS Scheme changes driven solely by changes to underlying funds1
5.

Prior approval is not required from the Commission in respect of changes to an
investment option that fall within 7.1 of the ILAS Code if such changes solely reflect the
changes made to the corresponding underlying SFC-authorized fund and provided that
such underlying fund changes have been approved, or are not required to be approved,
by the Commission pursuant to the Code on Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds (the “UFdriven Changes”).

1

For the purpose of this circular, underlying funds encompass the meaning of both “underlying fund(s)” and “reference fund(s)”
as these terms are disclosed in the ILAS offering documents.

6.

In this connection, the Commission’s authorization of the revised ILAS offering
documents is not required so long as the revisions are made solely to reflect the UFdriven Changes.

7.

However, ILAS issuers should notify policyholders of these scheme changes in
compliance with the notice requirement under the ILAS Code.

8.

The notice to policyholders and any consequential revisions to the ILAS offering
documents made solely to reflect the UF-driven Changes shall be filed with the
Commission within one week from the date of issuance pursuant to 7.3 of the ILAS
Code using the relevant filing form(s).

Revisions to ILAS offering documents to reflect the (i) merger, (ii) termination and/or (iii)
withdrawal of authorization of an investment option
9.

Following the (i) merger, (ii) termination and/or (iii) withdrawal of authorization of an
investment option (collectively, the “IO Events”) under an ILAS, the ILAS offering
documents shall be updated as soon as practicable to reflect the IO Events.

10. No further authorization from the Commission is required for the ILAS offering
documents which are revised solely to reflect the IO Events. Such revised offering
documents shall nevertheless be filed with the Commission within one week from the
date of issuance pursuant to 7.3 of the ILAS Code using the relevant filing form.
Revisions to ILAS offering documents to incorporate changes falling within 7.3 of the ILAS
Code
11. ILAS offering documents may be updated to incorporate changes falling within 7.3 of the
ILAS Code and be re-issued without further authorization. However, issuers are
required to file the revised offering documents with the Commission pursuant to 7.3 of
the ILAS Code within one week from the date of issuance using the relevant filing
form(s).
Obligations of ILAS issuers
12. The streamlined measures mentioned above are expected to enhance the flexibility and
efficiency of issuers when making changes to SFC-authorized ILAS and their offering
documents. Issuers are reminded that it is their duty to exercise due care and
professional judgement to decide whether a scheme change or revised offering
documents would meet the requirements that are set out herein and in the Relevant
FAQ.
13. Issuers are further reminded of their obligations to ensure that the offering documents
(including the product key facts statement) of their ILAS are up-to-date and contain all
information necessary for investors to make informed judgement about the ILAS.
Issuers must also keep ILAS policyholders fully informed of all updated information
relating to their ILAS which is necessary to enable policyholders to appraise the position
of their ILAS on an on-going basis.

14. Issuers must ensure that their ILAS and the offering documents are in compliance with
the ILAS Code and other applicable principles and requirements contained in the SFC

and Unlisted Structured Investment Products, as well as other guidelines that are
published by the Commission from time to time.
General matters
15. The streamlined measures mentioned above shall take effect on 25 November 2013
(the “Effective Date”) and will apply to all Applications submitted on or after the Effective
Date.
16. We note that the streamlined measures do not apply to all other ILAS scheme changes
and revisions of ILAS offering documents that are not covered herein for which SFC’s
prior authorization or approval is still required. In those cases, issuers are reminded to
submit the relevant application to us using the relevant form(s) for our processing.
17. For all existing Applications submitted to the Commission before, but authorization
and/or approval in respect of which have not been granted as at, the Effective Date (the
“Existing Applications”), the issuers are encouraged to adopt the streamlined measures
to the extent applicable. The issuers may, however, choose to continue with the
Existing Applications and not make use of the streamlined measures.
18. The Commission will conduct post-surveillance on the documents and materials filed
with us pursuant to the streamlined measures to monitor compliance. The Commission
may make follow-up enquiries and/or take other regulatory actions as it considers
appropriate. Issuers are expected to respond to any enquiries made by the
Commission promptly and in an open and co-operative manner.
19. The Commission will keep in view of the streamlined measures and may impose
additional requirements/changes from time to time where appropriate. The Commission
reserves its power to require issuers to submit any proposed scheme changes and/or
revisions to offering documents for review where the Commission considers it
appropriate with respect to particular cases.
20. If you wish to clarify any aspects of this circular and/or in case of doubt on any issues
arising from the application of the streamlined measures mentioned above, please
contact the relevant case supervisor or officer.
