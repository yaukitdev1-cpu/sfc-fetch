Circular to SFC’s Registered Firms and Exempt Persons
Guidelines on Transitional Arrangements
Guidelines on Waivers of Certain Licensing Fees
In the light of the impending commencement of the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO)
on 1 April 2003, the SFC today publishes the above two Guidelines to help intermediaries
migrate smoothly to the new regulatory regime with minimal disruption to their businesses.
Guidelines on Transitional Arrangements
The Guidelines on Transitional Arrangements explain the operation of the transitional
provisions under the SFO that are generally applicable to existing registrants and exempt
persons. They also set out detailed procedures to guide existing intermediaries how to
migrate to the new regime during the two-year transitional period following the
implementation of the SFO. The major topics of the Guidelines include:
• special arrangements during the transitional period (e.g. a licensed individual only
needs to complete the same number of Continuous Professional Training hours as
required under the current regime regardless of the number of regulated activities he
is deemed to have been licensed during the transitional period);
• application for new licence or registration by existing intermediaries;
• new regulatory requirements under the SFO; and
• comparison of the current and new licensing regimes.
Guidelines on Waivers of Certain Licensing Fees
The SFC has previously announced that certain fee waivers will be made available to existing
intermediaries during the transitional period so that they will not incur extra licensing costs
over and above the amounts they are now paying. The Guidelines on Waivers of Certain
Licensing Fees provide for and explain such fee waivers.
Furthermore, the Guidelines provide for a discount on annual fees to intermediaries seeking
to convert their licences under the new licensing regime in the first year of the transitional
period. Intermediaries which migrate early can enjoy a 5% early bird discount on their annual
fees till the end of the transitional period. Details are set out in the Guidelines.

The SFC plans to gazette the above two Guidelines under section 399(1) of the SFO on 21
March 2003. They will become effective at the same time when the SFO commences.
Meanwhile, the two Guidelines can be downloaded free of charge from the SFC’s website
(http://www.hksfc.org.hk) under “Licensing Related Matters” of the Intermediaries,
Licensing and Investment Products section. Printed copies are available for sale at the SFC’s
office ($100 each for the Guidelines on Transitional Arrangements1 and $20 each for the
Guidelines on Waivers of Certain Licensing Fees).
Should you have any queries on the above, please contact your responsible Intermediaries
