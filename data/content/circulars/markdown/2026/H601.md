Invitation to All Licensed Corporations
SFC and ICAC joint seminar on business ethics and prevention of corruption
The SFC and the Corruption Prevention Department of the Independent Commission Against
Corruption (ICAC) will host a joint seminar on business ethics and prevention of corruption in
the securities industry in October 2010. The seminar will focus on relevant provisions under
the Prevention of Bribery Ordinance, corruption-prone areas in the securities sector and
related preventive measures, among others.

Details of the seminar are as follows:
Date

: 27 October 2010, Wednesday (Cantonese session)
29 October 2010, Friday (English session)

Time

: 4:30 pm to 5:00 pm, Registration
5:00 pm to 6:00 pm, Seminar / Q&As

Venue

: 8/F, Chater House, 8 Connaught Road Central, Hong Kong

Enrolment
Interested parties should complete and return the attached enrolment form to the SFC by fax
at 2523 4598 or email (email address: is_jointseminar@sfc.hk) on or before 15 October
2010. The seminar is free of charge and one CPT hour will be offered to those who attend
the whole seminar. As seats are limited, each firm can nominate a maximum of two staff to
attend the seminar. Enrolments will be processed on a “first-come-first-served” basis and
priority will be given to those who selected a particular session as their first choice over those
who selected the same session as their second choice. The SFC will send a confirmation
slip directly to those individuals who have been allocated a place. Please present your
confirmation slip at the seminar reception counter for registration.
Should you have any questions regarding the above, please contact Ms Denise Chan at
2283 6188 or Ms Madonna Yip at 2842 7618.
