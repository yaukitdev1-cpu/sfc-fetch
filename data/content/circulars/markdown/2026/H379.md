Circular to Licensed Corporations which are Participants of the
Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited and/or Hong Kong Futures Exchange Limited
Fidelity Insurance for Exchange Participants
Further to our circular dated 31 March 2005 in relation to the captioned matter, this circular
aims to inform the licensed corporations concerned of the following matters:
1. Change in the underwriting entity of the Ace Insurance Group from The Insurance
Company of North America to ACE European Group Limited; and
2. Refinement of the premium allocation methodology for Category 2 (Futures exchange
participants).
Change in underwriting entity
Through the arrangement of Jardine Lloyd Thompson Limited (“JLT”), the insurance broker
and scheme administrator for the scheme year from 1 April 2005 to 31 March 2006, the
master policy of insurance is equally underwritten by the Ace Insurance Group and Federal
Insurance Company. The Ace Insurance Group has for administrative reasons changed the
underwriting entity from The Insurance Company of North America to ACE European Group
Limited (also part of the Ace Insurance Group).
ACE European Group Limited satisfies the credit rating requirement as stipulated in the
Securities and Futures (Insurance) Rules (the “Rules”).

As per the advice of JLT and insurers, the above discount model is a common methodology
used in the insurance market to avoid allocation anomalies under a collective insurance
scheme where there is a wide range of allocation factors among the scheme participants. In
considering the premium allocation methodology, the IWG has endeavored to adopt a fair
and equitable model that would be sustainable and consistent in future scheme years.
