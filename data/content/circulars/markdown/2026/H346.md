Dear Sirs,
Re: Mr Fung Chiu (“Mr Fung”) and Mr Lian En Sheng (“Mr Lian”)
On 18 October 2004 the Takeovers and Mergers Executive of the SFC (“Executive”) issued a
“cold-shoulder order” (the “Order”) for a 24 month period in respect of Mr Fung Chiu (“Mr
Fung”) and Mr Lian En Sheng (“Mr Lian”). Please see the enclosed copy of the Order.
The Order provides that you must not, without the prior consent of the Executive in writing,
act or continue to act directly or indirectly in your capacity as a licensed corporation, licensed
representative, registered institution or relevant individual for Mr Fung or Mr Lian or any
corporation controlled by either of them or knowingly assist directly or indirectly in a breach
of this Order.
The Order is not intended to cover normal banking services insofar as those services do not
relate to activities for which a licence, and therefore an exemption, is required under the
relevant Ordinances.
If you have any questions as to the scope and effect of the Order, please do not hesitate to
contact me.
