Circular

4 December 2013

Circular to Licensed Corporations in relation to the reporting of OTC
derivatives transactions to the HKMA trade repository – updated
Administration and Interface Development Guide (AIDG)

Following our earlier Circular on 21 June 2013 that was attached with the refined technical
specifications in the AIDG from the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) for the Reporting
Service of the over-the-counter (OTC) derivatives Trade Repository of the HKMA (HKTR),
the HKMA announced yesterday that the AIDG has been further updated. Please find
attached the HKMA announcement and updated AIDG.
Licensed Corporations with activities that may be subject to mandatory reporting obligations,
or that intend to participate in the Interim Arrangement on a voluntary basis, are advised to
take into account the refinements and clarifications made to the AIDG when making
preparations for reporting to the HKMA’s TR.
Should you have any queries regarding the above, please contact the HKMA at
hktr@hkma.gov.hk.
