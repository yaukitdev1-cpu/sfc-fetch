Circular to Intermediaries
Commencement of End-To-End (E2E) Test for the
Hong Kong Investor Identification Regime (HKIDR)
Reference is made to the Circulars on 18 March 2022 and 31 March 2022 regarding E2E Test
for the HKIDR.
End-To-End Test
The E2E Test for systems relating to the HKIDR will commence from 16 May 2022 until 15
July 2022. It is mandatory for all Relevant Regulated Intermediaries1 (RRIs) to participate in
the E2E Test. RRIs should refer to the SFC’s Circular dated 31 March 2022 for downloading
the HKIDR File Transfer Connectivity Guide and relevant E2E test package published by the
Stock Exchange of Hong Kong (SEHK) in order to get ready for the mandatory E2E Test.
Responsible Officers of the relevant licensed corporations and Executive Officers of the
relevant registered institutions can register for an E2E test account starting from 25 April 2022
via the SFC’s WINGS platform. There will be three rounds of test account registration/update2
the choice of which will depend on the readiness of individual RRIs. An RRI must complete its
test account registration/update in one of the three rounds in order to participate in the
mandatory E2E Test. Please refer to the Annex for instructions on E2E test account
registration or information update.
RRIs who wish to participate in the E2E Test on 16 May 2022 should apply for the E2E test
account via the SFC’s WINGS platform on or before 12pm on 13 May 2022. RRIs may also
choose to apply for or update the E2E test account by 12pm on 2 June 2022 or 24 June 2022
respectively in order to participate in the E2E Test. RRIs can perform the E2E Test upon the
activation or information update of the test account. The corresponding test account activation
or update effective date are as follows:

Third Round

After completion of the E2E Test, RRIs should submit the E2E test confirmation reply forms
(contained in the E2E test package) by 15 July 2022 to:
1. the SFC for E2E Test on the submission of the BCAN-CID Mapping File and Reporting
Forms to SEHK’s data repository (applicable to all RRIs); and/or
2. Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited (HKEX) for E2E Test on BCAN tagging
for order submission to the SEHK trading system (applicable to RRIs who are
Exchange Participants only).
Following the successful completion of the E2E Test, Market Rehearsals (MRs) for systems
relating to the HKIDR will be arranged in Q3 2022 for RRIs to verify their readiness before the
launch of the HKIDR. Details of the MRs will be announced in due course. Subject to the
market readiness, the HKIDR will be implemented tentatively in the later part of Q4 2022 at
the earliest4.
Templates of the Reporting Forms
To facilitate the E2E Test, SEHK has published the templates of the five Reporting Forms for
RRI’s reference and they are available on HKEX’s HKIDR web corner. RRIs can either use the
templates or refer to Section 3 of SEHK’s File Interface Specifications when preparing the
reporting forms. For the avoidance of doubt, SEHK will not provide a template for the BCANCID Mapping File, and RRIs should refer to Section 2 of SEHK’s File Interface Specifications
when preparing the BCAN-CID Mapping File.
Use of ECP Secure File Transfer Protocol (SFTP) Interface
SEHK has announced the procedures for installing HKEX’s Securities and Derivatives Network
(SDNet/2) testing line, so that RRIs can also choose to submit the BCAN-CID Mapping File
and Reporting Forms to SEHK’s data repository via the ECP (SFTP) interface. For details,
please refer to HKEX’s Circular regarding commencement of the E2E test and E2E Test
package for non-EP RRI separately.
