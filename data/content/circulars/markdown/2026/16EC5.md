29 January 2016

Circular to Licensed Corporations in relation to Trade Reporting of
OTC Derivatives Transactions

The Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) announced today that the Trade Repository
operated by the HKMA (HKTR) has published a revised version of the Supplementary
Reporting Instructions (SRI) on its website: https://hktr.hkma.gov.hk/.
The primary purpose of the revision is to defer the commencement date of shared and paired
unique trade identifier (UTI) from 1 February 2016 to 1 February 2017 (see paragraphs 61
and 63). The deadline extension is intended to relieve reporting institutions from the burden
of having to build different systems to achieve compliance in the interim and ultimately with
the upcoming international standards on UTI, which are expected to be published in mid2016. It is expected that further guidance in relation to the reporting of UTI will be provided
around mid-2016 taking into account the then prevailing developments with respect to
international standards.
Taking this opportunity, additional instructions have been incorporated into paragraph 68 for
reporting identifying information of an individual when the individual does not have a Hong
Kong identity card or passport. In addition, various miscellaneous amendments have been
made to enhance clarity of the instructions.
Licensed Corporations with activities that may be subject to mandatory reporting obligations
are advised to take the revised SRI into account when preparing for reporting to the HKTR.
