Fidelity Insurance for Exchange Participants
This circular concerns corporations which are licensed under the Securities and Futures
Ordinance (Cap. 571) (the “SFO”) and are under the following categories:
Category 1:
Category 2:

Participants of the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited and licensed for
Type 1 regulated activity (i.e. dealing in securities); and
Participants of the Hong Kong Futures Exchange Limited and licensed for
Type 2 regulated activity (i.e. dealing in futures contracts).

It informs you of the arrangements of the brokers’ fidelity insurance scheme applicable to
you for the year from 1 April 2007 to 31 March 2008 (the “first year”) and potentially 1 April
2008 to 31 March 2009 (the “second year”). This is the first time that a two-year agreement
has been taken up1.
The insurance requirements
Under the SFO, corporations that are licensed to carry on certain regulated activities are
required, as a condition of their licences, to take out and maintain insurance in the manner
prescribed by the Securities and Futures (Insurance) Rules (Cap. 571AI) (the “Rules”).
The Rules provide that, amongst other things, the licensed corporations mentioned above
must take out and maintain insurance in relation to specified risks, for not less than an
amount specified in the Rules, when the SFC has approved a master policy of insurance
applicable to them.

Arrangement of the scheme and approval of master policies of insurance
In order to arrange the insurance scheme for the purposes of the Rules, an Industry Working
Group (the “IWG”) comprising representatives of various brokers’ associations and
brokerage houses has been formed.2 The IWG operates with secretarial support from the
SFC.
Following a rigorous tendering exercise, the IWG has decided to select Jardine Lloyd
Thompson Limited (“JLT”) as the insurance broker and scheme administrator for the first
year (and potentially the second year) of the two-year agreement as mentioned above. JLT
will arrange placements of two master policies of insurance covering respectively the
licensed corporations in Category 1 and those in Category 2 for the first year (and potentially
the second year). The master policies of insurance are co-underwritten by Wurttembergische
Versicherung AG (50%), Heritage Syndicate (Lloyd’s of London) (20%), Chaucer Syndicate
(Lloyd’s of London) (20%) and American Home Assurance Company (10%).
Under each master policy of insurance, an insured participant will be indemnified against
financial losses due to fidelity risks relating to its activities of dealing in securities and/or
futures contracts (as the case may be), subject to an indemnity limit of $15 million per
regulated activity per year and a deductible amount of $3 million per claim/loss. For details
of risk coverage, indemnity level, deductible amount and other terms and conditions, please
refer to the insurance documents to be distributed to you separately by JLT.
Meanwhile, the SFC will provide JLT with the information necessary for the arrangement of
the master policies of insurance to be approved pursuant to the Rules. Such information will
include the names of the participants covered under the policies, their correspondence details
and information required by JLT to allocate the gross premium among the participants based
on the methodology agreed by the IWG (see below).
Premium allocation
For each scheme year, the IWG seeks professional advice from the selected insurance broker
regarding premium allocation methodology with a view to catering for any unforeseen
circumstances and looking for possible refinements to the methodology where appropriate.
Basic premium and variable premium
To allocate the gross premium among the participants, the IWG has approved to follow the
same allocation methodology adopted in the last scheme year, whereby the premium amount
that an insured participant is required to pay equals the sum of a minimum basic premium and
a variable premium.
