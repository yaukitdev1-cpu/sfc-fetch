(1) Frequently Asked Questions on Anti-Money Laundering and CounterTerrorist Financing (Financial Institutions) Ordinance (“AMLO”)
The Commission today posted on its website a set of Frequently Asked Questions
(“FAQs”) on the AMLO and the Guidelines1, which will come into effect on 1 April 2012.
The FAQs have been prepared by the four relevant authorities2 (RAs) based on questions
raised during recent industry workshops, and is intended to assist licensed corporations
(“LCs”) and associated entities (“AEs”) in meeting the statutory and regulatory
requirements.
The FAQs have been posted on the SFC’s website at
http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/intermediaries/supervision/prevention/prevention.html.
LCs and AEs are encouraged to download the FAQs for reference.

(2) Publicity and Education Materials
Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau (“FSTB”) has recently made available a
leaflet and a poster calling for the understanding and co-operation of members of the
public in providing the necessary identification information and documentation requested
by their financial institutions under the AMLO.
These public education materials are available on FSTB’s website at
http://www.fstb.gov.hk/fsb/aml/eng/pe/Poster_Final.pdf and
http://www.fstb.gov.hk/fsb/aml/eng/pe/Leaflet_Final.pdf.
Where appropriate, LCs and AEs may refer their customers to these materials.
Limited physical copies are available in all Public Enquiry Service Centers and
Information Services Department.
