Circular

8 July 2014

Circular to Licensed Corporations in relation to the reporting of OTC
derivatives transactions to the HKMA trade repository – Testing arrangement
for the New Phase of System Implementation

We refer to our Circular dated 2 April 2014 on the launch of a new phase of system
implementation for the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) trade repository (TR)
scheduled for September 2014 to cover a batch of 15 new products. Please note that the
HKMA announced on 4 July 2014 the testing arrangement in respect of the system
implementation and also the reminder to TR members on the updated reporting standards.
Attached to this Circular is the letter from the HKMA regarding the above announcement.
Licensed Corporations which have activities that may be subject to mandatory reporting
obligations should review matters raised in the letter and plan its preparatory work
accordingly.
Should you have any queries regarding the above, please contact the HKMA at
hktr@hkma.gov.hk.
