Circular to all Licensed Corporations and Registered Institutions in relation to the
consultation conclusions concerning the regulation of alternative liquidity pools
The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has today released the consultation
conclusions on proposals to enhance and unify the regulatory regime for alternative liquidity
pools (ALPs). ALPs are commonly known as “dark pools”.
For details of the conclusions, please refer to the SFC’s press release and the consultation
conclusions paper.
