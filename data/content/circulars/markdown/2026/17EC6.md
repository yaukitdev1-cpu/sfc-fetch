Circular to all Licensed Corporations
Workshops for submission of information on Manager-In-Charge of
Core Functions (MIC) and organisational charts
Further to the SFC circular to Licensed Corporations Regarding Measures for Augmenting
the Accountability of Senior Management issued on 16 December 2016, the SFC is
organising six workshops in February and March 2017.
In the workshops, we will introduce the new features of the SFC Online Portal, through which
licensed corporations will need to submit the required MIC information and organisational
charts. The content of each workshop is largely the same. The workshops will include an
opportunity to ask questions.
The workshops are open for enrolment until 3 February 2017 on a first-come-first-served
basis, with a maximum capacity of 160 participants per session. They will take place in the
SFC office on 35/F Cheung Kong Center, 2 Queen’s Road Central, Hong Kong.
