Circular

12 January 2015

Circular to Licensed Corporations in relation to the reporting of OTC
derivatives transactions to the HKMA trade repository – Training and testing
for commencement of the new OTC derivatives regime
We refer to the public consultation conclusions published in November 2014 on the
mandatory reporting and related record keeping obligations under the new over-the-counter
(OTC) derivatives regime, which indicated that the obligations under the regime for regulated
entities, including Licensed Corporations, are targeted to commence in the first quarter of
2015.
To prepare for the commencement of the said obligations, the Hong Kong Monetary
Authority (HKMA) announced that a series of training and simulation tests will be organised
in 2015. Attached to this Circular are the letter and enrolment form that includes the schedule
for the training and simulation tests in the first and second quarters of 2015 issued by the
HKMA.
Licensed Corporations which have activities that may be subject to mandatory reporting
obligations are advised to sign up for the reporting service of the HKTR and complete the
simulation test as early as possible. However, those who had already signed up and
completed a simulation test before are not required to do the same again.
Should you have any queries regarding the above, please contact the HKMA at
hktr@hkma.gov.hk.
