Circular to Intermediaries
Frequently Asked Question on Securities and Futures (Contract Notes, Statements of
Account and Receipts) Rules

The SFC today posted on its website a Frequently Asked Question (“FAQ”) on Securities and
Futures (Contract Notes, Statements of Account and Receipts) Rules in relation to after-hours
futures trading to be introduced by Hong Kong Futures Exchange (“HKFE”). The FAQ is issued
in response to questions raised by some HKFE Participants on the reporting of trades transacted
in an after-hours session in contract notes and client statements.
A copy of the FAQ is attached to this circular for your information. The e-version of the FAQ is
available for download on the SFC website at http://www.sfc.hk under the section “Regulatory
functions – Intermediaries - Supervision – FAQs - Contract Notes, Statements of Account and
Receipts Rules”. The FAQ will be updated from time to time and you are advised to check our
website for new information.
Should you have any queries regarding the contents of this circular, please contact Ms Lorraine
Chan on 2231 1751.
