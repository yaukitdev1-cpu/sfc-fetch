Invitation to HKEx OTC Clearing On-boarding Seminar
The regulators in Hong Kong are in the process of working out the proposed regulatory
regime, including mandatory clearing and reporting, for the over-the-counter (OTC)
derivatives market in Hong Kong to comply with global regulatory reform. The Board of
HKEx has approved the establishment of a new clearing house to clear OTC derivatives
traded in Hong Kong market to support the reform.
To enable the financial sector to have a good understanding of the new OTC clearing
services and the work required to prepare themselves for the introduction of OTC
derivatives clearing, HKEx has organized two OTC on-boarding seminars for banks and
licensed corporations in early March 2012. In view of the overwhelming responses received
in the last two seminars, HKEx is organizing another session on 4 May 2012 (Friday) in Hong
Kong for those banks and licensed corporations who did not join the previous seminars.
At the seminar, we will provide the audiences information about the proposed structure,
operations and risk management of the new OTC derivatives clearing services.
