Circular to issuers of listed debt securities and structured products
regarding the application of the Dual Filing regime

This circular is directed to issuers of debt securities and structured products and issuers
intending to list debt securities and structured products on the Stock Exchange.
Recently, the SFC has received a few enquiries as to the Dual Filing regime in relation to
proposed listings of debt securities and structured products.
The Dual Filing regime as set out in the Securities and Futures (Stock Market Listing) Rules
covers the listing applications of any securities other than those issued under capitalisation
issues pro rata to existing shareholders, scrip dividend schemes, rights issues, share
exchanges or employee share option schemes.
The SFC and the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited (the “SEHK”) entered into a
Memorandum of Understanding Governing Listing Matters on 28 January 2003 to set out
clearly their respective roles and responsibilities in relation to listing matters. The SFC has
agreed in the MOU that it will follow the SEHK’s assessment and recommendation in
confirming that it will not object the listing of structured products, convertible equity securities
and debt securities, provided that the listing is not part of any initial public offering of the
shares of the issuer.
In order not to duplicate regulatory effort the SFC has further agreed with the SEHK that it
will only review draft listing documents and other related documents for listing applications of
debt securities and structured products under Chapters 15A and 22 of the Rules Governing
the Listing of Securities on the Stock exchange of Hong Kong Ltd upon request from the
SEHK. Generally the SEHK will refer listing documents or other related documents to the
SFC in respect of any new or novel types of structured products or debt securities or where
there new or novel issues in the structure of a previously approved structured product or debt
securities.
In line with the procedures for IPO share applicants, debt and structured product issuers
need only submit their draft listing documents and other related documents to the SEHK,
which if necessary, will refer the relevant documents to the SFC.
