Circular to Licensed Corporations and Associated Entities
Anti-Money Laundering / Combating Terrorist Financing
Materials for the Briefing Seminar on the New Anti-Money Laundering and
Counter-Terrorist Financing Guidelines
Both the English and Chinese versions of the PowerPoint slides presented by the SFC at the
briefing seminars on the new anti-money laundering and counter-terrorist financing
(AML/CFT) guidelines held at the Lecture Theatre of the Hong Kong Central Library today
have been posted on the SFC’s website at
(http://www.sfc.hk/sfc/html/EN/intermediaries/supervision/prevention/prevention.html).
Licensed corporations (LCs) and associated entities (AEs) are encouraged to download the
PowerPoint presentation for reference and internal training.
Further to our circular issued on 30 January 2012, we wish to remind LCs and AEs to apprise
themselves of these guidelines and make all necessary preparations to ensure compliance
with all applicable requirements when these guidelines come into effect on 1 April 2012.
Should you have any queries regarding the contents of this circular, please contact Ms Seine
Luk on 2842 7696.
