Circular to all Licensed Corporations and Registered Institutions in relation to the
consultation paper concerning the regulation of alternative liquidity pools
The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has today initiated a two-month consultation
exercise concerning the future regulation of alternative liquidity pools (ALPs). ALPs are also
commonly known as “dark pools” and “alternative trading systems”.
For details of the consultation, please refer to the SFC’s press release and the consultation
paper.
