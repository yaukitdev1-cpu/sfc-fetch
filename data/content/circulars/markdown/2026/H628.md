FATF Statement on Iran and Democratic People’s Republic of Korea
Further to our circular issued on 3 December 2010, this is to inform you that the
Financial Action Task Force on Money Laundering (“FATF”) issued an updated
statement on 25 February 2011 concerning the anti-money laundering/combating the
financing of terrorism (“AML/CFT”) regimes in Iran and Democratic People’s Republic
of Korea, which are subject to periodic review by the FATF. The statement can be
found on the website of FATF (http://www.fatfgafi.org/documentprint/0,3455,en_32250379_32236992_47221771_1_1_1_1,00.html).
In this statement, the FATF reported on the progress of the abovementioned
jurisdictions in addressing the strategic deficiencies in their respective AML/CFT
regimes and called on its members and other jurisdictions to take specific actions as
follows:
Iran
The FATF welcomed the recent steps taken by Iran but remained concerned by Iran's
failure to meaningfully address the on-going and substantial deficiencies in its
AML/CFT regimes. The FATF reaffirms its call on its members and other jurisdictions to
continue to apply counter-measures to protect the international financial system from
the on-going and substantial money laundering and terrorist financing risk emanating
from the jurisdiction.
Licensed corporations (“LCs”) and associated entities (“AEs”) should continue to apply
the measures specified in our circular of 31 March 2009
(http://www.sfc.hk/sfcRegulatoryHandbook/EN/displayFileServlet?docno=H545) when
handling transactions related to Iran.
Democratic People’s Republic of Korea
The FATF welcomed the initial engagement by the Democratic People’s Republic of
Korea (“DPRK”) with the FATF but remained concerned by the DPRK’s failure to
address the significant deficiencies in AML/CFT regime. The FATF calls on its
members and other jurisdictions to apply counter-measures to protect the international
financial system from the on-going and substantial money laundering and terrorist

(2)

FATF Statement on Improving Global AML/CFT Compliance: On-Going Process
Additionally, please be informed that in a separate statement as part of the on-going
process to improve global AML/CFT compliance, the FATF has set out an updated list
of jurisdictions that have strategic AML/CFT deficiencies for which they have developed
an action plan with the FATF and the latest progress of their action plan to address the
identified deficiencies. Details can be found on the website of FATF (http://www.fatfgafi.org/documentprint/0,3455,en_32250379_32236992_47221809_1_1_1_1,00.html).
