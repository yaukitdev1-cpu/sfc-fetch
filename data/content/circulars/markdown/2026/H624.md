Circular to All Licensed Corporations
Investor Education on Initial Public Offering (IPO) and
Trading of Listed Renminbi (RMB) Securities
The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has recently published investor education
materials on key areas that investors should be aware of before participating in IPO and
trading of listed RMB securities. These materials include Points-to-note for IPO applications
(in diagram form), Dr. Wise column and Frequently Asked Questions, all of them have been
specially developed for the subject matter.
These materials aim at educating investors on how to get prepared for IPO and trading of listed
RMB securities. Specifically, investors are advised to do the following:

Open a RMB bank account in Hong Kong for settling relevant transactions, and make
sure they have sufficient RMB in that account;
Verify if their intermediaries are on the Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Ltd (HKEx)
list1, either by asking the intermediaries directly or checking the relevant list to be posted
on the HKEx website (www.hkex.com.hk);
Find out from their intermediaries details for trading of RMB securities and the related
settlement; and
Evaluate if the way their transactions will be handled suits their needs.

The SFC encourages the industry, in particular frontline staff who deal with retail investors, to
make use of these materials to facilitate their handling of enquiries from clients. New education
materials and updates will be published from time to time as new developments come into light.
All the SFC’s investor education materials can be accessed on the SFC’s investor portal – the
InvestEd website (www.InvestEd.hk).
