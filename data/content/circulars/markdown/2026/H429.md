Circular to Licensed Corporations and Associated Entities
Anti-Money Laundering Training Seminar
Anti-money laundering (“AML”) is complex and ever-changing. Do you know what you
should do to fulfil your AML obligations under the law and regulatory requirements? Do you
want to protect you or your firm being ensnared into the criminal money web? You can learn
more about this in the coming AML training seminar and you are strongly urged to attend.
The AML training seminar is a joint effort of the Narcotics Division, the Joint Financial
Intelligence Unit (“JFIU”) and SFC. It will be held on 6 September 2006. The principal
aims of the seminar are to enhance your knowledge and skills on AML, discuss matters of
common concern in this area and introduce the new suspicious transaction reporting system
to be launched by the JFIU. The invitation letter, programme and registration form for the
seminar are attached herewith.
The seminar will be conducted in English and is free of charge. Enrolments will close on 18
August 2006 and are processed on a first-come-first-serve basis. The SFC will confirm with
the admitted participants by e-mail or fax.
If you have any queries, please contact Ms Maggie Cheung at 2840-9455.

Anti-Money Laundering Seminar for Securities and Futures Companies
You are invited to nominate representatives to attend an anti-money laundering
seminar jointly hosted by the Narcotics Division, the Securities and Futures Commission
(“SFC”) and the Joint Financial Intelligence Unit (“JFIU”) on 6th September 2006, at the
Lecture Theatre of the Hong Kong Central Library, Causeway Bay.
The seminar forms part of a series organized for financial institutions and designated
non-financial businesses and professions to raise awareness, enhance knowledge and skills,
and discuss matters of common concern relating to anti-money laundering. Target audiences
are managing staff and compliance officers of licensed corporations. The Commissioner for
Narcotics will officiate over the opening of the seminar and speakers will be drawn from the
Narcotics Division, SFC and JFIU. Programme of the seminar is attached and further details
of the seminar can be found at www.nd.gov.hk. The seminar is recognized by the SFC as
fulfilling the CPT requirements and 3 CPT hours will be given to those attending the whole
seminar.
If your corporation is interested in the seminar, please complete and return the
registration form (one form for each representative) on or before 18th August 2006 to the SFC
by fax 2523-4598 or e-mail at mkccheung@sfc.hk. As seats are limited, each corporation
should only nominate no more than 2 representatives to the seminar. The SFC will confirm
the admission by fax or email. Please bring along the confirmation slip from the SFC to the
seminar to facilitate registration at the venue. If you have any queries, please feel free to
contact Ms. Maggie Cheung at 2840 9455 of the SFC .
I look forward to your corporation’s attendance at the seminar.

Yours faithfully,
(Nelson Cheng )
for Commissioner for Narcotics
