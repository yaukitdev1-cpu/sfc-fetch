Circular to Intermediaries licensed for dealing in securities
Some brokers have recently expressed interests in placing the Offer Shares1 of the United
Company Rusal Limited (“the Company”) and providing margin financing to their clients to
subscribe for or purchase the Offer Shares. This circular reminds intermediaries of the need
for prudent risk management and compliance with applicable requirements set out in the
conditions that the SFC is imposing on the listing of the Company (“Conditions”).
Intermediaries who provide margin financing to their clients to subscribe for or purchase the
Offer Shares are reminded of the existing requirements to prudently manage their risks. In
particular, the intermediaries should:
a)

Set a prudent credit limit for each client taking into account the financial situation of the
client;

b)

Assign a prudent margin ratio on the Offer Shares and collect adequate margin from
each client; and

c)

Monitor the overall financial risks arising from the provision of margin financing on the
Offer Shares and manage the aggregate exposure to the Offer Shares.

Intermediaries placing the Offer Shares should also familiarize themselves with all the
Conditions, including requirements on the eligibility of placees and compliance by the
intermediaries with the suitability requirements under paragraph 5.2 of the Code of Conduct
as more fully described in the Conditions. Firms are expected to put in place adequate
systems and procedures so as to ensure that all the applicable requirements set out in the
Conditions are fulfilled.
Imprudent risk management and breaches of the requirements under the Conditions by
intermediaries will be viewed seriously by the SFC.
