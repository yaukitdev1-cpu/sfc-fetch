Circular to all Licensed Corporations concerning Pilot Bond Grant Scheme
This circular aims to draw the attention of licensed corporations to the Pilot Bond Grant
Scheme (Scheme) announced by the Financial Secretary in the 2018-19 Budget. The
Scheme covers eligible enterprises issuing bonds in Hong Kong for the first time. The
commencement date of the Scheme will be the date of the completion of legislative process
for the 2018-19 Budget. The Scheme will be valid for a period of three years.
The Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) has issued a circular and a press release today
providing details of the Scheme, including eligibility criteria and application process.
Interested bond issuers and arrangers are encouraged to consult the HKMA at the early
stage of a bond issuance process.
Should you have any questions regarding the Scheme, please email to pbgs@hkma.gov.hk
or Mr Seth Lau (swclau@hkma.gov.hk) of the HKMA.
