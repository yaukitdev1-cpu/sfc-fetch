## CIRCULAR TO ALL REGISTERED AND LICENSED FIRMS ON INTERNET TRADING AND ADVISING

7 July 2000

## Circular to All Registered and Licensed Firms on Internet Trading and Advising

The increasing use of Internet technology has created new business opportunities for financial intermediaries. In particular, the Commission has noted that there is a growing trend for registered and licensed firms to expand their businesses to include trading and advising services via the Internet.

The Commission wishes to advise all registered and licensed firms that before they commence their Internet business activities, they should:

1. inform the Commission's Licensing Department in writing of their intended Internet activities and provide relevant information which includes the revised business plan, a description of the computer system, a description of account opening procedures and information on experience and qualification of responsible staff in charge of the Internet services;
2. prepare a demonstration to the Commission of the business operations via the Internet when ready; and
3. notify the Commission in writing the effective date of launching Internet services and the address of the website.

With respect to account opening procedures through the Internet, registered and licensed firms should establish appropriate measures to ensure compliance with the applicable Code and Guidance Notes including paragraph 5.1 of the Code of Conduct for Persons Registered with the Commission ("Code of Conduct"), paragraph 6.2.6 of the Guidance Notes on Internet Regulation ("Internet Regulation Guidance Notes") and paragraph 4.1 of the Revised Guidance Notes Regarding Money Laundering ("Money Laundering Guidance Notes").

If you have already commenced your Internet trading / advising activities without having previously notified the Commission, you should immediately provide the Commission with the information listed out in paragraph (1) above and strictly observe the Code of Conduct, Internet Regulation Guidance Notes and Money Laundering Guidance Notes.

Registered and licensed firms are reminded to comply at all times with the Code and Guidance Notes in conducting their businesses via the Internet. A copy of the Code and Guidance Notes could be obtained from the Commission's website at www.hksfc.org.hk .

Should you have any queries on the above, please contact your responsible manager of the Licensing Department.
