Circular to Licensed Corporations
Documentation Updates for OTC Derivatives Trade Reporting
The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) issues this circular to remind licensed
corporations (LCs) about the Hong Kong Monetary Authority’s (HKMA) updates to the
Reference Manual for Over-the-counter (OTC) Derivatives Trade Repository (HKTR) of the
HKMA Reporting Service (Reference Manual). A copy of the HKMA’s Notice (Notice) issued
today in this connection is attached to this Circular. The changes, as summarised below, focus
on the system implementation of data archiving and the outage window on Sundays.
ISO implementation of data archiving and revised outage hours on Sundays
Following the system implementation of ISO 20022 on 22 September 2025, the HKTR system
will be further enhanced to support the archiving of historical OTC derivatives transaction data,
along with associated information such as valuations, margin and collateral, effective 16 March
2026.
In line with these system enhancements, the HKMA announced on 19 December 2025 and on
6 February 2026 the system behaviour for data archiving and the revised schedule for system
maintenance and adjustment to the Sunday’s outage window. Data archiving for ISO will be
conducted regularly during the maintenance window from 12:00 PM (Sunday noon) to 00:30
AM (Monday). Pursuant to these updates, the Reference Manual has been refined for the
revised Sunday outage. Meanwhile, the existing operating hours on the weekdays (Monday to
Friday) and Saturdays remain unchanged.
For full details, please refer to the Reference Manual and the technical documents available
on the HKTR Infopage, including the Operating Procedures for the TR Reporting Service (User
Manual) and the Administration and Interface Development Guide.
LCs that may be subject to mandatory reporting obligation are advised to refer to the Notice.
For questions about the service, operation and testing arrangement of the HKTR, please
contact the HKTR at 8100 3115 or email to hktr@hkma.gov.hk. For questions about policy or
regulatory requirements, please email to otc_enquiries@sfc.hk (for LCs and Central
Counterparties).
