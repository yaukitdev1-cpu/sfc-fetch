(1)

United Nations Sanctions (Afghanistan) Regulation 2002 (“Regulation”) and United
Nations (Anti-Terrorism Measures) Ordinance (“UNATMO”)
This is to inform you that an updated list of names designated by the United Nations
Security Council Committee (“UNSCC”) was published in the Gazette (G.N. 6319)
on 4 October 2002 in accordance with section 10 of the Regulation. In addition, an
updated list of names designated by the UNSCC as terrorists or terrorist associates
was published in the Gazette (G.N. 6343) on 11 October 2002 in accordance with
section 4 of the UNATMO. These superceded all previous lists under the respective
legislation. A copy of these updated lists are available on the Government website
www.info.gov.hk/pd/content/index.htm.
The SFC reminds all registered intermediaries to check the names in the Government
notices against their records and to take all necessary steps to ensure compliance with
the Regulation and the UNATMO, including the reporting to the JFIU of any
transactions or relationships you have or have had with the named individuals or
entities. Previous circulars on this subject are available in SFC’s website
www.hksfc.org.hk.

(2)

Non-cooperative Countries and Territories
Further to our circular dated 10 July 2002, we would like to draw your attention to the
removal of the following four jurisdictions from the list of NCCTs by the Financial
Action Task Force (“FATF”).
Dominica
Marshall Islands

Niue
Russia

The FATF decided to remove these jurisdictions from the list of NCCTs at its recent
Plenary Meeting, recognising that they have addressed the identified deficiencies
through the enactment of legal reforms and by adoption of concrete steps to
implement these reforms.
Consequently, the procedures prescribed in
Note
Recommendation 21 of the FATF Forty Recommendations have been withdrawn.
No new NCCTs have been identified and the updated list of the remaining 11 NCCTs
is as follows:
Cook Islands
Egypt
Grenada
Guatemala

Indonesia
Myanmar
Nauru
Nigeria

Philippines
St. Vincent and the Grenadines
Ukraine

Registered intermediaries are advised to continue to give special attention to
businesses and transactions with persons, including companies and financial
institutions, in the above NCCTs, in accordance with Recommendation 21 Note of the
FATF Forty Recommendations.
The FATF has previously called on its members to apply further counter-measures to
Nauru because of its inadequate progress made to address the serious deficiencies in
its anti-money laundering regime. Due to the failure of Nauru to address the
deficiencies of its offshore banking sector, the FATF calls for its members to continue
to apply counter-measures against Nauru as set out in our previous circular dated 14
December 2001.
Furthermore, in view of the inadequate progress made by Nigeria and Ukraine to
address the deficiencies in their anti-money laundering regimes, the FATF has
recommended the application of counter-measures from 15 December 2002 in respect
of these two jurisdictions unless their governments enact significant legislation which

(3)

US President’s Executive Order
Further to our circular dated 9 September 2002, this is to inform you that the US
Government designated three additional lists of names under the US President’s
Executive Order 13224 (the “Executive Order”), on 30 September 2002, 10 October
2002 and 18 October 2002, respectively. A copy of the lists are attached for your
reference.
Registered intermediaries should check whether they have had transactions or
dealings with the entities or individuals identified in these lists. In order to fulfil the
obligations under section 12 of the UNATMO, registered intermediaries should
promptly report to the JFIU in the usual way if such reviews reveal any suspicious
accounts, transactions or dealings.
As the designated entities and individuals under the Executive Order may be updated
by the US Government from time to time, you are reminded to browse the website of
the Office of Foreign Assets Control under the US Treasury at www.ustreas.gov/ofac
for the latest information.
