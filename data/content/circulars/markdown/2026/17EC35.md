Circular to Licensed Corporations
Submission of information on Managers-In-Charge of
Core Functions (MIC) and organisational charts
With reference to the circular issued on 16 December 2016 regarding Measures for
Augmenting the Accountability of Senior Management, all licensed corporations should
submit their MIC information and organisational charts to the SFC on or before 17 July 2017.
The SFC wishes to remind licensed corporations, which have not yet submitted the required
information (including the organisational charts), to do so within the stated timeframe.
Furthermore, MICs of Overall Management Oversight function and Key Business Lines
function, who are not yet responsible officers, should apply for approval to become
responsible officers on or before 16 October 2017.
If you have any queries on the matter, please contact your Licensing case officers whose
contact details can be found on the SFC Online Portal.
