Circular to Management Companies of SFC-authorised Real Estate Investment Trusts
(“REITs”)
Electronic Dissemination of Public Announcements of SFC-authorised REITs
1.

The SFC notes that the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong (“SEHK”) will abolish the Main
Board requirement for paid announcements and launch the new regime for electronic
dissemination of regulatory information (referred to collectively as the “Electronic
Disclosure Project”) with effect on 25 June 2007. The SFC hereby clarifies the
implementation of the electronic dissemination regime among SFC-authorised REITs.

Background
2.

SFC-authorised REITs are required to make timely disclosure of information to their
unitholders by way of announcements. Note to Rule 10.2 of the Code on REITs provides
guidance on the manner of publication of announcements.

3.

In light of the market demand for more timely information disclosure, the SEHK has
embarked on the Electronic Disclosure Project and amassed market support for its
proposals. According to the SEHK, the Electronic Disclosure Project is to be
implemented in phases, with a transitional period of six months commencing on 25 June
2007, during which time all Main Board issuers are required to publish notifications in
the newspapers instead of the need to publish full announcements. One year after the
commencement of the transitional period, all issuers shall have their own websites and
publish their Listing Rules documents thereon for free access by investors.

4.

Upon the implementation of the Electronic Disclosure Project, Main Board issuers are
under a mandatory requirement to submit Listing Rules documents to the SEHK
electronically through the SEHK’s Electronic Submission System.

Dissemination of Public Announcements of SFC-authorised REITs
5.

Since SFC-authorised REITs are listed on the SEHK, the SFC has adopted the policy of
requiring SFC-authorised REITs to implement the same method of information
dissemination that is practised among other Main Board issuers.

6.

Note to Rule 10.2 of the Code on REITs provides that “electronic means of publication
may also be considered by the Commission”. In light of the new disclosure regime
among other Main Board issuers, and the enhanced timeliness of information
dissemination to be achieved as a result, the SFC now informs managers of SFCauthorised REITs that, beginning 25 June 2007, insofar as they adopt such disclosure
dissemination practice as that set out in the revised Listing Rules in relation to the
Electronic Disclosure Project, their electronic publication of announcements shall be
regarded as being in compliance with the Code on REITs requirement in respect of the
manner of publication.

7.

In line with the SEHK proposal, all REITs managers are required to publish notifications
in the newspapers during the transitional period.
Further, the issuance of press
announcements will be optional.

8.

REIT managers should and are reminded to consider whether amendments to the trust
deeds of the REITs are necessary before implementation of the new disclosure
dissemination regime, and if so, make such amendments in accordance with the terms of
the relevant trust deeds.

9.

For the sake of clarity, REIT managers are reminded that, prior to submission to the
Electronic Publication System, all announcements and notifications will remain to be
subject to approval by the SFC, unless otherwise informed by it.
