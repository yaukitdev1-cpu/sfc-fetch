The Securities and Futures Commission (the "SFC"), Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited ("HKEx") and Federation of Share Registrars Limited (the "Federation") invite market participants and interested parties to submit written comments on the [Joint consultation paper on a proposed operational model for implementing a scripless securities market in Hong Kong](openFile?refNo=09CP5) (available as Acrobat PDF file) before close of business on **31 March 2010**. Any persons wishing to submit comments should, where applicable, provide details of any organizations the views of which they represent.

**Please note that the names of the commentators and the contents of their submissions (including any personal information contained therein) may be published, in whole or in part, on the website of the SFC, HKEx and/or the Federation and in other documents to be published by any of them. In this connection, please read the [Personal Information Collection Statement](/sfcConsultation/html/EN/pics1.html). If you wish to remain anonymous as a commentator, please so indicate when submitting your input.**
