The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC), Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited (HKEX) and the Federation of Share Registrars Limited (FSR) welcome comments from market participants and interested parties on the subject consultation.


Please note:



- If you wish to submit the viewpoints or ideas of the organisation you represent, please make sure you include the name of the organisation in your submission.

- If you wish to suggest alternative approaches, we encourage you to send proposed amendments to the consultation document for our consideration.

- The names of the respondents and the content submitted may be published on the website of the SFC, HKEX or the FSR and in other documents published by any of them. Please see the [Personal Information Collection Statement](https://www.sfc.hk/web/EN/files/SOM/Consultation/PICS_Eng.pdf) for details.


If you wish to remain anonymous as a respondent, please so indicate in your submission.
