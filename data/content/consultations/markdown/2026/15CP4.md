The Hong Kong Monetary Authority (the &ldquo;HKMA&rdquo;) and Securities and Futures Commission (the &ldquo;SFC&rdquo;) welcome comments from market participants and interested parties on the subject consultation.


Please note:




- If you wish to submit the viewpoints or ideas of the organisation you represent, please make sure you include the name of the organisation in your submission.

- If you wish to suggest alternative approaches, we encourage you to send proposed amendments to the consultation document for our consideration.

- The names of the respondents and the content submitted may be published on the HKMA and/or SFC Web site and in other documents published by the HKMA and/or SFC. Please see [Personal Information Collection Statement](http://www.sfc.hk/web/EN/files/SOM/Consultation/Personal%20Information%20Collection%20Statement%20(Eng).pdf) for details.






If you wish to remain anonymous as a respondent, please so indicate in your submission.
